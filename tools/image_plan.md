# Blog image plan

Two images per blog: a 16:9 hero and one inline image placed after the named section.
Save them as hero.jpg and inline.jpg in output/<talk>/blog/assets/.

## Rules for every image (any generator)

Every prompt below already ends with the standard ending, so it can be pasted into any image generator as it is:

> *Warm natural light, cinematic, high detail, authentic Indian setting. 16:9 aspect ratio. No text, letters or writing anywhere in the image.*

- **No text:** no letters, words or signs in the image (generators garble lettering, Devanagari especially).
- **16:9**, warm natural light, cinematic, high detail; authentic Indian settings and people.
- **Deities and murtis:** respectful, traditional iconography, restrained ornamentation; check the number of arms and the objects held.
- **No clichés:** no neon or "New Age" glow, no stock-photo poses, no piles of gold coins.
- **Nothing literal that is hard to look at:** show death, suffering, violence or crime only through symbols.
- **No real people:** no recognisable face of Acharya Ji or anyone else.
- **Negative prompt** (for generators that take one, such as Stable Diffusion, SDXL, Flux): `text, letters, watermark, signature, blurry, deformed hands, extra limbs, nsfw`
