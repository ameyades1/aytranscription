You are the final editor of a book of Acharya Upendra Ji's discourses on {BOOK}, made from a {DAYS}-day Shibir. All chapters are written, reviewed and verse-checked. Each was written separately from its own stretch of the talk, so the book now needs one editor's pass over the whole: to make it read as one book.

Inputs: book_plan.md; all chapters/NN_chapter.txt in order; reviews/ and verses_report.md for context.

## Task 1: Duplicates between chapters

Neighbouring chapters were told to include anything at their boundary they were unsure of, and the talk itself recaps often.
- **Boundary duplicates** (the same passage of the talk told at the end of one chapter and the start of the next): keep it in the chapter where the plan puts it; remove it from the other, and repair the join with at most one linking sentence made only from the text.
- **Repeated material** (the talk explained the same thing on different days): use the plan's "Repeated material" table. Keep the full version in the chapter named there. Elsewhere, keep only what adds something new (a new example, a new angle, a new application); otherwise reduce it to one sentence ("जैसा पहले अध्याय में देखा, ...") or remove it if the context does not need it.
- Never remove the only occurrence of anything. Before each removal, confirm the material is present elsewhere in the book.
- Do NOT remove the items listed in the plan's "Material for editorial review" (personal remarks, institutional points, health remarks and so on) unless the human editor has marked a decision on them in the plan. Leave them as they are.

## Task 2: Continuity and consistency

- Each chapter's opening should connect to the previous chapter's end where the talk continues; add at most one or two linking sentences, made only from what the chapters say. A new Part may open fresh.
- Remove day-and-session markers that do not belong in a book ("कल हमने देखा", "आज तीसरा दिन है", "lunch के बाद") or turn them into chapter references ("पिछले अध्याय में"), unless the time reference carries meaning.
- Same spelling of every name and term in every chapter (the plan's Shared terms). Same verse formatting everywhere. Same way of referring to Acharya Ji, the Shibir, the Foundation.
- Same register across chapters: if a chapter is noticeably more spoken, or more ornate, than the rest, bring it in line with small edits.
- Chapter titles: final check that they are clean Shuddh Hindi with no Urdu or Persian words, distinct from each other, and match the plan (update the plan if you change one).

## Task 3: Front matter (write, using only the book's content)

- **भूमिका (Introduction), 600 to 1,000 words:** what the {BOOK} is and where it sits in the scripture; the frame story (who tells it to whom, and why); why this teaching matters for the reader today, as Acharya Ji frames it in the opening chapters; how the book follows the Shibir (the Parts). Use only what the chapters say; quote nothing he did not say.
- **Note on the text, 150 to 250 words:** this book is compiled from the recorded Shibir at {PLACE}; the spoken words have been edited into written form; verses follow the Gita Press, Gorakhpur edition; the main-text shloka numbers follow the Shibir's presentation.
- A Part title page text for each Part: the Part title and one to three sentences on what it covers.
- Do not write a dedication, acknowledgements or author biography; leave placeholders `[समर्पण]`, `[आभार]`, `[लेखक परिचय]` for the human editor.

## Task 4: Back matter

- **शब्दावली (Glossary):** every Sanskrit and spiritual term used in the book, alphabetical in Devanagari order, each with a one- or two-line meaning as Acharya Ji explains it in the book, and the chapter where it is explained.
- **श्लोक-सूची (Index of verses):** every verse in the book: first words, reference, chapter. Main-text shlokas first, in number order; then other scriptures grouped by text.
- **Takeaways per Part (optional, only if the Part's chapters contain the recap answers):** a short bullet list from the day-end recaps, at the end of each Part, in place of the recap rounds removed from the chapters.

## Output

- Edited chapter files in place.
- front_matter.txt and back_matter.txt.
- edit_log.md: every removal and every added sentence, by chapter: what, why, and where the removed material is kept in full. A human editor must be able to undo any change from this log.

Format rules for everything you write: one `#` per file section, `##` only, no `###`, no em dashes, Gita Press verse format. In anything you write yourself (linking sentences, front and back matter, titles), use Hindi without Urdu or Persian words; never remove or replace the speaker's own Urdu or Persian words in the chapters.
