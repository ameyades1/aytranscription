## What the raw input looks like

Expect all of these, and fix each:

1. **No punctuation, one short phrase per line**, sometimes with timestamps (`00:12:31.400 --> 00:12:34.900`) or segment numbers. Remove timestamps and numbering. Join the phrases into full sentences with Hindi punctuation (। ? ! , "").
2. **Hindi and English mixed in one sentence** (code-switching). Acharya Ji speaks Hindi with many English words and whole English sentences. Keep the language exactly as spoken:
   - Hindi stays in Devanagari.
   - English words and sentences stay in Latin script, spelled correctly ("Honesty is the only policy", "accept the present tense", "PhD").
   - When the recogniser wrote an English word in Devanagari (फॉचर, इंश्योरेंस, रिपेरिंग), write it in Latin script if he clearly said it in English (future, insurance, repairing). Common English words that are now normal in spoken Hindi (डॉक्टर, स्कूल, बस) may stay in Devanagari.
   - When the recogniser wrote a Hindi word in Latin script, write it in Devanagari.
   - Marathi or other-language phrases he uses stay as spoken, in Devanagari.
3. **Garbled Sanskrit.** Recognisers break Sanskrit badly (शर्णम्भ्रज for शरणं व्रज, श्रद्धावान लभते for श्रद्धावाँल्लभते). Restore every verse, mantra and Sanskrit phrase to its correct Devanagari form:
   - Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, Yoga Sutra, Ramayana, Bhaja Govindam, Chanakya Niti, Durga Saptashati and other well-known texts: use the Gita Press, Gorakhpur text.
   - The main text of the Shibir ({BOOK}): restore it to the Gita Press Mahabharata text where you can identify the shlok.
   - If you can identify a verse only partly, write what is clearly audible and mark the doubtful part `[?]`.
   - If he recites only part of a verse, write only the part he recited. Do not complete it here; that happens in the book stage.
   - Mark each verse with its reference in square brackets after it when you are sure: `[श्रीमद्भगवद्गीता ४.३३]`, `[अश्मा गीता, श्लोक ५]`. Leave it out if you are not sure.
4. **Proper names and terms** misheard (युधिस्टर, जनमेजय spelt five ways, जगनाथ). Use one correct spelling throughout: युधिष्ठिर, जनमेजय, वैशम्पायन, व्यास, जनक, अश्मा, जगन्नाथ, श्रीकृष्ण, पतञ्जलि, चाणक्य, अंतर योग, श्री विद्या, शिविर/शिबिर (keep whichever he says). Deity names, rishis, scriptures, places and the Foundation's programmes must be spelled correctly.
5. **Recognition loops and junk**: the same phrase repeated many times, "[Music]", "[संगीत]", "धन्यवाद धन्यवाद" hallucinated over silence, captions from a video playing in the hall. Remove loops and junk. If a video was played, write `[वीडियो चलाया गया: <title if said>]` once, and keep Acharya Ji's comments on it.
6. **Slides read aloud.** He often reads text from a presentation and then comments. Keep what he reads, and mark it so the book stage can tell it apart: `[स्लाइड] <text he read>`. Mark the slide's heading if he reads one (`[स्लाइड: अर्थ]`, `[स्लाइड: उदाहरण]`, `[स्लाइड: श्लोक १२]`).
7. **Other speakers.** Audience questions and answers, a moderator, a participant's answer in a recap round. Label each change of speaker on a new line: `आचार्य जी:` and `प्रश्न:` / `प्रतिभागी:` / `संचालक:`. If a contribution is inaudible, write `[अस्पष्ट]`. Do not invent what an inaudible person said.

## Clean-up allowed

- Fix grammar slips, wrong gender or number agreement, and spelling.
- Remove pure fillers and false starts: "मतलब", "तो जैसे कि", "ना", "you know", "right?" used as a tic, repeated words ("वो वो वो"), abandoned half-sentences that he restarts.
- Keep tags that carry meaning or tone: a real question to the audience ("आपके पास गीता की प्रति है?"), "ठीक है?" when he is checking understanding. The book stage will decide what to drop.

## Clean-up NOT allowed

- Do not translate. Do not summarise. Do not shorten examples, stories, jokes, asides, personal remarks, institutional remarks or recaps.
- Do not reorder anything. The transcript must follow the recording minute by minute.
- Do not add explanations, verse meanings, or anything he did not say.
- Do not "correct" his interpretation of a verse, his facts, numbers or claims. Fix only how it was recognised.

## Paragraphs

Break the text into paragraphs by topic, three to eight sentences each. A new verse, a new slide, a new example or a change of speaker starts a new paragraph.

## Non-teaching content

Logistics (breaks, "माइक ठीक करो", "PPT दिखाओ", meal times), channel promotions and subscribe requests: keep them in place but put each on its own line starting `[व्यवस्था]` so the book stage can drop them. Do not delete them.
