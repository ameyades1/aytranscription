Hindi Register of the Antar Yog Blogs

Before and after the register pass on all 146 Hindi blogs · 28 November 2026

Summary. The blogs were already strongly Sanskrit-leaning (tatsama). The register pass replaced 89 of the 126 Urdu/Persian words the rules target and 137 of the 154 other names for the speaker, in 77 blogs, word by word in context. Sanskrit-derived words now outnumber their Urdu/Persian counterparts by about 46 to 1 (it was 18 to 1). What remains (37 words, 17 names) was kept on purpose, with a recorded reason for each. All SEO checks pass and no English text changed.

1. What was measured

All 146 Hindi blogs, in the text that is published (the SEO-fixed text where it exists), about 136,000 words. Whole words only, with plural endings; scripture verse blocks are left out. "Before" is the text before any register edit; "after" is the text now. The word lists and rules are in tools/prompts/hindi_register.md; the counts come from tools/check_hindi_register.py.

5. Analysis

  • The register is now consistent. The Urdu/Persian words that carry the subject (असर, सवाल, ताकत, फर्क, सिर्फ) are almost gone, and the speaker is "आचार्य जी" throughout, apart from 17 places that are not about him.
  • It did not become archaic. Rule 3 words stay at 0, and the everyday words of Rule 2 (तरह, शायद, रिश्ता, हमेशा, कीमत; 228 in all) were deliberately left, so the text still reads naturally.
  • The change was small. About 226 words in 136,000 (0.17%), in 77 of 146 blogs. Nothing else in the texts changed, so meaning, SEO and images are intact.
  • The biggest gain was the name. 137 of the 226 changes were the speaker's name, which readers notice more than single words.
  • Judgement mattered. 50 places were kept on purpose. A blind search-and-replace would have broken SEO titles and idioms, and changed other people's words.
