*What do you really ask for when you go to a Guru?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that some disciples want instant remedies or peace, but the sattvic disciple asks where they went wrong. Like daily insulin, a remedy without understanding lets problems return.

💭 _When you last sought guidance, what did you ask for?_
