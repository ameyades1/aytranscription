*Why worship a murti if God has no form?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that as beings with form, we need a form to begin. Every tradition has a symbol, like a cross or a crescent. Ours gave it artistic shape.

💭 _When you bow before a symbol, have you asked what stands behind it?_
