*Why do sense pleasures never last?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that physical bliss is tied to outer objects, so when the object goes, the joy goes too. When the drink runs out, so does the pleasure, leaving only craving and habit.

💭 _Which of your joys today rests entirely on something outside you?_
