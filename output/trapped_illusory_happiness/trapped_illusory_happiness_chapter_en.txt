# Chapter: Physical Bliss, the Trap of Fleeting Pleasure

## The Types of Joy and Their Order

The scriptures describe many types of joy. Simply seeing them, one understands them; they are self-explanatory. Their order is this: first tamasic, then somewhat rajasic, and then somewhat sattvic.

So, just by looking, you will have understood what comes first: **physical bliss** (sharirik anand). Physical bliss means the happiness that comes from the body. It is an extremely inferior, low level of joy, and also the most temporary.

## Happiness from the Senses

This is the happiness that comes from sensory experience, such as food, music, external beauty and so on. It can come through the eyes, through the tongue, through the ears, through the skin.

Seeing whatever appears in the world is not wrong. The objection is to the intention with which you see it. You saw according to your own nature, touched, smelled, did whatever you did, but that will not be a joy that lasts forever. This is called physical bliss.

## The Object Goes, the Joy Goes

This joy is bound to external objects and situations that stimulate the senses, such as taste, touch, sound, smell and so on. That is, whatever exists in your five great elements, and the tanmatras, **sound, touch, form, taste, smell**, through these this joy is tied to external objects. When the external object goes, your joy goes too.

Someone wants liquor; the liquor is finished, and so is his joy. For one who seeks to satisfy lust, when the other person leaves, his joy is gone. In the same way, whatever is of a lower level, like money. Someone feels joy touching money, feels joy even seeing it; but now he cannot see it or cannot touch it, and the joy is gone. No food to eat, and the joy is gone. The same with smelling.

## Why It Is Tamasic

This form of happiness is considered **tamasic**. It is the lowest, because it is an **inert joy**, full of inertia. The very thing it comes from is inert, and so it is tamasic.

It often creates a cycle of craving and dependency, as the mind seeks repeated exposure to similar sensory pleasures. What happens in it? A cycle begins: I want more, I want more. When it is finished, you run after it. Then it becomes an intoxication, a habit forms, and you become dependent on it, reliant on it. Without it, no joy.

> "If he says sorry, you will be happy. For you to hear his sorry, it may take him ten lifetimes."

So you always want the same thing, and you keep running after that same experience.

## Examples from Daily Life

You already know the examples of such joy. Overindulging in junk and processed food to satisfy cravings, despite knowing it may harm health. All the junk food in the world, order it from here, order it from there; there is a warning on it, advertisements come, doctors forbid it, and still the desire to eat it: this is physical bliss.

You go on a picnic, and you will not pack and take food from home; you will order it there, and buy it at a high price. This too comes under physical bliss. Can you relate to it? In the picture, the girl's clothes and so on are all shown, and what she is eating.

As I said, we will learn this part very quickly, because there is nothing in it to teach, only something to understand. All these joys keep happening in your daily life; you keep seeing them. This is only a compilation of observations.

## Sleeping More Than Needed

The second is also tamasic, also a joy of the body alone: sleeping excessively out of laziness rather than for the genuine rest a person needs. Some people enjoy sleeping even more than the sleep a person needs. This is called tamasic joy.

He is giving joy only to the body, is he not? Lying around more, wasting the day, getting up at leisure. So what is all this? Physical bliss. Is there anything in it that needs explaining?

## Drowning in Indulgence

Another kind: someone goes to a bar, goes to a dance bar, goes somewhere else. Overindulging in leisure, like excessive partying or gambling, putting enjoyment above meaningful work. Leaving useful things aside and going mostly to such places, whether from a rich home or a poor one, whether going to a brothel or anywhere else, all these are physical bliss. There is nothing in it that needs explaining.

## The Joy of Laziness

Lying in bed for most of the day, avoiding productive activity and responsibility. In the first example, the boy was asleep; that was the joy of sleeping. This is the **joy of laziness**, in which nothing is to be done.

Perhaps someone will keep reading some needless book, or sit watching some needless movie. The picture shows it: the laptop is placed below and he watches it from a distance. Doing this while keeping important work aside also comes under physical bliss.

## Observing Yourself

In this way, you can think of the further types yourself. Look at all the things you yourself do. All of this is physical bliss.
