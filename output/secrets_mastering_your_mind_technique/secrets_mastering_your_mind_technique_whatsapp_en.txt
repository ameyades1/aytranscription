*Who is ruling your life: you, or your restlessness?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that you, the Self, are meant to lead your body, mind and breath. Distance from overstimulation, pranayama and asanas bring even racing breath under command.

💭 _Today, did you steer your life, or did your restless mind?_
