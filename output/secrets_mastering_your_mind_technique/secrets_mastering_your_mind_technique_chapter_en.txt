# Chapter: The Atharva Mind, the Secret of Becoming Ganadhipati

## Atharva: Steady and Calm Leadership

When one must step onto the field for some work, there is usually one leader who takes everyone along. The problem is that this leader is not **atharva**.

I am not saying you should not oppose anything, or should not go against adharma. Sometimes pick up a stone, there will be fights, wield the sword. But the problem is that the one doing it is not atharva. **Tharva** means restless, wavering; **atharva** means cool and stable. Such a steady-minded **Ganadhipati** is what is needed, and for that there is sadhana.

## Become the Leader of Your Own Life

You will say, "What does this have to do with me? I am not going to become a leader." It is not that you will not be a leader. You can be the leader of your home. Leave the home aside; we must at least be the leader of our own life. I should be able to lead my own life the way I want. One should not be the slave of a thousand people.

## Your Own Gana and Its Lord

Then it is said that you too have a gana. Body, mind, intellect, chitta and Atma: the combination of all these is also called a gana, and you are its leader. And who are "you"? The Atma.

So I must go into that **state of Omkara**. Only then will my mind be under me; like a king, I will be able to keep it under me. Only then will my intellect stay under control, my body will listen to me, move as needed, and not stray anywhere. My chitta, my breath, my prana will stay under my control. When I must do pranayama, I will decide.

## When Does Prana Listen to You?

If you keep running around a great deal, sunk in rajoguna, does your prana listen to you? Does your breath listen to you? It will run fast. Does your heart listen to you? Some things run on their own; the heart does not listen to you.

But when will it listen? When you give it a favourable environment. That is, you will not go too much into things that excite you, and you will do pranayama and asanas. Is that your choice or not? You can tell the body, "Do pranayama calmly." You can tell the mind, "Be calm." Then your prana too will be under you.

Our life, our lifespan, rests on prana, on the breath. How much to increase or reduce the number of years is in your hands.

So your entire body, mind, intellect, chitta, ego, the prana of all of these, the jnanamaya kosha, the anandamaya kosha: if this whole gana is counted, its lord too is you yourself, that is, the Atma. So this is not meant only for an outer king.

## Self-Confidence: True Faith

The one whose own Atma has come under control, in them arises **atmavishvas**, self-confidence, faith in oneself. That is why Swami Vivekananda used to say:

> "The old religion said that he who does not believe in God is an atheist; the true religion says that he who does not believe in himself is an atheist."

You may do a hundred thousand pujas, do this, do that, but if you cannot take a single decision for yourself, what is the use?

## Accepting Slavery as Life

Someone living in subjugation says about getting out of it, "What can I do now, this is how it is, our husband is just like this." Fine, but there is some choice. Try speaking back once. If you have not spoken even once, is the slavery confirmed or not?

"Our mother-in-law is like this." She is not a ghost! One should at least try speaking up once. I did not say go to court or get a divorce. There are ways: sama, dama. But if you never spoke, never expressed yourself, it means the Atma within you has died, self-confidence is finished, and you have accepted this as a way of life. If this happens, the mind will not be happy in any situation.

## The Defeated Mind, the Defeated Nation

The attitude of a slave is that it sees the glorious form of the other: he won, you lost. That is why it is said, **the defeated mind and the defeated nation**. When one mind is defeated, the whole nation is defeated too. Once India was made a slave, and then everyone became slaves. There may have been an odd exception, but overall, what became of the country?

It is said that when this goes on for a long time, the defeated mind, and because of it the defeated nation, begins to take the victorious nation as its ideal.

Whereas by nature we are **jivanmukta**. We came here into bondage. The Atma is "Aham Brahmasmi"; Brahman itself is trying to recognise Brahman.

> "Strength is life, weakness is death."

Every guru has said this. Swami Vivekananda said it, Chanakya said it, many have said it, Shri Krishna said it to Arjuna.

Because of seeing such weakness again and again, today we feel that whatever is British is good. Did they put this into our minds or not? And even today people think the same. Why? Because ours was a defeated country, the mind had become defeated, and we got used to it. Who was the victor? The British and the Mughals. Then unknowingly the self within you begins to consider them right: "At least they are powerful, so they must be right."

## The Same Rule at Home

The rule that applies to the nation, try applying it to yourself. If at home I am bearing slavery, bearing it, bearing it, then my mind is defeated. If I have accepted, "This is what life is," then the same mother-in-law and everyone else have become the victors, have they not? Then the inner self will say, "Quietly listen to them." Doing this, there remains no defence, and after a point the same filth, the same garbage, the same oppression is taken as a way of life.

Then this goes on to the next generation. Either you will tell your daughter from the start, "Be careful, don't do this, don't go there." Or if you were a rebel, the other extreme: she will speak very aggressively. Both methods are wrong. One had to go as atharva, calmly. That is why it does not happen.

## The Essence: You Too Can Become Ganapati

There is a lot to teach, but is everyone grasping the essence of what I am saying? I am also telling you its application to your personal life. Who knows which of you will be a Ganadhipati, who will become Ganapati, who will become Vinayaka. That is why all this vibration is going out.

## Not by Theory, but by Surrender

Nothing will happen through theory alone. Our Katha Upanishad itself says:

> **nāyam ātmā pravacanena labhyo na medhayā na bahunā śrutena**
> "This Atma is not attained by discourse, nor by intellect, nor by much hearing."
> (Katha Upanishad 1.2.23)

Medha means that even memorising things instantly will achieve nothing; learning, hearing it again and again in discourses, will not do it either. Then by what is it attained? It is said: by going to the Guru, by sharanagati. By quietly listening to what he has said.

After narrating the whole Gita, what does Shri Krishna say at the end? Forget all that I have told you so far, and at the end he says:

> **sarva-dharmān parityajya mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja**
> **ahaṁ tvā sarva-pāpebhyo mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ**
> "Abandoning all dharmas, take refuge in Me alone; I shall free you from all sins, do not grieve."
> (Bhagavad Gita 18.66)

The final answer is this.

## The Secret of Karma and the Guru

This is called **karma-rahasya**, the secret of karma. What does secret mean? Someone must know it. If nobody knows it, the secret has no meaning. So does the Guru know the secret of karma or not? The Guru must know, because the secret ends in the Guru only. Someone has to reveal the secret; if it is never revealed, it has no meaning. It is a secret for us, not for the Guru. The Supreme must know it, and the Guru-tattva and the Param-tattva are one. Shri Krishna himself says that it is I who come in the form of the living Guru. So the Guru is aware of this secret.

## The Switch, the Light and the Secret

Today you feel that you pressed a switch and the light came on. To a tribal person it would seem like magic; to you it does not seem like magic, because you have studied science. Yet if asked how the electron moves, will you be able to tell? You have no answer either. But the scientist will have his theory. He too has not seen the atom, the electron, the particles, yet he knows their consequences, he knows everything.

Likewise, in matters of such secrets, some things may seem a wonder even to the Guru, as it is said:

> **āścaryavat paśyati kaścid enam āścaryavad vadati tathaiva cānyaḥ**
> **āścaryavac cainam anyaḥ śṛṇoti...**
> "Someone sees this as a wonder, another speaks of it as a wonder, another hears of it as a wonder."
> (Bhagavad Gita 2.29)

But the Guru knows the secret.

## What Is the Secret? Quiet Obedience

And what is the secret? Quiet obedience. If told to rise, rise; if told to sit, sit. The one who knows how to do this, as the Guru has said:

> "Bow down to one person, and the world will bow down to you."

And the one to whom the world bows, has he become Ganapati or not? Has he become Vinayaka or not?

## Narada Bhakti Sutra: Rare, Inaccessible, Unfailing

However many scriptures you read, the final secret will be the same. The **Narada Bhakti Sutra** is the scripture of bhakti; I have not taught it to you yet, but when I teach it, the same will come up there. Who attains this? "Tadeva sadhyatam, tadeva sadhyatam." Only that one attains it. One sutra before that it is said, who? The one who understands the Guru-tattva and the Param-tattva as one.

Let me explain briefly. It says that one needs a Guru who connects to the Supreme, and such company is obtained by divine grace, it is very rare:

> **durlabho 'gamyo 'moghaś ca**
> "It is rare, inaccessible, and unfailing."
> (Narada Bhakti Sutra 39)

The company of great souls is rare, inaccessible, and unfailing.

**First, rare:** such a Guru will simply not be found. And even if found, you will not recognise him. Sometimes he comes in a T-shirt, sometimes in a kurta, sometimes in a dhoti, sometimes he will be a foreigner, sometimes in a suit. Swami Vivekananda wore a suit as well as saffron. Shri Krishna wore nothing special, with his flute, everything. So it is hard to recognise him.

**Second, inaccessible:** even if you recognise the Guru, his path is inaccessible. Because I am used to speaking freely; here everything feels like bondage. The Guru has said that just listening to him feels like slavery for now, but it is not slavery. That is why it is said that walking his path is inaccessible, nearly impossible to go by.

**Third, unfailing:** yet if someone has done it, it is unfailing: the result is certain, it will surely come.

So these two steps must be crossed. Though it is rare, I must make the effort and go to such a Guru. Once the Guru is found, I must listen to him; the inaccessible path must be made accessible. Once these two happen, the unfailing results are certain.

## Guru and the Supreme: One and the Same

The Narada Bhakti Sutra goes on to say that this Guru connects us to the Param-tattva. Then it says that the Param-tattva itself, by good fortune, gives such a Guru. Such a confusing sentence is put in the middle: the Guru takes us to the Supreme, and the Supreme itself makes us meet such a Guru. Then at the end it says that in reality both are one; the one who knows this is the true devotee. And at the end comes:

> **tad eva sādhyatāṁ tad eva sādhyatām**
> "That alone should be attained, that alone should be attained."
> (Narada Bhakti Sutra 42)

That is, this one alone will get moksha, this one alone will receive whatever is needed.

## Prema Eva Karyam

After this comes the question: having gone to such a Guru, to such a Supreme, what should I do? Should I then become a doctor, an engineer? They say, no. What is my work? The answer is, **prema eva karyam, prema eva karyam**. Your only work is love.

Love does not mean that film love in which one goes round and round between trees with the heroine. That is not called love; that is lust, or some attachment. Love is very different. Love is not only between man and woman. There is love for the nation, love for living beings, love between mother and son, love can be for nature, love is also for dharma.

The word prema means **oneness** with the other. That is called love. To a mother, her son feels like a part of her own body, doesn't he? He came from her, with the cord cut. That is why it is called love.

When such oneness comes to me with the nation, my **vaishya bhava**, the attitude of give and take, will go away. I will not expect a fee in return.

## Let the Direction of Love Be Atharva

Even a negative person becomes a suicide bomber. He feels it is his dharma. His path is misguided, that is a separate matter, but that love has come to him. Yet his direction is not atharva; he is working with a tharva, unsteady mind.

So do you understand what we must do now? To build an atharva mind and to become Ganapati, this is absolutely essential.
