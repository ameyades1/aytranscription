# Chapter: The Mystery of Pind Daan and the Crow

As Pitru Paksha arrives, certain questions often rise in our minds, yet we rarely know their right answers. Who are the Pitars (ancestors)? What is the true meaning of Shraddha and Tarpan? And what knowledge lies behind these traditions? In this chapter, Brahmarshi Acharya Upendra Ji explains these questions in simple language.

A seeker asked: If the pind is connected to our body, why is food offered in Shraddha? And why is that food fed only to the crow?

## The Meaning of Pind: This Body Is Also a Village

As explained earlier, pind means the body, mind and intellect of the very person performing the Shraddha. Every living being is called a pind. In some languages, such as Punjabi, pind also means a village. In the same way, **this body too is a village.**

In this village there are many impurities, many crooked and confused thoughts, and desires. Setting all of these aside, this pind is to be offered to the Supreme.

## The White Rice Pind: A Symbol of Purity

The pind is made only of rice, so it is white. White is the symbol of sattva (purity). It signifies that my mind, my intellect and my entire consciousness should be sattvic. My whole state should rise to that level. This is the first symbol of Pind Daan.

## The Crow Is No One's Ancestor

When we offer the pind to a crow, people take it to mean that the crow is some ancestor. Someone's uncle has passed away, someone's grandmother, someone's other relative. Now if the same crow goes to three or four homes and touches the pind, the question will arise: whose grandmother was it, whose aunt, whose father?

So that this question does not arise in your mind, so that this confusion does not happen at all, I am saying clearly:

> "The crow is no one's ancestor."

The crow, the dog or the cow have special powers by which they can know whether the ancestors are nearby or not. To see here means that they sense it; they experience it.

## The Ritual of Shraddha: Why the Ancestors Come

The ritual works like this. For the ancestors who have passed on, you know what their favourite food was. Such things are deliberately placed there so that they will come.

The second reason is their attachment to you. When you call them with love, the chance that they come quickly grows even more. And if their mind is still caught somewhere, that is, if they have not been liberated, they will surely come around you.

## The Crow Is an Indicator

The crow has this special power: it understands that there is some negative presence nearby. It may not itself know whether this is called a Pitar or something else, but it will not come forward.

Try this experiment on the balcony of your own home. On an ordinary day, when you are at home, feed a crow plain rice or anything at all, and it will eat at once. There is a little fear in it, but it can tell that much: that you will not harm it. But coming on an ordinary day is a different matter. Try it after specially invoking the ancestors, place black sesame seeds on the rice, and see what happens. Then it will not come.

In the same way, if someone's ancestors are already liberated and satisfied, there will be no negative energy there. The crow senses this, it feels no deception, and it eats immediately.

That is why the crow was called an indicator. People would watch and understand:

> "If the crow has touched it, it means those ancestors are liberated. If it does not eat, it means they are not liberated."

That is, they are still somewhere nearby.
