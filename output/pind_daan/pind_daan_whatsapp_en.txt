*Why does the crow sometimes refuse the pind?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that the crow is nobody's ancestor, but it senses whether ancestors are present. The rice pind stands for your own body and mind, offered purified to God.

💭 _When you offer the pind, will you also purify your own mind?_
