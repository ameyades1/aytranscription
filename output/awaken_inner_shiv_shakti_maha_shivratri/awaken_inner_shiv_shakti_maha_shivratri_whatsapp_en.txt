*As you pour water on the Shivling, do you know whom you worship?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that Shiva's form stands for our physical body, and the Shivling for the subtle body within. A dead body, good or wicked, can do nothing: the power that acted has left.

💭 _Who within you really acts?_
