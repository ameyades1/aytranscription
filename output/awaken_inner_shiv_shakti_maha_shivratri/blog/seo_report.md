**English (≈58 words):** In Acharya Upendra Ji's reading, Lord Shiva's form represents our physical body, while the Shivling represents the invisible linga sharira, the subtle body within us. On Maha Shivratri, true worship means recognising the Shiva within.

## 6. FAQ

1. **शिवलिंग किसका प्रतीक है? / What does the Shivling represent?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार शिवलिंग हमारे भीतर के लिंग शरीर, यानी सूक्ष्म शरीर का प्रतीक है, जबकि शिव का साकार रूप स्थूल शरीर का। / In Acharya Ji's reading, the Shivling stands for the linga sharira, the subtle body within us, while Shiva's form stands for the physical body.
2. **शिवलिंग पर तीन दल वाला बिल्व पत्र क्यों चढ़ाते हैं? / Why is a three-leaved bilva offered to the Shivling?**
   तीन दल तीन गुणों, तीन शरीरों और तीन कालों का संकेत हैं; बिल्व पत्र चढ़ाना इन तीनों से परे जाने का अर्पण है। / The three leaves stand for the three gunas, the three bodies and the three times; offering the leaf means offering them up and going beyond them.
3. **अलिंग किसे कहते हैं? / What is aling?**
   पुरुष और प्रकृति से परे, पूर्ण निराकार और अजन्मा पुरुषोत्तम को शास्त्रों में अलिंग कहा गया है। / The wholly formless, unborn Purushottama, beyond Purusha and Prakriti, is called aling in the scriptures. (The blog names no verse; do not add a citation unless the editor confirms one.)
4. **शिव और पार्वती किसके प्रतीक हैं? / What do Shiva and Parvati symbolise?**
   इस प्रतीक में शिव पुरुष हैं, यानी शरीर रूपी पुर में रहने वाले, और पार्वती प्रकृति हैं। / In this symbolism Shiva is Purusha, the one who dwells in the city of the body, and Parvati is Prakriti.
5. **शिव शब्द का अर्थ क्या है? / What does the word Shiva mean?**
   शिव का अर्थ है मंगलकारी, कल्याण करने वाला; आचार्य जी स्व में स्थित होने, यानी स्वस्थ होने को भी शिव कहते हैं। / Shiva means the auspicious one who brings well-being; Acharya Ji also calls being established in the Self (svastha) Shiva.

## 10. Authenticity flags

Items 1–11 come from the Sanskrit review (tools/review/awaken_inner_shiv_shakti_maha_shivratri.md, verdict "Needs correction"). Fix the three Major items before publishing.

1. **Major: three bodies and five koshas.** The blog puts the vijnanamaya kosha in the causal body; the standard scheme (Tattvabodha, Vivekachudamani) puts it in the subtle body, with the causal body = anandamaya kosha. Recommendation: apply the review's correction or attribute the scheme to the speaker. This is in the bilva section, which the secondary keyword targets.
2. **Major: Shadanana count.** "Body, five senses and mind" makes seven, not six; Kartikeya's title is commander of the gods' army. Recommendation: check the transcript and correct.
3. **Major: mind, intellect and ego as mula prakriti.** They are evolutes (apara prakriti, Gita 7.4), not mula prakriti. Recommendation: apply the correction.
4. **Minor: "Prakriti = prathama kriti"** is a teaching nirukti; mark it as Acharya Ji's reading.
5. **Minor: Gita claim on time and rebirth.** No verse says this in these words (nearest 8.23–8.26, 14.20). Recommendation: present it as Acharya Ji's explanation of the Gita. Do not cite a verse number in FAQ or schema.
6. **Minor: Shivling = linga sharira** is stated as fact; it is the speaker's reading, and the Linga Purana treats the linga as the unmanifest. This is the post's main SEO claim: titles, meta, the direct answer and the FAQ in this report already attribute it ("in Acharya Ji's reading"). Keep that attribution and fix the body.
7. **Minor: Parvati as "jada".** Sensitive for Shakta and Shaiva readers; mark it as symbolism.
8. **Minor: सत्व → सत्त्व** spelling.
9. **Minor: phalashruti** gloss is loose.
10. **Minor: "swastha"** is Sanskrit, not Hindi; fix the gloss.
11. **Minor: "Shi" feminine / left, "Va" masculine / right** has no source found; attribute it to Acharya Ji. Do not use it in titles or snippets.
12. **SEO: "true secret" / "असली रहस्य" in the H1.** Acceptable because the post delivers a full explanation, but the title tags avoid "true secret" and instead ask the post's own opening question. Do not escalate to "shocking truth" style titles.
13. **SEO: "phallic symbol" queries.** Many searchers ask whether the Shivling is a phallic symbol. The blog only reports that people of other faiths mock it, and does not engage the claim directly. Do not target that query or add claims about it that the talk does not make; keep the paragraph about other faiths respectful, as it is. Also do not target "Shiv Shakti" queries, which the talk covers only briefly.
