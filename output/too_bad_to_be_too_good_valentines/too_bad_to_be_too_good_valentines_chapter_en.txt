# Chapter: Even Excessive Goodness Becomes a Cause of Sorrow

## The Curse of Lakshmi

Lakshmi bears a curse that she must always go to some asura (demon). If she becomes the wife of Rama, Ravana will carry her away. If she becomes Radha, she will first be married to an asura like Ayan.

## The End of Rajoguna Is Sorrow

The end of rajoguna is sorrow. If you keep loving just anywhere, this problem is bound to come. You will have to endure that one sorrow; only after that will you find Vishnu.

Until then you will not understand that the heart is not used this way. Giving your heart anywhere, giving your money anywhere, pouring your life into anyone, becoming a devoted wife to any drunkard: this is not how it works.

## The Teaching of the Heart

This is said to Lakshmi: what is your heart like? Set aside the story of Lakshmi; what is being explained to you here is the principle of the heart, simply what the heart is like.

So you will have to come back after taking one blow. You loved this much, and that is why you got hurt. Do not do it this much.

> "Too bad to be too good."

That is, being excessively good is not good either. This is the lesson Lakshmi has to be taught. Have you understood or not?

## The Result of Living Only by the Heart

If you are only heart, you will have to go through this in every birth. At least once there will be a marriage to an asura, meaning you will run into such people. Then you will come to the Guru.

Whoever uses only the heart, this is what will happen to them. Now you understand what becomes of blind devotees.
