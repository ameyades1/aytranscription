*Can being too good also be a mistake?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that love without discernment becomes blind devotion. Through Lakshmi's curse of always ending up with a demon, he shows why giving your heart, money and life to anyone brings hurt.

💭 _Does discernment walk alongside your love and devotion?_
