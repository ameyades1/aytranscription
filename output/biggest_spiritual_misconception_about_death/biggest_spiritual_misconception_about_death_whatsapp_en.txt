*Does moksha come only after death?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that liberation must happen while we are alive. At death the body goes but desires remain; in liberation the body remains but attachment ends, like waking fully from a dream.

💭 _If I cannot free myself after death, can I keep putting it off?_
