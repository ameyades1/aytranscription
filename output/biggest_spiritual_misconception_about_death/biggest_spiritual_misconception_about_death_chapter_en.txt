# Chapter: Liberation After Death, the Greatest Misconception

## Liberation Is Not Attained After Death

Many people believe that moksha (liberation) comes only after death. This is a misconception. It is false that liberation will happen after death. Today I am revealing a great secret:

> "Liberation is not attained after death. Before dying, I have to become liberated while living."

There is a method for liberation after death as well, but you cannot do it by your own strength. Only one who is liberated while living can free your ancestors, and only such a one can bring a ghost or spirit under control or set it free. Liberation happens while living, not after death. After death you can do nothing by your own strength. If anything is to happen for you after death, only one who has already become free while living can do it.

## What Is Death?

It is essential to understand these two words, moksha and death, precisely. What does death mean? Put very simply, in spiritual language: **the living remained, but the body went.**

The body is no longer there, but the being still is. And were some desires, some latent cravings, still left or not? The body went, but the living remained. That is death.

## What Is Liberation?

And what would liberation be? Liberation would be its exact opposite: **the body remained, but the living ended.**

The living ended, meaning this worldly living of his came to an end. No interest in the world remains for him now. He will live the rest of his life as a witness, for the work of others, or for his own duties. It is a state of deep witnessing, of bliss. If there is something to do, he can enjoy it too; if there is not, he can simply pass the time.

## One Who Has Woken from the Dream

Understand this through an example. Suppose you have woken from a dream, but within the dream you wished it to continue, so the dream goes on. Now, if you wish, can you enjoy it or not? That is your choice. Or you can simply lie quietly.

But what joy would there be in it, when you know that everything is I? Would you then feel any desire to show someone a magic show? Would you want to display miracles or siddhis (supernatural powers), when you know that everything is I alone, that it is all imagination? Think and tell me. It is a dream, you have woken, and you know it.

Now the dream may be interesting too. When people wake from a half-finished dream, they say, "Oh, what an interesting dream! That heroine was just about to put the wedding garland around my neck. I was just about to see myself win the lottery." And they go back to sleep. I am not speaking of that state.

This is the state of complete wakefulness. Now the dream is only an option. Truly, think and tell me. If such a person wants to play at miracles, he will, but can you understand that state?

## The Difference Between Death and Liberation

So, did you understand the difference between liberation and death? Moksha means the decay of moha (attachment), its destruction. Remember it once again:

> "The body went, but the living remained: that is death. And the body remained, but the living ended: that is liberation."

This is the stage.
