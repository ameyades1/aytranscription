*Why are some people unhappy even when they have everything?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that whatever the mind keeps accepting becomes its nature. One boy danced in horse dung, sure a pony was nearby; his brother cried among new toys.

💭 _What feeling does your mind keep holding on to?_
