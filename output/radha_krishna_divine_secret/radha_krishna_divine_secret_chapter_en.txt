# Chapter: The Secret of Gopika Vallabha: Exchanging Roles in Love

## A Unique Leela of Radha and Krishna

Radha and Krishna, the Gopi and Vallabha, would sometimes exchange their roles. They would say that Radha must live Krishna's role and Krishna must live Radha's life.

> "This is an essential element of love."

## Living Another's Life for a Month

Tell your husband once that for the whole day he should stay in the kitchen with the children and take care of everything else. Then he will understand his wife's pain. And the wife, too, must live her husband's life exactly as he does. Radha and Krishna lived like this for a whole month.

## When Krishna Lived Radha's Life

Then the difficulties Krishna faced! Krishna puts on a girl's clothes and goes out carrying the matki (clay pot). Then the mother-in-law shouts. This is the leela that is described. Then, deliberately, the pot breaks once, it breaks twice, and he has to go back to the river again.

After all this, the flute plays. What the mobile ringtone is today, the flute was in that time. Once it was heard, she had to come. If she did not come, Krishna would shout. And she would say, "How can I come? There is trouble here." Then Krishna understands how much work that woman used to do.

## When Radha Took Up Vallabha's Work

After this, Vallabha's work comes to Radha. Now she has to do others' work, fulfil everyone's wishes, fetch this, fetch that. The one whose work was to solve others' problems: Radha knows nothing of that work. This role was given to Radha. Such is the leela they play.

## The Meaning of the Name Gopika Vallabha

That is why we say **"Gopika Vallabha"**. The role of the Gopika he played in reverse, and the role of Vallabha she played: completely opposite.

> "At times in life, playing this opposite role is essential. Only then is love expressed in its fullness."
