# The Secret of Gopika Vallabha: When Radha and Krishna Traded Places

## Do You Really Know What Your Beloved Goes Through?

How well do we know the lives of the people we love? Do we know how tired they get, what difficulties they face, what they struggle with from morning to night?

Radha and Shri Krishna, the **gopi** (cowherd maiden) and her **vallabha** (beloved), offer a beautiful answer. Every so often they would swap roles. Radha had to live as Krishna, and Krishna had to live Radha's life. Acharya Ji explains that this exchange is an essential part of love.

The Gopika Vallabha meaning is "the Beloved of the Gopis", a name of Shri Krishna. Acharya Ji sees something deeper in the name: as he tells it, Radha and Krishna sometimes swapped roles and lived each other's lives. Krishna learned the gopi's work, and Radha the beloved's. Only by living another's role does love become complete.

## A Month in Each Other's Shoes

Acharya Ji makes a simple suggestion. Ask your husband, just once, to spend the whole day in the kitchen with the children and handle everything else in the house. Only then will he understand what his wife goes through.

The wife, in turn, has to live her husband's life in exactly the same way. Radha and Krishna lived like this for an entire month.

## When Krishna Carried the Water Pot

Krishna puts on a girl's clothes and sets out with a clay pot. His mother-in-law scolds him. The pot breaks once, then twice, on purpose, and he has to trudge back to the river all over again.

After all of this, the flute begins to play. Acharya Ji puts it this way: the flute was the mobile ringtone of that age. Once you heard it, you were bound to come. Whenever Radha failed to come, Krishna used to complain loudly.

She would answer, "How can I come? I have my hands full here." Now, living in her place, Krishna finally understands how much work she carried every day.

*Have you ever spent a single day doing what you dismiss as "just housework"?*

## When Radha Became the Beloved

Then it is Radha's turn. Now she takes on the work of the vallabha, the beloved who looks after everyone. She has to run errands for others, fulfil everyone's wishes, fetch this and bring that.

Krishna's whole role was to solve other people's problems, and Radha has no idea how to do it. This, too, is part of the same **lila** (divine play).

## Why Is Krishna Called Gopika Vallabha?

This is why we call Krishna **Gopika Vallabha** (the Beloved of the Gopis). The name itself holds this exchange: Krishna played the gopi's part in reverse, and Radha played the beloved's. Each took on the exact opposite role.

Acharya Ji says that at times in life you must take on the opposite role. Only then does love find its full expression.

*Whose role in your life should you try living, at least once?*

## Key Takeaways

- Radha and Krishna sometimes swapped roles and lived each other's lives.
- This exchange of roles is an essential part of love.
- As Radha, Krishna discovered how much work a woman does.
- As the beloved, Radha felt the weight of solving everyone's problems.
- The name Gopika Vallabha points to this reversal of roles.
- Love finds its full expression when you live another person's role.
