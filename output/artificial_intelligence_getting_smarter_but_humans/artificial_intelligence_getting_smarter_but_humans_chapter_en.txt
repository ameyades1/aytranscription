# Chapter: Artificial Intelligence and the Test of Discernment

## Kali Yuga: The Age of Machines

The scriptures describe Kali Yuga as an age that will slowly become an age of machines. As this age moves towards its end and the time of the change of ages arrives, machines and devices will have established such a rule that man will forget why he took birth.

Today we are moving in that very direction. That is why today we must speak on this subject. Until now we have spoken of Ganapati, that is, of our own intellect and our discerning intellect (viveka buddhi). Today we will speak of artificial intelligence, a talk connected with science. We must understand how science can also become a cause of suffering.

There is no problem in invention. Artificial intelligence has achieved a great deal; such powerful machines have been built today. But the question is how this journey moved forward.

## It Began with the Calculator

Before it came into being, man did all his work himself. Then things like the calculator arrived. We thought, fine. Mathematics is a somewhat difficult subject; everyone accepts this. That is why only two to four percent of people become experts in it, and the rest do not know it. And mathematics is needed in everyday dealings. So when someone invented it, we did not object. It is useful for the dealings of a shop, so that no one can cheat, to know how much the bill came to, whether less money or more was taken.

Then gradually students began to use it in examinations as well. Calculators came into engineering. Then the work of our own intellect slowly began to decrease, because someone else is doing it anyway.

Earlier we remembered long telephone numbers; everyone remembered numbers. Today's new generation will not even know that numbers are meant to be remembered. Everything of theirs is in the mobile; they do not remember even a full number. What did we do with that power of memory? While losing our original intelligence, we made the outer intelligence grow.

> "The phone is becoming smart and we are becoming dumb."

Today it has everything in it. It is called a "smart phone", isn't it? But in whose hands is it kept? The one holding it knows nothing; it is the phone that knows everything.

Earlier we used to say that this way the book itself will become enlightened. A sacred book is not only for worship; it is for going within yourself.

As long as the calculator did only mathematics, it was fine. But today people use a calculator even to work out two times two. Even then we said, fine, it will do. It will not go beyond this; there is nothing to worry about.

## Reaching Creativity and Language

Then gradually this artificial intelligence entered creativity and graphics. The drawings that engineers made, which were called engineering drawing; there were architects, interior designers, and many others. Machines began to come for them, tools such as CAD-CAM. Their art came to an end. Now a quick command is given and the drawing makes itself. Still, at least one had to know how to run that software. There was such a time. Even then, artists like painters had everything of their own.

Then people were told that nothing more would happen. It will handle all the subjects, but it will not be able to do anything with language. Language means essay writing, our Hindi and Marathi question papers; in these it will not be able to do anything. But step by step this artificial intelligence reached there too. This is called machine learning.

Human progress has certainly happened in this; there is no doubt. And this progress has surely come from the human intellect alone. It must have been someone's siddhi (spiritual attainment); it was made from that. It did not come on its own.

## From Sattvic Hands to Rajasic and Tamasic Hands

But about what has been made, I want to warn you that this is an extremely dangerous situation. I am telling you this because it has gone into the hands of rajas and tamas.

Whenever something is first created, it is in the hands of a sattvic person. When the film industry was first created, the first film was "Satya Harishchandra", because at that time it was made with some purity.

For creation it has been said:

> **ahaṃ brahmāsmi**
> "I am Brahman."
> (Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.4.10)

Only when there is the feeling of being Brahman (brahma bhava) will you be able to create something. And when Saraswati comes along with Brahman, when sattva guna comes, only from this union does an invention happen. Those who are true scientists must surely have brahma bhava within them; only then are they able to invent. Without it, such science cannot happen at all. They know that this spirituality is within them.

But of their followers it has been said that they will be asuric (demonic). One who is only a follower of science has nothing to do with sattva guna. Such people go into the department of Shukracharya, who is called the guru of the daityas (demons). The inventor of the mobile phone will have had a great deal of Shri Vidya within him, a great deal of spirituality, but his followers will have no understanding. This is not my description; it is given in the scriptures. They become followers of Shukracharya, and luxury commodities are made for them.

> "It is hired intelligence, and on top of that it is free."

## Hired Intelligence and the Future of Jobs

Earlier, when tools like Google came, they began to find anything and give it in two minutes. After that, letter writing, which used to be a subject of examination, came to an end. Even your resume is now made by it.

The ChatGPT-like tool that comes to you is only an extremely basic form of artificial intelligence. Because of the higher-level forms beyond it, many jobs will simply not remain in the future.

People say that it will only replace this much, it will be like this, it will be like that. It is nothing like that. The scientists themselves have predicted that within ten years it can end humanity itself. That machines will take over everything is not my statement. Its creators themselves are telling us today what all can happen.

## Half a Litre of Water for Every Question

For every machine, a data center is built. You ask even useless questions, because it is available free. How to attract a girl, just question after question, passing time. You can ask in any language; you can ask in Tamil too. Today ChatGPT works in any language, and any answer at all is available.

> "Every time you ask a question, half a litre of this world's water is used up."

Do you know this? Every question you type, every query you give to Google, consider half a litre of water gone. This is not my research; search on Google yourself and see. The data centers heat up immediately, and water is needed to cool them. And that is drinking water; sea water does not work. Every time, they need cooling.

When America understood this, in the name of development they gave their data centers to be set up in India. And our people are happy, while our water will run out. Billions of people are asking questions, and for that long their machines keep heating up, just as the CPU of our computer heats up. These are very large data centers and rooms. That is why I want to warn you; you do not know what its consequence is.

## So Should Artificial Intelligence Be Banned?

So am I saying, ban artificial intelligence? No. Discernment says that it should be with the one who truly needs it, who is worthy of it (patrata), who is sattvic. If it reaches just anyone, it will prove fatal in the future.

Then, when it alone is doing the work of ten men, of forty men, where will jobs remain? On top of that it is drinking up water. And the other young people sit asking useless questions for free: how to come out of depression, how to do this, how to do that. Because they are not getting jobs. And because of this, the load comes back onto artificial intelligence itself.

Put even a small fee on it, one rupee per question, and then see what happens. You are eligible, you will get it only if you have this certificate. Whether you are using it for spiritual research, or for such-and-such work, or you may use it only after graduation. Some rule or other must be there. If this does not happen in the coming time, there will be a great problem; remember this.

This water is still running short in place after place, so scientists are now trying to find how to take data centers into the sky, into space, or how to set them up under the sea, how it will work with salt water.

Do you know that the cable of the entire internet also comes through the sea? We think everything is wireless, but wireless is only in your home, from here to there. The rest comes through wires inside the sea; every computer is connected by wire. All this is the cost involved.

## An Advertising Machine Built on "Free"

All this is happening for the sake of "free". Then, because of it, apps came. Then that artificial intelligence came into the mobile, and through apps you can order whatever you want. Everything is instant: this in ten minutes, that in so many minutes. Every time they keep hammering at you; it is just like advertising.

Your grocery app, I will not name any company, what do you think their business is? At one time there were video games; you played one over of cricket, and an advertisement came. Their income came from that; the game was free. In the same way your delivery apps, all the other apps, whether for quick groceries, or something else, or quick food, the app as such is free, but in everything its advertisement keeps coming. Sponsored ads come, offers come for you, they keep coming at the bottom, and all of it is to mess with your mind. Remember, it has surely become a marketing machine. Legally it is not wrong; it is advertising, fine.

But what is happening? Earlier we went ourselves and brought groceries. Today the business of the regular grocery shopkeeper ended long ago; he is totally out. But this comes home in ten minutes, and for the sake of ten minutes it makes you do shopping on top of shopping. And the one who is already rajasic and tamasic will become a pauper.

## Ganapati, the Discerning Intellect, Used Rightly

That is why I am saying that Ganapati, our discerning intellect, must be used properly. Artificial intelligence is an extremely dangerous thing. Its own scientists are saying ten years; they have not given more time than that. Within ten years it will end humanity.

How? First the resources will run out. Water will run out. Water has been called the symbol of prana (life force); where there is water, there is life. Even if the drinking water here has been used up, we still have to be careful. That is why, in this context of Ganapati, today I had to tell you about artificial intelligence.
