## 1. Summary

The talk traces how, from the calculator to Google to ChatGPT, we have handed our own thinking to machines: "the phone keeps getting smarter while we keep getting dumber". Acharya Ji reads this through the tradition: the Kali Yuga as an age of machines (his reading), science that has passed from sattvic hands into rajas and tamas, the hidden costs of "free" AI and apps (water for data centres, lost jobs, ad-driven shopping), and the answer: not a ban, but patrata (worthiness) and the use of one's own viveka, understood here as Ganapati. The main intent is informational with an emotional undertone ("is AI making me dumber?", "what will AI do to us?"). The opportunity: questions about artificial intelligence and the human mind are answered mostly by tech and psychology sites; a spiritual answer built on viveka and the three gunas is a distinct angle that only this teaching offers. The post belongs to the Mind & Self cluster, next to the mind-control posts.

1. **क्या AI हमें मूर्ख बना रहा है? / Is AI making us dumber?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार हाँ, यदि हम हर छोटा काम भी उसे सौंप दें: फ़ोन स्मार्ट होता जा रहा है और हम अपनी मूल बुद्धि खोते जा रहे हैं। / According to Acharya Ji, yes, if we hand it even the smallest task: the phone keeps getting smarter while we lose our original intelligence.

   नहीं। आविष्कार में कोई दोष नहीं; प्रश्न यह है कि विज्ञान किसके हाथ में है। आचार्य जी के अनुसार सच्चा आविष्कार ब्रह्म भाव और सत्त्वगुण से होता है। / No. Invention itself is not at fault; the question is whose hands science is in. In Acharya Ji's view, true invention comes from Brahma bhava and sattva.
3. **क्या AI पर प्रतिबंध लगना चाहिए? / Should AI be banned?**
   नहीं। विवेक कहता है कि यह उसके पास हो जिसे सच में इसकी ज़रूरत है और जिसकी पात्रता है; आचार्य जी सुझाव देते हैं कि कोई न कोई नियम हो, जैसे प्रति प्रश्न थोड़ा शुल्क। / No. Viveka says it should belong to those who truly need it and have the worthiness for it; Acharya Ji suggests some rule, such as a small fee per question.
4. **यहाँ गणपति का क्या अर्थ है? / What does Ganapati mean here?**
   इस प्रवचन में गणपति को अपनी विवेक बुद्धि के रूप में समझा गया है, जिसका ठीक से उपयोग इस युग में बचाव है। / In this talk Ganapati is understood as one's own discerning intelligence (viveka), and using it wisely is the way to stay safe in this age.

## 10. Authenticity flags

From the Sanskrit review (`tools/review/artificial_intelligence_getting_smarter_but_humans.md`, "Needs correction"):
1. **Kali Yuga as "an age of machines" is presented as scripture.** The Gita Press accounts (Bhagavata 12.2–12.3, Vishnu Purana 6.1) do not say this. Recommendation: adopt the review's wording ("Acharya Ji sees in this..."). Never write "scriptures say Kali Yuga is the age of machines" in a title, meta, FAQ or social card; for this reason it is not used as a keyword.
2. **"Brahma bhava" glossed as oneness with the Creator** confuses Brahman with Brahma. Recommendation: the review's gloss "oneness with Brahman, the infinite reality".
3. **First Indian film named "Satya Harishchandra"**; it was "Raja Harishchandra" (1913). Recommendation: correct before publishing; keep it out of SEO copy.
4. **"Half a litre of water per question"** is stated as fact in the takeaways and an H2. Published estimates give roughly that much for a whole conversation of many responses, and not all of it is drinking water. Recommendation: the review's takeaway wording and the H2 change in section 4. Do not put the half-litre figure in the title, meta, snippet or a quote card.
5. **सात्विक / सत्वगुण spelling.** The SEO fields use the Gita Press form सत्त्व गुण (also the existing tag). Recommendation: make the blog uniform (editor's choice).
6. **IAST "ahaṁ brahmāsmi"** → "ahaṃ brahmāsmi", consistent with the site.
7. **English gloss "I am Brahman, the infinite reality"** adds to the mantra. Recommendation: the review's fix, so both languages match.
8. **Shukracharya's camp "as the scriptures describe it"** is Acharya Ji's interpretation. Recommendation: attribute it to him, as the review suggests.
9. **"Shri Vidya" with no gloss** may read as a claim that the phone's inventor practised that Shakta tradition. Recommendation: the review's gloss "(here, sacred inner knowledge)".
10. **"Wireless exists only inside your home"** is inaccurate. Recommendation: the review's "the backbone of the internet runs on cables".

SEO-specific:
11. **"Artificial intelligence" is a broad head term.** The post answers it from Acharya Ji's spiritual view, not with research. Titles and meta frame the spiritual question and attribute the answer to him; do not add study results or claims the talk does not make.
12. **Statements about AI's creators predicting the end of humanity within ten years, and America "handing" data centres to India,** are the speaker's statements, not verified facts. Keep them out of titles, meta, FAQ and snippets; if quoted, attribute them to Acharya Ji.
13. **Ganapati as viveka** is a legitimate devotional reading (the review checked it) and is presented as "understood here". The FAQ keeps that framing; tags do not add "Ganesh", since the post is not about the deity.
