# The Phone Is Getting Smarter, Are We?

## An Old Sign of Kali Yuga

The scriptures describe Kali Yuga as an age that will slowly become an age of machines. By the end of the age, machines will rule so completely that man will forget why he took birth.

Today we are moving in that very direction. So this talk is about artificial intelligence, and even before that, about our own **discerning intellect** (viveka buddhi), understood here as **Ganapati**.

There is nothing wrong with invention. The question is whose hands science is in, and how we are using it.

## It All Began with a Small Calculator

Earlier, man did all his work himself. Then the calculator came. Mathematics is a difficult subject, only a few percent of people master it, and the daily accounts of a shop need it so that no one can cheat. So we did not object.

Then it came into examinations, into engineering. Slowly the work of our own intellect began to shrink, because someone else is doing it anyway. Today people pick up a calculator even for two times two.

Earlier we remembered long phone numbers. Today's generation does not even know that numbers are meant to be remembered.

> "The phone is becoming smart and we are becoming dumb."

While losing our **original intelligence**, we made the outer intelligence grow. The phone knows everything; but what does the one holding it know?

It used to be said that this way the book itself will become enlightened. A sacred book is not only for worship; it is for **going within**.

*When did you last bring back a fact, a calculation or a number from your own memory, without the phone?*

## "It Will Never Reach Language"

Then artificial intelligence entered creativity and graphics. Engineering drawing, architects, interior designers: machines like CAD-CAM came for all of them. A command is given and the drawing makes itself. Still, at least one had to know how to run the software, and the painter's own skill remained.

Then it was said that it will handle all subjects, but it will not be able to do anything with language. Essay writing, Hindi and Marathi question papers, surely not these. But with the help of **machine learning**, it reached there too.

This is human progress; there is no doubt. And this progress has come from the human intellect, from someone's siddhi (spiritual attainment). It did not come on its own.

## When Science Slips Out of Sattvic Hands

This is where we must be careful. It is a very dangerous situation, because it has gone into the hands of **rajas and tamas**.

When something is first created, it is in the hands of a sattvic person. When the film industry was created in India, the first film was "Satya Harishchandra", because at that time it was made with some purity.

At the root of creation is this feeling:

> **ahaṃ brahmāsmi**
> "I am Brahman."
> (Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.4.10)

When there is **brahma bhava** (the feeling of being Brahman), and along with it **Saraswati**, that is **sattva guna** (purity, clarity), only then does true invention happen. The true scientist knows that this spirituality is within him.

But the mere followers of science, who have nothing to do with sattva guna, go, according to the scriptures, into the department of **Shukracharya**, the guru of the daityas (demons). The inventor of the mobile phone must have had much Shri Vidya, much spirituality; but his followers lack that understanding. For them it becomes a luxury commodity.

> "It is hired intelligence, and on top of that it is free."

## Hired Intelligence and Jobs

Google came, and searching became two minutes' work. Then letter writing, which was once part of examinations, was gone. Now it even makes the resume.

The ChatGPT-like tool you have is an extremely basic form of artificial intelligence. The levels above it will end many jobs in the future.

This is not my statement. Its creators, the scientists themselves, are saying that within ten years it can end humanity itself.

## Every Question Has a Price: Half a Litre of Water

Behind every machine is a **data center**. And because it is free, we keep asking even useless questions. How to attract a girl, question after question, passing time, in any language.

> "Every time you ask a question, half a litre of this world's water is used up."

For every query you type, the data centers behind it heat up immediately, and water is needed to cool them. And that is **drinking water**; sea water does not work. This is not my research; search for it yourself and see.

When America understood this, in the name of development the data centers were given to be set up in India. And we are happy, while our water will run out. Billions of people are asking questions, and for that long these huge machines keep heating up.

Now scientists are thinking about how to take data centers into space, or keep them under the sea in salt water. And the entire internet also comes through cables laid inside the sea. Wireless is only inside your home; every other computer is connected by wire. That much is being spent.

*Of the questions you asked an AI today, how many were truly necessary?*

## So Should We Ban AI?

No. **Discernment** says it should be with the one who truly needs it, who has **patrata** (eligibility, inner worthiness), who is sattvic. If it reaches just anyone, it will prove fatal in the future.

Think: if it alone does the work of ten or forty men, where will jobs remain? And those who cannot find jobs sit asking how to come out of depression, how to do this, how to do that. And the whole load comes back onto AI.

A suggestion: put a small fee on it, one rupee per question, and then see what happens. There should be proof of eligibility, a purpose of use, for spiritual research or for some specific work, or permission to use it only after graduation. Some rule or other must be there. Otherwise there will be great trouble in the coming time.

## The Real Price of "Free"

AI came into mobile apps. This in ten minutes, that in so many minutes. Everything instant.

But what is the business of these apps? Remember the old video games: you played one over of cricket, and an advertisement came. The game was free; the earning came from ads. In the same way, grocery, food and delivery apps are free, but everywhere there are sponsored ads, offers, and constant provocation of the mind. Not illegal, but it has surely become a **marketing machine**.

The result? The neighbourhood grocer's business ended long ago. And for the sake of ten-minute delivery, shopping goes on upon shopping. The one who is already **rajasic and tamasic** will become a pauper.

## Awaken Your Ganapati

That is why **Ganapati**, our discerning intellect, must be used properly. AI is an extremely dangerous thing, and its own scientists are speaking of ten years.

How? First the resources will run out. Water will run out. And **water is the symbol of prana** (life force); where there is water, there is life.

## Key Takeaways

- Kali Yuga is called the age of machines, in which man will forget why he took birth.
- From the calculator to AI, at every step we handed the work of our original intelligence to something outside: the phone became smart, we did not.
- True invention comes from brahma bhava and sattva guna; but today this science is in the hands of rajas and tamas.
- Every AI question uses about half a litre of drinking water, so asking useless questions wastes resources.
- The solution is not a ban but eligibility: it should be with the one who truly needs it and is sattvic.
- Free apps are machines for advertising and shopping; without discernment they can make us paupers.
- Using Ganapati, our discerning intellect, properly is our protection in this age.
