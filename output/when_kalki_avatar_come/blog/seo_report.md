## 1. Summary

The talk answers "When will the Kalki avatar come?" by going back to the definition of an avatar in Bhagavad Gita 4.7, 4.8 and 4.9. Acharya Ji's answer: the Gita never tells us to wait for anyone. Everyone born in an age of declining dharma meets the first condition of an avatar; what is missing is the work (protecting the good, defeating the wicked, re-establishing dharma) and knowing the divine "in essence" (tattvatah). He then gives the four kinds of mukti (salokya, samipya, sarupya, sayujya), the example of Hanuman and the Mavale who served an avatar's mission, and Hanuman's three-level answer to Shri Rama (dvaita, vishishtadvaita, advaita). The close: no Kalki will come from outside until the Kalki within awakens. The main search intent is informational ("kalki avatar kab aayega", "when will Kalki come"), a high-interest query that is dominated by prophecy, date-prediction and fear content. The opportunity is an authentic, Gita-cited answer that does not predict a date, which sets this post apart from almost everything that ranks.

## 6. FAQ

**Q1. कल्कि अवतार कब आएगा? / When will the Kalki avatar come?**
हि: गुरुजी के अनुसार गीता में किसी की प्रतीक्षा करने की बात नहीं लिखी। जब तक हमारे भीतर का कल्कि नहीं जागेगा, बाहर का कोई कल्कि नहीं आएगा।
EN: Acharya Ji says the Gita never tells us to wait for anyone. Until the Kalki within us awakens, no Kalki will come from outside.

**Q2. गीता के अनुसार अवतार कौन है? / Who is an avatar according to the Gita?**
हि: गीता ४.७ और ४.८ के अनुसार जो धर्म की हानि के समय प्रकट होकर सज्जनों की रक्षा, दुष्टों का विनाश और धर्म की स्थापना करता है। गुरुजी कहते हैं कि पहली शर्त हम सब पर लागू होती है; फर्क कर्म का है।
EN: Per Gita 4.7 and 4.8, the one who appears when dharma declines to protect the good, destroy the wicked and establish dharma. Acharya Ji says the first condition applies to all of us; the difference lies in the work.

**Q3. "तत्त्व से जानना" का क्या अर्थ है? / What does knowing "in essence" (tattvatah) mean?**
हि: सिद्धांत और अनुभव, दोनों से जानना। जैसे शक्कर पर दस हजार पृष्ठ पढ़कर नहीं, चखकर पता चलता है कि वह मीठी है।
EN: Knowing through both principle and experience, the way you learn sugar is sweet by tasting it, not by reading ten thousand pages about it.

**Q4. मुक्ति के चार प्रकार कौन से हैं? / What are the four kinds of mukti?**
हि: सालोक्य (आध्यात्मिक लोगों के बीच रहना), सामीप्य (गुरु के समीप जाना), सारूप्य (उनके जैसा स्वरूप) और सायुज्य (उनसे एक हो जाना)।
EN: Salokya (living among spiritual people), samipya (nearness to the Guru), sarupya (taking on their nature) and sayujya (becoming one with them).

**Q5. हनुमानजी ने श्रीराम को अपना संबंध कैसे बताया? / How did Hanuman describe his relationship with Shri Rama?**
हि: तीन स्तरों पर: शरीर बोध से आप स्वामी, मैं दास (द्वैत); मन-बुद्धि से आप पूर्ण, मैं अंश (विशिष्टाद्वैत); आत्म तत्त्व से हम एक (अद्वैत)।
EN: On three levels: through the body, master and servant (dvaita); through mind and intellect, whole and part (vishishtadvaita); as the Self, one (advaita). (See flag 2: present this as the traditional teaching, without a scriptural source.)

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Date prediction (SEO pressure).** "When will Kalki come" searches reward pages that predict a year or a birthplace. The talk gives no date and says no outside Kalki will come until the inner one awakens. Recommendation: never add a date, prophecy or "signs of Kalki" list to the title, meta or FAQ.
2. **Hanuman's answer (review issue 2, and "could not verify").** The English puts Acharya Ji's explanations in quotation marks as if Hanuman said them, and the traditional verse "देहबुद्ध्या तु दासोऽहं…" has no verified textual source. Recommendation: remove the quotation marks in the English (as in the Hindi) and do not cite a scripture for the verse in FAQ or schema.
3. **"sama, dama, danda" (review issue 1).** The English gloss "inducement" belongs to "dana", not "dama". Recommendation: "sama, dana, danda" in the English; the Hindi idiom can stay.
4. **"आचार्य जी" vs "गुरुजी" (review issue 3).** Two Hindi sentences switch the speaker's name, and one repeats the previous paragraph. Recommendation: use "गुरुजी" consistently. Entity naming matters for AI search: schema and metas use "ब्रह्मर्षि आचार्य उपेंद्र जी" / "Acharya Upendra Ji".
5. **Swarajya and Baji Prabhu (review issue 4).** "Free of Mughal rule" paired with Baji Prabhu (who fought Adilshahi forces at Pavan Khind) is a simplification, and the English adds "lay down his life". Recommendation: "विदेशी सत्ता / foreign rule", and align the two languages.
6. **"We are all avatars" and the Nadi Granth claims.** Acharya Ji's statement that everyone is an avatar, and the Nadi readings about Shripad Shrivallabh and about himself, are his teaching and his statements, not scripture. Snippets and FAQ present them as "according to Acharya Ji"; do not turn them into general claims ("the Gita says everyone is an avatar") or into a headline.
7. **Politics.** The talk links dharma sansthapana with politics and "desh karya". Titles and metas stay on the spiritual teaching and do not frame the post politically.
8. **Four kinds of mukti.** Calling them sequential "steps" is the speaker's reading (Bhagavata Purana 3.29.13 lists five). The FAQ gives them as Acharya Ji explains them, without a verse reference.
