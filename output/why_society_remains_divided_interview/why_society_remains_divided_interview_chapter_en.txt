# Chapter: Hanuman Ji's Answer and the Unity of Society

## A Short Story

There is a short story of Shri Ram and Hanuman Ji. The day this story is truly understood, casteism will come to an end.

Hanuman Ji calls himself a devotee and servant of Shri Ram. One day Shri Ram asked him only this: "Hanuman Ji, what is the relationship between you and me?" Hanuman Ji answered on three levels.

## At the Level of the Body: You Are the Master, I Am the Servant

Hanuman Ji said: if you ask from body-awareness, from a sense of duality, then you are my master and I am your servant. This is my firm view; there is nothing else to it. I will not sit on your throne. Whatever you wish to do, I will serve it.

## At the Level of Mind and Intellect: You Are the Whole, I Am a Part

If you ask in the language of Vishishtadvaita or Dvaitadvaita, that is, at the level of mind and intellect, then you are the whole and I am a part of it. If you are fire, I am a spark. If you are the ocean, I am certainly a drop.

You are water and salt, and so am I. The only difference is that within you live fish and all marine life, ships sail upon you, everything happens in you; none of this happens on me. But remember, if every single drop were taken out, you would not remain either.

Today's union leaders know this too. A king is a king, but if the soldiers and all the people of the kingdom leave, the king is no longer a king. So Hanuman Ji says: I know that if we speak at this level, we too are one; I am a part of you.

## At the Level of the Self: You and I Are One

And if you speak in the language of Advaita, then at the level of the Self, you and I are exactly one, the same Supreme Lord. This is my settled conviction.

In tradition, this answer of Hanuman Ji is known in the form of this verse:

> **dehabuddhyā tu dāso'haṁ jīvabuddhyā tvadaṁśakaḥ**
> **ātmabuddhyā tvamevāham iti me niścitā matiḥ**
> (Traditional saying of Hanuman Ji)

"With the awareness of the body, I am your servant; with the awareness of the individual soul, I am your part; with the awareness of the Self, I am you alone. This is my settled conviction."

## Why Society Remains Divided

Until this feeling arises in all of us together, in every varna, the feeling of love cannot come. Until then the Manusmriti will keep being burned, and until then this very mindset will keep causing problems.
