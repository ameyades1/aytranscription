# One Answer of Hanuman Ji That Can End Casteism

Why does society still remain divided into castes and varnas? The answer is not hidden in a long scripture, but in a short story of Shri Ram and Hanuman Ji.

Acharya Ji says that the day this is understood, casteism will end.

## A Simple Question: What Is Our Relationship?

Hanuman Ji calls himself Shri Ram's **servant** (dasa). One day Shri Ram simply asked him, "Hanuman Ji, what is the relationship between you and me?"

Hanuman Ji's answer came not on one level but on three. And these three levels are the key to this story.

## The First Level: In Body-Awareness, You Are the Master and I Am the Servant

**Sharira bodh** (body-awareness) and **dvaita bhava** (duality) mean seeing oneself as a separate body. At this level Hanuman Ji says: you are my master and I am your servant.

This is his firm view. He will not sit on Shri Ram's throne. Whatever Shri Ram wishes to do, he will serve it.

## The Second Level: In Mind and Intellect, You Are the Whole and I Am a Part

**Vishishtadvaita** and **Dvaitadvaita** are philosophies that see God and the individual soul as whole and part. At this level of mind and intellect, Hanuman Ji says: you are the whole, I am a part of it.

If you are fire, I am a spark. If you are the ocean, I am a drop. You are water and salt, and so am I.

The only difference is that within you live fish and all marine life, and ships sail upon you. None of this happens on me.

But remember, if every single drop were taken out, you would not survive either.

Today's union leaders know this too. A king is a king, but if the soldiers and all the people leave, the king is no longer a king.

*Do you see the people around you who seem "small" as that drop, without which even the ocean cannot survive?*

## The Third Level: In the Self, You and I Are One

**Advaita** (non-duality) means that ultimately there are not two, only one truth. At this level Hanuman Ji says: at the level of the Self, you and I are exactly one, the same Supreme Lord. This is my settled conviction.

In tradition, this answer of Hanuman Ji is known through this verse:

> **dehabuddhyā tu dāso'haṁ jīvabuddhyā tvadaṁśakaḥ**
> **ātmabuddhyā tvamevāham iti me niścitā matiḥ**
> (Traditional saying of Hanuman Ji)

"With body-awareness I am your servant, with the awareness of the individual soul I am your part, and with the awareness of the Self I am you alone."

## So Why Is Society Divided?

Because this feeling has not yet arisen in everyone together. Until every varna awakens to the understanding that in service we are servants, in nature we are parts, and in the Self we are one, love is not possible.

Acharya Ji says that until then the Manusmriti will keep burning, and this mindset will only keep causing problems.

*Today, from which level do you see another person: the body, the mind, or the Self?*

## Key Takeaways

- If this story of Shri Ram and Hanuman Ji is understood, casteism can come to an end.
- In body-awareness, the relationship is of master and servant, one of service, with no desire for power.
- At the level of mind and intellect, God is the whole and we are His parts; without every drop, even the ocean cannot survive.
- At the level of the Self, God and the devotee are one.
- Until this feeling arises in all varnas together, love is impossible and problems in society will remain.
