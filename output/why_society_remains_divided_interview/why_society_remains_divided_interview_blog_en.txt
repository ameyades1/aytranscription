# One Answer from Hanuman That Could End Casteism

Why does society still stand divided into castes and varnas, the traditional social classes? The answer lies not in some lengthy scripture but in a short story about Shri Rama and Hanuman.

Acharya Ji says that the day people truly understand this story, casteism will come to an end.

## A Simple Question: What Binds Us Together?

Hanuman calls himself the **dasa** (servant) of Shri Rama. One day Shri Rama asked him a single question: "Hanuman, what is the relationship between you and me?"

Hanuman did not give one answer. He answered on three levels, and those three levels hold the key to this story.

## The First Level: In the Body, You Are the Master and I Am the Servant

**Sharira bodha** (body-awareness) and **dvaita bhava** (the sense of duality) mean seeing yourself as a separate body. At this level, Hanuman says, "You are my master, and I am your servant."

He holds to this firmly. He will never sit on Shri Rama's throne. Whatever Shri Rama sets out to do, Hanuman will serve it.

## The Second Level: In Mind and Intellect, You Are the Whole and I Am a Part

**Vishishtadvaita** (qualified non-dualism) and **Dvaitadvaita** (dualistic non-dualism) are schools of philosophy that see God and the individual soul as a whole and its part. At this level of mind and intellect, Hanuman says, "You are the whole, and I am a part of it."

If you are fire, I am a spark. If you are the ocean, I am a drop. You are water and salt, and so am I.

The only difference is that fish and all the life of the sea live within you, and ships sail across your surface. None of that happens on me.

Remember this, though: take away the drops one by one, and the ocean itself is gone.

Today's labour union leaders know this truth well. A king is a king, but if his soldiers and all his subjects walk away, he is no longer a king.

*Do you see the "small" people around you as that drop, without which even the ocean cannot survive?*

## The Third Level: In the Atman, You and I Are One

**Advaita** (non-duality) means that, in the end, there are not two realities but one. At this level, Hanuman says, "In the Atman (the Self), you and I are entirely one, the same Supreme Lord. Of this I am certain."

Tradition preserves Hanuman's answer in this well-known verse:

> **dehabuddhyā tu dāso'haṁ jīvabuddhyā tvadaṁśakaḥ**
> **ātmabuddhyā tvamevāham iti me niścitā matiḥ**
> "When I identify with the body, I am your servant; when I see myself as an individual soul, I am a part of you; and when I know myself as the Self, I am none other than you. This is my firm conviction."
> (Traditional words of Hanuman)

## Why, Then, Does Society Remain Divided?

Society remains divided because this understanding has not yet awakened in everyone together. Until people of every varna realise that in service we are servants, in nature we are parts of the whole, and in the Atman we are one, love cannot take root.

Acharya Ji says that until then, people will keep burning the Manusmriti, the ancient law code many blame for caste hierarchy, and this way of thinking will keep creating problems.

*From which level do you look at others today: the body, the mind, or the Atman?*

## Key Takeaways

- Understanding this story of Shri Rama and Hanuman can bring casteism to an end.
- In body-awareness, the bond is that of master and servant, rooted in service and free of any craving for power.
- In mind and intellect, God is the whole and we are His parts; without every drop, even the ocean cannot survive.
- In the Atman, God and the devotee are one.
- Until this understanding reaches every varna together, love remains impossible and society's problems will persist.
