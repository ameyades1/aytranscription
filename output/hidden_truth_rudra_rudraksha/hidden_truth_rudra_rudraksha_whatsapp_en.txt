*What does "Rudra" really mean?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that Rudra is whatever makes you weep once it is gone. Rudraksha means the Guru's tears: before every action, ask whether it would make the Guru weep.

💭 _Who or what in your life will you value only after it is gone?_
