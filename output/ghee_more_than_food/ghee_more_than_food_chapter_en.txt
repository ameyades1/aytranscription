# Chapter: Ghee, Far More Than Food

## The Natural Yajna in the Belly

Everyone needs ghee. A yajna is going on in your belly, a natural havan. Everyone prays before eating, because this yajna is going on within. What kindles that fire is called **tej** (radiance, fiery energy). Whatever is tej, understand it to be like ghee.

## The Three Parts of Ghee

Now the question is: what happens to the fat we take in? One part of it, as has been said, becomes the marrow inside the bones. The lubrication of our joints and bones, and wherever smoothness is needed in the body, all of it comes from this. That is its first part, its direct use.

The second part leaves the body as waste.

And the third part: **it becomes speech.**

## As the Ghee, So the Speech

Now it depends on which ghee I take. Do I take pure desi ghee from the cow, or something adulterated with chemicals, or the fat of a slaughtered animal, from non-vegetarian food? What difference will it make? To my speech.

Which chakra was speech said to be connected with?

> **sarvarogaharacakrasvāminī, rahasyayoginī**
> "The sovereign of the chakra that removes all disease, the secret yogini"
> (Shri Devi Khadgamala Stotram)

This is the Vishuddhi chakra, the centre of speech. So how pure I am can be understood from this: how much non-vegetarian food I am eating and how much vegetarian. According to that, my speech will become the same.

> "Gradually it will become like an animal's."
