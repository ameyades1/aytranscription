# Ghee Is More Than Food

## Is a Sacred Fire Burning Within You Too?

We tend to think of ghee as a matter of taste or nourishment. Acharya Ji sees it in an entirely different light.

Inside your stomach, a **yajna** (a sacred fire ritual) is taking place, a natural **havan** (an offering into the fire). This is why people pray before they eat.

The power that kindles this inner flame is called **tejas** (radiance, the energy of fire). Whatever carries tejas, think of it as ghee.

The spiritual significance of ghee, as Acharya Ji explains it, is this: digestion is a natural yajna in the stomach, and ghee is the tejas of that inner fire. Part of the fat we eat becomes our speech, so the ghee we choose shows in the way we speak.

*The next time you sit down to eat, can you see your meal as an offering?*

## Where Does the Ghee Go?

The fat we eat divides into three parts.

1. **The first part** becomes the marrow inside our bones, the lubrication of our joints, and the smoothness the body needs wherever it needs it. This is its direct use.
2. **The second part** leaves the body as waste.
3. **The third part** becomes our **speech**.

That third point is startling. What we eat finds its way into the way we speak.

## As the Ghee, So the Speech

Everything now depends on what you take in. Is it pure desi ghee from the cow? Something else, full of chemicals? Or the fat of a slaughtered animal, eaten as meat?

The difference is real, and it will show in your speech.

Speech is governed by the **Vishuddhi chakra** (the energy centre of the throat). Its goddess is remembered in these words:

> **sarvarogaharacakrasvāminī**
> **rahasyayoginī**
> "Mistress of the chakra that removes every disease, the secret yogini."
> (Shri Devi Khadgamala Stotram)

The word vishuddhi itself means purity. How pure am I? I can gauge it from how much of my diet is meat and how much is vegetarian. My speech will take shape accordingly, and Acharya Ji warns that, little by little, it will grow coarse and animal-like.

*What is your speech like today, and what does your plate say about it?*

## Key Takeaways

- A natural yajna burns in the stomach, and ghee is its **tejas**.
- The fat we eat divides into three parts: marrow and lubrication, waste, and **speech**.
- Pure cow's desi ghee and animal fat affect speech in very different ways.
- Speech is linked to the Vishuddhi chakra; purity of food becomes purity of speech.
