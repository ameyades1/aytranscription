The quality of what we eat (pure cow's desi ghee, chemical fats or animal fat) shows in our speech, which is linked to the Vishuddhi chakra. The main intent is informational ("ghee ka adhyatmik mahatva", "spiritual significance of ghee"). Most ranking pages on ghee are health and Ayurveda listicles; the opportunity is the spiritual angle (inner yajna, food and speech) that health pages do not cover. Avoid any health or medical promise: the talk makes none.

- **What is the spiritual significance of ghee?** Acharya Ji teaches that a natural yajna burns in the stomach and ghee is the tejas of that fire; whatever carries tejas can be thought of as ghee.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Khadgamala line given as the Vishuddhi goddess (Sanskrit review, Major).** In the Khadgamala, सर्वरोगहरचक्र / रहस्ययोगिनी belong to the seventh avarana of the Sri Chakra (home of the eight Vagdevatas), not to the Vishuddhi chakra; the Lalita Sahasranama names Dakini for Vishuddhi. Recommendation: apply the review's correction (present it as Acharya Ji's link to the goddesses of speech). Do not use "Vishuddhi goddess" or the stotra line in titles, meta or FAQ until fixed; the FAQ above avoids it.
2. **"सर्वरोगहर" (removes every disease) must not become a health claim.** Never write "ghee cures disease" or similar in titles, snippets or social posts. The talk makes no medical promise; "desi ghee benefits" health queries should not be targeted.
3. **Three-part division of fat (review, Minor).** The list (marrow, waste, speech) resembles Chandogya Upanishad 6.5.3 (bone, marrow, speech) but differs. Present it as Acharya Ji's teaching; optionally add the Upanishad reference as the review suggests. Do not claim in snippets that the Upanishad says "ghee becomes speech" in these words.
4. **Meat and "animal-like" speech.** This is the speaker's teaching and is attributed to him; keep it attributed and avoid fear or shame framing in titles and social cards.
5. **Vocative spelling स्वामिनि / योगिनि (review, Minor).** Editor's choice; no SEO impact.
6. **गुरुदेव / The Guru vs आचार्य जी / Acharya Ji (review, Minor).** Use Acharya Ji throughout for consistent entity naming (helps AI search attribution).
7. **English adds "coarse" (review, Minor).** The Hindi says only पशु-तुल्य; drop "coarse".
