# Ghee Is Not Just Food

## Is a Yajna Going On Inside You Too?

We think of ghee as just a matter of taste or nutrition. But Gurudev sees it from an entirely different angle.

A **yajna** (sacred fire ritual) is going on in your belly, a natural havan. That is why everyone prays before eating.

The power that kindles this fire is called **tej** (radiance, fiery energy). And whatever is tej, understand it to be like ghee.

*The next time you eat, can you see it as an offering into the fire?*

## Where Does the Ghee Go?

The fat we eat has three parts.

1. **The first part:** the marrow inside the bones, the lubrication of the joints, and wherever smoothness is needed in the body. This is its direct use.
2. **The second part:** it leaves the body as waste.
3. **The third part:** it becomes **speech**.

That third point is striking. What we eat comes down into how we speak.

## As the Ghee, So the Speech

Now everything depends on what you take in. Pure desi ghee from the cow? Something else full of chemicals? Or the fat of a slaughtered animal, from non-vegetarian food?

It will make a difference, and that difference will show in your speech.

Speech is connected to the **Vishuddhi chakra** (the energy centre of the throat), whose goddess is remembered in these words:

> **sarvarogaharacakrasvāminī, rahasyayoginī**
> "The sovereign of the chakra that removes all disease, the secret yogini"
> (Shri Devi Khadgamala Stotram)

Vishuddhi itself means purity. How pure I am can be understood from how much non-vegetarian and how much vegetarian food I am eating. My speech will become accordingly, and Acharya Ji warns that gradually it will become like an animal's.

*What is your speech like today, and what does your plate say about it?*

## Takeaways

- A natural yajna goes on in the belly, and ghee is its **tej**.
- The fat we eat has three parts: marrow and lubrication, waste, and **speech**.
- Pure desi cow ghee and animal fat affect speech differently.
- Speech is linked to the Vishuddhi chakra; purity of food becomes purity of speech.
