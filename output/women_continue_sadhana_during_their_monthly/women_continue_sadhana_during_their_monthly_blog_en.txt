# Why Sadhana Need Not Stop During the Monthly Cycle

## Things We Have All Heard

Do not go to the cremation ground. Do not do this with the Guru. Do not do too much recitation. Do not go often to the temple of Shani Dev.

You too must have heard such prohibitions for women somewhere. Acharya Upendra ji says these are all **misconceptions**. They are not entirely false, and not entirely true either. So let us first understand what lies behind them.

## The Fear of Shmashan Vairagya

A woman's mind is emotional. She is the homemaker who holds the household together. If she suddenly developed **vairagya** (dispassion, detachment from the world), what would happen to the home? It was out of this thinking that a male-dominated society made these rules.

The passing detachment that arises at the cremation ground is called **shmashan vairagya**. The fear was that if it came into a woman, there would be difficulty at home later.

> **annamayaṃ hi somya manaḥ**
> "O gentle one, the mind is made of food."
> (Chandogya Upanishad 6.5.4)

The mind is formed from food. And it is the woman who prepares the food at home; she is **Annapurna**. If her thoughts turned negative, it could affect the whole household.

## Going to the Guru: What Was the Fear?

But if a woman herself is strong like a goddess, if she is spiritual, this rule does not apply to her at all. Going to a Guru is never bad. The only fear was that she might leave her worldly life.

The answer is simple: just do not go to a Guru who tells you to give everything up. Acharya Ji says to go instead to the Shri Vidya Guru, the one who says that bhoga plus moksha can be attained together.

The tradition of **Shri Vidya** carries **bhoga** (worldly enjoyment) and **moksha** (liberation) together. Such a Guru teaches the balance of all three: Lakshmi, Parvati and Saraswati.

*Is your spiritual path taking you away from life, or teaching you balance within it?*

## Shani Dev's Temple: The Influence of the Atmosphere

In Shani's temple the mantra will be Shani Dev's, and the atmosphere will be Shani's too. From the mantras of suffering people and from that atmosphere, a feeling of dispassion and humility arises. Society feared that if this feeling came into a woman, who would run the home?

But Acharya ji says clearly, as a Guru, that you can go any time, do as much as you like, and offer abhisheka with as much oil as you wish; all of it is fine.

This freedom is for the one who is strong and whom the family also supports. There is no need to worry about it at all. The one dear to Shani Dev is the one who keeps **balance**.

## How Many Days a Month for Sadhana?

Many women ask: should we do sadhana for twenty-five days or thirty?

Spirituality has nothing to do with counting days. All thirty days, and in a month with thirty-one, all thirty-one, **sadhana is never to be left.**

Why? Because the Supreme is present everywhere. Just think:

- Did you stop eating during those five days? The goddess Annapurneshwari is in food.
- When your salary came, did you stop taking it? Lakshmi ji is in money.
- **Vastu Devata**, the deity of the dwelling, is in your home; did you stop going home too?

You will not be able to leave God behind anywhere.

*If God is everywhere, can there be a day when I am away from Him?*

## Then Where Did This Rule Come From?

This rule came from a **social reason**. At that time there were no hygiene methods like today's. If some problem arose in a public place, others could be distracted, and wrong tendencies and gossip could arise. So it was said: stay away.

If you can follow that rule, that is fine. But your own personal sadhana is a different matter. Acharya Ji explains that your own sadhana should be done on all thirty days, and Shani Dev likes that.

## Key Takeaways

- Many prohibitions placed on women are misconceptions: neither entirely false nor entirely true.
- A male-dominated society made these rules out of fear that a woman's dispassion would unsettle the household.
- These rules do not apply to a strong, spiritual woman, especially when her family supports her.
- Choose a Guru who teaches the balance of bhoga and moksha, like a Guru of Shri Vidya.
- The Supreme is everywhere, so continue your personal sadhana every day of the month.
- The prohibition linked to the monthly cycle came from the hygiene and social conditions of its time.
