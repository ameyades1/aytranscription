*Should a woman's sadhana stop during her monthly cycle?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that personal sadhana can continue all thirty days, because God pervades everything. You never stop eating, though Annapurna dwells in food.

💭 _If God is everywhere, can there ever be a day when you are far from Him?_
