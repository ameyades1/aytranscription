# Chapter: Should Women Continue Sadhana During Their Monthly Cycle?

## Common Prohibitions: Misconception or Truth?

You must have heard somewhere that women should not go to places like the temple of Shani. Many people say so: do not go to the cremation ground, do not do this with the Guru, do not do too much recitation, and do not go often to the temple of Shani Dev.

In reality, these are all misconceptions. They are not entirely false, and they are not entirely true either. First it is necessary to understand what lies behind them. This is an important subject that I want to explain to you.

## Why Were Women Kept Away from the Cremation Ground?

It was said: do not send a woman to these places. Why? Because a woman's mind is emotional. She lives in maya, she is the homemaker who holds the household together. What would happen if she suddenly developed vairagya (dispassion)? It was out of this thinking that a male-dominated society made these rules.

If she went to the cremation ground and, being emotional, developed vairagya (this is called *shmashan vairagya*, the dispassion of the cremation ground), then there would be difficulty at home later.

> **annamayaṃ hi somya manaḥ**
> "O gentle one, the mind is made of food."
> (Chandogya Upanishad 6.5.4)

The one who prepares the food in the home is she; she is Annapurna. If all her thoughts turned negative, the whole household could be affected. That is why this was said.

## The Fear of Going to the Guru

But if she herself has become strong like a goddess, if she is spiritual, then this rule does not apply to her. Going to a Guru is never bad. But men did not send her out of the fear that, by going to the Guru, she might leave her worldly life.

So simply do not go to a Guru who teaches you to give everything up. Go to a Guru of Shri Vidya, who says that **bhoga (worldly enjoyment) and moksha (liberation) can be attained together**. Go to one who teaches the right balance, the balance of Lakshmi, Parvati and Saraswati. Then going to such a Guru is not wrong.

## The Secret of Shani Dev's Temple

Finally, about Shani Dev. If you go to the temple of Shani, whose mantra will be there? Shani Dev's. Whose atmosphere will be everywhere? Shani's. From the mantras of those who are suffering, and from that atmosphere, a feeling of vairagya will arise, a humility will arise. If that feeling came into a woman, what would happen to the home? Who would run my household then? Out of this fear, a male-dominated society said: do not do this.

As a Guru, I want to tell you this:

> "The one who is very strong, whom even the family can support, for her there is no prohibition. Go any time, do as much as you like, go and offer abhisheka with as much oil as you wish, all of it is fine."

There is no need to worry about this at all. Shani Dev loves both; the one who keeps the balance is dear to him.

## How Many Days a Month Should Sadhana Be Done?

The second point concerns women. Women ask: how many days should we do sadhana, twenty-five days or thirty? Spirituality has no connection with twenty-five or thirty days.

> "All thirty of the thirty days, and in a month that has thirty-one days, all thirty-one, sadhana is never to be left."

Because the Supreme is present everywhere. So did you stop eating during those five days? In food there is grain, there is God, there is the goddess Annapurneshwari; you did not give that up. Then there is dealing with money: when your salary comes, did you take it or not? You took it. Lakshmi ji was in that money, yet you did not stop anything. There is Vastu Devata, the deity of the dwelling, so then you would have to stop going home too. You would not be able to give up anything anywhere.

## Why Did This Rule Come About?

Why did the rule come about that in those days one should not do this worship of God? It was for a social reason. At that time there were not as many hygiene methods as today, there was nothing. So when you went to a public place and some problem arose, other people could be distracted, wrong tendencies and gossip could arise among them. That is why it was said: stay away.

If you can follow that rule, that is fine. But **your own sadhana should be done on all thirty days.** Shani Dev, too, likes that.
