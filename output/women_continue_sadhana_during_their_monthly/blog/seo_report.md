## 1. Summary

A short talk (video 4:18) answering a question many women ask: should sadhana stop during the monthly cycle? Acharya Upendra Ji calls the common prohibitions on women (cremation grounds, the Guru, long recitation, Shani temples, and practice during menstruation) "misconceptions, neither wholly true nor wholly false", traces them to a society that feared a woman's vairagya would unsettle the home and to the hygiene conditions of an earlier time, and concludes that because God pervades everything, a woman's personal sadhana can continue on every day of the month. The main intent is practical and informational ("can I do puja / chant / do sadhana during periods?", "मासिक धर्म में पूजा कर सकते हैं या नहीं"), with a strong emotional layer: many readers feel excluded or guilty. Search demand is steady all year and rises around Navratri, Sawan and other vrat seasons. Most ranking pages either repeat the restriction as rule or dismiss tradition outright; this post's angle is a middle path from a Guru (the social reason was real, personal practice was never meant to stop), which is a genuine gap. The post is short (about 600 words), so it should be positioned as a clear answer, not a comprehensive guide.

## 6. FAQ

For the report only (no FAQPage schema: Google shows FAQ rich results only for authoritative government and health sites).

1. **क्या मासिक धर्म में साधना कर सकते हैं? / Can women do sadhana during menstruation?**
   हां। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि अपनी व्यक्तिगत साधना तीसों दिन करनी चाहिए, क्योंकि ईश्वर हर जगह है। / Yes. Acharya Ji teaches that one's personal sadhana should continue on all thirty days, because God is everywhere.
2. **तो मासिक धर्म से जुड़ा नियम क्यों बना? / Then why was the rule made?**
   सामाजिक कारणों से: उस समय स्वच्छता के साधन नहीं थे, और सार्वजनिक स्थान पर समस्या होने से दूसरों का ध्यान भंग हो सकता था। / For social reasons: there were no hygiene products then, and an accident in a public place could disturb others and invite gossip.
3. **क्या उस नियम को मानना गलत है? / Is it wrong to follow that rule?**
   नहीं। यदि आप पालन कर सकती हैं तो ठीक है; अपनी स्वयं की साधना की बात अलग है। / No. If you can follow it, that is fine; your own personal practice is a separate matter.
4. **स्त्रियों को श्मशान या शनि मंदिर जाने से क्यों रोका जाता था? / Why were women kept from cremation grounds and Shani temples?**
   इस डर से कि वहां का वातावरण वैराग्य जगाएगा और घर-संसार प्रभावित होगा। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि सशक्त स्त्री, जिसे परिवार का सहयोग है, कभी भी जा सकती है। / Out of fear that the atmosphere would awaken vairagya and unsettle the household. Acharya Ji says a strong woman whose family supports her may go whenever she likes.
5. **कैसा गुरु चुनें? / What kind of Guru should one choose?**
   ऐसा गुरु जो भोग और मोक्ष का संतुलन सिखाए, जैसे श्री विद्या का गुरु, न कि सब कुछ छोड़ देने को कहने वाला। / One who teaches the balance of bhoga and moksha, such as a Shri Vidya Guru, not one who tells you to give everything up.

## 10. Authenticity flags

Sanskrit review (tools/review/women_continue_sadhana_during_their_monthly.md, verdict "Minor issues"); none of these is applied yet:

1. **Partial verse not marked (review issue 1, Minor).** "अन्नमयं हि सोम्य मनः" is only the first part of Chandogya Upanishad 6.5.4. Add "अंश" / "part" to the reference as the review suggests. Not used in SEO text.
2. **Hindi meaning drops सोम्य (review issue 2, Minor).** Use "हे सोम्य! मन अन्नमय है (अन्न से बनता है)।" so Hindi and English match.
3. **"भोग plus मोक्ष" (review issue 3, Minor).** Replace the English word with "भोग और मोक्ष". This sentence holds a link anchor ("उस श्री विद्या गुरु के पास जाओ"), which is not affected by the fix.

From the review's "Not verified" list, and SEO-specific:

4. **The social-hygiene origin is the speaker's view.** Dharmashastra texts do lay down rules for rajasvala women. Titles, metas, the snippet and the FAQ attribute the explanation to Acharya Ji ("आचार्य उपेंद्र जी बताते हैं", "according to Acharya Upendra Ji") and never say "scripture does not forbid it" or "the rule is a myth". Do not target or headline "no scripture prohibits puja during periods": the blog does not make that claim.
5. **"Myth vs Truth".** The video thumbnail uses it, but the talk says the prohibitions are "neither entirely false nor entirely true" and that following the public-place rule is fine. Prefer "भ्रांति और सत्य" in Hindi (the talk's own word) and avoid presenting the whole tradition as a myth.
6. **Personal practice, not temple rules.** The talk's permission is for one's own sadhana; it does not tell women to ignore a particular temple's or family's custom. Keep SEO text to "personal sadhana / अपनी साधना" and do not promise anything about temple entry. The Shani temple freedom is given "to the woman who is strong and whose family supports her"; keep that condition if it is quoted.
7. **"A woman's mind is emotional by nature."** A generalisation presented as fact (the review leaves it to the editor). Keep it out of titles, snippets and social cards; it could put off the very readers this post serves.
8. **No health claims.** The post makes none; don't add any (for example about energy or the body during menstruation) to the meta or FAQ.
