*Does dying set a soul free?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that death takes only the body; unfulfilled desires bring the soul back. Moksha is the dissolving of attachment while alive. Only one free while living can free the ancestors.

💭 _If you left your body today, which desires would pull you back?_
