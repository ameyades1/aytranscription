# Chapter: The True Meaning of Shastra

## What Is Called a Shastra

A shastra is a text containing a list of **what is to be done and what is not to be done**. It lays down duty and non-duty (kartavya and akartavya). Whether it is written point by point or as a detailed description is secondary. The essential thing is that it states what one should and should not do.

And the one who writes it is an **authoritative person**, someone who has attained mastery in that subject, who has experienced it, who has become a genuine authority on it.

## Dharmashastra: The Code of One Who Has Lived Dharma

What, then, would Dharmashastra mean? Someone who has lived dharma, who has reached its higher levels, such an authority has set out duty and non-duty precisely, so that you benefit, your dharma succeeds, and other people are uplifted too.

For example, you were told to give in charity. How to give would have been written by the authority according to the scriptures: how to give, what to give, what not to give, how much to give, when to give, and whether to give in the spirit of "Om Tat Sat." The Gita says to give after considering place, time and recipient:

> **dātavyam iti yad dānaṁ dīyate 'nupakāriṇe**
> **deśe kāle ca pātre ca tad dānaṁ sāttvikaṁ smṛtam**
> "Charity given as a duty, to one who cannot return the favour, at the proper place and time and to a worthy recipient, is held to be sattvic."
> (Bhagavad Gita 17.20)

So are these not its do's and don'ts? **Give to the worthy, not to the unworthy.** Are these not rules? They are well-defined rules. Shri Krishna says this in the Gita, and Shiva says it in the Shiva Purana. That is, an authority has given it. And why has it been given? So that you are uplifted first.

Charity is only one part of dharma; there is much more in the Dharmashastra: forgive, do this, everything is in it. So that you are uplifted, and the people are uplifted too.

## Arthashastra: Wealth for the Sake of Dharma

In the same way there will be an Arthashastra. How to do business, how to produce, how to earn wealth: this will be a shastra written by some authority. It may be Chanakya ji, or those of Shri Vidya, Dattatreya or Agastya Muni and others, whom we call the Shri Vidya tradition. It is an authority's shastra about money, about artha.

It would first give the deeper meaning of artha: **wealth, honour, society, woman, time, self-interest, health.** All these would be among the things to do, and removing self-interest and the like would also be in it. So the do's and don'ts come, given by some authority, so that you become prosperous, in order to practise dharma.

> **sukhasya mūlaṁ dharmaḥ, dharmasya mūlam arthaḥ**
> "The root of happiness is dharma; the root of dharma is wealth."
> (Chanakya Sutra 1-2)

So how to bring in wealth: that book exists for it, so that you benefit too. Everything will be in it: how to earn, how to run your family, how to build assets, how to give charity to the people, how to give to the Guru for the Guru's work. And the spirit of yajna for the earth would also have been taught.

## Kamashastra: A Code That Leads Toward Liberation

Just as these shastras exist, there will be a Kamashastra too, because kama is also a purushartha (aim of life). Now, the mere act is known even to dogs and cats. Then what is there to teach, why write books on it? Because there is a shastra in it.

So some authority would have written it: either an authority on scripturally sanctioned kama and procreation, or a complete authority on brahmacharya. As the sage Vatsyayana said: having observed unbroken brahmacharya throughout my life, having gained mastery in dharma and artha, I now write this text in a state of meditation. Not to increase lust in people, but to move through it toward liberation, for the birth of worthy children, or so that it does not become an obstacle in sadhana.

So the authority has come into it. There will be do's and don'ts in it, some rules, from action all the way to intellect. And it will be for your good: whether or not to commit adultery, how to hold one's thoughts, all that would be given in it. What is good, what is beneficial; whether it is good or bad for a king to be under the sway of women. And the welfare of the people would be in it too.

## Mokshashastra and the Shastra of Every Discipline

In the same way there is a Mokshashastra. And physics, chemistry, mathematics, all of them would have a shastra like this, would they not? When some scientist explains something, there will be do's and don'ts in it. There will be a shastra of electricity, a shastra of astrology.

Now the word **shastra** is understood: a code of duty and non-duty, given by an authority from experience, so that you benefit and the people benefit too.
