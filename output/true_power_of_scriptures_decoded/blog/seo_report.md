## 1. Summary

The talk answers a basic question: what is a shastra? Acharya Ji defines it as a text of कर्तव्य-अकर्तव्य (do's and don'ts), written not by anyone with an opinion but by an authority who has lived the subject. He then walks through the four purusharthas: Dharma Shastra (dana by place, time and recipient, Gita 17.20), Artha Shastra (wealth for the sake of dharma, "धर्मस्य मूलम् अर्थः"), Kama Shastra (not to inflame desire but for moksha, worthy children and unobstructed sadhana) and Moksha Shastra, and extends the idea to every science. The main intent is informational ("शास्त्र का अर्थ", "what is a shastra"). The opportunity: most ranking pages give a dictionary definition or a list of Hindu texts; this post gives a clear working definition (rules from a lived authority) and shows it across dharma, artha, kama and moksha, which is exactly the "explain it simply" answer AI overviews and Hindi readers look for. The Kama Shastra section is a respectful, high-curiosity angle but must not become the hook (see flag 4).

It is written not by anyone with an opinion but by an authority with real mastery and experience. That is why dharma, artha, kama, moksha and every science have their own shastra.

## 6. FAQ

1. **शास्त्र का अर्थ क्या है? / What does shastra mean?**
   शास्त्र कर्तव्य-अकर्तव्य (क्या करें, क्या न करें) का विधान है, जिसे उस विषय का अनुभवी अधिकारी बताता है। / A shastra lays out what to do and what not to do in a field, as taught by an experienced authority in it.
2. **शास्त्र कौन लिख सकता है? / Who can write a shastra?**
   कोई भी नहीं; केवल वही अधिकारी व्यक्ति जिसे उस विषय में निपुणता और अनुभव मिल चुका है। शास्त्र राय नहीं, जिए हुए अनुभव से निकला मार्गदर्शन है। / Not just anyone: only an authority who has gained mastery and real experience in that field. A shastra is guidance drawn from lived experience, not opinion.
3. **गीता के अनुसार सात्त्विक दान क्या है? / What is sattvic giving according to the Gita?**
   गीता १७.२० के अनुसार जो दान "देना ही कर्तव्य है" इस भाव से, देश, काल और पात्र देखकर, प्रत्युपकार न करने वाले को दिया जाए, वह सात्त्विक है। / According to Gita 17.20, a gift given because giving is one's duty, at the right place and time, to a worthy recipient who does nothing in return, is sattvic. (Full verse per Gita Press; see flag 1.)
4. **धर्मस्य मूलम् अर्थः का क्या अर्थ है? / What does "dharmasya mūlam arthaḥ" mean?**
   चाणक्य सूत्र कहते हैं कि सुख का मूल धर्म है और धर्म का मूल अर्थ। आचार्य जी के अनुसार धन कमाना भी शास्त्र का विषय है, पर धर्म करने के लिए। / The Chanakya Sutras say the root of happiness is dharma and the root of dharma is wealth. Acharya Ji teaches that earning wealth is a proper subject of shastra, but as a means to live by dharma.
5. **कामशास्त्र किसलिए लिखा गया? / Why was the Kama Shastra written?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार काम वृत्ति बढ़ाने के लिए नहीं, बल्कि मोक्ष की ओर जाने, योग्य संतान की उत्पत्ति और साधना को निर्विघ्न रखने के लिए। / According to Acharya Ji, not to inflame desire, but as a path toward liberation, for worthy children, and so that desire does not obstruct sadhana.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Gita 17.20 quoted as a half verse (Sanskrit review issue 1, Major, not yet applied).** The blog gives only the second half and reduces sattvic dana to place, time and recipient, dropping "दातव्यमिति" (giving as duty) and "अनुपकारिणे" (to one who does nothing in return). The FAQ answer (Q3) uses the full Gita Press meaning; apply the review's correction to the blog so the page and FAQ agree. The secondary keyword "सात्त्विक दान क्या है" / "sattvic dana Bhagavad Gita 17.20" should be pushed only after the fix.
2. **Vatsyayana's "lifelong unbroken celibacy" and moksha as his stated aim (review issue 2, Minor).** The Kamasutra's closing verses speak of brahmacharya and samadhi, for worldly conduct, not to excite passion; moksha is Acharya Ji's reading. The FAQ (Q5) attributes the purpose to Acharya Ji. Apply the review's wording; no SEO text says "lifelong" or attributes moksha to Vatsyayana.
3. **Seven-fold meaning of artha (review issue 5, Minor).** Not a known scriptural list; mark it "आचार्य जी के अनुसार" / "According to Acharya Ji". Kept out of titles, meta and FAQ.
4. **Kama Shastra as a click hook.** "कामशास्त्र" / "Kama Sutra" has very high search volume, but the intent of most of those searches does not match this post, and the section is a short, respectful part of a broader teaching. Keep it as a low-priority secondary keyword ("purpose of Kama Shastra") and out of titles, meta and social cards; the recommended titles name it only as one of the four purusharthas.
5. **Chanakya Sutra sandhi and IAST (review issues 3 and 4, Minor).** "मूलम् अर्थः" vs "मूलमर्थः", "tad dānaṁ" vs "taddānaṃ", "ṁ" vs "ṃ". The secondary keyword uses the blog's current form "धर्मस्य मूलम् अर्थः"; if the editor changes the sandhi, update the keyword in seo.json and section 11 (searchers type both forms, so either is fine for SEO).
6. **Shiva Purana rules on dana (could not verify).** The blog says Shiva gives these rules in the Shiva Purana without a chapter. Not used in SEO text; add a reference (e.g., Vidyeshvara Samhita) only if the editor confirms it.
