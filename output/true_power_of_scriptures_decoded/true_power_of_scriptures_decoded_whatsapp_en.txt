*What exactly is a shastra?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that a shastra sets out what to do and avoid, written by a true master, not someone with an opinion. Even giving has rules: the right time, place and worthy recipient.

💭 _In life decisions, do you trust experience or mere opinion?_
