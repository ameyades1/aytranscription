# Chapter: From Illness to Liberation, the Four Steps of the Seeker

## A Sound Mind in a Sound Body

Where will a sound mind dwell? In a sound body. As it is said:

> "A sound mind dwells only in a sound body."

If the body itself is not well, the mind cannot stay well either. And the reverse is just as true: if my mind is not well, the body will not stay well.

I have taken birth as a body. The scriptures say that, descending step by step from the Self, I have now arrived at the body. Now, while nourishing this very body, I must step out of it again and again, slowly. An ailing state will not do at the time of samadhi.

## The Order of Sadhana: From Illness to Liberation

This journey has a definite order:

> "From the sick to the healthy, from the healthy to the enjoyer, from the enjoyer to siddhi, from siddhi to liberation."

First of all, I must become free of illness. No one attains moksha in a state of illness. Think of it plainly: if you have fallen ill through the sins of past lives, or through the sins of this life, there will be no moksha.

## Say "I Am Not the Body" in the Midst of Pain

"I am not this body; this body is mine." Through this feeling alone, moksha happens. But what of the one who is in pain every moment?

Even if a fingernail breaks, then, when the pain comes, say it and show me: "I am not this body, this body is mine, I only came here to imagine, to play." And I spoke only of a fingernail.

In reality, when there is cancer in the stomach, in the lungs, in the liver, and there is pain every day, every moment; when disease has reached the heart and a heart attack has come, then say it and show me: "I am not this body." At that time you become bound to the body itself. First tell me the truth.

That is why the scriptures say that first of all I must become free of illness. From the sick to the healthy.

## Only the Healthy Can Truly Enjoy

Only a healthy person can be a good enjoyer. Isn't that right? Think about it: what pleasures will a sick person enjoy?

You did Shri Vidya too, you did sadhana too, fine. You bought big houses and bungalows, but you cannot move about. Will that do?

All your life you toiled and toiled, so that later you could eat many fine dishes. Now diabetes has come. The doctor says no sweets. No sweets, no sweets, and while this goes on, next will come no salt, meaning heart trouble will begin. The journey of life goes something like this, doesn't it?

You worked hard and had such a beautiful sofa made, did everything. Now the doctor says there is a problem in the spine, you must now sleep on a flat surface, no sofa or anything like it. As if the sofa's pride had grown too great! So if you are not healthy, you do not get enjoyment either. Do you accept this much?

First understand its essence, understand its heart. Is becoming healthy from sick necessary first or not? Tell me that first. Only a healthy person can be a good enjoyer.

## If the Mind Is Sick, Even a Bungalow Is Worthless

You are sitting at home, you have bought a very big bungalow, but you cannot sit in it in peace. There are ailments of the mind, there is anguish in the mind. So did you truly enjoy that bungalow? No.

To speak of, you have everything, but as long as your body and mind are not truly healthy, you will not get real enjoyment either. All the materials of enjoyment are there, but there is no real enjoyment. Is that acceptable? Real enjoyment is nothing at all; it is zero.

## After Enjoyment: Become Vast, Become Powerful

All right, you have become an enjoyer. So can being an enjoyer be the final stage of my life? I have enjoyed all the pleasures of life. Now I must not remain an ordinary person.

> "My true nature is infinite, so there are infinite powers within me."

So I should become that kind of powerful person. Isn't that so? Should one become vast or not?

First comes pure feeling, a clean mind, a steady mind, a vast mind. After that I should think of becoming big, great, noble. If I am to open a hospital, why just a small one? I will open a whole chain of hospitals. If I give discourses, why only to four people? Let it reach the whole world. This is our nature. Try to become a powerful person.

## What Are Siddhis?

So become something of a siddha, attain siddhis. Siddhis means certain special powers. They can be physical powers too: becoming strong, becoming capable. And what is the second kind? Becoming spiritually powerful. But yes, do not misuse siddhis.

We say this of Hanuman ji too:

> **aṣṭa siddhi nava nidhi ke dātā, asa bara dīna jānakī mātā**
> "He is the giver of the eight siddhis and the nine treasures; such is the boon Mother Janaki gave him."
> (Hanuman Chalisa, Chaupai 31)

Hanuman ji is the giver of the eight siddhis and nine treasures. Every god performed some miracle or other. Which means, did they have siddhis or not?

Whether it is vak siddhi or sankalpa siddhi, anima siddhi, laghima siddhi or garima siddhi, there are a great many siddhis. Some give the power to become large, some to become small, there is everything. There are siddhis like parakaya pravesh, entering another body. Siddhis are infinite; there are a great many. That is why Hanuman ji is called the giver of the eight siddhis and nine treasures. There are very many small siddhis; these are the big ones, like flying in the air, which is called levitation.

So you should try to become a powerful man or woman. Have you understood this much?

## Even for Power, One Must First Be Healthy

But only if one is healthy will one be able to become so powerful, isn't that so? Think: someone is a leader, but he has no strength, nothing, no voice, he is weak, he sits taking medicine every day. Will that do?

He is a spiritual person too, so why only discourses? Someone else will work black magic on him. He needs protection from that, doesn't he? So he should have many siddhis.

What were our rishis and munis like? They had no weapons, but if they gave a single curse, people were afraid to come before them, weren't they? So you should have your own power, the power of self-protection. You will have to be strong.

Become physically powerful, or become spiritually powerful, or at least become so powerful in intellect that no one can challenge you in your field.

## The Power to Resist: The Shakti of Mahakali and Bhairavi

The shakti of Mahakali comes to you, the Bhairavi shakti comes. You should also have the ability to inspire fear in others. Never to become a terrorist, but so that no one can ever take unfair advantage of you. So the power to resist is needed, isn't it?

That is why it is said: become a siddha purusha. Become extremely powerful in your field.

## The Misuse of Siddhis: Sick Once Again

So, from the sick to the healthy, from the healthy to the enjoyer, from the enjoyer to siddhi. Now, after siddhis come, you may start doing many things for people.

One who has a siddhi of healing and the like, and starts opening his shop right there, then, because he works in the vaishya-bhava, the attitude of a trader, people's illnesses come back upon him. Now he too becomes a sick person. But this is not acceptable, because he simply misused the siddhis.

## Above Siddhi Is Liberation

Above siddhi is liberation. So one should first enter the liberated state. The scriptures say to first attain the state of moksha. One must not begin using siddhis right away, otherwise those siddhis will cause your downfall; so it is said.

> "Siddhis are said to be like excrement, fit to be given up."

But those same siddhis, when you become jivanmukta, liberated while living, you can use rightly. Shri Krishna must have used them many times; Shri Ram must have used them. Does every guru, every avatar have his own siddhis or not? Tell me that first. Did Sant Dnyaneshwar Mauli not make that wall fly? So siddhis can be used. Every single guru performed these miracles and the like. So after becoming liberated, you can do all this.

## The Illness of a Liberated Guru: The Illness of Others

Then an exchange of energy takes place. In that state he may once again become sick. But the illness that comes now will be someone else's illness, not his own. Have you understood this much?

That is why you see Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, who left his body with cancer, and you see Ramana Maharshi too. You see Vivekananda ji suffering from diabetes, and all the other gurus too are seen in some suffering or other. But they first became liberated, and then took the karma of others upon themselves. Their illness is either that of others, or has come because of karma. They will attain moksha even in this state of illness. You will not be able to. Have you understood this much? Have you understood its order?

## The Halahala Poison: The Guru Is Shiva

> "From the sick to the healthy, from the healthy to the enjoyer, from the enjoyer to siddhi, and from siddhi to liberation."

And after becoming liberated, taking people's karma upon oneself. Then the halahala poison begins, and only then do you become Shiva. Because the jivanmukta guru alone is Shiva, and Shiva alone is the guru.

Now he must take on people's halahala poison. Now if illnesses come to him, if he can remove them, fine; and if they become too many, he will have to bear them, according to karma, according to his prarabdha.

## The Difference Between the Illness of an Ordinary Person and of a Sadguru

The difference between the illness of an ordinary person and that of a guru is only this: the first will run at once: "I need medicine, I need medicine! It hurts, it hurts! Do something, doctor!" He will run here and there, this test, that test.

If it is a sadguru, it is not that he will not take medicine or not seek treatment. But he knows because of whom this illness has come. He has taken the halahala poison consciously, so he will not weep so much. If he gets medicine, fine; if not, that is fine too. He understands that this is prarabdha, and for how long it is.

That is why the difference between these two states becomes clear.
