*Why does health come first on the spiritual path?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that nobody attains liberation while lying sick. Break just a fingernail, he notes, and try saying "I am not the body" through the pain.

💭 _Does your belief that you are not the body hold firm in pain?_
