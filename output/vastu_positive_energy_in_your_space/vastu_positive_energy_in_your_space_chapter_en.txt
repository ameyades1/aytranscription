# Chapter: Vastu and the Energy of Your Home

## Introduction

This conversation took place between Mr. Nishant Bhava of the Australian company "Ghar Real Estate" and Acharya Ji of Antar Yog Vastu Rupantaran. Nishant Ji sells Australian properties, first only within Australia and now to people in India as well. His question was how people can find solutions to the problems of their lives through Vastu, and how those living in a distant country can benefit from it.

## The Three Foundations of a Place

Acharya Ji says that every residential space is made of three things.

The first is its **location**, that is, where it is situated. If the surrounding area is not good, it can affect your destiny. The second is its **structure and design**. If the design is not right, there is still a problem. And the third is the **energy** of the place itself, its vibrations.

## We Do Not Break Walls, We Raise Vibrations

> "We do not believe in breaking walls or changing directions. We raise the vibrations of the entire place itself, and that balances everything."

For this we conduct Mahayagyas, other Pujas and Abhishek. From them we get Bhasma and Kumkum. All of these are extremely powerful things. Then there are Yantras: the Mahamrityunjaya Yantra, the Sri Yantra, the Kali Yantra.

But which Yantra is used depends on the person. Looking at the horoscope of the owner of the house, the problem he is struggling with, and the current planetary positions, we give a solution suited to him. Such Yantras are energized and placed in your flooring. Many things are put into the plaster, the cement and the walls, and that becomes the energy of your entire house. Along with it, the Sankalp of the Guru also works.

We have seen wonders happen through this. If someone in the house is ill, he begins to recover because of it.

## Destiny and Vastu

In Vastu Rupantaran we always say that 33 percent of your destiny is affected by the Vastu you live in.

It must be accepted that every person has his own destiny, but that is only one part of the reason. Suppose a person is unable to do good business in a place. He sells it, a company takes it over, and its business starts running. Then the problem was not in the Vastu but in that person.

But if nobody is able to do business in that place, the place may be afflicted, it may not be good. Then Vastu correction is necessary. Sometimes the place itself is not suitable.

## A Different Yantra for Every Person

How do we correct it? The same Yantra is not for everyone.

- If you are starting a hotel, the **Annapurna Yantra** will be placed.
- If someone is suffering from problems such as black magic, we prefer to place **Mahakali, Hanuman Ji** or similar Yantras.
- If there are children or many students in the house, **Saraswati Yantras** will be needed.

Someone is in finance, someone is a consultant. So it is designed entirely for the person, according to the owner of the house and those who live there. We look at the horoscope of the main person, and a video walk-through of the house is enough for us to understand the defects.

## Nadi Jyotish: Destiny in a Thumb Impression

Along with this we have the **Antar Yog Nadi Jyotish Kendra**. This is an ancient Vidya in which Agastya Muni himself wrote predictions for all of us two thousand years ago.

You only have to give your thumb impression. Just by looking at it, our Nadi Guruji tells your name, your parents' names, your wife's name. He begins telling your whole horoscope, which planet is where. No name is needed, nothing else.

You confirm the present, that yes, this is my palm leaf, and then based on it they tell your past and future. It also shows whether there is a problem in your Vastu, whether there is any Pitru Dosha or an obstacle in your destiny. All of this is done online.

## Possible Even From a Distant Country

Nishant Ji asked how people in Australia, so far from India, can take consultations.

Acharya Ji explained that the consultation can be online, and the Nadi recommendation can also be online. If a builder, architect or interior designer is working on the project, we can provide them energized Yantras. These Yantras are energized in the divine environment of our own Gurukul. And it is not one or two: around **32 Yantras** are placed in your house. This greatly increases your energy.

> "All I can say is that these things are to be experienced."

People who were struggling with legal problems, health problems and relationship problems found their problems beginning to resolve on their own as the energy changed.

## Kala Yatra: Vastu That Changes With Time

Vastu is a mix of technology, science and spirituality. Earlier it was said that the owner should sit in the center of the house or office. Today in many Vastus people sit right in the corner. Why? Because technology has changed. Now there are CCTV cameras.

The purpose of sitting in the center was to keep an eye all around the office. That is no longer needed. You can sit at the edge and still see everything, because science has changed.

There are many aspects that we now call time-bound. Many concepts of the old traditional Vastu are no longer valid. Vastu is based on science, and it changes with time. We call this **Kala Yatra**: we have to adapt to the times. You have to understand the purpose. We do not believe in orthodox methods, though many things are still valid today.

## Vastu in an Already Built Building

Nishant Ji raised a practical question. In Australia many people think that whatever the builder makes is what it is. If something is placed under the floor or in a wall of a flat, it may affect the product's warranty. The regulations there may also not allow it. The flooring is already done, so will it be pulled up?

Acharya Ji first asked whether the question was about the builder, who owns the entire land, or about the owner of a single flat. When the space is in its bare-shell state, we prefer to do the work at that time. Just as time changes and people change, now the country is changing, so there is an alternative way as well.

**At the Builder's Level**

For the builder, who is initially the owner of the entire land, the first level of Upay is the foundation itself. That is where we place all the Yantras. During digging many Doshas can arise. Insects, creatures, snakes and so on may be hurt, and those curses also work. So at that very level a Shanti Puja or remedy is done. Then, when the building is complete, new Yantras are placed again at the level of the flat.

**At the Buyer's Level**

Nishant Ji asked: but that is the builder's Vastu. I am the buyer, the house is ready, I am entering an already built house. The floor cannot be touched; what then?

Acharya Ji said that it is possible. The walls can be used. If everything is ready it is a little difficult, but if the walls are to be painted or plastered again, we can put the Yantras into the cement at that time. Ideally, the floor is the best.

> "It is like surgery done from within, as in the human body."

Then there is an external remedy, in which Yantras are placed in the house. That is a different method. So the order is:

1. Best: Yantras in the floor.
2. If the floor is not possible, then in the walls.
3. If neither is possible, either wait until the interiors are done next time, or place the Yantras externally.

External placement will not be as powerful as placement within, but there is certainly a way.

## The Formula for Success in Business: Shri Vidya

Finally, Nishant Ji asked for guidance for his company. Ghar Real Estate is in Australia and wants to guide people in India to invest in Australia. What should they do, and what should they not do?

Acharya Ji said that for this one must turn not only to Vastu but to **Shri Vidya**. Shri Vidya gives abundant wealth and unstoppable success, in which we receive the power to attract people.

> "Money does not come in the rain, it comes from someone's pocket."

So first one has to attract the person. This is the Vidya of Mahalakshmi. It also gives the power of speech, how I communicate with you. Every business begins with communication; today too we are communicating. Whenever a deal is to be made, Saraswati comes first, and Lakshmi with her. Saraswati is the power of speech.

So you must do Saraswati Sadhana, and for Mahalakshmi we have the recitation of the Durga Saptashati. My suggestion is that you do Shri Vidya Sadhana and keep Shri Yantras at your place.

## Vishnu First, Lakshmi Follows

The second thing is that Lakshmi does not come alone. She comes with Vishnu; the two have to stay together.

> "Vishnu is none other than your customer, and Lakshmi is what is in his pocket."

You must take care of Vishnu first. Lakshmi is Pativrata; she will follow. So take care of your customers, guide them about all the benefits. Then the money will not just follow; the customers will bring others with them. Antar Yog has grown on a large scale in exactly this way. We call this **Vishnu-Lakshmi Yukta**.

If you bring only Lakshmi, Lakshmi is Chanchala. She does not stay; she comes and goes. So the focus must be on the customer. The more you work for his benefit, the more value you give.

## Honesty Is the Only Policy

In Shri Vidya we always say that honesty is the only policy. I will not even call it the best policy; it is the only policy. What you have promised, you must deliver. You cannot promise one thing and deliver something else.

Nishant Ji understood it this way: it is repeat business. Keep the customer happy and one person will bring ten others. That is, what matters most is focus on the customer, not on money.

## Value and Price

Acharya Ji went on to say that it need not always be like social service. You can charge. We always say, **"Haq se maango"** (ask for it by right). Charge double the price, become the most expensive in the market, there is no problem, provided you give five times.

There are two aspects: one is price, the other is value. The lock of your house may cost two hundred or five hundred rupees. That is the price. But its value is that it protects property worth crores. Just as when a doctor performs surgery and saves your life, its price may be a few thousand or a few lakh, but it is priceless.

You have to justify the price. If the price is a hundred rupees, your value should be at least a thousand, ten times.

## Regard Your Customer as Vishnu

When we in Antar Yog offer any service, the Dakshina is at times even higher than the market. But do not worry about that. Tell your customers: search the whole market, and if there is a better option, show me. But you yourself must be certain that you are giving the best; you must have faith in your work.

> "Give what you believe in, and trust your customer as if he were Vishnu himself. Lakshmi will follow."

Then there will be expansion. Not just Australia; the whole world will come to India.
