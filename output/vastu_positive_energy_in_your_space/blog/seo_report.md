## 1. Summary

A conversation between Nishant Bhava of Ghar Real Estate (Australia) and Acharya Ji of Antar Yog Vastu Transformation. Its message: a home rests on three things (location, structure and energy), and Antar Yog's approach corrects Vastu without demolition, by raising the vibration of the whole space through mahayajna, puja, abhishek, energised yantras set into floors and walls, and the Guru's sankalpa. It covers how much of destiny the space shapes (33 percent, as Vastu Transformation holds), why yantras are chosen per person (kundli, problem, planets), Nadi astrology, Vastu as "Kaal Yatra" (adapting to the times), what to do in a ready-built flat (floor, then walls, then external installation), and, at the end, Shri Vidya business advice: speech first, the customer as Vishnu, honesty as the only policy, value ten times the price. The main intent is **practical**: people who want to correct a home's Vastu but cannot or will not break walls, especially flat owners and Indians abroad. Ranking pages for "vastu remedies without demolition" are mostly listicles of mirrors, salt and colour tips; this post's angle (energy first, yantras placed during construction or renovation, a "Kaal Yatra" view of old rules) is distinctive. It is the only Vastu post in the collection, so it has no cannibalization risk but also no Vastu pillar to link to yet. Seasonal hook: griha pravesh and Diwali home-cleaning searches (October–November) and Akshaya Tritiya (property purchases); refresh before each.

## 6. FAQ

For the editor; not added to the page.

1. **क्या वास्तु सुधार के लिए तोड़-फोड़ ज़रूरी है? / Do you need demolition to fix your Vastu?**
   अंतरयोग का मार्ग दीवारें तोड़ने या दिशाएँ बदलने का नहीं; पूरे स्थान के स्पंदन ऊँचे करके संतुलन लाया जाता है। / Not in Antar Yog's approach: instead of tearing down walls or changing directions, the vibration of the whole space is raised.
2. **बने-बनाए फ्लैट में वास्तु कैसे सुधारें? / How can you fix the Vastu of a ready-built flat?**
   सबसे उत्तम फर्श में यंत्र; संभव न हो तो अगली रंगाई या प्लास्टर पर दीवारों में; वह भी न हो तो बाहरी स्थापना, जो कम शक्तिशाली पर फिर भी एक मार्ग है। / Best is yantras in the floor; if not, in the walls at the next repaint or replaster; failing that, external installation, which is less powerful but still a path.
3. **क्या सबके लिए एक ही यंत्र होता है? / Is there one yantra for everyone?**
   नहीं। घर के स्वामी की कुंडली, समस्या और ग्रह स्थिति के अनुसार यंत्र चुने जाते हैं, जैसे होटल के लिए अन्नपूर्णा यंत्र, विद्यार्थियों के लिए सरस्वती यंत्र। / No. Yantras are chosen by the homeowner's birth chart, problem and planetary positions, such as the Annapurna Yantra for a hotel or the Saraswati Yantra for students.
4. **क्या पुराने वास्तु नियम आज भी लागू होते हैं? / Do old Vastu rules still apply?**
   आचार्य जी इसे काल यात्रा कहते हैं: कई अवधारणाएँ समयबद्ध थीं, इसलिए उद्देश्य समझिए, रूढ़ि नहीं; हालाँकि बहुत सी बातें आज भी मान्य हैं। / Acharya Ji calls this Kaal Yatra: many concepts belonged to their era, so understand the purpose, not the custom, though much still holds.
5. **क्या विदेश में रहकर वास्तु सुधार हो सकता है? / Can you fix your Vastu while living abroad?**
   हाँ। परामर्श ऑनलाइन होता है; कुंडली और घर का वीडियो भ्रमण पर्याप्त है, और ऊर्जित यंत्र बिल्डर या इंटीरियर डिज़ाइनर को भेजे जा सकते हैं। / Yes. Consultation is online; a birth chart and a video walkthrough are enough, and energised yantras can be sent to your builder or interior designer.

## 10. Authenticity flags

2. **"Shri Vidya says honesty is the only policy" (review issue 2).** A modern maxim, not a Shri Vidya text. Attribute to Acharya Ji's teaching as the review suggests; not used in SEO text.
3. **Agastya and "two thousand years ago" stated as fact (review issue 3).** Tradition's claim; use the review's "according to tradition" wording. Not used in SEO text.
4. **"33 percent of your destiny" (review issue 4).** No scriptural source; the body attributes it to Vastu Transformation but the takeaway does not. Fix the takeaway; keep the figure out of titles and meta (it is not used there).
5. **श्राप → शाप (review issue 5), kumkum glossed as vermilion (review issue 6), "wife's name" → "spouse's name" (review issue 7).** Minor wording fixes.
6. **Health and problem-solving claims.** "Sick people regain their health in such homes" and legal, health and relationship problems "resolving on their own" are Acharya Ji's experience, correctly attributed in the body. Do not repeat them as promises in titles, meta, FAQ, social cards or schema; none of the SEO text here does. The FAQ makes no outcome claim.
