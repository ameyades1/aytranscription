# Vastu and the Energy of Your Home

Have you ever walked into a house and felt your mind grow lighter at once, while another place weighed on you for no reason you could name?

Nishant Bhava of Ghar Real Estate, a company in Australia, wanted to understand exactly this from Acharya Ji of Antar Yog Vastu Transformation. He asked how **Vastu** (the traditional Indian science of space and architecture) can resolve the problems in our lives, and how people living far from India can benefit from it. This article grew out of that conversation.

Are there Vastu remedies without demolition? In Antar Yog's approach, yes. Acharya Ji explains that a home rests on location, structure and energy, and that instead of breaking walls, raising the vibration of the whole space through mahayajna, puja and energised yantras set into the floor or walls brings balance, even in a ready-built flat.

## What Three Things Make Up a Home?

According to Acharya Ji, every home rests on three things.

The first is its **location**. If the surrounding area is unfavourable, it can affect your destiny. The second is its **structure and layout**. A flawed design also leaves problems in place.

The third is the subtlest of all: the **energy** of the place, its vibrations.

## Do You Have to Break Down Walls to Fix Your Vastu?

Many people hear the word Vastu and immediately fear demolition. The Antar Yog approach is different. Acharya Ji explains that they do not believe in tearing down walls or changing directions. Instead, they raise the vibration of the entire space, and that higher vibration brings everything into balance.

To do this, they perform a **mahayajna** (a great fire ceremony), puja and **abhishek** (the ritual bathing of a deity). The **bhasma** (sacred ash) and **kumkum** (sacred vermilion powder) that come from these rituals are extremely powerful. Then there are **yantras** (sacred geometric diagrams that hold and radiate energy), such as the Mahamrityunjaya Yantra, the Shri Yantra and the Kali Yantra.

These yantras are energised and then set into the floor, the plaster, the cement and the walls, until they become the energy of the whole house. Alongside them works the Guru's **sankalpa** (firm spiritual resolve). Acharya Ji shares that he has seen sick people regain their health in such homes.

## How Much of Your Destiny Depends on Your Home?

Vastu Transformation holds that the space you live in shapes **33 percent** of your destiny.

It is true that every person carries their own destiny. But consider an example. A man's business fails in a particular place, so he sells the property. A company buys it, and its business takes off. In that case the problem lay with the man, not with the Vastu.

If **nobody** can make a business work in that place, however, the place itself may be blocked. That is when correcting the Vastu becomes necessary.

*Is something stuck in your life because of what is inside you, or because of the place where you live and work?*

## No Single Yantra Fits Everyone

Vastu is not a ready-made prescription. Each solution is designed around the homeowner's **kundli** (Vedic birth chart), their particular problem and the current planetary positions.

- If you are opening a hotel, the **Annapurna Yantra**.
- If you face an obstacle such as black magic, the yantras of **Mahakali or Hanuman**.
- If there are children or students in the house, the **Saraswati Yantra**.

The birth chart of the head of the household and a video walkthrough of the home are enough to understand what is wrong.

## Destiny Written in a Thumbprint: Nadi Astrology

The Antar Yog Nadi Astrology Centre is built on an ancient science. Two thousand years ago, the sage Agastya wrote down predictions for every one of us.

All you need to give is your thumbprint. From it, the Nadi reader begins to tell you your name, your parents' names, your wife's name and your entire birth chart. You confirm these details about your present, recognising the palm leaf as your own, and then your past and future open up.

The reading also shows whether there is a flaw in your Vastu, or a **pitru dosha** (an affliction linked to one's ancestors). All of this can be done online.

## Living Abroad? Distance Is No Obstacle

Both the consultation and the Nadi recommendations can take place online. The yantras are energised in the divine atmosphere of the **gurukul** (a traditional spiritual school and hermitage) and can be sent to your builder, architect or interior designer. A typical home receives about **32 yantras**. Acharya Ji says these are things to be experienced, and shares that people struggling with legal, health and relationship problems found them resolving on their own once the energy changed.

## Kaal Yatra: Vastu Moves with the Times

Vastu blends technology, science and spirituality. In the past, the owner was told to sit at the centre of the office so he could see in every direction. Today there is CCTV, so you can sit in a corner and still see everything.

Many concepts of traditional Vastu belonged to their era. Acharya Ji calls this **Kaal Yatra** (the journey through time): adapting as the times change. Understand the purpose, not the custom. That said, much of the old wisdom still holds today.

## How Can You Apply Vastu to a Ready-Built Flat?

Countries like Australia have strict regulations. Placing anything under the floor can affect your warranty. So what can you do?

**For the builder:** The first level of remedy is the foundation. Digging can harm insects, small creatures, snakes and other beings, and their curses take effect too. That is why a peace puja and the installation of yantras take place right there. Once the building stands, new yantras go in again at the level of each flat.

**For the buyer:** Even when the home is already finished, there is a way.

1. Best of all, yantras in the floor. Think of it as surgery performed from within.
2. If the floor is not possible, place them in the walls the next time you repaint or replaster.
3. If neither is possible, wait for the next interior renovation, or install the yantras externally.

External installation is not as powerful, but it is still a path.

## Business Success: The Shri Vidya Formula

Finally, Nishant asked for advice for his own company. Acharya Ji pointed him to **Shri Vidya**, the science of abundant wealth and unstoppable success, which gives you the power to draw people to you. He explains that money does not fall like rain; it comes out of someone's pocket. You first have to attract the person, and the key to that is **speech**. Every business begins with a conversation. Saraswati, the goddess of speech and learning, comes first, and Lakshmi, the goddess of wealth, follows with her. For this he recommends Saraswati sadhana (spiritual practice), recitation of the **Durga Saptashati** (a sacred text of seven hundred verses in praise of Maa Durga) for Mahalakshmi, and a Shri Yantra in your place of work.

## Your Customer Is Vishnu

Lakshmi never arrives alone; she comes with Lord Vishnu. Acharya Ji explains that Vishnu is none other than your customer, and Lakshmi is what sits in his pocket.

Look after Vishnu, and Lakshmi, ever **pativrata** (devoted to her husband), will follow on her own. A happy customer brings others along. This is how Antar Yog has grown, and it is called being **Vishnu-Lakshmi yukta** (united with both Vishnu and Lakshmi). Chase Lakshmi alone, and you will find she is **chanchala** (restless, fickle): she comes and she goes.

*In your work, where is your attention: on the good of the person in front of you, or only on the money?*

## Honesty: Not the Best Policy, the Only One

Shri Vidya teaches that honesty is not the best policy; it is the **only policy**. Deliver exactly what you promised.

And do not hesitate to charge for your work. **"Ask for it as your right."** Charge double, provided you give five times as much. A house lock costs a few hundred rupees, yet it protects property worth crores. The care of a doctor who saves a life is priceless. If your price is a hundred, the value you give should be at least a thousand.

Tell your customer: look across the whole market and show me something better. To say that, though, you must be certain yourself that you are offering the best. Treat the customer as Vishnu himself, and Lakshmi will follow. Then not just Australia but the whole world will come to India.

- Every space rests on three foundations: location, structure and energy.
- Antar Yog Vastu brings balance by raising vibrations, not by demolition.
- Your Vastu shapes 33 percent of your destiny.

- A finished home still has remedies: the floor first, then the walls, then external installation.
- In business, Shri Vidya means the power of speech, the customer as Vishnu, honesty as the only policy, and value ten times the price.
