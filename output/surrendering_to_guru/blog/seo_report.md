## 1. Summary

The talk explains Bhagavad Gita 18.66 ("सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज"): the verse says to give up every *dharma*, not every *adharma*; Acharya Ji reads dharma here as the duty of each role (कर्तव्य कर्म), and the refuge as refuge in a living Guru who shows which path (which "train") is right. It closes with "चमत्कार को नहीं, ठीक से नमस्कार करो" (namaskar as "न मम", the dropping of ego). The main search intent is informational: the meaning of Gita 18.66, one of the most searched Gita verses in both Hindi and English ("sarva dharman parityajya meaning", "सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य का अर्थ"). Competition is high on the bare verse (ISKCON, Gita Press, Holy-Bhagavad-Gita and many verse-by-verse sites), but those pages give a literal word meaning. The opening for this post is the question those pages leave open ("if I abandon all dharma, do I stop being religious or truthful?") and the angle only this talk gives: dharma as role-duty, refuge as a living Guru's direction, and the train example. Secondary intent is navigational/emotional (surrender to the Guru, why a living Guru). The YouTube video (5:02) is short and embeddable, which helps for video results.

## 5. Direct answer paragraph

**Hindi (52 words):** गीता 18.66 "सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य मामेकं शरणं व्रज" में श्रीकृष्ण सब अधर्म नहीं, सब धर्म छोड़कर अपनी शरण में आने को कहते हैं। आचार्य जी समझाते हैं कि यहाँ धर्म का अर्थ मज़हब नहीं, हर भूमिका का कर्तव्य कर्म है, और शरण जीवित गुरु की है: अपनी धारणाएँ छोड़कर गुरु से पूछो कि तुम्हारे लिए क्या सही है।

**English (56 words):** In Gita 18.66, "sarva dharman parityajya mam ekam sharanam vraja", Krishna asks Arjuna to set aside every dharma, not every wrong, and take refuge in Him alone. Acharya Ji explains that dharma here means the duty of each role, not religion, and that the refuge is a living Guru: drop your own assumptions and ask the Guru what is right for you.

## 6. FAQ

For the page or for People-also-ask targeting. Not added as FAQPage schema (Google shows FAQ rich results only for authoritative government and health sites).

1. **सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य का अर्थ क्या है? / What does sarva dharman parityajya mean?**
   "सब धर्मों को छोड़कर केवल मेरी शरण में आ जा। मैं तुझे सब पापों से मुक्त कर दूँगा, शोक मत कर" (गीता 18.66). "परित्यज्य" का अर्थ है पूरी तरह छोड़ देना। / "Abandon all dharmas and take refuge in Me alone. I will free you from all sins; do not grieve." Parityajya means letting go completely.
2. **क्या सब धर्म छोड़ने का अर्थ हिंदू या सत्यवादी होना छोड़ना है? / Does abandoning every dharma mean giving up one's religion?**
   नहीं। आचार्य जी के अनुसार यहाँ धर्म का अर्थ मज़हब नहीं, कर्तव्य कर्म है: पुत्र, पिता, पति, पड़ोसी हर भूमिका का करणीय-अकरणीय। / No. Acharya Ji explains that dharma here means duty, the things each role (son, father, husband, neighbour) ought and ought not to do, not a religion. Nor does it mean giving up truthfulness.
3. **"मामेकं शरणं व्रज" में शरण किसकी है? / Whose refuge does the verse mean?**
   आचार्य जी इसे गुरु की शरण के रूप में पढ़ते हैं: अर्जुन के लिए कृष्ण थे, आपके लिए एक जीवित गुरु। / In Acharya Ji's reading, the refuge is the Guru: for Arjuna it was Krishna; for you it is a living Guru, since a future Guru or one who has left the body cannot guide you now.
4. **गुरु की शरण में जाने का व्यावहारिक अर्थ क्या है? / What does surrendering to the Guru mean in practice?**
   अपनी धारणाएँ छोड़कर गुरु से पूछना कि मेरे लिए क्या सही है, फिर वैसा करना। सही गाड़ी पकड़ ली तो मंज़िल अपने आप आती है। / Setting aside your own assumptions, asking the Guru what is right for you, and doing it. Board the right train and the destination comes on its own.
5. **"चमत्कार को नहीं, नमस्कार करो" का अर्थ क्या है? / What does "don't bow to miracles, bow properly" mean?**
   ठीक से नमस्कार का भाव है "न मम", मेरा नहीं, तेरा; इससे अहंकार जाता है। / Bowing properly carries the feeling "na mama", not mine but Yours; as that feeling grows, the ego dissolves.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **"माम्" read as the living Guru (interpretation, not translation).** The literal Gita Press meaning is refuge in Krishna (Parameshvara). The blog marks the Guru reading as Acharya Ji's ("आचार्य जी समझाते हैं", "is read as"). Titles, meta descriptions, FAQ answers and snippets above say "Acharya Ji explains" or pair "Gita 18.66" with "and the Guru"; never write "Gita 18.66 says surrender to a living Guru" as if it were the verse's literal meaning. Recommendation: keep this attribution in any future edit.
2. **Health and healing claims.** The talk links sins to illness, poverty and broken relationships, and mentions sudden healings during a session. These are the speaker's teaching and testimony, not medical claims. They are kept out of titles, metas, FAQ and the direct answer on purpose. Recommendation: never use "cure", "healing" or "remedy" as a keyword for this post; if the healing line is ever promoted, add that it does not replace medical care.
3. **समाधिस्थ (Sanskrit review, item 1).** In Hindi, "समाधिस्थ गुरु" can mean a living Guru absorbed in samadhi; the intended sense is a Guru who has left the body. Recommendation to the editor: "समाधि ले चुके गुरु भी आपको किसी जीवित गुरु के पास ही भेजते हैं।"
4. **Gita 2.47 quoted in half (review, item 2).** Add "पूर्वार्ध" / "first half" to the reference.
5. **IAST anusvara (review, item 3).** English verse uses ṁ; the review's standard is ṃ ("mām ekaṃ śaraṇaṃ vraja", "ahaṃ").
6. **"मामेकं शरणं व्रज" gloss drops "alone" (review, item 4).** "केवल मेरी शरण में आ जा" / "take refuge in Me alone". The SEO texts above use "Me alone" in the verse meaning.
7. **parityajya as "tyajya + pari" like atma → Paramatma (review, item 5).** A teaching analogy, stated in the English as grammar. Recommendation: frame it as Acharya Ji's comparison; do not use the derivation in a snippet or word-meaning box.
8. **"न मम" for namaskar (review, item 6).** A traditional nirukti, not the grammatical root (√नम्). The FAQ answer says "carries the feeling", not "derives from". Keep that framing.
9. **"Dharma does not mean religion."** A fair reading (Gita Press also glosses सर्वधर्म as सम्पूर्ण कर्तव्य कर्म), but keyword pages on 18.66 attract interfaith debate. Do not sharpen it into "the Gita says give up your religion" or its opposite for clicks; the blog's wording is balanced and should stay so.
10. **पतिव्रता धर्म example.** One of several role-duty examples; it is faithful to the talk but is a sensitive line out of context. Keep it out of social quote cards and snippets.
