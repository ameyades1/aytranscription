*Why wait until retirement to turn inward?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that spiritual life will not suddenly appear after sixty if never practised. Many retirees decline within two years, he notes, because nothing within sustains them.

💭 _If your work and role were taken away tomorrow, what would remain within you?_
