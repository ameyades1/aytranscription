*Is chanting Om at death enough for liberation?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains, through the Bhagavad Gita, that remembrance must fill the whole life. Death gives no warning, and at the end you remember the thought that ruled your life.

💭 _Does your spiritual practice come from hope of gain, or from love?_
