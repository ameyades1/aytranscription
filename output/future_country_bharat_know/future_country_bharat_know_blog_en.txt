# Whose Hands Hold the Future of Bharat

## The Guru's Dilemma: Sadhana First, or Teaching First?

There is so much to teach. Truly, so much.

Gurudev himself says he is in a dilemma: should he first lead **sadhana** (spiritual practice), or first teach? And in the teaching there is one big subject: how to change India. There is a complete plan for it.

## Is Changing India Only the Guru's Work?

We often assume that changing a country is the work of one great soul or Guru. But Gurudev says clearly: everyone has a role.

We need to understand where India falls short and how to fill that gap. If a new administration is to come, if everything new is to come, there is one foundation: **spiritual power must grow.**

*What role do you see for yourself in the changing of India?*

## The Coming Times: Bright, but Through Hardship

Gurudev shares a few predictions so that we stay **sajag** (alert, aware).

India's future is bright, but it will pass through many problems. Right from January 2024, a big problem will come. Corona has not gone anywhere. Some big problem is to come through 2024, 2025 and 2026, once again natural problems, many of them.

Who will stand firm in such times? Gurudev says that those who do spiritual sadhana will rise.

## 2029: A Spiritual Turn in Politics

Gurudev's boldest prediction concerns the 2029 election. According to him, not a single big politician of today will be able to get elected in it.

Whether Andhra, Maharashtra or Tamil Nadu, whether in power or in opposition, a leader can be elected only when they attain spiritual power. If they change, it will work. Not in their present state. That same person will not be able to win unless they transform themselves into a spiritual person.

Otherwise some local boy of today, who comes to the **Gurukul** or learns, will easily defeat him in 2029. Gurudev stresses: this is not about 2024, it is about 2029. For the first time, in 2029, it will certainly happen, and the work of Antar is going on for it.

## What Does the Nadi Shastra Say?

**Nadi Shastra** is an ancient Indian tradition of prediction. Gurudev says it contains a prediction about Antar: that ninety percent of the Parliament of 2029, or at the most 2034, will be Antar Yog sadhaks.

And Gurudev's conviction is unshaken: this too will certainly happen, for sure.

## Experience Before Belief

Gurudev knows that hearing this today, it is hard to believe. So he does not argue. He only says: first take your own experience for five days.

*Do you give something the chance to be experienced before you judge it?*

## Key Takeaways

- There is a complete plan to change India, and every Indian has a role in it, not only the Guru.
- A new administration and a new India will come only through the growth of spiritual power.
- Big problems, especially natural ones, may come from 2024 to 2026; those who do sadhana will rise.
- In the 2029 election, only leaders who transform themselves into spiritual persons will win.
- According to Nadi Shastra, 90 percent of the Parliament of 2029 or 2034 will be Antar Yog sadhaks.
- Before believing, take your own experience for five days.
