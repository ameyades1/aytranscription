# Who Holds the Future of Bharat?

According to Acharya Ji, the future of Bharat is bright, but it will come through many hardships. A new administration and a new Bharat can rest on one foundation alone: the growth of spiritual strength. Changing the country is not the Guru's job alone; every Indian has a part in it, and those who practise sadhana will rise.

## The Guru's Dilemma: Practice First or Teaching First?

There is so much to teach. Truly, so much.

Acharya Ji admits that he is torn. Should he first guide people through **sadhana** (spiritual practice), or should he begin by teaching? And among the things to teach, one subject looms large: how to transform Bharat. For this, there is a complete plan.

## Is Changing Bharat the Guru's Job Alone?

We often assume that changing a country is the work of one great soul or one Guru. Acharya Ji is clear that this is a mistake: everyone has a part to play.

We need to understand where Bharat falls short and how to fill that gap. If a new administration is to come, if everything is to be made new, it can rest on one foundation alone: **spiritual strength must grow.**

*What part do you see yourself playing in the transformation of Bharat?*

## What Is the Future of Bharat?

Acharya Ji shares a few predictions so that we stay **alert** and aware.

Bharat has a bright future, but it will reach it by passing through many troubles. A major problem will arrive as early as January 2024. Corona has not gone anywhere. Some serious crisis is bound to come through 2024, 2025 and 2026, and natural disasters will strike again, many of them.

Who will stand firm in such times? Acharya Ji says that those who practise spiritual sadhana are the ones who will rise.

## 2029: A Spiritual Turn in Politics

His boldest prediction concerns the 2029 election. According to Acharya Ji, not a single one of today's major politicians will be able to win it.

Whether in Andhra, Maharashtra or Tamil Nadu, whether in power or in opposition, a leader will win only by gaining spiritual strength. If these leaders change, they will be fine, but not as they are today. None of them will win until they transform themselves into spiritual people.

Otherwise, some local young man who comes to the **Gurukul** (a traditional school where students live and learn with the Guru), or who learns from it, will beat them easily in 2029. Acharya Ji stresses the point: this is not about 2024 but about 2029. It will happen for the first time in 2029, without fail, and Antar's work toward it is already underway.

## What Does Nadi Shastra Say?

**Nadi Shastra** (an ancient Indian tradition of foretelling the future) contains a prediction about Antar, Acharya Ji explains. It says that by 2029, or 2034 at the latest, 90 percent of the members of Parliament will be practitioners of Antar Yog.

Acharya Ji's faith in this is unshakeable. This too will happen, without a doubt.

## Experience Before Belief

Acharya Ji knows that this is hard to believe today. So he does not argue the point. He asks only one thing: first, experience it yourself for five days.

*Do you give something a chance to be experienced before you judge it?*

## Key Takeaways

- There is a complete plan to transform Bharat, and every Indian has a part in it, not the Guru alone.
- A new administration and a new Bharat will come only as spiritual strength grows.
- Serious problems, especially natural disasters, may come from 2024 to 2026; those who practise sadhana will rise.
- In the 2029 election, only leaders who transform themselves into spiritual people will win.
- According to Nadi Shastra, 90 percent of the members of Parliament in 2029 or 2034 will be Antar Yog practitioners.
- Before you believe, experience it yourself for five days.
