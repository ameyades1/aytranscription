# Chapter: Water, Your First Medicine

## Where Does the Water We Drink Go?

What happens to the water I drink? This is not only about water, but about all liquids. Whether I drink juice or take anything else, a part of it goes inside me as water, as liquid. Our body is almost seventy percent liquid. It is water itself, the **water element**. And what was the rest? The **earth element**. That is why we called flesh and the like the earth element.

Now the second point, about water. It too has three parts. Whatever I take, whether I take liquor, coconut water, milk, sambar or something else, we are speaking of whatever liquid comes into me.

1. The first part becomes liquid itself; it goes as far as forming blood. There are many liquids in our body, and some combination of them must form further on.
2. The second part leaves as urine.
3. The third part becomes **prana**. Prana means the complete life force, life energy.

## What Happens First at the Hospital?

That is why, when you go to a hospital, when you are unwell and are admitted, do you know what they do first? Do they give you a vada to eat? Do they welcome you with great pomp? Nothing of the sort. They lay you down and first put you on saline.

Why do they begin with liquid? Why not give food first? The person must have arrived tired, after all.

## Welcoming a Guest: Water First, Then Food

Leave the patient aside. When anyone comes in from outside after running around, or when you go to someone's house, what is said first of all? Give water, give water. Give water, give juice, give sharbat.

Did you ever, as someone arrived, say to them, "Come, eat this poha, eat something"? Did you ever put an idli into someone's mouth the moment they arrived? Has that ever happened? At the door, you would most likely have given water.

When you go to a function, who comes first with a tray? The vada, the hara bhara kebab, these snacks come later, once the person is inside. Who goes around outside first? The welcome-drink server. Go to a hotel and it is the same. Whether he gives a cold drink, plain water, juice, tea or coffee, what he gives is a liquid.

Why does this happen? Because the person has arrived tired, their prana has gone, and prana needs liquid.

> "You arrived tired, the prana went, so for prana you need liquid."

## The First Principle: Water for Prana

This first principle has settled in the mind. The point is very clear about what I need. I need prana energy, so I need liquid, water, or something in the form of water.

At drinking parties they probably serve liquor; prana comes from that too. But along with prana its quality will also come, because it has gone into the stomach. A little while ago I said: eat liquid, eat solid, it is bound to form the mind a little. But prana will also be formed from it.

That is why, when a person reaches old age, it is mostly said of them: do not let their body run short of water, they must not get dehydrated. So for prana to come, water is essential for me.

## Above the Annamaya Kosha Is the Pranamaya Kosha

Now, what is greater than my **annamaya kosha**? Was the annamaya kosha not the smallest? Above it is the **pranamaya kosha**. So my body will need more liquid. This much is understood.

> "Prana energy is far superior. Prana controls the annamaya kosha."

Prana energy is far superior. It is prana that governs the annamaya kosha. So I must keep greater control over liquids, over what I take in as liquid, because my prana can be formed from it.

## As the Body, So the Cosmos

My body is not seventy percent water by chance. In our world too, about seventy percent, technically seventy-one percent, is water: the oceans and so on. How much is land? Only twenty-nine or thirty percent is stated. This is a symbol of the same thing, because my own make-up is the same.

Why has this been said? Because it is said:

> **yathā piṇḍe tathā brahmāṇḍe, yathā brahmāṇḍe tathā piṇḍe**
> "As in the body, so in the cosmos; as in the cosmos, so in the body."
> (Traditional yogic saying)

Pinda means my body. What is in the cosmos is in this body. Our body is a replica of that same cosmos.

## The Moon, Water and the Mind

According to astrology, the **Moon** is the significator of water and also of the mind. So through how much water is in us and what kind of water has gone in, the Moon will also influence the mind. So whatever you take in, from this angle too it can affect the mind.

About food it has been said directly:

> **annamayaṃ hi somya manaḥ**
> "O gentle one, the mind is made of food."
> (Chandogya Upanishad 6.5.4)

O gentle one, the mind is made of food, that is, the mind is formed from the food we take in. Not only from food, but from water too. In this way, everything is affecting your mind. This much is understood.
