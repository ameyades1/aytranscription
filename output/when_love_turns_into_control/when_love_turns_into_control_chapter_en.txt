# Chapter: Why a Mother's Bill Is Always Zero

## Let Go of Insisting on the Fruit

Do your work and let go of insisting on the fruit; that is all. Our tradition has taught us:

> **mātṛdevo bhava, pitṛdevo bhava, ācāryadevo bhava**
> "Regard your mother as God, regard your father as God, regard your teacher as God."
> (Taittiriya Upanishad, Shikshavalli 1.11.2)

Now tell me, for everything a mother does for her son, how much does she bill him? What happens there is the renunciation of the fruit. The scriptures say that this state is the highest. Let go of insisting on the fruit; simply do your work.

## The Son's Bill and the Mother's Reply

There is a famous story. A boy writes a letter to his mother: "Yesterday I went to the market and brought all these things for you, twenty-five rupees. I did this task, twenty-five rupees. I did that task, fifty rupees. I did all these jobs for you, ten rupees. You told me every day to hang the clothes to dry, twenty-five rupees." In this way he draws up a bill for his mother.

What does the mother reply? You know this story. She writes: "I raised you from a little child, the price is zero. I breastfed you, the price is zero. When you were ill I took you to the hospital, the price is zero. When you were weak and your friends scolded you, shouted at you and hit you, I went to protect you, the price is zero. I had you vaccinated, the price is zero."

## Why a Mother Does Not Want the Fruit

Why did the mother put the price of everything at zero? Because she does not want the fruit. And why does a mother not want the fruit? Because she feels this child is directly one with her. The child is her own limb, a part of her. In such a case, direct love is natural.

When a son or daughter is born, does the mother know whether the child is dark or fair? She does not. Whether it will be tall or not, whether it has a disability, whether its eyes are crooked, whether it is beautiful, she does not know that either. Whether this child will one day become her enemy, she does not know that either. And yet how does love arise in her at once? Because "this is a part of me." She has to give it everything.

At that very moment **motherly feeling** (*mātṛbhāva*) awakens in her, and her **heart chakra** is awakened. Until the previous day, that woman may have been living in the lower chakras.

## The College Classmate

I saw this myself in my college days. There were some girls with us, the kind who today would be called tomboys, carefree and always out and about. One of them used to say, "I don't like small children; they are such troublemakers. If one comes in front of me, I'll give it a couple of slaps."

I was in college with them myself, so I asked, "What about when you have your own child?" She said, "I won't put up with as much as my mother did. If the child doesn't listen, I'll give it a couple of slaps."

Then time passed, she married, had a child, and by the very next day she had completely changed. I had thought, now let us see how much she hits the child. But I saw an entirely different scene.

Why did she change? Because nature, by default, at least for one's own child, awakens motherly feeling and the heart chakra in a woman at that very moment. That is why it is said: mātṛdevo bhava, pitṛdevo bhava, ācāryadevo bhava.

## When Expectation Comes, Motherhood Shrinks

What happened at that moment was the renunciation of the fruit. The fruit does come. If she gives good values, the son will do everything, he will be the staff of her old age. If it is a daughter, she too will do everything. But the day the mother starts expecting, she is no longer a good mother; so say the scriptures. A mother simply keeps giving. The scriptures say that this state is the highest.

## From Mother of One to Mother of the World

Everything else, meditating, gaining knowledge, has this purpose: that you reach this state. Now think, what knowledge had the mother acquired? What meditation had she done? She simply started doing it.

She did it for one child. Now, little by little, behave the same way with the whole world, and you too will become the **Mother of the World** (*Jaganmātā*). Right now she is the mother of only one. Show the same behaviour to your neighbour, even to your enemy's son, show it everywhere, and then you will become the Mother of the World. Have you understood?
