# Why a Mother's Bill Is Always Zero

Most of us love, but quietly we also keep accounts. "I did so much, what did I get in return?" The moment that account begins, love slowly starts turning into control and expectation.

In this short discourse, Acharya ji uses the example of a mother to explain what love without accounts is, and why it is the highest spiritual state.

## Do Your Work, Let Go of the Fruit

The discourse begins with a simple teaching: do your work and let go of insisting on the fruit. And with it, the root line of our tradition:

> **mātṛdevo bhava, pitṛdevo bhava, ācāryadevo bhava**
> "Regard your mother as God, regard your father as God, regard your teacher as God."
> (Taittiriya Upanishad, Shikshavalli 1.11.2)

Why is the mother called God? Because she naturally lives **renunciation of the fruit** (*phala tyāga*, action without desire for the result). Acharya Ji asks how much a mother bills her son for all that she does for him.

## A Son's Bill

There is a famous story. A boy writes his mother a letter and puts a price on every task. Brought things from the market, twenty-five rupees. Did this task, twenty-five rupees. Did that task, fifty rupees. Hung the clothes to dry every day, twenty-five rupees.

The mother sends her own bill in reply. Raised you from a little child: zero. Breastfed you: zero. Took you to the hospital when you were ill: zero. Came to protect you when your friends scolded and hit you: zero. Had you vaccinated: zero.

*Think about it: in your relationships, which bill do you write, the son's or the mother's?*

## Why Doesn't a Mother Ask for the Fruit?

Why did the mother put the price of everything at zero? Because she does not want the fruit at all. And she does not want it because she feels the child is not separate from her. The child is her own limb, a part of her.

At birth the mother knows nothing. Whether the child is dark or fair, beautiful or not, whether it has a disability. Not even whether it will one day become her enemy. Yet love comes at once, because "this is a part of me."

At that moment **motherly feeling** (*mātṛbhāva*) awakens in her and the **heart chakra** (the centre of love and compassion) is awakened. Until a day earlier, that same woman may have been living in the consciousness of the lower chakras. Acharya Ji points out that when the child is born, she does not even know whether it will later become her enemy, and still she loves it at once, because it is a part of her.

## The College Girl Who Changed

Acharya ji recalls an incident from his college days. With him was a carefree, tomboyish girl who used to say she did not like small children, and if one came in front of her she would slap it.

When asked what she would do with her own child, she said she would not put up with as much as her mother did; if the child did not listen, she would slap it.

Then she married, had a child, and by the very next day she had completely changed. The whole scene was different. Why? Because nature, by default, at least for one's own child, awakens motherly feeling and the heart chakra at that very moment.

## When Expectation Arrives

The fruit does come. Give good values, and the son becomes the staff of your old age; a daughter too will do everything. But the scriptures say that the day a mother begins to expect, she is no longer a good mother.

This is the fine line where love begins to turn into control. A true mother simply keeps giving. And Acharya ji says this state is the highest.

*Is there some expectation sitting quietly inside your love?*

## From Mother of One to Mother of the World

Meditation, knowledge, spiritual practice: the goal of all of them is to reach this state. But what knowledge had the mother acquired? What meditation had she done? She simply started doing it.

She did it for one child. Now, little by little, behave the same way with the whole world. With your neighbour, even with your enemy's son, everywhere. Acharya Ji says that whoever does this for the world, little by little, also becomes the Mother of the World.

**Jaganmātā** means the Mother of the whole world. Right now we live without accounts only for our own. Spiritual practice is the name for widening that circle.

## Key Takeaways

- Do your work and let go of insisting on the fruit; this is the highest state.
- A mother bills every service at zero, because she does not want the fruit.
- Seeing the child as part of oneself makes love natural; at that moment motherly feeling and the heart chakra awaken.
- When a mother starts expecting, she is no longer a good mother.
- The goal of meditation and knowledge is to reach this selfless state.
- Extend the feeling you have for one to the whole world, and you become the Mother of the World.
