## 1. Summary

A short talk that joins two meanings of "Guru": the planet **Jupiter (गुरु ग्रह, Devguru Brihaspati)**, presiding power over knowledge, stability, honour, wealth, marriage, children, health and spiritual life, and the **Guru as the one who reveals God**. It quotes the Guru Gita ("शिवे रुष्टे गुरुस्त्राता"), Kabir's "गुरु गोविंद दोऊ खड़े" and Bhagavad Gita 7.25 (God veiled by yogamaya), tells the hide-and-seek story of the mother who points to the hidden child, and reads Brihaspati as Vachaspati, Brahmanaspati and Ganapati, the marks of a true Guru. It also introduces the Antar Yog Guru Beej Mantra Sadhana (76,000 chants in 56 days, part of the Navagraha chanting campaign). Main intent: informational ("गुरु ग्रह का महत्व", "significance of Jupiter"), with a practical/remedy edge ("weak Jupiter effects") that must be handled without fear-selling. The opportunity: Jupiter queries are dominated by astrology portals that list effects and paid remedies; this post adds what they lack, the Guru principle behind the planet, backed by cited verses. It also fits the Guru cluster as a supporting post.

## 6. FAQ

**Hindi**
1. **गुरु ग्रह किन बातों के अधिष्ठाता हैं?** शिक्षा, ज्ञान, स्थिरता, मान-सम्मान, धन, सुखी वैवाहिक जीवन, संतान, आरोग्य, धर्म कार्य, मानसिक शांति, आध्यात्मिक प्रगति और सत्संग।
2. **कमज़ोर गुरु के क्या लक्षण बताए गए हैं?** निर्णयों में भ्रम, सही मार्गदर्शन का अभाव, प्रयासों के बाद भी असफलता, अस्थिरता और बिना कारण संघर्ष। पर समाधान भी है।
3. **गुरु को भगवान से पहले नमन क्यों?** क्योंकि परमात्मा सृष्टि रचकर छिप जाते हैं (गीता ७.२५), और गुरु बताते हैं कि वे कहाँ हैं, जैसे कबीर कहते हैं "गोविंद दियो बताय"।
4. **अंतर्योग गुरु बीज मंत्र साधना क्या है?** आचार्य उपेंद्र जी के सान्निध्य में एक ऑनलाइन सामूहिक जप साधना, जिसमें हर साधक घर बैठे 56 दिनों में 76,000 गुरु बीज मंत्रों का जाप करता है।
5. **सच्चे गुरु की पहचान क्या है?** वाचस्पति (वाक् सिद्धि), ब्रह्मणस्पति (नया भाग्य रचने की सामर्थ्य) और गणपति (विवेक बुद्धि) के गुण, और लक्ष्य परमात्मा से मिलन।

**English**
1. **What does Jupiter govern?** Education, knowledge, stability, honour, wealth, a happy marriage, children, health, righteous work, peace of mind, spiritual progress and satsang.
2. **What are the signs of a weak Jupiter?** Confused decisions, no proper guidance, failure despite effort, instability and struggles with no apparent cause. Yet a remedy exists.
3. **Why bow to the Guru before God?** Because God hides Himself, veiled by yogamaya (Gita 7.25), and the Guru shows where He is; as Kabir says, the Guru "showed me Govind".
4. **What is the Antar Yog Guru Beej Mantra Sadhana?** An online collective jap sadhana with Acharya Upendra Ji in which each seeker chants 76,000 Guru beej mantras over 56 days from home.
5. **What marks a true Guru?** The qualities of Vachaspati (perfected speech), Brahmanaspati (power to create a new destiny) and Ganapati (discernment), with union with God as the aim.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Guru Gita line translated as "भगवान / God" and "no other remedy"** (Sanskrit review, minor): the verse names Shiva, and "न कश्चन" means "no one (can protect)". Recommendation: adopt the review's wording ("यदि शिव (परमात्मा) रुष्ट हो जाएँ… कोई भी रक्षा नहीं कर सकता" / "If Shiva (God) is displeased… no one can protect you"). The secondary keyword "शिवे रुष्टे गुरुस्त्राता का अर्थ" should only be pushed once the meaning is corrected, since that query wants an exact translation.
2. **Partial verses not marked; no Guru Gita verse number** (review, minor): mark Guru Gita and Gita 7.25 as "पूर्वार्ध / first half". Do not add a Guru Gita verse number in any snippet or schema; the numbering differs between editions and is unverified. "ग्रंथराज / Granthraj" for the Guru Gita is also unverified; keep it out of titles and meta.
3. **"शास्त्र कहता है / the scriptures tell you" to bow to the Guru first** (review, minor): the source is Kabir's doha, a saint's saying. Recommendation: "संत कहते हैं / the saints tell you". FAQ 3 above already attributes it to Kabir.
4. **"Four forms" heading vs three names** (review, minor): the H2 promises four forms of Brihaspati, but the text and summary give three, with Omkara as a quality of Ganapati. Fix the heading (or the opening line) before using "four forms of Brihaspati" anywhere in SEO copy; section 6 FAQ 5 uses three.
5. **"self-restraint" for ब्रह्मचर्य** (review, minor, English only): use "brahmacharya (continence and conduct rooted in Brahman)". The Ganapati link anchor is unaffected.
6. **Astrological fear-selling risk**: the "weak Jupiter" section lists debt, health problems and blocked marriage. Titles, meta and FAQ name these only as signs and always pair them with the post's hopeful line ("समाधान भी है"); never write "dosh will ruin…" or similar. Recommendation: keep title option 3 ("कमज़ोर गुरु ग्रह के लक्षण…") as an alternative only, not the default.
7. **Outcome promises for the sadhana**: the blog says stability, clarity, success and Guru grace "pour into every participant's life", and lists health among Jupiter's domains. No snippet, meta or schema states a guaranteed or medical result; keep it that way, and do not use "remedy for" + health conditions as keywords.
8. **"Guru beej mantra" without the mantra**: the blog never states the mantra, so a reader searching "guru beej mantra" for the text would not find it. It is kept as a secondary, navigational keyword ("…sadhana") only. If the Foundation wants to rank for the mantra itself, the editor must add the correct mantra from an authoritative source; do not add it for SEO.
9. **Time-bound facts**: the sadhana (the thumbnail gives a start of 13 October 2025), "5 लाख से अधिक / more than 500,000" chants "so far" and the May 2025 campaign start date age quickly. Keep them out of titles and meta (they are), and review the post when the next Guru chanting round is announced.
