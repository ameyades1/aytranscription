# Chapter: The Guru Is Shiva, the Glory of Devaguru Brihaspati

## The Grace of Bhagavan Guru

Without whose grace none of your works can succeed, whose unfavourable position renders even the auspicious yogas of your birth chart fruitless, and by whose blessing even an inauspicious period can turn into an auspicious time: everyone wishes to receive the grace of such a supremely divine Bhagavan Guru.

Describing the greatness of this Bhagavan Guru, the king of scriptures, the Shri Guru Gita, has a famous line:

> **śive ruṣṭe gurustrātā gurau ruṣṭe na kaścana**
> "If Shiva is displeased, the Guru protects; if the Guru is displeased, no one can."
> (Shri Guru Gita)

That is, if God is displeased with you, it is Gurudev who saves you; but if Gurudev is displeased, no other remedy works.

Sant Kabir Das says:

> **guru govinda doū khaṛe, kāke lāgūṁ pāṁya**
> **balihārī guru āpane, govinda diyo batāya**
> "Guru and Govinda both stand before me; whose feet shall I touch first? I bow to my Guru, who showed me Govinda."
> (Sant Kabir Das)

If the Guru and God stand before you together, it is best to bow your head at the Guru's feet, because it is by the Guru's grace that one receives the vision of God.

And the presiding power over all these qualities is the planet Guru, that is, Devaguru Bhagavan Brihaspati (Jupiter).

When Guru is balanced in the birth chart, we receive self-confidence, the grace of the deities, clear guidance in every field, high position, prosperity, family peace, discernment, positivity, deep devotion and depth in spirituality. In today's age, where confusion, unrighteousness, greed and demonic forces are growing, it is the planet Guru that gives life the right direction.

But when Guru is afflicted, we see confusion in decisions, lack of right guidance, failure despite effort, instability, debt, obstacles in education and marriage, health problems, needless struggle in life and auspicious yogas going to waste. But where there is a problem, there is surely a solution too.

## The Guru Is Shiva

Acharya Ji says the Guru is Shiva, and Shiva is the Guru. Shiva means the Supreme. When the Guru and the Supreme stand face to face, scripture says: bow first to the Guru, and to the Supreme afterwards. Because the Supreme creates everything, but after creating the world, hides Himself. The Lord Himself has said:

> **nāhaṁ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yogamāyāsamāvṛtaḥ**
> "I am not manifest to all, veiled by My Yogamaya."
> (Bhagavad Gita 7.25)

That is, I am covered by My Yogamaya, and so I do not appear before everyone. And the Guru, appearing before all, tells us where the Supreme is hiding.

## The Game of Hide and Seek

All children play hide and seek. Among them there is one little child whom we call the "kachcha biscuit". For a long time he cannot find anyone. The other children enjoy this, but the mother feels bad. Then the mother tells him, "He is hiding there, there, behind the curtain." So he goes and catches him. Then the other children shout, "Why did you tell? We had hidden so well!"

Exactly the same is true of the Supreme. The Supreme creates all this and then hides, and we keep searching and searching. Bhagavan Guru does not like this. Then Guru Mauli tells us, "Arey, He is there, look there." This is exactly the Guru's work.

> "The Supreme creates all this and later hides, and we keep searching. And Bhagavan Guru does not like that."

## The Purpose of a True Guru in Life

So prayer to the Guru, having a Guru in life, is very important. Its purpose is union with the Supreme. For great achievements in the world you will find business gurus, love gurus, law gurus, experts in every field. We are not speaking of those gurus here. This Guru is Bhagavan, the Guru of the gods.

## Brihaspati, Vachaspati, Brahmanaspati, Ganapati

Brihaspati is Vachaspati, He is Brahmanaspati, and He is Ganapati. From this we understand what a Guru should be.

**Vachaspati** means he should have complete mastery over speech; he should have vak siddhi, the power of perfected speech. Such a Guru is called shrotriya brahmanishtha.

**Brahmanaspati** means he should have the capacity to create something new. And creation here means your whole new destiny, a new life.

**Ganapati** means that, finally, he should also be the lord of the ganas. All the qualities of Ganapati, discerning intellect, the practice of brahmacharya, the subtle intellect that recognizes joy and sorrow, should be in him. And ultimately Ganapati is the very form of Omkara.

The one in whom all these qualities are united is called Brihaspati.
