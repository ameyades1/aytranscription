# The Guru Is Shiva: The Secret of Devguru Brihaspati's Grace

Some blessings are so essential that no undertaking succeeds without them. When they are withdrawn, even the auspicious combinations in a birth chart bear no fruit. When they are given, even an unfavourable period turns into a fortunate one.

This is the grace of **Lord Guru**. Let us understand why the tradition places the Guru even before God.

## Who Saves You When God Is Displeased?

The Shri Guru Gita, honoured as **Granthraj** (the king of scriptures), contains a famous line:

> **śive ruṣṭe gurus trātā gurau ruṣṭe na kaścana**
> "If God is displeased, the Guru protects you. But if the Guru is displeased, no other remedy can help."
> (Shri Guru Gita)

Sant Kabir Das says the same:

> **guru govinda doū khaṛe, kāke lāgūṁ pāṁya**
> **balihārī guru āpane, govinda diyo batāya**
> "If the Guru and God both stand before you, bow first at the Guru's feet, for it is by the Guru's grace that you see God."
> (Sant Kabir Das)

## Jupiter: The Power That Gives Life Meaning

Education, knowledge, stability, honour, wealth, a happy marriage, children, good health, righteous work, peace of mind, spiritual progress and satsang: these are what make a life meaningful.

All of them are governed by Jupiter, known in Vedic astrology as Guru, who is Devguru Brihaspati, the teacher of the gods. He is their **adhishthata** (presiding power).

When Jupiter is well balanced in your chart, it brings self-confidence, the blessings of the gods, clear guidance, high position, prosperity, peace at home, **viveka** (discernment), a positive outlook, sincere devotion and depth in spiritual life.

Today, as confusion, unrighteousness, greed and demonic forces grow stronger, Jupiter is the planet that sets life on the right course.

## When Jupiter Is Weak

An afflicted Jupiter clouds your decisions and leaves you without proper guidance, so that failure follows even sincere effort. Instability, debt, obstacles in education and marriage, health problems and struggles with no apparent cause begin to appear. Even auspicious combinations in the chart go to waste.

*Have you ever felt that you had everything, yet could not see which way to go?*

Wherever there is a problem, though, a solution is sure to exist.

This online **sadhana** (spiritual practice), held in the presence of Acharya Upendra Ji, a jivanmukta (liberated while living) spiritual Sadguru and the founder of the Antar Yog Foundation, is a way to awaken the Guru principle in your life.

## Why Does God Hide?

Acharya Ji says that the Guru is Shiva, and Shiva is the Guru.

Here, Shiva means God. When the Guru and God stand face to face, the scriptures tell you to bow to the Guru first. Why? Because God creates the world and then hides Himself in it.

> **nāhaṁ prakāśaḥ sarvasya yogamāyā-samāvṛtaḥ**
> "Veiled by my **yogamaya** (divine power of concealment), I am not revealed to all."
> (Bhagavad Gita 7.25)

The Guru, on the other hand, appears before you and shows you where God is hiding.

## Hide-and-Seek and a Mother's Hint

Acharya Ji gives a lovely example. Children are playing hide-and-seek, and among them is a little one, the "kaccha biscuit" (the youngest child, let into the game though no one takes them seriously). For a long time this child cannot find anyone.

The other children enjoy it, but the mother feels sorry for the little one. She whispers, "There, behind the curtain." The child runs over and catches the one hiding, and the rest protest, "Why did you tell? We were hidden so well!"

Acharya Ji explains that God, too, creates all of this and then hides, while we keep searching. The Guru does not like to see us struggle.

So the Guru Mauli (the Guru as a loving mother) says, "Look, He is right there." This is the Guru's work.

*Are you searching for God too, waiting for someone to point the way?*

## Which Guru?

Having a Guru in life matters deeply, and the purpose is **union with God**.

For worldly success you will find all kinds of gurus: corporate gurus, love gurus, legal gurus, experts in every field. Acharya Ji makes it clear that he is not speaking of them. This is Lord Guru, the teacher of the gods.

## Four Forms of Brihaspati, Four Marks of the Guru

Brihaspati is also Vachaspati, Brahmanaspati and Ganapati. These forms reveal what a true Guru is like:

- **Vachaspati:** mastery over speech, known as **vak siddhi** (the power of perfected speech). Such a Guru is called **shrotriya brahmanishtha** (learned in the scriptures and established in Brahman).
- **Brahmanaspati:** the power to create something new. Here, creation means a new destiny and a new life for you.
- **Ganapati:** the lord of the ganas (divine attendants), who has discernment, self-restraint and the subtle intelligence to see through pleasure and pain.
- **Omkara:** ultimately, Ganapati is the very form of Om, the primordial sound.

The one in whom all of these qualities come together is called Brihaspati.

## Key Takeaways

- The Guru Gita teaches that the Guru protects you when God is displeased, but nothing helps when the Guru is displeased.
- Devguru Brihaspati presides over knowledge, stability, honour, prosperity and spiritual life.
- An afflicted Jupiter brings confusion, obstacles and needless struggle, yet a remedy exists.
- The Antar Yog Guru Beej Mantra Sadhana is a collective practice of 76,000 Guru beej mantras in 56 days.
- God creates the world and hides, and the Guru, like a mother, shows you where He is.
- A true Guru carries the qualities of Vachaspati, Brahmanaspati and Ganapati, and aims for union with God.
