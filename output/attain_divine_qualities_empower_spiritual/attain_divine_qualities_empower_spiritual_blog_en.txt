# True Lakshmi Worship: The Art of Awakening Divine Wealth

Every year we perform Lakshmi puja. We light lamps, we pray, and in our hearts we wish for wealth.

But have you ever wondered what Lakshmi really is? The qualities Shri Krishna calls **daivī sampatti** (divine wealth) in the sixteenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gita are the true Lakshmi. And in those three verses, money is not mentioned anywhere.

## How Do You Know Your Lakshmi Worship Is Working?

The test is simple. To the extent these qualities of divine wealth have entered me, to that extent my Lakshmi worship is being done properly.

> **abhayaṁ sattva-saṁśuddhir jñāna-yoga-vyavasthitiḥ**
> **dānaṁ damaś ca yajñaś ca svādhyāyas tapa ārjavam**
> "Fearlessness, purity of heart, steadfastness in the yoga of knowledge, charity, self-control, sacrifice, study of scripture, austerity and straightforwardness."
> (Bhagavad Gita 16.1)

## Where Does Fear Come From?

The first quality is **abhaya**, the complete absence of fear.

Fear comes only when there is an "other". An object, a person, a place or a job. The moment we say "my job", there are two: I and the job. If the job goes, there is fear.

When the feeling of **advaita** (non-duality) comes, that everything is God, that all this is my own creation, the mind says: if this has gone, it has gone; another will come. But this does not mean being careless.

*What is the "other" in your life whose loss you fear the most?*

## More Jewels of the First Verse

- Purity of the inner being and constant firm steadiness in the yoga of meditation
- **Sattvic charity**, for charity is of three kinds
- **Restraint of the senses**, that is, control over the senses
- Worship of God, the gods and the gurus, and noble acts such as the agnihotra
- Study and teaching of the scriptures: both reading oneself and teaching others
- Singing the name and qualities of God
- Bearing hardship to fulfil one's own dharma
- Simplicity of body, senses and inner being

This is only the first verse. And this is Lakshmi.

> **ahiṁsā satyam akrodhas tyāgaḥ śāntir apaiśunam**
> **dayā bhūteṣv aloluptvaṁ mārdavaṁ hrīr acāpalam**
> "Non-violence, truthfulness, freedom from anger, renunciation, peace, absence of slander, compassion for all beings, freedom from craving, gentleness, modesty and steadiness."
> (Bhagavad Gita 16.2)

## Does Non-Violence Mean Never Fighting?

No. Not causing pain to anyone through mind, speech or body is **ahiṁsā** (non-violence), but sāma, dāma and daṇḍa (persuasion, inducement and punishment) include punishment too. The condition is that even the punishment must be for the other's good.

Mother Mahishasuramardini killed Mahishasura for his liberation. Shri Krishna killed Kamsa for his liberation. There must be no hatred in the mind, that is all.

## The Art of Speaking Is Also Lakshmi

Speak the truth, speak pleasantly, speak what is beneficial: **satyaṁ vada, priyaṁ vada, hitaṁ vada**.

Knowledge is given by Saraswati. But when, how and in what manner to speak, and sustaining it continuously, this art comes from Lakshmi.

## How to Respond Without Anger?

Not being angry even with one who harms you. But this does not mean he should not be taught a lesson. One can respond with a calm mind, after planning, judging on merit and weighing one's own strength.

Chandragupta was taught the same: do not fight until you have the strength. First become a warrior, then go and fight. Acharya Ji says that one must not act in anger today; one must plan.

The Lord says, **kāma eṣa krodha eṣa rajo-guṇa-samudbhavaḥ** (Bhagavad Gita 3.37), "it is desire, it is anger, born of rajas". When rajas grows, desire grows. If the desire is not fulfilled, anger; if it is fulfilled, ego.

Rajas is itself a form of Lakshmi, but it needs the support of Saraswati. Only then does it become merit, only then does it become divine wealth.

## Why Is a Mother's Anger Different?

This does not mean you should never be angry with a child. When the time comes, one has to scold too. But in that scolding there is love, the feeling "may he improve". Even when a mother hits her child, she does not become his enemy.

## The Difference Between Slander and Constructive Criticism

Do not **slander** anyone. But constructive criticism can be offered, in which the other person's good lies.

The daughter-in-law of the house does not work? Perhaps she comes from a different background, raised with pampering. If you keep saying "your mother gave you no values" and reduce her personality to zero, that is like committing brahmahatyā on her. On the contrary, you will incur sin. To show the importance of work, show it by doing it yourself; that is what works.

## Is Your Compassion Truly Selfless?

**Compassion without a motive** is needed towards all beings. Feeding pigeons so your debt is cleared, feeding a cow or a dog so Saturn is pacified, that is a deal, not compassion.

*The next time you give, pause for a moment and ask: is there an expectation hidden behind it?*

Having no attachment even while in contact with sense objects, and **gentleness**, are also jewels of this verse.

## Modesty: Discernment With Two Eyes

There should be shame at conduct that goes against both society and scripture.

If on some beach people are shamelessly doing anything, and you say "everyone was doing it, so I did too", there shame before scripture should arise. And if somewhere worship is going on but behind it there is DJ music and disorder, there shame before society. The tendency to get up and leave such a place is also divine wealth.

## Hours Lost to the Screen

**Absence of pointless activity** is also divine wealth. Unnecessary serials, gossip, watching short videos for hours, all this is pointless activity, unless some knowledge is coming from it.

*How many hours did you spend today that taught you nothing and did no one any good?*

> **tejaḥ kṣamā dhṛtiḥ śaucam adroho nāti-mānitā**
> **bhavanti sampadaṁ daivīm abhijātasya bhārata**
> "Radiance, forgiveness, fortitude, purity, absence of enmity and absence of pride: these, O Bharata, belong to one born with divine wealth."
> (Bhagavad Gita 16.3)

## Radiance: Words That Change Lives

**Tejas** (radiance) is that power of great souls by whose influence even people attached to the senses and of low nature give up injustice and engage in noble actions.

Valmiki was changed by one sentence. When Chanakya spoke, the whole village listened. After the diet camp, many people gave up non-vegetarian food; after the brahmacharya camp, many young men gave up promiscuity. That is tejas.

This tejas comes from the practice of **brahmacharya**. First tejas, then the power of intellect.

## Who Is a Steadfast Person?

**Fortitude** means holding on to something for a very long time. "Dhī" means intellect. One whose intellect is steady is a dhīra, a steadfast person. One who held on with fortitude and reached samādhi is a dhīra.

## Struggle Without Enmity

- **Śauca**: external purity is also necessary
- **Adroha**: no enmity towards anyone, yet using persuasion, inducement and punishment for one's rightful due
- **Amānitā**: not the pride of "I am worthy of worship", but "God is working through me"

## This Is Mahalakshmi

In simple language, the name of these three verses is **Mahalakshmi**.

In all our years of worship, did we ask for this? And where is money mentioned in them? Lakshmi is Shri Krishna's own consort, he is Vishnu himself, and it is he who is telling this to Arjuna.

Next the Lord says that hypocrisy, arrogance, pride, anger, harshness and ignorance are the marks of **demonic wealth**.

> **daivī sampad vimokṣāya nibandhāyāsurī matā**
> **mā śucaḥ sampadaṁ daivīm abhijāto 'si pāṇḍava**
> "Divine wealth is held to lead to liberation and demonic wealth to bondage. Do not grieve, O Pandava, for you are born with divine wealth."
> (Bhagavad Gita 16.5)

Divine wealth liberates, demonic wealth binds. Therefore, O Arjuna, do not grieve; you are born with divine wealth.

## It Is Already Within You

If you are a seeker, these qualities are somewhere within you. You only need to go to the guru and remove the veil over them.

One who has no inclination to give is hard to change. But the wish to give must have awakened in you at some time, which is why you have reached Lakshmi worship today. Acharya Ji explains that outside, all three kinds of people are doing Lakshmi worship, but not everyone's worship bears fruit.

## Key Takeaways

- The divine wealth of the Gita's sixteenth chapter is the true Mahalakshmi, and money is not mentioned in it.
- Fearlessness comes from non-duality, but it does not mean carelessness.
- Non-violence and freedom from anger do not mean weakness; respond without hatred, with a calm mind and a plan.
- Saraswati gives knowledge, but the art of speaking is Lakshmi.
- If you criticise, let it be constructive; reducing someone's personality to zero is a sin.
- Compassion must be without motive, not a deal.
- Pointless activity, such as watching short videos for hours, diminishes divine wealth.
- Radiance comes from brahmacharya; fortitude is the name of a steady intellect.
- These qualities are already within the seeker; go to the guru to bring them out.
