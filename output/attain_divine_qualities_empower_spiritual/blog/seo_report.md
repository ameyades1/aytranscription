## 1. Summary

The talk recasts Lakshmi Puja: the true Lakshmi is the **daivi sampatti** (divine wealth) that Shri Krishna lists in Bhagavad Gita 16.1-3, and those verses never mention money. It walks through the qualities with practical examples: fearlessness that comes from non-duality (without becoming careless), ahimsa that still allows firm action without hatred, anger replaced by calm planning, the art of speech as Lakshmi, constructive criticism instead of slander, compassion without a motive, modesty before both society and scripture, freedom from pointless activity such as hours of short videos, tejas from brahmacharya, and dhriti as a steady intellect. It ends with Gita 16.5 (divine wealth liberates, demonic wealth binds) and the reassurance that a seeker already has these qualities, which the Guru helps uncover. The main intent is **informational and seasonal**: "लक्ष्मी पूजन का असली अर्थ" and "true meaning of Lakshmi Puja" peak around Diwali, while "दैवी संपत्ति के गुण", "daivi sampatti" and "divine qualities Bhagavad Gita chapter 16" are evergreen study queries. Ranking pages for Lakshmi Puja are mostly vidhi and muhurat lists; Gita 16 pages are verse-by-verse commentaries. This post's distinct angle joins the two: the Gita's list as the real Lakshmi Puja, explained through modern situations (jobs, screens, family conflict). Diwali/Lakshmi Puja 2026 falls in early November (check the exact date in the panchang), so the post is already inside the 4-6 week window and should go live now.

## 6. FAQ

1. **असली लक्ष्मी पूजन क्या है? / What is the true Lakshmi Puja?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार गीता के सोलहवें अध्याय की दैवी संपत्ति ही सच्ची लक्ष्मी है; ये गुण भीतर जितने उतरें, उतना ही लक्ष्मी पूजन ठीक से हो रहा है। / According to Acharya Ji, the divine wealth of the Gita's sixteenth chapter is the true Lakshmi; your Lakshmi Puja succeeds to the extent these qualities take root in you.
2. **दैवी संपत्ति के गुण कौन-से हैं? / What are the qualities of divine wealth?**
   गीता १६.१-३ में: अभय, अंतःकरण की शुद्धि, ज्ञान और योग में स्थिति, दान, दम, यज्ञ, स्वाध्याय, तप, सरलता, अहिंसा, सत्य, अक्रोध, त्याग, शांति, अपैशुन, दया, अलोलुपता, मार्दव, लज्जा, अचापल, तेज, क्षमा, धृति, शौच, अद्रोह और नातिमानिता। / In Gita 16.1-3: fearlessness, purity of heart, steadiness in knowledge and yoga, charity, sense control, sacrifice, study, austerity, straightforwardness, non-violence, truthfulness, freedom from anger, renunciation, peace, refraining from slander, compassion, freedom from greed, gentleness, modesty, steadiness, radiance, forgiveness, fortitude, purity, freedom from malice and absence of excessive pride.
3. **क्या अहिंसा का अर्थ कभी न लड़ना है? / Does ahimsa mean never fighting?**
   नहीं। मन, वाणी और शरीर से किसी को कष्ट न देना अहिंसा है, पर दंड भी दिया जा सकता है, यदि उसमें सामने वाले का भला हो और मन में द्वेष न हो। / No. Ahimsa means causing no pain through thought, word or deed, yet punishment has its place when it serves the other person's good and the heart holds no hatred.
4. **दैवी और आसुरी संपदा में क्या अंतर है? / What is the difference between divine and demonic wealth?**
   गीता १६.४ दंभ, घमंड, अभिमान, क्रोध, कठोरता और अज्ञान को आसुरी संपदा कहती है, और १६.५ के अनुसार दैवी संपदा मुक्ति देती है, आसुरी संपदा बाँधती है। / Gita 16.4 names hypocrisy, arrogance, pride, anger, harshness and ignorance as demonic wealth, and 16.5 says divine wealth leads to liberation while demonic wealth binds.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **अमानिता vs नातिमानिता (Sanskrit review issue 1, Minor, not yet applied).** The verse word in Gita 16.3 is नातिमानिता; अमानिता is from 13.7. Apply the correction before publishing. The FAQ uses the correct verse word.
2. **"Sama, dana, danda" as "the classical means" (review issue 2, Minor).** The classical upayas are four (sama, dana, bheda, danda). Apply the English correction. No SEO text uses this line.
3. **"These three verses are called Mahalakshmi" (review issue 3, Minor).** This is Acharya Ji's reading, not a traditional name. Apply the correction. Titles, metas, snippet and FAQ attribute "divine wealth is the true Lakshmi" to Acharya Ji ("according to", "बताते हैं"); never write titles such as "The Gita says Lakshmi is…" or "Mahalakshmi in the Gita".
4. **Dhriti and "dhi" (review issue 4, Minor).** A teaching nirukti, not the verse word's grammar. Not used in any SEO text; apply the correction.
5. **Hindi "वे स्वयं विष्णु हैं" ambiguity (review issue 5, Minor).** Apply the correction. Not used in SEO text.
6. **IAST ṁ vs ṃ and hyphenated compounds (review issue 6, Minor).** Style fix; no SEO effect.
7. **संपत्ति / संपदा, sampatti / sampada (review issue 7, Minor).** The SEO text uses "दैवी संपत्ति" / "daivi sampatti" (the blog's main form, and the commoner search form) and "दैवी और आसुरी संपदा" as a secondary keyword (the Gita Press form). If the editor standardises the blog on one form, keep both forms as keywords, since people search both.
8. **"सत्यं वद, प्रियं वद, हितं वद" (review: could not verify).** Keep it as a saying; never cite a scripture for it in a snippet, FAQ or social card.
9. **No money or prosperity promise.** Lakshmi Puja queries invite "how to attract wealth" framing. The post's teaching is that the true Lakshmi is not money; titles and metas must not promise financial gain from these qualities or from worship.
10. **Folk remedies (Shani, pigeons).** The talk cites feeding animals to appease Shani as an example of a transaction, not as advice. Don't target remedy queries ("शनि उपाय") with this post.
11. **Brahmacharya link.** The blog's English glosses brahmacharya as "celibacy and mastery over one's energies"; the linked talk should be read for its own nuance. The anchor ("after the Brahmacharya Shibir, many young men gave up promiscuity") describes the talk's example, not a health claim.

Category note: `gallery_topics.txt` places the talk under `dharma`, copied as is. Diwali is the occasion, not the subject, so it is a tag, not the `festivals` category; Lakshmi is a tag rather than `deities`, because the post is about Gita 16 qualities more than about the goddess.
