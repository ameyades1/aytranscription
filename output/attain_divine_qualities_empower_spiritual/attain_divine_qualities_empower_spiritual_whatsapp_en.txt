*What is the true Lakshmi we worship each Diwali?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that the real Lakshmi is the divine wealth Shri Krishna describes in Gita chapter 16: fearlessness, purity, charity, truthfulness and gentleness. Those verses never mention money.
