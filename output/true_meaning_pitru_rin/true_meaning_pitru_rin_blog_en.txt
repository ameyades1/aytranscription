# The True Meaning of Pitru Rin: Service Before Shraddha

As Pitru Paksha arrives, many questions arise in the mind. Who are the Pitars? What is the real meaning of **Shraddha** (a rite performed with reverence for the ancestors) and **Tarpan** (satisfying the ancestors through offerings of water)?

A dialogue took place with Brahmarshi Acharya Upendra Ji on these very questions. And one question was the deepest of all: those who do not respect their elders while they are alive, but perform Shraddha with great ceremony after they pass away, do they receive any benefit from it?

## Why Don't We Value What Is Near?

Acharya Ji says this is the glory of Kali Yuga. As long as a thing or a person is with us, it has no importance. When it goes away, we search for it.

The same happens with our parents and ancestors. We are so surrounded by our **kamanas** (desires) that we cannot give them as much respect while they are with us.

They want to meet us with love, to share a few words. But we have no time. The job, travel, looking after the children, the worry about money. Then one day they are suddenly gone.

Then loneliness fills the mind, sometimes fear. Some perform Shraddha as a responsibility, some out of fear.

*Is there someone in your life too who just wants to share a few words with you, and you keep saying "later"?*

## When Does One Become a Pitar?

Here Acharya Ji shares a profound point. The scriptures say that we are not meant to enter the **Pitar state** (pitar yoni) at all. That happens only when someone leaves the body in a state of happiness and bliss.

Elders may be ill, that is natural. But they become sorrowful when the people of the home do not give them happiness and do not pay attention to them. Acharya Ji says that no one should depart in such a neglected state, because that is when they become Pitars.

## Even If You Wake Late, It Is Morning

So is there no path for the one who woke up late? Acharya Ji says: **when you wake, it is morning.**

If someone's eyes opened later, there is nothing wrong in their performing Shraddha later. The scriptures say that they can still reform. Through charity, good deeds and service, they can still become the **kul-deepak** (lamp of the family). Just as Valya the bandit became Valmiki, such a transformation is possible.

But two things must be remembered:

- Shraddha should not be **for show**, not in the spirit of "that's it, my responsibility is over."
- Shraddha should not be done **out of fear**.

## But Why Delay at All?

The biggest question is this: why should we land in a "better late than never" situation at all? We should live an ideal life while they are alive. The scriptures are proclaiming it loudly:

> **mātṛdevo bhava, pitṛdevo bhava, ācāryadevo bhava**
> "Treat your mother as God. Treat your father as God. Treat your teacher as God."
> (Taittiriya Upanishad, Shikshavalli 1.11.2)

That is, regard your mother, father and teacher as divine.

Even this body was given by our parents. It was they who sent us to school, carried us on their shoulders, and cared for us in everything from food to every illness.

## Be Not Only the Staff, but the Joy

In old age, they have just one expectation: that their children become their staff (lathi). But Acharya Ji goes further than this: children should not become only the staff, they should give them joy as well.

Spend time with them. They have made so many sacrifices for us. **This is Pitru Rin, and to repay it we must give at least that much time.**

*How much time do you give your parents or elders today, only as a responsibility, or as joy?*

## A Home That Never Needs Shraddha

For the family that lives this way, the scriptures say there will be no need for Shraddha in that home, because the samskaras (values) there were so good.

Such families need only do **gau-seva** (service of cows) for the sake of their ancestors, give charity to Brahmins, or read a little scripture at home, and their ancestors will remain forever liberated and keep blessing the whole family.

## The Law of Sadhana, Like Science

Acharya Ji gives a beautiful example. Science says: press the button and the fan or light will come on, whether a king presses it or a poor man, a renunciate or a thief, an old person or a young one.

In the same way, the **phalashruti** (fruit of sadhana) of the sadhanas of Antar Yog comes swiftly, whether it is Ganesh Vidya, Shri Vidya, Saraswati Diksha, the sadhana of Pitru-mukti and healing, or Shiva sadhana. Most people receive this fruit right here and now, as direct experience.

## Key Takeaways

- Recognise the value of what is near while it is still with you; after it is gone, only regret remains.
- Those who leave the body in a sorrowful, neglected state become Pitars.
- Even if you wake late, it is morning: Shraddha performed later is not wrong, but it should not be for show or out of fear.
- Matridevo bhava, Pitridevo bhava: serving your parents while they are alive is the true repayment of Pitru Rin.
- Be not only the staff of their old age, but also their joy.
- In a home where elders find happiness, Shraddha is never needed; the ancestors remain liberated and give their blessings.
