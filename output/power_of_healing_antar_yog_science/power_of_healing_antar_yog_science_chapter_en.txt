# Chapter: The Healing Power of Faith

## The Science of Healing: Placebo and Nocebo

Whatever the healing may be, in the language of healing it is called the **placebo effect**. Those who are doctors will understand well what I am saying. It has two sides: one is the placebo effect and the other is the **nocebo effect**.

## The Story of the Village Doctor

Understand what placebo means. A doctor we know once went to a village, and he was telling a story about the people there. He gave a patient medicine for some small illness, probably a few tablets. At once there was an uproar in the whole village: some bad doctor has come, he has come to kill us, he has come to cause trouble.

Why? Because he did not give an injection! What was their understanding? The medicine may be perfectly correct, but if no injection is given, the doctor himself is bad.

Then his senior doctor said, "What is there to worry about in this?" He came up with a very good idea. He gave injections of distilled water. He must have given them to everyone, as if a fountain were running. By doing this, the doctor became a good doctor, and the patient also became well.

Do you know why this happened? Because of his faith. It was only distilled water, and the patient did not know it. "Not medicine; if I get an injection, I will be well." That was his belief.

## Faith Is What Heals

> "Belief heals."

Remember this much: **your belief in the doctor, belief in yourself and belief in the medicine.** You need not even know the name of the medicine, only the feeling, "What I am taking is going to make me well." If this feeling is missing, there is no cure. Most often, it is the patient who gives up hope.

That is why this science of self-confidence is taught to doctors: you should develop the placebo effect in your patients. Isn't it so? Doctors, tell me, yes or no? It is part of your medical ethics that you have to build this faith. An allopathic doctor will have to give allopathic medicine, but that placebo effect must arise within the patient himself.

If you yourself do not believe, the effect will never come. "I am taking the medicine because you are telling me to." Such a feeling will not have much effect. On the contrary, the poisonous effect will come at once: the negative effect will happen, not the positive one.

## Nocebo: The Poison of Disbelief

Nocebo means that I gave you even the best medicine, but you said, "This cannot work," so the medicine will not work. Gradually its effect will keep diminishing until it is gone. This is called the nocebo effect. There is nocebo and there is placebo.

## Shraddha: A Complete Science

In our own language this is called **self-confidence**, your faith, your **shraddha**. This is a complete science.

Someone says, "I take four opinions, I ask four doctors." This means he has no faith in the first one. Someone says, "I act after asking four gurus." This too means he has no faith in the first one.

> "Choose the first one so well that you can surrender to him completely."

## The Age of the Family Doctor

In earlier times there used to be family doctors. The doctor's real name was hardly ever used. Doctor Sharma, Doctor Verma, Doctor Kulkarni: nothing like that. "Doctor Kaka has come, Doctor Mama has come, Doctor Chacha has come." They were like members of the family.

They would come home, chat, "So, how are things going, what is going on," and in the middle of the talk they must even have popped two tablets into your mouth, "Here, take this, you are fine." Why did this work? Because the feeling was, "He has given it, that means I am cured."

I am talking about forty or fifty years ago, not about something very old. People of that generation can recall what the family doctor used to be. He would come home; there was a Doctor Kaka, a Doctor Mama, wasn't there? There was, wasn't there?

## The Problem Today

And what is it today? The patient searches the internet and looks everything up himself, and tries to do half of his treatment on his own. There is no faith in anyone. That is the problem. That is why the placebo effect is decreasing.
