# Chapter: The Power of Speech, Woman, Saraswati and Mahakali

## Women and Leadership

If women in this country are to rule in the times ahead, what will they need to learn? That is what I am telling you today. What qualities does it take to become Mahakali? The practice is of Saraswati, but how does it connect to Kali? That is what we need to understand. Because the next point is the same: vak, that is, speech.

## Power Requires Speech

If you want power, your speech must be good. Have you ever seen a leader who does not speak at all? Or whose voice is so weak that he cannot talk and nobody listens to him? Does such a leader ever exist? By some destiny there may be an exception somewhere, but we are not discussing exceptions. I have not seen one yet.

If someone's voice is gone, if his throat gives out in the middle of a speech, people laugh at the rest of it. Then they say, "Oh, what will this one do?" That is the effect a voice has. So even if people have not seen his work, his speech must be strong. That will at least bring votes. But only if he truly works as well will he last. We do not want only speeches.

## Fiercest and Gentlest

Now tell me, who can give a better speech, a man or a woman? A harsh voice belongs to men. In a male-dominated society this is what we have seen: ministers all babbling on. But who can actually speak more? **Fiercest and gentlest**: this is the description given of woman. The fierce form she shows at home, she must also know how to show outside. This does not mean be harsh outside.

## The Glories of Women in the Gita

Today I wanted to show that very verse, the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita, chapter ten, verse thirty-four. This is the chapter of Vibhuti Yoga. In it Shri Krishna himself says that in women He comes as speech.

> **mṛtyuḥ sarva-haraś cāham udbhavaś ca bhaviṣyatām**
> **kīrtiḥ śrīr vāk ca nārīṇāṁ smṛtir medhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā**
> (Bhagavad Gita 10.34)

"I am all-devouring death and the origin of all that is yet to be; and among women I am fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forgiveness."

Of these, **fame, prosperity and speech** belong to outward activity. Prosperity shows outside, fame is outside, speech is outside. **Memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forgiveness** are within; they all come from inside. Among them, Shri is called the daughter of Bhrigu, Vak the daughter of Brahma, and Kirti, Smriti, Medha, Dhriti and Kshama the daughters of Daksha Prajapati.

## The Primacy of Saraswati in Woman

Memory is named among women: that is a quality of Saraswati. Intelligence is in women too: again, the role of Saraswati. Dhriti, the power to hold steady: that too belongs to Saraswati. Only one who has intelligence and discernment can forgive: that too is the work of Saraswati. Yes, for that one also needs heart and courage; there Lakshmi and Kali become supporting. But who is the main one? Saraswati.

In memory Saraswati is direct, in forgiveness too there is Saraswati, and then there is vak, speech. So this much of Saraswati has been given to women. **Shri** is Lakshmi. And **fame** belongs only to one who has Mahakali, because only tales of valour are heard. All three are described, but Saraswati is described the most. If it is Saraswati, which quality should be present? **Sattva guna**. So women should have the most sattva guna. That is the direct answer.

## What Is Vak?

Among these, our question is vak. I said that a leader also has vak, and from it comes Mahakali. But what is vak? In truth, vak is Saraswati's very name. She is called both the daughter of Brahma and the wife of Brahma. How can she be both? She is both. Because these two characters are in any case forms imagined for the sake of seekers, and that imagined form is within ourselves.

> **sādhakānāṁ hitārthāya brahmaṇo rūpa-kalpanā**

## The Secret of the Story of Brahma and Saraswati

The story says that when Brahma saw Saraswati, he had an emission of seed, and she at once became his wife. People will laugh at this. But the emission is no physical act at all, because there is no man with four faces. It is the Brahma-nature within me: the four Vedas, the four of everything; when I begin to know and rise above the four varnas, that is what is being spoken of. The emission means radiance arising, radiance coming forth.

On seeing whom did it happen? Brahma means the creator. The making of destiny happens on seeing knowledge. And in one place knowledge is also born of him, because only when one has to create something does knowledge come. When do we start to think? They say necessity is the mother of invention. Invention needs intelligence, but first the necessity must come. So for the one who has to create, she is both daughter and wife.

People of other religions laugh at this: "What is this, feeling so on seeing a daughter?" And we too start thinking the same way. Look, the intelligence of Ganapati must come within us. From today, do not make that mistake.

## Vak and Mahakali: The Fire of Leadership

Vak is in women, which means ideal leaders too come from there, because the quality of a leader is Mahakali. Whoever has vak within has the fire of speech. If you want tender, loving talk, to whom do you go? To Lakshmi. Vishnu's work is different; Vishnu with the Sudarshan Chakra is one thing, the Narayana state is another. Are you understanding these different concepts?

## The Ability to Punish

If Mahakali is to be awakened, that is, if you want power, the next quality is: you must know how to punish. The one who can punish the most is the one whose kingdom expands.
