*What does freedom from old age and death really mean?*

Drawing on Bhagavad Gita 7.29, Acharya Upendra Ji explains that the body may age, but if the mind grows old, you will be born again and again.

💭 _Does the thought "I'm too old for this" come from your body or your mind?_
