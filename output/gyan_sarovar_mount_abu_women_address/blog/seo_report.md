## 1. Summary

A short (6.5-minute video) address by Acharya Upendra Ji to more than 500 women at the Brahma Kumaris' conference "अध्यात्म द्वारा महिला सशक्तिकरण" at Gyan Sarovar, Mount Abu (3 July 2025). Its hook is a direct answer to a very common question, "is a woman equal to a man?": not equal, at least a thousand times more powerful. It supports this with Durga Saptashati 5.13 (atisaumyā atiraudrā), Ardhanarishvara as the balance India lacks, a woman's natural intuition, dhriti, smriti and medha, and the line "understand the mother to understand the Guru, and the Guru as a mother to understand Bharat Mata". The main intent is informational with a strong emotional layer (women looking for confidence and dignity, not a gender-war argument). The opportunity is the equality question itself, answered from scripture rather than from opinion pieces; a secondary navigational opportunity is the Mount Abu / Brahma Kumaris event. The same event is covered at length in `future_lies_in_hands_message_to_women`, so this post must target the equality question and the event, not the Gita 10.34 qualities (see section 7).

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **"न दैन्यं न पलायनम्" attributed to the Mahabharata tradition** (Sanskrit review, Major). It is the second half of a popular subhashita about Arjuna ("अर्जुनस्य प्रतिज्ञे द्वे..."), with no known Gita Press Mahabharata locus. Recommendation: never use it in titles, snippets or schema as a Mahabharata quote; the editor should apply the review's correction.
2. **Gloss inside the quoted translation of "न दैन्यं न पलायनम्"** (review, Minor): "कर्तव्य के पथ पर..." / "Keep moving forward..." is explanation, not the Sanskrit; दैन्य is better "meekness" than "helplessness". Editor's decision.
3. **Durga Saptashati 5.13 is quoted as a half verse without saying so** (review, Minor). The reference is correct; mark it "पूर्वार्ध / first half". The FAQ and direct answer cite the verse only for what it says.
4. **धृति defined as "retention"** (review, Minor): the scriptural meaning is steadfastness (Gita 18.33-35); "holding on to grievances" is Acharya Ji's playful application. Do not put "dhriti means retention" in a snippet or FAQ answer; the FAQ lists the powers without defining dhriti.
5. **Hindi takeaway "गुरु मां को समझे बिना"** can be read as "Guru Maa" (review, Minor). FAQ 5 uses the clear wording "गुरु को मां के रूप में समझे बिना"; the editor should fix the takeaway.
6. **"हजार गुना शक्तिशाली" / "a thousand times stronger"** is Acharya Ji's emphatic teaching, not a measurable claim. Titles and snippets attribute it to him ("आचार्य जी के अनुसार" / "According to Acharya Ji") and avoid framing it as a put-down of men or a rejection of equal rights. The recommended titles lead with the question rather than the claim for that reason.
7. **Event details** (3 July 2025, Mount Abu, 500+ women, conference title, the introducer's words, service since 2005) come only from the talk and cannot be checked against another source. The video title confirms Gyan Sarovar, Mount Abu and 500 women; the date and the rest are kept as stated and not expanded.
8. **Spelling and IAST** (review, Minor): "निस्वार्थ" → "निःस्वार्थ"; English "dainyaṁ" → "dainyaṃ" and "palayana" → "palāyana". No SEO text depends on them.

Nothing in the post sells fear, makes health promises or disparages another tradition; the Brahma Kumaris setting and the remark about other faiths are respectful.
