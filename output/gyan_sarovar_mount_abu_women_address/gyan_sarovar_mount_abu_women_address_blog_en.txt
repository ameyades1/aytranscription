# Woman Is Not Equal, She Is a Thousand Times More Powerful

3 July 2025. The quiet valleys of Mount Abu. A national conference of the Brahma Kumaris on the theme "Women's Empowerment through Spirituality". More than 500 influential women from every corner of the country were present.

The special guest at this gathering was Sadguru Acharya Upendra Ji, founder of the Antar Yog Foundation. And what he said left the whole hall spellbound.

## No Helplessness, No Retreat

After the lighting of the lamp, a senior Brahma Kumari sadhika introduced Acharya Ji. She said that he has served the motherland selflessly since 2005, and that he does not merely teach the scriptures but lives them.

She called his life "a burning lamp in the darkness of maya" and recalled Arjuna's proclamation:

> **na dainyaṃ na palāyanam**
> "No helplessness, no retreat."
> (Arjuna's proclamation, Mahabharata tradition)

That is, neither **dainya** (helplessness) nor **palayana** (running away). Moving forward on the path of duty with firm resolve.

## Is a Woman Equal to a Man?

Every debate today stops at "equality". Acharya Ji turned the question itself upside down. He said that a woman is never equal to a man; she is at least a thousand times more powerful.

He gave the proof from scripture. In the Durga Saptashati the Goddess is saluted in both forms:

> **atisaumyātiraudrāyai natāstasyai namo namaḥ**
> "Salutations again and again to Her who is supremely gentle and supremely fierce."
> (Shri Durga Saptashati 5.13)

She who is supremely **saumya** (gentle) and supremely **raudra** (fierce): salutations to Her again and again.

But Acharya Ji also put forward a bitter truth. A woman's fierce form often comes out at home, in front of her husband, while outside she remains gentle. However much injustice she faces outside, she bears it, when she was not meant to bear it.

*Does your strength show itself where it is truly needed?*

## What Does India Lack?

According to Acharya Ji, India has all the knowledge and all the powers it needs to become the Vishwa Guru, the teacher of the world. Only one thing is missing: India has not yet reached the **Ardhanarishvara** state.

Ardhanarishvara means the union of Shiva and Shakti in a single form, the balance of the masculine and the feminine. Society is still male-dominated. He asked: how many women have become Prime Minister in this country? Only one. Why not more?

That is why he began this work, because this change will come only through the empowerment of women. But how? First, a woman's own powers must be recognised.

## A Twenty-Minute Journey and Intuition

Acharya Ji gave a very ordinary example. You board a bus or a train compartment. All the window seats have been taken by men. The journey is only twenty minutes.

Even so, a woman senses in a moment: "This one doesn't seem right, that one looks a bit like a goon." And she goes and sits right beside the one who is decent. Acharya Ji says that she has that intuitive power.

This **intuition** is not something learned; it is natural to a woman.

## Dhriti, Smriti and Medha: A Truth with a Smile

**Dhriti** means the power of retention, holding on to something for a very long time.

Acharya Ji explained it lightly. If a man asks, "Where did I go wrong?", he tells him: just go and tell your wife that she once made a mistake. Then watch! She will tell you every mistake that you, your mother and your family made, and when.

And at exactly the right time, at some function, with the right words. This is the meeting of **smriti** (memory), **dhriti** (retention) and **medha** (intelligence). The hall laughed, but the point was deep: these powers are wonderfully developed in a woman.

## Mother, Guru and Mother India

Now the address reached its deepest point. Acharya Ji said that until we understand a mother, we cannot understand the Guru, and until we understand the Guru as Mother, we cannot understand Mother India.

And if India is to become the Vishwa Guru, consider this: the rest of the world is made up of people of other faiths. When and why will they listen to us? Acharya Ji's question was: are we ready to understand them as a mother would?

*Can you understand others with the same patience with which a mother understands her child?*

## The Response in the Hall

The women present felt that in Acharya Ji's eyes a woman is not ordinary but deeply worthy of reverence and immensely powerful. After the session, many women met him personally to express their gratitude.

They found his words practical and logical, and found solutions to problems in their own lives. Many expressed a wish to learn more about the work of the Antar Yog Foundation.

## Key Takeaways

- A woman is not equal to a man; she is at least a thousand times more powerful.
- The Goddess is both supremely gentle and supremely fierce; a woman should bring out her fierce form where there is injustice, rather than bear it.
- India has everything except the Ardhanarishvara balance, and that will come only through the empowerment of women.
- Intuition, dhriti, smriti and medha are a woman's natural powers.
- Without understanding a mother you cannot understand the Guru, and without understanding the Guru as Mother you cannot understand Mother India.
- To become the Vishwa Guru, India must understand the world as a mother would.
