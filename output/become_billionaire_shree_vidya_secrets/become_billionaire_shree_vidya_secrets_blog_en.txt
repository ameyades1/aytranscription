# Shree Vidya: The Ancient Secret of Wealth, Dharma and Liberation

Is earning wealth against the spiritual life? Is it a sin to desire money?

In this discourse, given at the Antar Yog Shree Vidya camp, Acharya Ji breaks this old belief. He explains that **Shree Vidya** is a rare sadhana that awakens the power to earn wealth within the seeker as a **siddhi** (spiritual capacity).

## Why Are Some Very Rich and Some Very Poor?

Earning wealth is not easy for everyone. Many people work hard all day, and only then do they get a little money, on which they get by.

On the other side is a class of billionaires and trillionaires. Acharya Ji says he has seen great inequality in our country. Some are very rich, some very poor.

## A Dream Beyond a Job

Our education system does not teach this knowledge at all. A child leaving school or college first thinks about where to get a job.

Even the most gifted child thinks only of how to go abroad and get a job. Acharya Ji wants him to become a great **udyojak** (entrepreneur), to do something big. And even for any work of dharma, wealth is needed.

*When did you last think that you were made not just to hold a job, but to create something?*

## Why Be Ashamed of Needing Wealth?

Acharya Chanakya's sutra makes this truth clear:

> **sukhasya mūlaṃ dharmaḥ, dharmasya mūlam arthaḥ, arthasya mūlaṃ rājyam**
> "The root of happiness is dharma, the root of dharma is wealth (artha), and the root of wealth is the state."
> (Chanakya Sutra 1-3)

So the state must be prosperous, and there is nothing to be ashamed of in needing wealth.

Lord Vishnu is the sustainer, and to sustain, Mother Lakshmi is always with him. In the Vedic age India was called the golden bird. Dwarkadhish Shri Krishna and Shri Ram both had no lack of wealth.

> "The thinking we have been given, that money is bad, the rich are bad, wealth is bad, that it spoils the mind, nothing like that is true."

## Shree Vidya Is Not Just Earning Money

The real knowledge is how to **plan** the use of wealth, how to manage it, and how to do dharma and charity from it. Whoever learns this properly understands Shree Vidya.

Shree Vidya is called the knowledge that grants **bhoga and moksha** together. Here **bhoga** means the comforts and means of the world, and **moksha** means liberation.

> "It is the knowledge that turns enjoyment into material for yoga and leads toward liberation: Shree Vidya."

That is, not running away from the world, but making the world's means themselves the medium of sadhana.

*Is your wealth binding you, or leading you toward liberation?*

## The Same Knowledge Abroad

Acharya Ji says this very knowledge is being used abroad today. On one hand they took wealth by looting India; on the other, they took our own Shree Vidya with them. The great industrialists there learned this knowledge from here.

## The Lineage of Masters and the Shree Yantra Sadhana

The tradition of Shree Vidya is very ancient. Adi Shankaracharya possessed the **Saundarya Lahari** and Shree Vidya. The lineage of Agastya Muni, Dattatreya, Machhindranath and Gorakhnath also carries it. Acharya Chanakya's **Kautilya Arthashastra** is famous all over the world today.

Its rare sadhanas are also given at the camp. The **Shree Yantra** sadhana, the sadhana of Mother Lakshmi, is done by a special method. It has a special **vigraha** (a sacred form for worship), whose construction is done according to science. It is an ancient, rare and secret sadhana.

## A Resolve to Reach Thirty Million People

This event is not for a single day. Acharya Ji wants to take it to every city and every village of India. The resolve is that Shree Vidya should reach at least two percent of the country, around 28 to 30 million people.

India's economy is growing, heading toward five trillion dollars, and that is a matter of pride. But Acharya Ji saw that only a handful are becoming very rich, while the rest stay as they were. His view is that at least thirty to forty million people should become entrepreneurs themselves.

Today this knowledge is taught only to the son of the rich. Only a politician's son gets to learn politics. Others do not get it in school or college, or if they do, it is not widespread.

## If the Country Survives, Dharma Survives

Acharya Ji calls upon the Education Minister, the Finance Minister and everyone concerned to bring this knowledge to the whole country, so that India can soon become an **economic superpower**. This wealth will help in works of dharma, in protecting the country and in supporting our armed forces.

> "If the country survives, dharma survives; if dharma survives, culture can flourish in it."

Only then does wealth truly benefit. Even to keep one's body healthy, wealth is needed. Acharya Ji calls on industrialists and all citizens of the country to join this programme.

## Key Takeaways

- There is no shame in needing wealth; according to Chanakya, the root of dharma is artha.
- Lakshmi is with Vishnu; Shri Krishna and Shri Ram had no lack of wealth.
- Shree Vidya is not only earning money; it teaches planning, dharma and charity with wealth.
- It is the knowledge that makes enjoyment the material of yoga and leads to liberation.
- Adi Shankaracharya, Agastya Muni, Dattatreya, the Nath lineage and Chanakya are all linked to this tradition.
- The aim is to make tens of millions of people entrepreneurs beyond jobs and make India an economic superpower.
