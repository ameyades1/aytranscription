*Is it a sin to want wealth?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that needing money is nothing shameful: Chanakya says the root of dharma is wealth. Shri Vidya teaches how to earn, plan and share wealth so it leads to liberation.

💭 _Is your wealth binding you, or carrying you toward freedom?_
