# Chapter: Shree Vidya, the Rare Path of Prosperity and Liberation

## The Antar Yog Shree Vidya Camp

Today the Antar Yog Shree Vidya camp is being held here. In it we are giving everyone initiation into the extremely rare Shree Vidya sadhana, through which the power to earn wealth awakens in the seeker on its own, as a siddhi.

## The Deep Gulf Between Rich and Poor

Earning wealth is not possible for everyone. Look at the world, look at our own country. Many people toil all day, and only then do they get a little money, on which they somehow get by. There is one class of billionaires and multimillionaires, whom we even call trillionaires, and on the other side are those living in poverty. I have seen that in our country there is great inequality. Some people are very rich and some are very poor.

Even our system of education does not teach this knowledge. The children coming out of schools and colleges today first think about where to get a job. Even the most gifted child thinks only of how to go abroad and get a job. He may study at a big college or anywhere else, but the only thought that comes to him is a job. We want him to become a great entrepreneur, to do something big. And even to do any work of dharma, wealth is needed.

## No Shame in Needing Wealth

Acharya Chanakya has said:

> **sukhasya mūlaṃ dharmaḥ, dharmasya mūlam arthaḥ, arthasya mūlaṃ rājyam**
> "The root of happiness is dharma, the root of dharma is wealth (artha), and the root of wealth is the state."
> (Chanakya Sutra 1-3)

Therefore the state must be fully prosperous. Wealth is needed, and there is nothing to be ashamed of in that.

Lord Vishnu is the sustainer, and to sustain, Mother Lakshmi is always with him. They are a pair. So Lakshmi is necessary, and there is nothing wrong in it. When we look back to the Vedic age, India was called the golden bird. Our Dwarkadhish, Shri Krishna, had no lack of wealth. Shri Ram had no lack of wealth either. So there is no problem in having wealth.

> "The thinking we have been given, that money is bad, the rich are bad, wealth is bad, that it spoils the mind, nothing like that is true."

## A Knowledge That Gives Both Enjoyment and Liberation

How to plan the use of wealth, how to manage it, how to do dharma and give charity from it: if a person learns this properly, that itself is the knowledge. Shree Vidya is not limited to earning money. It is called the knowledge that grants both bhoga (enjoyment) and moksha (liberation) together.

> "It is the knowledge that turns enjoyment into material for yoga and leads toward liberation: Shree Vidya."

You will not believe that abroad, too, this very knowledge is being used today. On one hand, they first took wealth by looting India and grew big that way; on the other, they took this Shree Vidya of ours with them. All the great industrialists and powerful people you see there today learned this knowledge from here.

## The Great Lineage of Shree Vidya

Adi Shankaracharya possessed the Saundarya Lahari and Shree Vidya. The lineage of Agastya Muni, Dattatreya, Machhindranath and Gorakhnath also carries the tradition of Shree Vidya. And there is our Acharya Chanakya, whose Kautilya Arthashastra is famous all over the world. We are giving the knowledge of all this to everyone in this camp.

Along with it, its rare sadhanas are also being given. The sadhana of the Shree Yantra, which is the sadhana of Mother Lakshmi, is done by a special method. It has a special vigraha (consecrated sacred form), whose construction is also done according to science. This is our very old, ancient, rare and secret sadhana, which is being given to people in this camp this time.

## Shree Vidya to Every Village

This event is not just for today. I want to tell everyone that we want to take this event to every city in India and to every village. Our resolve is that this Shree Vidya should reach at least two percent of the people of this country, that is, around 28 to 30 million people.

Today we hear that India's economy is growing, that it is becoming a five trillion dollar economy. When such a statement comes from the government, we feel very proud. But I came to understand that a handful of people are becoming very rich while everyone else remains as they were. My view is that the nation is growing, which is good, but at least thirty to forty million people should be able to become entrepreneurs themselves.

Today this knowledge is taught only to the son of the very rich. If someone is a politician, only his son gets to learn politics. Nobody else is given this knowledge in school or college, and even if it is given, it is not widespread, or people are not aware of it. That is why this is an extremely rare sadhana.

## A Call to the Nation

I want to call upon our Education Minister, Finance Minister and all those concerned: we want to give all this knowledge to this country. With it, India can soon become an economic superpower too. This wealth will help us do dharma and will help in protecting the country and supporting our armed forces.

> "If the country survives, dharma survives; if dharma survives, culture can flourish in it."

Only then does that wealth truly benefit. Wealth brings many benefits. Even to keep one's body healthy, wealth is needed.

Along with this, I would say to the many industrialists and entrepreneurs of our country, and to all its citizens: connect with us, contact us, and become part of this programme. This is my wish. Thank you.
