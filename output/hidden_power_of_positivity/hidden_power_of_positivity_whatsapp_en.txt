*Does "good" mean whatever feels good?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that in the mantra Bhadram Karnebhih, auspicious means what leads to your well-being, not what pleases. A teacher's scolding is bitter medicine; a vulgar song is pleasant but not auspicious.

💭 _Was the harsh word you brushed aside today actually good for you?_
