# From Shri Vidya to Prosperity: The Forgotten Secret of Becoming a Billionaire

Today people toil day and night and work hard, yet they barely make it to the end of the month. Meanwhile, another group of people employs them and grows enormously wealthy.

Why is the gap so wide? Acharya Ji opens his talk at this grand Antar Yog Shri Vidya **Shibir** (a gathering for spiritual practice and teaching) with exactly this question.

## Why Does the Same Effort Bring Such Different Rewards?

Look at our own country or at the whole world. One group of people works extremely hard and earns a modest salary, while another grows highly prosperous from that very same labour.

Acharya Ji explains that the second group knows **Shri Vidya**, the ancient Indian spiritual tradition centred on the worship of Devi Lalita Tripurasundari. Most of our people never received it. It was hidden from them, lost over time, or deliberately kept secret.

*Have you ever wondered what link is missing between your hard work and your prosperity?*

## A Rare and Divine Lineage

To see how powerful this knowledge is, look at the lineage behind it. Adi Shankaracharya's **Saundarya Lahari** (a celebrated hymn to the Divine Mother), the Shri Vidya tradition of the sage Agastya, the great masters Dattatreya, Matsyendranath and Gorakhnath, and Kautilya's **Arthashastra** (the classical Indian treatise on wealth and statecraft) are all rare treasures of ours.

Acharya Ji then makes a startling claim. The great wealth we see abroad came in one of two ways: through plunder, as we all know, or through intelligence. And that intelligence, too, was India's own Shri Vidya.

## "How to Become a Billionaire": The Shibir's Resolve

This Shibir sets out to bring the most advanced and powerful practice of Shri Vidya to India's young people, its entrepreneurs and anyone who wants to build a business. The programme's very name says it: **How to Become a Billionaire, How to Earn up to a Billion.**

Acharya Ji takes pride in India growing into a five-trillion-dollar economy. Yet he asks a pointed question. If only a handful of entrepreneurs prosper along the way while ordinary people stay where they are, five, ten or even twenty trillion will do the nation little good.

His resolve is that at least **two percent** of the country, roughly **thirty million** people, become wealthy entrepreneurs through Shri Vidya.

## The Secret Practice of the Shri Yantra

The scriptures describe a special sacred form, the **Shri Yantra** (the holy geometric diagram that embodies Shri Vidya). Practitioners perform a special practice on it through **kumkum abhishek** (a ritual offering of sacred vermilion powder).

This has always been a secret practice, and at this Shibir it is being opened to the public for the first time.

## The Golden Bird and the Myth of the "Poor Brahmin"

In the Vedic age, India was known as the **sone ki chidiya** (the golden bird), a land of fabulous wealth. Acharya Ji observes that it is no longer so, and that we should not accept the India we have today.

We have always grown up hearing stories that begin, "Once there was a poor Brahmin." Acharya Ji rejects this outright: a Brahmin is not meant to be poor. A Brahmin who truly knows the Vedas is bound to be wealthy, for that is the blessing of Lakshmi, the goddess of prosperity.

## Wealth Lies at the Root of Dharma

A teaching of Acharya Chanakya lays the foundation for this idea:

> **sukhasya mūlaṃ dharmaḥ |**
> **dharmasya mūlam arthaḥ |**
> **arthasya mūlaṃ rājyam ||**
> "The root of happiness is dharma, the root of dharma is **artha** (wealth and resources), and the root of wealth is the state."
> (Chanakya Sutras 1-3)

Whether you want to live by **dharma** (righteous duty) or take care of others, it inevitably takes money. You cannot get anywhere by avoiding the subject.

## Why Don't Schools Teach Us About Wealth?

Does any school or college actually teach you about money? Management courses train you to manage other people's money, but no class shows you how to become wealthy yourself.

That is why Acharya Ji wants this knowledge taught in schools and colleges as part of the curriculum. He hopes everyone from the Education Minister and the Finance Minister up to the Prime Minister will understand the plan. The work is now reaching every city and every village in India.

*Your education taught you to earn, but did it ever teach you to become wealthy?*

## Wealth, Strength and the Defence of the Nation

Wealth creates strength. It keeps the armed forces strong and the country protected. When the nation survives, Acharya Ji points out, our dharma survives with it.

If the land itself is lost, what remains? Defence needs money too. Caring for your body, running a business or building a company: every undertaking needs wealth.

## Wealth Is Not Evil: Enjoyment and Liberation Together

We were taught that rich people are bad, that money is evil and that it corrupts the mind. Acharya Ji says this belief is wrong. Did wealth ever harm Shri Rama, the king of Ayodhya, or Shri Krishna, the lord of Dwarka?

Shri Vidya is more than a science of money. It teaches you how to handle wealth wisely and how to attain **bhoga** (worldly enjoyment) and **moksha** (liberation) together. The Guru empowers the seeker through **shaktipat diksha** (initiation in which the Guru transmits spiritual energy).

## A Call to Everyone

In closing, Acharya Ji calls on everyone across the country to come to the Antar Yog Shri Vidya practice, Shri Vidya initiation and the Shri Vidya Shibirs.

## Key Takeaways

- The gap between hard work and prosperity, Acharya Ji says, is a gap in the knowledge of Shri Vidya.
- The Shri Vidya lineage links Shankaracharya, Agastya, Dattatreya, Matsyendranath, Gorakhnath and Kautilya's Arthashastra.
- The Shibir's resolve: two percent of India, about thirty million people, become wealthy entrepreneurs.
- The secret practice of kumkum abhishek on the Shri Yantra is now open to all.
- Chanakya teaches that wealth is the root of dharma, so no one can avoid the subject of money.
- Wealth is not evil; Shri Vidya brings enjoyment and liberation together.
