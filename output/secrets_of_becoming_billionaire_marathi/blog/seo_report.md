## 1. Summary

A short Marathi interview (6:59) in which Acharya Upendra Ji introduces the Antar Yog Shri Vidya Shibir "How to Become a Billionaire". He asks why equal effort brings unequal reward, names Shri Vidya as the missing knowledge, presents the Shri Yantra kumkum abhishek as a secret practice now opened to the public, rejects the "poor Brahmin" story and the idea that money is evil, cites Chanakya ("dharmasya mūlam arthaḥ"), and states a resolve that 2% of India (about 3 crore people) become wealthy entrepreneurs. Main intent: informational with a strong aspirational/life-problem layer (people who work hard but stay financially stuck), plus navigational interest in the Shibir. Opportunity: "secret of becoming a billionaire" queries are dominated by generic self-help and finance listicles; a page that answers from Indian tradition (Shri Vidya, Shri Yantra, Chanakya, bhoga and moksha together) has a clear, distinctive angle, as long as it does not promise wealth as a guaranteed result. This talk overlaps closely with `become_billionaire_shree_vidya_secrets` (the longer Hindi/English talk on the same Shibir), so it targets the "secret of becoming a billionaire" / Shri Yantra / "poor Brahmin" / schools angle and leaves "Shri Vidya and wealth" to that post.

## 6. FAQ

1. **श्रीविद्या क्या है? / What is Shri Vidya?**
   भारत की प्राचीन साधना-परंपरा, जो देवी ललिता त्रिपुरसुंदरी की उपासना पर आधारित है। / An ancient Indian spiritual tradition centred on the worship of Devi Lalita Tripurasundari.
2. **क्या धन कमाना आध्यात्मिक रूप से बुरा है? / Is money evil in Hinduism?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार नहीं। श्रीराम और श्रीकृष्ण को धन से कोई कष्ट नहीं हुआ; श्रीविद्या धन को सँभालना और भोग व मोक्ष दोनों साथ पाना सिखाती है। / According to Acharya Ji, no. Wealth did not harm Shri Rama or Shri Krishna; Shri Vidya teaches how to handle wealth and attain bhoga and moksha together.
3. **"सुखस्य मूलं धर्मः" का अर्थ क्या है? / What does "sukhasya mūlaṃ dharmaḥ" mean?**
   चाणक्य सूत्र: सुख का मूल धर्म है, धर्म का मूल अर्थ है, और अर्थ का मूल राज्य है। / Chanakya Sutras 1-3: the root of happiness is dharma, the root of dharma is artha (wealth), and the root of wealth is the state.
4. **श्रीयंत्र पर कुंकुम अभिषेक क्या है? / What is kumkum abhishek on the Shri Yantra?**
   श्रीयंत्र श्रीविद्या का पवित्र ज्यामितीय विग्रह है; उस पर कुंकुम अर्पित करके की जाने वाली विशेष, गुप्त साधना को इस शिविर में लोगों के सामने खोला गया। / The Shri Yantra is the sacred geometric form of Shri Vidya; the special, secret practice of offering kumkum on it was opened to the public at this Shibir.
5. **स्कूलों में धन क्यों नहीं सिखाया जाता? / Why don't schools teach about money?**
   प्रबंधन की पढ़ाई दूसरों के धन का प्रबंधन सिखाती है, स्वयं धनवान बनना नहीं; आचार्य जी चाहते हैं कि यह विद्या पाठ्यक्रम में पढ़ाई जाए। / Management courses teach how to manage other people's money, not how to become wealthy yourself; Acharya Ji wants this knowledge taught as part of the curriculum.

## 10. Authenticity flags
