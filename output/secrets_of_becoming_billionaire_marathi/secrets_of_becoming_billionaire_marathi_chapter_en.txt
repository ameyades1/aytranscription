# Chapter: Shri Vidya and the Secret of Becoming a Billionaire

## The Gap Between Toil and Prosperity

Today we are holding a grand Shri Vidya camp here. Today we see that ordinary people have to run around a great deal for money. People toil day and night, work hard, and receive a meagre salary. On that they somehow get through life; somehow the month passes.

In this hectic life, if we look, within our own nation, our own country, or the whole world, one class is toiling hard and getting a small salary. And there is another class that gets the work done by them and becomes extremely wealthy.

So what is so special that one class gets so much and the other does not? Why is this gap so large? The gap exists precisely because one class knows Shri Vidya, this old, ancient knowledge that exists in our Bharat; they have it. And from most of our people it was hidden, or it was lost, or it was simply not given much; it was kept secret.

## The Divine Lineage of Shri Vidya

This knowledge is that powerful. Like Shankaracharya's Saundarya Lahari, the Shri Vidya lineage of Agastya Muni, our Dattatreya, Machhindranath and Gorakhnath who came in that lineage, or Kautilya's Arthashastra; all these are our rare means.

You will not believe it, but even abroad, the great wealth we see has come in only two ways. Either it was brought by plunder, which we all know; or else they used intellect, and the intellect they used was ours: they earned their wealth by using the Shri Vidya of Bharat.

## The Purpose of the Camp: How to Become a Billionaire

The thought behind organising this programme was that this divine sadhana, the divine and rare Shri Vidya, and its most advanced and most powerful practice, should reach our Indian children, our Indian people, entrepreneurs, and anyone who wishes to do business. That is why we are giving this initiation here. This is no small, ordinary initiation. Our programme is: how to become a billionaire, how to earn up to a billion.

We are told that our country will become a large five trillion economy. That is certainly praiseworthy, if our Bharat is growing so large. But if in that process only a few select entrepreneurs grow big and the common person stays the same, then whether it is five trillion, ten trillion or twenty trillion, the nation does not gain much from it.

It is good, and we are proud of it. But our resolve is that in this country at least two percent of people become very big, renowned, Shri Vidya wealthy people; that they become what we call great entrepreneurs. Even two percent of Bharat would be around three crore people. Reaching that many people across Bharat is our resolve.

## The Secret Sadhana of the Shri Yantra

There truly is a knowledge of how one becomes a billionaire through Shri Vidya; you will not believe it is real. We had scriptures. In them there is a special structure, its sacred form, that of the Shri Yantra. A kumkum abhisheka is performed on it and its special sadhana is undertaken, which will be done in this camp. It is a secret sadhana, and today we are offering it openly to the people. Advanced sadhanas of this kind are being given here.

## The Golden Bird and the Myth of the Poor Brahmin

The benefit is simply this: we used to say that in the Vedic age Bharat was the golden bird. Now it is no longer so; we do not want Bharat to be like this.

We have always believed that there was a poor Brahmin. A Brahmin is not poor. If he truly knows the Veda, he has the very boon of Lakshmi that he must be wealthy.

## The Root of Dharma Is Artha

In this country our Acharya Chanakya has said:

> **sukhasya mūlaṃ dharmaḥ. dharmasya mūlam arthaḥ. arthasya mūlaṃ rājyam.**
> "The root of happiness is dharma, the root of dharma is artha (wealth), and the root of artha is the state."
> (Chanakya Sutra 1-3)

He says that here, even if you want to practise dharma, it takes great wealth. Whatever you want to do, whomever you must sustain, wealth will be needed. This subject of wealth cannot be avoided.

## Where Is the Education About Wealth?

Today, in our schools and colleges, is anything taught about wealth anywhere? It is a basic subject. We teach management, where one learns how to manage the wealth of the wealthy. But is there a class anywhere on how to become wealthy yourself, how to become a billionaire yourself? No.

So our thought is that this knowledge should be taught in our schools and colleges according to their respective curricula. And it is my wish that our education minister, finance minister, everyone else, even up to the Prime Minister, all should surely understand this plan. We are doing this across Bharat, in place after place, in every city, up to every village, because wealth is something everyone needs.

## Wealth Builds Strength, Strength Protects

If you have wealth, you can also build strength. You can keep everything powerful, from your armed forces onward, and protect the country.

> "If your country survives, your dharma endures."

This is my belief. If your land itself is gone, if you could not protect it, what remains? Even protection takes wealth. Every task takes wealth; today even caring for your body takes wealth, as do your business and your company.

## Wealth Is Not Bad: Bhoga and Moksha Together

So what we were taught, that the rich are bad, money is bad, money spoils everyone's mind, is wrong. Whether Shri Rama, Lord of Ayodhya, or our Shri Krishna, Lord of Dwarka, what trouble did wealth cause them? None.

How to handle wealth, how through it one gains both bhoga and moksha together, that is this knowledge. It is not only a knowledge of wealth; how you can gain liberation along with wealth, both are properly accomplished in it. You are given power through shaktipat initiation.

## An Appeal to the Nation

The programme organised today, when we hold it everywhere like this, then through you I will appeal to all the people of our country: do come, do come, all of you, to the Antar Yog Shri Vidya sadhana, the Shri Vidya initiation, and the Shri Vidya camps. Thank you.
