# Creation Takes a Moment, Sustaining Takes a Lifetime

We tend to treat beginnings as the biggest events of our lives: birth, first love, a new business. Acharya Upendra Ji asks a simple question about them. How long did that beginning actually last? And who looked after everything that came in the long stretch afterwards?

The answer shows why **Lord Vishnu and Goddess Lakshmi** hold such a central place in **Sanatan Dharma** (the eternal way, the Hindu tradition).

## How Long Did Your Birth Last?

When you were born, how many minutes did that moment of creation take? A fraction of a second. By the very next instant, you had already been born.

Death will come one day too. How long will that moment be? Very brief.

Relationships work the same way. You see someone for the first time and fall in love at first sight. A second glance follows, then a third, and the feeling carries on. Yet that first glance, that moment of **creation**, was over in a few heartbeats. The feeling that stirred within you for the first time also lasted just an instant.

Business is no different. You file the papers, complete the registration, and the beginning is done. Endings don't take long either. As Acharya Ji puts it, the moment of creation is very short.

## Where Does All the Time Go?

Here is the real question. How much of **Brahma and Saraswati** (the power of creation and knowledge) do you need in life? How much of **Kali Mahakali** (the power of endings and transformation)? And how much of **Vishnu and Lakshmi** (the power of sustenance and prosperity)?

The answer is clear. **Most of your time goes into sustaining what exists.**

Look at any level you like: the body, a business, a kingdom or a family. Everywhere, you need a great deal of the **Lakshmi tattva and Vishnu tattva**, where **tattva** means a principle or essential force.

*What in your life takes the most energy to keep going right now?*

## Small Stories Within the Big One

This doesn't mean Brahma appears only once. Something new is being created at every moment.

A business begins with one small branch. Later a second branch opens, a new unit forms, someone new joins the team. Within the larger act of creation, smaller ones keep unfolding.

Life follows the same pattern. You were born, grew a little, and then went to school. That started something new in your life, and once again you needed **Saraswati**.

School has to end at some point, though. Acharya Ji asks with a smile: what are you doing at seventy-two, still sitting in the fourth grade? Every small cycle has its own ending, because within the big story, smaller things keep arising and coming to a close.

Don't assume, then, that you no longer need Kali or Saraswati. New cycles will keep arriving along the way.

## Marriage: Building a New World

Then, one day, comes marriage. It opens a new **chapter** between birth and death. Something new has been created, and it calls once more for Brahma, Saraswati and a clear mind. From there, a whole new world takes shape.

That chapter will also close someday. It might end through death, a quarrel, an accident, a soldier's death in battle, or work that takes two people to different places. We cannot know how it will happen. One partner may go first, while the other lives on.

*What new chapter has just begun in your life, and what are you giving to sustain it?*

## Why Mahalakshmi Matters Most

The essence is this: you cannot say you have no need for Saraswati right now, or that you can do without Kali. Creation and endings both return again and again.

Yet even in each small story, what does it take to keep alive whatever was created? The same **Vishnu** and the same **Lakshmi**. This is why, Acharya Ji explains, our Sanatan Dharma gives the highest importance to **Mahalakshmi** (Goddess Lakshmi in her supreme, all-encompassing form).

## Key Takeaways

- The moments of creation and of ending are both very brief.
- In life, the body, business, family and kingdom alike, most of the time goes into sustaining.
- Sustaining calls above all for the **Vishnu tattva and Lakshmi tattva**.
- Smaller cycles keep turning within the big story: school, a new branch, marriage. Brahma, Saraswati and Kali each play a role in every one.
- This is why Sanatan Dharma gives the highest importance to **Mahalakshmi**.
