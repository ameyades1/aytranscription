# Chapter: The Moment of Creation and the Life of Sustenance

## How Long Is the Moment of Creation?

So tell me, when you come into being, that moment of birth, that moment of creation when you were made, how many minutes did it last in your life? When you were created, how much time did it take? Death too will come one day, when you depart. How many minutes will that moment last? Only a fraction of a second. You were born, and from the very next moment onward you are already born. How long was that moment of being born?

A relationship began with someone. At the start you saw someone, looked into their eyes, and there was what is called love at first sight. Then there is a second sight and a third sight as well, and it continues. But how many moments did it take for the first sight to end? That was the creation. After that a feeling must have arisen within, and that too arose for the first time. What came from the second time onward, what was that? So the moment of creation is very short.

You want to start a business. You applied for the papers, completed a registration, and the beginning was over. Ending does not take long either, just as beginning does not.

## Most of the Time Goes into Sustenance

So think: how much will you need Brahma and Saraswati, how much Kali Mahakali, and how much Vishnu Lakshmi? Most of the time goes into sustenance. Whether it is the body or a business, whatever you speak of, whether running a kingdom or anything else, at whatever level you go, running a family, all of it **will require the Lakshmi principle and the Vishnu principle a great deal.**

> "Most of the time goes into sustenance."

## Small Creations Within the Great Creation

But during all this we cannot forget that Brahma is also there. Not just one moment: in every moment something or other new keeps arising. Take a business. Earlier, when there was a small branch, or when something was just beginning, it was created. Now I will open a second branch, open a new unit, appoint someone new, and that too is new. Within the great creation there are small creations.

You were born, just as you were. From a small child you grew a little, and then you have to go to school. That is a new creation, meaning something new began in life from there. Once again Saraswati will be needed.

But school should end at some point, shouldn't it? Or will you keep studying all your life? At the age of seventy-two, what are you doing? Still in the fourth grade! So at some point that too comes to an end.

In this way, within the great story, small stories arise again and come to an end. This does not mean that Kali and Saraswati are not needed now. New cycles will keep coming in between. Is that clear?

## Marriage: A New Chapter of Life

Then at some point there will be a marriage. Between your main creation and your main end, did a new chapter arise or not? Something new was created. To run it, once again a new Saraswati, everything, Brahma will be needed. From there a new world will be built, and for that, intellect will again be needed.

That too will end some day. Either with your death, or with a quarrel, or in an accident, or by attaining a hero's death in battle. Anything can happen. Or the two may have to go to different places for their work. We do not know how it will happen, or whether it will happen at all. But one day, perhaps with the death of the body. One may go first, but the other still lives on.

## Vishnu and Lakshmi for Sustenance

So what I am saying is that, in between, something new will begin again, some new business will start. So you cannot say that right now you do not need Saraswati, or that right now you do not need Kali. Within the great story there are small stories.

> "Within the great story there are small, small stories. But even in that small story, what will be needed to maintain what has arisen? Once again, that Vishnu and that Lakshmi."

Even so, in that small story too, what is needed to sustain what has arisen? Once again, the same Vishnu and the same Lakshmi. That is why in our Sanatan Dharma **Mahalakshmi has been given the greatest importance.**
