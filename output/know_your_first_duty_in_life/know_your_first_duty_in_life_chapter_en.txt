# Chapter: The First Duty of Life

## The Fire of Knowledge: The Very First Action

What is the first action in life? It is said that kindling the fire of knowledge within oneself is the very first action. If I am to do anything, the first thought should be this: since I have taken this life, there should be a Guru in my life. A Guru who has attained realization himself, and who takes me **from nara to Narayana**, from the human to the Divine.

## The Promise Made in the Womb

This is the very promise each of us makes to ourselves while in the womb. Read the Garuda Purana sometime. In the womb, the soul thinks that it will come out. It remembers the Vaitarani river and the rest, and nothing feels good. Even inside the mother's womb it does not feel good. It understands where it is going, and that it has committed sins.

Then it resolves that the first action I take after coming out will be this: I will again meet a Guru, I will attain the **Vishnu tattva**, the Vishnu principle, and I will resolve the mistakes of my previous birth.

## The Memory of Twelve Days

It is said that when a child is born, for twelve days it remembers this first thing. After the twelfth day, in **Vaishnavi Maya**, it forgets again and gets entangled, forgetting what its first duty was and how it was to become the doer.

That is why the naming ceremony too is prescribed on the twelfth day, after twelve days are complete. Because for twelve days the memory of the past birth continues in that child.

> "The past birth is still remembered, but the child cannot express it."

That is why a small child sometimes cries now and then. We think it has not got milk, or something else, and so it is crying. No. It is crying for this reason: do not leave me again, I have not found it yet, and in twelve days I am going to forget again.

Have you read all this in the Garuda Purana and the Katha Upanishad or not? That much study you will surely do. It has been taught in detail in the Pitru Mukti Shivir.

## The Guru Only Reminds You

My first action does not need to be imposed on me by someone else. By reading the scriptures, you should remember it yourself. Guruji or Acharya Ji only reminds you what you yourself had said your first action would be.

## The Order of Becoming the Doer

The resolve was that I will become the doer, and its order was this:

1. First I will search for the Guru, my worthy Guru in this birth.
2. After that, the wrong karmas I had accumulated in past births, I will cut those first. For the sins I committed, I will do **prayaschitta**, atonement.
3. Only after doing this will I do new meritorious work, something good, and some **sakar karya**, tangible work, that is, work that is visible to the world, concrete and real.
4. And by meditating, removing all attachments of this world, attaining **moksha**, the dissolution of attachment, I will merge.

## Who Is the True Doer?

This was your first action. This is the duty of every one. This is what is called the true doer.

Whatever else you did, leaving this aside, you are still the doer in it, but right now you are doing it in sleep.

> "You have not risen up yet, you have not woken up yet. Till now you are in a sleeping state."

Has this been understood properly?
