# Beyond the Art of Living: The Art of Leaving This World

## What Does Life Actually Guarantee?

Stop and consider this for a moment. Is there anything in life that is truly **eternal**, anything you can count on completely?

You may find a job, but nothing guarantees it. You may marry, but nothing guarantees that either. If you do, there is no promise the marriage will last, and if it lasts, no promise it will make you happy.

Money may come your way, with no guarantee. Even if it comes, nobody knows how long it will stay. What, then, is certain? If you were born, **death** is. That much is bound to happen.

## No Lifespan Lasts Forever

Some people leave at the age of one, and some die even as they are being born. There are records of people living to 180. Yogis in the Himalayas are said to have lived for 400 and even 800 years.

Accounts from the time of the saint Dnyaneshwar describe Changdev Maharaj as being 1,400 years old. The scriptures also speak of the **chiranjeevi** (great beings believed to be immortal).

Yet however long a life runs, it can **never be endless**. For ordinary human beings the rule is plain: whoever is born will die.

## Do You Know When Your Day Will Come?

Something this certain calls for preparation. But can anyone tell you whether that day is today, tomorrow, or a hundred years from now?

We spend our lives doing things for others: for our ancestors and the departed, for our neighbours. Yet your own turn may come before you have finished doing any of it for them.

You could slip and fall, or meet with an accident. A heart attack could strike while you sit at home, or an earthquake could hit. You were fine yesterday, and today anything could happen. Acharya Ji asks: if you do not know the date, and you still have to be ready for it, how should you live?

*If you had to leave the stage today, would you be ready?*

## The Art of Living and the Art of Leaving

Plenty of people can teach you the Art of Living: how to conduct yourself here and what your duties are.

Acharya Ji, however, speaks of another art: the **Art of Leaving** this world. Life is a play, and one day the play is bound to end. Learning how to step off the stage is therefore a vital branch of knowledge.

Where can you learn it? From a film star? From a businessman, a politician or a schoolteacher? No.

For this, you must go to a **jivanmukta** (one who is liberated while still living) Sadguru. Here you have no choice in the matter; it is essential.

## You Can Only Give What You Have

If you want wealth, you have to go to someone wealthy. Only a person who has plenty can give it.

Only someone whose heart is full of love can offer love. Only someone forgiving will forgive.

In the same way, only one who is **free**, who is jivanmukta and **videhamukta** (beyond the limits of the body), can lead you to liberation. Everyone else can only talk about it.

If you are to experience it for yourself, the Guru must know how to give **shaktipat** (the Guru's transmission of spiritual energy into the disciple), both to you and to your ancestors.

*Has the person you are learning from lived through what they teach?*

## Theory Versus Experience

That is why the only true authority is a living Sadguru who abides in the state of liberation while alive. Everything else is mere book learning.

Acharya Ji offers an example. Imagine someone who knows the entire theory of mechanical engineering but has never held a spanner, or who understands how computers work yet has never pressed a single key.

Or picture someone teaching a martial art who knows all the theory but has never held a sword. Would you gain anything by learning from them?

You have to learn from someone who has attained it and has seen that other world for themselves. Only then does your giving bear fruit, and the same holds for your learning and your meditation. Whatever else you do, Acharya Ji says, is a waste of time, money and life.

## Key Takeaways

- Death is the one certainty in life; nothing else comes with a guarantee.
- However long a life runs, it is never endless, and no one knows the day it will end.
- Alongside the Art of Living, you also need to learn the Art of Leaving.
- Only a jivanmukta, videhamukta Sadguru can teach this art.
- You can only give what you have; only one who is free can lead you to liberation.
- Theoretical knowledge without experience brings no real benefit.
