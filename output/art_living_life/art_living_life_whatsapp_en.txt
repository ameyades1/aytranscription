*Many teach the Art of Living. Who teaches the Art of Leaving?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that death is certain, yet no one knows its date. Only a jivanmukta Sadguru can teach us to step off life's stage.

💭 _If you left the stage today, would you be ready?_
