# Chapter: Who Is the Doer? Karma, Ego and the Gunas of Prakriti

## The Present, a Reflection of the Past

If your present is very good, it means that at some time in the past you must have done a great deal of good. I am speaking of the bank balance of punya, of merit.

First let us understand this life properly; then we will understand life after death. Understand life first, and life after death will be understood. Understand what came before this life, and this life will be understood. Do you agree? If you want to understand the future, your present must first be good.

It is said, is it not, that to have good teeth tomorrow, you must brush today? Leave the scriptures aside; even ordinary medical science says this, does it not? Whatever good you do today will come back as good tomorrow.

So if the present is good, what does that mean? Your teeth are good today, which means you must have done something good yesterday, and that is why they have lasted. And had you ruined them yesterday by chewing paan and bidis, what would have happened? Today they would have been ruined.

## The Bank Balance of Merit and the Spiritual Meter

If your present is very good, you must have done much good at some time in the past. But that can also run out one day. So you should understand how much bank balance is left, should you not? I am speaking of the bank balance of merit.

Do you have any meter to count how much it is? I am certainly spending afresh, buying a car, a horse, a bungalow, enjoying myself, but there should be some meter to know how much remains.

That meter is given to you in this shivir. It will not be an electronic device; it will be your **spiritual meter**. With it you can calculate your own karma. Whatever karma has already gone wrong can be set right from here on.

## "I Alone Do It": A Sentence Full of Ego

How many of you feel that the success you are to receive comes only and solely from your own effort? How many believe, "Only when I do it does it come; nothing else matters"? Think carefully before you answer.

"I alone do it": if such is the understanding, it is a sentence full of ego. So says Shri Krishna, in the twenty-seventh verse of the third chapter of the Bhagavad Gita.

> **prakṛteḥ kriyamāṇāni guṇaiḥ karmāṇi sarvaśaḥ**
> **ahaṅkāra-vimūḍhātmā kartāham iti manyate**
> "All actions are in every way performed by the gunas of prakriti; yet one whose inner being is deluded by ego thinks, 'I am the doer.'"
> (Bhagavad Gita 3.27)

In truth, all actions are performed in every way by the gunas of prakriti, and yet the ignorant one, whose inner being is deluded by ego, believes "I am the doer."

All actions means every single thing you do is included. Your office work is included, your housework is included, even eating is included. Everything is included, is it not? These are the words of the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita itself.

## A Little Faith Is Needed

This is what Shri Krishna has said. Now the Gita is surely not false; that much belief you all must have. With this thought I will go on. Even if you have at least five percent shraddha, faith, only then is there any meaning in talking.

If even that is absent, and the understanding is "I know more than Krishna," then there is always a next birth. Then we can talk in the next birth with some other guru; it will not happen here. This we call, "Better luck next birth."

## Why Do People Become Pitras?

In this birth we must first free our ancestors. It is so that they do not have to take another birth that we must do all this. Perhaps they too made this same mistake. So first understand properly why they become pitras, departed ancestors, and what pitra means.

The very first misunderstanding must have been: "I do it, I have done it." Because of this one reason it seems, "I must do all this; if I do not, what will happen?"

"Oh, how can I attend this shivir? I have to look after the family, look after my father, look after my mother, look after my mother-in-law, look after my daughter-in-law, look after the neighbour, even look after the dog." You have every reason in the world. Is that not so?

But have you truly known whether all this actually depends on you? Do you feel so? Do you think they really need you? Or is it the opposite, that you need them? Tell me honestly.

## Today Your Ego Will Be Struck

Today your ego will be struck hard, because we are speaking of freedom from pitra rina, the debt to the ancestors. That means we are speaking of your moksha, your liberation. So today, in a single day, I will strike your ego many times.

Whoever feels, "I must serve, I do it," in truth it is exactly the opposite. Whoever feels, "I am serving society," in truth it is he who needs prestige in society. Do you really know this?

Whoever feels, "I must heal so many patients," does not want to heal; he needs patients. If everyone became healthy, his hospital would not run.

I am speaking of the ordinary person. I am not speaking of great souls, or of those who have reached the position of a guru. This is about ordinary intellect. The understanding of ordinary intellect is "I am doing it," whereas in truth it is not so. Do you understand?

## Understanding the Verse Step by Step

What has Shri Krishna said? Now we will go step by step.

**"In truth, all actions"**: everything you do is included. Whether at home or outside, everything.

**"In every way"**: it is not that only one way counts. Whether you act in a yogic way, a Vedic way, a Puranic way, a worldly way, or through science, in whatever way you act, it is all included.

And then comes the important phrase: **"are performed by the gunas of prakriti."**

## Prakriti and the Five Great Elements

In short, **prakriti** is all this that you see. In simple language we also call it Parvati, Devi, Shakti, Maya. All these words mean prakriti.

Within this prakriti are the pancha mahabhuta, the five great elements, and all the rest: fire, earth, air, water and space, of which we are made. And of which this whole world is made too, for here there is earth, there is fire, there is water, space and air. Whatever is visible is the five great elements.

## Prapancha: The Play of Five

So it is only the play of five that is going on, and that is why it is called **prapancha**. Whose play is going on? That of the five.

After someone's marriage people ask, do they not, "How is your prapancha going?" It means they are indirectly asking, "How entangled are you in Maya?" It is not an innocent question. The question really worth asking was that one was not meant to get entangled at all. We do not even know the meaning of the words we use every day.

## I Am Not the Doer

We have understood prakriti. Now another word has come: **guna**. Action happens through the gunas of prakriti. "In truth, all actions are in every way performed by the gunas of prakriti." So what do these gunas mean?

After this it says, "Yet the ignorant one, whose inner being is deluded by ego, believes 'I do it.'" He considers himself the doer, whereas in truth he never does anything at all.

> "None of us does that karma. It happens through us."

How does this happen? As long as we are ignorant, it seems, "This is the body, and I am giving it orders, so it is acting."
