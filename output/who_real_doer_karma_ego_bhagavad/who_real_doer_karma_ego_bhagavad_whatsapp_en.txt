*Is your success purely the fruit of your own effort?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains, through Bhagavad Gita 3.27, that nature carries out all actions, yet ego says "I am the doer." Even healthy teeth today come from care taken yesterday.

💭 _Do you believe your home and work would stop without you?_
