# Chapter: A Good King Is One Who Offers an Alternative

## The Core Difference Between a Good and a Bad Leader

There is a very important difference between a good leader and a bad one, and I will tell you what it is.

Suppose you have gone somewhere on a trip, you are at a tourist place, and suddenly the local policeman tells you, "You must not park your vehicle here." He is the representative of the administration. We do not like it that he simply said, "Don't do it here." We ask, "Then where should we park?" And he answers, "That I don't know. Just don't park here, or you will be fined." We do not like such a policeman.

## When a Prohibition Comes With an Arrangement

But if he had said, "The government has said not here, but an arrangement has been made for you over there, it will cost five or ten rupees," we would gladly have accepted it.

> "A good king or a good leader is one who gives at least some good alternative in return."

Merely removing the earlier arrangement achieves nothing. One who only removes and gives nothing is called a bad king or a bad administration. The second side of good governance is the **alternative**.

## Its Role in a Sanatan Nation

In the context of a Sanatan Rashtra (a nation founded on Sanatan Dharma), a very important role of this principle will become clear.
