ideal_king_sanatan_rashtra_interview: SEO report

## 1. Summary

A very short interview clip (video uploaded 2025-05-20, 1:03) in which Acharya Ji gives one principle of good governance through an everyday example: a policeman who only says "you can't park here" is resented, while one who says "not here, but a place has been arranged over there" is obeyed gladly, even when it costs money. A good king or leader does not just remove or forbid; he gives an alternative. The blog applies this to the vision of a Sanatan Rashtra and turns it into a question for the reader's own family and work. The main intent is informational ("अच्छे नेता की पहचान", "what makes a good leader", "achhe raja ke gun"), with a secondary practical angle for parents and managers. Head terms on leadership are crowded with management and business content, so the realistic opportunity is the specific, memorable angle only this talk offers: a "no" must come with a way forward. The post is thin (about 300 words per language) and works best as a supporting post in the Bharat & Society cluster, linked from the Ram Rajya and future-of-Bharat posts.

**English (about 55 words):** According to Acharya Upendra Ji, the real difference between a good and a bad leader is whether they offer an alternative. Removing the old system alone achieves nothing.
