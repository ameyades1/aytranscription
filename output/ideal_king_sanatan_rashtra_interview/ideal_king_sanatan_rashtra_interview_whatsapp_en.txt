*What separates a good leader from a bad one?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that a good king never only forbids. A policeman saying "no parking here" annoys you; one who shows a paid spot is gladly obeyed.

💭 _Do you only say "no", or do you also suggest a better way?_
