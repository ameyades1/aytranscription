*Why do most festivals fall in the monsoon months?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that wandering sages stayed in one village through the rains, guiding its observances. Festivals return yearly to ask whether we have changed.

💭 _Since last year's festivals, what has truly changed within you?_
