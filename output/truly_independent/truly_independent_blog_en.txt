# Are We Truly Free

## A Long Name, a Deep Meaning

Have you ever noticed how long India's introduction in our Constitution is? Sovereign, socialist, secular, democratic republic, and a country run by a federal government.

There is a story behind every word. Let us understand two of these words through Gurudev's eyes.

## Why Did We Need to Say "Sovereign"?

**Sovereign** means that no one else rules over us; the rule is our own. No foreign power governs us.

But a question arises: why did this word have to be added to our name at all? Because at one time someone else did rule over us. A country to which this never happened has no need to make such a declaration.

> "We are free. We are a sovereign country."

## By the People or Buy the People?

The second word is **democracy**, meaning the entire system of the country is in the hands of the people. Government of the people, by the people, for the people.

But how much of "for the people" remains today, and how much of "of the people", is hard to say. Gurudev says with a smile that "by the people" has definitely become "buy the people". A "U" has been added in the middle, because you have forgotten yourself.

> "You have forgotten what your strength was. The Guru has to come to remind you of it."

The system has become a game. Whatever is given in the name of schemes is your own money; no one gives from their own pocket. It is the king's duty to spend in the public interest, not for personal gain.

*Do you remember what your real strength as a citizen is?*

## Chanakya's Warning

Acharya Chanakya said that the day democracy becomes very popular, understand that the dark age of Kali Yuga has arrived. Gurudev makes clear that these are not his words but Acharya Chanakya's.

Why? Because in a democracy the people themselves choose who their king will be. When everyone's vote is equal, the question is: who are the ones choosing?

According to Chanakya, democracy is **meant for the learned**. The candidates should be capable, the options should be good, and those choosing should also be wise. Only when an election is held in such an assembly does democracy work in the true sense.

## Have You Read Your Constitution?

Now ask yourself. If more than half the people have no understanding of politics, economics and the economy, what value does an election have left?

How many of us have read our Constitution? It was taught in school, but who actually read it?

*If you have never read the Constitution, how did you decide whether the government is running according to it or not?*

## Key Takeaways

- The word "sovereign" reminds us that we were once subjugated, which is why freedom had to be declared.
- Democracy means government of the people, by the people, for the people; but today it is turning into "buy the people".
- The money given through schemes is the people's own; the king's duty is to spend it in the public interest.
- According to Acharya Chanakya, democracy is meaningful only when both those who choose and those who are chosen are learned.
- True freedom begins with remembering your own strength and knowing your Constitution.
