*Why does a hard situation never seem to change?*

Drawing on Bhagavad Gita 2.14, Acharya Upendra Ji explains that no illness, poverty or quarrel is permanent. Yet it will not leave by itself: endurance is only the first step, and action must follow.

💭 _Which situation in your life have you accepted as permanent?_
