## 1. Summary

This talk asks how well we really know the God we worship every day. Acharya Ji uses the image of falling in love with, and "marrying", a stranger's photograph to show that devotion without knowing turns into habit, need or blind faith. He then says that the scriptures ask us to know God, not merely believe, and closes with the Ganesha Gita's teaching (1.19, 1.21) that seeing no difference between Shiva, Vishnu, Shakti, the Sun and Ganesha is true yoga. The main intent is informational and reflective: "भगवान को जानना या मानना", "what is true bhakti", "are Shiva and Vishnu the same", "Vaishnava vs Shaiva vs Shakta". The opportunity is the Ganesha Gita angle. Few pages explain the abheda-buddhi verse in plain language, and most "Shiva vs Vishnu" results are sectarian debates. This talk answers them with a unifying vision taken from a cited scripture. Three posts on the site use the same photograph example (`gita_warning_worship_without_understanding`, `only_way_know_god`, `know_your_lord_before_worship`), so this post should own the "know vs believe" query and the Ganesha Gita / unity-of-deities angle, not murti puja (see section 7).

**English:** The scriptures ask us to know God, not merely believe in Him. According to Acharya Upendra Ji, "loving" a form you do not know is like marrying a stranger's photograph. ## 6. FAQ

**Hindi**
1. **भगवान को जानना और मानना में क्या अंतर है?** मानना किसी के बताए रूप को बिना समझे स्वीकार करना है; जानना उसके बारे में "क्यों" पूछकर उसे पहचानना है। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि शास्त्रों में भगवान को जानने की बात कही गई है।
2. **भगवान को रूप क्यों दिया गया?** निराकार को समझना कठिन है, इसलिए साधकों के हित के लिए साकार रूप दिया गया। रूप सीढ़ी है, मंज़िल नहीं।
3. **सच्ची भक्ति की नींव क्या है?** प्रेम। जो संबंध केवल मांगने पर टिका है, वह स्वार्थ है। सच्चा प्रेम हो तो जानने की इच्छा अपने आप जागती है।
4. **क्या शिव, विष्णु और गणेश अलग-अलग हैं?** गणेश गीता (१.१९, १.२१) में भगवान गणेश कहते हैं कि वे ही महाविष्णु, सदाशिव, महाशक्ति और सूर्य हैं, और इनमें अभेद देखना ही यथार्थ योग है।

**English**
1. **What is the difference between knowing God and believing in God?** Believing is accepting a form someone handed you without understanding it; knowing means asking "why" until you recognise Him. Acharya Ji says the scriptures speak of knowing God.
2. **Why was God given a form?** The formless is hard to grasp, so a form was given for the good of seekers. The form is a ladder, not the destination.
3. **What is the foundation of true bhakti?** Love. A bond built only on asking is self-interest. Where there is real love, the wish to know Him awakens on its own.
4. **Are Shiva, Vishnu and Ganesha different Gods?** In the Ganesha Gita (1.19, 1.21), Lord Ganesha says He is Mahavishnu, Sadashiva, Mahashakti and the Sun, and that seeing no difference between them is true yoga.

(FAQ 2 avoids attributing the verse to Vyasa; see section 10, flag 1.)
