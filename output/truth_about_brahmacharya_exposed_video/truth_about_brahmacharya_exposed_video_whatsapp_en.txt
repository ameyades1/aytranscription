*Does saffron make you celibate?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that brahmacharya means moving in Brahman, known by its fruits: a sharp intellect, a radiant face, mastery over anger. Someone unmarried who cannot control anger has not found it.

💭 _Do you judge your self-control by outward rules, or by your anger?_
