# Chapter: The Truth of Brahmacharya

## A Question for Those Who Condemn Marriage

Many renunciates, and ordinary people along with them, start saying "Shiva Shiva, Rama Rama" at the slightest thing. If someone is married, it is as if they have become wrong. Our Saptarishis, the seven great sages, are married. Some of them have many sons. All of our avatars are married. There are one or two exceptions like Hanuman ji, that is all. So many of our saints and great souls have been married. Eighty to ninety percent are married; ten or twenty percent, or perhaps even fewer, are shown as unmarried. There is nothing worthy or unworthy in this.

And those you regard as brahmacharis, thinking they are unmarried, where were they born from? Did they all come from the sky? Did they have parents or not? What act of their parents would it have been? That same kama purushartha, from which these renunciates were born. Then with what face do they say that it is wrong?

Shri Krishna himself says there are four purusharthas, and kama is one of them. So never, ever say that it is wrong.

## Testing Brahmacharya: By Its Fruits

Look at the practice of brahmacharya through its phalashruti, its fruits. One does not go into someone's bedroom to find out whether they keep brahmacharya. *Brahmaṇi carati iti brahmacaryam*: one who moves in Brahman, that is brahmacharya.

You may not have married, but if you have no control over anger, your anger itself shows that you have no brahmacharya. Desire and anger go together; it means desire and lust must still be running in the mind. We can see that you cannot forgive, that you have no heart, that there is still no brahmacharya.

There are eight kinds of maithuna; this is not understood from marriage alone. Brahmacharya is tested by your medha shakti, the power of intellect, it is seen in your radiance, it is seen in the youthfulness of your face.

## The State of the Gopis: Where Neither Man Nor Woman Remains

Look at the qualities described of the gopis. Even fully unclothed before Shukadeva ji and before Shri Krishna, they feel no shame. Why? Because for those who reach the state of the Self, neither man nor woman remains. At that level, even without clothes, they feel no fear. This is the state of brahmacharya.

Now tell me, does this come merely from not marrying? Does this state come merely from wearing saffron robes? You still have to say "Shiva Shiva." Did you say to your own parents too, "Shiva Shiva, what have you done, I have been born"?

## Scriptural Kama or Complete Brahmacharya

I say this as an open challenge to every guru who says this is wrong, who sometimes tells film people "you are wrong," and sometimes tells someone else "you are wrong." Read the scriptures sometime, then you will understand.

Scriptural kama, but not any frivolous kama. This I say firmly, even between husband and wife. The kama purushartha that is properly taught in the Gita, that is taught in the sixth chapter of the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad: either live that life, directly as the Gita describes; or else directly complete brahmacharya, like Hanuman ji, like Swami Vivekananda. Do not speak of anything in between the two. That is exactly brahmacharya.

## The Evidence of the Scriptures

What does the Mahakalika Vidya say? One who worships constantly during the day, remembering Shiva; and on the other side one who is absorbed in full union all night, but is taking the name of that same Durga, of the Supreme Lord or of the Guru, both of these become equal to Smarahara Shiva. Have we read this or not? If not, read it and come.

I am giving the references: read the Mahakali Stava, the Vidya of Kali, the Bhairava Tantra. I am giving so many references with such authority. Read the Bhagavad Gita, where Shri Krishna himself says:

> **dharmāviruddho bhūteṣu kāmo'smi bharatarṣabha**
> "In beings, I am desire that is not contrary to dharma."
> (Bhagavad Gita 7.11)

> **prajanaś cāsmi kandarpaḥ**
> "Of progenitors, I am Kandarpa, the god of love."
> (Bhagavad Gita 10.28)

So this will be according to the scriptures, won't it? Therefore both are called brahmacharis. Do you understand this much? The problem is with the one in the middle.

## Not the Bedroom, but the Life Reveals It

To see the fruit of brahmacharya, to know whether someone is worthy or not, one does not have to go into the bedroom, whether of a yogi or of a film actor. The poor actor is visible on screen anyway. Where is his test? How much medha shakti he has, how much radiance, how much youthfulness on his face, and how safe women feel in front of him.

And if it is a woman, a man should not feel about her that she is like a prostitute. Even if an apsara stands before him, she should not seem merely a dancing-and-singing woman, should not seem an outsider. Whether woman or man, this is the test of brahmacharya.

## The Intuitive Power of Women

We have so many women seekers with us; they can check. They understand; they have so much intuitive power about whether a person is right or wrong. Yes, perhaps in an affair they may forget this, because then they come down from the intellectual level and may err at the mental and emotional level. The rest of the time, at the level of the Self or the higher levels, God himself has given women a greater sense.

Shri Krishna himself described all the qualities of women in the thirty-fourth verse of the tenth chapter:

> **kīrtiḥ śrīr vāk ca nārīṇāṁ smṛtir medhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā**
> "Among women, I am fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forgiveness."
> (Bhagavad Gita 10.34)

Memory too is greater in them; they have that sense. So would they not understand beforehand that this man is bad, worthless?

## The Example of the Bus Journey

I always say: on a twenty-minute bus journey, if boys are sitting in all the seats by the windows, then even for a twenty-minute trip a girl has been given the intelligence to go and sit beside the least dangerous and safest boy among them. If someone is following her, she immediately recognizes his wrong gaze.

Boys perhaps do not understand this. They go to anyone, to any girl. Very rarely, only a yogi understands the trick that a girl is wrong. But a girl surely understands. This is one more indication of whether this person keeps brahmacharya or lives scriptural kama.

Perhaps, unless told, she may not be able to classify whether this is because of scriptural kama or because of the restraint of brahmacharya. But both states are described as exactly the same. You have no right to separate them.

## Bowing Before a Householder Guru

Up to the level of the rishis and munis, did everyone have marriage, sons and so on, or not? So if there is true observance of yama, then even if a married person is the guru, those in saffron or anyone else must bow their heads before him.

Today all the others, the renunciates and so on, are teaching Shri Krishna's own Gita, aren't they? Why are they teaching it? Then they should have been higher; they are not married, so that would be the qualification. Everyone reads the story of Shri Rama and speaks of Rama Rajya. Why? He was married. Will you say that he was a householder, that he wept crying "Sita, Sita," is this the mark of a renunciate? Then why do you want such a kingdom?

One who does not understand this would do better to speak less on the subject. It is you who have not properly understood yama. You are still a student. I say this with authority. One who has never seen it simply does not know.

## Adi Shankaracharya and Mandana Mishra

When Shankaracharya ji had his debate with Mandana Mishra, Mandana Mishra was married. His wife saw that he had lost, and she asked a direct question on kama itself: "Answer about this."

Then Shankaracharya ji knew that without experience he would not be able to answer. Without taking direct experience himself, through the vidya of parakaya pravesha, entering another body, he had to enter the body of a king who had died, and take that experience, and understand it. It is not that he had no sense of it; if he had, he would have had it through his own body too. But no. I do not want to get into that controversy now.

## A Simple Truth for the Pure of Heart

But you, whose heart is clean, who have sattva guna within, will understand this simple, straightforward point. What the Puranas say, what the Upanishads say, what the Gita says: this is brahmacharya, both.

So for the phalashruti there is no need to go into the bedroom. What did I say? The girl should feel safe, and the reverse is also equally true. There should be medha shakti, there should be radiance, youthfulness should show.

## The Fruit of Brahmacharya: Valor

Bhairava shakti has already come into such a person. Daring comes, because the phalashruti of practicing brahmacharya is said to be that one becomes a hero. So heroic that the daring arises in him to make even the greatest enemy afraid; the enemy bows down on his own.

This is the fruit given for brahmacharya: **the true observance of yama**.
