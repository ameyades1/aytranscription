# Chapter: The Rigid Mind and the Flexible Intellect

## Medha and Durmedha

A person of *medha*, true intelligence, thinks, because all the scriptures are seated within them. All the scriptures have been followed, and radiance has come. Such a person knows that destiny can be changed. Destiny is pre-written, but it can also be written anew. The person of medha knows this.

And what will a person of *durmedha*, a closed and clouded intellect, say? "We are just like this. In our home it has always been this way. Nobody has ever changed until now. Show me some other ideal, then perhaps I will change." Such people of durmedha remain very, very far from the Supreme, far from the Guru. Ganapati is not even near them. Ganapati means the discerning intellect and the steady intellect, and these are nowhere near them.

Have you understood this much? The obstacles in your life are coming for this very reason, because you are rigid, because you are stubborn. Everywhere else, circumstances will keep changing anyway; everyone else's intellect will be different anyway.

## One Room, Two Points of View

Now understand the example I am giving quickly. One person is standing here, another is standing there. They are talking to each other. Tell me, whose words are true and whose are false?

The person on this side says, "Look, there is a wall; look, a door; look, a cupboard; clothes are kept at the back; there are tiles on the floor." And the other says, "No, look, there is a window; outside you can see a tree; there is the curtain." He is describing everything he sees. Now, which of them is right and which is wrong?

What did I say? The first person is seeing the inside of the room from his point of view. The second, from where he stands, is seeing what lies outside the window and outside the door. So who is right and who is wrong? Each one's point of view is correct. What one sees is correct, and what the other sees is also correct.

## When Both Dig In

Now if both hold on to their own view like this, stay rigid, and say, "Nature cannot change, I will accept only my own view, I will do only what I want," what will happen? Will they quarrel or not? These two will never meet in this world.

So what has to be done? One of them will have to change his nature and, for some time, stand in the other's place, won't he? When that happens, he will see that this one was right and that one was right too.

## When Will You See the Other's Life?

When a poor person says, "Sir, I cannot manage this, it does not work for me, I have to come by train every day, my wife does this at home," or when your house help says, "Sir, my husband comes home drunk, creates trouble, kicks me," and you, in your attitude, in the pride of your money, say, "How can you not come? We also go to the office."

Have you lived her life? Does your husband kick you? How much insult, how much abuse she has to bear! Does she have as much money as you? And sometimes you easily tell her, "This is just your nature." When will you look at her life? This is why her intellect works from her angle, and your intellect works from your angle.

## Whoever Wants to Rise Must Be Flexible

That is why whoever wants to rise higher, whoever wants to become a *dharta*, one who upholds and holds everyone together, whoever wants to become a king, an emperor, or a manager in any high position, must be this flexible. He will have to take on every kind of nature. One single nature does not work.

> "You can run on one goal; you cannot run on one nature."

Have you understood this much? The purpose of the Supreme, that one goal, can always go on.

## God in All, but Treatment on Merit

I will see God everywhere, I will see Him even in a dog, but I will treat the dog the way a dog should be treated.

> "See God in all, but treat on merit."

That is, see God in everyone, but treat each one according to their merit. The vision of God everywhere, but not the same conduct with everyone.

God is in the poor too; there is Shani Dev, but the way of treating them is different. The treatment will not be bad, it will only be good, but the method is different. There, volunteers will have to be placed, because Shani is controlled by Mangal (Mars). Mangal means the police, the volunteers, those who maintain order. Then even people of lower consciousness will be able to eat their meal properly.

But if a saint or sadhu of high standing has come, there is no need to make him stand in a line to be called. His honour is different, his time is different. Good food for him, good food for this one, good food for the dog too. God in each one, but a fitting treatment for each one.

## There Is Not Just One Way to Love

Will I have to change this nature of mine or not? Only this much awareness should dawn in me. Am I a dog, that I should behave with everyone the same way, licking this one and licking that one too? There is not just one way to love. Perhaps that is why the word *chatukar* (flatterer, literally "one who licks") came about; he behaves the same way with everyone, because that is the only way he knows.

But you should have the intelligence to act differently in different places, doing what is right where it is right. The more you want to become Ganapati, the more you want to be the lord of the *ganas*, the higher the position you want to reach, the more flexible you will have to make your intellect. This is the essence of the teaching.
