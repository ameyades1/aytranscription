*Why do our festivals, from Ganesh Chaturthi to Diwali, come in this order?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that each festival is a step on the seeker's inner journey, from Ganapati's discernment to Padwa's golden aura. Even Diwali's firecrackers were meant to burst within, as bliss.
