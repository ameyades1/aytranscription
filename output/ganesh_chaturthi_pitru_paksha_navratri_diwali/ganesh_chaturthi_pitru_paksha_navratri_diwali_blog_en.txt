# The Hidden Spiritual Order of the Festivals from Ganpati to Diwali

## Are Holidays Only for Travelling?

India is the land where the most festivals are celebrated and the most holidays are given in a year. We assume they are for fun.

But Gurudev says clearly: this is not a vacation. The real question is, whom is the real holiday actually for?

Just think: why does Ganpati come first? Why does Pitru Paksha (Mahalaya) come only after it? Then Navratri, then Diwali, Narak Chaturdashi, Lakshmi Puja and Padwa, why in this very order?

*Have you ever questioned the order of your festivals?*

## Ganpati: The Awakening of Discerning Wisdom (Vivek Buddhi)

Ganpati means **vivek buddhi**, the inner understanding that recognises right from wrong. Only when it awakens does the journey ahead begin.

If we ourselves are stuck in desire and have not reached **turiya** (the fourth, witness state of consciousness), how will we free the ancestors who were themselves stuck in tamas? Only the one whose ten days of Ganpati have truly been completed within is eligible to free their ancestors.

## Pitru Paksha and Navratri: Freedom from Debt, Mastery over the Gunas

A person freed from **pitru rin** (the debt to one's ancestors) can now gain full control over the three gunas and become their master. They attain power, wealth and wisdom together.

Doing the sadhana of the Durga Saptashati, they become a great **Shri Vidya** sadhak.

## Narak Chaturdashi: The Last Darkness

Even after becoming a sadhak, negativity returns within once more. Just as Rahu comes at the very end of the churning of the ocean to steal the nectar, that is what is called Narakasura here.

That too goes. Then comes Lakshmi Puja, Mahalakshmi Puja, and then the sadhak can reach Om.

## The Firecrackers Were Meant to Burst Within, Not Outside

A firecracker is a symbol of the state of inner joy.

Acharya Ji says that just as laddoos burst in the mind, the firecrackers too were meant to be burst in the mind, not outside.

The scriptures do not speak of outer chemical firecrackers; they describe firecrackers that burst within. We set off plenty outside, but nothing happened within.

*This Diwali, what lit up within you?*

## Padwa: Becoming Worthy of Sharing the Golden Aura

After this the sadhak sees the whole world as one within, and everything begins to look auspicious. Then comes Padwa, when people give one another "gold" (the apta leaf).

This is nothing but the **golden aura**. Now the sadhak becomes entitled to share it. The full secret of Vijayadashami, Diwali and the three and a half muhurtas will be explained in detail at the camp.

## Raksha Bandhan: Brahmacharya Is the Real Protection

Raksha Bandhan means the sadhak begins to see a mother and a sister in all women, not only in their own sister.

They understand that if I do not practise **brahmacharya** (restraint of the senses), one day this same danger will come upon my sister too. Someone will trap her, rob her, and ruin her character and her life.

Acharya Ji explains that protecting your sister does not mean taking up a gun; only by practising brahmacharya yourself can you protect her.

This is spiritual protection, not worldly protection. That is why Raksha Bandhan comes before Ganpati: first, this sacred form must be seen in all women. Bhaubeej in Diwali is a festival of the same spirit. The rishis and munis did not give this order without reason.

## Land of Enjoyment versus Land of Liberation

Abroad there are hardly five or six days of holiday, and they think we do not work. But theirs is a **bhog pradesh** (land of enjoyment); our Bharat is a **moksha pradesh** (land of liberation).

Each of our festivals is a break taken for the journey from **nar to Narayan**, from the human to the Divine. Every holiday is a check: did I reach from nar to Narayan? Then a little worldly work, and then the next festival, the next time for nar to Narayan.

Acharya Ji says that it is not a vacation but an important break from the worldly maya, given for going from nar to Narayan.

Gurudev says: do not blindly imitate the West. If you wish, become the world's teacher and teach them too what the real holiday is for.

## What Is Your Spiritual Age?

The rishis and munis were not mad to keep so many festivals. If we have not understood this, our spiritual age is still only zero to six years. If watching a Krishna Leela serial we only laugh, play and cry, then understand that we are fifteen to seventeen years old.

Gurudev says this to wake us up, and assures us that whoever leaves here having learnt will leave having understood complete Vedanta.

*When the next festival comes, will you treat it as a holiday, or as a step from nar towards Narayan?*

## Key Takeaways

- Ganpati is the awakening of discerning wisdom; it is the doorway to the journey ahead.
- In Pitru Paksha, only one who has risen above desire and tamas can free the ancestors.
- Navratri is the festival of mastery over the three gunas and of Shri Vidya sadhana.
- Narak Chaturdashi is the destruction of the last inner negativity; firecrackers are symbols of inner joy.
- The gold of Padwa is the golden aura, which the sadhak becomes entitled to share.
- The essence of Raksha Bandhan is spiritual protection through brahmacharya.
- Bharat is a land of liberation; every festival is a break for the journey from nar to Narayan, not a vacation.
