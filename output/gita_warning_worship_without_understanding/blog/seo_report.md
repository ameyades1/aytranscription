## 1. Summary

This talk (Ganesh Series, Ep 4) asks whether we know the God we worship. Acharya Ji says the final goal is the formless (nirakar). Every murti is a deliberate code pointing to it. He says that, by the Gita's warning, worship that never grasps this meaning leads only to another birth. His central image is marrying a stranger's photograph. The main intent is informational and reflective: people asking "is idol worship wrong", "मूर्ति पूजा का अर्थ" and "why worship without knowledge is useless". The opportunity is a balanced answer. Most ranking pages either attack or defend murti puja. This talk respects sakar worship as the first step and asks the reader to go beyond it with a living Guru. Its closest neighbours on the site (`only_way_know_god`, `know_your_lord_before_worship`) use the same photograph example, so this post should own the narrower query "worship without understanding" and the Gita angle (see section 7).

## 6. FAQ

**Hindi**
1. **क्या मूर्ति पूजा गलत है?** नहीं। प्रवचन साफ़ कहता है कि साकार की पूजा से मना नहीं किया गया। रामकृष्ण परमहंस, तुकाराम और नामदेव भी साकार पूजते थे, पर अर्थ जानकर। मूर्ति पूजन पहली सीढ़ी है।
2. **मूर्तियाँ क्यों बनाई गईं?** निराकार दिखाई नहीं देता। जिन्होंने उसे अनुभव किया, उन्होंने सबसे निकट की परिभाषा मूर्तियों के रूप में भर दी। हर मूर्ति निराकार की ओर इशारा करती एक उंगली है।
3. **बिना अर्थ जाने पूजा का क्या फल है?** आचार्य जी के अनुसार, गीता के शब्दों में, केवल प्रतीक पकड़कर की गई पूजा से अगले जन्म के सिवा कुछ नहीं मिलता; वे इसे अधोगति कहते हैं।
4. **मूर्ति का अर्थ कौन बताएगा?** प्रवचन के अनुसार कोई जीवित गुरु, क्योंकि जिन संतों ने यह जाना, वे समाधि में चले गए।

**English**
1. **Is idol worship wrong?** No. The discourse says plainly that worship of God with form was never forbidden. Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, Tukaram and Namdev worshipped God with form, but knowing the meaning. Image worship is the first step.
2. **Why were sacred images made?** The formless cannot be seen. Those who experienced it gave the closest description they could a shape, as murtis. Each murti is a finger pointing toward the formless.
3. **What happens when we worship without understanding?** According to Acharya Ji, citing the Gita, worship that clings only to the symbol brings nothing beyond another birth. He calls this adhogati, spiritual downfall.
4. **Who can reveal the meaning of the murti?** The discourse says a living Guru, because the saints who knew it have entered samadhi.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **The "Gita's warning" has no verse reference (Major, from the Sanskrit review).** The recommended titles, the meta and FAQ 3 lean on the Gita. The Gita does not literally say that image worship without understanding leads to adhogati or a lower birth. This matters for SEO because AI overviews often quote takeaway lists.
