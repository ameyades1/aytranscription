*Do you know the God you worship?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that worship without understanding is like marrying a stranger's photograph: it stirs attraction, never union. Every murti points to the formless, and the saints worshipped knowing its meaning.

💭 _You pray daily, but do you know who God truly is?_
