# Chapter: Know Your God

## The Meaning Hidden Behind the Symbol

> "You should know your God."

Know your God. Our ancestors did not shape the form of each deity at random. Whatever qualities we need from a particular deity, they must have designed its form accordingly. Now, if that meaning has not entered your understanding, what is the use of holding on to the symbol alone? No name, no address, just a picture placed in your hands: can you become one with it? Tell me honestly. Today you will be made to think about exactly this.

The truth is that all of us actually have to reach the formless (nirākāra) reality. The final worship is of the formless alone. But it cannot be seen. Even the great ones who went there and returned cannot fully describe it. So whatever definition seemed nearest to them, they must have poured all of it into the forms of the murtis.

## A Sign Placed Thoughtfully Behind Every Murti

Mother Saraswati is the goddess of knowledge. Brahma ji is the creator, and Saraswati is with him. Brahma ji was shown as one aged in wisdom, an old man with a beard. Knowledge of all four directions is needed, which is why he has four faces. I hope you now understand how meaning has been placed behind every murti with careful thought.

All the other deities are shown with perfectly sculpted bodies, the abdominal muscles perfectly defined, because the idea of youth, vigour and strength had to be shown. But poor Ganapati is shown with a rounded belly. This means that the form of each deity must have been designed according to the qualities we need from it.

Its height, its colour, how many hands and feet it has, its clothing, the colour of the clothing, what is offered to it: in all of these they must surely have placed some signs, some symbols, for reaching the formless. Isn't that right?

## Worship Without Knowing the Essence: The Gita's Warning

If you do not understand this meaning, what is the use of clinging to the symbol alone? Tell me just this. You may sit worshipping all the murtis in the world your whole life, and still you will gain nothing except another birth. And even that next birth may well be one step lower.

I am not the one saying this; the Bhagavad Gita itself says it. Shri Krishna tells us that it leads to a downward fall (adhogati). Worship and rituals performed without knowing their essence have no meaning.

## Marriage to a Mere Picture?

Understand this through an example. Suppose you have to get married. I am speaking of oneness, of becoming one. There is the form of Ardhanarishvara; husband and wife are called each other's half (ardhāṅga).

Now suppose you were given only a photograph from a marriage proposal, or someone brought you a statue of a girl, and said, "Just live with this picture." Who is it, whose picture is it, nobody knows. I searched the internet, or took a social media profile, and handed you a picture of some random girl or boy and said, "This is your husband" or "This is your wife." Will that work? Tell me that first.

No name, no address, only a picture. Dark or fair, you do not know; you know absolutely nothing about the person. Such a picture is placed in front of you: can you become one with it? Tell me honestly. Can there be love with it, can there be a marriage?

And if someone does fall in love with the picture itself, is that not madness? "I fell in love with a picture, and I even got married to the picture": is this sentence right or wrong?

## Beyond the Picture: Knowing, Meeting and Oneness

Arre, you have understood the picture; now ask for the biodata. After asking for the biodata, understand who this girl is, what she is, what she does. Then the question will come: where does she live? Who are her parents? Who are the family members?

Then will you go to meet them or not? Some day you will go to meet them. After she is attained, you will marry her and bring her to your home, or, if you are the girl, you will go there. And from that the next generation will come. Now if you stay stuck on the picture, will that work?

## Have We Stopped at the Level of the Picture?

You will be made to think about this, because I have to give you knowledge (vidyā). I do not want to give you just a mantra or two; all that will come at the end, in sādhanā.

So do you not feel that all of us have stopped at the level of that picture, and we believe we have been married to the picture itself? Whose picture it is, we do not know; what picture it is, we do not know; no biodata, no idea where they live, and yet we are married!

Whom do you love? "I love Ganapati." Whom do you love? "Lakshmi ji." Who was Hanuman ji? Where was he? What is his true form? Never seen, not known, nothing understood.

> "That is your foolishness, believe it or not."

This is your foolishness, believe it or not. Know your God. First you should at least understand what it is that you are asking from.

## Why Did the Saints Worship Form?

Those who attained were all saints and renunciates. Did they worship only this, or had they realised that these were symbols? Go and ask them. But who will tell you this? Only a living guru will tell you, because those saints have gone into samādhi. So look into their life stories as well.

In truth, they reach that step after which they no longer need murti worship at all. Murti worship is only the first step.

Then you will see why Ramakrishna Paramahamsa was worshipping Kali. Then you will see why our Tukaram or Namdev kept calling "Pandurang, Pandurang." When did we ever say, do not worship God with form (sākāra)? But they did it knowing its meaning. That is what I am talking about. You too should know that meaning.

## The Difference Between Attraction and Love

I am not saying, do not look at your beloved's picture. If you are going to marry and you know all the references, then does looking at the picture have some meaning or not? But search the internet for any girl's name and find some picture: is that real love? Is there any scope of love in it?

At most there can be infatuation, just an attraction. It will not be love, and union will certainly not happen.

## Only One God

God is one. In Hinduism too there is only one God. It is that one, same, single Lord. Then what are these thirty-three koti? What are all these different deities? This question is left with you to reflect on further.
