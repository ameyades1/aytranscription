# Chapter: Every Human Being Is Spiritual by Nature

## Not Two Worlds, Only One

Many people feel that the spiritual and the worldly are two separate worlds. You have met such people, haven't you, to whom these seem like two different worlds? You will have to tell them that there are no two worlds. There is only one world, and that is the spiritual world.

> "There are no two worlds; there is only one world, and that is the spiritual world."

## The Meaning of a Two Hundred Percent Life

We say: if you live, live a two hundred percent life, one hundred percent spiritual and one hundred percent worldly. People take this to mean twelve hours in the office and twelve hours in the temple. It is not so. These two hundred percents lie one over the other; they overlap completely.

If you are to marry and that seems to you to be the world, then that very thing is one hundred percent your spirituality too. A goddess has come into the home; this is the pair of Shiva and Parvati. Then whatever I have to do, it is all for the Lord in some form, and spirituality covers it twenty-four hours a day.

If you do business, the customer is God. He has come bringing Lakshmi; he is Vishnu himself. So how must I treat him? This is Shri Vidya applied in life. In this way, twenty-four hours a day, every day, my life is nothing but spirituality.

## Dutiful Action Is Dharma

In truth, whenever I perform any duty, dutiful action itself means dharma. If I am a medical doctor treating a patient, I am doing dharma, am I not? If I loot people, I am doing adharma. A person may be aware of this or unaware; the point remains.

## Even the Lustful Person Seeks the Self

A lustful person, extremely lustful, gripped by desire, when he longs for a woman, is not aware that he too is longing for that same principle of the Self. It is not merely the body that he likes. Whether she is his girlfriend, or he goes to a brothel, or anywhere else, even the lustful person, out of ignorance, loves the soul and not the body.

For if tomorrow that woman were gone, could he love her dead body? He is unaware. He does not know whom he is really loving. That power by which the woman moves and behaves as he wants: it is that power he actually desires.

> "Even the lustful person, out of ignorance, loves the soul and not the body."

## A Simple Test

The test is simple and straightforward. If that power departs, will he love the dead body? Answer this question. He will not be able to. Nor will a wife be able to love her husband's dead body. This is what Adi Shankaracharya says in Bhaja Govindam: a person cannot love a dead body.

That is why I always keep saying: bring that woman's flesh and bones and place them before him, and then show me your love. Put her bone in your hand, and show me your love. Her excrement and urine, filled in a box and handed to you: go on, love this. Her blood, filled in seven or eight bottles and given to you: go on, wasn't this her?

Join all her bones into a skeleton and hang it under the fan, and let it start swaying in the breeze. Imagine it: that same girlfriend, now only a frame of bones. Now love her. Can he? It takes a moment for this to sink in, which is why I made you visualize it.

## Living Spiritually Without Knowing It

Shankaracharya says that a person loves only the living power, the soul. The only difference is that, out of ignorance, when things go according to his wishes he calls it love, and when they do not he calls it hatred. He does not understand this.

> "Even the lowest person keeps listening only to the inner self, and unknowingly keeps living a spiritual life."

So even the lowest person keeps listening to the soul, the inner self. Unknowingly, he keeps living a spiritual life. Is this much clear?
