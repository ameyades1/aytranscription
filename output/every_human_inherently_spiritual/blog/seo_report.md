## 1. Summary

The talk challenges the belief that life has two halves, one spiritual and one worldly. Acharya Ji says there is only one world, and it is spiritual: live "two hundred percent", one hundred percent worldly and one hundred percent spiritual, fully overlapping. In marriage the couple are Shiva and Parvati; in business the customer is Vishnu bringing Lakshmi, which he calls applied Shri Vidya. Dharma is duty done rightly (the healing doctor vs the exploiting one). Even a lustful man unknowingly loves the Self and its living power, not the body, as the corpse and skeleton test shows (Bhaja Govindam); so no one stands outside the spiritual, and the only difference is awareness. The main intent is **informational with a life-problem angle**: people search "spiritual vs worldly life", "how to balance spiritual and material life", "संसार में रहकर अध्यात्म कैसे करें". Ranking pages are mostly generic work-life-balance or "be spiritual while working" listicles. This post's distinct angle is the "200 percent" overlap (not a time split) and concrete examples (marriage, customer, doctor). No festival peak; evergreen.

## 6. FAQ

1. **क्या आध्यात्मिक और सांसारिक जीवन अलग हैं? / Are spiritual and worldly life separate?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार नहीं: संसार केवल एक है, और वह आध्यात्मिक है। जिसे हम सांसारिक कहते हैं, वही पूरी तरह आध्यात्मिक भी है। / According to Acharya Ji, no: there is only one world, and it is spiritual. Whatever we call worldly is, at the same time, wholly spiritual.
2. **दो सौ प्रतिशत जीवन क्या है? / What does living two hundred percent mean?**
   सौ प्रतिशत सांसारिक और सौ प्रतिशत आध्यात्मिक, एक-दूसरे के ऊपर रखे हुए; इसका अर्थ बारह घंटे ऑफिस और बारह घंटे मंदिर नहीं है। / Being one hundred percent worldly and one hundred percent spiritual, fully overlapping; it does not mean twelve hours at the office and twelve at the temple.
3. **व्यापार में आध्यात्मिकता कैसे लाएं? / How can business become spiritual?**
   ग्राहक को भगवान मानें: वह लक्ष्मी लेकर आया है, वह स्वयं विष्णु है। आचार्य जी इसे जीवन में उतारी गई श्री विद्या कहते हैं। / See the customer as God: he brings Lakshmi and is Vishnu himself. Acharya Ji calls this Shri Vidya brought into daily life.
4. **धर्म का सरल अर्थ क्या है? / What is dharma in simple terms?**
   कर्तव्य कर्म, यानी अपना कर्तव्य ठीक से करना। रोगी का उपचार करता डॉक्टर धर्म करता है, लूटने वाला अधर्म। / Doing your duty rightly. A doctor who heals a patient practises dharma; one who exploits the patient practises adharma.
5. **क्या हर मनुष्य आध्यात्मिक है? / Is every human being spiritual?**
   हां: हर कोई, अनजाने में भी, जीवित शक्ति यानी आत्मा से ही प्रेम करता है; मृत शरीर से कोई प्रेम नहीं कर पाता। अंतर केवल जागरूकता का है। / Yes: everyone, even unknowingly, loves only the living power, the Self; no one can love a corpse. The only difference is awareness.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Love/aversion teaching attributed to Shankaracharya (Sanskrit review issue 1, Minor, not yet applied).** No specific work is named, and Bhaja Govindam does not say this; the idea matches Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 2.4.5 (Yajnavalkya to Maitreyi). Apply the review's wording ("वेदांत की दृष्टि से, जैसा शंकराचार्य जी समझाते हैं" / "As Shankaracharya explains in the light of Vedanta"). The SEO text (snippet, FAQ 5, schema) gives the teaching without naming a source; keep it that way and do not write "Shankaracharya says in Bhaja Govindam that we love only the Self".
2. **Bhaja Govindam paraphrase (review issue 2, Minor).** The verse says the wife fears the body once the breath leaves, not that she cannot love it. Apply the review's closer wording if desired. FAQ 5 says "no one can love a corpse", which is the talk's own point and not presented as a quote of the verse.
3. **आध्यात्म → अध्यात्म (review issue 3, Minor).** The SEO text uses the correct noun अध्यात्म (secondary keyword "संसार में रहकर अध्यात्म", title option 3, schema `about`). No link anchor contains the misspelt form, so applying the correction does not break any link.
4. **आत्म तत्व → आत्मतत्त्व (review issue 4, optional).** Not used in any SEO text or link anchor.
5. **Lust example and body imagery.** The talk's strongest hook is the lustful man and the skeleton image. Do not use "lust", "कामी" or the corpse/skeleton image in titles, metas, thumbnails or social cards; it would invite the wrong clicks and misrepresent a talk whose point is that everyone is spiritual. The link to `truly_desire_or_infatuation` uses the neutral anchor "loves the Atman, not the body".
6. **"Duty is dharma" simplification.** The blog defines dharma as duty performed rightly, a fair simplification (review: checked and correct). The secondary keyword "duty is dharma" is used only in that sense; do not expand it in SEO text into a general definition of dharma.
7. **"Applied Shri Vidya".** This is Acharya Ji's own framing, presented as such in the blog. FAQ 3 attributes it to him; do not present it as a classical definition of Shri Vidya, and do not target Shri Vidya queries with this post (they belong to `become_billionaire_shree_vidya_secrets`).
8. **Thumbnail vs title.** The thumbnail says "You Are More Spiritual Than You Realise"; the recommended title leads with the spiritual-vs-worldly question, which the page answers directly, and title option 2 carries the video's "every human is inherently spiritual" phrase. Both are faithful.

Category note: `gallery_topics.txt` places the talk under `dharma`, copied as is. All four tags already exist in other talks' seo.json (Advaita Vedanta, Shri Vidya, Karma Yoga, Duty / अद्वैत वेदांत, श्री विद्या, कर्मयोग, कर्तव्य); "Dharma" is not used as a tag because it restates the category.
