*Why wait until sixty for spirituality?*

Acharya Upendra Ji asks who can guarantee your later years. No one can promise a fifteen-year-old they will live past fifty-four, so the spirit of renunciation can begin at twenty-five.

💭 _If life comes with no guarantee, why put off your spiritual life?_
