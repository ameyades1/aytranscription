## 6. FAQ

**1. चार आश्रम कौन से हैं? / What are the four ashramas?**
हिंदी: ब्रह्मचर्य (सीखने का चरण), गृहस्थ, वानप्रस्थ (आसक्ति छोड़ने का अभ्यास) और संन्यास। परंपरा सौ वर्ष की आयु को पच्चीस-पच्चीस वर्ष के चार भागों में बाँटती है।
English: Brahmacharya (the student stage), Grihastha (householder), Vanaprastha (practising the release of attachment) and Sannyasa (renunciation). Tradition divides a hundred-year life into four parts of about twenty-five years each.

**2. वानप्रस्थ का असली अर्थ क्या है? / What does Vanaprastha really mean?**
हिंदी: आचार्य जी के अनुसार इसका अर्थ विवाह या कार्य का अंत नहीं, आसक्ति छोड़ने का अभ्यास है: इक्यावन के बाद और न बढ़ाना, रुचि को घटने देना, और साठ तक दूसरों को सौंपने की तैयारी।
English: In Acharya Ji's words it does not mean married life or work ends; it means practising detachment: stop expanding after fifty-one, let interest fade, and be ready to hand things over by sixty.

**3. क्या वर्ण बदल सकता है? / Can a person's varna change?**
हिंदी: गीता ४.१३ में भगवान कहते हैं कि चारों वर्ण गुण और कर्म के विभाग से रचे गए हैं। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि जब आधार गुण और कर्म है, तो वर्ण भी बदल सकता है, जैसे वाल्या वाल्मीकि बने।
English: In Bhagavad Gita 4.13 the Lord says the four varnas were created by the division of qualities and actions. Acharya Ji reasons that if varna rests on these, it can change, as when Valya became Valmiki.

**4. संन्यास वृत्ति क्या है? / What is sannyasa vritti?**
हिंदी: घर छोड़ना नहीं, बल्कि हर कार्य करते हुए भीतर से अनासक्त रहना। आचार्य जी कहते हैं: संन्यासी मत बनो, पर संन्यास वृत्ति से रहो, और यह पच्चीस से ही हो सकता है।
English: Not leaving home, but staying inwardly unattached while doing every task. Acharya Ji says: do not become a sannyasi, but live with this attitude, and you can begin at twenty-five.

**5. एक ही दिन में चारों वर्ण कैसे? / How can we live all four varnas in one day?**
हिंदी: साधना और प्रार्थना में हम ब्राह्मण, सुरक्षा के कार्य में क्षत्रिय, आजीविका कमाते समय वैश्य और विश्राम या सामान्य सेवा में शूद्र हैं। चारों से ऊपर उठना मोक्ष का मार्ग है।
English: In prayer and sadhana we are Brahmins, when protecting we are Kshatriyas, when earning we are Vaishyas, and when resting or doing ordinary service we are Shudras. Rising above all four is the path to liberation.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Prahlada "on his own" (Major, review item 1).** The Bhagavata says Prahlada's devotion came from Narada's teaching heard in the womb. Recommendation: apply the review's wording; never use "on his own" in snippets or social cards.
2. **Gita 4.13 Hindi meaning drops "मया" (Major, review item 2).** Recommendation: apply the Gita Press meaning ("मेरे द्वारा रचे गए हैं") before targeting the verse keyword "चातुर्वर्ण्यं मया सृष्टं अर्थ".
3. **Half-verse not marked (review item 3).** Mark "(४.१३, पूर्वार्ध)" / "(4.13, first half)" or quote the full verse; the second half (the Lord as non-doer) should not be implied away in a verse-meaning snippet.
4. **IAST anusvara (review item 4).** Use "ṃ" (cāturvarṇyaṃ ... sṛṣṭaṃ) for consistency with standard IAST.
5. **Valya and Narada (review item 5).** The robber-to-sage story is traditional, not in the Valmiki Ramayana, and versions differ on who turned him. Present it as the popular account; FAQ 3 avoids naming Narada.
6. **25-year blocks as "scripture" (review item 6).** The exact 25/50/75/100 split is a convention from a 100-year life, not text in Manusmriti. Use "tradition" wording; FAQ 1 already does.
7. **"All four ashramas for every varna" (review item 7).** This is Acharya Ji's teaching, not the classical Dharmashastra rule. Attribute it to him in any SEO copy; do not present it as the scriptural position.
8. **"Brahmacharya and sannyasa are brothers" (review item 8).** No source found; attribute to Acharya Ji, or cite Jabala Upanishad 4 for renouncing straight from brahmacharya.
9. **"Brahma bhava" in English (review item 9).** Reads as the deity Brahma; gloss as "the sense of Brahman".
10. **English gloss in the Hindi text (review item 10).** "(renunciate attitude)" should be a Hindi gloss such as "(भीतर की अनासक्ति)", since "संन्यास वृत्ति क्या है" is a target keyword.
11. **Varna and caste sensitivity.** The post reads varna by guna and action, not birth, and mentions a fifth "forbidden" group and bandits. Keep titles, metas and social cards on the guna-based, changeable reading; never use "caste" framing, rank varnas, or quote the "forbidden side" line out of context, which could read as an attack on a community.
12. **Video title "Unlock Your Full Potential".** The talk does not offer a self-help method to unlock potential; it is about varna, ashrama and not postponing spiritual life. The SEO titles deliberately avoid the "full potential" promise; keep it out of WordPress copy apart from the video's own name.
