# Chapter: A True Disciple Is One Whose Knowledge Becomes Action

## Become Like Guru and Disciple

The knowledge of Ganesha is being taught; the Atharvashirsha is being studied. So do not stop merely at the relationship of speaker and listener, and do not stop merely at giver and holder (the one who receives and holds). Become like the anūchāna and the disciple.

By becoming a disciple I can become a giver, and if through that I become a listener, it is even better. If by becoming a listener I become a speaker, that is good. It is said:

> "The speaker is the best listener."

Whatever I myself speak, I keep listening to it very carefully. I say nothing casually. Later I remember what I said. Some detail about a person, my child, I may forget a little sometimes, but everything I have spoken, I remember. So, as a guru or as such a disciple, do what is to be done. Is this much clear to all of you? Then we can move ahead.

## What the Guru Should Be, What the Disciple Should Be

In this there is a prayer, "Protect me." That is, this guru-disciple pair is to be protected, when I myself reach that state.

So what should the guru be like? The guru should first be a **sattvic speaker**, a **sattvic giver** and a **sattvic anūchāna**. And the disciple? A **sattvic listener**, a **sattvic holder** and a **sattvic disciple**.

## The Journey of the Guru's Words: From the Ear to Samskara

What had I said? The guru's words should first go into the ears. Then into the head, meaning they should be understood rightly. Then they should descend into the heart, then mix into your blood. Then they should come into kriti, that is, conduct, and finally become samskara forever.

You must become such a disciple. Only then will all obstacles go away.

## Shruti Always Ends in Action

> "Shruti always ends in action."

Shruti means the Veda, and the Veda is the process of knowing. And "you have known" is said only when that knowing comes into action. Is this much understood?

I learned engineering. Alright? What use did I make of it? Nothing. The engineering books went to the scrap pile. Or I kept them on my head: "The computer is in my head." What does that mean? It is not about carrying it on your head and wandering here and there. Knowledge should be there, and then its action. Did you do some programming for anyone? I learned about air conditioners (AC); did I repair people's ACs? Do I myself even know how? One did study to be a doctor.

## The Test of Higher Vedanta: Control of Anger

This Shruti is of a low level. Now let us speak of higher Vedanta. Anger should not arise; you heard this. Did it turn into action? "From today my peaceful speech has begun": when this happens, when it has become Atharva, then you have learned the Shruti. Merely remaining a listener is not enough.

It is understood, it is now clear: control your anger. But if it never turned into action, that knowledge is useless.

## The Right Meaning of "What Did You Learn Today?"

Whenever I ask, "What did you all learn today?", people immediately take out their notes: I read this, I read that. I did not say, revise what I taught, what I explained. What do I ask? What did you apply immediately? What changed in life today itself?

## Experiencing One Ovi Is Enough

Dnyaneshwar Mauli says:

> **eka tarī ovī anubhavāvī**
> "Experience at least one ovi."
> (Sant Dnyaneshwar)

That is, at least experience one ovi (verse). Out of what I am saying, if you have brought even one shloka, one sentence into life, tell me. That is more than enough.

This does not come from reciting crores of mantras, from reading scriptures, from hearing many discourses of ever new mahatmas. They are saying it again and again until their throats go dry. So what is to be done? Start applying one lesson in life. There is Shri Vidya, isn't there? Start applying it. Then you will truly become rich.
