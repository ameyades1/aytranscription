## 1. Summary

In this short session from the Ganapati Atharvashirsha teaching, Acharya Ji asks when you can truly say you have learned something. He moves from the speaker-listener and giver-receiver bonds to the anuchana-disciple bond, traces the Guru's words from the ear to the head, heart, blood, conduct and finally the samskaras, and teaches that shruti always ends in action: knowledge that never changes behaviour (for example, anger) is wasted. "What did you learn today?" is answered by one thing applied today, and living even one ovi is enough. The main intent is informational with a strong self-reflection angle ("what makes a true disciple?", "why don't teachings change me?"). The opportunity: "true disciple" and "सच्चा शिष्य" queries are mostly answered by generic quote lists; the six-stage journey of the Guru's words is a clear, snippet-friendly framework that only this talk offers.

## 6. FAQ

**Hindi**
1. **सच्चा शिष्य कौन है?** जिसके भीतर गुरु के शब्द कान से सिर, हृदय, रक्त, कृति और अंत में संस्कार तक पहुंचते हैं।
2. **कैसे पता चले कि मैंने सच में कुछ सीखा?** जब वह कर्म में उतर आए। जैसे क्रोध न करने की शिक्षा तभी सीखी मानी जाएगी जब आपका वचन शांत हो जाए।
3. **"आज क्या सीखा?" का सही उत्तर क्या है?** नोट्स दोहराना नहीं, बल्कि यह बताना कि आज के आज क्या लागू किया और क्या बदला।
4. **"एक तरी ओवी अनुभवावी" का अर्थ क्या है?** कम से कम एक ओवी (पद) का अनुभव करो। एक वाक्य भी जीवन में उतर जाए, तो वह पर्याप्त से अधिक है।

**English**
1. **What makes a true disciple?** One in whom the Guru's words travel from the ear to the head, heart, blood and conduct, and finally settle into the samskaras.
2. **How do I know I have really learned something?** When it shows up in action. A teaching such as not getting angry counts as learned only on the day you begin to speak calmly.
3. **What is the right answer to "What did you learn today?"** Not your notes, but what you put into practice right away and what changed in you today.
4. **What does "ek tari ovi anubhavavi" mean?** Live out the experience of at least one ovi (verse). If even one sentence enters your life, it is more than enough.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Attribution of "एक तरी ओवी अनुभवावी" (critical, from the Sanskrit review).** The line closes Sant Namdev's abhang in praise of the Jnaneshwari ("नामा म्हणे ग्रंथ श्रेष्ठ ज्ञानेश्वरी। एक तरी ओवी अनुभवावी॥"); the blog credits Sant Dnyaneshwar. The talk itself says "ज्ञानेश्वर माउली बोलते हैं", so keep Acharya Ji's words but correct the source line: "(संत नामदेव, ज्ञानेश्वरी के विषय में)" / "(Sant Namdev, on the Jnaneshwari)", as the review suggests. Until then, the FAQ, keywords and snippets here give the line's meaning without naming its author.
