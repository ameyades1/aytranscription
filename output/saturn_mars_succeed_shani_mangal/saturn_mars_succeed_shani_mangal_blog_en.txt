# The Real Secret of Brakes: What Saturn and Mars Teach Us About Success

Have you ever wondered whether the things that hold you back in life might be there to move you forward? In this discourse, Acharya Upendra Ji opens a deep secret with a simple question: why does a car have brakes?

The answer holds the link between Mars and Saturn, and the meaning behind Lord Ganesha's two forms, as the one who places obstacles and the one who removes them.

## Mars Means Speed, but Speed Alone Is Not Enough

In astrology, **Mars** stands for speed and energy. Yet speed by itself does not bring success, Acharya Ji says. You need control along with it.

His example is very direct. One person types a hundred and twenty words a minute, but makes a hundred and nineteen mistakes. There is speed, but no control. Another types three words a minute with perfect accuracy. There is control, but no speed.

Both fall short. Success comes into your hands only when **speed and control** work together.

*What is missing in your life right now: speed, or control?*

## A Train With No Stations

Picture a train that leaves Kashmir and never stops anywhere, not even at Kanyakumari, the southern tip of India. It runs straight on into the sea. Would such a train serve any purpose?

Stations matter as much as the tracks. Acharya Ji says these "stations" of life, its obstacles, are necessary to break the **ego**. When the ego breaks, you become grounded, and your feet stay firmly on the earth.

## Why Do Brakes Exist? A Surprising Answer

Acharya Ji asked his listeners: why does a car have brakes? The answers came quickly. To stop, to stay in control, to stay safe, to slow down.

Acharya Ji smiled. They were excellent answers, he said, but not one of them was truly right.

The real purpose of brakes, he explains, is to let us drive fast and drive well.

You press the accelerator all the way down only when you trust that the brakes will work.

## When You Learn the Brakes Have Failed

Now imagine that before you start the vehicle, someone tells you its brakes have failed. It could be a bicycle, a scooter, a boat or an aeroplane.

What happens? At first you will be afraid even to touch it. If you do drive it, you will go with great caution. The accelerator itself will frighten you, and **you will start to fear speed itself.**

That is the heart of this example. The power that can stop you is the very thing that gives you the courage to move ahead.

## Lord Ganesha: Creator and Remover of Obstacles

Speed belongs to Mars, and the force that always balances it is **Saturn**. Acharya Ji says that if Lord Ganesha were absent from your life, you could never move ahead at full speed. Something always keeps you in check, and God stands behind it.

This is why Lord Ganesha's story calls him both **Vighnakarta** (the one who places obstacles) and **Vighnaharta** (the one who removes obstacles). The two forms are two sides of a single truth.

## Who Are the Brakes in Your Life?

Who holds us back in life? Acharya Ji answers: the Guru, who takes away our ego. In the early years, our parents and schoolteachers. Later, our experiences, our enemies and the crises we face.

Everything we call negative, he says, is not negative at all. These are the brakes of our life.

Because these brakes exist, you can pick up speed. When a crisis arrives, a voice rises from within: "Yes, I will fight. I have to prove I can do it."

*Could the difficulty you see as a roadblock today be the very thing giving you the courage to move forward?*

## Without Challenges, There Is No Momentum

Now look at it the other way. There is no competition, no fear and no worry from any side. Everything is comfortable, prosperous, settled and unmoving. What happens then? You never pick up speed, and the car goes nowhere.

That is why brakes are necessary, and why Lord Ganesha is necessary. Even before we drive a car, this is what we check: do the brakes work?

## Saturn and Mars Complete Each Other

Astrology shows this relationship beautifully. Mars is exalted in **Makara** (Capricorn), and Capricorn is the sign ruled by Saturn. Saturn brings obstacles and moves slowly, "śanaiḥ śanaiḥ" (slowly, step by step). Mars brings speed. Each one completes the other.

Acharya Ji explains that brakes exist to give you the nerve to accelerate at full speed.

When Lord Ganesha removes obstacles, he is **Mangalmurti** (the embodiment of auspiciousness), who carries you forward at great speed. When he places obstacles in your way, he acts like **Shanidev** (Saturn as a deity).

## Key Takeaways

- Success needs both **speed (Mars)** and **control (Saturn)**.
- Brakes exist not to stop you but to give you the courage to move at full speed.
- Like a train with no stations, a life without obstacles has no direction; obstacles break the ego and keep us grounded.
- The Guru, parents, teachers, experiences, enemies and crises are not negative; they are the brakes of our life.
- Where there is no challenge, there is no momentum.
- Lord Ganesha both places and removes obstacles; Mars being exalted in Capricorn shows how Saturn and Mars complete each other.
