# The Real Secret of Brakes: Learning Success from Saturn and Mars

Have you ever wondered whether the things that stop you in life might be there precisely to move you forward? In this discourse, Acharya Upendra Ji opens a deep secret with a simple question: what are brakes in a vehicle for?

Hidden in the answer is the relationship between Mars and Saturn, and the meaning of both forms of Ganapati, Vighnakarta and Vighnaharta.

## Mars Means Speed, but Speed Alone Is Not Enough

In astrology, **Mars (Mangal)** is the symbol of speed and energy. But Acharya Ji says that speed alone does not bring success. Along with speed, you need control.

His example is very simple. Someone types one hundred and twenty words a minute, but with one hundred and nineteen mistakes. There is speed, but no control. Another types three words a minute with one hundred percent accuracy. There is control, but no speed.

Both are incomplete. Only when **speed and control** come together is success in your hands.

*What is missing in your life: speed, or control?*

## A Train Without Stations

Imagine a train that leaves Kashmir and never stops anywhere. Not even at Kanyakumari. It goes straight into the sea. Does such a train have any meaning?

Stations are as necessary as the tracks. Acharya Ji says that these "stations" of life, the obstacles, are necessary for the **ahaṅkāra** (ego) to break. The breaking of the ego means grounding, your feet staying on the ground.

## What Are Brakes For? A Surprising Answer

Acharya Ji asked the listeners: what are brakes in a vehicle for? The answers came: for stopping, for control, for safety, for reducing speed.

Acharya Ji smiled. Very good answers, but not one of them was actually right.

> "The real purpose of brakes is that you can drive the vehicle well and at high speed."

You can press the accelerator with full force only when you trust that the brakes work.

## When You Learn the Brakes Have Failed

Now imagine that before you even start the vehicle, someone tells you its brakes have failed. Whether it is a bicycle, a scooter, a boat or an aeroplane, anything at all.

What happens? First, you will be afraid even to touch it. And if you do drive it, you will drive very cautiously. You will be afraid of the accelerator itself. **You will begin to fear speed itself.**

This is the essence of the example. The power that stops you is what gives you the courage to move forward.

## Ganapati: Creator of Obstacles and Remover of Obstacles

Speed is Mars, and its complementary force is always **Saturn (Shani)**. Acharya Ji says that if there is no Ganapati in your life, you will not be able to move forward at high speed. Something keeps you under control, and behind it is the Supreme Lord.

That is why, in the character of Ganapati, he is called both **Vighnakarta** (the one who brings obstacles) and **Vighnaharta** (the one who removes obstacles). Both forms are two sides of a single truth.

## Who Are the Brakes in Your Life?

Acharya Ji asks: who are the ones who stop us in life? Our Guru, who takes away our ego. In the beginning, our parents and school teachers. Then our experiences, our adversaries, the crises of our life.

> "All these things that we call negative are not negative things; they are the brakes of our life."

They are brakes, and that is why you can pick up speed. When a crisis comes, a voice rises from within: Yes, I will fight, I have to prove it by doing it.

*The difficulty you see as an obstacle today, could it be giving you the courage to move forward?*

## Where There Is No Crisis, There Is No Speed

Now think of the opposite. No competition, no fear, no worry from anywhere. Everything happy and prosperous, stable and still. What happens then? You will not pick up speed at all. The vehicle will not move forward at all.

That is why brakes are necessary, and Ganapati is necessary. Even before driving, this is what we check: do the brakes work?

## Saturn and Mars: Complements of Each Other

In astrology this relationship shows itself beautifully. Mars is exalted in **Capricorn (Makara)**, and Capricorn is the sign of Saturn. Saturn brings obstacles and moves slowly (śanaiḥ śanaiḥ). Mars gives speed. Each completes the other.

> "The purpose of brakes is to give you the courage to accelerate at full speed."

When Ganapati removes obstacles, he is Mangalmurti, carrying you forward at high speed. And when he brings obstacles, he is like Shani Dev.

## Key Takeaways

- Success needs both **speed (Mars)** and **control (Saturn)**.
- The real purpose of brakes is not to stop you, but to give you the courage to move forward at full speed.
- Like a train without stations, a life without obstacles has no direction; obstacles break the ego and keep us grounded.
- The Guru, parents, teachers, experiences, adversaries and crises are not negative; they are the brakes of our life.
- Where there is no challenge, there is no speed either.
- Ganapati is both Vighnakarta and Vighnaharta; the exaltation of Mars in Capricorn shows the complementary bond of Saturn and Mars.
