# Chapter: Speed and Control, the Secret of Mars and Saturn

## Mars Means Speed

What does Mars mean? Speed. One who has speed, and along with speed also has a means of stopping, will also have control. And when speed and control both come together, success is in your hands.

I always give an example. Someone was asked, what is your typing speed? He said, one hundred and twenty words a minute. Very good, the speed is excellent. And how many mistakes? One hundred and nineteen. So is there anything good in it? No, because there is no control in it at all.

In the same way another person was asked, what is your typing speed? Three words a minute. Very good, but what is the accuracy? One hundred percent. So is there any benefit in such control either? There is no speed in it at all.

That is why in life both speed and control are necessary. That speed is being Mangalmurti, and along with it there must also be restraint. Both Vighnakarta and Vighnaharta, the one who creates obstacles and the one who removes them, are necessary.

## A Train Without Stations

If someone asks what the benefit of brakes in a vehicle is, remember the example of the train. If a train had no stations, if it never stopped anywhere, would it work? A train would leave Kashmir, not stop even at Kanyakumari, and go straight into the sea. Does such a train have any meaning?

So obstacles are needed too. He who creates obstacles and he who removes them are one and the same. Stations are necessary too. We said earlier that they are necessary for the ego to break. The breaking of the ego means your grounding, your feet staying on the ground.

## What Are Brakes For?

Now the question is: what are brakes in a vehicle for? You will have a car, a scooter, or at the very least a bicycle. What are its brakes for?

The listeners answered: for stopping, for keeping the vehicle under control, for controlling speed, for safety, for avoiding obstacles, for reducing speed. Very good answers. But not one of them was actually the right answer.

> "The real purpose of brakes is that you can drive the vehicle well and at high speed."

So that you can press the accelerator at full speed. You can accelerate only when you know the vehicle has brakes. The moment you understand that the brakes are not working, you will not be able to press the accelerator at all. Think about it.

## When You Are Told the Brakes Have Failed

Suppose someone tells you that the brakes of this vehicle do not work. What happens then? Whether it is a bicycle, a scooter, a rocket, an aeroplane, a boat, anything at all, its brakes have failed. You have not even started the vehicle yet, and you have been told its brakes do not work. Now what happens?

First, you will be afraid even to touch that vehicle. Second, you will not drive it with speed; you will drive it very cautiously. You will be afraid of the accelerator itself. You will be afraid of speed itself, won't you?

## The Complementary Force of Mars: Saturn

Speed is Mars, but its complementary force is always Saturn. That is why, if there is no Ganapati in your life, you will not be able to move forward at high speed. Something or other keeps you under control, holds you back. Behind it is the Supreme Lord.

Remember this example I am giving you; it is extremely important. There are brakes, and that is why the courage to press the accelerator comes. That is why, in the character of Ganapati, he is called both Vighnakarta and Vighnaharta. I am giving you this example of the car to explain exactly this.

## Who Are the Brakes of Life?

Do you know who the ones that stop me in life are? They are my brakes. My Guru, because who takes away my ego? My Guru. In the early stage, my parents, my school teachers, my experiences, my adversaries, the crises of my life.

> "All these things that we call negative are not negative things. They are the brakes of our life."

They are brakes, and that is why you can accelerate. Then a voice rises from within: Yes, I will fight, I will do something, I have to prove it by doing it.

## Where There Is No Crisis, There Is No Speed

If there is no crisis, if everything is calm, if everything is running on its own, you will not do anything with speed. Understand that there is no competition, no fear, no worry about the king, no worry from anywhere at all; then you will not pick up speed at all. Everything is happy and prosperous, everything is stable and still; so tell me, what will happen? Will the vehicle move forward? No.

Brakes are necessary, Ganapati is necessary. Without Ganapati the vehicle will not even start. What do we check first? Whether the vehicle is in good shape, whether the brakes work. That is the first thing we say, isn't it: check it.

## The Mutual Relationship of Saturn and Mars

So never forget this example:

> "The purpose of brakes is to give you the courage to move forward at full speed."

That is why Saturn and Mars are always complementary to each other, and become exalted in each other's sign. Mars is exalted in Capricorn (Makara), and Capricorn is the sign of Saturn. Saturn brings obstacles; he is the one who moves śanaiḥ śanaiḥ, that is, slowly. In this way the two complete each other.

Both Vighnakarta and Vighnaharta are Ganapati himself. When he removes obstacles, he is Mangalmurti and carries you forward at high speed. And when he creates obstacles, he is like Shani Dev.
