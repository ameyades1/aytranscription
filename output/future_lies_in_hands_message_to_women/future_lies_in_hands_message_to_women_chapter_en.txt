# Chapter: The Future Lies in the Hands of Women

## Beginning: First Visit to Mount Abu

Namaskar, Om Shanti. My greetings to all the dignitaries on the stage and to all the Didis. This is my first visit to Mount Abu. Sheela Didi ji from Mumbai invited us here to speak on the subject of women's empowerment.

I was told that I had to speak for fifteen minutes. Hearing this, I became worried about what I could say in fifteen minutes, because I am known for speaking for at least ten to fifteen hours at a stretch. Whatever the camp, the majority who attend, around seventy percent, are women. In those sessions there is neither bhajan nor anything else; there is only pure knowledge, which brings immediate transformation. Today I will quickly share a few glimpses from that, without wasting time, because there are many points.

## Why Has India Not Become the World Guru: The Missing Ardhanarishvara

Today everywhere people are discussing that India should become the world guru. I have met many leaders of this country, and many gurus. I hold one conviction: all the knowledge and all the powers needed for India to become the world guru are with us. Only one thing is missing: India has not yet reached the state of **Ardhanarishvara**. It is still a male-dominated society.

I have visited many gurus, the biggest of them; I will not name anyone. In their organisations I saw that there are women, there are female seekers, there is everything, but they are not in the highest position. In politics too I saw that women are very few. How many women have become Prime Minister in our country? Only one. So why not the others?

So I said, come, let us begin the work, because this change will come only through women's empowerment. But how will it happen?

## Woman Is Not Equal to Man; She Is a Thousand Times More Powerful

If India is to become the world guru, the Ardhanarishvara form must come. Today we say that woman should become equal to man, and woman too is seeking this equality, saying I am equal to men. But I am a guru who says only one thing: **woman was never equal to man; she is at least a thousand times more powerful.**

Let me tell you when she was a thousand times more powerful, and when she will be. Whoever comes into the world, man or woman, comes from a mother's womb. When he comes from the mother's womb, the mother's power over him is not a thousand but lakhs of times greater. When he was a small child, the mother raised him with great love and pampering. Whereas Chanakya says:

> **lālayet pañca varṣāṇi daśa varṣāṇi tāḍayet,**
> **prāpte tu ṣoḍaśe varṣe putraṃ mitravad ācaret.**
> "Pamper a son for five years, discipline him for the next ten, and when he reaches sixteen, treat him as a friend."
> (Chanakya Niti 3.18)

That is, nurture the son with love for five years, teach him with discipline and correction for the next ten years, and when he turns sixteen, treat him like a friend.

If at that time the child does not listen and is not corrected, then remember, your power was a thousand times then; that was the time to use it. It is from the same society that someone becomes a thief, a goon, a dacoit or a rapist. That is why, through our organisation, we keep teaching all women to use this power at the right time.

## The Deeper Meaning of Raksha Bandhan: The Protector's Brahmacharya

Then there are sisters, the Didis. They celebrate Bhai Dooj or Raksha Bandhan, and according to our values they expect that my brother will protect me. But only that brother will be able to protect who himself observes **brahmacharya**. Understand this for certain: one who troubles another woman will not be able to protect us in a time of crisis.

Sita Mata too was abducted. She stayed with Ravana in Lanka, yet she remained protected. Why? Because Rama here was then in a state like brahmacharya; he had the power of his tapasya. At the same time, Sita Mata had her own pativrata dharma as well, and I do not deny that. I keep explaining this to all women.

## Without Understanding the Mother, the Guru Is Not Understood

Now comes the subject of the mother. The mother should be the refuge of shakti. Today's modern boys do not give their parents that much respect, and the same is the situation with today's modern girls. But until you understand a mother, you will never understand the guru. Because we call the guru "Guru Mauli"; we address the guru as mother.

Within all of us there are two aspects, male and female. Whether the outer body is male or female makes no difference; both elements are present within. I say that if you want liberation, if you want moksha, these two elements within, male and female, must be balanced. You must know how to be firm at times, and also how to give pure love; both should be there.

Compassion, love, forgiveness: whose qualities are these? Women's. In Maharashtra we call Sant Dnyaneshwar "Dnyaneshwar Mauli". Whoever the guru is there, he is called Mauli, that is, mother. Until someone has the qualities of a mother, he cannot become great. A guru too is called a guru only when the female element in him is greater, even if the body is male.

That is why I say to women: compassion, love, forgiveness, charity, all this is already within you, isn't it? It already is. Therefore for you, attaining moksha is very easy. But where does the difficulty arise?

## The Seven Divine Glories of God in Woman

In the tenth chapter of the Bhagavad Gita, Shri Krishna tells in which qualities of women He is especially present:

> **mṛtyuḥ sarva-haraś cāham udbhavaś ca bhaviṣyatām,**
> **kīrtiḥ śrīr vāk ca nārīṇāṃ smṛtir medhā dhṛtiḥ kṣamā.**
> "I am all-devouring death, and the origin of all that is yet to be; among women I am fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forgiveness."
> (Bhagavad Gita 10.34)

That is, I am death that takes away all, and the origin of those yet to be born; and in women I am fame, Shri, Vak, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forgiveness.

**Shri** means auspiciousness, the sacred and blessed. **Vak** means speech. After that come dhriti, smriti, medha and kshama. Shri Krishna says that He is present in all these qualities. Now it is very important to understand in what form God is present in me, and what I do with it.

## Shri: Lakshmi Comes to a Home Three Times

Shri means Lakshmi, that is, divine wealth should be abundant within me. Forgiveness, compassion and love are already in a woman; there is not much difficulty there. When she goes to a home as a daughter-in-law, we do not say that Griha Saraswati has come or Griha Kali has come. We say **Grihalakshmi** has come. Here Lakshmi does not mean only outer wealth; with her coming, an atmosphere of love and peace comes into the home.

Later she will also become a mother. And as soon as a woman becomes a mother, in a single moment, without fail, the feeling of the Supreme arises within her by itself. By then she has not even seen what her son's body or appearance is like, whether he is dark or fair, whether he is disabled or not. Yet how does that love come? That love begins to work on its own from the Atma tattva. This feeling is essential.

That is why the first quality of woman is called Shri, and it should be within us. The Devi Purana says that real Lakshmi comes to a home three times.

First, she is present in the form of my **mother**. Until I accept her properly as mother, until I listen to her, I do not understand her love either. We do eat the laddoos she makes, but if anything is in a mother's mind twenty-four hours a day, it is only the thought of her son or daughter. Perhaps she is unaware of the world, perhaps she does not know the latest things, and we say that sometimes one should reject advice. In this way we reject her, and Shri does not come much into our home.

The second time Lakshmi comes is when a **daughter-in-law** comes into the home from outside through marriage. In a home where the daughter-in-law who has come is not respected, Shri leaves once again.

The third time she comes is by being born as a **daughter**. And people say, "Oh dear, it would have been better if it were a boy." Remember, in such a home Shri mostly does not stay. Even if Shri comes, it is not enjoyed, and even if it is enjoyed, it brings no joy.

## Vak: Extremely Gentle, Extremely Fierce

Shri Krishna says that I come in woman in the form of Vak. Vak means speech. So what should a woman's speech be like? We usually say it should be loving and compassionate. Fine, there is no objection to this. But that alone is not enough. In the Durga Saptashati, the form of Goddess Durga is described:

> **atisaumyātiraudrāyai natās tasyai namo namaḥ.**
> "Salutations again and again to Her who is extremely gentle and extremely fierce."
> (Durga Saptashati, Chapter 5)

That is, salutations again and again to the Goddess who is both extremely gentle and extremely fierce.

What happens is that a woman's fierce form usually appears before her husband, and her gentle form outside. However much oppression there is outside, she tolerates it, whereas she was not supposed to tolerate it. Where should the gentle form have been? At home her extremely gentle form should have been. And outside, in the office or anywhere, where injustice or oppression is happening to her, there she must be extremely fierce too. This must be learned.

In the Durga Saptashati, the first episode is the worship of Mahakali, the second of Mahalakshmi and the third of Ma Saraswati. Therefore **Kali is extremely essential**. Why Kali shakti is needed in empowerment, especially in the Kali Yuga, I will come to later.

So with Vak it became clear how one should speak. Somewhere you will have to be firm. The mother-in-law is speaking, she goes on speaking; how far should you keep listening? You have to set your boundaries. I always tell women that the image you build in your in-laws' home in the first one or two years will stay forever.

I have met many women and conducted many camps and seminars. And this is perhaps the world's largest organisation of women. So when Didi invited me, I said that I must have some karmic bond with this place. I will certainly come here, and perhaps keep coming. So this talk had to be given here.

## Smriti, Dhriti and Medha: The Qualities of Vedanta Belong to Woman

After Shri and Vak come smriti, medha and dhriti. Shri Krishna says these are the special qualities of women.

**Smriti** means memory. **Dhriti** means the power of holding, holding on to something for a very long time. In Ashtanga Yoga, among yama, niyama, asana, pranayama, pratyahara, dharana, dhyana and samadhi, dharana is exactly this, holding on to something and demonstrating it.

And the third is **medha shakti**. When there is the tapas of brahmacharya for twelve years, then the vital fluids become ojas, then that radiance comes within, whether man or woman, and after that comes medha shakti. Medha means that if you have heard something even once, you can present it exactly as it is at the right time. In spirituality, in Vedanta, all these qualities are essential. Shri Krishna says that He has given these qualities to women in abundance, and in the woman who has them, I, the Supreme, become manifest.

In the Kali Yuga the situation is that we men have to develop this power with effort. "Ji, bring the vegetables." And on returning he says, "I forgot, ji." Whereas the woman remembers completely what her sister-in-law said to her twenty years ago. That is smriti. "I'll deal with this one," that is dhriti. If a man asks me that his wife caught one mistake of his, where did I go wrong, I always tell him, just go and ask your wife what mistake I made. She will tell you what mistakes you, your mother and your family made and when, and at the right time too. That is smriti and dhriti. And saying it at the right time, at a function itself, that is medha. Woman has these qualities.

But the scripture also says that the best of men is the one who forgets a matter in five minutes. Which matter? Not the words of the guru; those are to be held all life long. All the other matters. The middling man is the one who forgets in a week. And the lowest man is the one who perhaps carries matters from after 1857 to this day. That is why quarrels keep going on: "Your grandfather, your great-grandfather did this, your great-grandmother said such and such." This chain just keeps going.

So the qualities needed to learn Vedanta are with women. There is only one thing, as Shri Krishna says: what to remember and what to forget. This habit of forgetting is to be taken from men. Tell the husband in the evening to bring something on the way back, and he comes empty-handed: "I forgot." Forgetting is a male quality, and the qualities described above are the woman's. Where we have to make a conscious effort for them, you have been given them naturally.

## Kshama: But Not for Everyone

Then comes **kshama**, forgiveness. It is said that woman is forgiving. But it is not said to forgive everyone. Somewhere a form like Durga is also necessary. That is why I always say: make your boundaries clear in time. If you do not, there will be a big problem in the family. In the office too, certain limits have to be kept.

## Choosing a Life Partner: Where Does Intuition Go?

The vehicle of life starts with the choice of a life partner. Consider marriage a bus or a train. Suppose you board a compartment, all the window seats have been taken by men, and your journey is only twenty minutes. Even then a woman has so much intuitive power that she recognises, "This one's manner doesn't look quite right, this one seems like a goon." And she goes and sits near the one who seems decent.

If this power is within you, where does it go when choosing a life partner? There you fall into maya. On the bus you used your intellect, but here something happens and you come under the sway of emotion.

Our scripture says there are four levels: body, mind, intellect and Atma. Men live especially at the level of body and intellect. One is a rationalist, a scientist, who will apply logic every time; this is how men are made. The other is body-conscious, doing heavy labour like an animal. And woman mostly works at the level of the mind. I am not saying that women have no intellect or that men have no mind. But woman works at the level of mind, of emotion; she is very emotion-driven. And second, she is directly at the level of the Atma tattva, as on becoming a mother, where she is both full of feeling and completely free. Woman has only these two states.

What is the difficulty in this? Even if there is a woman police officer and someone says, "Didi, please do this one thing, let me go in a little," the woman police officer will listen; a man will not. Therefore a woman has to be careful while choosing a life partner too. The other person can mislead you with intellect, can create trouble. In our camps we always teach how not to lose this power. We have as much power within us as in the bus example; it has to be used.

## The True Meaning of Pativrata Dharma

Now I come to the subject of the pativrata woman. It has become settled in our values that a pativrata woman means one who endures everything, even injustice. This is not pativrata dharma. A woman who brings her husband a glass of liquor is not called pativrata. Pativrata is she who makes him give up drinking. For this she may fight as much as she wants. If the husband goes on the wrong path, that much Kali shakti should come within her.

I want to tell you that from the time of the British and the Mughals, certain changes were made in the scriptures to weaken women. The description of pativrata dharma has been somewhat diluted and changed. It is said that when the husband before you is like Shiva, then you are to become like Parvati. Otherwise, through pativrata shakti he will indeed change, but you must also understand for whom I should waste so much of my time. When to quit, when to stop, all this must be known.

## Moksha Even for the Woman Who Wears Lipstick

I have already said that all the points cannot be covered in this much time. I was asked to fill my ocean into a pot and give a little. So I am giving just that much, telling everything briefly.

Perhaps I am one of those gurus who say just one thing to women: **even a woman who wears powder and lipstick can attain moksha.** Otherwise remove the picture of Lakshmi ji, because she is shown in that very form. And if it is not so, why do we teach that form? Kali and Lakshmi are also forms of the Goddess; it is not only the Saraswati form. That is one form, one aspect. There are other forms too, and they should also be respected. It is now the Kali Yuga; nothing will work without wealth. The scripture says:

> **dharmasya mūlam arthaḥ.**
> "The root of dharma is wealth."
> (Chanakya Sutra)

That is, the root of dharma is artha, that is, wealth.

## Three Powers: Will, Action and Knowledge

Which powers should come in women's empowerment? **Iccha shakti, kriya shakti and jnana shakti**: the power of will, the power of action and the power of knowledge. All three are equally important, and their forms are Mahakali, Mahalakshmi and Mahasaraswati.

The power of Mahakali is physical protection. We say one should learn karate; so start, that is the basic level. Higher than this is the protection of spiritual power. If that is there, the enemy will not even look at you with an evil eye. All these things should come, but this does not happen by saying one sentence.

Only that guru can put such power within you who himself understands, lives, sees and feels the pain of a woman, and at the same time has the male element within him in equal measure, as said of Ardhanarishvara. That is why around seventy to seventy-five percent of our organisation are women, whose immediate transformation I see.

## Health: What Is Not Spoken Becomes Disease

The second thing is your health. Just as we spoke of wealth, it is also said that health is wealth. Women keep working all day. And in today's family situations, what happens is that they cannot speak. Society is such that you just have to listen and listen. The mother-in-law said something, someone else said something.

If you do not express yourself, you get **thyroid** problems. We conduct healing camps, so I have studied this in India: most women in India get thyroid problems. The reason is that the voice gets stuck up to the throat and does not come out. Second, she develops a fear of the unknown, that at home this person will say this, that person will say that; from this comes **knee pain**. You think it comes from working; it does not. This is research we have done; it comes for this very reason.

Then we advised women that you will have to express yourself sometime. So they started expressing it by shouting. I said that if you do this, you will not get thyroid problems, but you will get a heart problem or some blood pressure problem. You have to say it, but in a proper tone.

## Without Knowing History, No Future Is Built

Woman has become modern. But it is said that if you want to create something new, to shape a new future, you first need to know history. When you go to a doctor, what does he ask first? Your medical history, what happened earlier. Without that, no diagnosis is possible.

In the same way, if women's empowerment is to happen in this country, we must see what happened to woman before. What happened in the last two thousand years? How many atrocities took place? Why did they take place? All this must be seen. Only after that can we move forward.

## The Guru in the Form of a Mother: The Path to Bharat Mata

I said that India has to become the world guru. Then first it must become a guru, and what did I say about the guru? **The guru should be in the form of a mother.**

If you know astrology, in which sign is Guru, that is Jupiter, exalted? In Cancer. Cancer is the sign of the Moon, and the Moon is the form of the mother. The true teacher is the one who can teach like a mother and can give that much time. A mother never asks her child for any fee. In Sagittarius, Guru is in its own sign, but it is a fire sign, harsh; people do not like such a teacher. In Pisces it is emotional, and that too is not liked as much. The guru should be like a mother.

If you want to do bhakti, first a woman's heart must come within you. Until you understand a mother, you cannot understand the guru, you cannot understand the Guru Ma. And until you understand the Guru Ma, you cannot understand Bharat Mata.

If India is to become the world guru, think: apart from India, all the other countries of the world have people of other religions. When will they listen to you, what will they listen to? Are we ready to understand them like a mother? Not yet. Neither do they see us as a mother, nor do we today see them as our children. Our work is going on well for exactly this.

## You Must Win Your Place Yourself

Then I said, do you think someone will give you this power on their own? You must attain your place yourself. It is the Kali Yuga; think and tell me. We do say:

> **yatra nāryas tu pūjyante ramante tatra devatāḥ.**
> "Where women are worshipped, there the gods rejoice."
> (Manusmriti 3.56)

That is, where women are worshipped, there the gods dwell in delight.

Fine, but who will do the worship? There must be some priest. The Kali Yuga is the reign of the asuras; who will worship here? Worship is offered only to her who is powerful. Before the Goddess, neither Chanda-Munda nor Shumbha-Nishumbha could prevail. Those qualities must be brought.

This organisation of yours is doing very good work. Coming here, I saw it; I asked you, I asked Chakradhar Didi, I asked everyone a little; the work is very good. They told me that this is what we teach here, this is the knowledge.

## Attire and Form: Saraswati, Lakshmi and Kali

One thing about attire too. All of you have taken on the Saraswati form in white clothes. But I say that there are also the forms of Lakshmi and Kali. Some woman says, I want to become a police inspector; someone is in politics; everyone else is doing this too, it is not some wrong work. In that too you should be able to see the Kali form of the Goddess. Someone becomes a businesswoman; earning wealth is not wrong either, it is the form of Lakshmi. And someone is Saraswati, the form of knowledge. All three. We keep no such ban or restriction.

## What Kind of Guru Is Needed for Women's Empowerment

I am mindful that I must not take much time. But a guru will be needed for women's empowerment. What should that guru be like? He should have a woman's heart within him. But if there is only a woman's heart, people take undue advantage. So he will have to become a little male too, in a form like Ardhanarishvara, a little firm as well.

I saw here, from what you told me, that you are already doing this. There is a lot of the gentle form too, and that is also needed. But I say that **both forms, extremely gentle and extremely fierce, must be there.**

I will not say more now; I conclude with these few words. Thank you.
