## 1. Summary

The full discourse Acharya Upendra Ji gave to a large gathering of women at Mount Abu. It starts from the "Vishwa Guru" dream and names the missing link (India has not reached the **Ardhanarishvara** balance), then turns the equality debate around (a woman is not equal to a man but a thousand times stronger, and as a mother must use that power in time). The core of the post is **Bhagavad Gita 10.34**, where Shri Krishna names seven of His glories in women (kirti, shri, vak, smriti, medha, dhriti, kshama), and Acharya Ji opens six of them with practical teaching: Lakshmi enters the home as mother, daughter-in-law and daughter; be "अति सौम्य, अति रौद्र" (supremely gentle at home, supremely fierce against injustice); learn what to remember and what to forget; forgiveness, but not for everyone. Around this sit pativrata (not enduring injustice), lipstick and moksha, the three Shaktis (Kali, Lakshmi, Saraswati), and the Guru as mother. The main intent is informational ("गीता में नारी के गुण" / "qualities of women in the Bhagavad Gita"), with a strong emotional and life-problem side (in-laws, boundaries, choosing a partner, a husband's drinking). The opportunity: pages on Gita 10.34 are mostly bare verse translations, and "women empowerment" pages are mostly secular essays; a post that explains each quality from the verse and applies it to a woman's daily life is a distinct, authentic answer. The same Mount Abu event is also covered by `gyan_sarovar_mount_abu_women_address`, so the two posts must target different queries (section 7, cannibalization).

## 6. FAQ

1. **गीता के अनुसार नारी के सात गुण कौन से हैं? / What are the seven qualities of women in the Bhagavad Gita?**
   गीता १०.३४ में श्रीकृष्ण कहते हैं कि नारियों में कीर्ति, श्री, वाक्, स्मृति, मेधा, धृति और क्षमा मैं हूं। / In Gita 10.34 Shri Krishna says that among women He is fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forgiveness.
2. **पतिव्रता का सही अर्थ क्या है? / What does pativrata really mean?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार पतिव्रता का अर्थ अन्याय सहना नहीं; पतिव्रता वह है जो पति को गलत मार्ग से लौटाए, जैसे उसकी दारू छुड़ाए। / According to Acharya Ji, pativrata does not mean enduring injustice; a true pativrata turns her husband away from the wrong path, for example helps him give up drinking.
3. **घर में लक्ष्मी किन रूपों में आती है? / In which forms does Lakshmi enter the home?**
   आचार्य जी बताते हैं कि लक्ष्मी तीन बार आती है: माता, बहू और पुत्री के रूप में; तीनों का सम्मान न हो तो श्री नहीं टिकती। / Acharya Ji explains that Lakshmi comes three times, as mother, daughter-in-law and daughter; where these three are not honoured, prosperity does not stay.
4. **क्या पाउडर-लिपस्टिक लगाने वाली स्त्री को मोक्ष मिल सकता है? / Can a woman who wears make-up attain liberation?**
   हां; आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि लक्ष्मी जी को भी इसी रूप में दिखाया जाता है, और सरस्वती के साथ लक्ष्मी और काली भी देवी के स्वरूप हैं। / Yes; Acharya Ji says Lakshmi herself is shown this way, and Lakshmi and Kali are forms of the Goddess alongside Saraswati.
5. **"अति सौम्य, अति रौद्र" का अर्थ क्या है? / What does "supremely gentle, supremely fierce" mean?**
   दुर्गा सप्तशती देवी को अति सौम्य और अति रौद्र कहती है; आचार्य जी के अनुसार स्त्री को घर में सौम्य और बाहर अन्याय के आगे रौद्र होना चाहिए, उल्टा नहीं। / The Durga Saptashati praises the Goddess as both; Acharya Ji says a woman should be gentle at home and fierce against injustice outside, not the other way round.

## 10. Authenticity flags

Sanskrit review (tools/review/future_lies_in_hands_message_to_women.md, verdict "Needs correction"); none of these is applied yet:

1. **Gita 10.34 Hindi meaning gives only the second half (review issue 1, Major).** The primary keyword rests on this verse, so apply the review's full Hindi meaning before publishing. The direct answer and FAQ (sections 5 and 6) quote only the "among women" half, which is accurate as a partial meaning.
2. **Durga Saptashati half-verse: Hindi meaning drops the salutation, and the reference has no verse number (review issue 2, Major).** Apply the review's Hindi meaning and "५.१३, पूर्वार्ध" once the Gita Press numbering is confirmed. FAQ 5 says only that the Saptashati praises the Goddess as both, which holds either way.
3. **Dhriti stated as the dharana of Ashtanga Yoga (review issue 3, Minor).** Apply the review's wording that Acharya Ji links the two. Keep "dhriti = dharana" out of all SEO text.
4. **"Devi Purana" source unverified (review issue 4, Minor).** The three forms of Lakshmi are a secondary keyword; FAQ 3 and all SEO text attribute it to Acharya Ji only, never to a Purana, until a source is found.
5. **Chanakya Niti verse called a "sutra" (review issue 5) and its meaning leaves out "पुत्रं" (review issue 6), Minor.** Apply both. Not used in SEO text. "ताडयेत्" literally means to chastise or strike; never promote this verse as a parenting "tip" in a social card.
6. **Manusmriti 3.56 is a half-verse not marked as such (review issue 7, Minor).** Add "पूर्वार्ध" / "first half". If the line is used on a quote card, keep it as a half-verse with the reference.
7. **"धर्मस्य मूलम् अर्थः" sandhi (review issue 8, Minor).** Use "धर्मस्य मूलमर्थः" in Hindi.
8. **"Saraswati" for Mahasaraswati in the order of the charitras (review issue 9, Minor).** Apply.
9. **IAST "ṁ" vs "ṃ" (review issue 10, Minor).** Follow the site's convention consistently.
10. **Seven qualities, six discussed.** The title says "seven qualities" because the verse names seven; the post discusses six (not kirti). This is accurate, but the title must not be read as a promise of seven sections: the direct answer lists all seven from the verse, which delivers what the title promises.

11. **Thyroid and knee-pain claims (review issue 11).** These are Acharya Ji's observations from healing camps, not medical facts. Never use them in a title, meta, FAQ, snippet or social card, and do not target health queries. Add the review's optional line advising a doctor's consultation.
12. **"A thousand times more powerful" and the "when to walk away" line are the speaker's teaching.** Attribute them to Acharya Ji in all SEO text. The pativrata FAQ uses the talk's own example (helping a husband give up drinking) and does not turn "when to walk away" into marriage advice or a hook.
13. **No gender-war or fear framing.** Don't headline "women superior to men", the "thief, bandit or rapist" warning, or "Shri leaves the home" as a threat to families with daughters. The talk's tone is empowering; the titles use the Gita's qualities, not fear.
14. **Opinions on history.** The dilution of pativrata under the Mughals and British and the "two thousand years" of injustice are the speaker's view; keep them out of titles, metas and FAQ, and never present them as historical fact.
