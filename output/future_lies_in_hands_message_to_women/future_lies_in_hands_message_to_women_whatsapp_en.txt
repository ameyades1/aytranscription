*What are the seven qualities of women in the Bhagavad Gita?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that fame, prosperity, speech, memory, intelligence, steadfastness and forgiveness already live within women, which makes liberation easier for them. A mother's power over her young child is immense.

💭 _Within you, which weighs more: firmness or tenderness?_
