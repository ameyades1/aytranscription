## 1. Summary

A short, practical talk on sanga (company) and the three gunas. Acharya Ji says that keeping one's sattva is simple: stay close to sattvic people who lead you towards truth, because thoughts shape conduct and company shapes thoughts. He then deals with the hard part: what to do when you have grown beyond your family and old circle. Do not go back to show off your achievements; meet relatives now and then on their level, lift them if they are willing, and bring new friends of your level into your life. He grounds this in the homes of Shri Rama, Shri Krishna and Shivaji Maharaj, and in a reference to the Ganesha Purana. The main intent is emotional / life-problem ("my family pulls me down", "how to stay positive around negative relatives", "why does my peace fade") with an informational layer (sangati ka prabhav, satsang ka mahatva, sattva guna). The opportunity is large: "company you keep" and "sangati ka asar" queries are served mostly by quote lists and self-help listicles; this post gives a spiritual reason (the gunas) and a humane, non-hostile way to handle family, which few pages offer.

## 6. FAQ

Answers come only from the talk.

**Hindi**
1. **साधना से मिली शांति क्यों टिकती नहीं?** — क्योंकि संगति राजसिक हो तो गुण धीरे-धीरे नीचे आने लगता है। विचार संगति से बनते हैं और आचरण विचारों से।
2. **क्या आगे बढ़ने के लिए परिवार से नाता तोड़ना होगा?** — नहीं। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि जन्म के रिश्तों से कभी-कभी उनके स्तर पर मिलने जाएँ, और जीवन में नए स्तर के मित्र लाएँ।
3. **पारिवारिक समारोह में कैसे व्यवहार करें?** — उनके स्तर पर आकर वही बात करें जो उन्हें अच्छी लगे; अपनी उपलब्धियाँ न गिनाएँ। कुछ कहना हो तो उन्हें ऊपर उठाने की बात करें।
4. **महापुरुष घर क्यों छोड़ देते हैं?** — बहुत कम लोगों का पूरा परिवार उनके साथ ऊपर आता है; सतोगुण बनाए रखने के लिए उन्हें अधिक से अधिक समय गुरु के साथ रहना पड़ता है।

**English**
1. **Why does the peace from my practice fade?** — Because rajasic company slowly pulls your inner state down. Your thoughts are shaped by your company, and your conduct by your thoughts.
2. **Do I have to cut off my family to grow spiritually?** — No. Acharya Ji says to visit relatives by birth from time to time, on their level, and to bring friends of a new level into your life.
3. **How should I behave at family functions?** — Come down to their level and talk about what they enjoy; do not list your achievements. If you speak, speak in a way that lifts them up.
4. **Why do great souls leave home?** — Very few people have their whole family rise with them; to keep their sattva intact they need to spend as much time as possible with the Guru.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Ganesha Purana attribution (Major, from the Sanskrit review).** Both versions say that in the Ganesha Purana Lord Ganesha himself says most relatives are there for their own gain; no khanda, adhyaya or verse is given and the reviewer could not place it. Recommendation: do not use this claim in the title, meta, FAQ, snippets or social cards (none of them do). Apply the review's wording ("आचार्य जी गणेश पुराण का उल्लेख करते हुए बताते हैं..." / "Referring to the Ganesha Purana, Acharya Ji explains...") in the section and in Key Takeaways, and soften the "What Does the Ganesha Purana Say?" heading until the verse is found.
2. **"Relatives are selfish" as a hook.** Titles like "Why your relatives pull you down" or "toxic family" would draw clicks but turn a teaching about keeping sattva into a grievance against family. The talk says to meet relatives on their level, lift them if they are willing, and not break the relationship. Recommendation: keep titles and meta on company and sattva (as chosen), and never frame the post as "cut off your family".
3. **"Why great souls leave home".** Useful as a query, but it could be read as advice to renounce family. The blog presents it as what happens to those doing great work, with the Guru as the reason, not as general advice. Recommendation: keep it as an H2 and FAQ only with the context given in section 6.
4. **Kamsa called राक्षस; versions differ (from the review).** Kamsa is an asura (Kalanemi reborn, Bhagavata 10.1), and the English adds "his own maternal uncle", which the Hindi lacks. Recommendation: use the review's correction ("उनके मामा कंस थे... असुर") so both versions match.
5. **Soyarabai's quoted words (from the review).** A retelling presented as a direct quotation; historical, not scriptural, and unverified. Recommendation: keep it out of snippets and FAQ (done); consider rendering it as indirect speech unless a Maratha history source confirms the wording.
6. **Heading calls Shivaji Maharaj an avatar (from the review).** "अवतारों के घर में भी यही हुआ" / "Even the Avatars Faced It at Home" covers Shivaji Maharaj, who is not an avatar. Recommendation: "अवतारों और महापुरुषों के घर में भी यही हुआ" / "Even Avatars and Great Souls Faced It at Home" (section 4).
7. **Term consistency (from the review).** सात्विकता / सतोगुण / सत्त्व / सत्त्वगुण are mixed in Hindi (Gita Press spells सात्त्विक); the English drops "rajas" where the Hindi has रजोगुण; viveka is glossed as only "right from wrong". Recommendation: apply the review's corrections. The Hindi focus and secondary keywords use the common search spellings (सात्विक, सतोगुण), which is fine for queries; the body can still follow the Gita Press spelling.
