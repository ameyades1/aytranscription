*Why does the peace from your practice fade?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that the people around you shape your thoughts, and your conduct follows your thoughts. Sattvic company keeps your peace strong; restless, showy company slowly pulls it down.

💭 _Are the five people closest to you leading you towards truth?_
