# The Company You Keep Shapes Your Destiny

## The Simple Secret of Sattva

Have you ever wondered why the peace you gain from sadhana fades within a few days? Acharya Ji answers this very simply.

Maintaining the **sattva** within you (the quality of purity, clarity and calm) is very simple. It comes from staying close to people of sattva guna, and from keeping the company of those who lead you towards truth.

If your company is **rajasic** (full of restlessness, show and desire), your guna will slowly come down. The surroundings you live in and the people around you are what form the samskaras within.

> "As your thoughts are, so is your conduct."

And how are thoughts formed? By the kind of people you live among.

*The five people you spend the most time with today: are they leading you towards truth, or away from it?*

## When You Move Ahead of Your Family

When you rise in life, you feel you have gone far ahead of all your cousins and relatives. Acharya Ji says: then understand that that world is separate and this world is separate. You do not need to meet them very much.

If you go to a family function, come down to their level. At that time, talk only about what they like. Do not go on chattering that I achieved this, I achieved that.

If you must say something, talk about lifting them up: "You too can do business." If they like it, fine; otherwise, simply come down to their level.

## "It Is the Company You Keep"

> "It is the company you keep and decide."

The company you keep decides your direction. You have come this far, so you will have to give up some old relationships to an extent; this is **tyaga** (renunciation).

Friends of a new level should come into your life. Then your whole circle will change. Go and meet the blood relations you were born into once in a while. This is how your **sattva** will be maintained.

## Where Does the Fall Begin?

By the grace of the Guru you rise. Happiness starts coming into life, and you get bound in that very happiness. Along with it there is also the attachment of **tamo guna** (the quality of inertia and delusion).

That is why the mind is pulled back towards those same old relationships. You come down and start showing them your rajo guna, your achievements.

But they have no interest in how high you have gone. Then you feel bad about them, they speak badly about you, and slowly your sattva guna starts to fall.

*Have you too ever gone to show your progress to people who had no interest in it at all?*

## What Are Your Options?

Acharya Ji lays out clear paths:

- Either bring them up to your level.
- Or bring your new friends among them.
- Otherwise, come down and meet them for that much time, but that time will be wasted.

So bring new people into this level of your life. New friends, everything new, a new family.

## Why Do Great Souls Leave Home?

This is why great souls often leave home. Of the two, you get only one. If you achieve something very big in the outer world, this home slowly falls away.

Very few people have their whole family rise along with them. To maintain sattva guna they have no other choice. You have to spend the maximum time with the Guru.

> "That is how the nature is made."

At home there will often be people of rajas and tamas, caught in **maya**. That is simply how nature is made.

## What Does the Ganesha Purana Say?

Acharya Ji refers to the Ganesha Purana. In it, Lord Ganesha himself says that most relatives are there for their own benefit. This is stated as a **tattva** (a fundamental truth), one that applies to every family.

## It Happened Even in the Homes of Avatars

What happened in Shri Ram's own home? "Send him to the forest in exile for fourteen years." This happened in Shri Ram's own home.

In Shri Krishna's family there was Kansa. There was a demon in his own home.

In the home of Shri Shivaji Maharaj too, Soyarabai used to say, "Keep this kingdom for my son. Give your Sambhaji Raje the south below, give Rajaram the part above."

When this happens even in the homes of such great avatars and great personalities, how can you say there are no rajasic or tamasic people in your home?

## The Decision of Discernment

A person with **viveka buddhi** (the ability to discern right from wrong) understands that most people around him and in his relationships will be of rajas and tamas. Then clear paths lie before him:

- I am doing a great work, so I must rise above them.
- Or I must lift them up and bring them with me.
- Or I must come down to their level and talk about what they like.
- Or I must build my own new family, everything new.

And if you cannot break the relationship, go and meet them once in a while, becoming of their level.

> "That is how you can maintain sattva guna. Otherwise you cannot."

*Which relationships in your life lift you up, and after meeting whom do you feel yourself sink inside?*

## Key Takeaways

- The simple way to sustain sattva is the company of sattvic people and those who lead you towards truth.
- As your thoughts are, so is your conduct; and thoughts are formed by your company.
- After rising, showing off your achievements in old relationships pulls your sattva guna down.
- Meet old relatives once in a while at their level, and bring friends of a new level into your life.
- According to the Ganesha Purana, most relatives are there for their own benefit; it was so even in the homes of Shri Ram, Shri Krishna and Shivaji Maharaj.
- To maintain sattva guna, spending the maximum time with the Guru is essential.
