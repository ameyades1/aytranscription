# Chapter: The Company You Keep Shapes Your Destiny

## How to Sustain Sattva Within

How do you keep the sattvic quality within yourself steady and continuous? The answer is very simple. It comes from staying close to people of sattva guna, and from keeping the company of those who lead you towards truth.

If your company is rajasic, your guna will slowly come down. What the people around you are like, the surroundings you live in, that is what goes inside you as samskara. As your thoughts are, so is your conduct. And what your thoughts are like depends on the kind of people you live among.

> "As your thoughts are, so is your conduct. And thoughts are formed by the people you live among."

## When You Rise Above Your Family

If you have risen above your family, you feel that you have climbed far above all your cousins. Then understand that that world is separate and this world is separate. Leave that world, meaning you do not need to meet them very much.

If you go to a family function, come down to their level. At that time, talk only about the topics they like. Do not go on chattering that we are at such a high level, I achieved this, I achieved that. If you must say something there, talk about lifting these people up too, like "You too can do business." If they like that, fine; otherwise, come down to their level.

## The Company You Keep Decides

> "It is the company you keep and decide."

The company you keep is what decides your future. You have come this far, so you will have to give up these relationships to some extent. In place of those who seem to be at a lower level, friends of a new level should come into your life. Then your whole circle will change. The blood relations you are born into, go and meet them once in a while. Only then will your sattva be maintained.

## Where the Problem Begins

What is the problem? By the grace of the Guru you rise higher and higher, happiness starts coming into life, and you get bound in that happiness. Along with it there is also the attachment of tamo guna. That is why you feel the desire to go back to those same lower-level relationships.

You come down from here, and then you start showing them your rajo guna. But they are not the least bit interested in how high you have gone. Then you feel bad about them, then they keep speaking badly about you, and then your sattva guna slowly starts to fall.

## There Are Only Three Paths

Either bring them up to your level, or bring your new friends among them. If you cannot do that, then come down and interact with them for that much time. But that will only be a waste of time.

So bring new people into your life at this level. New friends; you will have to bring in everything new, a new family. Have you understood or not?

## Why Great Souls Leave Home

That is why great souls often leave home. Of the two, you will get only one. If you achieve something very big in the outer world, this home slowly falls away. Very few people have their whole family rise along with them.

So to maintain sattva guna, they have no other choice. You have to spend the maximum time with the Guru. At home there will often be people of rajas and tamas, caught in maya. That is how nature is made.

## The Principle of the Ganesha Purana

Just read the Ganesha Purana. The Ganesha Purana says that most relatives are there mainly for their own benefit. Lord Ganesha himself says this. It is stated as a principle, meaning it will be so in everyone's family, in ours too and everywhere around us.

## Even in the Homes of Avatars

Was it not so in Shri Ram's own home? "Send him to the forest in exile for fourteen years": did this happen in Shri Ram's own home or not? Was there Kansa in Shri Krishna's family or not? Was there a demon in his own home or not? So how can you say that there are no rajasic and tamasic people in our home? First tell me, will there be or not? Why, even in the homes of avatars and gods this happens.

In the home of Shri Shivaji Maharaj too there were such people. Soyarabai used to say, "Keep this kingdom for my son. Give your Sambhaji Raje the south below, and give Rajaram the part above." When this happens even in the homes of such great personalities, why would it not happen in yours? There too will be people of rajas and tamas.

## The Decision of a Discerning Person

The Ganesha Purana states this clearly. A person with viveka, the power of discernment, understands that most of the people around him will be of rajas and tamas, in his relationships too.

So he thinks: I am doing a great work, so I must rise above them, or else I must lift them up and bring them along with me. Otherwise I will have to come down to their level and talk only about what they like. Otherwise I will have to build my own new family, everything new. And if I cannot break the relationship, I should go to them once in a while, becoming of their level.

> "That is how you can maintain sattva guna. Otherwise you cannot."
