## 1. Summary

A short discourse (3:21) on why years of satsang can leave a seeker unchanged. Acharya Ji describes three listeners: the one who remembers only the event and the food, the one who keeps a mental notebook of other people's faults, and the one who leaves satisfied that "nothing was said for me". The good listener sets ego aside, asks "where did I go wrong?", and applies every word to themselves; only after living a teaching do they earn the right to pass it on. The main intent is emotional / self-reflective ("why don't discourses change me?") with an informational layer ("what is satsang", "how to listen in satsang"). The opportunity: results for "satsang" are mostly event listings, bhajan pages and dictionary definitions; a page that answers why satsang does not transform the listener, with a clear "good listener" framework, is a distinctive answer. The post is short and quotes no scripture, so it should rank on clarity and the self-reflection angle rather than depth.

**English (52 words):** According to Acharya Upendra Ji, satsang does not change your life when you listen on behalf of others. A good listener listens for their own spiritual growth, not to find fault with others. Setting ego aside, they apply every word to themselves and ask, "Where did I go wrong?" Only then does the teaching transform.

## 6. FAQ

**Hindi**
1. **सत्संग का अर्थ क्या है?** सत्संग का अर्थ है सत्य का संग। पर वह तभी होता है जब सुनने वाला सही भाव से सुने।
2. **सत्संग का पहला विघ्न क्या है?** स्वयं को छोड़कर दूसरे को देखना, यानी गुरु की बात को दूसरों के दोषों पर लागू करना।
3. **केवल सुनने और धारण करने में क्या अंतर है?** केवल सुनने से कान पवित्र होते हैं; बात को भीतर उतारने (धारण) से हृदय पवित्र होता है।
4. **दूसरों को कब सिखाना चाहिए?** जब तक आप ज्ञान को स्वयं जीवन में नहीं उतारते, तब तक दूसरों को सिखाने के अधिकारी नहीं हैं।

**English**
1. **What does satsang mean?** Satsang means the company of truth. That presence becomes real only when you listen with the right attitude.
2. **What is the first obstacle in satsang?** Looking away from yourself and watching others instead, using the Guru's words to point at other people's faults.
3. **What is the difference between hearing and holding a teaching?** Listening alone purifies the ears; holding the teaching within purifies the heart.
4. **When should you teach others?** Until you bring a teaching into your own life, you have not earned the right to pass it on.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **अनूचान spelling (minor, from the Sanskrit review).** Hindi "अनुचान" should be "अनूचान" (long ū); English "anuchana" should be "anūchāna" or "anoochana". Not used in any SEO field; fix in the blog text.
2. **"dharana" in English (minor, from the Sanskrit review).** The Hindi has धारण (holding within), but English "dharana" reads as धारणा, the sixth limb of Ashtanga Yoga (Yoga Sutra 3.1). Recommendation: "dharan" or drop the transliteration. For SEO, do not target "dharana" queries from this post; the FAQ here says "holding the teaching within", not "dharana".
3. **"Purified ears" is a teaching image, not a scriptural claim.** The FAQ and snippets present it as Acharya Ji's words, and no scriptural source is attached to it.
4. **SEO pressure: "satsang" as a head term.** People searching "satsang" often want events near them or bhajans. The title and meta make clear this is about how to listen, so the click matches the content; do not add event or "satsang near me" wording.
5. **Gendered example.** The "for the wife: stay calm" line is a humorous list of a listener blaming family members; the snippets here do not quote it, which keeps them neutral.
