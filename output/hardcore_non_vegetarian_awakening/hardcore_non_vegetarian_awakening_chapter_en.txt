# Chapter: Eat Yourself First, an Open Challenge

## The First Question

My first question is this: leave the children aside, first eat yourself and show me.

From tomorrow, give up chicken fry, brain fry, all of this and all of that. Let the poor chicken roam about a little. Instead, tomorrow cut off this much of your own body, just this much.

## An Open Question to the Hardcore Meat-Eater

This is for the one who eats meat with great relish, savouring every bite. To every such hardcore meat-eater, whatever religion they belong to, this is my open question.

Every day you have to cut off this small a piece of your own body. Put as much salt on it as you like, oil, ghee, do whatever you want. Add curd, add every vegetable in the world, turn it this way and that, chop it to pieces, make it halal, do whatever you want.

## Cut It While Chanting a Mantra

It is said, isn't it, that if something is cut while chanting a mantra, it becomes pure. Fine then, chant a mantra and cut.

> "I am not even asking you to take a life."

Just chant a mantra while cutting this small piece, then cut it and eat it. Will you be able to do it? Think it over and tell me.

## A Challenge for Every Person on Earth

This is an open challenge for every person on planet Earth. And I am not talking about a one-day stunt. Every day you have to cut one piece.

How big the piece is, that choice is yours. But it must be a sizeable chunk, it must at least be visible. By the way, how much do you usually eat? I am not even asking you to cut that much. I never said the size of a chicken or the size of a goat. Just this much.

Eat this much every day, cut off this much of a finger, fry it too, and eat it.

> "So who will be able to do it, tell me? This is an open challenge."
