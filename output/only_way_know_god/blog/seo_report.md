## 6. FAQ

1. **क्या मूर्ति पूजा गलत है? / Is murti worship wrong?**
   नहीं। आचार्य जी के अनुसार साकार पूजा पहली सीढ़ी है; रामकृष्ण परमहंस, तुकाराम और नामदेव भी पूजा करते थे, पर मर्म जानकर। / No. Worship of God with form is the first step; Ramakrishna Paramahamsa, Tukaram and Namdev worshipped too, but knowing its essence.
2. **जीवित गुरु क्यों चाहिए? / Why is a living Guru needed?**
   जिसने ईश्वर का स्वरूप देखा है वही उसका वर्णन कर सकता है, और मानचित्र में आज के गड्ढे नहीं दिखते; गुरु आज का चालक है। / Only one who has seen God's nature can describe it, and a map cannot show today's potholes; the Guru is the driver for today.
3. **"ब्रह्मणो रूपकल्पना" का क्या अर्थ है? / What does "brahmaṇo rūpakalpanā" mean?**
   जो ब्रह्म चिन्मय, अद्वितीय, अंशरहित और अशरीरी है, उसके रूप की कल्पना साधकों (उपासकों) के लिए की गई है। / Brahman is pure consciousness, without parts or body; a form for it is imagined for the sake of seekers (worshippers).
4. **क्या केवल शास्त्र पढ़ना काफ़ी है? / Are the scriptures alone enough?**
   आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि केवल मानचित्र भी व्यर्थ है, पर बिना मानचित्र के मार्ग कठिन है; शास्त्र और गुरु दोनों चाहिए। / A map alone gets you nowhere, yet the road is hard without one; you need both the scriptures and the Guru.
5. **"चमत्कार को नमस्कार" पर आचार्य जी क्या कहते हैं? / What does Acharya Ji say about bowing to miracles?**
   चमत्कार को नमस्कार मत करो; नमस्कार ठीक से करो, चमत्कार अपने आप होंगे। / Don't bow to miracles; bow properly, and miracles will follow on their own.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Verse reading (Major, from the Sanskrit review).** The second pada "साधकानां हितार्थाय" is the popular form; the Rama Purva Tapaniya Upanishad reads "उपासकानां कार्यार्थं". Recommendation: apply the review's fix (quote the source reading, or keep the popular form and say so in the reference). Until then, do not quote the second pada in titles, snippets or schema; the FAQ answer mentions only "brahmaṇo rūpakalpanā" and gives "seekers (worshippers)". The secondary keyword "रूपकल्पना का अर्थ" is safe either way.
2. **निष्कल translated as "निराकार" (Minor, from the review).** Hindi meaning should read "चिन्मय, अद्वितीय, निष्कल (अंशरहित) और अशरीरी" to match the English. The FAQ already uses "अंशरहित".
3. **IAST style (Minor, from the review).** "sādhakānāṁ" → "sādhakānāṃ", and dandas in place of "|" / "||".
4. **"Only way" claim.** The video is titled "The Only Way to Know Your God". Using "only way" in a title tag invites an exclusivity claim the blog does not argue in those words, and can read as clickbait. Recommendation: keep "only way" out of titles and metas (as done); the method is described as Acharya Ji's teaching.
5. **"गणपति का यह रूप असली नहीं" / "This form of Lord Ganesha is not the real one".** Out of context this could read as dismissing deity worship. The blog balances it ("पर गणपति का अर्थ असली है"; the saints did not reject the murti). Recommendation: never quote the first half alone in snippets, social cards or FAQ; use the pair "रूप काल्पनिक है, अर्थ सत्य है" / "the form is imagined, the meaning is real".
6. **Saints and murti worship.** The blog says that once a seeker reaches that state, murti worship is no longer needed. This is Acharya Ji's teaching, not a biographical claim that Ramakrishna, Tukaram or Namdev gave up worship. Recommendation: keep it attributed to Acharya Ji in any snippet; do not write "the saints stopped worshipping murtis".
