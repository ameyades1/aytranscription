# Chapter: What Is a Guru?

## The Guru: A Person, or the Door to the Supreme Truth

What is a Guru? Is the Guru only a person, or the very door to the Supreme Truth?

The scriptures say that without the Guru there is no knowledge, and without knowledge there is no liberation. Yet in our times, understanding a true Guru has become the hardest thing of all.

## The Guru Gita of the Skanda Purana

The **Guru Gita**, which appears in the Skanda Purana as a dialogue between Shiva and Parvati, is a divine scripture that reveals the true nature of the Guru, the Guru's glory, and the complete secret of the Guru-disciple relationship. It explains why the Guru is necessary, how the Guru's grace works, and how, by taking true refuge in the Guru, the ignorance of life comes to an end and every problem also finds its solution.

It is not merely a collection of verses but **a scripture that gives direction to life**. Jivanmukta Sadguru Brahmarshi Acharya Upendra Ji has presented this scripture in simple language, explaining the meaning of every verse with a moving commentary and with real examples from life, so that even an ordinary person can now read and understand it with great ease.

## The Release of the Book

This important scripture was released at the hands of Honourable Shri Nitin Gadkari Ji, Minister of Road Transport and Highways, Government of India. On this occasion he said:

> "We do not say, may I be well; we say, may the world be well. This is the greatest feature of our culture."

He expressed his confidence that, drawing inspiration from this, all our lives in society may become peaceful and the world may be well. He said that Acharya Ji gives everyone guidance for awakening this feeling, and for studying, reflecting on and reading these teachings on the basis of our religious modes of worship, especially from a **scientific point of view**. Greeting Acharya Ji, he expressed his faith that the work Acharya Upendra Ji has begun will surely succeed with God's blessings.

## Before Becoming the World's Guru

Acharya Upendra Ji says that everywhere today people are discussing that India should become the Vishwaguru, the Guru of the world.

> "If we are to become the Guru of the world, then everyone should first understand what a Guru is."

## Whose Refuge?

Shri Krishna too said in the Gita:

> **sarva-dharmān parityajya mām ekaṁ śaraṇaṁ vraja**
> **ahaṁ tvā sarva-pāpebhyo mokṣayiṣyāmi mā śucaḥ**
> "Abandoning all duties, take refuge in Me alone. I shall free you from all sins; do not grieve."
> (Bhagavad Gita 18.66)

That is: leave aside all dharmas and come to My refuge alone. I will free you from all sins, do not worry.

For Arjuna, at least, Shri Krishna was there in person. Now the question before us is: whose refuge should we take? Who is a Guru, how many kinds of Gurus are there, and what does one actually do after taking refuge?

Acharya Ji explains that no book or scripture is available today for this, and where a scripture is available, its commentary is not. So he wrote a commentary on the Guru Gita of the Skanda Purana in great detail, and in this publication he has **given everything**.
