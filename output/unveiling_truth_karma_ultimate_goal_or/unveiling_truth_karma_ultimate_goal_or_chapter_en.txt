# Chapter: The Staff of Karma and the Summit of Knowledge

## The Game of Pole Vault: Understanding Karma Yoga

Do you know this game or not? It is called pole vault. There is a long pole, with whose support the athlete leaps. Ahead, high up, a long bar is fixed, over which he has to cross.

But when he crosses that bar, does he take the pole along? Does he say, "No, I want this, I will keep it with me like a sword"? That does not happen. He has to let go of the pole, and only then does he reach the other side. Yet it is equally true that without this pole he could never reach that end at all.

This pole is **Karma Yoga**. And where you must reach is your **Jnana Yoga**; that is where steadfastness in knowledge lies. Ultimately, before death, that is where one has to reach, but without action no one can get there.

## Leaving Action and Going Straight to the Himalayas? Impossible

If someone says, I never performed any action, I simply gave up action and went to the Himalayas, that is not possible. What Arjuna says will simply not happen. It is impossible.

Devotion, knowledge and meditation were spoken of as higher than action; Yoga, then Karma Yoga and devotion were spoken of. Yet it was still said that giving up action is not possible; you will have to do it. The example of the pole vault is to explain exactly this. And that the pole must be dropped afterwards was also said later on.

## Only Action for the Whole of Life? That Won't Work Either

So shall I keep doing only action my whole life? That will not work either. Yes, for the major part of your life you can do so; that will work.

Running a full lap of the track, the athlete comes from so far away, running and running, and until then the pole travels with him. Then the pole plants, and on it, because of it, he rises. But after that he must let go of it. You have to drop the whole world. So the purpose of Karma Yoga is to take me one step further.

## Set a Target for Yourself

Let us say this is not understood today; even so, at least two years before death this state should come. But when death will come, no one knows. For that we would have to take up the eighth chapter again. The part of Nadi astrology that tells of death does not come to everyone either. Who has seen tomorrow?

So you need to set a target for yourself: at least for these many years I will work. It is not that the target will surely be achieved, because:

> **karmaṇy evādhikāras te mā phaleṣu kadācana**
> "Your right is to action alone, never to its fruits."
> (Bhagavad Gita 2.47)

But some definite limit there must be. "I will work my whole life" cannot happen. This much you should be fully aware of.

## Experience Old Age Today

Young people have great enthusiasm: I will work, I will work a lot. For them I have a suggestion. Spend a little money. Go to the market and buy a small chain, the kind tied to the feet of prisoners in jail. Tie it between your two feet. Then bend halfway down, and tie another chain from your neck to the chain on your feet. And then try walking.

Do you know why I am telling you this? When a person grows old, his gait becomes exactly like that. The foot moves only that much forward; one has to walk bent, stooping all the way. Even if he wants to, he cannot do anything. Now tell me, in that state how much work can you do?

First feel that state. Then you will understand: will I be able to act my whole life? The principles I want to live by, will they last until the end?

## At That Age, Only Two Things: Contemplation or Worry

And what will that time be like? At that time I will have nothing except contemplation, or else worry. Only these two things can exist at that age. And one day you will surely reach that state.

Is there any such agreement with anyone? You will say, "I won't stoop like that," but is there any guarantee? I will certainly grow old.

## Earning Wealth and Preparing for Old Age

Today, in youth, this is not understood. In young age it is not understood. But one who contemplates today will understand this and make some arrangement for income. Today you have money because you are working actively. You earned it and blew it. In old age your source of income will also close, because it ran on active work.

Since we are speaking of Karma Yoga, wealth will also come up. There is an aim in it; earning wealth is also an aim.

Now do you know what happens in old age? Your work stops, so the income coming from it also stops. Either it shrinks, or only a pension remains. On top of that, expenses are higher. Because your present actions will not have been anything particularly great, illnesses will also come. You will have indulged in anger and all of that; there was no Karma Yoga, so the outcome will be illness. And if illness comes, its expenses will rise.

So expenses have risen and income has fallen. On top of that, you will not have loved much. We stayed alone in the enthusiasm of our own work, so there will be no one to help either. Then a servant will have to be kept, and if a servant is kept, more money goes.

## The Karma Yogi Is One Who Knows Beforehand

Therefore the planning for this should happen from childhood: until when will I be able to work? It is good that you remain happy and healthy till the end of your life; we all will pray for that. But even by God's grace that capacity cannot return in old age.

This should be known in youth itself: one fine day I will grow old. From that angle, one day I should retire on my own. It should not be that one day the government forces me to retire, or nature does.

The person who works accordingly is called a **Karma Yogi**. He knows. His vision is clear: I have to work for ten years, after that this work will happen; that is my plan. Nadi astrology says that until two years before death, to the very end, you will have work. But I have a plan that I must do it this way.

## A Successor Equal to Oneself

When all this was settled, they also gave this same message to someone else: your dream will be fulfilled when you appoint someone equivalent to yourself, equal to you.

Because if you have become a guru, become a Guru Mauli (a guru like a mother), then you have many children. And if you have them, the mother cannot leave them and go. Yes, if she simply dies, what can anyone do; that is another matter. But otherwise she must hand them over to an alternative before going. Otherwise they will become like orphans, so it has been said, if the guru suddenly departs.

## Swami Vivekananda and Ramakrishna Paramahamsa

I too have been asking this question for six years now: when is retirement? Then an incident of Swami Vivekananda came to mind.

He too used to say exactly this to his guru: "Now I have received all the knowledge, what more is needed? Now I will go." Then Sri Ramakrishna said to him, "Will you become so selfish? You have attained knowledge; will you not now give it to people?"

By that one word he understood and began teaching others. To the very end, as you know, he had thirty-one illnesses. Despite all that, he worked for the world.

So do such work, but after a limit you have to stop. Is that clear?

## The Karma Yogi's End Point: A Resolve

A Karma Yogi decides an end point as well. When the end will come there is no guarantee, but he keeps an end point in mind.

Someone went to America for a job, a big job. Then he has to think: when do I come back to India? Or is it permanently there? You have to think. No planning at all, "if it goes, it goes": this "it goes" attitude does not work.

A Karma Yogi will have a definite end point in mind. This end point too is a **sankalpa** (resolve), an **iccha shakti** (willpower). I am not talking about a wish to die. There is a wish to work up to here, and after that there is also a wish to live in peace. That too is a wish. And for it one has to gain the knowledge and perform the corresponding action.
