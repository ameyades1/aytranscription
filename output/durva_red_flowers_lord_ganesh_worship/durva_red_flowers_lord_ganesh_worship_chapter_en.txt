# Chapter: Red Flowers and Durva, the Secret of Ganapati Worship

## The Color Red and Rajas Shakti

Lord Ganapati loves the red flower. Red means *rakta*, blood. His meditation verse returns to this red color again and again:

> **raktaṁ lambodaraṁ śūrpakarṇakaṁ raktavāsasam |**
> **raktagandhānuliptāṅgaṁ raktapuṣpaiḥ supūjitam ||**
> "I meditate on the red one, with a large belly and ears like winnowing fans, dressed in red, his limbs anointed with red sandal paste, well worshipped with red flowers."
> (Ganapati Atharvashirsha, dhyana mantra)

Here *rakta*, *rakta*, *rakta* is said again and again. It means **rajas shakti**, the energy of action. The Purana says that after slaying the demons, Ganapati smeared their blood all over his body. But in reality it means that **I must be ready to work with blood and sweat.** Only then does one become Vinayaka.

## Not Fearing the Battle

To destroy the demons, I will not live in fear. If someday I too must enter the battlefield, the only question is whether I will win or lose. In battle, both sides get their heads broken, both sides will bleed. That is, some energy will certainly be spent.

Red is the symbol of that rajas shakti. The whole sadhana of Ganapati is like this, and that is why the red flower offered to him is symbolic.

## Durva: Offering the Threes

Now about durva. Durva is the symbol of the sets of three that the scriptures speak of: **gunatraya** (the three gunas), **dehatraya** (the three bodies), **kalatraya** (the three times) and **avasthatraya** (the three states).

While offering durva, the feeling is that I am offering these one by one. As many as I can, I am offering.

## The Grass That Bends and the Tree That Breaks

Durva is a kind of grass. A big tree breaks in the storm because of its ego, but durva bends when the wind comes. This humility should be my state. Then my energy matches with Ganapati.

There is science behind this too, which is called energy. If an energy level like Ganapati's is measured, then according to science durva matches it.
