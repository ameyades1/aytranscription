# Why Ganapati Loves Red Flowers and Durva

We all offer red flowers and durva grass in Ganesh puja. But have you ever wondered what deep meaning lies behind them? Acharya Upendra Ji reveals the symbolic secret of this tradition.

## What Does the Color Red Say?

Ganapati loves the red flower. Red means *rakta*, blood. This word appears again and again in his meditation verse:

> **raktaṁ lambodaraṁ śūrpakarṇakaṁ raktavāsasam |**
> **raktagandhānuliptāṅgaṁ raktapuṣpaiḥ supūjitam ||**
> "I meditate on the red one, with a large belly and ears like winnowing fans, dressed in red, his limbs anointed with red sandal paste, well worshipped with red flowers."
> (Ganapati Atharvashirsha, dhyana mantra)

Red complexion, red clothes, red sandal paste, red flowers. This repeated "rakta" points to **rajas shakti** (the energy of action and movement).

## The Purana's Story, Life's Meaning

The Purana says that after slaying the demons, Ganapati smeared their blood all over his body. But its real meaning is connected to our own life. Acharya Ji says that I must be ready to work with blood and sweat; only then will you become Vinayaka.

Becoming Vinayaka does not happen through worship alone. It needs the readiness to work hard with complete dedication.

## Am I Afraid of the Struggle?

One must not live in fear of fighting the demons. If one must ever enter the battle, the only question is whether it will be victory or defeat.

In battle both sides get hurt, both sides bleed. That is, some energy must be spent. The red flower is the symbol of that rajas shakti.

*Which battle in your life are you backing away from out of fear?*

## Durva: Offering the Threes

Durva is the symbol of the sets of three described in the scriptures:

- **Gunatraya**: the three gunas
- **Dehatraya**: the three bodies
- **Kalatraya**: the three times
- **Avasthatraya**: the three states

While offering durva, the feeling is that I am offering these one by one to Ganapati, as much as I can.

## Bending Is What Saves

Durva is ordinary grass. But it holds a big lesson: a big tree breaks because of its ego, while durva bends even when the wind comes.

When this bending becomes my state, my energy matches with Ganapati. Acharya Ji says there is a science of energy behind this too: durva matches Ganapati's energy level.

*Is the "big tree" inside you waiting to break in some storm?*

## Key Takeaways

- The red flower symbolizes **rajas shakti**: the readiness to act with full effort.
- The story of smearing the demons' blood means working with blood and sweat.
- Do not fear the struggle; energy will be spent anyway.
- Durva symbolizes offering the gunatraya, dehatraya, kalatraya and avasthatraya.
- The humility of the bending grass aligns our energy with Ganapati.
