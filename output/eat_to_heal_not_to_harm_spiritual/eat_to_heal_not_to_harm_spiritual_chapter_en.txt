# Chapter: Food and Non-Violence, Offer Only What Grows Back

## A Seeker of Liberation Cannot Kill

If liberation (moksha) is the goal of life, it will become impossible for me to kill another being. Why? Because do I ever wish to cut off a part of my own body? Does anyone say, cut off my own limb?

When I begin to see that everything is God, God is me, I am That, then the same Supreme Lord is seen in and through everything. Then I will neither want to eat nor want to kill a part of my own self. Did you understand?

## Nails and Hair: What Returns Even When Cut

Suppose I consider myself one complete whole. If a nail is extra material in it, do I not cut it myself? I do not even feel pain. Even if I do, there is no worry, because a new one grows back. Isn't that right? It grows back, and that is exactly why we are able to cut it. But if someone said, cut off your finger, how many people would do it?

In our country there are great temples where people are asked to offer their hair. People say, "We have come back after offering our hair." Offering hair was meant to be the offering of one's desires and cravings, handing them over to God. The hair stands for that. That is why hair from all over the world goes there, wigs are made from it, much else happens, and money comes in. Are there not such temples in India?

People can give so much hair only because hair is capital that keeps growing back. Cut as much as you like, new hair is bound to come.

## What If a Finger Were Asked For?

If, even by mistake, it were announced that from tomorrow God will be pleased only if you cut off a small piece of your finger and offer it, tell me, how many people would go to the temple? How many would go?

No one, because people know hair is supposed to grow back. It is something that grows. If it is gone, what difference does it make? It will be back in a month.

## What Is Called Violence?

So if I offer a part of myself which causes me no pain to break off, or negligible pain, and which is going to grow back, can I call that violence? "Oh, how much hair he is cutting, what violence!" Does anyone say that? Now think and tell me.

Hair is fine for beauty, and in general it may serve some medical purpose, keeping off the sun. But there are bald people too. How much effect does it have? So even if hair is cut, does it make much difference?

My sweat is flowing, coming from somewhere. If I collected my sweat and gave it to someone or threw it away, would I grieve over that sweat? No, because new sweat is bound to come again. So such a thing is not called violence. But if a limb of mine is broken off, or I myself am killed, that is violence.

## The Secret of Vegetarian Food

That is why, do you know why vegetarian food is accepted? Because only that part of a tree or plant is plucked in which fruits and flowers come. The whole tree is not broken by it. That part grows back again.

It has very little sensation. Life energy (prana) remains in it for a long time. No curse attaches to it.

In the scriptures, the Goddess says that I will come as the power of Shakambhari, and you shall eat only vegetables, your food shall be vegetables.

> **tato'ham akhilaṁ lokam ātma-deha-samudbhavaiḥ |**
> **bhariṣyāmi surāḥ śākair āvṛṣṭeḥ prāṇa-dhārakaiḥ ||**
> **śākambharīti vikhyātiṁ tadā yāsyāmy ahaṁ bhuvi |**
> "O gods, then until the rains come I shall nourish the whole world with life-sustaining vegetables born of my own body, and I shall then become renowned on earth as Shakambhari."
> (Shri Durga Saptashati 11.48-49)

In this way, a diet of plants became so clear for human beings. That is why it is said so.
