# Six Hours of Sadhana in Ten Minutes with a Bija Mantra

## Can Sadhana Come in a Capsule?

To understand the bija mantra, take a simple example. When you eat spinach, you get iron. But an iron tablet or syrup gives the same result.

For vitamin D, sit in the sun, or take a tablet. The result is the same. A big meal and a small capsule both do the same job.

In the same way, changing the impressions of a whole lifetime and constantly holding high thoughts in the mind is not possible for an ordinary person. So the Guru gives him a small **mantra**, like a capsule.

## Which Mantra Are You Chanting Every Day?

The word **mantra** means: that through whose contemplation the mind is protected.

> **mananāt trāyate iti mantraḥ**
> "That which protects through contemplation is a mantra."
> (Traditional etymology)

Whatever we hold in the mind again and again becomes our mantra. Keep thinking "I am poor, I am poor," and that becomes the mantra, and the person stays poor. Keep repeating "My husband is bad," and he will never change.

Acharya Ji explains that whatever we hold in the mind non-stop, such as "I am poor," becomes a mantra.

The true mantra is the one that **frees** the mind from its problems.

*Which sentence did you repeat most in your mind today? Is it binding you or freeing you?*

## Bija Mantra: The Power Hidden in a Dot

A **bija mantra** (seed mantra) contains a dot, the **anusvara**, as in laṁ, kaṁ, caṁ. A sadhana made of such sounds is called a **bija mantra sadhana**.

These are called **nada** (silent sound). Their meaning is not grasped by ordinary intellect, because they work through the power of sound.

## Arjuna and Shri Rama Did This Sadhana Too

In the Mahabharata, Shri Krishna gave Arjuna the teaching of the Gita; everyone knows this. But before that, he had said: "I will not wield my discus; you must fight yourself."

Fighting needs three things: **power, wealth and wisdom**. So Arjuna was made to recite the **Durga Saptashati**, and only then did he win the war.

The same happened with Shri Rama. When Ravana carried Sita Mata away to Lanka, Shri Rama performed the Durga Saptashati sadhana, and by its strength defeated Ravana.

## Not a Choice, a Necessity

If you must overcome the great problems of your home and your country, or awaken the **kundalini shakti** (the dormant spiritual energy), the Durga Saptashati is not an option. It is essential.

## A Six-Hour Recitation: How Possible Is It Today?

One recitation of the Sanskrit Durga Saptashati takes at least three hours, and that only when the pronunciation is perfect. With the **samput** (mantras added before and after), it reaches five or six hours.

Then when will the **Shatachandi** and **Sahasrachandi** happen? And when will the rest of the work get done? After looking at many institutions and speaking with Brahmins, it became clear that this would not work.

## The Solution for Kali Yuga: The Tantric Bija Mantra Sadhana

It is said that in Kali Yuga, only the **yantras** and **tantras** of Kali will work. So Gurudev, in a state of meditation, composed the **tantric bija mantra sadhana** of the seven hundred verses of the Durga Saptashati.

According to Gurudev, it is not taken from anyone else but composed divinely. Those who understand bija mantras are negligible today, and being able to compose them is rarer still.

Acharya Ji explains that other Gurus give initiation, but they cannot create mantras.

This composition comes not from a passing thought but from a **siddhi**. The result is a mantra so short, yet one that should give its full **phalashruti** (the fruit of the sadhana).

## Now and Here

The fruit that comes from six hours of worship should come in ten or twenty minutes of sadhana. That is the promise of this sadhana.

Acharya Ji says that he believes in the now and here.

*Have you been putting off your sadhana for lack of time? What if ten minutes were enough?*

## Key Takeaways

- A bija mantra is like a capsule: small, yet giving the full result.
- The thought we hold in the mind again and again becomes our mantra.
- The true mantra frees the mind from its problems.
- Both Arjuna and Shri Rama performed the Durga Saptashati sadhana before their victories.
- For solving great problems and awakening the kundalini, the Durga Saptashati is essential.
- The tantric bija mantra sadhana was composed to give the fruit of a six-hour recitation in ten or twenty minutes.
