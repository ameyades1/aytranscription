# Chapter: The Bija Mantra Durga Saptashati

## The Bija Mantra: A Capsule of Sadhana

First, let us see what a bija mantra is. A bija mantra is like a capsule. When you eat spinach, you get iron, and much more besides; that is why people speak of green leafy vegetables. But there is also an iron tablet, and an iron syrup, and they give the same result.

Eat fruit, eat everything, and you will get all the nourishment. But you can also get something as small as Glucon. Tablets of vitamins and minerals are available too. To get it from food you would have to eat so much, yet the result is the same. Take a vitamin D tablet or sit in the sunlight; the result is one and the same.

In the same way, changing the impressions (samskaras) of a whole lifetime, changing one's thinking, and holding high thoughts in the mind without a break is not possible for an ordinary person. So the Guru gives him something like a capsule: a small mantra.

## The Meaning of Mantra

The meaning of mantra is:

> **mananāt trāyate iti mantraḥ**
> "That which protects through contemplation is a mantra."
> (Traditional etymology)

Whatever we have held in the mind without a break becomes a mantra. If I keep thinking, "I am poor, I am poor, I am poor," that becomes my mantra. Then I can never become rich; I will always remain poor. If someone keeps thinking, "My husband is bad, bad, bad," he will remain bad; he can never change.

So whatever is held through contemplation is also a mantra. And the true mantra is the one that frees the mind from its problems.

## How to Recognise a Bija Mantra

A bija mantra contains a dot, the anusvara, as in laṁ, kaṁ, caṁ. A sadhana made of such sounds is called a bija mantra sadhana. This sadhana of seven hundred verses is extremely powerful.

## The Origin of the Durga Saptashati: Arjuna and Shri Rama

Its origin is this. When Arjuna had to win the Mahabharata war, Shri Krishna gave him the teaching of the Bhagavad Gita; this we all know. But before that, he had said, "I will not wield my discus; you must fight yourself."

To fight, one needs the union of power, wealth and wisdom. Strength is needed, money is needed, everything is needed, and intelligence too. That is why Arjuna too was made to recite the Durga Saptashati, and only then did he win the war.

The same happened with Shri Rama. When Ravana carried Sita Mata away to Lanka, Shri Rama too had to perform the Durga Saptashati sadhana, and only by its strength could he defeat Ravana.

## Not a Choice, but a Necessity

So first understand its importance. If you must overcome the great problems of your home and your country, if you must awaken the kundalini shakti, then the Durga Saptashati is not an option; it is essential.

## The Difficulty of the Traditional Recitation

The Durga Saptashati is recited in many large institutions. If you regularly read the Sanskrit Durga Saptashati, it takes at least three hours, and that too for someone whose Sanskrit pronunciation is perfect. If you add the samput, with mantras before and after, it will take five or six hours. That is just one recitation.

Then when will you do the Shatachandi and the Sahasrachandi? And if you keep doing only this, when will you do the rest of your work? It is impossible. I looked at many institutions, spoke with Brahmins, and realised that this would not work.

## The Solution for Kali Yuga: The Tantric Bija Mantra Sadhana

That is why a rare truth has been stated: in Kali Yuga, only the yantras and tantras of Kali will work. So I had to compose, in a state of meditation, the seven hundred verses of the tantric bija mantra sadhana. This is not taken from someone else; it has truly been composed divinely.

People do not understand bija mantras; they do not understand their meaning. They are called silent sound, nada. Understanding them is far off; one must know how to compose them, and only then does their poetry take shape. Today those who understand them are negligible. And the one who can compose them: today, on planet earth, there is only one person, sitting alive in front of you. Only one knows how to compose the mantras of tantra. Other Gurus give initiation, but they cannot create mantras.

## The Fruit of Six Hours in Ten Minutes

I have recorded that entire design. I create something new, but not because it came to my mind; it is a siddhi. After that, the mantra becomes so short, yet you should receive its full phalashruti. The fruit that comes after six hours of worship should come to you in just ten or twenty minutes of sadhana.
