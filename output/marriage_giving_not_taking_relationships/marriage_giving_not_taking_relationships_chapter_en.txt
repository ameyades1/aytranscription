# Chapter: Marriage: A Relationship of Giving, Not Taking

## Marriage Is Not a Business

Many people think, "I am married now, so my husband should give me love; that is his duty." In truth, asking for love is not my duty. I did not come here to do business.

So many people feel that marriage means going there in order to be happy. This is a very great misunderstanding. Both think, we are married, now I am happy, and then they live unhappily all their lives. The reason is that they forgot:

> "This marriage was not about taking; it was about giving."

## Giving From Your Own Abilities

Whatever you know, whatever your talent is, whatever you understand, that is what you are to give. If a woman knows how to cook well, let her give that. If she knows how to earn, let her give that too. The scriptures describe the different forms of a wife: in a spirit of friendship, as a minister, through dialogue and planning, she also lends her support. The pleasure of intimacy is also described; her role in the bedroom is mentioned as well. Cooking and feeding like a mother is another of her forms. She has many different roles.

But all these actions were hers to do for her own purification, simply for the sake of doing them.

The same holds for the husband. If I know how to earn, if I can do something more; if I enjoy travelling, then I should take her travelling too; if I can offer protection, or whatever my task may be, that is what I must do. Have you understood or not?

## Dutiful Action: A Means of Purifying the Mind

> "I am born to give, not to take."

The action I perform now will be my dutiful action. It exists only to purify the mind, not for trade. Getting something out of it is not its purpose at all. Attaining things happens through something else. It is said that **things are attained through thought alone.**

"This wife is a goddess, I am making her happy, she has become happy": all of this, too, is something you yourself have to think.

## Understanding a Woman's Mind

Then one must also gain knowledge of what is to be done in a marriage. There is a woman. What is a woman like? What are her qualities? Acharya Chanakya says that trying to understand this, perhaps twenty-seven lifetimes will pass, and still it will not be understood.

Everything is taught, but what a woman is and what her mind is, that is difficult to understand. When one cannot even understand one's own mind, understanding a woman's mind is extremely difficult. Chanakya says, do not even get into it. It is the learned Chanakya saying this, not I.

But do understand this much: she is a woman. Being a woman, she will be restless; at times there will be emotional ups and downs. She lives at the level of the mind. You live by awareness of the body or awareness of the intellect, so you can stay hard. But for her, the same thing becomes a page of history. She feels it deeply, she cries over it, and she will remember everything in the world.

So you should have enough knowledge of how to treat her, that she is delicate, what she is like.

## My Wife's Nature and My Karma

On top of this, one must also see whether my wife is rajasic, tamasic or sattvic. What is her desire? This can be understood even at the time of marriage. Am I giving her what she needs or not? That is my action for my own purification.

If I got a quarrelsome, nagging wife, that is my past prarabdha. What I now do with her is my karma. She came to cut away my karma. Whether to divorce or to live with love can be my new decision, my new thought.

Is this much understood? In the end, everything comes down to thought alone.
