*Is marriage a deal for happiness?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that marriage is for giving, not taking. A wife who cooks well gives that; a husband who can protect gives that. All to purify the mind.

💭 _In your relationships, do you think more about what you can give, or what you can get?_
