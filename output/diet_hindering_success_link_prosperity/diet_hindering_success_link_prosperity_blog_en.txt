# Do Your Earnings Change Your Food

We think the effect of food depends only on what is on the plate. Acharya Upendra Ji goes further.

> "Your food is also connected to how you earn your money, did you ever know that?"

## The Money Behind the Plate

Food is said to have three qualities: **sattvic** (pure, calm), **rajasic** (stimulating) and **tamasic** (bringing inertia). Acharya Ji explains that the fruit of food also depends on the money it came from.

- Sattvic food cooked with black money gives a different fruit.
- Tamasic food eaten by mistake with hard-earned, honest money is a different combination.
- Money earned with a rajasic **vritti** (way of earning), followed by Ayurvedic food on a doctor's advice, also has a different result.

*The food on your plate: what money did it come from?*

## Can Looted Money Give Pure Food?

Someone steals, causes people suffering, takes money by looting, and then eats the most sattvic food with that money.

> "But will this person get the same phalashruti? It would have been done by snatching someone's due, by crooked means."

**Phalashruti** means the fruit of an action. The food may be sattvic, but money taken by snatching someone's due changes its fruit.

## When a Good Person Is Deceived

A hard-working, sattvic person has little money because of **prarabdha** (the fruit of past karma). A wicked person mixes something wrong into his food.

Acharya Ji asks: will his devotion, his **sattva guna** and his **dravya shuddhi** (purity of wealth) not protect him?

## Meerabai's Devotion

> "If this were not so, Meerabai could not have digested the poison despite being given it. Yet she digested it, through her devotion."

Devotion within overcame the poison from outside.

*What protects you, your food or your devotion?*

## Key Takeaways

- The fruit of food is also tied to the money it came from.
- The way you earn and the quality of food together decide the result.
- Sattvic food bought with unjust money does not give its full fruit.
- The devotion of a sattvic, devoted person protects them even from wrong food.
- Meerabai digested poison through devotion.
