# Chapter: The Purity of Food and Wealth

## The Link Between Food and Money

Food is connected not only to what lies on your plate but also to the way you earn your money. Have you ever thought about this?

If someone earned money wrongfully, as black money, and with it cooked sattvic food at home, the result will be different. On the other hand, if someone's money came from honest hard work, from satsang, from labouring among good people, and by mistake they ate tamasic food, that too is a different combination. Someone earns with a rajasic disposition, then on a doctor's advice begins to eat sattvic, Ayurvedic food, and the result of that may also be different.

This is a new perspective. Are you understanding what I am saying?

## Sattvic Food Bought with Unjust Money

Suppose I steal outside, cause people suffering, take money through plunder. Then with that money, however sattvic the food I take, whether it was stolen, or obtained through some half-baked deal in a rajasic way, or I come home and cook it myself in a sattvic state, will such a person receive the same fruit?

He earned that wealth by snatching someone's due and by wrongdoing. The food is sattvic, it remains what it is, but the impurity of the wealth changes its fruit.

## The Sattvic Person and Impurity from Outside

Now take a second example. A person is very hardworking, very sattvic, and his conduct outside is good. For some reason, due to prarabdha (destiny from past karma), he has little money or none at all. The person in front of him is wicked; he mixed something into this man's food, gave him something tamasic, mixed something wrong into his meal.

So what will the result be? Will his devotion, his sattva guna, his sattvic **dravya-shuddhi** (purity of wealth) not protect him at least a little?

## Mirabai and the Power of Devotion

If that were not so, how could what happened to Mirabai have happened? Mirabai was given poison. It was a poison she could not have digested, yet she digested it, through her devotion.

> "Even though Mirabai was given poison, she could not have digested it. Then she digested it through her devotion."

Is this making sense or not? This is one more aspect of food: not just the food itself, but the money that buys it and the inner purity of the one who eats it, all of these together decide its result.
