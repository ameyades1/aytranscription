# Chapter: Food, Money and Devotion

## Food and Money

Do you know that your food is also connected to how you earn your money?

If you earned black money and cooked sattvic food at home with it, its fruit will be different. And if your money is white, hard-earned, come by honestly and while doing good to people, but by mistake you ate tamasic food, that too is a combination.

If someone earned money with a rajasic disposition and later, on a physician's advice, ate sattvic Ayurvedic food, the result can again be different. Are you understanding this new angle?

## Sattvic Food from Stolen Money

Suppose I steal a great deal outside, harass people, take money by looting. Then, with that money, I take food however sattvic, whether by stealing it, or getting rajasic food through some half-baked deal, or coming home in a sattvic state and cooking it myself. Will such a person get the same phalashruti?

He has done all this by snatching someone's due, by crooked means. The food is sattvic, but will the fruit be the same?

## Poison in a Good Man's Food

On the other side is a hard-working person. He worked very hard, he is very sattvic, his conduct is good. Yet for some reason, due to prarabdha, he has little money or none.

The person facing him is wicked. He mixed something into his food, gave him something tamasic, mixed in something wrong. So what will the result be? Will his devotion, his sattva guna, his sattvic purity of wealth not protect him even a little?

## Meerabai

If that were not so, Meerabai could not have digested the poison she was given. But she digested it through her devotion.

Is this making sense? This is one more aspect of food.
