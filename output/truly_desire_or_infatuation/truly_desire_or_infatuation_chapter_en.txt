# Chapter: The True Meaning of Love and Kama

## Where Love Comes, Kama Will Come

The scriptures say that where love has come, kama will surely come. But in our definition we have counted only marriage, or the bond between a boyfriend and a girlfriend, as the subject of love. In Kaliyuga our thinking goes only that far. So was everything else not love? Where there is love, kama will come along with it on its own.

## Two Levels of Desire

Kama brings kamana, that is, desire. There is a low level of desire and a higher level of desire. The scriptures do not even count the low level of desire; leave that aside, it will go into sakama bhakti (devotion with selfish motive). But a higher level of energy, lofty thoughts about the good of another, all this we can certainly do, can we not?

We say that love should be selfless, free of desire. And here it was said that kama joins it on its own. If love has come, kama will surely come.

## The True Definition of Kama

Kama means some desire. Kama also means kamavasana, that is, touch. So touch will also come. But the real definition of kama is not what we read in the Kamashastra.

Understand it according to the Vishnu tattva and the Lakshmi tattva. I stroke even a dog with my hand, don't I? I touched it, so there was touch. There was love in it, and the feeling that some good may come to it. A friend came, so I shook his hand; there was touch, and there was some desire, which would be of a higher level. This is what is called kama.

> "Where there is love, there will be touch."

Someone came to give a blessing and placed a hand on the head; there was touch. That too is touch, and there is a desire within it. Love is not limited to marriage. First understand this much. What was in the mind until now was madness.

## Kalki: Avatar of Kama and Avatar of Love

In Kaliyuga people will not understand this. Shri Krishna said that even in this avatar of mine it was difficult to make people understand, though I am only the avatar of love. When I come as the avatar of kama, what will happen? The avatar of kama means teaching kama, and the avatar of love means teaching love.

Therefore it was said of the form of Kalki that he will have to come as both the avatar of kama and the avatar of love, to explain what kama is. And this will be difficult. Krishna at least did not have to explain kama; he had to teach love.

It is said that in Kaliyuga the greatest problem will be that of kama. Man takes kama itself to be love: "We have fallen in love." That was never love at all; it was lust. And what he goes on to do is not even called kama purushartha.

## The Example of the Doctor

Now understand the concept. There is a doctor, a gynecologist. He has to touch; it is his profession. So will his wife say, "How many women have you married? How much kama is this?" She is interfering in his work; on the contrary, she is the one heading towards divorce.

There will be touch, but what is the intention behind it? He loves the patient, but not in the sense you are thinking. His love is for his profession, and touch happens. This is what is called kama.

> "Love is not limited to marriage."

Is the concept clear?
