## 1. Summary

The talk (Ganesh Series, episode 7) asks a question every family faces before Ganesh Chaturthi: who decides the form of the Ganpati idol we bring home? Idols now come as politicians, soldiers, schoolchildren or Sai Baba. Acharya Ji's answer is that you may begin with your own taste, but the form should come from the sages' description, because Ganeshotsav is not a decorating contest but the journey from nar to Narayan. Worship of the form (sakar), done with knowledge, is the ladder to the formless (nirakar), and the idol is only the "balwadi" of sadhana. The main intent is practical and seasonal ("गणपति की मूर्ति कैसी होनी चाहिए", "how to choose a Ganesh idol", "which Ganpati idol to bring home"), with an informational side ("why every Ganesh idol looks different"). Ranking pages for these queries are mostly shopping guides (eco-friendly, size, trunk direction, vastu). None of them asks whether the form itself should follow scripture; that is the angle only this talk offers. The opportunity peaks every year in the 4–6 weeks before Ganesh Chaturthi (Bhadrapada Shukla Chaturthi, usually late August or September), when idol booking happens.

## 6. FAQ

1. **गणपति की मूर्ति कैसी होनी चाहिए? / What kind of Ganesh idol should we bring home?**
   ऋषियों ने गणपति का जो स्वरूप वर्णित किया है, उसी रूप की मूर्ति। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि अपनी कल्पना पहले साल के लिए ठीक है, पर उसके बाद मूल रूप आना चाहिए। / One in the form the sages described. Acharya Ji says your own idea is fine for the first year, but after that the idol should take the original form.

2. **हर गणपति की मूर्ति अलग क्यों दिखती है? / Why does every Ganesh idol look different?**
   क्योंकि आजकल रूप दुकानदार और लोगों की पसंद से तय होता है: नेता, सिपाही, संत या स्कूल के बच्चे जैसे गणपति। आचार्य जी का प्रश्न है कि यह रूप किसकी पसंद से आता है। / Because today the form is often set by shopkeepers and buyers' taste: Ganpati as a politician, a soldier, a saint or a schoolchild. Acharya Ji asks whose choice that form really reflects.

3. **क्या गणपति को साईं बाबा या किसी और रूप में बनाना गलत है? / Is it wrong to make Ganpati in the form of Sai Baba or another figure?**
   आचार्य जी साईं बाबा या ज्ञानेश्वर को परमात्मा का रूप मानने से मना नहीं करते। उनका कहना है कि गणपति का वर्णन अलग है; साईं बाबा की पूजा करनी हो तो सीधे साईं बाबा की करें। / Acharya Ji does not deny that Sai Baba or Dnyaneshwar may be forms of God. His point is that Ganpati is described differently, and if you want to worship Sai Baba, worship Sai Baba directly.

4. **मूर्ति पूजा का उद्देश्य क्या है? / What is the purpose of worshipping an idol?**
   निराकार परमात्मा तक पहुंचना। ऋषियों द्वारा वर्णित साकार स्वरूप सीढ़ी है, और साकार-निराकार दोनों की पूजा ज्ञान के साथ करने से ही वह अवस्था मिलती है। / To reach the formless Divine. The form the sages described is the ladder, and only worship of both form and formless, with knowledge, leads there.

5. **मूर्ति को साधना की पहली सीढ़ी क्यों कहा गया है? / Why is the idol called the first step of sadhana?**
   आचार्य जी मूर्ति को बालवाड़ी जैसा बताते हैं: बच्चे को पहली कक्षा में जाना ही है। पहले ऋषियों का स्वरूप, फिर उसका प्रतीकात्मक अर्थ समझना, यही आगे की यात्रा है। / Acharya Ji compares it to kindergarten: the child must move up to first grade. First the sages' form, then understanding its symbolic meaning, and that is how the journey continues.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **"सोला" vs "sovale" (Sanskrit review issue 1, not yet applied).** The Hindi and English name different garments. Check the audio and align them before publishing. No title, alt text or snippet here uses either word.
2. **"मूर्ति शून्य से छह वर्ष की आयु के लिए है" (review issue 2, not yet applied).** This is Acharya Ji's balwadi analogy for the first stage of sadhana, not a rule about a person's age or a scriptural limit on murti worship. Apply the review's bridging wording. Never write "idol worship is only for children" or similar in a title, quote card or YouTube description: it misrepresents the talk and would offend readers. FAQ Q5 keeps the analogy explicit.
3. **"The scriptures describe Him" (English) vs "उनका वर्णन / ऋषियों का वर्णन" (Hindi).** The talk names no specific text (Ganapati Atharvashirsha, Mudgala Purana, Ganesha Purana or a dhyana shloka). The meta texts and FAQ say "the sages' description", as the Hindi does. Do not add a claim such as "the Ganesha Purana forbids theme idols". If the editor wants to make the point concrete, cite an actual dhyana shloka after a Sanskrit review.
4. **Right-trunk and left-trunk idols.** Searchers often ask which trunk direction is auspicious. The talk mentions both only as examples of variety and makes no claim; do not target "dahine soond wale ganpati" or promise an answer the post does not give.
5. **Sai Baba and other figures.** Acharya Ji is respectful ("साईं बाबा भी परमात्मा का रूप होंगे"). Do not use headlines like "Stop making Ganpati as Sai Baba" or anything that reads as an attack on Sai Baba's devotees; keep the talk's framing (follow Ganpati's own description; worship Sai Baba directly if that is your wish).
6. **"ऋषि-मुनियों ने शायद... कहा होगा" / "the sages probably said".** This is Acharya Ji's surmise, not a quotation. Keep the hedge if the line is reused; don't present it as a scriptural saying.
