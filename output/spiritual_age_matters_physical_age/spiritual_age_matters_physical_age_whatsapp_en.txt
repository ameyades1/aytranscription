*How old are you, spiritually?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that spiritual age is measured by understanding, not years. A ninety-five-year-old who visits temples only for the decorations is spiritually zero to six.

💭 _At the temple, do you seek the meaning behind the idol, or admire the decorations?_
