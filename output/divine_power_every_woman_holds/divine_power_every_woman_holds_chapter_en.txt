# Chapter: Woman, a Thousandfold Power and Responsibility

## The Question of Equality

We are always fighting over who is superior, woman or man. Today there is talk of women's liberation, women's rights, human rights and freedom. Movements go on for all of these.

On this I always say one thing. Women are fighting to become equal to men, but I say with full authority:

> "Women were never equal to men, in any age. They were always at least a thousand times more powerful than them."

They are only thinking about equality, while in truth they are a thousand times more powerful.

## Why Woman Is Called Shakti

That is why our tradition calls woman **Shakti** (power). Goddess Parvati is called Shakti, Lakshmi is called Shakti, Saraswati is called Shakti.

You will say that physically it does not look like that; she is even called *abalā*, the weak one. But this power is not in the language of body building, of how much weight she lifts or what she does. Even so, she is powerful.

## A Mother Holds a Thousand Times the Power

A woman carries a very great responsibility. Which one? It concerns the time when she gives birth to a son or a daughter. At that time, did that mother not have at least a thousand times more power than the child?

Look at Gandhari, the mother of Duryodhana. When Duryodhana was small, did Gandhari not have at least a thousand times more power than him? From the moment he was born, when she was rocking him in the cradle, look from that time; when he was at school, look then. That power was not used then.

If the child is spoiled with so much pampering, if he is not given knowledge at the right time, and one is oneself rajasic and tamasic, then the sin of that child's whole life also comes upon the mother.

## Every Woman's Responsibility

You may say, I never married. Even so, you will be someone's sister, you will be in some relationship or other. So women have a great many karmas.

> "She has not been called a sinner; a great responsibility rests upon her."

This is not said to blame women. It is said because the one who holds a thousand times the power also carries the greatest responsibility.
