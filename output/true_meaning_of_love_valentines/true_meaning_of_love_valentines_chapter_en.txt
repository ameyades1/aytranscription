# Chapter: The True Nature of Love

## The Incarnations of Love and Their Separation

Love: we all speak this word so easily. But first understand why those who were themselves the incarnations of love had to be separated. Why did they part, and how did they remain whole within despite the separation? This must be understood. It will not come to you in a day, and certainly not in a moment.

Why is their relationship not called adultery? Why did they become the very image of love? Because for many births they have been Lakshmi and Vishnu themselves. They came to play out a divine *leela*. I am telling you this only to help you understand.

## Love Is God: The Lakshmi Principle

Love is God. Love is the Lakshmi principle. In the worship of Mahalakshmi that we will do, the divine wealth I spoke of will certainly include love. Causeless compassion, fearlessness, the feeling that this whole world is my own, not becoming angry even at someone who did wrong, forgiving: what are all these? They are all signs of love. And all of these lead us to the heart chakra, to love.

## Is Love Only Between Husband and Wife?

We feel that love exists only between husband and wife. But in Kaliyuga it will be least there; the Devi Purana itself says so. It will be outside, because there is more time of contact outside. This is not my word; it is the word of Swami Vivekananda, the word of the Purana. In Kali especially, outside.

Look, there has just been a quarrel at home, and someone else comes to the house. At once the voice of the girl and of the boy changes. For that person, everything becomes loving. And here, where love should be, why not? This relationship is not normal, understand. It may be someone of the opposite sex; it may be anyone. One has just scolded, and a friend of the husband or of the household arrives, and so that they do not sense anything odd, the tone changes instantly.

So first understand the nature of this feeling that arises. We are not promoting adultery. Why it happens, what its nature is: to show this, the story of their births has been told. The question arises that if the two are opposites, then desire will naturally come in too. If we count human beings only as opposite sexes, then these two things, love and desire, must first be understood properly and separately.

## Entering Household Life Does Not Bring God

If love is God, then it cannot be that I entered the householder stage of life and found God. If that were so, there would be no purpose to the stages of Vanaprastha and Sannyasa. Liberation while living after Sannyasa, samadhi, kaivalya: none of these would be needed. God is found after all of these, and only then is real love understood.

> **bahūnāṃ janmanām ante jñānavān māṃ prapadyate**
> **vāsudevaḥ sarvam iti sa mahātmā sudurlabhaḥ**
> "At the end of many births, the wise one, knowing 'all is Vasudeva', surrenders to Me; such a great soul is very rare."
> (Bhagavad Gita 7.19)

If I find the Supreme Lord in the final birth, and the Supreme Lord is what we call love, then whatever I called love before my final birth was not love.

## What Is It That We Call Love?

It will be either attachment, or some desire to control. It will be possessiveness, insecurity, or holding someone stuck, forcing them, making them subordinate to oneself, casting a spell over them. It may be any of these, but it will not be love. Or it will be lust, that is, sensual desire.

> "All the other relationships that you call love are not love."

Until the second-to-last birth these relationships are not love, because God is understood only in the final birth, and the nature of God itself is called love.

> **īśvaraḥ sarva-bhūtānāṃ hṛd-deśe 'rjuna tiṣṭhati**
> "O Arjuna, the Lord dwells in the heart of all beings."
> (Bhagavad Gita 18.61)

Whenever that nature of God appears in your heart, for example if that feeling of a mother arises toward her son even for a moment, it can be called love. Yes, it can.

## Love Flows Both Ways

Does love flow from both sides, or does a one-sided affair also work? You have all seen that film. The hero brings a flower and plucks its petals one by one: she loves me, she loves me not, she loves me, she loves me not. At the end one petal is left, and in his joy he jumps down from the terrace. This is one-sided. Does it have anything to do with love?

> "One-sided love is not called love at all."

It is a waste of time. When the love of the one you love also comes back, then it is love. Krishna's love without Radha's will not do. Sita's and Rama's, both are needed, from both sides.

I love the nation, and the nation, thinking me a traitor, puts me in jail: what is the use? You stole, and you think you have done much for the country. Love must be equal from both sides.

## The Meaning of the Word Hridaya

I explained the meaning of the word *hridaya* (heart) earlier. "Hri" means *harati*: I run to people again and again and give to them, and people give to me as well. "Da" means *dadati*, giving: I give something of my own to the other, and in the same way they give something special of their own to me. And "Ya" means *yama*: till my last breath, till the last minute, even taking small breaks, I keep doing this as much as I can.

## A Mother's Attachment

A mother loves her son, and keeps on loving him. The son even ran away with his wife, married outside the caste. Still the mother says, "This property is yours, I will give it to you." She will not give it to the guru. Saying "blood is thicker than water", she gives it there. This is not called love, because nothing comes back in return. It is called a mother's attachment, not love.

## Understanding the Lakshmi Principle Before Shri Vidya

First try to understand the subject of love, what the Lakshmi principle is. Until this is understood, advanced Shri Vidya does not begin. The seeker does not even understand where to love and where not to, and whether it is attachment that is arising.

## The Nation and Those Who Serve It

Some cricketer of this country has done very good work, he is serving the nation, and if the nation does not honour him with something like the Bharat Ratna, it is incomplete. What happened recently with the "God of Cricket" was right; there the love was from both sides. The soldier who wins the Param Vir Chakra brings victory, and the nation too honours him. Only when the nation does as much as he does is it called love. Love must come from both sides; it never happens from one side.

## The True Meaning of Pativrata Dharma

The wife walks behind her husband, and the husband does not even look at her, looks elsewhere or does something wrong: this cannot be love. This is not *pativrata dharma* (a wife's devotion) at all.

The husband always asks for liquor and the wife keeps serving it to him: this is not being a pativrata. If her love can make him give up liquor, then she is a pativrata; so it is said. He may drink, that is his *prarabdha* (destiny); but what she does with him, that is love.

This much of the foundation is now understood: love is never one-sided.
