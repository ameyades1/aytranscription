*Do we really know what love is?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that love is God, marked by compassion, fearlessness and forgiveness. Often what we call love is attachment: we quarrel at home, yet our voice turns sweet when a guest arrives.

💭 _Do you save your gentlest voice for family or for outsiders?_
