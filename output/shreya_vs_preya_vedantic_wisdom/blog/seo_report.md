## 1. Summary

Acharya Upendra Ji takes the Katha Upanishad's teaching of shreya (the good) and preya (the pleasant) (1.2.2) and uses it to explain what noble action is: its fruit is sattvic (Bhagavad Gita 14.16), the desire for it awakens only when the goal of life is to rise "from nara to Narayana", and even a business can become noble action when knowledge guides it. The talk ends with Karma Yoga: "not everything is mine" and work as worship. The main search intent is informational ("shreya and preya meaning", "श्रेय और प्रेय क्या है"), with a strong emotional and life-choice side ("do what you like or do what is right"). Most pages that rank now are short dictionary entries or quote cards for the Katha Upanishad verse. The opportunity is a page that goes from the verse to everyday choices (anger, revenge, business, work), which is the angle only this talk offers. Hindi and English both fit the `dharma` category (Dharma & Scriptures), where `gallery_topics.txt` already lists this talk.

## 5. Direct answer paragraph

**Hindi (≈55 words):** कठोपनिषद् (१.२.२) के अनुसार मनुष्य के सामने दो मार्ग आते हैं: श्रेय और प्रेय। श्रेय वह है जो श्रेष्ठ है, जिसे शास्त्र आदर्श और कर्तव्य बताते हैं; प्रेय वह है जो केवल प्रिय लगता है। आचार्य उपेंद्र जी बताते हैं कि रजस और तमस वाले लोग प्रेय के पीछे चलते हैं, जबकि सात्त्विक व्यक्ति श्रेय चुनता है, जिसका फल सुख, ज्ञान और वैराग्य है।

**English (≈57 words):** In the Katha Upanishad (1.2.2), two paths come before every person: shreya, the good, and preya, the pleasant. Shreya is what the scriptures hold up as the ideal and as your duty; preya is simply what you like. Acharya Upendra Ji explains that people driven by rajas and tamas chase preya, while a sattvic person chooses shreya, whose fruit is happiness, knowledge and detachment.

## 6. FAQ

For the page or for later use; answers come only from the blog and its cited verses.

1. **श्रेय और प्रेय क्या है? / What are shreya and preya?**
   श्रेय वह है जो श्रेष्ठ है, जिसे शास्त्र आदर्श और कर्तव्य बताते हैं; प्रेय वह है जो मुझे प्रिय लगता है। कठोपनिषद् (१.२.२) कहता है कि दोनों मनुष्य के सामने आते हैं। / Shreya is what is truly higher, what the scriptures call the ideal and your duty; preya is whatever you happen to like. The Katha Upanishad (1.2.2) says both present themselves to every person.
2. **श्रेष्ठ कर्म का फल क्या है? / What is the fruit of noble action?**
   गीता (१४.१६) के अनुसार अच्छे कर्म का फल सात्त्विक और निर्मल है: सुख, ज्ञान और वैराग्य। / According to the Bhagavad Gita (14.16), the fruit of good action is sattvic and pure: happiness, knowledge and detachment.
3. **श्रेष्ठ कर्म की इच्छा कब जागती है? / When does the desire for noble action awaken?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार, जब जीवन का उद्देश्य ही नर से नारायण बनना हो। यह उद्देश्य न हो, तो मन अपने आप प्रेय की ओर जाता है। / In Acharya Ji's teaching, when the purpose of life is to rise from nara to Narayana. Without that purpose, the mind drifts toward preya on its own.
4. **क्या व्यापार भी श्रेष्ठ कर्म हो सकता है? / Can business be noble action?**
   हां, जब वह ज्ञान युक्त हो: निरंतर सीखना, सबसे श्रेष्ठ उत्पाद, और बदला लेने के बजाय अनुभव से सीखना। / Yes, when knowledge guides it: constant learning, the best possible product, and learning from bitter experiences instead of taking revenge.
5. **श्रेय का मार्ग कर्मयोग तक कैसे ले जाता है? / How does the path of shreya lead to Karma Yoga?**
   सत्त्वगुण बढ़ने पर "सब कुछ मेरा नहीं है" का भाव आता है, व्यक्ति उत्साह और प्राण शक्ति से कर्म करता है और कर्म ही उसकी पूजा बन जाता है। / As sattva grows, the feeling "not everything is mine" takes root; the person works with enthusiasm and prana shakti, and work itself becomes worship.

## 10. Authenticity flags

Flags 1 to 7 come from the Sanskrit review (`tools/review/shreya_vs_preya_vedantic_wisdom.md`); none of the SEO text above repeats the flagged wording.

1. **Katha Upanishad 1.2.2 cut off at "मनुष्यमेतस्" / "manuṣyametas" (review: Major).** The quote stops inside the sandhi "मनुष्यमेतस्तौ". Recommendation: quote the first half in full ("श्रेयश्च प्रेयश्च मनुष्यमेतस्तौ सम्परीत्य विविनक्ति धीरः।") or mark it as the opening with "मनुष्यमेतः ...". This verse is the page's main keyword source, so fix it before publishing; a search snippet showing a broken verse hurts trust. The second half (श्रेयो हि धीरोऽभि प्रेयसो वृणीते प्रेयो मन्दो योगक्षेमाद्वृणीते) supports the blog's point and the direct answer; the editor may add it. The tag "विवेक" / "Viveka" rests on this mantra's "विविनक्ति" (tells them apart).
2. **"क्योंकि गणपति सत्त्व के अंतर्गत आते हैं" / "because Lord Ganesha belongs to the realm of sattva" (review: Minor).** The Ganapati Atharvashirsha calls Ganapati beyond the three gunas. Recommendation: attribute to Acharya Ji ("आचार्य जी के अनुसार ..."). Not used in any title, snippet, FAQ or tag.
3. **"तो राजस से पतन की ओर ही जाना है" / "Then rajas is bound to lead you downward" (review: Minor).** Gita 14.18 places rajas in the middle and tamas below. Recommendation: "रजस और तमस से" / "rajas and tamas", or attribute to Acharya Ji. The direct answer and FAQ 3 avoid this claim and say only that the mind drifts toward preya.
4. **Gita 14.16 quoted as a half verse without marking (review: Minor).** Recommendation: add "(पूर्वार्ध)" / "(first half)". FAQ 2 cites it only for the sattvic fruit, which the first half supports.
5. **IAST "ṁ" (review: Minor).** Recommendation: "sāttvikaṃ nirmalaṃ".
6. **"सात्विक" vs "सात्त्विक" (review: Minor).** The Hindi direct answer uses "सात्त्विक" (Gita Press spelling). Recommendation: correct the blog text throughout.
7. **"कठोपनिषद" vs "कठोपनिषद्" (review: Minor).** The Hindi title, meta, secondary keyword and schema use "कठोपनिषद्". Recommendation: correct the blog text. The common search spelling without halant is covered by Google's matching; no separate keyword is needed.
8. **"Vedantic Wisdom" in the YouTube title (SEO).** The talk draws on the Katha Upanishad, which is a Vedanta source, so the label is fair; the blog titles use "Katha Upanishad" instead, which is more precise. No change needed.
9. **Business and revenge example (SEO).** The meta and FAQ 4 present "learn from experience, not revenge" and "befriend a lawyer" as Acharya Ji's personal example, not as legal or business advice. Keep it that way in any social snippet.
