# Chapter: Shreya and Preya, the Path of Noble Action

## The Pure Fruit of Noble Action

The fruit of noble action is said to be sattvic, that is, the pure fruit of happiness, knowledge, detachment and the like.

> **karmaṇaḥ sukṛtasyāhuḥ sāttvikaṁ nirmalaṁ phalam**
> "The fruit of good action is said to be sattvic and pure."
> (Bhagavad Gita 14.16)

If you have performed an action, which action? A noble action. Then what fruit will you receive? You will receive a sattvic fruit. Receiving a sattvic fruit means you will gain happiness, you will gain knowledge, you will gain detachment, and much more will come with it. That is why it is called the **pure fruit**.

## Shreya and Preya: Two Kinds of Action

So what does it mean to perform noble action? The Kathopanishad says there are two kinds of action: **Shreya** and **Preya**.

> **śreyaśca preyaśca manuṣyametaḥ**
> "Both the good and the pleasant approach a human being."
> (Kathopanishad 1.2.2)

Shreya means that which is noble. What the scriptures declare as the ideal, what is our duty, that is Shreya. And Preya means that which feels pleasant to me. People of rajas and tamas perform actions of Preya. And what will a sattvic person do? Shreya, that is, noble action.

## When Does the Desire for Noble Action Arise

When does the desire to perform noble action arise in me? It is said: when my very aim is to rise **from nara to Narayana**, from the human to the divine. Passing through the path of heaven, moving toward the Self, toward liberation; only when this is my desire can I perform noble action. This is what the Upanishad says.

And if I do not have that aim, then from rajas the only way is downward. No aim is even needed for that; it is decided on its own that I must go down, and then I will be drawn only toward Preya. Is this much clear?

> "Everything depends on what my thought is."

What is my thought? I want to go from nara to Narayana. Then your body will by itself be drawn to noble action, and your actions will be the same. Qualities such as happiness, knowledge and detachment will be within me.

## Business Grounded in Knowledge

I also did business, but to make it noble action I joined it with Shri Vidya, so it will be grounded in knowledge. If it is a business grounded in knowledge, then I will study customer psychology, I will study marketing psychology, I will learn product positioning, I will learn management, I will understand the psychology of staff, I will learn communication skills.

Where will my focus be? On knowledge. My product should be the very best. How to stay in competition, how one has to live, practical knowledge; knowledge, knowledge, knowledge, I will gain a great deal of knowledge.

Then if experiences come, there is no problem. I would want to learn from experience, not take revenge. I will not keep filing fifty court cases. I will make my terms and conditions sound. I will befriend a lawyer and learn the law from them.

## The Growth of Sattva

Is it becoming a little clear? Knowledge is growing a little, sattva guna is growing, because Ganapati falls under sattva. So such a person's focus is on knowledge and happiness. Taking this knowledge, their action now rises level by level, going higher and higher. They receive the happiness of it.

Even with so much knowledge and happiness, they do not get stuck in ego, they do not get stuck in rajoguna. Only with knowledge, happiness and detachment do they move on to the higher path.

## Toward Karma Yoga

Now, little by little, they must also give to people on the path of dharma; the attitude that everything is not mine will slowly grow in them. And a sense of living awareness, chaitanya, will also come within them, meaning they will be more energized.

They will not be excited about doing action, action, action; there will be enthusiasm, a life force energy will arise within them. Is it clear? Like the goal of life, of becoming better and rising higher, they will make this the path of liberation.

> "Work is worship."

They will move toward Karma Yoga. This is how such a person acts with a sattvic spirit.
