*Is love found only in marriage?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that love is one, with many names: affection for the young, friendship for equals, respect for elders, and highest of all, love for the Guru, who teaches us to see everyone as God.

💭 _Have you learned love from someone who truly lives it?_
