# Chapter: The Forms of Love and Learning Love from the Guru

## Love Is Not Limited to Marriage

What is love? We often see love only in connection with marriage. But as was said a little earlier, love is not only in marriage; love exists everywhere else too.

Love is of three kinds: first, toward someone younger than oneself, such as a child; second, toward someone of one's own age; and third, toward someone older than oneself. Can there be love for all of them? Yes, there can.

## The Different Names of Love

Love toward someone younger we call **affection** (sneh). Love toward an equal we give a name like **love** or **friendship**. And love toward those above us we refer to as **regard** and **respect**.

Love is one; only its name changes according to whom it flows toward.

## The Fourth Form: Love for the Guru

When love is there, the first question that arises is where I am to express it. There is also a fourth form: when I go to the Guru or to some respected person. Even if I go to a king, I can express love there too. But among all of these, the highest place will be the Guru's.

## Where Should We Go to Learn Love?

So where must I go first to learn love? It is said that we should hold a feeling of **Advaita** (non-duality) toward all. And it is said: first go to the Guru, because he himself must be practising it, Advaita with all of them.

> "First go to the Guru, because he himself must be practising it."

Go to him and understand that love. Then how to behave with a child, how to behave with those of your own age, how to behave with your elders, all of this will be understood on its own. Then you will experience that it is possible to love everyone as God.

> "It is possible to love all as God."
