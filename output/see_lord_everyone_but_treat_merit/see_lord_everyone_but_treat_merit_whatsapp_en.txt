*Does seeing God in everyone mean treating everyone alike?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that the Gita asks us to see God in all, but deal with each on merit. God lives in a dog and a minister, yet you would not serve both a bone.

💭 _Does discernment walk alongside your compassion?_
