*Have you ever truly loved?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that love itself is God, and that what we usually call love is moha, attachment and attraction. Love is learned from a Guru whose heart chakra has awakened.

💭 _Look at your own relationships: how much of them is love, and how much is attachment?_
