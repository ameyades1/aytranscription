# Guru and Disciple, the Highest Form of Love

Have we ever truly loved? Or is what we call love something else? In this short discourse, Acharya Upendra Ji opens up the deep connection between the Guru, love and discipleship.

## Recognising the Guru Means Recognising God

The day someone recognises the Guru in their true form, in that very moment they recognise God as well.

And on that same day they understand what love is.

> "Love is God."

## Is What We Call Love Really Love?

Real love is understood in the final birth. This path is not that easy. Had it been that simple, everyone would understand the life of Shri Krishna and Radha.

In the births before the final one, there is no love. There is **moha** (delusion), **asakti** (attachment), **akarshan** (attraction), compulsion, much else, but not love.

*Look at your own relationships: how much of them is love, and how much is delusion or attachment?*

## Who Can Teach Love?

To learn love, one must go to the Guru who has experienced it, whose **heart chakra**, the energy centre of love and compassion, has awakened.

## The Mark of a True Disciple

The true disciple is one who develops the habit of listening to the Guru in full, exactly as the Guru has spoken.

> "The one who develops the habit of listening exactly as they have spoken is called the true disciple."

*Do I listen to the Guru as they speak, or as I wish to hear?*

## Key Takeaways

- Recognising the Guru in their true form is recognising God.
- Love is God, and true love is understood in the final birth.
- In earlier births there is delusion, attachment, attraction and compulsion, not love.
- Love is learned from the Guru whose heart chakra has awakened.
- The true disciple listens to the Guru in full, exactly as it was said.
