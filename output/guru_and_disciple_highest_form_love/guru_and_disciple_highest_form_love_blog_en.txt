# The Guru and the Disciple: The Highest Form of Love

Have you ever truly loved? Or is what you call love something else entirely? In this short discourse, Acharya Upendra Ji opens up the deep bond between the Guru, love and discipleship.

## Recognise the Guru, and You Recognise God

The day you recognise the Guru in their true form, you recognise God in that very moment.

On that same day, you understand what love really is. As Acharya Ji puts it, love itself is God.

## Is What We Call Love Really Love?

True love dawns on you only in your final birth. This path is far from easy. If it were that simple, everyone would understand the lives of Shri Krishna and Radha.

In the births before the last one, there is no love. There is **moha** (delusion), **asakti** (attachment), attraction and compulsion, and a great deal more, but not love.

*Look at your own relationships: how much of them is love, and how much is moha or asakti?*

## Who Can Teach You Love?

To learn love, you have to go to a Guru who has lived it, one whose heart chakra, the energy centre of love and compassion, has awakened.

## The Mark of a True Disciple

True disciples are those who form the habit of hearing the Guru's words in full, exactly as the Guru spoke them.

*Do I hear the Guru's words as they are spoken, or as I want to hear them?*

## Key Takeaways

- Recognising the Guru in their true form means recognising God.
- Love itself is God, and true love dawns only in the final birth.
- Earlier births hold moha, asakti, attraction and compulsion, not love.
- You learn love from a Guru whose heart chakra has awakened.
- True disciples hear the Guru's words in full, exactly as they were spoken.
