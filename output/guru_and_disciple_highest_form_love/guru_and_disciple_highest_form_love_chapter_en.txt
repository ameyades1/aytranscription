# Chapter: Guru and Disciple, the Highest Form of Love

## Recognising the Guru Is Recognising God

The day anyone recognises the Guru in their true form, understand that in that very moment they have recognised God as well. And on that same day they will also understand what love is, because love is God.

> "Love is God."

## The Secret of Love Opens in the Final Birth

What true love is becomes clear only in the final birth. This path is not so easy, not so simple. Had it been that simple, everyone would understand the life of Shri Krishna and Radha. A person understands it only in the final birth.

This means that in all the births before the final one, there is no such thing as love. In them there will be **moha** (delusion), or **asakti** (attachment), **akarshan** (attraction), compulsion, much else, but not love.

## Going to the Guru to Learn Love

To learn love, one must go to the Guru who has experienced it, whose **heart chakra** has awakened.

## Who Is the True Disciple

Second, the one who one day develops the habit of listening to the Guru in full, exactly as the Guru has spoken, is called the true disciple.

> "The one who one day develops the habit of listening to the Guru in full, exactly as they have spoken, is called the true disciple."
