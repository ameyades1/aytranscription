## 1. Summary

A very short discourse (the video is under two minutes; about 300 words per language) in which Acharya Upendra Ji ties three ideas together: recognising the Guru in their true form is recognising God, and on that day one understands love, because love itself is God; what we call love in earlier births is moha, asakti, attraction and compulsion, and true love is understood only in the final birth; love is learned from a Guru whose heart chakra has awakened, and the true disciple hears the Guru's words exactly as they were spoken. The main intent is informational with a strong emotional layer ("what is true love", "is my love really attachment"), plus a devotional query around the Guru–disciple bond ("guru shishya ka prem"). Generic "true love" queries are crowded with relationship and quote sites; the realistic opportunity is the narrower spiritual query about love between Guru and disciple, and long-tail questions on love versus moha, where a clear, first-hand teaching can stand out. Because the post is short, it should work as a supporting post in the Guru & Disciple cluster and link outward to the deeper talks on love and discipleship.

## 6. FAQ

For the page or WordPress FAQ block only; not marked up as FAQPage (see section 8).

**Hindi**
1. **गुरु शिष्य का प्रेम सर्वोच्च क्यों है?** क्योंकि गुरु को असली रूप में पहचानना ही परमात्मा को पहचानना है, और आचार्य जी के अनुसार प्रेम ही परमात्मा है।
2. **मोह और प्रेम में क्या अंतर है?** आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि अंतिम जन्म से पहले के जन्मों में मोह, आसक्ति, आकर्षण और ज़बरदस्ती होती है, पर प्रेम नहीं।
3. **प्रेम कौन सिखा सकता है?** वह गुरु जिसने प्रेम को अनुभव किया है और जिसका हृदय चक्र, यानी प्रेम और करुणा का ऊर्जा केंद्र, जागृत है।
4. **असली शिष्य की पहचान क्या है?** असली शिष्य गुरु की बात पूर्ण रूप में, ठीक वैसी ही सुनता है जैसी गुरु ने कही है।

**English**
1. **Why is the love between Guru and disciple called the highest?** Because recognising the Guru in their true form is recognising God, and as Acharya Ji teaches, love itself is God.
2. **What is the difference between love and attachment?** Acharya Ji says that in the births before the last one there is moha, attachment, attraction and compulsion, but not love.
3. **Who can teach love?** A Guru who has lived it, one whose heart chakra, the energy centre of love and compassion, has awakened.
4. **What is the mark of a true disciple?** A true disciple hears the Guru's words in full, exactly as the Guru spoke them.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **"asakti" in the English** (from the Sanskrit review). Without the long ā it reads as a + sakti, close to "asakta" (unattached, Bhagavad Gita 3.19, Gita Press), the opposite meaning. Recommendation: change to "aasakti" or "āsakti" in the body, the reflection line and the key takeaway. Until then, the English titles, meta description, FAQ and schema use "attachment" or "moha", never "asakti".
2. **"श्री कृष्ण" vs "श्रीकृष्ण"** (from the Sanskrit review). Gita Press joins the honorific (श्रीकृष्ण). Optional; keep one form across the site. The in-text link anchor copies the blog's current form and must be updated if the editor changes it.
3. **Heart chakra and love/compassion** (from the Sanskrit review). The traditional name is अनाहत (anahata); linking it with love and compassion is a modern reading, not the classical description (Shatchakra Nirupana). Keep it as the speaker's description; the FAQ and snippets attribute it to the talk and add no scriptural claim. Optionally add "(अनाहत चक्र)" / "(anahata)" after checking whether the speaker named it.
4. **"True love dawns only in the final birth"** (from the Sanskrit review). This is Acharya Ji's teaching, not a scriptural statement. Do not add Bhagavad Gita 7.19 as a source in titles, FAQ, snippets or schema: that verse speaks of jnana and surrender, not love. The FAQ attributes this point to Acharya Ji.
5. **Broad "true love" queries.** The secondary keywords "सच्चा प्रेम क्या है" / "what is true love spiritually" attract readers in relationship distress. The post answers them honestly but briefly and from a spiritual angle (love as God, learned from a Guru); do not promise relationship advice in titles or social snippets. The link to true_meaning_of_love_valentines gives those readers a fuller answer.
