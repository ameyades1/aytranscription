# Tulsi, Bilva Leaves and Durva Grass: The Science Behind Our Offerings

## Do the Gods Have Their Own Preferences?

Have you ever noticed this while arranging the puja plate? Lord Vishnu receives **tulsi** (holy basil) leaves, Lord Shiva receives **bilva** (leaves of the wood apple tree), and Lord Ganesha receives **durva** (a sacred grass).

Each deity seems to have a favourite. Are we sitting in some marketplace, then, where each one calls out, "I want this, I want that"?

*If your child asked you why this is so, would you have an answer?*

So why different offerings to different gods? In short, Acharya Ji explains that the gods have not divided up their favourites; behind each offering lies a science of symbols and energy. Colour, sound, mantra and plants all carry energy, and each offering's energy matches the result we seek from that deity, such as intelligence from Ganesha.

## Sharing Out Toys, or a Deeper Mystery?

Think of how small children quarrel and we settle it by dividing things between them: "You take this outfit, you take two sweets. Now sit quietly and stop fighting."

Did the gods settle their claims the same way? "You take the durva, you take the bilva"? That cannot be the case.

Behind these offerings lies a **symbolic science**.

## Why Different Offerings to Different Gods? Every Object Carries Energy

Everything radiates some kind of energy. **Colour, too, is energy.** And energy can change from one form into another.

Certain sounds, the **mantras**, hold energy as well. The plants we offer also carry **prana** (life force energy).

This energy matches the result you are seeking. When you worship Ganesha, you are asking for **intelligence**. The vibration and level of energy that intelligence requires follow their own mathematics, their own science.

## Not a Miracle, but a Science

Don't worry if all of this doesn't make complete sense today. In intensive, personal classes you move step by step through a scientific method, and you become a **person of knowledge**.

Shri Krishna says that those who have knowledge attain **moksha** (liberation). That final liberation will come later, but well before it, your troubles begin to ease right away.

Acharya Ji says he makes no claim that miracles happen. Instead, he explains the science behind them.

How does science work? Press the switch and the fan is bound to turn. In the same way, take the right **action**, understand it fully, and the result is bound to follow.

## First Know Your God, Then Your Worship

The first step is **to know your God**. The next is **to understand your worship**. Why did you offer what you offered?

What does "vastraṁ samarpayāmi" (I offer clothing) mean? What does "pañcāmṛta snānaṁ samarpayāmi" (I offer a bath of the five nectars) mean? The deity is bathed in milk, curd, ghee and honey, and then comes "śuddhodaka snānaṁ samarpayāmi" (I offer a bath of pure water).

The idol is made of metal or stone. Why would it need a bath at all? Why, then, do we bathe it?

*Have you ever asked what any of your rituals actually means?*

## Abhisheka Is No Ordinary Bath

We perform **abhisheka** (ritual pouring of water or other liquids over a deity), pouring water over and over like a fountain. Yet have we ever asked why?

The word appears in two places. One is the abhisheka of God. The other is **rajyabhisheka** (the consecration of a king). A king receives an abhisheka, and so does God.

Acharya Ji points out that this is no ordinary word, and the ritual is no ordinary bath. It must carry some symbolism, and a very deep one.

## Why Betel Nut and Betel Leaf?

Every puja includes **pugiphala** (betel nut) and **tambula** (betel leaf). Why? What do they stand for?

How many people truly know? Ask yourself honestly.

## Can You Become That Form?

Each deity's **form** must have been shaped around the very qualities we need. If you don't understand those qualities, what will you do with the symbol alone? You know neither the meaning of the name nor anything else. All you have is a picture.

*Can you bring that form to life within yourself?*

## Key Takeaways

- A symbolic science lies behind each deity's offering: tulsi for Vishnu, bilva for Shiva, durva for Ganesha.
- Colour, sound, mantra and plants all carry energy, and that energy matches the result you seek.
- Knowledge leads to liberation, and even before that, your troubles begin to ease right away.
- First know your God, then learn the meaning of each step of your worship.
- Abhisheka is no ordinary bath; like a king's consecration, it is a deep symbol.
- Each deity's form symbolises the qualities we need to grow within ourselves.
