## 1. Summary

The talk (Ganesh Series, episode 3) asks why each deity receives a different offering (tulsi for Vishnu, bilva for Shiva, durva for Ganesha) and answers that it is not a "division among children" but a symbolic science: colour, sound, mantra and plants carry energy that matches the result the worshipper seeks. It then widens to the meaning of puja steps (vastra, panchamrita snana, abhisheka, pugiphala and tambula) and ends with the call to understand, and become, the deity's form. Main intent is informational ("why do we offer X to Y", "puja ka arth"), with a strong parent/child angle ("if your child asks you"). The opportunity is the broad comparison query (why different gods get different offerings), which most ranking pages answer only one deity at a time with lists of "benefits"; Acharya Ji's single explanation across all three, without fear or promises, is the distinct angle. Single-deity queries (why bilva to Shiva, why tulsi to Vishnu) have high volume, but the blog only names these pairings and does not explain each one, so they are kept as secondary at most.

## 6. FAQ

**Hindi**
1. **अलग-अलग देवताओं को अलग अर्पण क्यों किया जाता है?** आचार्य जी के अनुसार यह बच्चों जैसा बँटवारा नहीं, प्रतीकात्मक विज्ञान है: हर अर्पण की ऊर्जा उस परिणाम से मेल खाती है जो हम चाहते हैं।
2. **गणेश जी की आराधना से क्या चाहा जाता है?** बुद्धि। और बुद्धि के लिए जो स्पंदन और ऊर्जा का स्तर चाहिए, उसका अपना गणित और विज्ञान है।
3. **क्या अभिषेक केवल स्नान है?** नहीं। अभिषेक शब्द भगवान और राजा (राज्याभिषेक) दोनों के लिए आता है; आचार्य जी कहते हैं यह साधारण स्नान नहीं, एक गहरा प्रतीक है।
4. **पूजा का अर्थ समझना क्यों ज़रूरी है?** पहले अपने भगवान को जानना, फिर पूजा के हर चरण का अर्थ जानना; केवल प्रतीक या चित्र रखने से वह रूप भीतर नहीं उतरता।

**English**
1. **Why are different offerings made to different gods?** According to Acharya Ji, it is not a division of favourites but a symbolic science: each offering's energy matches the result we seek.
2. **What do we seek when we worship Ganesha?** Intelligence. The vibration and level of energy that intelligence needs follow their own mathematics and science.
3. **Is abhisheka just a bath for the deity?** No. The same word is used for a king's consecration (rajyabhisheka); Acharya Ji says it is no ordinary bath but a deep symbol.
4. **Why understand the meaning of puja?** First know your God, then the meaning of each step of worship; a symbol or picture alone does not bring that form to life within you.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Panchamrita lists four ingredients (Sanskrit review issue 1, Minor, not yet applied).** The blog says "दूध, दही, घी, शहद" / "milk, curd, ghee and honey" while glossing "the five nectars"; sugar (शक्कर) is missing. Apply the review's correction before publishing; no SEO text above lists the ingredients.
2. **Puja formula spelling and IAST (review issues 2 to 4, Minor).** Write "पञ्चामृतस्नानं" / "शुद्धोदकस्नानं" as compounds and use ṃ in the IAST. Keep these formulas out of titles and meta until corrected (they are).
3. **Bilva gloss (review issue 5, Minor).** "Leaves of the wood apple tree" should be "leaves of the bael tree" (Aegle marmelos). Use "bilva" or "bael" in English keywords, not "wood apple".
4. **Questions raised but not answered.** The talk asks why abhisheka, panchamrita, pugiphala and tambula, but does not explain them. Do not write FAQ answers, snippets or meta that claim to explain "the meaning of betel nut in puja" or give a full abhisheka meaning; the FAQ above only says what the talk says (abhisheka is a deep symbol, like rajyabhisheka). "Meaning of abhisheka" is kept as a secondary keyword only because the post discusses it; a fuller post would serve that query better.
5. **"Science" and "energy" framing is Acharya Ji's interpretation.** Every SEO text attributes it to him. Avoid "scientifically proven", and avoid health or wish-fulfilment promises ("tulsi chadhane ke fayde"); the talk makes none.
6. **Gita paraphrase.** "Shri Krishna says the knower attains moksha" is a paraphrase without a verse number (the review notes Gita 4.39 and 7.17–18 as consistent). Don't add a verse number in FAQ or schema unless the editor confirms one.
7. **Classes mention.** The blog mentions intensive personal classes; keep this out of titles and meta, so the post reads as teaching rather than a promotion.
