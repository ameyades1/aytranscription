# Chapter: The Spiritual Science Behind Different Offerings

## Why Does Each God Have a Different Preference?

Have you ever paid attention to what you offer in worship? To Lord Vishnu, tulsi leaves; to Lord Shiva, bilva leaves; and to Lord Ganesha, durva grass. In this way each god has their own, different preference. Isn't that so?

So are we sitting in some market, where someone says, I want this, I want that? Why is there this difference? If your children asked you this very question, would you be able to answer?

Is it that, just as little children quarrel among themselves and the elders divide things among them, "You take this dress, you take this, you take two sweets", the gods too have been given a share each? "Baba, you take durva, you take this, you take bilva leaves, sit quietly, don't fight." Can it be like that? No. Behind this too there is a symbolic science.

## Energy in Colour, Sound and Plants

Everything emits an energy. Colour too is energy. And energy can be converted from one form to another. In the same way there will be certain sounds, there will be mantras, and they too will carry energy. Colour too will carry this energy.

So the plants we offer will also carry some energy, a life force. That thing matches the outcome you want. If you want Ganesha, you want intelligence. The vibrations needed for intelligence, the level of energy needed for it, will have some mathematics, some science behind it.

## Freedom from Trouble Through Knowledge

Today you may feel confused. Even if you do not understand today, do not worry at all. In in-depth, one-to-one classes, through a scientific method, fully systematic, step by step, you will become a person of knowledge.

And as soon as one becomes a person of knowledge, Shri Krishna says, the one with knowledge attains moksha. That supreme liberation will come later, but before that, freedom from your troubles will begin. Immediately. You will see how this magic happens.

> "I don't claim that magic happens. I say, I will tell you the science behind it."

Results begin to come immediately. How is it in science? Press the button, and the fan must run, mustn't it? In the same way, do such-and-such action and know this much, and there the result must follow.

## First Know Your God, Then Know the Worship

So the first step is knowing your god. The next step is knowing the worship. Such-and-such was offered, so why was it offered?

What does "vastraṁ samarpayāmi" mean? Tell me that first. What does "pañcāmṛta snānaṁ samarpayāmi" mean? Tell me that first. With milk, with curd, with ghee, with honey, that is, the honey bath, and then "śuddhodaka snānaṁ samarpayāmi". What does it mean, does anyone know?

If you look at it, it was some metal or an idol. So what need did it have of a bath? Then why was it bathed? Does anyone have an answer to this?

## Abhishek: No Ordinary Bath

Then we perform abhishek. Does anyone know what abhishek means? We keep pouring the water of the whole world, like a shower. But did you ever ask why it is to be poured? Nobody asked.

The word abhishek is used in two places. One, abhishek is done on God. And where is the other abhishek done? Rajyabhishek, the coronation of a king. So it is an important word. Rajyabhishek is performed on a king, and abhishek is also performed on God.

> "This word is not ordinary. It is not an ordinary bath. There must be something symbolic, a very deep symbol."

## The Secret of Pugiphal and Tambul

The elements placed in worship, pugiphal and tambul, that is, betel nut and betel leaf, why are they placed there? What do they mean? How many of you know? Tell me truthfully today.

## Can You Become That Form?

You should know your god. The form of each god must have been designed according to the qualities we need from them. If you do not understand that, what will you do by holding on to the symbol alone? No name, no details, only a picture has been given.

So can you become that one form? Tell me truthfully. Just tell me this much. Now you will be compelled to think about this.
