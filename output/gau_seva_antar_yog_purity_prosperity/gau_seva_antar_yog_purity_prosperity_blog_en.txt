# Gau Seva That Turns Gratitude into Prosperity

## Why Do We Call the Cow Our Mother?

Gau Mata, Mother Cow, whose selfless contribution has blessed human life. The scriptures, saints and great souls have held her importance supreme.

India has been an agricultural land for centuries, and here the cow is worshipped as a mother. **Mother Cow is the foundation of our culture.**

Milk, curd, ghee, cow urine, medicine, farming, organic produce and the livelihood of millions: her contribution to all of these is priceless.

*When did you last remember this silent contribution behind the milk and grain on your plate today?*

## An Opportunity for Gratitude

Antar Yog gives you a priceless opportunity to express your gratitude to Mother Cow through **gau seva**, service to the cow.

Under the divine guidance of Sadguru Acharya Upendra Ji, the Antar Yog Foundation organises gau seva from time to time, so that together we can nurture this divine heritage.

## Service, Prosperity and the Aura

Mother Cow is regarded as a symbol of wealth, prosperity and abundance. It is believed that serving her brings at once the blessings of the thirty-three koti gods and goddesses and immense **punya** (spiritual merit).

When we serve with the right feeling, we draw prosperity and good fortune into our **aura**.

Acharya Ji teaches the scriptural way of gau seva, so that we experience Mother Cow's **motherly energy** (a mother's affection) and find peace of mind and joy.

## Not Mechanical Service, but Service with Feeling

For gau seva, Antar Yog chooses a place where the cow is truly honoured as a mother: a vast place where hundreds of cows and their little calves live.

Acharya Ji says that service should not be mechanical, but done by seeing a part of the Supreme in the cows, in this spirit:

> **oṃ tat sat**
> "That alone is the Supreme Truth."
> (Bhagavad Gita 17.23)

That is, He alone is the Supreme Truth, and every action is offered to Him. In this spirit, the team of volunteers lovingly offers every service to Mother Cow.

*When you serve, is it only a task, or is your heart in it too?*

## When the Calves Run Up for Affection

When Acharya Ji and Guru Maa lovingly stroke the cows and feed them fodder, the whole herd seems to recognise their divinity.

The calves come forward again and again, longing for their affection. And here everyone gets the chance to experience this affection and to feed Mother Cow with their own hands.

## Sadhana in the Gaushala: A Thousandfold Fruit

The special feature of this gau seva is the special **sadhana** (spiritual practice) done in a sacred place like the gaushala, the cow shelter. The scriptures say that such sadhana bears a thousandfold fruit.

This divine sadhana is not found anywhere else today.

## Service Is Never Complete

Heartfelt thanks to the dedicated workers of the gaushala, whose devotion keeps this eternal tradition alive today.

And Acharya Ji teaches us to stay humble: however much service we do, it is too little. We may give them medicine, give them water and clean everything, and even then it is still too little. He explains that gau seva is the root of our life and culture.

## Key Takeaways

- Mother Cow is the foundation of our culture, and gau seva is the duty of every Indian.
- Gau seva is a way to express gratitude, and service done with the right feeling brings prosperity and good fortune into the aura.
- Service should not be mechanical; it should be done seeing a part of the Supreme in the cows, in the spirit of "Om Tat Sat".
- Sadhana done in a sacred place like the gaushala bears a thousandfold fruit.
- However much we serve, it is too little; humility is part of service.
