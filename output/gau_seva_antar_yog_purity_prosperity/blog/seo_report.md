## 1. Summary

A short event-style blog on Antar Yog's gau seva: why the cow is called Gau Mata, gau seva as a way of giving thanks for her silent giving, the traditional view that serving her brings punya, prosperity and the blessings of all the gods, Acharya Ji's teaching that seva must be done with feeling ("ॐ तत्सत्"), not mechanically, sadhana in the gaushala, and humility in service. The main intent is informational with a practical edge: "गौ सेवा का महत्व", "गौ सेवा के लाभ", "importance of gau seva", "why is the cow called mother". These queries are served mostly by thin listicles and gaushala donation pages. The angle only this post offers is *bhava over mechanism*: seva as gratitude and an inner offering, not a transaction for merit. The post is short (about 550 words), so it is best positioned as a navigational and supporting post (Antar Yog's gau seva) inside the Seva cluster, linked from the Guru seva post, rather than as a pillar.

## 6. FAQ

**गाय को माता क्यों कहते हैं? / Why is the cow called mother?**
भारत सदियों से कृषि प्रधान देश रहा है; दूध, दही, घी, खेती और लाखों लोगों की आजीविका गौ माता के निःस्वार्थ योगदान से चलती है, इसलिए वे हमारी संस्कृति की आधारशिला हैं। / India has lived by farming for centuries; milk, curd, ghee, farming and millions of livelihoods depend on the cow's selfless giving, so she is the cornerstone of the culture.

**गौ सेवा के क्या लाभ बताए गए हैं? / What are the benefits of gau seva?**
मान्यता है कि गौ सेवा से तैंतीस कोटि देवी-देवताओं का आशीर्वाद और पुण्य मिलता है, और सही भाव से की गई सेवा आभामंडल में समृद्धि और सौभाग्य लाती है। / Tradition holds that gau seva brings the blessings of all the gods and goddesses and punya; served with the right feeling, it draws prosperity and good fortune into your aura.

**गौ सेवा कैसे करनी चाहिए? / How should gau seva be done?**
आचार्य जी के अनुसार सेवा यांत्रिक नहीं होनी चाहिए; गौओं में परमेश्वर का अंश देखकर, "ॐ तत्सत्" के भाव से, हर सेवा प्रेमपूर्वक अर्पित करनी चाहिए। / According to Acharya Ji, seva should never be mechanical: see a spark of God in every cow and offer each act lovingly, in the spirit of "oṃ tat sat".

**क्या गौ सेवा कभी पूरी होती है? / Is gau seva ever complete?**
नहीं। दवा दें, पानी दें, सब साफ करें, तब भी सेवा कम ही है; विनम्रता सेवा का हिस्सा है। / No. Give medicine, bring water, clean everything, and it still falls short; humility is part of service.

The "thousandfold" sadhana claim is left out of the FAQ until its source is checked (flag 2).

## 10. Authenticity flags

From the Sanskrit review (tools/review/gau_seva_antar_yog_purity_prosperity.md):
1. **Gita 17.23 meaning (Major).** The quoted "ॐ तत्सत्" is printed with the meaning "every action is offered to It", which is Acharya Ji's application, not the verse's meaning (17.23: Om, Tat, Sat are the threefold name of Brahman). Recommendation: adopt the review's correction (verse meaning plus "आचार्य जी के अनुसार..."), mark it as part of the verse. Do not use the verse in snippets or FAQ with the current gloss; section 6 attributes the spirit to Acharya Ji only.
2. **"Thousandfold" fruit of sadhana in a gaushala (Major).** No source; a commonly cited verse gives a hundredfold (शतगुणं) for a cow-pen. This is also an H2 and a Key Takeaway. Recommendation: verify or soften to "many times over"; kept out of the titles, meta, direct answer and FAQ.
3. **"Thirty-three crore" (Minor).** The English renders कोटि only as "crore". The linked 33_crore_gods post discusses this; adopt the review's wording if the editor agrees (the anchor in seo.json must then be updated).
4. **IAST "oṁ tat sat" vs "तत्सत्" (Minor).** Make the two match; prefer ṃ.
5. **Spellings निस्वार्थ / गौवंश (Minor).** Prefer निःस्वार्थ, गोवंश. The direct answer above already uses निःस्वार्थ.
6. **"Placed her above all else" (Minor).** Goes further than the Hindi "महत्व सर्वोपरि". Use "held her importance to be supreme".
7. **"Duty of every Indian" in Key Takeaways (Minor).** Not said in the body; replace as the review suggests.
8. **"Nowhere else today" (Minor).** An exclusive, promotional claim; soften to "a rare opportunity". Not used in any SEO text.

SEO-specific:
9. **Prosperity framing.** The title promise "from gratitude to prosperity" is faithful to the H1 and the body ("सही भाव से सेवा करने पर... समृद्धि और सौभाग्य"), but must never become a guaranteed-wealth claim ("do gau seva and get rich"). Keep "tradition holds" / "मान्यता है" wherever benefits are stated; the meta and FAQ do so.
10. **Video title vs blog.** The video title and thumbnail promise "Liberation" / "Liberation of Your Ancestors", which the blog does not discuss. Do not target pitru, moksha or ancestor queries with this post; if the YouTube description is reused for VideoObject, that is fine, but keep the post's own title and meta to what the blog says.

## 12. SEO checklist

### Keywords
- [x] Hindi and English each have a focus keyword
- [x] The focus keyword isn't used by another post (no cannibalization)
- [x] 2–4 secondary keywords per language
- [x] Hindi and English keywords researched separately, not translated; Hinglish variants considered
- [x] Focus keyword taken from wording the article already uses (in the `_seo` texts, which are what gets published; the original texts did not use it, see section 2)
- [x] If people search a different wording, it is in the secondary keywords and the mismatch is flagged (no other searched wording; the focus keywords are the searched wording and are now in the `_seo` texts)
- [x] Priority given as High / Medium / Low, based on intent and competition; no invented numbers

### Focus keyword placement (SureRank)
- [x] In the SEO title
- [x] In the meta description
- [x] In the body text (`_seo` texts)
- [x] In the URL slug (English keywords)
- [x] Within the first 100 words (preferred) (in the direct answer at the end of the opening section of the `_seo` texts)

### SEO title
- [x] Present in both languages, ≤ 60 characters
- [x] Keyword near the start
- [x] Faithful to the talk, no clickbait

- [x] Present in both languages, ≤ 160 characters

- [x] Gives a clear reason to click

### URL slug
