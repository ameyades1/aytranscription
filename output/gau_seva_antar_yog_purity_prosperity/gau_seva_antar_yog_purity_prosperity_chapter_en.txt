# Chapter: Gau Seva, from Gratitude to Prosperity

## Mother Cow: The Foundation of Our Culture

Gau Mata, Mother Cow, whose selfless contribution has blessed human life, has been held supreme by the scriptures and by saints and great souls. Especially in our India, which has been an agricultural land for centuries, the cow is worshipped as a mother.

**Mother Cow is the foundation of our culture, and gau seva, service to the cow, is the foremost duty of every Indian.** From milk, curd, ghee, cow urine and medicine to farming, natural (organic) produce and the livelihood of millions of people, her contribution is priceless.

## An Opportunity to Express Gratitude

That is why Antar Yog offers you a priceless opportunity to express your gratitude and thankfulness to Mother Cow through gau seva. Under the divine guidance of Sadguru Acharya Upendra Ji, the Antar Yog Foundation organises gau seva from time to time, so that together we can nurture this divine heritage.

## A Symbol of Prosperity and Good Fortune

Mother Cow is regarded as a symbol of wealth, prosperity and abundance. It is believed that serving her brings at once the blessings of the thirty-three koti gods and goddesses and immense merit.

By serving Mother Cow with the right feeling, we draw prosperity and good fortune into our aura. Acharya Upendra Ji teaches the proper way of gau seva as laid down in the scriptures, so that we can experience Mother Cow's **motherly, loving energy**, attract prosperity and good fortune, and find peace of mind and joy.

## The Spirit of Service: Om Tat Sat

For gau seva, Antar Yog chooses a place where the cow is truly honoured as a mother: a vast place where hundreds of cows and their little calves live.

Acharya Ji says that gau seva should not be done mechanically, but by seeing a part of the Supreme in the cows:

> **oṃ tat sat**
> "That alone is the Supreme Truth."
> (Bhagavad Gita 17.23)

That is, everything is offered to that Supreme Truth. With this feeling, the team of volunteers lovingly offers every service to Mother Cow.

## The Affection of Mother Cow

When Acharya Ji and Guru Maa lovingly stroke the cows and feed them fodder, the whole herd seems to recognise their divinity and grows eager to be near them. The calves come forward again and again, longing for their affection.

Everyone gets the chance to experience this affection of Mother Cow, and also the chance to feed her with their own hands.

## Sadhana in the Gaushala: A Thousandfold Fruit

The special feature of gau seva with Acharya Upendra Ji and Antar Yog is the special sadhana done in a sacred place like the gaushala, the cow shelter. Our scriptures say that such sadhana bears a thousandfold fruit. This divine sadhana is not found anywhere else today.

## However Much We Serve, It Is Too Little

Finally, we offer heartfelt thanks to the dedicated workers of the gaushala, whose devotion and service keep this eternal tradition alive today.

Acharya Ji says:

> "Do not think that we have done a lot. However much service we do, it is too little. Give them medicine, give them water, clean everything; even when that is done, it is still too little."

**Gau seva is the root of our life and culture, and an essential duty of every human being.** With the blessings of Mother Cow, invite growth in wealth, grain and happiness, and peace of mind into your life, and fulfil your duty towards society and the nation.
