# Gau Seva: Turning Gratitude into Prosperity

## Why Do We Call the Cow Our Mother?

**Gau Mata** (Mother Cow) has blessed human life through her selfless giving. The scriptures and the great saints alike have placed her above all else.

For centuries India has lived by farming, and here the cow is worshipped as a mother. **Gau Mata is the cornerstone of our culture.**

Milk, curd, ghee, cow urine, medicine, farming, organic produce and the livelihoods of millions: her contribution to all of these is beyond price.

The importance of gau seva lies in gratitude for this silent giving. Acharya Upendra Ji teaches that such service should never be mechanical: see a spark of God in every cow and serve with love and humility. Service offered with the right feeling draws prosperity and good fortune into your aura, and brings peace of mind and bliss.

*When did you last remember the silent giving behind the milk and grain on your plate today?*

## Why Do Gau Seva?

Antar Yog offers you a precious opportunity to express your gratitude to Gau Mata through **gau seva** (selfless service to the cow).

Under the divine guidance of Sadguru Acharya Upendra Ji, the Antar Yog Foundation organises gau seva from time to time, so that together we can nurture this sacred heritage.

## What Are the Benefits of Gau Seva?

People regard Gau Mata as a symbol of wealth, abundance and splendour. Tradition holds that serving her brings, all at once, the blessings of the thirty-three crore gods and goddesses and immense **punya** (spiritual merit).

When you serve with the right feeling, you draw prosperity and good fortune into your **aura**.

Acharya Ji teaches the way of gau seva laid down in the scriptures, so that you can feel Gau Mata's **vatsalya** (a mother's tender love) and find peace of mind and bliss.

## Serve with Feeling, Not by Rote

For gau seva, Antar Yog chooses a place where the cow is honoured as a mother in the truest sense: a vast sanctuary that is home to hundreds of cows and their little calves.

Acharya Ji says that service should never be mechanical. You should see a spark of God in every cow and serve in this spirit:

> **oṁ tat sat**
> "That alone is the Supreme Truth, and every action is offered to It."
> (Bhagavad Gita 17.23)

With this feeling, the team of volunteers lovingly offers every act of service to Gau Mata.

*When you serve, is it just another task, or do you put your heart into it?*

## When the Calves Come Running

When Acharya Ji and Guru Maa lovingly stroke and feed the cows, the whole herd seems to recognise the divinity in them.

The calves keep pushing forward, eager for their affection. Here everyone gets the chance to feel that same love and to feed Gau Mata with their own hands.

## Sadhana in the Gaushala: A Thousandfold Reward

What makes this gau seva special is the **sadhana** (spiritual practice) performed in a holy place like the **gaushala** (cow shelter). The scriptures say that such practice bears fruit a thousand times over.

You will not find this divine sadhana anywhere else today.

## Service Is Never Complete

Heartfelt thanks go to the devoted staff of the gaushala, whose dedication keeps this timeless tradition alive.

Acharya Ji also teaches us humility: however much you serve, it is never enough. Give the cows their medicine, bring them water, clean everything, and it still falls short. Gau seva, he explains, is the root of our life and our culture.

## Key Takeaways

- Gau Mata is the cornerstone of our culture, and gau seva is the duty of every Indian.
- Gau seva is a way to express gratitude, and service offered with the right feeling brings prosperity and good fortune into your aura.
- Service should never be mechanical; see a spark of God in every cow and serve in the spirit of "oṁ tat sat".
- Sadhana performed in a holy place like the gaushala bears fruit a thousand times over.
- However much you serve, it is never enough; humility is part of service.
