# Chapter: Punya and Paap, the Three Gunas and the Three Shaktis

## Trigunatmak Durga and the Impassable World

Lakshmi has been called divine wealth. Those who have recited the Durga Saptashati will know that Goddess Durga is trigunatmak, that is, made of three gunas. These three gunas are **sattva, rajas and tamas**. Durga's form is made of the combination of these three gunas.

The word Durga means she who slew the demon Durgam. The demon is maya, and *durgam* (hard to cross) is this world. To cross this world means to cross these three gunas.

## Leaping from One Guna to Another

Knowingly or unknowingly, I keep leaping from one guna to another. You think someone is meditating, but in reality he has slipped into tamas, he has fallen asleep. He thinks he has entered yoga nidra. Perhaps he was simply asleep.

It is not always easy to tell that he is asleep. You cannot say whether someone is sitting like a saint, free of all worldly worries, or lying there drunk. He too has no worry about the world. Telling the two apart is very, very difficult.

## Sattva and Tamas Look Alike from Outside

Our scriptures say that **sattva guna and tamo guna look the same from outside**. Someone may give charity with sincere feeling, but charity can also be for show, or for the ego. That is why it is said that sattva and tamas look very similar.

Giving charity is an act. The part of it that is action is rajas. The knowledge and feeling inside, behind it, can be sattva or it can be tamas.

## The Definition of Paap and Punya

This is where the definition of paap (sin) and punya (merit) comes from. Lakshmi is action, she is rajas. Rajo guna by itself is neutral; it has no meaning of its own. With whom, where and with what feeling the same action is done, for example in front of everyone, or with someone else's wife, is what can turn that same action into a problem.

> "If rajas is joined with tamo guna, it becomes paap; and when that same rajo guna is joined with sattva, it becomes punya."

You went to the gym and lifted dumbbells. What is this? It is raja shakti. What is its benefit? The body becomes strong. Fine. Now with that same body you become a thug and go to beat someone, to rob someone; you have joined rajas with tamas. Now it will be paap. When you join that same strength with Saraswati, it will be punya.

## The Difference between Guna and Shakti

Kali has not been called bad. She is called **tamas shakti**, not tamo guna. There are always two factors: tamo guna and tamas shakti; rajo guna and raja shakti; sattva guna and sattva shakti. Shakti means the Goddess.

We must always use all three shaktis. But if we are to keep a guna, which one? **Sattva guna**. Let all the other gunas go, but do not give up their shakti.

## The Example of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj

When Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj went to battle, tamas shakti, the shakti of Kali, would have been with him. Bhavani Mata means Kali herself. He used that shakti, but what was his guna? Supreme sattva.
