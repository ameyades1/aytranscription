punya_vs_paap: SEO report

## 1. Summary

This talk answers a question many seekers ask in plain words: why does the same action count as a sin in one case and a virtue in another? Acharya Ji's answer uses the three gunas. Every action, as action, is rajas. It becomes paap (sin) when rajas draws its support from tamas, and punya (virtue) when sattva guides it. He illustrates this with Durga as trigunatmika, meditation that is really sleep, three kinds of charity, the gym and the thug, guna versus shakti (Kali as tamas shakti), and Shivaji Maharaj, who took up Kali's power while his own guna stayed supreme sattva. The main intent is informational ("पाप और पुण्य में अंतर", "difference between sin and virtue", "paap punya kya hai"), with a reflective, self-help side (am I meditating or just asleep, what is my energy serving). Search results for these queries are mostly thin moral lists or religion-comparison pages. A short, clear answer based on the gunas, with Hindu examples, is a distinct angle that Acharya Ji's talk provides. The post is short (about 4 minutes of video), so the direct-answer paragraph and the FAQ matter a lot for featured snippets.

## 5. Direct answer paragraph

**Hindi (about 50 words):** आचार्य उपेंद्र जी के अनुसार हर कर्म की क्रिया रजस है, और रजोगुण अपने आप में तटस्थ है। वही क्रिया जब तमोगुण के सहारे होती है, जैसे बल से किसी को लूटना, तो पाप बनती है; और जब सत्त्व, यानी ज्ञान और शुद्ध भाव, से जुड़ती है, तो पुण्य बनती है। इसलिए कर्म नहीं, उसके पीछे का भाव निर्णय करता है।

**English (about 55 words):** According to Acharya Upendra Ji, the act in every deed is rajas, and rajas in itself is neutral. When that activity draws its support from tamas, like using strength to rob someone, it becomes sin. When it is guided by sattva, by knowledge and a pure intention, it becomes virtue. The intention behind the act, not the act, decides.

## 6. FAQ

1. **पाप और पुण्य में क्या अंतर है? / What is the difference between sin and virtue?**
   HI: क्रिया दोनों में रजस ही है। रजस को तमोगुण का सहारा मिले तो पाप, सत्त्व का सहारा मिले तो पुण्य।
   EN: The activity is rajas in both. Rajas backed by tamas is sin; rajas backed by sattva is virtue.
2. **क्या एक ही कर्म पाप भी हो सकता है और पुण्य भी? / Can the same action be both a sin and a virtue?**
   HI: हां। व्यायाम से बना बल लूटपाट में लगे तो पाप है, ज्ञान से जुड़े तो पुण्य।
   EN: Yes. The strength built at the gym is sin when used to rob people and virtue when joined with knowledge.
3. **मैं ध्यान कर रहा हूं या सो रहा हूं, यह कैसे जानूं? / How do I know if I am meditating or just asleep?**
   HI: बाहर से दोनों एक जैसे दिखते हैं। प्रश्न यह है कि आपकी शांति जागरूकता की है या नींद, यानी तमस, की।
   EN: From outside they look alike. Ask whether your calm comes from awareness or from sleep, which is tamas.
4. **गुण और शक्ति में क्या अंतर है? / What is the difference between a guna and a shakti?**
   HI: हर गुण के साथ एक शक्ति होती है। तीनों शक्तियों का उपयोग करना है, पर गुण केवल सत्त्व रखना है।
   EN: Each guna carries its own power. Use all three powers, but keep sattva as your only guna.
5. **क्या काली बुरी हैं? / Is Kali evil?**
   HI: नहीं। काली को तमस शक्ति कहा गया है, तमोगुण नहीं। शिवाजी महाराज ने यही शक्ति ली, पर उनका गुण परम सत्त्व था।
   EN: No. Kali is called tamas shakti, the power of tamas, not the guna of tamas. Shivaji Maharaj used this power while his guna stayed supreme sattva.

## 10. Authenticity flags

Flags 1 to 8 come from the Sanskrit review (`tools/review/punya_vs_paap.md`); none of the SEO text above repeats the flagged wording.

1. **"भवानी माता का अर्थ ही काली है" / "Bhavani Mata ... is Kali herself" (review: Major).** Bhavani is a name of Parvati, and Tulja Bhavani is worshipped as a form of Durga. Recommendation: apply the review's wording ("as Acharya Ji sees it, carried the tamas shakti of Kali") before publishing. FAQ 5 and the meta mention Shivaji only in the supported way. Note: if this sentence is reworded, the link anchor "काली की शक्ति" / "the power of Kali" in the previous sentence is unaffected.
2. **"शास्त्र कहते हैं कि सत्व और तम बाहर से एक जैसे दिखते हैं" / "The scriptures say ..." (review: Major).** No shastra source is known; the idea is usually quoted from Swami Vivekananda. Recommendation: attribute it to Acharya Ji. The snippet and FAQ 3 do not say "the scriptures".
3. **"यह असुर है माया" / "That demon is maya" (review: Minor).** The asura Durgama is in the Durga Saptashati; calling it maya is Acharya Ji's symbolic reading. Recommendation: "आचार्य जी के अनुसार" / "As Acharya Ji reads it". Not used in any title or snippet.
4. **English "the scriptures call it sin" and "The scriptures do not call Kali evil" (review: Minor).** The Hindi is passive and names no source. Recommendation: match the Hindi. The direct answer and FAQ 1 present the rajas/tamas/sattva rule as Acharya Ji's teaching, not as scripture, and FAQ 5 says "Kali is called tamas shakti" without "the scriptures".
5. **Spelling "सत्व" (review: Minor).** Gita Press spelling is "सत्त्व". The Hindi title, meta, snippet and tag use "सत्त्व"; the secondary keyword "सत्व रज तम गुण" keeps the common search spelling on purpose, as a keyword only. Recommendation: correct the blog text to "सत्त्व".
6. **तम / तमस, रज / रजस and English "raj shakti" (review: Minor).** Recommendation: standardise on "तमस शक्ति", "रजस शक्ति" and English "rajas shakti". The SEO text avoids "raj shakti".
7. **"त्रिगुणात्मक" for the Devi (review: Minor).** Recommendation: "त्रिगुणात्मिका". Not used in the SEO text.
8. **"लक्ष्मी को दैवी संपत्ति कहा गया है" / "divine wealth" (review: Minor, optional).** In the Gita, दैवी सम्पद् means the divine qualities. The in-text link on "दैवी संपत्ति" / "divine wealth" goes to the Lakshmi Puja post, which itself treats Lakshmi as divine qualities, so the link helps the reader with this nuance. If the editor rewrites the sentence to "दैवी संपदा", update the Hindi link phrase in seo.json and in section 11.
9. **YouTube title hype (SEO).** "The Real Difference That Will Change Your Life Forever" promises more than a 4-minute talk can; the blog titles and meta avoid it. Recommendation: if the YouTube title is ever revised, use "Punya vs Paap: Why the Same Action Can Be Sin or Virtue | Acharya Upendra Ji".
