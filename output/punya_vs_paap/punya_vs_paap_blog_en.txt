# Why the Same Action Is Sometimes Punya and Sometimes Paap

Lakshmi has been called divine wealth. And Goddess Durga is called trigunatmak, made of three gunas. These three gunas are **sattva** (purity, knowledge), **rajas** (motion, action) and **tamas** (inertia, darkness).

Durga's form is made of the combination of these three gunas. And in them lies the secret of paap and punya.

## Whom Did Durga Slay?

The word Durga means she who slew the demon Durgam. This demon is **maya**. And *durgam*, that which is hard to cross, is this world.

To cross this world means to cross these three gunas.

## Am I Really Meditating?

Knowingly or unknowingly, we just keep leaping from one guna to another.

Someone thinks he is in meditation, that yoga nidra has set in. But in reality he has slipped into tamas, he has fallen asleep.

> "You think I am meditating; actually he has gone into tamas, he has fallen asleep."

From outside, this is not easy to recognise. One person sits like a saint, free of all worldly worries, and another lies there drunk. Neither has any worry. Telling them apart is very difficult.

*When you sit with your eyes closed, is that stillness awareness, or is it sleep?*

## Alike Outside, Different Inside

The scriptures say that **sattva and tamas look the same from outside**.

Take charity. One person gives with sincere feeling. Another gives for show, another for the ego. To the eye, all three look the same.

The act of giving is **rajas**. But the knowledge and feeling inside, behind it, can be sattva or it can be tamas.

## A Simple Definition of Paap and Punya

Lakshmi is action, she is rajas. Rajo guna by itself is neutral. With whom, where and with what feeling the same action is done is what can turn it into a problem.

> "If rajas is supported by tamo guna, it is called paap; and when that same rajo guna is supported by sattva, it becomes punya."

A simple example. You went to the gym and lifted dumbbells. This is **raja shakti**. The benefit? The body becomes strong.

Now if someone uses that same body to become a thug and go beat or rob someone, rajas has joined with tamas. That is paap. When that same strength joins with Saraswati, that is, with knowledge, it becomes punya.

*Today, what feeling is your energy and ability joined with?*

## Let Go of the Guna, Not the Shakti

Kali has not been called bad. She is called **tamas shakti**, not tamo guna.

Every guna has a shakti with it:

- Tamo guna and tamas shakti
- Rajo guna and raja shakti
- Sattva guna and sattva shakti

Shakti means the Goddess. We must always use all three shaktis. But only one guna is to be kept: **sattva guna**.

> "Let all the other gunas go, but do not give up their shakti."

## The Secret of Shivaji Maharaj

When Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj went to battle, tamas shakti, the shakti of Kali, was with him. Bhavani Mata means Kali herself.

He used that shakti fully. But what was his guna? **Supreme sattva**.

## Key Takeaways

- Durga is trigunatmak; crossing the world means crossing sattva, rajas and tamas.
- Sattva and tamas look alike from outside: meditation and sleep, sincere charity and show.
- The action in every deed is rajas; the feeling behind it decides what it becomes.
- Rajas + tamo guna = paap; rajas + sattva = punya.
- Guna and shakti are different: use all three shaktis, but keep only sattva as your guna.
- Shivaji Maharaj took the shakti of Kali, but his guna was supreme sattva.
