# Chapter: The Real Role of Maha Kali

## Durga Devi of the Three Gunas

Durga Devi is made of the three gunas. She takes three forms. What are the three gunas? Sattvic, rajasic and tamasic. Which one is sattvic? Saraswati, who brings knowledge, as we said earlier. Then who is rajasic? Mahalakshmi. And the third? Maha Kali.

Now, whom do we need to understand? Maha Kali.

## For Whom Does Maha Kali Fight?

If Maha Kali fights, for whom does she fight? For the other two. It is wealth that gets looted. That is why so many people are needed to protect it. And the intellectual property of knowledge gets stolen. That is why all this defence is needed.

So I asked: to do non-violent work, to spread dharma, what do you need first? First you need a watchman, security.

## The Boundary and the Watchman Before Construction

If you want to build something, first comes the plot of land. Before digging the pit for the foundation, a boundary is put up outside. And outside the boundary sits a watchman. Why does he sit there? Because everything begins with Maha Kali.

If you leave the plot open and come back to work after a week, you will find a hut already standing there. To remove it you will have to bring another force, the police. Rather than doing all that, why not put your security in place first? That is called Maha Kali. Before you start anything.

> "Whenever you start anything, what do you start with? With Maha Kali."

That is why, of the three parts of our Durga Saptashati, which comes first? Maha Kali. So whenever you begin anything, you must begin with Maha Kali. This has two or three meanings.

## Indra's Hundred Eyes in Kali Yuga

Now you have started a company, the office is up and running. What must be done first of all? A camera there, a camera there, a camera there. What is it for? So that no one makes trouble here and we can sleep without worry. It can be seen on a mobile from anywhere in the world. For me to relax, I need someone watching.

It is said that Indra has a hundred eyes. In Kali Yuga those hundred eyes of Indra come from the Kalika yantra, the machine. All these eyes, the spies, all of this work, that is Kali's first job. Then the watchman below. All this is **external protection**.

## Protecting Your Name and Your Creation

After that come your copyrights and patents. Is the name "Antar" visible there? It is written right here, isn't it? Is there an "R" written next to Antar or not? There is. Why? So that tomorrow no one else takes that name. You will do great work, and after that someone will certainly try to take the name.

> "Whether you believe it or not, somebody will try to come."

## Guna-Vritti-Virodha: Ravana and Sita

This world has three gunas, and among them **guna-vritti-virodha**, the conflict of the gunas, is described. The higher you try to rise, the more someone will pull your leg. Those who do not accept this, all the scriptures call foolish. Ravana will carry them off as he carried off Sita.

Ravana is the tamasic people. And this rajas shakti, Sita, Lakshmi, rajas, they will carry it away. And whatever sattva you have, that is, your creation: some poems you have written, a song, or your scientific formula. Point a gun at a scientist, and the formula is gone in one minute. So patents, copyrights, everything has to be done first, before you even start anything.

## Nature Wants Expansion

Because nature says that everything should expand. Man keeps searching for infinite bliss. At first he does not know that infinite bliss will come from within. He thinks it is outside.

That is why we say: **pure feeling, a clear mind, a still mind, a vast mind**, and then surrender, "everything dissolves", in Shiva's time. But until then, while the mind is growing vast, we all try to expand. Each one tries as much as he can.

So when you expand, protection first. As soon as something begins, as Antar began, I should not stop at teaching three people. The next thought is: why not a hundred? A hundred, then why not a thousand? A thousand, then why not ten thousand? Then why not the whole world? And the moment you expand, if it is a hospital, then hospital branches; if it is pizza, then a pizza franchise. That is how a person grows big.

## The Evil Eye on Those Who Grow

And as soon as you think of growing big, nature says the dark eye, the evil eye, will fall on you, because not everyone can bear to see it. Growing big in this world is very hard. It is said that maya is very hard to cross; it is extremely difficult to get across.

And of the one who makes this effort, the Bhagavad Gita says:

> **yad yad vibhūtimat sattvaṁ śrīmad ūrjitam eva vā**
> **tat tad evāvagaccha tvaṁ mama tejo'ṁśa-sambhavam**
> "Whatever is glorious, radiant and powerful, know it to be an expression of a fraction of My splendour."
> (Bhagavad Gita 10.41)

So that must be something powerful. Whoever is powerful, whoever is wealthy, whoever is radiant, that is, someone famous, like Miss World or Miss Universe, everyone wants to do something to them: throw acid, do this, ruin her face. People will spoil even beauty. There is a tendency to ruin even what is good.

> "You need to protect what is beautiful."

**Satyam Shivam Sundaram.** What is beautiful must be kept safe.

## The Example of the Platform and the Police

Otherwise you build such a fine platform, and from the next day on, people spit paan on it. So the police have to stand there. Platforms abroad or on our metro, why do they stay clean? Why do they look good? Because police stand there. On our regular platforms, if no one is watching, people will do anything. So first you need to have defence in place.

## The Hotel Branch and the Dead Cockroach

So whatever you have, if it is Lakshmi, then beauty, money, wealth, whatever you want to do, or if it is the work of knowledge, what do you need first? If you only expand, what will people do? Because they cannot rise themselves.

You opened a hotel, and it grew to ten branches. Someone will deliberately go to one branch. You serve tea, you serve coffee. He will bring a dead cockroach in his pocket and drop it in: "Look what was in their tea." He came with a camera and brought back a video. Then what will you do?

You also need a camera above, showing what he did. The camera is always placed so that if someone tries something here, or tries to switch it off, it can be seen from the other side what he did. There is complete tracking. Today this is done digitally; earlier it was physical, with checking ten times over.

So now you understand why it is essential. This is the real role of Maha Kali: to protect what is sattvic and rajasic, knowledge and Lakshmi, and this protection must come before anything begins.
