# Why Maha Kali Comes First: The Guardian Power of Every Auspicious Beginning

We often see Maha Kali only in her fierce form. But have you ever wondered why the first part of the Durga Saptashati belongs to Maha Kali?

In this discourse, Acharya Upendra Ji uses simple, everyday examples to explain the real role of Maha Kali, and why every undertaking should begin with her.

## Durga of the Three Gunas: Three Qualities, Three Powers

Durga Devi is **trigunatmak**, that is, made of the three gunas. The three gunas are sattvic, rajasic and tamasic.

- **Sattvic:** Saraswati, who holds knowledge
- **Rajasic:** Mahalakshmi, who holds wealth and prosperity
- **Tamasic:** Maha Kali

Now the question is: if Maha Kali fights, for whom does she fight? The answer: for the other two.

## Protecting Whom? Knowledge and Lakshmi

It is wealth that gets looted. That is why so many people are needed to protect it. And the intellectual property of knowledge gets stolen. That is why all this security is needed.

Acharya Ji asks: if you want to do non-violent work, to spread dharma, what do you need first? The answer: a watchman, security.

*Whatever you are building in your life, have you also thought about how to protect it?*

## First the Boundary, Then the Foundation

If you want to build something, first comes the plot. Before digging the foundation, a boundary is put up outside, and a watchman sits outside the boundary.

If you leave the plot open and come back after a week, you will find a hut already standing there. To remove it you will have to bring in the police. Better to put security in place first. That is called Maha Kali. Acharya Ji explains that whenever we start anything, we start with Maha Kali.

That is why, of the three parts of the Durga Saptashati, the first belongs to Maha Kali.

## Indra's Hundred Eyes in Kali Yuga

You started a company, the office is ready. What do you do first? A camera here, a camera there. So that no one makes trouble and you can sleep without worry. You can see everything on your mobile from anywhere in the world.

It is said that Indra has a hundred eyes. Acharya Ji says that in Kali Yuga those hundred eyes come from the **Kalika yantra**, the machine. These eyes, these spies, are **Kali's first job**. Then the watchman below. All this is **external protection** (bahya rakshan).

## Protecting Your Name and Your Creation

After external security come copyrights and patents. Why is there an "R" next to the name "Antar"? So that tomorrow no one else takes that name. If you do great work, someone will certainly come to take the name. Acharya Ji says that whether we believe it or not, somebody will try.

## Guna-Vritti-Virodha: When Someone Pulls Your Leg

The world is made of three gunas, and the scriptures describe **guna-vritti-virodha**, the natural conflict between the gunas. The higher you try to rise, the more someone will pull your leg.

Those who do not accept this, the scriptures call foolish. Ravana will carry off their Sita. Here Ravana is the tamasic people, and Sita is rajas shakti, Lakshmi.

And your sattva, that is, your creation: a poem, a song, a scientific formula. Point a gun at a scientist, and the formula is gone in a minute. So patents, copyrights, everything must be done before you even begin.

## Nature Wants Expansion

Nature says everything should expand, because man keeps searching for **infinite bliss**. At first he does not know that infinite bliss is within; he looks for it outside.

Acharya Ji describes this journey like this: **pure feeling, a clear mind, a still mind, a vast mind**, and then surrender, "everything dissolves", in Shiva's time. But before that, while the mind is growing vast, we all want to expand.

Taught three people, so why not a hundred? A hundred, then why not a thousand? A thousand, then ten thousand, then why not the whole world? A hospital has branches, pizza has franchises. That is how a person grows big.

*Is your expansion running ahead of your protection?*

## The Evil Eye Comes as You Grow

As soon as you think of growing big, nature says the dark eye, the evil eye, will fall on you, because not everyone can bear to see it. Maya is very hard to cross; getting across it is extremely difficult.

In the Bhagavad Gita, the Lord says:

> **yad yad vibhūtimat sattvaṁ śrīmad ūrjitam eva vā**
> **tat tad evāvagaccha tvaṁ mama tejo'ṁśa-sambhavam**
> "Whatever is glorious, radiant and powerful, know it to be an expression of a fraction of My splendour."
> (Bhagavad Gita 10.41)

People want to strike at everything powerful, wealthy, beautiful or famous. With a Miss World or Miss Universe, there is even the urge to throw acid and ruin her face. There is a tendency to ruin what is good, which is why Acharya Ji says we need to protect what is beautiful.

**Satyam Shivam Sundaram.** What is beautiful must be kept safe.

## Why Does a Platform Stay Clean?

You build a new platform, and from the next day people spit paan on it. Why do platforms abroad or on our metro stay clean? Because police stand there. Where no one is watching, people will do anything. So security comes first.

## The Hotel and the Dead Cockroach

You opened a hotel, and it grew to ten branches. If you only expand, what will people do? Those who cannot rise themselves will come to pull you down.

Someone will deliberately walk into a branch, take a dead cockroach out of his pocket, drop it into the tea, and leave with a video on his camera. Then what will you do?

You also need a camera above, showing what he did. Complete tracking. Earlier this was physical, with checking ten times over. Today it is digital. The principle is the same.

*Where is the "Maha Kali" in your life that protects your knowledge, wealth or beauty?*

## Key Takeaways

- Durga is made of three gunas: Saraswati (sattvic, knowledge), Mahalakshmi (rajasic, wealth), Maha Kali (tamasic, protection).
- Maha Kali fights to protect the other two, knowledge and Lakshmi.
- Every undertaking should begin with Maha Kali, that is, with protection. That is why the first part of the Durga Saptashati is Maha Kali's.
- Both external protection (cameras, watchmen) and protection of your name and creation (patents, copyrights) are needed.
- Because of guna-vritti-virodha, whoever rises gets their leg pulled.
- Nature wants expansion, but expansion brings the evil eye. So protection before expansion.
- Satyam Shivam Sundaram: what is beautiful must be protected.
