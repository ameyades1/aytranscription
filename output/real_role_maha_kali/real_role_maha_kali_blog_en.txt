# Why Mahakali Comes First: The Power That Guards Every Auspicious Beginning

We often see Mahakali only in her fierce form. But have you ever wondered why the first section of the **Durga Saptashati** (the seven hundred verses in praise of the Divine Mother) belongs to Mahakali?

In this discourse, Acharya Upendra Ji uses simple, everyday examples to explain Mahakali's real role, and why every undertaking should begin with her.

## Durga of the Three Gunas: Three Qualities, Three Powers

Maa Durga is **trigunatmika** (made of the three gunas, the basic qualities of nature). These three qualities are sattvic, rajasic and tamasic.

- **Sattvic:** Saraswati, who holds knowledge
- **Rajasic:** Mahalakshmi, who holds wealth and splendour
- **Tamasic:** Mahakali

The question then arises: if Mahakali fights, whom does she fight for? The answer is the other two.

## What Needs Guarding? Knowledge and Wealth

Wealth is what thieves come after. Guarding it takes a small army of people. Learning gets stolen too, in the form of intellectual property. That is why every kind of security arrangement is needed.

Acharya Ji asks: suppose you want to do something entirely non-violent, such as spreading dharma (the righteous way of life). What do you need first? A watchman. Security.

*Have you thought about how you will protect what you are building in your life?*

## First the Boundary, Then the Foundation

When you set out to build a house, the plot comes first. Before anyone digs the pit for the foundation, a boundary wall goes up around it, and a watchman sits outside that wall.

Leave the plot open and come back a week later, and you will find a shack already standing on it. Removing it will then take a police force. Far better to put security in place from the start. This is what we call Mahakali. Whatever you begin, Acharya Ji explains, you have to begin with Mahakali.

That is why, of the three sections of the Durga Saptashati, the first belongs to her.

## Indra's Hundred Eyes in the Kali Yuga

Say you have started a company and set up an office. What is the first thing you do? A camera here, a camera there, so that nobody can get up to mischief and you can sleep in peace. From anywhere in the world, you can watch everything on your phone.

Indra, king of the gods, is said to have a hundred eyes. In the **Kali Yuga** (the present dark age), Acharya Ji says, those hundred eyes come from the **Kalika yantra** (a play on words: the machines of Kali's age). These eyes, these spies, are **Kali's first task**. Below them sits the watchman. All of this is **bahya rakshan** (external protection).

## Protecting Your Name and Your Creations

After physical security come copyright and patents. Why is there an "R" beside the name "Antar"? So that nobody else can claim that name tomorrow. If you do something great, someone is bound to come after the name. Whether we accept it or not, Acharya Ji says, someone will try.

## Guna-Vritti-Virodha: When Someone Pulls You Down

The world is made of the three gunas, and the scriptures describe **guna-vritti-virodha** (the natural conflict between the qualities). The higher you try to climb, the more surely someone will try to pull you down.

The scriptures call those who refuse to accept this foolish. Ravana will carry off their Sita, just as he abducted Shri Rama's wife in the Ramayana. Here, Ravana stands for tamasic people, and Sita for the rajasic power: Lakshmi, your wealth.

Your sattva, meanwhile, is your creative work: a poem, a song, a scientific formula. Point a gun at a scientist and the formula is gone in a minute. That is why patents, copyright and the rest must be in place even before you begin.

## Nature Wants to Expand

Nature says that everything should grow and spread. This is because human beings keep searching for **infinite bliss**. At first we do not know that this bliss lies within, so we look for it outside.

Acharya Ji describes the journey this way: **a pure feeling, a clear mind, a steady mind, an expansive mind**, and then surrender, when everything dissolves, the stage that belongs to Lord Shiva. Before that, while the mind is still expanding, all of us want to grow.

If you have taught three people, why not a hundred? If a hundred, why not a thousand? Then ten thousand, and why not the whole world? A hospital opens branches, a pizza place sells franchises. This is how people grow.

*Is your growth running ahead of your protection?*

## Grow, and the Evil Eye Follows

The moment you think of growing, nature warns that the evil eye will fall on you, because not everyone can bear to see it. **Maya** (the illusion that binds the world) is very hard to cross.

In the Bhagavad Gita, Shri Krishna says:

> **yad yad vibhūtimat sattvaṁ śrīmad ūrjitam eva vā**
> **tat tad evāvagaccha tvaṁ mama tejo'ṁśa-sambhavam**
> "Whatever is glorious, radiant or mighty, know that it springs from a spark of My splendour."
> (Bhagavad Gita 10.41)

People want to strike at anything powerful, wealthy, beautiful or famous. Some are even driven to throw acid on a Miss World or Miss Universe and disfigure her face. There is an urge to spoil whatever is good, which is why, as Acharya Ji says, beauty has to be protected.

**Satyam Shivam Sundaram** (truth, goodness, beauty). Whatever is beautiful must be kept safe.

## Why Does a Platform Stay Clean?

A new railway platform is built, and from the very next day it is covered in streaks of spat-out paan. Why do platforms abroad, or on our own metro, stay clean? Because police stand there. Where nobody is watching, people will behave however they please. That is why security comes first.

## The Hotel and the Dead Cockroach

You open a hotel, and soon you have ten branches. If all you do is expand, what will people do? Those who cannot rise themselves will come to bring you down.

Someone will walk into one of your branches on purpose, take a dead cockroach out of their pocket, drop it in the tea, record a video and leave. What will you do then?

You need a camera overhead too, one that shows exactly what they did. Full surveillance. In the past this was physical, with checks carried out again and again. Today it is digital, but the principle remains the same.

*Where is the "Mahakali" in your life that protects your knowledge, your wealth or your beauty?*

## Key Takeaways

- Durga embodies the three gunas: Saraswati (sattvic, knowledge), Mahalakshmi (rajasic, wealth) and Mahakali (tamasic, protection).
- Mahakali fights to protect the other two, knowledge and wealth.
- Every undertaking should begin with Mahakali, that is, with security. That is why she has the first section of the Durga Saptashati.
- You need both external protection (cameras, watchmen) and protection for your name and creations (patents, copyright).
- Because of guna-vritti-virodha, whoever rises will find someone trying to pull them down.
- Nature wants to expand, but expansion draws the evil eye. Put protection in place before you grow.
- Satyam Shivam Sundaram: whatever is beautiful has to be protected.
