# Chapter: Control Your Mind Before It Controls You

## Entertainment Is Not Forbidden

If I am watching television, watching television is not forbidden. I do not say, "No, not at all, never." Of all the worlds of the nine planets, you may even watch a Bollywood film; there is no problem. Your mood at that time may go up and down. You watched it, fine. Just do not watch what is extremely obscene and filthy; the Guru will not permit that. But if you are watching some ordinary entertainment connected with Shukra, some serial, that is fine.

## The Choice of Sattvic Intellect

You are absorbed in what you are watching. This ability to absorb the mind is within you, so whatever you do, do it with full concentration. But if the sattvic intellect has joined with it, together with **dhriti**, it will first choose whether this serial should be watched or not, whether it will spoil my mind or not.

> **dhṛtyā yayā dhārayate manaḥ-prāṇendriya-kriyāḥ**
> **yogenāvyabhicāriṇyā dhṛtiḥ sā pārtha sāttvikī**
> "O Partha, the unwavering steadiness by which, through yoga, one holds the activities of the mind, the life breath and the senses, that steadiness is sattvic."
> (Bhagavad Gita 18.33)

O Partha! The unwavering power of holding by which a person, through yoga, holds the activities of the mind, prana and senses, that dhriti is sattvic.

## When the Child Comes Crying

Now, that programme is over. After that your child comes to you crying: "Mother, I have to do this homework," "Mother, I hurt my foot." Should you now forget your duty, that I am this child's mother or father? The child's pain, the child's crying, is far more important than your serial. This decision comes from the discerning intellect.

And after that you must know how to withdraw your mind from that serial just as quickly. As easy as it was to absorb the mind, you must know how to withdraw it just as easily.

Only one who has firmly held that this is an activity of my mind can do this always. When I was watching television, even through it my aim was the Supreme Lord; my holding was unwavering in the Lord. But at that very moment another duty arrived. About the sattvic intellect, duty and non-duty are also spoken of; it makes that decision. A higher duty has come here, so there must also be the ability to withdraw the mind from there at once. Otherwise a person will tell the child, "Let your crying go on, let your blood flow, my serial will end in half an hour, then I will come."

## The Seven Hundred Rupee Investment

Someone has had an accident, and you must get there at once. Someone has passed away. And you say, "Oh, we had a three-hour film, we are sitting in the theatre, we have spent money on the film. The ticket is four hundred rupees, I took popcorn in my hand, that was three hundred rupees more. Seven hundred rupees are already spent, it is an investment! I will not understand the film later." "Oh, if he died, he died, it's fine." Like that?

At that time watching the film seemed right. Perhaps you had gone with your wife to watch the film; there was no problem. But a duty greater than that duty arrived.

## As Much to Absorb, As Much to Withdraw

Because the sattvic intellect understands duty and non-duty, and if its power of holding has become sattvic, the mind will be under its control. Then whenever it wishes, it places the mind, unwaveringly in the Lord and in the higher duty. As much as it can absorb the mind, that much power to withdraw it from there also comes to it.

If you have firmly held that the mind must be under my control, I can absorb it whenever I wish and withdraw it whenever I wish, the mind is not to run me by its own will, then you are the master.

## The Mind Is the Master, You Are the Slave

It should not be that I see something in the market and I simply have to go. What was that point about tendency? I see liquor, so I must go. Now the mind has become the master and you have become the slave. I am saying the opposite: control must be in your hands. Do you understand the difference now? You saw a woman, lust awoke, and you went after her at once. That is the tendency of desire.

So the control I am speaking of is of the mind: its staying within, and coming out just as fully. Its power of concentration must be that strong. What it makes the mind do, again and again removing it from here and placing it there, is not from the rajasic tendency. It is from the sattvic tendency, because it is being done for a higher duty. Here there is more bondage, there is liberation, so one should go there, withdrawing the mind at once. Is this clear to you now?

## The Prime Minister and the World Cup

A sports tournament was going on in the country. It is the World Cup, and the country has reached the semi-final, the final. The Prime Minister came to the ceremony and sat down. It is his duty; today the World Cup is to be presented by his hands, whether to our own country or to another. So his presence there is necessary.

It is an international event. The Prime Minister will not come for some small triangular series, but the World Cup is a special event for him, and our own country has reached it too. So he came to the ceremony and sat down.

He watched four overs, and a phone call came: "Sir, there has been a bomb blast in the neighbouring state." Now, would it be proper for the Prime Minister to keep sitting at that match? Even if he is the chief guest, tell me, will he have to withdraw his mind from that match at once or not? That was an international event, the World Cup, not some small event. But above sports, in the duty of a king, does saving the lives of the people of the kingdom come or not?

## Protection Above Entertainment

Organising sports is also the king's work; the sports department is also a department. But from here the mind must be withdrawn at once, in a single jolt. There the king is entertaining the people, giving them delight. But now he must also protect, and **protection is higher**.

When he understands this, the one in whom this tendency lives will become a good **dharta**, a good upholder, a good maintainer. Do you accept this?

But this should not be like the rajasic tendency. This is his control over duty and non-duty: whether to place the mind here or there. A person of this quality can become a good maintainer. He can become a good **Vishnu**, a good sustainer, a good protector. This is the good upholder.
