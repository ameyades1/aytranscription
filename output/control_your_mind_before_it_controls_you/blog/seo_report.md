## 1. Summary

Acharya Upendra Ji answers a common seeker's question, "does spiritual life mean giving up entertainment?", with a sharper one: who holds the key to your mind? Using Bhagavad Gita 18.33 (sattvic dhriti), he teaches that real control means withdrawing the mind from something as easily as it settles into it, and illustrates it with a crying child during a TV serial, a seven-hundred-rupee cinema ticket and a Prime Minister leaving a World Cup final for a bomb blast. The main intent is informational with a strong practical and emotional edge (people who feel ruled by screens, habits and cravings). "How to control the mind" is a crowded query dominated by generic self-help; the opportunity is the narrower Gita-based query (dhriti, 18.33) plus the "is watching TV/movies wrong for a spiritual person" question, which few authentic sources answer without moralising.

**English (≈55 words):** According to Acharya Upendra Ji, controlling the mind does not mean giving up entertainment. It means the mind can leave something as easily as it settles into it. The Bhagavad Gita (18.33) calls this unwavering power of holding firm sattvic dhriti: when a higher duty arrives, it turns the mind away in a single stroke.

## 6. FAQ

1. **क्या आध्यात्मिक व्यक्ति के लिए फ़िल्म या धारावाहिक देखना गलत है? / Is it wrong for a spiritual person to watch films or serials?**
   नहीं। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि सामान्य मनोरंजन में कोई समस्या नहीं, बस अश्लील और गंदा न देखें, और जो देखें पूरे मन से देखें। / No. Acharya Ji says ordinary entertainment is fine; only avoid anything vulgar or obscene, and give whatever you watch your full attention.
2. **सात्त्विक धृति क्या है? / What is sattvic dhriti?**
   गीता १८.३३ के अनुसार वह अव्यभिचारिणी (कभी न डगमगाने वाली) धारणा शक्ति, जिससे मनुष्य योग द्वारा मन, प्राण और इंद्रियों की क्रियाओं को धारण करता है। / Per Gita 18.33, the unwavering steadfastness with which a person, through yoga, holds steady the workings of the mind, life breath and senses.
3. **मन पर सच्चा नियंत्रण कैसे पहचानें? / How do you know your mind is truly under control?**
   जब मन जितनी आसानी से किसी चीज़ में लगता है, उतनी ही आसानी से ऊँचे कर्तव्य के लिए वहाँ से हट भी जाए, जैसे रोते बच्चे के लिए धारावाहिक छोड़ देना। / When it leaves something for a higher duty as easily as it got absorbed, like leaving a serial at once when your child comes in crying.
4. **मन कब मालिक बन जाता है? / When does the mind become the master?**
   जब कुछ दिखते ही उसके पीछे जाना ही पड़े, बाज़ार की वस्तु, मदिरा या काम-वासना; यह प्रवृत्ति है, और तब हम मन के गुलाम हैं। / When you must chase whatever you see, an item in the market, alcohol or lust; this habitual pull (pravritti) makes the mind the master and us its slaves.
5. **क्या बार-बार मन हटाना और लगाना राजसिक नहीं है? / Isn't constantly shifting the mind rajasic?**
   नहीं, जब यह ऊँचे कर्तव्य के लिए हो तो यह सात्त्विक वृत्ति है: बंधन से निकलकर मोक्ष की ओर जाना। / No; when it serves a higher duty it is sattvic, moving from bondage towards liberation.
