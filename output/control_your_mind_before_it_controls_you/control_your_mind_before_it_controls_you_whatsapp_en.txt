*Does a spiritual life mean giving up entertainment?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that true control is withdrawing your mind as easily as it settles in. Enjoy your TV serial, but if your child comes crying, leave it at once.

💭 _Can your mind leave something as easily as it gets absorbed?_
