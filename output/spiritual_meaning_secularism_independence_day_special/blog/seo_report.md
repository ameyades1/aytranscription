The real question, he says, is not whether the country is secular but how well I live my own faith: show its phalashruti (visible fruit), do not hate others, welcome the guest as God but with discernment, and before calling yourself a Hindu or a yogi, live the five yamas of Patanjali. ## 6. FAQ

For the page or WordPress only; no FAQPage schema (see section 8).

1. **धर्मनिरपेक्षता का आध्यात्मिक अर्थ क्या है? / What is the spiritual meaning of secularism?**
   Hindi: इसका आधार ऋग्वेद का वाक्य "एकं सद्विप्रा बहुधा वदन्ति" है: सत्य एक है, विद्वान उसे अनेक प्रकार से कहते हैं। जब तक सब उसी एक सत्य की बात करते हैं, सब ठीक है।
   English: Its root is the Rig Veda line "ekaṃ sad viprā bahudhā vadanti": Truth is one, and the wise describe it in many ways. As long as all paths speak of that one truth, all is well.
2. **क्या भारत के सेक्युलर होने से हिंदू धर्म कमज़ोर होता है? / Does a secular India weaken Hindu dharma?**
   Hindi: आचार्य जी के अनुसार मुद्दा यह नहीं कि देश सेक्युलर है या नहीं; मुद्दा यह है कि मैं अपना धर्म कितनी अच्छी तरह जीता हूं। जो अपनी आस्था की फलश्रुति दिखा सके, उसे कोई तोड़ नहीं सकता।
   English: Acharya Ji says the issue is not whether the country is secular, but how well I live my own faith. Whoever can show the fruit of their faith cannot be broken.
3. **"अतिथि देवो भव" का क्या अर्थ है? / What does "atithi devo bhava" mean?**
   Hindi: तैत्तिरीय उपनिषद् (१.११.२) का यह वाक्य कहता है: अतिथि को देवता मानो। आचार्य जी जोड़ते हैं कि स्वागत विवेक के साथ हो; स्वागत का अर्थ अपना घर सौंप देना नहीं।
   English: This line of the Taittiriya Upanishad (1.11.2) means "Treat your guest as God". Acharya Ji adds that welcome must come with discernment; it does not mean handing over your home.
4. **योग के पांच यम कौन से हैं? / What are the five yamas of yoga?**
   Hindi: पतंजलि योगसूत्र २.३० के अनुसार अहिंसा, सत्य, अस्तेय, ब्रह्मचर्य और अपरिग्रह। आचार्य जी कहते हैं, स्वयं को हिंदू या योगी कहने से पहले इन पांच की फलश्रुति जीवन में दिखाओ।
   English: According to Yoga Sutras 2.30: ahimsa, satya, asteya, brahmacharya and aparigraha. Acharya Ji says: before calling yourself a Hindu or a yogi, show the fruit of these five in your life.
5. **फलश्रुति का क्या अर्थ है? / What is phalashruti?**
   Hindi: शास्त्र में फलश्रुति वह अंश है जो किसी पाठ या साधना का फल बताता है; आचार्य जी इसे साधना के प्रत्यक्ष, दिखाई देने वाले परिणाम के अर्थ में लेते हैं।
   English: In scripture, phalashruti is the passage that states the fruit of a recitation or practice; Acharya Ji uses it for the direct, visible result of one's sadhana.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Meaning of Rig Veda 1.164.46 (Sanskrit review, item 1, Major).** The blog renders "एकं सद्विप्रा बहुधा वदन्ति" as "Truth is one, the truth of God"; "the truth of God" is the speaker's gloss, and the verse says the wise call the one Reality by many names. It is also only a pada of the mantra. Recommendation: apply the review's correction (mark it as part of the mantra; give the meaning "Reality is one; the wise call it by many names", with Acharya Ji's reading as a separate sentence). The SEO copy (meta, snippet, FAQ) says only "Truth is one, the wise describe it in many ways" and does not repeat "the truth of God".
2. **Phalashruti presented as the word's meaning (review, item 6).** The blog defines फलश्रुति as the visible result of a practice; in scripture it is the passage stating the fruit. The FAQ answer (Q5) gives both, attributing the extended use to Acharya Ji. Apply the review's correction in the blog.
3. **Politically charged analogy.** The guest who takes over the bedroom, kitchen and wealth and sends the host to a "refugee quarter", and the remark that rules for people "from another religion or another land" are very old, can be read as commentary on a specific community or on migration policy. The talk does not name anyone or discuss policy. Recommendation: keep it out of titles, meta, tags, slugs and social cards (done); do not target immigration or "Hindu rashtra" queries; the in-text link goes to a teaching on treating each on merit, not to political content. The editor may consider a one-line framing that the analogy is about discernment in one's own home and life.
4. **Other review items for the editor:** Hindi Yoga Sutra 2.29 meaning drops "these are the eight limbs" (item 2); the yama list puts satya before ahimsa, unlike sutra 2.30 (item 3; the FAQ uses the sutra order); "पतंजलि योगसूत्र" vs "पातंजल योगसूत्र" (item 4); double danda (item 5); "अतिथि देवो भव" in the Hindi heading and takeaway vs "अतिथिदेवो भव" in the verse (item 7); "यम के पांच नियमों" (item 8). Note that the Also read placement copies the current Hindi heading "अतिथि देवो भव, पर विवेक के साथ"; if the heading is corrected, update `also_read.after.hi` in seo.json.
5. **"Truth is one" in the English meta and keyword "is secularism against Hinduism".** The talk does not say secularism is or is not against Hinduism; it says the real question is how one lives one's faith. The FAQ (Q2) answers from that, and no title claims a verdict. Keep it so.
6. **Arthashastra.** The blog says the Guru wants to share lessons from Chanakya's Arthashastra but gives none in this talk. Do not promise "Chanakya's rules for guests" in any title or snippet; the Also read box points to truly_independent, which has a Chanakya section.

- [x] Gives a clear reason to click
