was_ram_rajya_like_federal_or: SEO report

## 1. Summary

A short Independence Day discourse (video uploaded 2026-08-15, 6:32, "राम राज्य कैसा था? Federal या Unitary?") in which Acharya Ji asks whether a federal system of government can ever bring about Ram Rajya. His answer: the great kingdoms (Shri Rama, Chandragupta Maurya, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj) were completely unitary, a single hand and a single voice; a system in which the centre and the states may hold opposing views turns into a "khichdi". Through the examples of a spiritual organisation's branches and a pizza company whose branches sell dosa, he gives the formula: surrender (sharanagati) to the centre's word, with each local unit free to serve its own people in its own language. The main intent is informational ("राम राज्य कैसा था", "what was Ram Rajya like"), with a strong civic and seasonal pull around 15 August and 26 January. "राम राज्य" / "Ram Rajya" is a broad, competitive query (Wikipedia, news, political commentary); the opportunity is the narrower question this post answers uniquely, "was Ram Rajya federal or unitary", and the post must stay a teaching on governance and surrender, not a partisan argument.

   आचार्य जी के अनुसार पूर्णतः एकात्मक: एक ही हाथ, एक ही आवाज़। / According to Acharya Ji, completely unitary: one hand at the helm, one voice.

## 10. Authenticity flags

From the Sanskrit review (`tools/review/was_ram_rajya_like_federal_or.md`, minor issues):

1. **"Shri Rama's kingdom was entirely unitary" is stated as fact.** "Unitary" is a modern category; in the Valmiki Ramayana allied and subordinate kingdoms keep crowned kings (Sugriva, Vibhishana, Shatrughna at Madhura, the princes' new cities, Kusha and Lava). Recommendation: attribute the sentence and the matching takeaway to Acharya Ji, as the review proposes. In SEO copy, the titles, metas, direct answer and FAQ already frame it as a question or as "according to Acharya Ji"; never write "the Ramayana says Ram Rajya was unitary".
2. **Shivaji Maharaj and "foreign travellers".** No traveller is named and the wording cannot be traced. Recommendation: use the review's attributed wording; keep Shivaji Maharaj out of titles, metas and schema.
3. **"Local employment" in the takeaway** is not in the body (which speaks of language and priority on the service team). Recommendation: the review's correction. The SEO copy here uses only "language and service".
4. **Political sensitivity.** The topic (federal vs unitary government, released for 15 August) can draw partisan searches and comments, and "a federal government can never deliver Ram Rajya" can read as a policy position. The talk's argument is built on an organisation's branches following one teaching, and its formula is surrender and service. Recommendation: target "Ram Rajya" queries, not "federalism in India", "one nation one election" or party queries; keep metas on the teaching; present the view as Acharya Ji's.
5. **Optional term:** "बंगाली भाषा" → "बांग्ला भाषा" (review item 4). Not used in SEO copy.
