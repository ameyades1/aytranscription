# Chapter: Ram Rajya, Federal or Unitary?

## What Does Federal Government Mean?

Our country has a federal government. What does that mean? How many of you even know that our country is like this? Does the word make you think the country could get "fed up"? Now listen carefully to what is meant by a federal government.

We dream of Ram Rajya, and it can never come from a federal government. A federal government means a country with more than one government, whereas power should be in a single hand. We have a central government and a state government, two of them, don't we?

You will say, what is the problem with two? Two governments make it a federal government, and when there is only one, it is called a unitary system.

## Shri Ram's Kingdom: Highly Unitary

Shri Ram's kingdom was highly unitary. First understand the difference. Chandragupta Maurya's kingdom was highly unitary. Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's government is described the same way: not just unitary, highly unitary. I am telling you what foreign travellers describe.

It means a single hand. As far as that hand reaches, my word runs, and there only one voice must be heard.

A federal government means the centre runs one part and the state runs another, and there is no guarantee that the man running things below belongs to the one above. The centre can be run by one party and the state by another. You can see this, can't you? The party at the centre can think one way and the party in the state can oppose it. Those who are the opposition at the centre can be ruling here in the state. So can the country run well like this?

## The Example of an Organisation

Let me give a simple example of why it cannot run, and why this system has to change as early as possible. Otherwise, I also have an alternative remedy. First let us see what happens.

Suppose Antar Yog Foundation is an organisation. If its head office is in Mumbai, which government is that? That is its central government. Head office means central government. Now suppose branches open in other cities, in other states, say a branch in Rajasthan. That becomes like the state government there.

Can I sit everywhere? No. But my orders can go there. Still, the work there will be run by some local head, secretary or president.

Now tell me this: I sit here teaching our principles, such as the Lord coming in the form of a person, Purusha and Prakriti and so on, and our Rajasthan branch starts teaching some entirely different tradition. What will happen? That too is spiritual, but they say they will teach in their own way. A third branch says it will teach in its own way.

Swami Vivekananda taught something. Wherever his branches are, if one teaches Vivekananda, another teaches some other religion and a third runs some other syllabus, will that work? Why not, then?

## The Example of a Business

Let us leave spirituality aside and think of business. There is a big company with a head office and many branches. Say there is a pizza centre with many branches. In one, thalipeeth is being made, in one dosa, in one pizza.

The poor headquarters says everywhere, "Make pizza!" The branch says, "No, we are in the opposition. We will make only dosa; that is what we want to sell." Here the burger man says he has started burgers, and the branch says, "No, vada! That is what we will sell here." And he is left saying, "But my company is a burger company!"

So do you understand what happens in a federal government? If you give freedom to two governments that can hold opposite views, what happens? It simply cannot run.

## Then What Is the Role of the State Government?

Could Shri Ram alone run things in every state? No. Then in every place there must be a soldier or minister of his who governs according to his view alone. Agreed?

Then what is the benefit of the local region? What is the use of the state government? This is the difference. Our teachings taught in Mumbai should be the same in Kolkata. But that government, meaning our branch head, will have just enough freedom to teach in Bengali as much as possible. The teachings must be mine.

At most he can give preference to Bengali people there, so that most of your seva team is Bengali. This is possible: local employment, because the person from there will understand the people there better. But what about the teachings? Can he create his own new teaching? Can he oppose my teachings? No.

## Where Is Our System Failing?

So do you understand where our government is failing? Here there is one fight at the sarpanch level, another at the mayor level, another at the chief minister level and another for the country. Then the friend here is the enemy there and the enemy there is the friend here. That is how it all is, isn't it? Everything has been turned into a complete khichdi. So now you understand the problem with the federal government.

## What Does Ram Rajya Need?

If we want to build a kingdom like Shri Ram's, as we dream, if we want to bring Ram Rajya, what must we bring? A highly unitary system. That means everyone listens only to the person sitting at the centre, exactly as it was said, without editing it even a little. That much **surrender (sharanagati)**.

And along with that, what should be done? The upliftment of each state, meaning in its own respective language, with its own priorities, you can do everything. That is permitted.

First, did you understand the flaw, where we are going wrong?
