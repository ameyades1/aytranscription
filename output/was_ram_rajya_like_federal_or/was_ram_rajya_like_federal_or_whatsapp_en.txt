*Was Ram Rajya federal or unitary?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that Shri Rama's rule was completely unitary, one hand at the helm. Like a pizza company whose branches sell dosa, a system with clashing governments cannot work.

💭 _If every member of your family set their own direction, how far could it go?_
