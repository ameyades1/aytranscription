# Chapter: Where You Are Born, There Is Your Food

## The Importance of Local Food

This is a very important point: **consume locally grown food.** It means that a Chinese person should eat Chinese food, not you. You can taste Chinese food for a while, but it cannot become your way of life. Is that understood?

Whether it is Mexican food or Thai food, you can eat it in an emergency, or when there is a party, for a day or two. But if you make it your lifestyle, then God will say to you, *"You will fall ill."*

## Soil, the Earth Element and Prarabdha

For a Chinese person, the food of China will be the suitable diet. The secret of his karma lies in this; that is why he was born there. That soil is meant for him, that **earth element (prithvi tattva)** is meant for him. Whatever grows from that earth is meant for him.

A creature eats those very plants and trees that grow there, and whatever it eats, those same qualities come into it. That food is ordained for it by its **prarabdha** (destiny shaped by past karma). Is this becoming clear to you or not, what I am telling you?

## The Food of Your Own Region

You were born in India and you live in India, so Indian food is for you, and within it, the food of your own region. If you are in Bengal, the food of Bengal will suit you better. If you are in Maharashtra, then the food of Maharashtra, grown right here, will be dearer to you. And it will not only be dear to you: **God will have made that very food suitable for you.**

## Taste Others, but Keep Your Own as the Foundation

That is why I said earlier:

> "Develop a taste for your local food."

I did not say do not explore other foods at all. All the other grains, all the other recipes, try them for a change of taste, that is fine. Pick them up too, all right.

Now you will say, "I live in Maharashtra, but I really like idli-sambar. Is that all right?" It is all right, no problem, I am not forbidding it. Then understand it this way: I am not only from Maharashtra; there is a vast land called India, and **I am an Indian.** So the foods of India are fine for you.
