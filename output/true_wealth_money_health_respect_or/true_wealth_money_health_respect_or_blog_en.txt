# What Is True Wealth?

## Does Hard Work Bring Wealth?

Since childhood, we have heard that wealth comes to those who work hard. Acharya Ji turns this belief on its head. Money, he explains, does not come to the hardworking; it comes to the intelligent.

He admits that this may not be pleasant to hear. Still, it is the fact, and it is the principle.

## Beyond the Intellect: The Atman

Is wealth guaranteed to the intelligent, then? There is no guarantee of that either. Their chances, at least, are better.

The reason lies in the scriptures. The senses are subtler than the body, the mind is subtler than the senses, and the intellect is subtler than the mind. Far beyond even the intellect is the **Atman** (the Self).

> **indriyāṇi parāṇyāhur indriyebhyaḥ paraṁ manaḥ**
> **manasas tu parā buddhir yo buddheḥ paratas tu saḥ**
> "The senses are said to be higher than the body; higher than the senses is the mind; higher than the mind is the intellect; and what lies beyond even the intellect is the Self."
> (Bhagavad Gita 3.42)

It follows that those who act from the level of the Atman should hold all the wealth in the world.

## Why, Then, Do Saints Have No Wealth?

The question comes naturally, and the answer is that they do have it. Whenever Adi Shankaracharya wished, he brought down a shower of gold through the **Kanakadhara Stotram** (a hymn to the goddess of wealth, whose name means "the stream of gold").

The only difference is that saints remain **anasakta** (unattached). Whenever they choose, they can walk away from wealth just as easily.

## Who Is the Poorest of All?

Many of today's wealthiest people may hold great fortunes, yet they cannot let go of them. That is why they count as the poorest of all. As Acharya Ji puts it, the poorest man is the one who has nothing but money.

*Could I let go of what I earn if the need ever arose?*

## The Six Forms of Wealth

Wealth means far more than money. True wealth includes all of these: property, respect, community, time, family and relationships, and health.

Imagine having money but no time for yourself or for others. Imagine having money while your relationships fall apart, or having money without respect, or having money after your health is gone. Such money brings no real benefit, because **Lakshmi** (the goddess of wealth and prosperity) **is simply not present there**.

*Which of these six forms of wealth is weakest in my life?*

## Key Takeaways

- Wealth comes through intelligence, not hard work alone, and the Atman stands even higher than the intellect.
- Saints hold the power to bring wealth, yet they never become attached to it.
- Those who cannot let go of their wealth are the poorest of all.
- True wealth means property, respect, community, time, family and health.
- Where there is only money and everything else has broken down, Lakshmi is absent.
