# Chapter: What Is True Wealth?

## The Illusion of Hard Work and Wealth

There is a very widespread belief in our society that whoever works hard will gain wealth. Today I want to overturn this belief. Wealth does not come to the one who works hard; wealth comes to the one with intelligence.

> "Wealth does not come to the one who works hard; it comes to the one with intelligence."

To say that money comes to the one who works hard is a great falsehood. Remember, this will not sound pleasant, but this is the fact, this is the principle.

## The Level of Intellect and the Self

So does wealth certainly come to the intelligent? There is no certainty in that either, but at least the intelligent person has a greater chance. For the scriptures say that beyond the body are the senses, beyond the senses is the mind, beyond the mind is the intellect, and far beyond even the intellect is the Self (Atma).

> **indriyāṇi parāṇy āhur indriyebhyaḥ paraṁ manaḥ**
> **manasas tu parā buddhir yo buddheḥ paratas tu saḥ**
> "The senses are said to be superior; superior to the senses is the mind; superior to the mind is the intellect; and that which is beyond even the intellect is the Self."
> (Bhagavad Gita 3.42)

So one who works at the level of the Self should have the wealth of the whole world.

## Why Do Saints Not Seem to Have Wealth?

You will ask: saints and sages work at the level of the Self, so why do they not have wealth? They do have it. Adi Shankaracharya, through the Kanakadhara Stotram, caused a shower of gold whenever he wished.

The only difference is that they are not attached to wealth. Whenever they wish, they can let it go as well.

## Who Is the Poorest?

The richest person you see today perhaps has wealth, but will not be able to let it go. And that is why he is called the poorest. In truth it is said:

> "The poorest man is the one who has only money."

## The Real Meaning of Wealth

Because wealth does not mean only money. What have we learned? Wealth includes all of these:

- **Property**
- **Respect**
- **Society**
- **Time**
- **Wife** (family and relationships)
- **Health**

Whoever has all of this is truly wealthy. If someone has money but no time for himself or for others; has money, but all his relationships have broken; has money, but no respect; has money, but no health left, then that money is of no benefit. **Lakshmi is not there at all.**
