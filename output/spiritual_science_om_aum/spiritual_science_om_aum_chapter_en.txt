# Chapter: The Spiritual Science of Om

## Why Om?

Why did our Sanatan Gurus say "Om" and nothing else? Is this making sense to you? In place of Om, they could have said Som, Bom, Tom or Chom, anything at all. So why did they call it Om?

The answer is just as I explained earlier: it is between one end and the other that dharma flows, that all the momentum of life flows. The same is true of syllables. Syllables are connected to our mouth, you will agree with that.

## The Three States of the Mouth

The mouth can be in only three states. First, fully open. Second, fully closed. And third, half open, the middle state, in which the lips come close together, as happens when saying "O".

Our shastra, which is itself a science, and those who know sound therapy too, say that if the mouth is kept closed, the most sound that can come out is **"M"**. Nothing beyond that will come. Try it yourself: close your mouth properly and try to speak. Only "M" will come out. The vibration of "M" is what arises there.

When you speak with the mouth open, the sound **"A"** is produced. And when you half close the mouth, you have to say **"U"**. "U" cannot be said with an open mouth, nor with a closed mouth. So the sound of the middle state is "U".

## A, U and M

In this way we get three sounds: A, U and M. Joining the first two gives "O", and to say "O" you have to bring the mouth into that same middle state.

But this is symbolic. It is not about how you pronounce it. The point is that **all the words of the entire world are contained within it.** This is what the rishis want to say. The true meaning of A, U, M is: **that Brahman.**

## Every Word Is Brahman

If I gave you two abuses, I gave them with that same mouth, didn't I? What I am abusing you for, what I am fighting about, is another question. But that word too is a form of the same Brahman, and it too will remain within these A, U, M. Is that much clear?

## The Vision of the Rishis

About all the syllables there are, all the words there are, I am telling you the thinking of our rishis and munis. I am not adding anything new of my own. It may be new to you, you may not have heard it before, but do your own research.

I am saying exactly what our rishis and munis teach: that all sounds, whether you can draw a meaning from them or not, were all Brahman. All of it was truly **Omkar**. Is that much clear?
