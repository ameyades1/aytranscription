*Why did the rishis choose Om?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that the mouth has three positions: open gives "A", half open "U", closed "M". Every word, even an insult, falls within A, U and M.

💭 _When you chant Om, will you notice your mouth pass through all three positions?_
