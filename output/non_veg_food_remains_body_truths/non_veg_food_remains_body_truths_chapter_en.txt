# Chapter: Non-Vegetarian Food and the Truth of the Body

## Look at Your Own Digestive Structure

If you look at it a little from a medical point of view, open up and examine your small intestine, look at the structure of the whole digestive process that goes on inside, you will find that the human digestive system is very long. By contrast, the digestive system of meat-eating animals is extremely short, very small. Research will tell you this, and medical science knows it too.

Why would that be so? Because meat must leave their body quickly. There is a great deal of waste in it.

## Food That Rots Inside the Body

There is a difference between vegetarian and non-vegetarian food. The stomach holds non-vegetarian food for a longer time. Our intestines are coiled, so if that food stays inside for so long, it will ferment within. Because it is already a dead thing, its decomposition has already begun. If it stays inside the body for so long, fermentation and decomposition will take place, and disease will come.

That is why even what a person ate ten years ago keeps accumulating in the intestines. Some of it starts fermenting right there; not everything comes out. Gradually its layers keep growing and the passage of the intestines keeps narrowing.

## The Example of Two Pipes

Have you seen the pipe under a wash basin? It is often white and corrugated, with rings along it. And there is another pipe that is completely smooth, like an ordinary garden hose for watering.

Through the pipe that is perfectly smooth, oil, ghee and grease will all pass out at once, won't they? And if the pipe is corrugated, what will happen? Everything will get stuck.

> "Who would have a smooth one? The one whose food has to pass out at once will have a smooth one."

Vegetarian creatures are built one way and meat-eating creatures another. The smooth passage belongs to the one through which food must leave immediately. Humans are not like that. Our structure is made for absorption; we have to draw a great deal of essence and nourishment from food. So our structure is different. The structure of meat-eating animals is smooth, so that nothing much gets stuck and everything passes straight out.

## The Lion Does Not Fall Ill, So Why Do Humans?

However many deer a lion eats, it will never fall ill. But you will.

Has this example become clear? What kind of pipe do we have? A corrugated pipe, with everything arranged to extract all it can. Theirs is made to pass things out quickly: what is needed stays, and everything else is thrown out.
