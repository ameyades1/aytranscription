*Is the human body built to eat meat?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that our long, ridged intestine is made to absorb nourishment, while a carnivore's short, smooth tract expels it fast. In us, meat lingers, ferments and brings disease.

💭 _When you choose a meal, will you remember how your body is made?_
