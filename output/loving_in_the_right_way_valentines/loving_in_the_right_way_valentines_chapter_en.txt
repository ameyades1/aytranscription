# Chapter: Express Love in Action

## Knowledge Until Love, Devotion After It

Until love happens, Saraswati is needed, that is, knowledge and discernment. But if Saraswati alone keeps being used even after love has happened, it will lead to downfall. Is that much clear?

This does not mean you should not take in new information or new knowledge. The problem comes when it is overused. Understand everything once: what love is and whom you are to love. Then love has happened.

## Love Is Not Only in the Heart and in Words

In love, one has to bring gifts. Which gift to bring is a whole new art, and that is all fine. But gifts have to be brought. Is all this needed or not?

Someone says, "I love you, but I will not make you tea." And he too says, "I will do everything, but I will not do any housework." Is that love?

> "Love has to be expressed, not only in the heart, not only in words."

Saying "I love you", sending messages, bringing cards: all this is the work of speech, the work of Saraswati. That is done. Now **show it in action**.

## The Gifts of Shri Krishna

Shri Krishna gave Radha countless things as gifts. He made whole forests for her, made lakes for her. He gave grand and divine gifts, as if saying, "My position is that of Dwarkadhish, so I give you gifts befitting it."

So someone says that even a poor person should give that much according to their means. Sometimes give a ring, give something. Love should be expressed in that. In words alone it always sounds nice.

## Offering a Shoulder Is Not Enough

Offering a shoulder once is not called love. If you only come at the very end to offer your shoulder and nothing else comes, that much alone is not enough of an offering. Along with the shoulder, you actually have to give something. Now and then you have to give clothes.

This applies even to your own wife; this is not about giving to someone else. "She will bring her own things" or "She is my wife, I gave her money, go and get it yourself": that does not work. Sometimes you have to go along, and do what is necessary.
