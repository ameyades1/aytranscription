# Love That Shows in Action

Love has happened, so what next? Is saying "I love you" enough? In this short discourse, Acharya ji says something very direct: love has to be shown in **kriti** (action).

## Saraswati Before Love, and After?

**Saraswati** means the power of knowledge, discernment and speech. It is needed until love happens: to understand what love is and whom to love.

But if only Saraswati keeps being used even after love has happened, it leads to downfall.

This does not mean you stop taking in new knowledge. The problem lies in its **overuse**. Once you have understood and love has happened, things must move forward.

## Is This Really Love?

Acharya ji gives an everyday example. Someone says, "I love you, but I will not make you tea." The other says, "I will do everything, but I will not do housework."

So is this love?

> "Love has to be expressed, not only in the heart, not only in words."

Saying "I love you", sending messages, bringing cards: all this is the work of speech, the work of Saraswati. That is done. Now show it in action.

*How much of your love today is in words, and how much shows in your actions?*

## How Shri Krishna Expressed Love

Shri Krishna gave Radha countless gifts. He made whole forests for her, made lakes for her. Grand and divine gifts, as if saying, "My position is that of Dwarkadhish, so I give gifts befitting it."

Not everyone is Dwarkadhish. But even a poor person can give according to their means. Sometimes a ring, sometimes something else.

> "Show it in action."

## Merely Offering a Shoulder Is Not Love

Offering a shoulder once is not called love. Just showing up at the very end to offer your shoulder is not enough. Now and then you have to give something, sometimes clothes, sometimes a gift.

And this applies to your own wife too. "She is my wife, I gave her money, go get it yourself": that attitude does not work. Sometimes you have to go along, and do what is necessary.

*When did you last spend time with your loved one, going beyond just money or words?*

## Key Takeaways

- Knowledge (Saraswati) is needed until love happens; overusing it after love can bring downfall.
- Love must be expressed not only in the heart or in words, but in action.
- Shri Krishna gave Radha grand gifts; we too should give according to our means.
- Being present only at the very end is not love; giving now and then and going along matter.
- This applies just as much to your own spouse.
