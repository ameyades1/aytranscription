# Love That Shows in Action

You have fallen in love. What comes next? Is saying "I love you" enough? In this short talk, Acharya Ji makes a very plain point: love has to show itself in **kriti** (action).

## Saraswati Before Love, and After?

**Saraswati** (the goddess of knowledge, discernment and speech) stands for the power to understand and to express. You need her until love arrives, to grasp what love is and whom to love.

Once love has come, though, if you keep relying on Saraswati alone, she leads you downhill.

This does not mean you should stop learning. The trouble lies in **overusing** her. Once you have understood and the love is there, the relationship has to move forward.

## Is This Really Love?

Acharya Ji gives an everyday example. One partner says, "I love you, but I won't make you tea." The other says, "I'll do anything for you, except housework."

Can you call that love?

Love has to be expressed, Acharya Ji says, and not only in the heart or only in words.

Saying "I love you", sending messages, bringing a card: all of this is the work of speech, the work of Saraswati. That part is done. Now show it in what you do.

*How much of your love today lives in words, and how much can people see in your actions?*

## How Shri Krishna Expressed His Love

Shri Krishna gave Radha countless gifts. He created whole forests for her and built lakes for her. These were grand, divine gifts, as if he were saying, "I am the **Dwarkadhish** (the King of Dwarka), so I give gifts worthy of that position."

Not everyone is a king. Even a poor man can give within his means, though: a ring one day, something else another. Love needs to show up in action.

## Showing Up Only at the End Is Not Love

In India, to "give your shoulder" means helping carry someone's bier to the cremation ground. Offering your shoulder once is not love. Turning up only at the very end is not enough. Along the way you have to give something, sometimes clothes, sometimes a gift.

The same holds true for your wife. The attitude "She's my wife; I've given her the money, she can go and buy it herself" does not work. Sometimes you have to go with her and do whatever the moment needs.

*When did you last spend real time with someone you love, going beyond money or words?*

## Key Takeaways

- Knowledge (Saraswati) is essential until love arrives; overusing it afterwards can lead to a downfall.
- Love must show itself in action, not only in the heart or in words.
- Shri Krishna gave Radha grand gifts; we too can give within our means.
- Being there only at the end is not love; giving and showing up along the way matter.
- This applies just as much to your life partner.
