*Why does Ganesh Chaturthi begin on the fourth day?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that consciousness has four states: waking, dreaming, deep sleep and the fourth, Turiya. There discernment awakens, and discernment is Lord Ganesha.

💭 _Have you noticed that the same "I" is present in both dreaming and waking? Who is that?_
