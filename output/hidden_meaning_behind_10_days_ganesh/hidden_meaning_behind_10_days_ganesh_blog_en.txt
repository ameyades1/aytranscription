# The Hidden Meaning of Ganesh Chaturthi: The Turiya State and the Birth of Discernment

Every year Ganapati arrives on Chaturthi and departs on Anant Chaturdashi. We celebrate, we make modaks, we perform the immersion.

But has the question ever arisen in your mind: why does this festival begin on Chaturthi? This discourse answers that question, and the answer lies not in the calendar but in our own consciousness.

## Why Chaturthi, Why Not Pratipada?

Why not from the first of the month to the tenth? Why not from Pratipada to Dashami? Why not from Ashtami, or past the full moon to Tritiya?

The answer is simple: **Chaturthi is not important because of the calendar.** There are four states of consciousness, four steps. Chaturthi points to that fourth step.

## The First State: The Waking World

The first is **jagrat avastha** (the waking state). The world we experience with open eyes, through the body, is the waking state.

This is the world we consider "real". But is it truly so real?

## The Second State: The World of Dreams

When the eyes close, another world opens: **svapna avastha** (the dream state). The body is doing nothing, but the mind is at work, and the intellect too is working a little.

You say, "I was working, I don't know when I dozed off, and suddenly I was in a forest." Notice this: **the "I" was present in both places.** The body was in bed, and yet the "I" was in the forest.

On waking you say, "A lion was chasing me, I was frightened in my dream." This means I am not this body. I was someone else, who was watching. I was the **sakshi** (witness).

Acharya Ji explains that you are neither the one in the dream nor the one who is awake. You are the seer of both; you are that Self.

The day you know this, life will change. And the coming of this understanding is what we call Ganapati.

*Have you ever noticed that the "I" remains the same in both dream and waking? Who is that?*

## The Third State: A Town Without Light

The third is **sushupti avastha** (the deep sleep state). No mind, no intellect, no awareness of the body. Total darkness, not a single thought.

There is no pain there, no pleasure, no sorrow. Whether it is someone with the biggest income tax problem, or a thief locked in jail, in deep sleep or under anaesthesia, all troubles vanish for that while.

But there is one thing: **here there is no realisation of the Supreme either.** It is a town where the power has gone out. There is peace, but no light.

## Three Cities, and One Witness

These three states are called **pur**, three cities: waking, dream and deep sleep.

Now see a subtle point. Waking from deep sleep you say, "I slept like a log for six hours, I didn't know a thing." The one who "didn't know a thing" still remembers that he didn't know a thing! Whose memory is this?

That is the pointer: **I am not these three states; I am the witness of all three.**

## The Fourth State: Turiya

When you rise above these three, that state is called **Turiya** (the Fourth State).

But Turiya is not a separate room you have to enter. To explain it, Gurudev gives a beautiful example: *sona*. Not *sona* as in sleep, but *sona* as in gold.

## The Example of Gold

Make an earring from gold, a nose ring, a neck chain, or a bangle. Each has a different shape, each a different name. We have given each a **nama-rupa** (name and form).

But melt all four, and what remains? Only gold.

Now if someone says, "You keep this earring's name and shape, give me the gold," is that possible? Not at all. Because right up to the last edge of the shape, it is gold. Wherever you touch, it is gold.

Acharya Ji explains that the Turiya state runs in and through all three states, and that is the real you.

The three states seem separate, but one essence runs through them inside and out. That is why you see the waking world, you see the dream, and it is you who experiences deep sleep. The one who experiences is the Self, and that is you.

The day this is understood, there will be realisation, and that very day your liberation is certain.

*Even if there is no direct experience today, is something becoming clear? Can you see yourself as the one who passes through these three states?*

## Chaturyam: The Birth of Discernment

This fourth state is also called **Chaturyam**. Not "chatur" (clever), but Chaturyam. From it came the word Turiya.

In this state the real intelligence is found: **viveka buddhi** (discernment). Discernment is what can see all three states clearly, is not bound by any of them, and can make the right decision, because it looks at the world as a witness.

And that is Ganesh. **Discernment is Ganesh.**

That is why the birth of Ganesh is held to be on Chaturthi. Ganesh being born on Chaturthi means the awakening of your discerning intellect, which is found in the Turiya state.

## Anant Chaturdashi: The Journey to Turiyatita

Then, gradually, one has to move towards the **Turiyatita** (beyond Turiya) state. The ten days from Chaturthi to Anant Chaturdashi are ten steps: **the five organs of knowledge and the five organs of action.** By Anant Chaturdashi these are crossed.

## Not a Holiday, but a Sadhana

India celebrates the most festivals, and has the most holidays in a year. But Gurudev reminds us: **this is not merely a vacation.** The excerpt ends with the question of whom the real holiday is meant for.

## Key Takeaways

- The significance of Ganesh Chaturthi comes not from the calendar but from the fourth state of consciousness.
- Waking, dream and deep sleep are three cities; I am none of them, I am their witness.
- Turiya is not a separate state; just as gold is in every ornament, Turiya runs through all three states, and that is the real "you".
- In Turiya the discerning intellect awakens, and discernment is Ganesh; that is why his birth is on Chaturthi.
- The ten days from Chaturthi to Anant Chaturdashi are a journey of crossing the five organs of knowledge and the five organs of action towards Turiyatita.
