# The Hidden Meaning of Ganesh Chaturthi: Turiya and the Birth of Discernment

Every year, Lord Ganesha arrives on **Chaturthi** (the fourth day of the lunar fortnight) and departs on **Anant Chaturdashi** (the fourteenth day, when the festival ends). We celebrate, we make **modaks** (sweet dumplings offered to Lord Ganesha), and we carry out the **visarjan** (the immersion of the idol in water).

But have you ever asked yourself why the festival begins on Chaturthi? This discourse answers that question, and the answer lies not in the almanac but in our own consciousness.

## Why Chaturthi and Not the First Day?

Why not from the first of the month to the tenth? Why not from **Pratipada** (the first lunar day) to **Dashami** (the tenth)? Why not from **Ashtami** (the eighth), or past **Purnima** (the full moon) to **Tritiya** (the third)?

The answer is simple: **the almanac is not what makes Chaturthi important.** Consciousness has four states, four steps. Chaturthi points to that fourth step.

## The First State: The Waking World

The first is the **jagrat** (waking) state. When you open your eyes and experience the world through the body, you are in the waking state.

This is the world we consider "real." But is it truly as real as we believe?

## The Second State: The World of Dreams

When your eyes close, another world opens up: the **svapna** (dream) state. The body is doing nothing, yet the mind is at work, and even the intellect is partly active.

You say, "I was working, I don't know when I dozed off, and suddenly I was in a forest." Notice this: **"I" was present in both places.** The body lay in bed, and still "I" was in the forest.

When you wake, you say, "A lion was chasing me, and I got scared in the dream." This means I am not this body. I was someone else, someone who was watching. I was the **sakshi** (witness).

Acharya Ji explains that you are neither the one who dreams nor the one who is awake. You are the seer of both. You are that essence, the **Atman** (the Self).

The day you know this, your life will change. The dawning of this understanding is what we call Ganapati.

*Have you ever noticed that the same "I" remains in both dreaming and waking? Who is that?*

## The Third State: A City Without Light

The third is the **sushupti** (deep sleep) state. Here there is no awareness of mind, intellect or body. There is complete darkness and not a single thought.

There is no pain there, no happiness and no suffering. Whether you face the biggest income tax problem imaginable or sit locked in prison as a thief, in deep sleep or under anaesthesia every trouble vanishes for as long as it lasts.

There is a catch, though: **you have no awareness of God here either.** Deep sleep is like a city where the power has gone out. It is peaceful, but there is no light.

## Three Cities and One Witness

These three states are called **pura** (cities): waking, dreaming and deep sleep.

Now look at a subtle point. Waking from deep sleep, you say, "I slept like a log for six hours and knew nothing." Yet the one who "knew nothing" remembers knowing nothing! Whose memory is that?

That is the clue: **I am not these three states. I am the witness of all three.**

## The Fourth State: Turiya

When you rise above all three, you reach the state called **Turiya** (the fourth state of consciousness).

But Turiya is not a separate room you have to enter. To make this clear, Acharya Ji offers a lovely example: gold.

## The Parable of Gold

Use gold to make an earring, a nose ring, a necklace or a bangle. Each has its own shape and its own name. We have given each one a **nama-rupa** (name and form).

Now melt all four. What remains? Gold, and nothing else.

Suppose someone says, "You keep the name and shape of this earring, and give me the gold." Is that possible? Not at all, because right up to the very edge of its shape, the earring is gold. Wherever you touch it, you touch gold.

Acharya Ji explains that the Turiya state pervades all three states, and that Turiya is the real you.

The three states seem separate, yet one essence fills them, inside and out. That is why you are the one who sees the waking world, the one who sees the dream, and the one who experiences deep sleep. The one who experiences all this is the Atman, and that is you.

The day this sinks in, you have a direct realisation, and on that very day your **moksha** (liberation) is assured.

*You may not have the experience yet, but is something becoming clear? Can you see yourself as the one who passes through all three states?*

## Chaturyam: The Birth of Discernment

This fourth state is also called **Chaturyam**. Not "chatur" (clever), but Chaturyam. The word Turiya comes from it.

In this state you gain true intelligence: **viveka** (discernment). Discernment can see all three states clearly without getting bound to any of them, and it can make the right decision because it views the world as a witness.

And this is Lord Ganesha. **Discernment itself is Lord Ganesha.**

That is why tradition holds that Lord Ganesha was born on Chaturthi. His birth on Chaturthi means the awakening of your discernment, which you gain in the Turiya state.

## Anant Chaturdashi: The Journey Beyond Turiya

From there, you move step by step towards **Turiyatita** (the state beyond Turiya). The ten days from Chaturthi to Anant Chaturdashi are ten steps: the **jnanendriyas** (the five senses of perception) and the **karmendriyas** (the five organs of action). By Anant Chaturdashi, you have gone beyond them all.

## Not a Holiday but a Practice

India celebrates more festivals than any other country, and it also has the most holidays in the year. Yet Acharya Ji reminds us: **this is not merely a break from work.** The section closes with a question: who is a real holiday truly meant for?

## Key Takeaways

- Ganesh Chaturthi matters because of the fourth state of consciousness, not because of the almanac.
- Waking, dreaming and deep sleep are three cities; I am none of them, I am their witness.
- Turiya is not a separate state; just as gold is present in every ornament, Turiya pervades all three states, and it is the real "you."
- Discernment awakens in Turiya, and discernment is Lord Ganesha; that is why his birth falls on Chaturthi.
- The ten days from Chaturthi to Anant Chaturdashi are a journey past the five senses of perception and the five organs of action towards Turiyatita.
