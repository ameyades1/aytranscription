# Chapter: The Secret of Chaturthi: The Turiya State and the Birth of Discernment

## Why Chaturthi?

The Ganesh festival begins on Chaturthi and continues until Anant Chaturdashi. But have you ever wondered, why Chaturthi? Why not from the first day of the month to the tenth? Why not from Pratipada to Dashami? Why not from the first day to the last? Why not from Ashtami in the middle, or past the full moon and back to Tritiya? Why not up to the eighteenth? Why not from Dashami to Panchami?

What is so special about Chaturthi? Chaturthi is not important because of the calendar. Its secret lies elsewhere. There are four states of consciousness, four steps.

## The First State: The Waking State

The first state is your waking state, *jagrat avastha*. This world that we experience with open eyes, through the body, is called the waking state.

## The Second State: The Dream State

We have another world, one that opens when we close our eyes. In that world the body is not doing anything, but the mind certainly is at work, and the intellect is working a little too. When I dream, do I not go somewhere else in the dream?

You say, don't you, "I was working away nicely, I don't know when I dozed off, and suddenly I was in a forest." But does this "I" remain continuous or not? I was somewhere; I was here, and I was there too. This much makes it clear that I am not this living body. Otherwise, after waking, you would not say, "I was frightened in my dream."

So I was someone else; this much you understand. I was the witness, the *sakshi*. I was watching the play of my waking state. Then I myself go into another world to watch. There this physical body is not present, yet I am there.

And it is I who wake up and return from there as well. "I was fine until now, I don't know when I dozed off, and a lion was chasing me." Even after waking, he says this.

This means that the waking state you were watching is not true. And what you saw in the dream also disappears once you wake up. You are not the one in the dream, and you are not the one who is awake. **You are the seer of both; you are that Self, the *atma tattva*.** The day you know this, your life will change. The coming of this understanding is what we call Ganapati. I am coming to that state.

## The Third State: Deep Sleep, a Town Without Light

The third state is *sushupti*, deep sleep. In it the mind and intellect do nothing at all, there is no awareness of the body, we have forgotten everything. Total darkness, not a single thought. And yet, the Supreme has not been realised either.

It is a town where the power has gone out. It is dark; there is nothing. There is no pain, no pleasure, no sorrow, no suffering.

However big someone's income tax problem may be, or even if he is a thief locked up in jail, when he falls into deep sleep, or is put to sleep with anaesthesia, with chloroform, do all his sorrows and troubles not vanish for that much time? However big the tax problem, once he is in deep sleep he forgets it all. But the Supreme Reality has still not awakened. This is a town without lights.

## The Three Cities and Their Witness

So there are three cities. These three are called **pur**. *Pur* means a town or village. The first is the waking state, the second the dream state, the third the deep sleep state.

But notice: when you wake from deep sleep, you immediately say, "Oh, I slept like a log for six hours, I didn't know a thing." "I didn't know anything": this sentence comes. And "for six hours I did nothing": this too he remembers. There is memory. Otherwise he would not know of that period in between at all.

So the basic point is this: **I am not these three states; I am the witness of these three states.**

## The Fourth State: Turiya

When you rise above these three states, that fourth state is called the **Turiya state**. For those hearing this word for the first time, I repeat it: its name is Turiya.

## The Example of Gold

Understanding the Turiya state is something like *sona*. Not *sona* meaning sleeping, but *sona* meaning gold.

From gold you can make an earring, a nose ring, a neck chain. Are their forms different or not? The earring has a different shape, the nose ring a different shape, the chain another, and if you make a bangle, its shape is different too. And each has its own name as well: this is for the ear, this for the nose, this for the neck, this is a bangle. We have given each of them a name and a form.

The names and forms became different, but essentially, are they not all gold? If I melt all four, only gold remains. Everything is gold.

Now if I say to you, "Brother, keep the name and shape of this earring with you, and give me the gold," is that possible? Keep the earring's name, keep the earring's shape, and hand me the gold. It is not possible. Why? Because right up to the border of the shape, it is gold. Wherever I touch, it is gold.

So in truth the fourth state is not a separate state. In and through all three states there is only that gold. Call it an earring, a nose ring, a neck chain, a bangle, convert it into anything; basically it is the same gold, only the name and form differ. Only name and form are changing, but the thing is exactly the same.

## Turiya Is the Real "You"

In the same way, your dream state, waking state and deep sleep state seem to you to be three separate states. In reality the fourth is nothing separate from them. **The Turiya state runs in and through all three states, and that is the real you.**

That is why you can see this world, you can see the dream, and it is you who experiences deep sleep as well. The one who experiences is the Self, and that is you.

> "The day this is understood, that day there will be realisation, and that very day your liberation is certain."

Have you understood this? Today the direct experience will not come, but something is being understood. The day it is truly understood, "I am not these three; I am the one who passes through all three," that fourth state is called **Chaturyam**. I am not saying "chatur" (clever); I am saying "Chaturyam". From it came the word Turiya. The Turiya state is the fourth step.

## Discernment Is Ganesh

In this state the real intelligence is attained: **viveka buddhi**, the discerning intellect.

> "Discernment is nothing but Ganesh."

That which can see all three states clearly, is not bound by any of them, and can make the right decision, because it sees this world as a witness, that is discernment.

That is why the birth of Ganesh is said to be on Chaturthi. To be born on Chaturthi means the awakening of your discerning intellect, which is attained in the Turiya state.

## Anant Chaturdashi: Towards Turiyatita

Then, gradually, one has to go into the Turiyatita state. When Anant Chaturdashi comes, the ten steps in between, of which we spoke, will be crossed: the five organs of knowledge and the five organs of action.
