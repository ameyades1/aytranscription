# Chapter: How Big Is Pitru Dosh? The Depth of Pitru Rin

## Who Are the Pitars?

First, understand this question: who are the *pitars*, the ancestors? And if we have once performed the rite of *pitru mukti*, the liberation of the ancestors, should it be done for one year only, or every year? This next question will surely come.

What do logical people say? "Oh, the ancestors are common to everyone. Once I freed them, I freed them, and the matter is over. They became liberated, so what is my problem now? Why should I do it every year?" Will they say this or not? Yes, their logic is correct in its own place. If they were liberated, they were liberated for the whole family. When grandfather went, he went for everyone. So it should be done only once.

But then why does scripture tell us to do it every year? Were the people who composed the scriptures mad? No. The point is that we have not understood, because we do not even know where all the ancestors are seated, and how new ancestors keep being formed. When this is understood, pitru dosh will be understood: how big this **pitru dosh** and **pitru rin** really are. We will learn about both, the *dosh* (affliction) and the *rin* (debt), in the shivir.

## Pitru Dosh, and Pitru Labh Too

We will also explain how *pitru labh*, ancestral benefit, comes about. Just as there is pitru dosh, there is also pitru labh. Can you leave two crores in the bank for your children just like that, or not? In the same way, if our ancestors did some good deeds before they left, there can be benefit from it; and there can also be suffering because of our ancestors.

## Rule One: Your Progress Is in Your Hands

First understand that this is the current birth, and this is you. So you will have some karma of your own, won't you? Does everything depend on nature alone? Along with nature, I have also spoken of the nature within you, haven't I? So there will also be the matter of your own karma. You yourself will also be responsible for your own life.

So the first thing, rule number one: **your progress is in your own hands.** The second will be about the ancestors.

## Ninety-Eight Percent in Kali Yuga

In fact this happens to everybody. Some people repay this debt. For some people, their ancestors left no such burden at all that their descendants would have to do anything. But usually, since this is Kali Yuga, about ninety-eight percent of people suffer from pitru dosh, but they do not even know it. I am not giving an exact figure, it may be more or less, but almost ninety-eight percent of people suffer, and they have no understanding of it.

Because in Kali Yuga the rate of liberation is simply not that high. The world's population keeps on increasing, which means people are not getting liberated, and animals too, birth after birth, are coming up to the human form.

## The Tree of Seven Generations

So this is you, in the current birth. One generation above are your parents; they too are called pitars. You will have to bear their karma as well. Why you will have to bear it, why we have to suffer their karmas, come to the shivir to understand that, not today. Do they curse us, is there a curse from them or not, or are they themselves cursed: all this we will learn in the shivir.

So first the parents; you will have to bear theirs too. Then come your grandparents. Then there will be both sides, the mother's side and the father's side. After that the great-grandfathers, one, two, three generations back; then above them, one more, one more, one more: up to seven generations. Can you see this picture or not? Even if you grasp the idea of the picture, that is plenty. **Some karma or other of the ancestors up to seven generations back will come upon you.**

## Names Forgotten, Yet They Were There

Many of them you would not even know by name. Do you know your parents' names? All of you will remember; there is no need even to ask, unless someone is an orphan. Many people will perhaps know their grandparents' names. Do you know your great-grandfather's name? His grandfather's name? Do you remember the names of those above him?

So does not knowing the name mean they did not exist? Just because you don't know doesn't mean they were not there. Did they exist or not? You cannot see them, you do not understand, you do not remember, but they were there. And because they were there, you are here. Or did great-grandfather come about automatically? Just as you know your father, and he knew his father, there must be a tree above, mustn't there? That we have forgotten does not mean they were not there. In fact, they were, and that is why we are.

Why we have to bear their karmas too, a whole shivir will definitely go into that, because it is simply not possible to explain it in one day, in one hour.

## The Branches Keep Growing

But do you think that is all there is? The *namavali*, the list of names we bring at the shraddha, has one, two, three, four names; and those you have just spoken from the father's side. So in the same way there will be names from the mother's side too, won't there? There will. Then here there were two divisions, weren't there? From there, two divisions too? Then from there more branches will keep growing, won't they? And by the time it comes down to these, there will be branches here as well, won't there?

So think how many ancestors you may be indebted to! First I want to make you feel this much: you do not even know what lies further and further back. We have to go seven, seven generations up.

## Ancestors of Past Births

After that, one more point. Right now you have been born as something, Hindu, Muslim, Christian, haven't you? People of all religions come to this shivir, because death is common to all, so the concept will be common too. Right now, in this very shivir, there are Christians, there are Muslims, there are Hindus, and Jains are sitting here too.

So can you say that in your previous birth too you were a Muslim, or a Christian, or a Hindu? You must have been something in your previous birth, that much is certain. You have come from somewhere.

Scripture says that in a previous birth you could have been a Salman, a Wasim, a Kasim, anything, couldn't you? Then in that birth, could you not also have had ancestors with names like Salma, Abdul Kadir, Waqar, Ayesha? Think and tell me. And could their later generations be alive somewhere today or not?

In your previous birth you died; did that tree end? Just as your grandfather and great-grandfather passed away, yet you are still here below, aren't you? In the same way, in your previous birth you were someone; you died in it, but that tree must still be alive somewhere, one you would not even recognise today. But were there ancestors there too or not? There were. Were they your own ancestors or not? They were. So was their debt not upon you as well?

Because the *sanskaras* of seven births remain within you, don't they?

> "We carry everything of seven births with us."

This much you have surely heard.

## How Deep This Problem Is

So I want to make you realise how deep this problem is. It is not that easy. All of these are yours too. But in this birth we are so caught in *maya* that we do not understand "this one too was me, and that one too was me." Who comes under whom: all these questions I will explain in your regular shivir, don't worry.

But this and that were your own journey; do you understand that or not? This horizontal journey, the spread of generations within one birth, is also yours, isn't it? Then the vertical journey, the chain of birth after birth, will also be yours, won't it? So the karmas from there must have come into the sanskaras of this very body, this final mind, mustn't they? The sanskara is yours, after all. And birth happens carrying that very dosh, doesn't it? So **there is dosh, and there is rin.**

Your continuation spans seven births. So in some birth you were a Michael, a Pinto, a Gracy, or you could have been a George, couldn't you? It is not necessary that I am speaking only of Christians, Muslims or Jains. It may be that all seven births were Hindu, or all seven were Muslim. This example is only to make you understand the intensity that scripture describes.

## Where This Knowledge Comes From

And I do not speak a single word of my own. Only the words of the scriptures are being told, explained, and made to be felt. This is not found in any book. It is seen on going into the state of meditation, the liberated state, the state of samadhi, and it is given to you in the form of a diagram through the *gyan chakshu*, the eye of wisdom.

## The Burden of Forty-Nine

In some other birth there may be others too. All these are pitars. And what did we say? Here too there are small branches: the fourth birth, the fifth birth, the sixth birth, the seventh birth. Seven generations times seven births: **every person keeps carrying on their head the negative karma of forty-nine times the ancestors.**

You have seen this diagram. A little time has been kept for it so that you realise its vastness. And even this diagram is incomplete; its branches will go a little further still. But where do we have the time to understand this? And if we do not understand, how will we know?

So all these many are your own ancestors. Do you understand? **So it is you who has to liberate them.**
