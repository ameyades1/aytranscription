# How Deep Pitru Dosh Runs When Your Ancestors Span Seven Births

## Isn't One Shraddha Enough?

Many people think that once *pitru mukti* (the rite for the liberation of the ancestors) is done, the matter is over. The logic is simple: "Once I freed them, I freed them. They are liberated, so why should I do it every year?"

Gurudev does not dismiss this logic. He says yes, it is correct in its own place. If grandfather was liberated, he was liberated for the whole family.

But then why does scripture tell us to do it every year? Were those who wrote the scriptures foolish? No. The real point is that **we do not even know where all the ancestors are seated, and how new ancestors keep being formed.**

## Pitru Dosh and Pitru Labh: Both Are Real

**Pitru dosh** (the burden that comes from the ancestors' karmas) and **pitru rin** (our debt to the ancestors) are two different things. And alongside them there is a third: **pitru labh** (ancestral benefit).

Gurudev gives a simple example. Just as someone can leave two crores in the bank for their children, if the ancestors did good deeds, their benefit also comes to us. And their karmas can bring suffering too.

## Rule One: Your Progress Is in Your Hands

Not everything can be laid on the ancestors. You are in this current birth, and you have your own karmas as well. You are responsible for yourself.

So **rule number one: your progress is in your own hands.** The ancestors come after that.

## Ninety-Eight Percent, and No One Knows

Some people repay this debt. Some people's ancestors left no such burden at all. But Gurudev says that in Kali Yuga about ninety-eight percent of people suffer from pitru dosh, and do not even know it.

He gives a sign of this too: in Kali Yuga the rate of liberation is very low. The world's population keeps increasing, meaning people are not getting liberated, and animals too, birth after birth, are coming up to the human form.

*Have you ever wondered whether some of the knots in your life may not be yours alone?*

## The Tree of Seven Generations

Now picture a diagram. At the very bottom is you. One generation above are your parents; they too are pitars, and you have to bear their karma as well. Then your grandparents, on both sides: the mother's side and the father's side. Then the great-grandparents, and above them, and further above: **up to seven generations.**

> "Some karma or other of the ancestors up to seven generations back will come upon you."

Why do we have to bear these karmas? Do they curse us, or are they themselves cursed? Gurudev says this subject is so vast that it is explained not in an hour but over an entire shivir.

## Names Forgotten, So They Never Existed?

Everyone remembers their parents' names. Many remember their grandparents'. Your great-grandfather's? His grandfather's? Beyond that?

> "Just because you don't know doesn't mean they were not there."

They were, and that is why you are. Great-grandfather did not just appear on his own. There is a whole tree above us; we have simply forgotten it.

And this tree is not a straight line. In the shraddha *namavali* (the list of ancestors' names) we write four or five names from the father's side. But there are names from the mother's side too. Two divisions at every generation, then more branches from those. Think how many ancestors you may be indebted to!

## Ancestors of Past Births Are Yours Too

Now it goes deeper. People of every religion come to Gurudev's shivir, Hindus, Muslims, Christians, Jains, because death is common to all, so this concept is common to all.

Can anyone say they were born into the same religion in their previous birth? Scripture says that in a past birth you could have been a Salman, a Wasim or a Kasim, with ancestors named Salma, Abdul Kadir, Waqar or Ayesha. In another birth you could have been a Michael, a Pinto, a Gracy or a George.

Gurudev makes it clear that this is only an example. All seven births may well have been in the same religion. The point is not religion; it is to convey the **intensity** of this truth.

You died in that birth, but that tree did not end. Its generations may still be alive somewhere today, people you would not even recognise. And the ancestors there were your ancestors too.

> "We carry everything of seven births with us."

## Horizontal and Vertical: One Journey

Gurudev explains this in two directions:

- **Horizontal journey** (*kshaitij pravas*): the spread of generations within one birth
- **Vertical journey** (*urdhva pravas*): the chain of birth after birth

Both are your own journey. All the karmas from there come into the **sanskaras** (deep impressions) of this final mind, and birth happens carrying that very dosh. That is why there is **dosh**, and there is **rin**.

But we are so caught in *maya* that we do not see that "that one too was me."

## A Burden Forty-Nine Times Over

Now look at the arithmetic. Seven generations, and seven births.

Every person keeps carrying on their head **the negative karma of forty-nine times the ancestors**. And Gurudev says even this diagram is incomplete; its branches go further still.

*If this tree of ancestors is so vast, can a single shraddha really be enough?*

## Where Does This Knowledge Come From?

Gurudev says he does not speak a single word of his own; all are the words of the scriptures. Yet this is not found in any book. It is seen in **the state of meditation, the liberated state and the state of samadhi**, and is given in the form of a diagram through the **gyan chakshu** (the inner divine eye of wisdom).

## It Is You Who Must Free Them

All these many are your own ancestors. And the responsibility to liberate them is also yours.

## Key Takeaways

- Pitru dosh, pitru rin and pitru labh are all real; the ancestors' good deeds bring benefit too
- Rule one: your progress is in your own hands
- In Kali Yuga about ninety-eight percent of people suffer from pitru dosh without knowing it
- The karma of ancestors up to seven generations, from both mother's and father's sides, comes upon us
- Not remembering their names does not mean they did not exist; they were, and that is why we are
- The ancestors of our past seven births are ours too, whatever religion they belonged to
- Seven generations times seven births: every person carries the karma of forty-nine times the ancestors
- That is why scripture asks for ancestral rites every year, and liberating them is our own responsibility
