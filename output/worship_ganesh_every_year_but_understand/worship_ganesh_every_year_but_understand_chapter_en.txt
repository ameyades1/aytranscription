# Chapter: Ganpati Puja Every Year, But Do We Understand It?

## A Festival of Faith

In every home, in some relative's home, or somewhere or other, Ganpati is seated. All right, even if not in your home, at least all of you believe in him, which is why you have come here today. You at least believe that there is someone called Ganpati Bappa. Very good.

So Ganpati is in the home, or in the family tradition; there is something. And everything that is customary, the thousandfold recitation (sahasravartan) of the Atharvashirsha and so on, everyone does. There must be daily worship too, and the ringing of the bell. Twenty-one blades of durva grass must have been offered. And everyone must surely have eaten a modak. Everything else may have been dropped, but this part will not have been missed, that much is certain. All I want to ask is: at least this much must have been done.

And dancing and singing, you must also have gone for the immersion (visarjan), saying "Ganpati Bappa Morya, come back early next year." Then you must have enjoyed immersing him in the sea or in some river. The aarti too must have been done every day.

## The Core Question of This Retreat

Now comes the question this retreat (shivir) is about. If all that was all we had to do, what was the need for a retreat? Where does knowledge (vidya) come into it?

So first tell me: what is Ganpati the deity of? What is he known for? What are his functions?

First is **intellect and learning**. That is one department: intelligence. Someone wrote the removal of obstacles, meaning he is also **Vighnaharta**, the remover of obstacles. Right. So these are two important things. After that he is also **Vinayaka**. Vinayaka means the special leader (nayak); he is a leader too. So there must be something connected to the quality of leadership as well.

## So Much Worship, Yet Why Obstacles?

Now the question arises: as much worship of Ganpati as happens in India surely happens nowhere else in the world. Of course, here we worship every god, the Goddess, everyone. But the question is: with so many modaks offered here, so much durva, so many red flowers, obstacles should never have come to India at all. Because God should have been so pleased, and every single person should have had discernment and wisdom.

Yet our own country's happiness index is among the lowest in the world. Look it up yourself and you will understand. This is the reality. We have everything, and still India lags far behind in happiness. And the diabetes capital of the world is India too.

> "So how does all this happen, if everything here is the holiest and everything is going right?"

## Faith and Knowledge: Both Are Needed

It means something is not being done properly; that much you must have understood. Either something is being done without understanding; or there is faith, but it may also be blind faith; or there is faith, but no knowledge.

The scriptures say both must be present. One should be a **devotee with knowledge (jnani bhakta)**; so says Shri Krishna in the Bhagavad Gita. The devotee with knowledge, the knower, is said to be worthy of liberation (moksha).

Energy can be converted from one form to another. So that matches whatever outcome you want.

## The Meaning of Abhishek

What does abhishek mean? Does anyone know?

Even if you do not understand it today, do not worry at all. The result must come. Release from your troubles will begin even before they arrive, immediately. You will see for yourself how this miracle happens.
