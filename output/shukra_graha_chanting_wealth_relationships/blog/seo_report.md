## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **"संतान सुख" under "शास्त्र कहते हैं"** (Sanskrit review, minor): classically Guru (Jupiter), not Shukra, is the karaka of children; Shukra is the karaka of spouse and marriage. Recommendation: adopt the review's wording ("दांपत्य सुख मिलता है" / "Married life is happy"), or attribute it to Acharya Ji if the transcript confirms he said it. Children are kept out of every title, meta, snippet and FAQ answer here.
2. **"करोड़ों" translated as "millions"** (review, minor, English only): करोड़ों is "tens of millions". Recommendation: "tens of millions" in both places. No SEO copy uses the figure.
3. **"शास्त्र कहते हैं / The scriptures say" without a named text** (review, minor): a general summary of Jyotisha, not a quotation. Recommendation (optional): "ज्योतिष शास्त्र के अनुसार / According to Jyotisha". The snippet in section 5 says "वैदिक ज्योतिष में / In Vedic astrology" rather than "the scriptures say", and no schema or meta cites a scripture.
4. **"Women" listed beside fashion, gadgets and luxury goods** (review, minor, sensitivity): accurate (Shukra is stri-karaka) but the Key Takeaways bullet can read as objectifying. Recommendation: the review's rewording ("स्त्री और दांपत्य जीवन के साथ-साथ…" / "it signifies women and married life, and also…"). FAQ 3 lists only fashion, luxury goods and devices.
5. **Time-bound programme facts**: "पहली बार ऑनलाइन / for the first time online", the 43-day sadhana (the video thumbnail gives 9 January 2026 as the start) and "5 लाख से अधिक / more than 500,000" Navagraha chants have dated by October 2026. Keep them out of titles and meta (they are), and update or add an editor's note when the next Shukra chanting round is announced.
6. **Outcome promises and fear-selling**: the video title says "Attract Wealth… Heal Your Emotions", and the blog says the collective chant "fills life with good fortune, love and prosperity". No title, meta or snippet here promises wealth, a spouse or healing; the weak-Venus signs are always paired with "wherever there is a problem, a solution exists". Do not use "remedy for marriage delay" or similar guarantee-style keywords, and do not take "Attract Wealth" from the video title into the SEO title.
