# Chapter: Shukra, the Planet of Love, Lakshmi and Beauty

## Why Does Everything Come Easily to Some and Not to Others?

In many people's lives, love, a unique aura, wealth, good fortune, artistry, beauty and comfort arrive effortlessly. Yet others, however hard they work, never find sweetness in relationships, financial stability, a good life partner, art, or the means of comfort.

Why does this happen? Because all of this comes into life only when your **Shukra (Venus) is awakened and auspicious**.

## A Strong Shukra and an Afflicted Shukra

The scriptures say that where Shukra is strong, love is stable, marriage succeeds, personality blossoms, the joy of children is attained, and Lakshmi comes naturally.

And when Shukra is weak or afflicted, all of these appear:

- Disagreements in relationships and instability in love
- Obstacles in marriage and a lack of happiness in life
- Financial loss, addiction and instability in business
- Disinterest in art and the absence of an impressive aura

But where there is a problem, there is surely a solution. That solution is Antaryog's **Shukra Graha Beej Mantra Sadhana**.

## What Falls Under Shukra?

Acharya ji explains that Shukra is a rajasic planet. In general, women come under Shukra (Venus), and women's clothing and fashion all fall under Shukra. Being rajasic, your electronic things, computers and laptops also come under Shukra.

Understand this through an example. You say, "I had a big presentation, and I don't know how my laptop suddenly shut down in the middle." Acharya ji says:

> "Your laptop was Shukra. To you it looked lifeless."

To you it seemed only an inert electronic device. But if you deceive your wife there, it is as if the laptop says, "I will betray you in your important presentation." How this happens and what happens, Acharya ji teaches in the sadhana.

The laptop is only one example. All technical and electronic devices, all luxury items, clean and beautiful places, and all fashionable things are called Shukra.
