## 1. Summary

The talk asks how the word of God differs from the word of a saint. God's word (**परावाणी**, Paravani) is universal: it holds in every land, age and person. A saint's word sometimes repeats that universal truth and sometimes applies it to one time and place for a social reform; that part is temporary. The example is modesty and brahmacharya, which took the form of the ghunghat in India and the burqa elsewhere: the rules differ, the principle is one. Every religion has three parts (fundamentals, mythology, rituals); the fundamentals are the same for all, and people caught in duality fight over the outer two. It closes with "करो तो ठीक से करो" (teach religion with its meaning) and the point that saying "my God is greater" makes your own God smaller, supported by "साधकानां हितार्थाय ब्रह्मणो रूपकल्पना". The main intent is **informational**, with a reflective, interfaith angle: "धर्म का असली सार", "धर्म हमें क्या सिखाता है" (the video's own title) and "true essence of dharma" / "what does dharma teach us" are evergreen queries. Ranking pages are mostly dictionary-style definitions of dharma or generic "all religions are one" essays. This post's distinct angle is a practical test (universal vs. time-and-place teaching) and the three-part model of religion, which no definition page gives. There is no festival peak; the talk is episode 15 of the Ganesh Series, so it can be linked from the Ganesh Series cluster during Ganesh Chaturthi refreshes.

**English (55 words):** According to Acharya Upendra Ji, the true essence of dharma is the one foundation every religion shares: the Supreme Reality, the Atman. God's word is universal and holds in every land and age, while part of a saint's word serves a particular time and society. Stories and rituals differ from one religion to another; the essence is one.

## 6. FAQ

1. **संत-वाणी और परमात्मा-वाणी में क्या अंतर है? / How does the word of a saint differ from the word of God?**
   परमात्मा की वाणी (परावाणी) सार्वभौमिक है: हर देश, हर काल, हर व्यक्ति पर एक समान लागू। संत भी कभी वही सार्वभौमिक बात कहते हैं, पर कभी अपने समय और समाज के सुधार के लिए नियम देते हैं; उतना भाग देश-काल से बंधा, अस्थायी होता है। / God's word (Paravani) is universal: it applies equally in every land, age and person. Saints sometimes speak that same universal truth, and sometimes give rules for the reform of their own time and society; that part is bound to time and place.
2. **हर धर्म के तीन भाग कौन-से हैं? / What are the three parts of every religion?**
   मूल तत्त्व (परम तत्त्व, आत्मा), कथा या पुराण (महापुरुषों के युग की कहानियां), और विधि-विधान (पूजा, यज्ञ, कर्मकांड)। मूल तत्त्व सबका एक है। / Fundamentals (the Supreme Reality, the Atman), mythology (the stories of its great figures), and rituals (worship, fire offerings, ceremonies). The fundamentals are the same for all.
3. **क्या घूंघट या बुर्का जैसे नियम सार्वभौमिक हैं? / Are rules like the ghunghat or the burqa universal?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार नहीं: ये अपने लोगों, जलवायु और समाज को देखकर दिए गए नियम हैं। इनके पीछे का तत्त्व, लोक-लज्जा और ब्रह्मचर्य, एक है। / According to Acharya Ji, no: they are rules given with a people, a climate and a society in mind. The principle behind them, modesty and brahmacharya, is the same.
4. **"मेरा भगवान बड़ा" कहना गलत क्यों है? / Why is it wrong to say "my God is greater than yours"?**
   तुलना करते ही हम अपने इष्ट देवता को सीमित कर देते हैं और द्वैत खड़ा कर देते हैं; जो अनंत था, उसे सीमा में बांध देते हैं। रूप साधक की सुविधा के लिए है, ब्रह्म अनंत है। / The moment we compare, we confine our own chosen deity and create duality, putting limits on the Infinite. A form is there to help the seeker; Brahman has no end.
5. **लोग धर्म के नाम पर क्यों झगड़ते हैं? / Why do people fight in the name of religion?**
   जो अभी द्वैत में हैं, वे केवल कथा और कर्मकांड के बाहरी भेद देखते हैं और मूल तत्त्व तक नहीं पहुंचते; इन्हीं भेदों को पकड़कर झगड़ा होता है। / Those still caught in duality see only the outer differences of stories and rituals, never reaching the shared essence, and they cling to these differences and fight.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **"साधकानां हितार्थाय ब्रह्मणो रूपकल्पना" reference (Sanskrit review issue 1, Minor, not yet applied).** The Upanishad's own wording is "उपासकानां कार्यार्थं ब्रह्मणो रूपकल्पना" (second half of Ramapurvatapaniya Upanishad 1.7); the blog quotes the popular reading. The Hindi says so, the English "as commonly cited" does not. Apply the review's correction. In SEO text (snippet, FAQ, social cards), never write "the Upanishad says 'sādhakānāṁ hitārthāya…'"; the FAQ only paraphrases ("a form is there to help the seeker").
2. **"ऐसा शास्त्र कहता है" / "So the scriptures say" (review issue 2, Minor).** No verse says in these words that comparing your God makes Him smaller. Apply the correction ("शास्त्र का भाव यही है" / "This is the spirit of the scriptures"). The FAQ answer for "my God is greater" gives the teaching without a scriptural attribution; keep it that way in titles and social posts.
3. **"thirty-three koti" gloss (review issue 3, Minor).** Apply the correction (koti as crore or "kinds"). The link to `33_crore_gods_truth_about_hinduism` uses "fifty different forms of the Divine" as the English anchor, so no SEO text repeats the one-sided gloss.
4. **Burqa example (review issue 4, Minor, sensitivity).** Muslims trace covering to the Quran, which they regard as God's word. The English frames the example as a hypothetical; the Hindi states it as fact. Apply the Hindi correction ("मान लीजिए…"). Do not target burqa, hijab or "Islam vs Hinduism" queries with this post, and keep burqa out of titles, metas and social cards; the FAQ question attributes the view to Acharya Ji and names the ghunghat first.
5. **"one God, whether Allah or Jesus" (review issue 5, Minor, sensitivity).** Apply the correction. Not used in any SEO text.
6. **Paravani gloss (review issue 6, optional).** The SEO text uses "परावाणी" / "Paravani" only as the blog defines it (the word of the Supreme). If the editor adds the "para vak" note, nothing here needs to change.
7. **तत्व / तत्त्व spelling (review issue 7, Minor).** The SEO text (meta, snippet, FAQ, title option 2) uses the Gita Press spelling तत्त्व. The Hindi link anchor "तत्व एक है, कहानियां अलग हैं, कर्मकांड अलग हैं" is copied from the current blog text; **if the editor applies the तत्त्व correction, update that anchor in seo.json (and section 11) and rebuild**, or the link will not be found.
8. **Interfaith respect.** The talk speaks of Jesus, Krishna, the Bible and the Quran with respect and says the problem is not only Hindus'. Titles and metas avoid "my religion vs yours" or "which religion is true" framing that would invite combative clicks; the secondary keyword "मेरा भगवान तेरे भगवान से बड़ा" / "my God is greater than yours" is targeted only to answer it the way the talk does.
9. **Thumbnail vs title.** The video thumbnail headlines "करो तो ठीक से करो, वरना कुछ मत करो", a short section of the talk. The blog title follows the main teaching (saint's word vs God's word), which is what the page delivers. For YouTube, a title such as "धर्म का असली सार: संत-वाणी और परमात्मा-वाणी | Ganesh Series Ep 15" would match the page and its keyword.

Category note: `gallery_topics.txt` places the talk under `dharma`, copied as is. The Ganesh Series is a tag, not a category, and no Ganesh deity tag is added because the post is not about Ganesha. "Interfaith Harmony" / "सर्वधर्म समभाव" is a new tag; it fits at least `ultimate_source_learn_about_every_religion`, `spiritual_meaning_secularism_independence_day_special` and `need_murtis_symbols_understand_god`, so reuse it there. ## 12. SEO checklist

### Keywords
- [x] Hindi and English each have a focus keyword
- [x] The focus keyword isn't used by another post (no cannibalization)
- [x] 2–4 secondary keywords per language
- [x] Hindi and English keywords researched separately, not translated; Hinglish variants considered
- [x] Focus keyword taken from wording the article already uses
- [x] If people search a different wording, it is in the secondary keywords and the mismatch is flagged
- [x] Priority given as High / Medium / Low, based on intent and competition; no invented numbers

### Focus keyword placement (SureRank)
- [x] In the SEO title
- [x] In the meta description
- [x] In the body text
- [x] In the URL slug (English keywords)
- [x] Within the first 100 words (preferred)

### SEO title
- [x] Present in both languages, ≤ 60 characters
- [x] Keyword near the start
- [x] Faithful to the talk, no clickbait

### Meta description
- [x] Present in both languages, ≤ 160 characters
- [x] English is 140–155 characters
- [x] Gives a clear reason to click

### URL slug
- [x] Lowercase Latin, hyphenated, unique across posts
- [x] Short enough that the full URL stays ≤ 90 characters

### Headings
- [x] At least one subheading (H2)

### Images
- [x] Every image has alt text in both languages
- [x] Alt text is descriptive, in the post's language, based on looking at the image
- [x] Captions add meaning (not a repeat of the alt text or title)

### Internal linking
- [x] Links point only to real posts, never to the same post
- [x] Anchor text is a natural phrase from the body (not a heading or verse)
- [x] 3–5 in-text links per language
- [x] One "Also read" link, placed after the section it extends best
- [x] 3 related posts at the end, the ones that suit this reader best

### Category and tags
- [x] One category, from the approved 14
- [x] 3–6 tags per language
- [x] No tag repeats a category name
- [x] Existing tags reused; no near-duplicates
- [x] Each tag can be shared by at least 3 posts
- [x] No broad labels that only restate a category
- [x] Series tags named exactly as the series
- [x] `festivals` only when the post is about the festival itself; Pitru topics in `pitru`

### Structured data (in this report, not on the page)
- [x] One JSON-LD block per language: BlogPosting, Person, Organization, VideoObject (if there is a video), BreadcrumbList
- [x] Hindi and English versions linked as translations

### Content trust
- [x] Titles, metas and snippets faithful to the talk
- [x] No unsupported scriptural claims
- [x] Health promises, fear-selling and attacks on other traditions flagged

### Not covered
- Live broken links (internal links only work once the linked post is published)
- Canonical and Open Graph tags (SureRank adds these itself)
