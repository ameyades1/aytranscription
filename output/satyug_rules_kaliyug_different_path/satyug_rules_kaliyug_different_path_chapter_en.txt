# Chapter: The Rules of Satya Yuga, the Path of Kali Yuga

## An Important Session

Namaskar. Today's session is extremely important; listen to it with full attention. If you listen attentively, perhaps something good will come of it for our country and our dharma in the future. Very deep knowledge is about to be given today. When the time for such knowledge comes, only then is it revealed before everyone, and perhaps that time is today, is now. Going forward, India will greatly need this knowledge, especially the propagation we are doing in the name of Sanatana Dharma.

So are you ready for such a session? You must listen with your ears fully open and every door of your mind open. Today a new and important thing has to be told, without following which, in deep Kali Yuga, the path is said to lead to pralaya and destruction. The first sign of that destruction is that our own people begin to slip away. How can we rise out of that? If we are to become Vishwaguru, the teacher of the world, that shastra must be understood today. So let us begin.

## Kālāya Tasmai Namaḥ

There are four yugas: Satya Yuga, Treta Yuga, Dwapara Yuga and Kali Yuga. At present we are in Kali Yuga. The shastras say, "kālāya tasmai namaḥ", meaning we must live according to the time we are in.

What happens to one who does not live according to the time? Begin with dress. Wear the garment of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj's time, so honoured as it is, and go to the office today; see what happens. Go further back and wear the attire of Shri Krishna and Arjuna. Did they wear something wrong? Were those gods, those avatars, those great beings wrong? No. What most people do today and what is also accepted has nothing wrong in it. That is what one has to do.

The same is true of technology. Earlier there were bullock carts, and people went on horseback. Today say, "From tomorrow I will go to the office only on a horse, a tonga, a chariot." Someone may think, "My way of doing business will remain the old one too; I will send no message by modern means. Just as they used to roll up a khalita, a letter scroll, carry it and open it to read, I will do the same, and deliver it myself." Can you become successful like this? You can do it, there is no crime in it. But will you get business? Perhaps more in drama and theatre. If you go against the times, neither your vehicle will run nor your technology.

Still, no one can stop you. "I will send the khalita on foot; I will tell my servant, go, deliver it all the way to Delhi." No one can stop you from this. But you cannot become a fully successful person; this has to be accepted. And yet in some age someone did become successful by that very method, that very tantra.

That is why the rule has been given, kālāya tasmai namaḥ. You will have to adopt the ways of Kali Yuga. You may talk of Satya Yuga, of Dwapara and Treta; no one will stop you. You may dream a hundred thousand dreams that we must become like this. But as long as it is the time of that age, time itself is the most powerful.

## Parikshit and the Entry of Kali

You know the story of the beginning of Kali Yuga. In the time of King Parikshit, a glimpse was shown of Kali's entry and of what would happen in Kali Yuga. That too had a fixed time. Shri Krishna had given sixty years, and as soon as that period was over, Kali found some way or other to enter. Everything aligned itself in his favour on its own. All his companions, desire, anger, greed, attachment and ego, kept creating the same atmosphere, and the same kind of people kept turning up. This happens on its own. The influence of time does come. It is said that even Parikshit could not stop it, because if time has come, then the time for Parikshit to depart must also have come. If you read the Bhagavata Purana, you will understand this story.

In exactly the same way, when the time comes to move from Kali towards Satya Yuga, the time for the asuric, demonic, forces to depart will also come. And they will leave only by the method the shastras have prescribed for driving them out, not by any method of your choosing. According to the time, until then, they will certainly remain.

## Personal Liberation and Success in the World

There are two separate things here. One is your personal moksha. No one will stop it. For your own liberation, adopt the path of Satya Yuga or of Treta; it makes no difference, because there you are alone.

But today's subject is something else. If you want to become successful in this world, to gain expansion, to take your message across the whole world, then the rule of Kali applies. If you want to apply your path to others and want its result to be seen in the world, you may well become a role model, but you must understand what happens to it afterwards.

What did our great saints, rishis and munis, who would also have attained moksha, say? In Kali Yuga, only the yantra and tantra of Kali will work. Shankaracharya himself said this, and the same is found described everywhere in our shastras. Kali Yuga will be the age of the Agama shastra. Agama and tantra are synonyms. The Devi Purana says the same, and the Shakta tradition describes it especially. That is why it is said that whoever recites the Durga Saptashati, which balances the three gunas, not only by the Vedic method but by the tantric method, will be victorious in Kali Yuga. This age gives quick results in the shortest time.

## Shruti, Smriti, Purana and Agama

What knowledge works in Satya Yuga? Vedic knowledge, the knowledge of the Vedas, and its culmination, Vedanta. Personal success can be had from anything in all four yugas. Here we are speaking of the knowledge whose result can be seen across the whole world and for a long time, which people want from within, from the heart, not under pressure. In Satya Yuga, whoever propagates Veda and Vedanta will be victorious, and it will quickly spread across the world.

For Shri Rama's age, Treta Yuga, the place of smriti is given, such as the Manusmriti and the Yajnavalkya Smriti. What does smriti mean? First the Vedas were given, called shruti, meaning the knowledge that came from guru to disciple by hearing with the ears, not from a book. Smriti is that from which understanding comes, the past events that have become imprinted in the mind. For example, a vehicle accident happened, and after it a quarrel; that event passed, but a lesson was learnt that this will happen among human beings. The smritis record exactly this: what disputes will arise between religions, what will happen with the varnas, what will happen from the gunas, from what is fit and unfit to eat. Rules from every angle come in them: politics, society, family, spirituality and dharma. To follow these, an age of dharma is needed; only then will it become the ideal. In Shri Rama's time, whoever propagated the perfect observance of dharma would have expanded, because people were ready to listen to it.

For Shri Krishna's time, Dwapara Yuga, the place of the Puranas is given. Whoever spoke, heard and explained the knowledge of the Puranas the most, people would act according to it. In it the rasa of devotion is greater, with a little blend of knowledge.

The same comes in the sankalpa of the puja we perform: "śruti-smṛti-purāṇokta phala-prāptyartham... japaṁ kariṣye, pūjāṁ kariṣye." For which yuga were these shruti, smriti and Puranas? Does this mean puja is wrong? No; we perform puja in our Gurukul too. But it will work for personal liberation. If one wants expansion, then for Kali Yuga the Agama shastra and tantra vidya have been prescribed, and it is these that will expand across the world.

## The Meaning of Agama and Tantra

What does the word Agama mean? Generally, the Agamas are those texts in which Lord Shiva spoke to Parvati. Most of them contain some deep secret that has been kept kilita, meaning locked, or cursed, so that it is not used in the wrong place. Some are composed in such a way that if anyone reads them and practises without a guru, he will fall. Such Agama or tantra shastra has been declared suitable for Kali Yuga.

In the word Agama, "ā" is the symbol of all-pervasiveness and "gama" of motion. That is, to pervade everything with dynamism. The word tantra contains "tra", whose deep meaning is protector of the prana: that knowledge or siddhi because of which I am saved. This is not the literal meaning but the hidden one. Protection with the help of knowledge, knowledge that is expansive and vast.

Put simply, today's internet is also a tantra. Why? Because in an instant it can spread your message across the whole world. It becomes all-pervading, it is instant, it happens while sitting in one place. This too is a siddhi of Kali, a yantra of Kali, and tantra is in it too. Whoever knows how to use it will be more successful in Kali Yuga. Some time ago this technology did not exist; no matter. The technology of that time gave success. One has to adopt it. Whoever says "I will not" will not have that much expansion.

So does Omkara have no importance in Kali Yuga? It does, and it will never go. Does truth have no importance? It has great importance, and we will come to that later.

## Two Thousand Years of Bondage and the Reality of Becoming Vishwaguru

Why does India look the way it does from outside today? Was it the leela of such a long time? What must change now? We have to become Vishwaguru. But a country that remained in bondage for two thousand years will also need time to rise. And will those who ruled over us accept us as their guru?

Think of it realistically. Until yesterday you had a servant, or you had held him captive, since the time of your father, grandfather and great-grandfather. You kept his son a beggar. Now if he comes and says, "Accept me as your guru", what will happen? First think of it from the angle of two individuals, then of two families, then of the whole world. A human being is a human being. If it is hard on a small scale, it will be even harder on a large scale. That those who ruled for two thousand years and kept believing "these people know nothing" should become their disciples overnight is itself a problem. It will not happen so easily. They will have to be given a fitting reply. How that happens and why, we must first understand.

## Venus: The Planet of Kali Yuga

Last time an introduction to this subject was given; today let us go into detail. The subject is "The Secret of the Wondrous Powers of the Planet Venus".

The question begins right here. The Shukra Stotra says, "himakundamṛṇālābhaṁ daityānāṁ paramaṁ gurum." So why are we worshipping the guru of the daityas, the demons? Because in Kali Yuga it is the daityas who are in the majority.

Tantra shastra describes the order of the spiritual path in the four yugas like this:

> **kṛte śrutyukta ācāras tretāyāṁ smṛtisambhavaḥ**
> **dvāpare tu purāṇoktaḥ kalāv āgamasammataḥ**
> "In Krita the conduct taught in the shruti, in Treta the conduct born of the smriti, in Dwapara that taught in the Puranas, and in Kali that approved by the Agamas."
> (Kularnava Tantra)

That is, in Satya Yuga Vedic knowledge held the chief place, in Treta the smriti shastras, in Dwapara Puranic conduct, and in Kali Yuga the Agama is the most important.

Whether you accept it or not, bring as many traditions as you like, bring a peaceful tradition, bring anything, the Agama is what it is. Shiva himself has said this. In Kali Yuga the role of Agama and tantra is considered the most important. Whether you understand it or not, whether your guru gave it to you or not, whether your ancestors told you or not, we do not know. If some guru did not tell you, that must have been the leela of your state of ignorance. But the truth is that all gurus till today have told of it and have also used it. Perhaps the leela was such that we could not understand. All the gurus had siddhis. We only kept saying, "Guruji, do something for me." We never said, "We want to become like you", never asked, "How did this come to you?"

That is why it is said that for spiritual progress and success in Kali Yuga, the path of Agama and tantra is the most effective. Notice that this speaks not of complete moksha but of spiritual progress, and that too quickly, because in Kali Yuga life itself is short. And of material success as well.

Does this mean the other paths do not work? One can go from Kanyakumari to Delhi by bullock cart too. But if you want to arrive quickly, the right vehicle is an aeroplane or a train. The problem comes when one takes the wrong path. The other paths may be good, your car may be excellent. But if you must travel this particular road, this means is indispensable.

Agama shastra means the knowledge that brings progress with speed. Progress with speed matters because in Kali Yuga time is short and life is brief. And tantra means that which is useful for the expansion of knowledge and which protects all living beings. In the end, knowledge should increase through it. And tantra should be in the hands of a worthy person.

## Artificial Intelligence: Tool or Master?

Artificial Intelligence can be helpful; it too is a siddhi. But knowledge itself is under sattva guna. Knowledge, happiness, dispassion, consciousness and bliss are qualities of sattva. If knowledge belongs to sattva, then intellect too will be under it. This artificial intelligence too should be a tool in the hands of a sattvic person, not a rajasic or tamasic one. Yes, the rajasic and tamasic person needs help. If he wants to rise and uses it as a tool for learning, that is fine. The aim should be learning, not just taking a shortcut to success.

Today people immediately tell their AI, "Write this letter for me." The intellect of some sattvic person has become their servant. Below it, also write, "Tell me the reason why this letter was written like this." You must learn from it. Whenever I use it, for my texts or for decoding hidden meanings, I always ask: "Why did you do this? Under which rule did you write this? Give me the source." And I learn from it and develop my own intellect. I do not become subordinate to its artificial intellect.

Tantra is that which expands knowledge and finally gives you trāṇa, protects you. And Agama is that which is all-pervading, dynamic, and brings progress.

## The Knowledge of Shukracharya

All these qualities come under the planet Venus, and so Venus is extremely important in Kali Yuga. Remember, this is the knowledge of Shukracharya. He had knowledge such as the Mritasanjivani mantra; he was a siddha. He is called "sarvaśāstrapravaktāraṁ", the expounder of all shastras. Whoever today knows data analysis will be very successful. Shri Vidya, cunning statecraft, Kautilya's Arthashastra, the knowledge of Chanakya, all these will be more useful in this age.

The definition of Agama is given in this verse:

> **āgataṁ śivavaktrebhyo gataṁ ca girijāmukhe**
> **mataṁ ca vāsudevasya tasmād āgama ucyate**
> "It came from the mouths of Shiva, went to the mouth of Girija, and is approved by Vasudeva; therefore it is called Agama."
> (Rudrayamala Tantra)

Lord Shiva gave the teaching of tantra to Bhagavati Parvati from his five mouths. These tantras were also accepted by the Vasudeva form of Lord Vishnu, because Shiva and Vishnu are each other's object of worship. These same tantras later spread in all directions and are called the five Amnaya tantras. Vasudeva is the sustainer. So for sustaining, for Kali Yuga, our own texts have affirmed that you must have this knowledge.

## Two Paths, One Destination

Now let us understand the paths properly. There are two ordinary paths: "neti neti" and "iti iti", nivritti and pravritti, Sankhya yoga and Karma yoga. We know this. If in the end both meet at the same place, we have no objection. From outside the two roads look different, but if the final destination is the same, that is fine. Whoever wants to go by bullock cart may go, because that is not even his goal. No one stops him. What we are talking about is different: what must be done to live in this world in comfort and peace.

So Lord Shiva said that tantra will be accepted in Kali, and Lord Vishnu also accepted it. That is why the word tantra must be properly learnt.

## Kali Yuga: The Age of Quick Success

Kali Yuga is an age that wants quick success. Its peculiarity is that results come late, but people want everything immediately. If something truly can be had quickly, there is benefit in that too; otherwise a person will on his own be drawn to the wrong path. That is why in this age the knowledge of Agama is especially necessary for attaining great goals. Not for small goals, and I am not even speaking of ultimate moksha, although if you want speed in that too, it will come from this. If you want some extraordinary, special accomplishment, Agama is necessary.

Kali Yuga does not oppose wealth, dreaming big or attaining success. Kali Yuga will never say that. It will say, "Do more." That is Kali's very nature. It does not oppose power, desire, showing influence and travelling to many places; rather, it supports them, and the same kind of people come before you. So this knowledge teaches that these should be used for a higher purpose, or renounced in the proper way. There are only two ways: attain success at a high level and then give it up, or attain it and use it rightly. To conquer is to renounce. Attain and leave, or attain and use.

## Muladhara and the Question of Two Gurus

Now a very important point about Kali Yuga. Under tantra there are four stages, for success at successively higher levels.

You know the basic concept that the seeker rises through Kundalini shakti. In the Muladhara chakra, the first place is said to be Parvati's. Muladhara is the symbol of inertia, of the earth element. She is below in the Himalaya, and above on Kailash sits Shiva in meditation, the Supreme, Purusha. In between are the six chakras, the whole of Prakriti, and from below to above the two must unite. If even this union were brought about instantly, would there not be greater benefit?

Now I will ask some questions that need a yes or no answer.

In Kali Yuga there are two gurus and the goal is expansion. Let us talk about the Muladhara chakra. One guru gave correct knowledge, taught ideal brahmacharya, taught it extremely well, explained its importance, spoke of the power of intellect and radiance. This is not wrong either. He made Hanumanji its ideal. Then he said, go, teach this message to the whole world. In Rama's age he would immediately have found many followers. How many will he find in Kali Yuga? Is the knowledge wrong? No. Is that state wrong? No. Are its benefits wrong? No. But how many will be its worshippers? We have to be frank and honest. Will he not get moksha? He will; its benefits will certainly come to him. But the expansion will be less.

On the other side is a person who teaches the shastra of the same Muladhara chakra, kama, desire, and shows how through it too one can reach moksha by the path of purushartha. He reconciles the two and shows how they are equivalent, just as Shri Krishna says in the Gita, "prajanaś cāsmi kandarpaḥ", I am the procreative desire. We are talking about Venus, about Kali Yuga, so the talk will begin from Muladhara and then gradually rise. This person says that this too gives the same merit, the same benefit, and gives references from the Shakta tradition, from Durga Devi, from everything, and also that Shiva himself has said that the fruit is the same in both states, as I have explained in many camps. Now tell me, who will have more followers?

In the same way, in Kali enjoyment is to be had. Is there more enjoyment in many colours, or in sitting in a single-coloured garment? I am not rejecting anyone; I am only telling whose fruit will be greater in this age. Whoever says, "Even a woman who wears lipstick will attain moksha", more people will come to him, and as the word spreads, they will come quickly. Because it is Kali Yuga, the worship of the Devi will have to be spoken of first, the women will have to be spoken of first, and after that the men. The general public too will spread this more.

One guru says, "Child, all this is moha and maya, attachment and illusion; leave it, renounce wealth and property, no one has ever benefited from it." That guru's state is also good, he will also get moksha, his path is also excellent. But how many followers will he have? And another says, "This is Shri Vidya; it does not spoil the intellect, it will bring you wealth; see how great Shri Rama was." Where will people go more in the future? Not right now, but when the choice is given, they will go to the one who proved that this too brings benefit.

Every guru's time comes. As soon as people understand, a crowd suddenly gathers there. Just as when jaggery is kept somewhere, as long as the ants do not know, it lies alone like gold. But as soon as one ant finds out that there is food here, within minutes a line forms from all directions. The delay is only the short time it takes to understand.

I am not concerned with which method is right and which is wrong. I am saying both methods are right. The question is whose expansion will be quick according to the time, which guru will become bigger and faster, whose propagation will last longer. Did I say the influence of the others will be zero? Their influence will remain too; they will also remain famous forever. But in Kali Yuga, within a short time, their followers will forget them. Since no other option is available, they will bow to them too, but from within they will want that by bowing to them they get the same enjoyments. "O Gurudev, give me a car, a horse, a bungalow": to whom will people go to ask for this? Those who know the path of renunciation are fine. But one who wants this path should get it.

## The Wise Should Act for the Welfare of the World

Here, look at two verses from the third chapter of the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita.

> **saktāḥ karmaṇy avidvāṁso yathā kurvanti bhārata**
> **kuryād vidvāṁs tathāsaktaś cikīrṣur lokasaṅgraham**
> "O Bharata, as the ignorant, attached to action, act, so should the wise, unattached, act, desiring the welfare of the world."
> (Bhagavad Gita 3.25)

The general public is ignorant, and in Kali Yuga ignorance is greater, because this is the time of Kali. They are attached to actions, that is, rajasic; there is tamas in attachment too, and when "ignorant" is said, tamas has already come in. So the general public, a mixture of rajas and tamas, is doing its actions in its own way. Someone is getting married; that too is action. Someone is performing puja and rituals properly, going to the temple. Someone is doing business, someone is running a state, someone is becoming a doctor. Doing action is not wrong. But they are attached to action, not yet unattached. "Why am I doing this action": no one knows.

Then it says, "the wise, unattached, too": here the guru is being spoken of. He should act in the same way, desiring lokasangraha, gathering people together, on a large scale. Note that this was said in the previous age itself, that the guru too will have to do all the same things, will first have to do them himself to be an example before thousands of people. Is marrying wrong? The Saptarishis also married; it is action. Is puja wrong? No. Is doing business wrong? No. Everything is permitted. So the guru too must do the same actions, but for lokasangraha, to show people an example.

> **na buddhibhedaṁ janayed ajñānāṁ karmasaṅginām**
> **joṣayet sarvakarmāṇi vidvān yuktaḥ samācaran**
> "Let the wise man, firmly established, not create confusion in the minds of the ignorant who are attached to action; performing all actions well himself, let him engage them in all actions."
> (Bhagavad Gita 3.26)

"The wise man firmly established in the nature of the Supreme Self should not create confusion, that is, lack of faith in action, in the minds of the ignorant who are attached to the actions prescribed by the shastras; rather, performing all prescribed actions well himself, he should get them done in the same way."

This is the definition of a self-realized guru, a supreme guru. The Supreme has no form; svarupa, nature, means its qualities: compassion, love, contentment, all-pervading knowledge, happiness, consciousness. "satyaṁ jñānam anantaṁ brahma", Sat-Chit-Ananda. What is the command given to such a guru, firmly established in that nature?

This is Shri Krishna's direct teaching. "Actions prescribed by the shastras" means actions according to the shastras. The shastras speak of eating food, so it is an action. How to eat properly, the guru should teach by holding a camp on diet, and should himself eat in the same way. Someone married; not wrong, it is his choice, but its shastra must be taught to people in the right way. Is earning money, business, the vaishya way of life wrong? No. Shri Vidya is a siddhi; there should be no hesitation in teaching it. Is puja wrong? No; action itself is yajna.

So should the guru teach how to run a liquor den? A question arises here. If someone says, "Our liquor den is not doing well, our brothel is not doing well", is it necessary for the guru to teach that? Has it been said that for lokasangraha he should demonstrate that too, because it is Kali Yuga and everything topsy-turvy will go? No. The clear words are "actions prescribed by the shastras". Confusion is not to be put into their minds, nothing is to be said against action. That is, one must not say, "All this is moha and maya, leave it." It was said at the very beginning of Kali that the guru of Kali should never say this. This is what our Shiva tells us, who is the object of worship of Shri Krishna, that is, of Vishnu: Harihara. If you teach suppression in Kali Yuga, there will be problems. Teaching control is one part, but never suppression. If you want expansion, what has been taught is to sanctify desires.

## A Clarification

Listen to what I say today ten times, with this big disclaimer: I have not proved any path wrong or right. I have only said which method will expand in which time, who will gain the world's acceptance. Both are shastra, both are right. We are only telling whom it will favour, when and how. If in the end both give the same destination, that is fine.

The guru must not create in people's minds a lack of faith towards any action, or even towards any enjoyment. Someone says, "I want a child", and if the guru says, "Oh, what is the use of a child?", that is not right. If you know a remedy through siddhi, tell it; if not, give whatever knowledge you have and leave it. Otherwise seekers will not come to you. He will first run to the test-tube baby clinic, or wherever he gets a result through some other tantra. Because it is Kali Yuga, he will go after any tantra; it is not that he will come only to your spiritual tantra. In Kali Yuga he will go by both roads, the yantra and the tantra of Kali. If it does not happen here, he will try there, and when everything there is exhausted, saying "now prayer is needed, not medicine", he will come here. Both are permitted in Kali Yuga. Whatever can give expansion, success and progress quickly, both have their place.

## A Slap on One Cheek, and the Irony of East and West

Now a second example. One preacher says, "If someone strikes you on one cheek, offer him the other cheek too." Another says, "If he struck one, strike two back, and silence him with the very first blow so that the second never comes." In Kali Yuga, who will get more followers? Today I am not saying who is right and who is wrong, or whose power is greater. I know this discourse will be very useful in the future, will become very popular, because very mysterious things are being told in it.

The first teaching too was given by rishis and munis, but I am asking according to the time. This teaching was given by Jesus Christ. Swami Vivekananda wrote clearly about this irony in his book "The East and the West". Jesus Christ told people there that if someone strikes you on one cheek, offer the other too, and yet the whole West, the whole of Europe, remained engaged in war, in conquering the whole world, even though their guru had said this to calm their power. And to Indians, every guru, in every avatar, gave weapons into the hands of the gods and said, fight. Yet here everyone sat down in surrender. Here too the opposite happened, and there too the opposite happened. In Kali Yuga it has mostly looked like this. Swami Vivekananda himself said this.

In the West the asuric tendency was greater, so the guru there said, "Be calm, be calm", but they could not become calm. Here everyone said, "Become a karma yogi, rise, do something, take the power of Kali", but our people did not listen. This is Swami Vivekananda's authentic book, which he himself wrote in Bengali and whose translation is available in the market today. It is clearly written there. I have spoken about this in another camp too.

Now the question arises: even to kill the enemy, one has to love the enemy. One says, only offer the other cheek. The other says, you could also have done this, you could have answered properly, taught him a lesson once.

## Chanakya and the Thorny Plant

What did Acharya Chanakya do? Early in his life he was once walking along a road and his foot landed on a thorn. He looked around to see where it had come from. He saw a small plant full of thorns. He understood that the thorn must have fallen from there. Immediately he took buttermilk from a nearby shop and poured it around the plant and over it. His disciples did not understand. They did not have the courage to ask the guru directly, so later, in private, they asked what he had done.

Chanakya replied, "The trouble I suffered today should not come every day, again and again, so I have arranged that ants and other insects will come here. They will eat away this plant and finish it completely. When the plant is finished, the thorn is finished, and this problem will never raise its head again."

A problem has to be wiped out from the root, before it rises again. This should be the knowledge for Kali Yuga. Chanakya told this about two thousand years ago, in the time of Alexander. From that very time the dominance of Europe began to grow. Even then he showed the way: the problem has to be pulled out from the root, the root cause has to be healed.

Returning to the second half of that verse of the Gita, Shri Krishna clearly says that the wise one should, "performing all prescribed actions well himself, get them done in the same way." I did not write this; Lord Shri Krishna himself said it, whom we worship. The guru too must perform all prescribed actions well and get people to do the same.

This means according to place, time and person. The guru has to show by doing it himself. He has to show by doing how business is done. He has to show how women are to be uplifted. He has to be a living example of the teaching of all four, dharma, artha, kama and moksha. The fruit comes in this too, so nothing is improper. He has to perform puja too, and show what fruit comes through puja; the murtis are the same, but how benefit comes quickly through the mantras of tantra. On one side those of brahmacharya are showing their example, on the other side by increasing the followers of this path too, but in the end the same state must be reached.

## The Slipperiness of Kali Yuga and the Worthy Guru

Now where is the trap in this? Is Kali Yuga an age of fall or of rise? In Kali Yuga the slippery path of falling is greater. This guru will expand quickly too, but if the seeker is not worthy, he will fall just as quickly. The same technology, the same tantra, the same science can take one very high and can also cut one down. That is why they said to join Vedanta to it as well. If you take only the yantra of Kali, only tantric knowledge, and your mind has not become sattvic, you will go into kali vidya, black magic. Marana, mohana, vashikarana, killing, enchanting and controlling others: this easy path will start to appeal to you. Then the chance of destruction is also greater, because Kali's very nature is to move towards destruction. So one has to be very careful. Without this there is no success, but it must be done with care. That is why the importance of a worthy guru is very great.

## The Dakshina Marga and the Vama Marga

The two paths we spoke of, the nivritti path of Sankhya and renunciation of action, and the pravritti path, have other names: Dakshina Marga and Vama Marga. Dakshina Marga does not mean the southern direction. Dakshina means right, as in right eye and left eye, right hand and left hand. What the world considers normal and right is the Dakshina Marga.

"Man is born to love, to live in a spirit of non-duality." This is the ideal, the perfect ideal; there is nothing wrong in it. "You were not born to quarrel; love. Who has ever benefited from hatred? If he sins, why should you?" The textbook ideal definition fits perfectly in the Dakshina Marga.

This Dakshina Marga is a perfectly straight and simple path. But is the age that straight and simple? Shri Krishna himself describes the three gunas in the fourteenth chapter of the Gita. In the Durga Saptashati the Devi says, I am of the nature of the three gunas, I am full of the three gunas. Patanjali too says that there will be conflict among the tendencies of the three gunas, from which sorrows come. The Devi herself tells us that an opposing force will surely come, and then I come as the force that confronts it. Problems like Madhu and Kaitabha will come, Mahishasura will come, Chanda and Munda will come, Shumbha and Nishumbha will come, problems like Raktabija will keep coming, messengers like Sugriva will come, all kinds of asuras will come.

So will they let the simple person who eats dal and rice, who eats fruit, live? Did they let the simple rishis and munis live in peace? Not even in Rama's age. Even in Rama's age the asuras came, and Shri Rama was told to take up his bow and arrows and slay them.

To do non-violent things, you need people around you who are capable of force. I say this again and again. But who will those violent ones be? The violence that establishes non-violence is not called violence; it is called non-violence itself, and no sin attaches to it. The army that guards the border, the police, the watchmen, the security guards, all come under Mahakali; to protect everyone, they destroy the one who attacks. When they were needed in that age, will they not be needed in this age? For what did God make the white blood cells in your body? For protection. If you work by denying them, it will never work.

## Four Paths: Dakshina, Vama, Siddhanta and Kaula

Now my question. Two paths suit Kali, and two paths suit the other yugas. The first is the Dakshina Marga, which looks good on paper. The second, which seems its opposite, is the Vama Marga. Now each of the two has a higher state. The higher state of Dakshina is Siddhanta, the Vedantin who knows Vedanta. And above Vama is Kaula.

The shastra says that the worshipper of the Vama Marga is better than the worshipper of the Dakshina Marga. What does this mean? One who only rings the bell in the temple: "O God, may I get a job, may this happen, may that happen, may I find a partner, may that girl say yes, may I get married." Is this follower of the Dakshina Marga doing wrong? No. He is relying on fate; he will eat the peda prasad and wait. "My case is going on in court", and he cannot even kill a cockroach; he is a little frightened. This is a perfect follower of the Dakshina Marga. There is no problem in this; there are such people in the world too.

One step above them, the shastra says, the follower of the Vama Marga will always win. Who is he? The one who knows for certain which official to meet to buy a house, which form to fill quickly, whom to give what and where. Tell me honestly, who gets things quickly in Kali Yuga? It is a fact. I have taught this in the Shri Vidya camp too: he gets the result quickly.

In the camp on Venus and Rahu too you learnt that the asuric children have a special boon from Shiva and Parvati that they will become young quickly and will also get siddhis quickly, because Prakriti, that is, Parvati herself, gave them this boon. You read this in your own Bhagavata Purana. That is why the follower of the Vama Marga gets results quickly; this is certain.

But in what form is he described? He is an asura, he is a daitya, he could be a rakshasa, he could be a danava. These are synonyms of each other, parts of each other, which we will look at later. But he will get success quickly. Read in the Bhagavata Purana and in the Navagraha shastra the story of how Rahu became a deity; read the story of Shukra. He gets success quickly.

So you will say, "Is there no benefit at all to the poor Dakshina Marga? Then why was I doing so much puja in the temple? Acharya ji, why did you teach that honesty is the best policy?" This will be the next question.

## The Slow Train and the High-Speed Train

There is success in the Vama Marga, but we just said that the chance of falling in it is also great. In it the fall is quick. The Dakshina Marga is a slow train, very slow. The Vama Marga is a super-fast train, and it has no brakes either; remember this. It takes you there quickly, but it also crashes hard. One is so slow it never arrives, the other so fast it arrives and crashes. Now tell me, which is better?

I did not even say that the slow train will arrive slowly. It is so slow that it never reaches the destination; it is not even certain when it will leave the station. Forty years have passed and it is still moving at a micro-micro pace, as if its speed were tending to zero. And the other's speed is tending to infinity; it will certainly crash at the end, but it did reach the destination. If the goal is the destination, which of the two will you choose? "I must arrive, but in one piece or in two", who said that? The goal was to arrive. So certainly Vama is better, because at least it delivers.

Whether it is the asuric path or anything else, the jolt also comes quickly to him; he has to go to jail, Shani's punishment reaches him quickly. Through this there is a chance of his reforming quickly, or, after gaining success, of Valya becoming Valmiki, precisely because of his negative side.

## The Yoga of the Rajarshis

In the opening verses of the fourth chapter of the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita it is clearly said that this yoga was first known by the rajarshis, royal sages, not by the brahmins alone. A king is one in whom rajas is greater. It is said that sin arises from rajas: "kāma eṣa krodha eṣa rajoguṇasamudbhavaḥ". Yet in the tradition it is the king who understands first, that is, the rajarshi, who is also sattvic.

How many departments a king has to look after! He has to look after defence, culture, the spiritual people, taxes, medicine, foreign policy, income, his own health, and science too, and take scientists along. Problems from so many areas come to him, and only he can solve them. In rajas there is action, with action come problems, and when problems come, some quick solution has to be found. He has to reach the destination somehow; he has to find some solution.

> **imaṁ vivasvate yogaṁ proktavān aham avyayam**
> **vivasvān manave prāha manur ikṣvākave 'bravīt**
> "I taught this imperishable yoga to Vivasvan; Vivasvan told it to Manu, and Manu told it to Ikshvaku."
> (Bhagavad Gita 4.1)

The Lord said, "I taught this imperishable yoga to the Sun; the Sun told it to his son Vaivasvata Manu, and Manu told it to his son King Ikshvaku." Its meaning is: the Sun, that is, the Self; from the Self this yoga should descend into the mind, and through the mind this self-knowledge should descend to the senses, not the other way round.

> **evaṁ paramparāprāptam imaṁ rājarṣayo viduḥ**
> **sa kāleneha mahatā yogo naṣṭaḥ parantapa**
> "Thus handed down in succession, the royal sages knew this yoga; but through long lapse of time this yoga was lost to the world, O scorcher of foes."
> (Bhagavad Gita 4.2)

"O Arjuna, scorcher of foes, the rajarshis knew this yoga received thus through the tradition, but after that, through a long time, this yoga became almost lost on this earth." In the original Sanskrit it says "rājarṣayo viduḥ"; this is the key word. They are rishis, but rajarshis, in whom there is the power of rajas. This yoga becomes lost at the end of an age, and Shri Krishna comes at that very time.

> **sa evāyaṁ mayā te 'dya yogaḥ proktaḥ purātanaḥ**
> **bhakto 'si me sakhā ceti rahasyaṁ hy etad uttamam**
> "That same ancient yoga I have told you today, for you are my devotee and friend; this is indeed the supreme secret."
> (Bhagavad Gita 4.3)

"You are my devotee and dear friend, therefore I have told you today this same ancient yoga, for it is a supreme secret", that is, a subject fit to be kept hidden.

In just the same way this yoga had become lost once again, and now the period of the change of yuga has come. You are friends, you are devotees, so a guru has come to explain this secret once again, one who understands this cunning statecraft, to whom tantra is known, in whose horoscope such a crooked yoga can exist, and who knows which way to go.

That is why Shri Krishna again and again points to the power of rajas and says, "Rise, act." It is not only about sitting in meditation. To understand yoga, one first has to become a rajarshi. Where is your rajas? And do not talk about Satya Yuga right now. We have to go to Satya Yuga, but today we are in Kali Yuga. We have to go from Kali towards Satya, and only one who lives rightly in Kali will be able to reach Satya. So in Kali, which of these two is greater? Dakshina or Vama? According to the principle I gave, Vama.

## Siddhis: To Be Shunned, or Divine?

Now the next question. In Vama, tantra is used; there will be quick methods, there will be siddhis, spiritual siddhis. In it too there are three kinds of tantra: sattvic, rajasic and tamasic. There will be practices like marana, mohana, vashikarana, uchchatana and stambhana, and good siddhis too.

Sant Dnyaneshwar Mauli made a wall move; was it then that he won, or did people just let him win? "Bring a certificate of purity!" Did he sit with a book, ringing a bell, saying, "O Lord, I am a good sannyasi, I am pure, please do something for me"? Would his work have been done by that? He quietly showed a miracle through tantra, and then everyone accepted. "There is Atman in all", so will the Vedas come out of this buffalo's mouth? He had siddhis; only then could he do it and show it. Nothing happens merely by pleading. He too used it.

I meet many gurus of our own dharma who, especially in Kali Yuga, tell me this principle: "Oh, why talk of siddhis? Siddhis are bad, they always bring harm. The shastras say so, the gurus say so, Ramakrishna Paramahamsa said that siddhis are like excrement; throw them away." With full respect to all those gurus, they did write this; I know that too. But in what context it was written is important. Maharshi Patanjali, in the fifty-first sutra of the third chapter of the Yoga Sutras, the Vibhuti Pada, explains that these siddhis can cause a fall and should be renounced like poison.

I met many gurus. When I said that in my inner vision I see this and this, I see siddhis, they said, "You are of a very low level. We have no siddhis at all; we never took that path." I said, "You are gurus of the Dakshina Marga." This is an incident that really happened, and you should know where this discussion went.

I told them that every one of our avatars had siddhis. What Shri Krishna did, bringing dusk in the middle of the day, bringing shade and sun, what was that? Was it not a siddhi? Sai Baba lit lamps with water; what was that? Today the same is done with technology: hydrogen is extracted from water and a lamp is lit. The value is not the same, but someone applied his intellect and did the same work that Sai Baba once did. He did it with the tantra of the Agama shastra, and this one showed the same siddhi with the yantra of Kali. So are Sai Baba and the water-powered lamp sold on the roadside of the same state? The siddhi is similar; this one too was able to do it, and something more will come in future. But the difference is that this one began to sell it, did it for commercial work; it became genuine vama tantra. And he used it only once.

So I told them that Hanumanji is called "the giver of the eight siddhis and nine treasures". When he went to Lanka, he made a seat of his raised tail; he went into a crocodile's mouth and came out; he entered in a tiny form. What is all this? Anima, laghima, garima, prapti, prakamya: these are the siddhis. So how did they become wrong? Are Hanumanji and all the gods wrong? You must have seen in television serials how, by uttering a mantra, they released one arrow and it became fifty. That happened through siddhi, through tantra. If all this is wrong, then you are calling God wrong.

Then I told them that today someone has made a guided missile with technology. Sitting before a computer, one can send it wherever one wants; it keeps following its target across the country, across the world. It is launched by a rocket and breaks into pieces that fall as fifty bombs. Is this not the same siddhi of today? Someone did it with the yantra of Kali, they did it with spiritual tantra, but the work done was the same. The difference is of purpose. But both became successful. In an ancient battle, if one arrow came from one side and twenty-five from the other, who would win? The one with twenty-five. It will be the same today.

At last I reminded them, "Maharaj ji, if you are still not convinced, look at the Navadurga. In the Navadurga the highest, ninth, state is called Siddhidatri. That form of the Devi is the highest in which she gives siddhi itself to others. So how did siddhis become wrong?" The highest form of the Devi is one where siddhi can be given to disciples. The problem is that you do not know how to handle it, so it is said, throw it away, it is bad.

I do not want to persuade anyone; I only want to convey the message: the question is for what purpose you are using it. An ordinary person cannot handle it; he is a disciple of the Dakshina Marga, and that is why they say so. If you meet such gurus, tell them humbly, "Maharaj ji, your path is different, ours is different. We will both meet in Vaikuntha. You go by your path, we go by ours. We are in Kali Yuga." Let each walk the path that suits him. In Vama there is fire, there is guru-tattva, there is more power. If you can handle it, it is right for you. If you cannot, the peaceful path is right for you.

## Bhairava Tantra: The Power of Fearlessness

Now the question is that whether the path is Vama or Dakshina, if tantra is to be used, tantra has five means. To explain them there is a shastra, the Bhairava Tantra.

Bhairava is the fearsome form of Lord Shiva that creates fear, and his shakti is Bhairavi. In the whole tantra of the ten Mahavidyas there is one power, the Bhairavi shakti, in which the opponent, just by seeing your radiance, becomes afraid before fighting. This is necessary. It is said of Kali Yuga that people will slap you and walk away, will rob you. So in Kali Yuga, whoever has more Bhairavi shakti, more Bhairava Tantra, will be fearless, abhaya, and will be troubled less.

Such a person will find people ready to give their lives for him. People will be ready to work for him without pay. This same power can make martyrs like Bhagat Singh, and in the opposite direction, suicide terrorists. In Kali Yuga such a person is said to be needed more. It is not said that the peaceful person does not attain liberation; he will too. But in Kali Yuga the importance of Shivaji Maharaj will be greater. Merely spinning the charkha and asking will bring less benefit; more benefit will come from the one who answers the opponent's weapon with an equal weapon. This is Shiva's description. That is why we are suffering. First understand the root cause: do what ought to be done in the age you are in.

## The Panchamakara: The Five Means of Tantra

The means of tantra are the Panchamakara, the five elements beginning with "ma", through which any tantra or shastra becomes successful. Since it is the Vama Marga, its means also seem inverted.

The first is **madya**, wine. It intoxicates and shuts down the intellect. The second is **mamsa**, meat, which comes only after a killing. The third is **matsya**, fish. The fourth is **mudra**. Mudras are the gestures made with the hands, like the jnana mudra. They contain the symbols of the five great elements; through them energy flows, everything is regulated, prana is balanced. This is not acupressure or acupuncture. It is called a secret knowledge; very few gurus know which mudra is to be connected with which bija mantra. Mudras too are a secret knowledge of tantra. And the fifth is **maithuna**, the union of man and woman.

To hear them, they sound entirely like the Vama Marga: madya, mamsa, matsya, mudra and maithuna.

## The Glory of the Bhairava Principle

The Bhairava Tantra describes the glory of the Bhairava principle. It is said for Kali Yuga that among all the tantras, whoever attains the Bhairava Tantra, the Rudra form of Shiva, will have such radiance even from his spirituality that people will fear him. Shri Krishna says that whatever is glorious, radiant and powerful, know it to be a manifestation of a portion of me. So become that powerful. Kali Yuga is the age of Mahakali. The more strength you have within, spiritual, intellectual, mental or physical, the more people will fear to face you. Become so powerful that no one even comes in your way.

If a daughter-in-law is weak, she will be oppressed all her life. Her life will be sorrowful, and a sorrowful mind cannot be joyful. Without joy there is no Sat-Chit-Ananda, and without Sat-Chit-Ananda, how will there be liberation? But if she has so much radiance within that the mother-in-law cannot even dare to brand her with hot tongs, then she should have that much radiance. This knowledge is to be taken not for fighting, but so that I remain so powerful that my boss does not oppress me, that my army has such strength that no foreigner can raise his eyes towards me. This is said to be essential in Kali Yuga. Whoever does not have Bhairava vidya, no tantra of his will work.

How were the ancient rishis and munis? They took water from their kamandalu, and that was their tantric knowledge, their weapon, from which the result came in a moment. That is why they were not afraid; they had brahmatej, the radiance of Brahman. In no age did our gurus get beaten. I am telling you from our own texts. Shiva told this to Parvati, it is given in our Puranas, it is written in our Vedanta, how to live in Kali. Bhairava shakti comes from the Bhairava Tantra.

The Bhairava principle is a helping principle, not a destroying one. In life the Bhairava principle is very useful. Without it no one can do anything alone, whether a guru, a siddha, or a follower of any path, Dakshina or Vama. The Bhairava principle means a friend, a helper, a servant, a disciple in your life, someone who believes only in you, listens only to you, wishes only your good. Him you can call your Bhairava.

The guru on whom Bhairava's grace rests gets devoted disciples. This grace rests abundantly on your guru; that is why I am teaching this; now is the state of Siddhidatri, and I am giving it to you. That is why in our Gurukul you see disciples who can stay day and night, who come at a single call to work together, who come and stay for three days at a time and do not even think about money. For their numbers to grow, fortune is needed too; that time will come.

The commander or king on whom Bhairava's grace rests gets soldiers ready to sacrifice their lives. Shivaji Maharaj had the Bhairava Tantra; that is why he worshipped Jagadamba, not only Lakshmi, but the fierce form. Before the Mahabharata war Arjuna was told to praise Durga, also in her fierce form. Shri Rama too had to do the sadhana of Devi Durga when going to fight Ravana, and only then could he fight with the vanara army.

The leader on whom Bhairava's grace rests gets devoted followers and workers. The officer on whom Bhairava's grace rests is always obeyed by his staff. The person on whom Bhairava's grace rests has friends ready to do anything for him. In the same way, a disciple endowed with the Bhairava principle lays down his life for his guru. Bhairava is a principle that is ready to do anything only for you.

When Sati burned herself in Daksha's yajna, Virabhadra arose from Shiva's anger. He was Bhairava himself. Virabhadra fought Vishnu and the other gods only for Shiva. Kala Bhairava cut off Brahma's fifth head for Shiva alone. The Bhairava principle is in soldiers too, which is why they sacrifice themselves for the country without looking to personal gain. Because of this principle, revolutionaries mount the gallows smiling. The Bhairava principle is not selfish. For its master, for its ideals, for its country and state, it can go to any length. It is your protector, and in Kali Yuga protection will be greatly needed. Along with the spiritual Bhairava principle, the principle of Kali's yantra will also be needed. This is not the work of those who sit quietly at home.

As long as Bhairava's grace is upon you, no one can harm a hair on your head. And for two thousand years harm kept coming to us. First accept this; then the guru has come to show the way forward. From time to time some people came who taught the invaders a lesson. They had twice their Bhairava shakti, and that is why the invaders were afraid. What did the Mughals say? "If you must have an enemy, let him be like Shiva." They feared Shivaji. Why? Because Bhairava was in him. He did not sit merely ringing bells.

The Bhairava principle destroys your enemy. Shiva is present with all the goddesses in the form of Bhairava. Here the Shiva principle becomes the helper of the Shakti principle; remember this. Because of Bhairava's grace, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj and Maharana Pratap found countless heroes ready to die for them. Because of this grace, at a single call from Lokmanya Tilak, Netaji Subhas Chandra Bose and Gandhiji, people were ready to sacrifice themselves for the country. If there is such a person in your life who is ready to do anything for you, understand that Bhairava's grace is upon you.

Why is the dog Bhairava's vehicle? Because the Bhairava principle is believed to be in the dog, which is why it is loyal to its master. That is why it is said that feeding a black dog a roti averts many obstacles. A dog even gives its life for its master. The Bhairava principle is like this too: loyal to you, obeying your every command, falling upon your enemies and opponents, your protector, close to you like your shadow, ready to have its head cut off and give its life for you, and capable of finishing your enemy.

If you want such a person in your life in any form; if you want to be free of obstacles from enemies; if someone always troubles you and you can do nothing against him; if you are caught in a problem where no way is visible and no one is ready to help; if you consider yourself very weak and helpless; if you want some invisible power always to protect you, then do the Bhairava sadhana and be free of worry. By Bhairava's grace, spoiled tasks begin to set themselves right on their own. Help comes from somewhere, in an unknown form. "yoga-kṣemaṁ vahāmy aham", this is how the effect of tantra comes.

One who does not listen to you will begin to listen. Your enemies will be defeated. The Bhairava sadhana also removes the afflictions of Rahu and Shani, because the guru of these planets is Shukra, the guru of the daityas. If some tantric act, some black magic, has been done on you, the Bhairava sadhana frees you from it, which no other remedy does. Someone is not returning your money; he will return it. Someone torments you; he will kneel before you. If someone's drinking has to be stopped, if servants do not stay in your home, if a family member troubles you without reason, if someone at the office gives you trouble, try this sadhana too.

If you want to become a good disciple, you must do this Bhairava sadhana in your life. Remember, without the Bhairava principle, the principle of discipleship has no value. If the mahadasha of Rahu is running, if there is any obstacle in life, do it with faith, and the fruit will surely come. The Bhairava sadhana has been prevalent in India since very ancient times and is highly regarded in the field of tantra. Every one of our great beings and avatars has used it. This sadhana can be done by all three methods, sattvic, rajasic and tamasic. If you are a householder, adopt the sattvic method.

You may have seen that wine is offered to Bhairava. The Kala Bhairava temple of Ujjain, of the one who is the very form of time, is famous; there wine is offered to Bhairava, which he drinks daily. Where the wine offered there for thousands of years goes, no one knows.

Along with the ten Mahavidyas there are also ten Bhairavis: Patala Bhairavi, Shmashana Bhairavi, Tripura Bhairavi and others. The "Bhairavi Sadhana Vivechan" says that according to the tantric view, five chakras are mentioned symbolically in the Rigveda, and one of them is the Bhairavi Chakra. The Bhairavi Chakra too is of two kinds, the Chinachara Chakra and the Shaiva Mata Chakra. Following these five chakras, the Panchamakaras were added. In the Vama Marga, the path of the seeker's progress was paved through madya, meena, mamsa, mudra and maithuna. In it siddhis were sought through the power of tantra, mantra and yantra, Bhairavi sadhana was given a special place, and the seeker was even permitted the companionship of a Bhairavi. That is why, in the context of the Durga Saptashati too, there is a tradition of offering these things to the Devi.

## The Tamasic Panchamakara

Bhairava has been called necessary in Kali Yuga, so he must have been necessary in the earlier yugas too. Shri Rama too needed him. One who is on the genuine Vama Marga will need it, and so will the follower of the Dakshina Marga or one higher than him. But it must be understood properly, because you will say, "Oh, what is he teaching?" It is given in the shastra, so it must be understood. If Sanatana Dharma is to be established, if we are to become Vishwaguru, then if the one in whose horoscope Vishwaguru is written does not teach it, who will? There is a prophecy that all the others too will have to listen to this.

Tantra is of three kinds: tamasic, rajasic and sattvic. First let us understand the tamasic, in which the direct meanings of these five are taken.

Those who are terrorists, who do negative deeds, who are rapists, are usually seen drinking, eating meat and visiting prostitutes. What is the secret of this? When someone drinks, first of all his intellect shuts down. The bodily senses keep working, but the thought that was placed in the mind earlier is made firm by the drink. If a thought of rape, of revenge, of killing someone has come into someone's mind, then to put that thought into action the intellect has to be shut down, and for that drink is needed.

Look at the history of the terrorist attack on Mumbai. Those terrorists first went into the hotel and drank their fill there. Unless there is the energy of tamas within, these Bhairavas cannot create terror at all. Such people are seen going only towards dance bars and brothels. There is a reason for this. The energy of the women there, which is spoken of in maithuna, and of those who are drawn to them, is not low but high, but tamasically high, extremely negative. The power of so many negative people is drawn towards him and comes to him as sin. In the brahmacharya camp you have learnt the principle of kama according to the shastra: woman is a receiver, she receives the refuse from all sides and holds it. When union takes place, that tamasic power increases further through the exchange. Without this tamas such people could not do that work at all. ## The Sattvic Panchamakara

Now which knowledge should we learn? If we learn their knowledge, what is the benefit? Will a Vishwaguru teach you to start drinking wine and eating meat from tomorrow? Then why make you give up so much? Did Shivaji Maharaj drink wine? What is offered to Bhairava is symbolic.

Now listen carefully to the sattvic, which is the real fruit of sadhana.

**Madya:** First it is said, drink soma rasa. Soma rasa means the nectar of devotion: so much nectar of devotion, so much nectar of the guru's sadhana, that it can give exactly the same state. In the nectar of devotion to the nation, devotion to the country, devotion to the guru, the sattvic Bhairava is born. The sattvic Bhairava drinks such madya. The problem is that we are not drinking enough of this madya either, though it is greatly needed. Shivaji Maharaj had it, for the nation, towards his guru. Chandragupta had it. The vanara army had it, the Mavalas had it. This too will do exactly the same work. If you want to become a sattvic Bhairava, drink this first makara deeply. When that passion comes, no one can harm a hair on your head. Once the thought has come that I must live for the nation, work for the nation, I must fight, whether against my Sade Sati, or for the nation, or against the unrighteous; such devotion to the guru, such surrender that if the guru says "rise", he rises, and if he says "sit", he sits; the one who is intoxicated with this becomes a sattvic Bhairava.

**Mamsa:** The second is mamsa, meat, in which there is violence. The Vedas and the Manusmriti give the derivation of the word mamsa:

> **māṁ sa bhakṣayitāmutra yasya māṁsam ihādmy aham**
> **etan māṁsasya māṁsatvaṁ pravadanti manīṣiṇaḥ**
> "He ('sa') whose flesh I eat in this life will eat me ('mām') in the next; this, the wise declare, is the meat-ness of meat."
> (Manusmriti 5.55)

This is the definition of the word mamsa. According to the bond of the gunas this cycle keeps going. Enjoy as much as you like today; tomorrow it will surely kill you, and until then you will have to keep taking birth. Eat thousands, hundreds of thousands of fish in a lifetime, and that many births remain, because you cannot die ten times in one birth; in each birth one of them will come to bite you.

So if a sattvic person also wants Bhairava shakti, it is said that first of all, if brahmahatya, the killing of a brahmin, happens, it happens through the tongue. With the tongue one can kill someone by tormenting him again and again. So the first is the vow of silence. Silence does not mean I will not speak at all; it means I will control my speech a thousand times, I will kill no one with it. And remaining in such a state, quietly, being ready to offer one's whole body, one's flesh: "I offer myself completely to the country, to the guru." This is called atma-nivedana, self-offering. For the sattvic Bhairava this is the means of mamsa, and one who does this also attains Bhairava siddhi.

**Matsya:** The third is matsya. Fish gives restlessness, makes one do opposite things, takes one to extremes. In the sattvic sense, matsya is the inhalation and exhalation of prana, and the prana moving up and down in the river-like nadis called ida, pingala and sushumna. That is the fish. From it comes the power to rise above cold and heat, gain and loss, victory and defeat, honour and dishonour. For the sattvic person this is matsya, and its effect is exactly the same. Now he can hold both extremes. He can love, and if needed, he can also strike.

If I can move prana in such a way that the act of desire is going on below but the attention is above, then it can be kama according to the shastra. Wealth is coming from the Svadhishthana, but I am able to distribute it while doing the guru's work. I am loving from the heart, but if needed I can also strike. Mahishasura will be slain from this state of matsya. This matsya is in the river within me. The outer fish also has the same effect, but the tendency is tamasic, because there is violence in it. We are told to practise with the inner fish. This is not my interpretation; it is Shiva's own, already defined, and it comes only through the guru-disciple tradition.

**Maithuna:** Tamasic maithuna is the union of man and woman from which more tamas comes within. The sattvic person also needs protection, needs the energy of Kali. So here maithuna means the union of Purusha and Prakriti, the Ardhanarishvara state: understanding the diagram of Purusha and Prakriti properly, understanding the formed and the formless together, having the knowledge of non-duality. One who has this has accomplished maithuna, that is, the purpose of the union of man and woman has been fulfilled. This power too will give the same effect, but this time sattvic.

There is no problem in performing the act of desire according to the shastra either. In it the woman is the form of the Devi and the man is Shiva. Both gain merit from that act, not sin. This is one form. Wealth too is Prakriti. I spoke of the physical woman because I was speaking of the Muladhara, but wealth too is Prakriti. The union of wealth and me is also maithuna: I am Shiva, this is Shakti; am I making this union while honouring her? Power too is Prakriti, and my union with it is also the Ardhanarishvara state. To uplift everything here through this Prakriti, and without getting stuck in it, remaining free through dispassion, this is called maithuna. Even if the physical act is according to the shastra, sattvic power should come into it, not the reverse, tamasic power. This balance with wealth and power can also give Bhairava shakti.

The Ardhanarishvara state means that within me there is both the masculine and the feminine. There should be enough kshatriya in me to point a gun at someone's head, and enough brahmin to let him go after frightening him. He should be so frightened that he does not come to attack again. But if it is not necessary, no blood need be shed. We are against killing.

## Sanatana Dharma: Do Not Frighten, Do Not Fear

For a serpent it is not necessary to bite, but it is necessary to hiss. This is Sanatana Dharma. It says, if necessary we will strike too. Do not frighten anyone, but do not fear anyone either. And if someone frightens you, go and frighten him twice as much. This is what our real Sanatana Dharma says. ## The Fourth Path: Kaula, the Father Even of Vama

It is called Kaula.

Here is an important point. The root word of Kaula is "kula". "Ku" signifies the earth element, that is, the Muladhara chakra. "Kula" means the seat. "Ku" is that power, and "la" is laya, dissolution, where it dissolves. Prakriti is not only in the Muladhara; it extends from the Muladhara upwards. Wherever it rose and dissolved, for some from Muladhara up to the heart, for some up to the Svadhishthana, for some it stayed in the Muladhara itself, for some it reached all the way up, that Kundalini shakti is called "Kula-Kundalini".

From this same word comes the Kuladevata, the family deity. We had kula, gotra and varna; there was no caste. Kula means which kula your family belongs to, that is, how high a state it has reached from the Muladhara, and according to that it has a goddess. For example, Renuka Mata is the goddess of the heart chakra, so in that kula there will be more healers. If by good fortune you have come to such a guru, hold on to him firmly; do not let go, even unto laying down your life. People will speak against him: "Your guru is like this and like that; he comes wearing a T-shirt." They will try to drive you away, to break you. You will have to drive them away: "I have Bhairava shakti; my guru gave it to me; I know what he is." Tomorrow someone will say that your guru has affairs, this and that. Their intellect will go only as far as artha and kama, wealth and desire. That is how people are broken. "See how much dharma he does, how much liberation he has attained": no one says this. When an enemy is to be broken, there is a strategy: break them. Till today they have kept breaking us. They will break seekers too, and our countrymen too. Atheists and tamasic people will break us again. Do not break.

This time say, "We know our guru, what he is like, what he is." They will talk only about these two: "He takes money, he is commercial", and "He is lustful, he has affairs." Apart from this, what else do they have? Till today, what other accusation has been made against any guru? Not even the greatest gurus were spared: not the Buddha, not Vivekananda, no one. It came to Eknath Maharaj too, and to Tukaram Maharaj. Everyone has to go through it. But their disciples did not waver, because there was Bhairava shakti in them. Such shakti should come into you.

What happens when someone speaks against your mother? If someone says a little about your father, it is not tolerated, though sometimes we let it go. But if someone says something about your mother even by mistake, you grab him by the collar. If someone says, "Your mother is like that", do you first go to your mother and ask, "Mother, are you like that? The world is saying so"? No. First you silence him, and then, if there is any doubt, you may ask your mother, "Mother, tell me the truth." This is accepted. So one who understands the mother understands the Guru Mauli, the guru as mother; he will understand Bharat Mata, and he will understand how India will become Vishwaguru. So when someone speaks about the Guru Mauli, answer there first, and ask here later.

Whichever guru you are with, people will speak against your guru. Then never break. This is Bhairava shakti. Remember, in Kali Yuga there will be a great deal of breaking and tearing apart. Do not break. At the right place, firmly, drink the madya of the nectar of devotion; offer such mamsa, that is, the sacrifice of self; practise such matsya as can bear even opposites; such maithuna, in which Shiva and Shakti unite, Purusha and Prakriti are one; and such mudras as become akarma and leela. "What I do, you do not see, and what you see, I do not do": these are my mudras.
