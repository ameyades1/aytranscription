## 1. Summary

A long discourse (video uploaded 2026-01-26, 2:39:23) in which Acharya Ji argues that each age has its own means: personal liberation is possible on any path, but to spread a message in Kaliyuga one needs the Agamas (tantra), the Venus principle and Bhairava Shakti. Along the way he explains the Kularnava verse on Shruti, Smriti, Purana and Agama, the four paths (Dakshina, Vama, Siddhanta, Kaula), the three-level reading of the Panchamakara, siddhis, and why the coming years decide whether India becomes Vishwaguru again. The main intent is informational ("kaliyug me kaun sa marg", "satyug vs kaliyug", "dakshin marg vam marg", "panchamakara meaning"), with a strong navigational pull from the YouTube title "Stop Following Satyug Rules in Kaliyug". Head terms such as "satyug vs kaliyug" are crowded with thin listicles on the yugas; the realistic opportunity is the narrower question this talk alone answers well: which spiritual path works in Kaliyuga, and why. The tantric material (Vama, Panchamakara) is high-curiosity but easy to sensationalise, so the SEO copy leads with the sattvic reading the blog itself stresses (flags 1 and 2).

## 6. FAQ

For the page or for People-also-ask targeting; answers come only from the talk. Not for FAQPage schema (see section 8).

1. **क्या कलियुग में सत्ययुग के मार्ग से मोक्ष नहीं मिल सकता? / Can one not attain liberation in Kaliyuga by the path of Satyuga?**
   मिल सकता है; व्यक्तिगत मोक्ष किसी युग का मोहताज नहीं। युग का नियम संसार में विस्तार के लिए लागू होता है। / It can; personal liberation does not depend on any age. The rule of the age applies to expanding in the world.
2. **किस युग में कौन सा शास्त्र प्रधान है? / Which scripture leads in which age?**
   कुलार्णव तंत्र के उद्धृत श्लोक के अनुसार सत्ययुग में श्रुति, त्रेता में स्मृति, द्वापर में पुराण और कलियुग में आगम। / Per the verse quoted from the Kularnava Tantra: Shruti in Satyuga, Smriti in Treta, the Puranas in Dvapara and the Agamas in Kaliyuga.
3. **दक्षिण, वाम, सिद्धांत और कौल मार्ग क्या हैं? / What are the Dakshina, Vama, Siddhanta and Kaula paths?**
   साधना के चार स्तर: दक्षिण सीधा-सादा प्रार्थना का मार्ग, वाम शीघ्र परिणाम वाला, सिद्धांत शांत और निवृत्ति वाला, और कौल वह अवतारी स्थिति जो ऊपर जाकर फिर नीचे लौट आती है। / Four levels of sadhana: Dakshina, the simple path of prayer; Vama, the path of quick results; Siddhanta, calm and withdrawn; and Kaula, the avatar's state that rises to the top and returns.
4. **पंचमकार का सात्विक अर्थ क्या है? / What is the sattvic meaning of the Panchamakara?**
   भक्ति का रस, आत्मनिवेदन, प्राण का संतुलन, अर्धनारीश्वर स्थिति और सहज लीला। / The nectar of devotion, self-offering, balanced prana, the Ardhanarishvara state and effortless divine play.
5. **मेरे लिए कौन सा मार्ग सही है? / Which path is right for me?**
   यह निर्णय स्वयं मत लीजिए, गुरु से पूछिए। / Do not decide this yourself; ask the Guru.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Panchamakara and Vama marga invite sensational traffic.** The blog lists madya, mamsa, matsya, mudra and maithuna and calls the tamasic level dangerous; its point is the sattvic reading. Recommendation: never put the literal five Ms, "vama marga practice" or "tantra for power/wealth" in titles, meta, slugs or social cards (done); the FAQ gives only the sattvic meaning.
2. **"Vama is better" out of context.** The blog says Vama is better than Dakshina only for reaching the goal, then says at once that it is not safe and that the path should be chosen by asking the Guru. Recommendation: no snippet or social quote should lift "वाम बेहतर है" / "the Vama path is better" on its own; the direct answer paragraph ends with "ask the Guru" (done).
3. **Claim about other religions and the Bhavishya Purana.** Unsourced and could offend (Sanskrit review, item 2). Recommendation: keep it out of all SEO copy (done) and apply the review's wording that marks it as Acharya Ji's own view.
4. **Gita 3.26 meaning and the Hindi H2.** अज्ञानाम् and युक्तः are dropped, and the Hindi heading can read as the reverse of the verse (review, item 1). This verse is a natural snippet target; do not use it in SEO copy until fixed.
5. **Attributions presented as fact.** "कालाय तस्मै नमः" is Bhartrihari, not scripture (item 3); the Kularnava verse has no location (item 4); the Shankaracharya statement has no known source (item 9); "Bhairava Tantra" is a class of texts (item 7); the Shukra verse is from the Navagraha Stotra (item 8). Recommendation: the FAQ cites the Kularnava verse as "quoted from" and no SEO copy uses the Shankaracharya or Bhartrihari lines; the editor applies the review's corrections.
6. **Translation details:** Manusmriti "today/tomorrow" for इह/अमुत्र (item 5), mudra as "gesture" instead of parched grain (item 6), Gita 18.78 "righteousness" for नीति (item 16), Parikshit retelling (item 10), सात्विक / सत्त्व spellings (item 12), साम-दाम (item 13). Not used in SEO copy; fix in the text per the review. The seo.json links use the blog's current spelling "सात्विक" and must be updated if the text changes to "सात्त्विक" (see flag 10).
7. **Teaching readings, not derivations.** Agama, kula and rajarshi etymologies are Acharya Ji's nirukti (items 14, 15). The FAQ and direct answer avoid them.
8. **Key Takeaways adds Vedanta for women** that the body does not say (item 11). The SEO copy does not repeat it.
9. **Clickbait in the video packaging.** The thumbnail reads "The Truth Gurus Never Told YOU" and the title "God Has Given YOU a Different Path". Recommendation: do not reuse that phrasing in the blog title or meta (done); for YouTube, consider "Which Spiritual Path Works in Kaliyug? | Acharya Upendra Ji".
10. **Linked phrases depend on the current text.** If the editor applies the review's spelling fix (सात्विक → सात्त्विक), the link phrase "यह साधन सात्विक हाथों में होना चाहिए" must change too, or the page generator will report it not found.
11. **Sensitive dated and violent content.** The 2029 to 2034 window and the remarks about Mumbai attackers, guns and "violence that establishes non-violence" are kept out of all SEO copy; they are the speaker's views in context and would mislead as headlines.
