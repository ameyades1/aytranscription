*Do you work to become happy, or from happiness?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that work done only for a return ends in suffering. A painter paints out of joy, and money follows; when your work feels like play, it becomes Karma Yoga.

💭 _Is there anything in your day that feels like play?_
