## 1. Summary

A short talk on how the three gunas produce action. Everything (places, people, objects) is divided according to sattva, rajas and tamas; at any moment one guna dominates a person, and every place and object has its own guna too. Action arises when the inner guna meets the outer one, which is what Gita 3.27 points to: all actions are done by the gunas of prakriti, yet the ego-deluded person thinks "I am the doer". Acharya Ji illustrates this with a rajasic person on a Goa beach (the suit becomes a floral silk shirt), a tamasic liquor den (the sattvic person leaves, the tamasic one rushes in) and a saint in kaivalya who is untouched. The outer nature cannot be changed, so changing one's own gunas is one's duty; one who dies believing "I am the doer" becomes a pitar. The main intent is **informational / self-understanding** ("why do I behave this way", "how gunas affect behaviour", "कर्म कौन करवाता है"). Ranking pages on the gunas are mostly definition lists and diet/personality-type pages; few explain the *meeting* of inner and outer gunas with everyday examples, which is this post's angle. The pure verse query (Gita 3.27 meaning, "who is the doer") belongs to `who_real_doer_karma_ego_bhagavad`, which explains the same verse step by step (see section 7, cannibalization). Evergreen; no festival hook.

## 6. FAQ

For the report only (not added to the page and not marked up as FAQPage).

1. **त्रिगुण क्या हैं? / What are the three gunas?**
   सत्व (शुद्धता और शांति), रज (गति और भोग की चाह) और तम (जड़ता और अंधकार)। संसार के स्थान, व्यक्ति और वस्तुएँ इन्हीं के अनुसार बँटे हैं। / Sattva (purity and calm), rajas (restless activity and craving for enjoyment) and tamas (inertia and darkness); every place, person and object is shaped by them.
2. **कर्म कौन करवाता है? / Who really makes us act?**
   गीता ३.२७ के अनुसार सारे कर्म प्रकृति के गुणों द्वारा होते हैं; आचार्य जी समझाते हैं कि भीतर का गुण और बाहर का गुण मिलकर कर्म बनाते हैं। / Gita 3.27 says all actions are done by the gunas of nature; Acharya Ji explains that the inner guna meeting the outer guna produces the action.
3. **एक ही व्यक्ति अलग जगहों पर अलग व्यवहार क्यों करता है? / Why does the same person behave differently in different places?**
   क्योंकि हर स्थान का अपना गुण है। राजसिक गोवा तट पर राजसिक व्यक्ति सूट छोड़कर रंगीन कमीज़ पहन लेता है; तामसिक मदिरालय से सात्विक व्यक्ति लौट जाता है। / Because every place has its own guna: on a rajasic Goa beach a rajasic executive swaps his suit for a floral shirt, while a sattvic person walks out of a tamasic liquor den.
4. **क्या संत पर भी वातावरण का असर होता है? / Does the environment affect a saint?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार कैवल्य तक पहुँचे संत पर वातावरण का कोई फर्क नहीं पड़ता। / According to Acharya Ji, surroundings make no difference to a saint who has reached kaivalya.
5. **हमारे हाथ में क्या है? / What is in our control?**
   बाहर की प्रकृति नहीं, पर अपने गुण बदलना हमारा कर्तव्य है; वरना वातावरण ही हमसे कर्म करवाता रहेगा। / Not the nature outside, but changing our own gunas is our duty; otherwise our surroundings keep making us act.

## 10. Authenticity flags

From the Sanskrit review (`tools/review/three_gunas_karma.md`, 7 minor issues); none is fixed in the blog yet.
1. **Gita 3.27 attached to the speaker's wording (Minor).** The takeaway puts "(गीता ३.२७)" after "inner guna meets outer guna", which is Acharya Ji's application, not the verse. Recommendation: apply the review's correction. The meta descriptions cite 3.27 next to the question "do you act?", which the verse does support; the direct answers and FAQ 2 separate the verse from Acharya Ji's explanation.
2. **"falls under one of them" (English, Minor).** Every thing is made of all three gunas with one dominant (Gita 14.10). The first English link anchor uses this sentence as it stands; if the blog is corrected, update the anchor in seo.json. FAQ 1 already uses "shaped by them".
3. **सत्व vs सत्त्व (Minor).** Gita Press spells सत्त्व/सात्त्विक. The secondary keyword "सत्व रज तम और व्यवहार" keeps the common search spelling; the about list uses "सत्त्व गुण".
4. **तम vs तमस heading (Minor).** Consistency only; no SEO impact.
5. **पितर unglossed in Hindi (Minor).** Consistency only.
6. **"Who dies believing 'I am the doer' becomes a pitar" (Minor).** No scripture states this in these words; it is Acharya Ji's teaching. Do not use it in titles, meta or snippets as a scriptural fact, and do not target pitru/ancestor queries with this post; the Also read box hands those readers to `true_meaning_pitru_rin`.
7. **IAST dandas (Minor).** Formatting only.

SEO-specific:
8. **"Predetermined" and free will.** The blog says even the saint's response is "पहले से तय" / "predetermined", but also says changing one's gunas is one's duty. Do not target fatalism queries ("is everything predetermined", "do we have free will") with this post; it does not answer them fully.
9. **Behaviour/psychology framing.** The video title uses "Understanding Human Behaviour"; the English keyword follows it, but the post is a teaching on the gunas, not psychology. Avoid personality-test or mental-health claims in snippets.
