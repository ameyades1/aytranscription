# Chapter: The Guru's Duty in an Age of Reason

## Body, Mind, Intellect and Soul

A person who thinks scientifically is a person of reason. And the scriptures declare the intellect to be higher than the mind. Lord Shri Krishna says the mind is higher than the body, the intellect is higher than the mind, and higher than all three is the soul.

> **indriyāṇi parāṇyāhur indriyebhyaḥ paraṁ manaḥ**
> **manasas tu parā buddhir yo buddheḥ paratas tu saḥ**
> "The senses are said to be higher than the body; higher than the senses is the mind; higher than the mind is the intellect; and that which is beyond even the intellect is the Self."
> (Bhagavad Gita 3.42)

So when an intellectual comes, a person who speaks the language of science, then according to Shri Krishna he is truly a person of a higher level.

## Who Will Answer the One Who Questions?

Today our temples are filled mostly with people of devotion. They are connected through the mind, through feeling, and that is all fine. But the person who asks questions at a higher level, the kind of questions I had as a child, needs someone of intellect, or someone beyond the intellect, to answer him. Such a one is called a realised master, one established at the level of the soul. A person of emotion alone cannot answer him, and so the questioner remains unconvinced.

## Kali Yuga: The Age of Intellect

And which age is this? Kali Yuga. What will Kali Yuga be? An age of machines, an age of intellect. That is why there are so many rationalists today. They are not wrong. The problem is that we cannot answer them.

They ask: how eight arms? How twelve arms? What does the elephant's trunk mean? What is the logic of it? If I do this worship, does it truly bear fruit? And we are unable to show the phalashruti, the promised fruit, of mantra sadhana. That is the problem, is it not?

**The fruit cannot be shown.** Your mantras have not been perfected, have not been awakened. Our very method went wrong. We never reached the level of the soul, never reached a true Guru. Only devotion goes on, and only action goes on, and that too at the level of body-consciousness.

## The Prophecy of Swami Chinmayananda

Swami Chinmayananda used to say that a time would come when rationalists would greatly increase, and then the people of devotion alone would not be able to answer them. Then, somewhere, the Guru would have to awaken and answer them in the language of science.

## Decoding the Scriptures

When I remembered this, we began decoding all our discourses, all our scriptures, this Durga Saptashati. Decoding means explaining it so that the person of today can understand.

Not "Lord Shiva said to Parvati," but my own soul said to my mind and intellect. If Ganapati does this, it means the discriminating intellect within you must awaken. In this language we then began writing our books.
