**English (≈55 words):** According to Acharya Upendra Ji, questioning is not a lack of faith. In the Bhagavad Gita (3.42) Shri Krishna places the mind above the senses, the intellect above the mind and the Atman beyond the intellect. Feeling alone cannot answer the rational seeker of Kali Yuga; the bridge between science and spirituality is a realised Guru who explains the scriptures from the level of the Atman, in the language of reason.

## 6. FAQ

For the page or WordPress only if the editor wants it; answers come from the talk.

**Hindi**
1. **क्या धर्म में प्रश्न पूछना अश्रद्धा है?** नहीं। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि प्रश्न पूछने वाला बुद्धिवादी व्यक्ति गलत नहीं, ऊँचे स्तर का है; गीता ३.४२ में बुद्धि को मन से श्रेष्ठ कहा गया है। समस्या प्रश्न में नहीं, उत्तर न दे पाने में है।
2. **केवल भक्ति से बुद्धिवादी को उत्तर क्यों नहीं मिलता?** भक्ति भाव से जुड़ी है और अच्छी है, पर ऊँचे स्तर के प्रश्नों का उत्तर वही दे सकता है जो बुद्धि से भी परे, आत्मा के स्तर पर स्थित साक्षात्कारी गुरु हो।
3. **मंत्र-साधना का फल क्यों नहीं दिखता?** आचार्य जी के अनुसार हमारी पद्धति चूक गई: हम आत्मा के स्तर तक, सच्चे गुरु तक नहीं पहुँचे, और भक्ति व कर्म शरीर-बोध के स्तर पर ही चलते रहे।
4. **शास्त्रों का डिकोडिंग क्या है?** शास्त्रों की कथाओं को आज की भाषा में, भीतर की कथा के रूप में समझाना: "शिवजी ने पार्वती से कहा" का अर्थ, मेरी आत्मा ने मेरे मन और बुद्धि से कहा।

**English**
1. **Is asking questions a lack of faith?** No. Acharya Ji says the rational person who asks questions is not wrong but stands on a higher level; Gita 3.42 places the intellect above the mind. The problem is not the question but our inability to answer it.
2. **Why can't devotion alone answer a rational seeker?** Devotion is a bond of feeling and is good, but higher questions need someone established beyond the intellect, at the level of the Atman: a realised Guru (sakshatkari).
3. **Why doesn't mantra sadhana seem to bear fruit?** In Acharya Ji's view our method went astray: we never reached the level of the Atman or a true Guru, and devotion and ritual stayed at the level of body-consciousness.
4. **What does decoding the scriptures mean?** Reading their stories as the story of your inner life: "Shiva said to Parvati" becomes "my own Atman spoke to my mind and intellect".

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Gita 3.42, Hindi meaning drops the senses** (Sanskrit review, major). The Hindi says "शरीर से मन श्रेष्ठ है", but the verse says the mind is above the senses (इन्द्रियेभ्यः परं मनः); the English is correct. Recommendation: use the review's correction ("इन्द्रियों को (स्थूल शरीर से) श्रेष्ठ कहते हैं, इन्द्रियों से श्रेष्ठ मन है, ..."). The direct answer and FAQ above already give the full sequence; the meta description cites 3.42 only for "intellect above mind", which is correct.
2. **Key takeaway on Gita 3.42** (Sanskrit review, minor). Both languages say "mind above body" with the reference attached. Recommendation: add the senses as the review suggests.
3. **IAST "paraṁ"** (Sanskrit review, minor). Standard IAST is "paraṃ". Editorial fix only; no SEO text depends on it.
4. **Swami Chinmayananda's saying is unverified** (Sanskrit review). It is correctly attributed to him as a saying, but the heading "A Prophecy That Came True" presents it as a fulfilled prophecy. Recommendation: do not use "prophecy" or his name as a hook in titles, meta, schema or social snippets; consider the softer heading in section 4.
5. **"Science" framing.** The talk does not offer scientific proof of scripture and does not answer the eight-arms or elephant-trunk questions it raises; it says the Guru must answer in the language of science and decode the stories inwardly. Recommendation: no title or snippet may promise "scientific proof", "science behind" a ritual, or answers to those questions. "Why do Hindu gods have many arms" is kept as Low priority and not used as a focus or secondary keyword; the in-text links send those readers to the posts that answer them.
6. **Mantras "not awakened" and Kali Yuga as "the age of machines".** Both are Acharya Ji's assessment, not scriptural statements. Recommendation: attribute them to Acharya Ji in any FAQ or snippet (as done above), and avoid "why mantras don't work" clickbait framing that implies the practices themselves are useless.
