# When the Questions Are Scientific, the Answer Must Come from the Soul

Have you ever stood in a temple and wondered why the Goddess has eight arms? What Ganapati's trunk means? If such questions arise in your mind, you are not alone. Acharya Ji says the person who asks such questions is not wrong, but is a person of a higher level.

## Is Questioning a Lack of Faith?

A person who thinks scientifically is a person of reason. And Lord Shri Krishna declared the intellect to be higher than the mind.

> **indriyāṇi parāṇyāhur indriyebhyaḥ paraṁ manaḥ**
> **manasas tu parā buddhir yo buddheḥ paratas tu saḥ**
> "The senses are higher than the body; higher than the senses is the mind; higher than the mind is the intellect; and beyond the intellect is the Self."
> (Bhagavad Gita 3.42)

The mind is higher than the body, the intellect is higher than the mind, and higher than all is the **Atma** (the Self). So when an intellectual comes with questions, in Shri Krishna's eyes he truly is a person of a higher level.

> "Rationalists are not wrong. The problem is that we are unable to answer them."

## Why Does Feeling Not Give the Answer?

Today our temples are filled mostly with people of devotion. They are connected through feeling, and that is good. But who will answer the one who questions at a higher level, the way Acharya Ji himself had questions as a child?

Answering him needs someone of intellect, or someone beyond the intellect: a **sakshatkari** (realised master), established at the level of the soul. A person of emotion alone cannot satisfy him.

*When a spiritual question arose in your mind, did you find an answer that also satisfied your intellect?*

## Kali Yuga: The Age of Machines and Intellect

This is **Kali Yuga**, the age of machines, the age of intellect. That is why rationalists have increased. They ask: how eight arms? How twelve arms? What is the logic of the elephant's trunk? Does worship truly bear fruit?

And this is where we fall short. We cannot show the **phalashruti** (the promised fruit of a practice) of mantra sadhana.

## Where Did the Method Go Wrong?

Acharya Ji says it plainly: the mantras were not perfected, not awakened, because our very method went wrong. We never reached the level of the soul, never reached a true Guru.

Only devotion goes on, and only action goes on, and that too at the level of **sharira-bodha** (body-consciousness).

## A Prophecy That Came True

Swami Chinmayananda used to say that a time would come when rationalists would greatly increase, and the people of devotion alone would not be able to answer them.

> "Then, somewhere, the Guru will have to awaken and answer in the language of science."

## Decoding the Scriptures

Remembering this, Acharya Ji began to **decode** his discourses and the scriptures, the Durga Saptashati too, that is, to explain them in a language the person of today can understand.

Not "Lord Shiva said to Parvati," but my own soul said to my mind and intellect. If Ganapati does this, it means the **viveka-buddhi** (discriminating wisdom) within you must awaken. In this language the books then began to be written.

*If every story in the scriptures is a story within you, which story are you living today?*

## Key Takeaways

- The mind is higher than the body, the intellect higher than the mind, and the soul higher than the intellect (Gita 3.42).
- The rationalist who asks questions is not wrong, but of a higher level.
- Kali Yuga is the age of intellect; feeling alone cannot answer its questions.
- The fruit of mantra sadhana is not seen because we have not reached the level of the soul, a true Guru.
- According to Swami Chinmayananda, the Guru will have to answer in the language of science.
- Decoding the scriptures explains their stories in the language of our own soul, mind and intellect.
