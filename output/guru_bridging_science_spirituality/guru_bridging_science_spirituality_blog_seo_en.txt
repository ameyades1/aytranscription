# When the Questions Come from Science, Let the Answers Come from the Self

Have you ever stood in a temple and wondered why the Goddess has eight arms, or what Lord Ganesha's trunk stands for? If questions like these stir in your mind, you are not alone. Acharya Ji says that the person who asks them is not at fault at all. Such a person stands on a higher rung.

The bridge between science and spirituality is a realised Guru who, at the level of the Atman, answers in the language of science. According to Acharya Ji, questioning is not a lack of faith: the Bhagavad Gita (3.42) places the intellect above the mind and the Atman beyond the intellect. Feeling alone cannot answer the rational seeker of Kali Yuga.

## Is Questioning a Lack of Faith?

People with a scientific bent live by reason. And Shri Krishna himself places the intellect above the mind.

> **indriyāṇi parāṇy āhur indriyebhyaḥ paraṁ manaḥ**
> **manasas tu parā buddhir yo buddheḥ paratas tu saḥ**
> "The senses are said to stand above the body; above the senses is the mind; above the mind is the intellect; and beyond even the intellect is the **Atman** (the Self)."
> (Bhagavad Gita 3.42)

In Shri Krishna's eyes, then, a thinking person who arrives with questions truly belongs on a higher level.

Rational people are not wrong, Acharya Ji points out. The real problem is that we cannot give them answers.

## Why Can't Devotion Alone Answer the Seeker?

Most people in our temples today come through devotion. Their bond with God is one of feeling, and that is a good thing. But who will answer the person whose questions reach higher, the kind of questions Acharya Ji himself wrestled with as a child?

Such a person needs someone who lives by the intellect, or better still, someone who has gone beyond it: a **sakshatkari** (a realised master) established at the level of the Atman. Feeling alone cannot satisfy that kind of mind.

*When a spiritual question last arose in you, did you find an answer that also satisfied your reason?*

## Why Are Rational Thinkers Growing in Kali Yuga?

We live in **Kali Yuga** (the present dark age of the Hindu cycle of time), an age of machines and of the intellect. No wonder the number of rational thinkers keeps growing. They ask: How can anyone have eight arms, or twelve? What is the logic behind an elephant's trunk? Does worship actually bear fruit?

This is exactly where we fall short. We cannot show them the **phalashruti** (the promised fruit of a practice) of mantra sadhana.

## Where Did the Method Go Wrong?

Acharya Ji puts it plainly. The mantras have not come to fruition or come alive, because our very method has gone astray. We never reached the level of the Atman, and we never reached a true Guru.

What remains is devotion on one side and ritual action on the other, both carried out at the level of **sharira-bodha** (body-consciousness).

## Answering in the Language of Science

Swami Chinmayananda, the renowned teacher of Vedanta, used to say that a time would come when rational thinkers would far outnumber the rest, and devotion alone would no longer be able to answer them. Then, somewhere, the Guru would have to awaken and reply in the language of science.

## Decoding the Scriptures

The moment Acharya Ji recalled this, he began to **decode** his discourses and the scriptures, including the **Durga Saptashati** (seven hundred verses in praise of Maa Durga). Decoding here means explaining them in a language that people today can grasp.

Instead of "Lord Shiva said to Parvati", the reading becomes: my own Atman spoke to my mind and intellect. When Lord Ganesha acts in a certain way, the message is that your inner **viveka-buddhi** (discriminating wisdom) must awaken. Books then began to be written in this very language.

*If every story in the scriptures is a story of your inner life, which one are you living today?*

## Key Takeaways

- The mind stands above the body, the intellect above the mind, and the Atman above the intellect (Gita 3.42).
- A rational person who asks questions is not wrong but stands on a higher level.
- Kali Yuga is an age of the intellect, and devotion alone cannot answer its questions.
- Mantra sadhana fails to bear visible fruit because we have not reached the level of the Atman or a true Guru.
- Swami Chinmayananda foresaw that the Guru would have to answer in the language of science.
- Decoding the scriptures reads their stories in the language of our own Atman, mind and intellect.
