*Who is the real Lord Ganesha?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that the story of Ganesha's birth from Parvati's bath is a symbol: it shows how viveka, our discernment, is born and rises to master the senses.

💭 _Who makes the decisions in your life: your senses, or your discernment?_
