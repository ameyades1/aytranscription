# The Source of All Religions Is One

*The story of a seeker, Geeta Dangi: how a hidden prejudice in the mind turned into equanimity and respect.*

Geeta ji says herself that she is not used to speaking. So that nothing is missed, she reads out her experience. Yet her simple, honest words reveal a deep transformation.

## Is There a Hidden Prejudice in My Mind Too?

Before coming to Antar Yog, she held a very wrong notion about religion.

An India-Pakistan cricket match would be on, the other side's players would play brilliantly, but instead of admiring their skill, a bias already sat in her mind: "They are Pakistanis." The feeling of religion became bigger than the joy of the game.

"All religions are equal," she would say with her lips, but the discrimination in her mind never went away.

*Do you also say something that your mind does not yet fully believe?*

## When the Scriptures Were Placed Side by Side

Then, in one session, Guruji read out passages from the Bhavishya Purana, the Bible and other Puranas. He explained beautifully how much the Bhagavad Gita and the scriptures of all religions share at the level of **tattva** (essential truth).

While watching a film about Jesus, too, Guruji explained in detail how the principles of the Gita appear in the events of Jesus's life.

The result? The wrong notions and the wrong **samskaras** (deep impressions) in her mind were removed. Now she feels **samatva bhav** (equanimity) and great respect toward all religions.

## "I Am Not Hindu"

For Geeta ji, the biggest realisation was that our dharma is **Sanatan Dharma**: based on the principles of the Vedas, the most ancient and the first religion in the world.

Later, according to the needs of place, time and circumstance, other religions gradually arose.

She gives an example. Some selfish people performed yajnas to fulfil their own desires and made animal sacrifice an established custom in them. Violence grew so much that, to stop it, Gautama Buddha founded Buddhism on the principle of non-violence.

> **ahiṃsā paramo dharmaḥ**
> "Non-violence is the highest dharma."
> (Mahabharata, Anushasana Parva)

So Buddhism too came from Sanatan Dharma. In the same way many religions arose, but the root source of all of them is Sanatan Dharma.

> "Religion should actually exist for the welfare of humanity, but instead it became the cause of humanity's destruction and downfall."

## How Did Religion Go from Welfare to Conflict?

With so many religions, society should have progressed. Instead it declined, because discrimination crept in: I am superior, you are inferior.

And from this began the vicious cycle of hatred, violence and war.

## Jesus: When Dharma Declined

In the Gita, the Lord says:

> **yadā yadā hi dharmasya glānir bhavati bhārata**
> **abhyutthānam adharmasya tadātmānaṃ sṛjāmy aham**
> "O Bharata, whenever there is a decline of dharma and a rise of adharma, I manifest Myself."
> (Bhagavad Gita 4.7)

Whenever dharma declines and adharma grows, an avatar takes birth. According to Geeta ji, at that time Jesus Christ came as an avatar.

He kept giving knowledge and showing the right path, but people did not understand. When he performed miracles, people felt there was something in him, and they began to follow him.

Guruji also always says, "Chamatkārāśivāy namaskār nāhī", that is, without a miracle, no one bows. But this tendency is wrong. A miracle does not prove any truth.

*Do I recognise truth by its depth, or only by seeing a miracle?*

## How Rare a Surrendered Disciple Is

Jesus spent his whole life teaching people the essence of dharma, yet his significance was not understood until the end. His disciples were only twelve, and even among them, how rare a truly **sharanagat** (fully surrendered) disciple is.

## Forgiveness on the Cross: The Gita Made Alive

Even amid unbearable agony on the cross, Jesus forgives everyone, and even there he sees only God.

> **antakāle ca mām eva smaran muktvā kalevaram**
> **yaḥ prayāti sa madbhāvaṃ yāti nāsty atra saṃśayaḥ**
> "Whoever leaves the body at the time of death remembering Me alone attains My being; of this there is no doubt."
> (Bhagavad Gita 8.5)

Whoever remembers Me alone at the end attains Me. Geeta ji says Jesus taught this verse by living it himself.

> "This is that very verse; Jesus taught it by living it himself."

## Religion Does Not Decay; Its Interpretation Does

The original teachers of all religions give the same kind of knowledge, because the principles of all are the same. No religion is good or bad, greater or lesser.

But later followers distort religion according to their own convenience and self-interest. Religion slowly slides down, the ordinary person puts their faith in that, and moves far away from the essence of the original scripture.

## The Solution: Learning from the Right Guru

> "The true knowledge, the true essence, we must learn only from a shrotriya and brahmanishtha guru."

**Shrotriya** means one versed in the scriptures, and **brahmanishtha** means one established in Brahman. Learning from such a guru is the right path for everyone.

Only then will wars, quarrels and discrimination in the name of religion stop, society and the nation progress, and the purpose of life, **mukti** (liberation), become attainable.

## A Feeling of Gratitude

Geeta ji says that in this difficult Kali Yuga, Guruji, Acharya Upendra Joshi ji, is doing great work, and being with him is good fortune. Her wish is to take part in Guruji's work, giving her full hundred percent. And finally, sashtang dandavat to Guruji.

## Key Takeaways

- Saying "all religions are equal" and truly believing it in the mind are two different things.
- All scriptures share a deep common ground at the level of essential truth.
- Sanatan Dharma, based on the Vedas, is the most ancient religion and the root source of all religions.
- Discrimination and the feeling of "I am superior" turned religion from welfare into conflict.
- Miracles do not prove truth; a truly surrendered disciple is rare.
- Through forgiveness and remembrance of God on the cross, Jesus lived out Gita 8.5.
- True knowledge must be learned from a shrotriya and brahmanishtha guru; this is the path to peace and liberation.
