*Why do we struggle despite trying our best?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that we aim at pleasure instead of understanding. A lesson satsang could teach at twenty becomes a crisis at sixty. Learn through knowledge, or through bitter experience.

💭 _What are you working so hard for today: pleasure, or understanding?_
