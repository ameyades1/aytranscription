The goal of every action should be learning and the highest spiritual knowledge, not pleasure. The talk also covers reflecting before acting (ask the Guru, think first, or at least reflect afterwards), the marriage-at-twenty-two example of understanding that comes too late, good times as a time for service and bad times as a time for reflection, study and meditation (with Sade Sati as an example), Acharya Ji's own exalted-Jupiter dasha, selfless service and Gita 9.22 (yogakshema), and wisdom as already within us, covered by layers of ego, ignorance and attachment. ## 6. FAQ

1. **पूरी कोशिश के बाद भी संघर्ष क्यों होता है? / Why do I keep struggling despite trying my best?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार, यदि लक्ष्य ज्ञान के अतिरिक्त कुछ और है, तो संघर्ष निश्चित है। ज्ञान आना ही है, समझ से नहीं तो कटु अनुभव से। / Acharya Ji says that if you aim at anything other than knowledge, struggle is guaranteed. The lesson comes anyway; if not through understanding, then through bitter experience.
2. **ज्ञानयुक्त कर्म क्या है? / What is action guided by knowledge?**
   पहले समझना, फिर कर्म करना: गुरु से जानकर, कर्म से पहले स्वयं सोचकर, या कम से कम कर्म के बाद विचार करके। यही कर्मयोग है। / Understanding first, then acting: by asking the Guru, thinking it through beforehand, or at least reflecting once you have acted. This is Karma Yoga.
3. **बुरे समय में क्या करना चाहिए? / What should we do in bad times?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार बुरा समय चिंतन, ज्ञान और ध्यान का समय है, और अच्छा समय दूसरों की सेवा का। / According to Acharya Ji, bad times are for reflection, study and meditation, and good times are for serving others.
4. **साढ़ेसाती में क्या करें? / What does Sade Sati ask of us?**
   आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि लोग साढ़ेसाती में और भागदौड़ करते हैं, जबकि शनि उन्हें बिठाता है ताकि वे भीतर देखें। / Acharya Ji says people rush around even more during Sade Sati, while Shani is making them sit still so that they look within.
5. **योगक्षेम का क्या अर्थ है? / What does yogakshema mean?**
   जो नहीं है उसे देना और जो है उसकी रक्षा करना। गीता ९.२२ में भगवान अनन्य भाव से उपासना करने वालों का योगक्षेम वहन करने का वचन देते हैं। / Providing what is lacking and protecting what one has. In Bhagavad Gita 9.22 the Lord promises to carry the yogakshema of those who worship Him with undivided devotion.

FAQ 5 states the verse's own meaning; Acharya Ji's reading of it as selfless service is kept out of the FAQ (flag 2).
