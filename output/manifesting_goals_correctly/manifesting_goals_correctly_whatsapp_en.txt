*Does a passing wish ever come true?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that even a faint wish is karma and bears fruit. Someone who idly wished for cars all around may become a traffic constable, until they say, "I don't want this."

💭 _Which of your wishes have you never worked for wholeheartedly?_
