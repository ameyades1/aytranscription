# Life Is Shaped Not by Wishes but by Intense Willpower

Have you ever wondered what happens to every small longing that arises in your mind? We think such thoughts simply come and go. But from the view of Karma Yoga, even a mild wish is karma, and its fruit also comes.

Let us understand the difference between a **wish** and **intense willpower**, and how nature treats each of them.

## What Do You Get by Merely Wishing?

It is said, "Seek and it shall be given to the seeker", that is, whoever seeks receives. It is not said that whoever merely wishes will receive.

> "It is not 'wish and it shall be given to the wisher.' Wishes will not do it."

There is an old saying, "If wishes were horses, beggars would ride."

Repeating "I am a king, I am a king" achieves nothing. But it is also important to know what the result of such passing thoughts is. If you want to learn Karma Yoga, you must understand its negative side too.

## Is Even a Mild Wish Karma?

Yes. Suppose someone wishes, "There should be cars in front of me and behind me." But this is not an intense desire, just a mild longing.

Its **phalashruti** (result) may be that after a few years, or in the next birth, he stands as a traffic constable at a busy intersection. Cars in front, cars behind!

There he will meet drivers who quarrel, or see the cost of maintaining a car. And then he himself will say, "I don't want a car."

## The Wish to Be Like an Actress

Someone else wishes to wear clothes like some big film actress of today, to do something like her. Not intense willpower, just a wish.

In the next birth she may become the washerwoman of an actress, who has to wash her designer clothes every day. Then she will understand how much hardship there is in it, how much time it takes. And she will say, "I don't want it now."

> "The wish was made; its fruit must come, without fail."

Notice: being an actress is not bad. Having cars in front and behind is not bad either. The problem is that the thought was not serious; it was only a **passing thought**.

*Which wishes in your mind only come and go, without your ever doing anything for them wholeheartedly?*

## The Dream of Being King, Without Preparation

A third example. Someone simply had the wish to become a king, but there is no intense willpower.

Such a person may become the assistant of some big prime minister, a servant in his house, or even a dog, from where he will see everything. Or he may be born in the house of a king who is greatly troubled and always under tension. So that nature tells him, "Don't become a king."

Why does nature do this? Because this person was not doing his work properly at the smaller level; he just wanted to reach the top directly. Such a person will not become a king anyway. His karma will take him to places where he learns that this is not to be done.

And the truth is also that the public should not get such a king.

## When the Wish Is Intense

But the reverse also happens. One whose wish is good and intense automatically reaches the right place, some political party. There he keeps finding people who encourage him, who take him up, and further up.

This is the miracle of **intense willpower**. Then the path keeps appearing on its own.

## Think Big, but Be Patient

If someone says, "I want to become an actress," there is no problem in that. If the willpower is intense, opportunities will come. But will a big hit film come at the very first attempt? This did not happen even to the biggest stars. They too came up through small serials. Many people forget this.

> "If you think, think big."

This is a statement of **Shri Vidya**. Thinking big is not bad. But before the big goal comes a whole series of small acts of willpower. Nature tests again and again.

The bigger someone's vision, the more often nature brings them down to do small work. Why? Because a **Karma Yogi** has to take care of other people too. And all this happens to wipe out the **ahankara** (ego) within.

*Are you doing the small tasks on the way to your big dream with the same wholeheartedness?*

## Four Rotis at Home Are Also Karma Yoga

The Karma Yoga of great work is one thing. But in making four rotis at home too, one must do well. It is not that there is no Karma Yoga there. There is Karma Yoga on a small scale as well, and it will be discussed later.

## Key Takeaways

- Merely wishing brings nothing; the one who seeks receives.
- Even a mild wish is karma, and its fruit surely comes.
- Passing wishes often turn into experiences that teach us, "I don't want this."
- With intense willpower, the path keeps appearing on its own.
- Think big, but be ready for small steps and tests before the big goal.
- Small tasks come to wipe out ego and to teach us to understand others.
- There is Karma Yoga even in small household work.
