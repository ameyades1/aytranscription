# Chapter: Not Wish, but Intense Willpower

## Seek, Do Not Merely Wish

It is said, "Seek and it shall be given to the seeker." It is not said that whoever merely wishes will be given. Wishes alone achieve nothing. There is a saying, "If wishes were horses, beggars would ride."

A passing thought that comes and goes in the mind can do nothing. Repeating "I am a king, I am a king" will achieve nothing. But it is also necessary to learn what the result of such passing thoughts is. If you want to learn Karma Yoga, you must also know its negative outcome.

## Even a Mild Wish Is Karma

If you say there is no intense desire, only a small desire, is that not also karma? Suppose someone wishes, "There should be cars in front of me and behind me." But this is not an intense desire.

Now its *phalashruti* (fruit) may be that after a few years, or in the next birth, he stands as a traffic constable at a very busy traffic signal. He wanted many cars in front and behind, didn't he! But there was no intense willpower. Then he will meet just such drivers, people who quarrel, or he will see the cost of maintaining a car so clearly that he himself will say, "I don't want a car."

## The Actress's Washerwoman

Or there may be someone who strongly wishes, about some big film actress of today, "I want to wear clothes like hers, I want to do something like her." There is no intense willpower, only a wish.

In the next birth she will become the washerwoman of some actress, who has to wash her designer clothes every day. How much hardship there is in it, how much time is wasted: she will learn this lesson only then, certainly. And then she herself will say, "I don't want it now."

But the wish was made, and its fruit must come. And its purpose will be that the thought was not a serious thought. Is being an actress a bad thing? Is having cars in front and behind a bad thing? No. But it was a passing thought, and that is why the problem arises.

## The Half-Formed Wish to Be a King

A third example. Someone has no intense willpower to become a king; a wish has merely arisen. Now he will somewhere become the personal assistant of some big prime minister, or a servant in his house, or a dog, or something, where he will see everything. Or he will be born in the house of a king who is greatly troubled and always under tension, so that nature tells him, "Don't become a king."

Do you understand what nature is doing? In his smaller times, this person was not carrying out his ideas properly; he wanted to go straight to the top. Such a man will not become a king anyway. So he will end up working at such a king's place. That karma will take him to such places. He will learn the lesson that this is not to be done, and that is good. But in reality, the public should not get such a king, which is why this happens.

## When the Wish Is Intense

But the reverse can also happen. One whose wish is good will automatically reach such a king, or some political party, where he keeps finding people who encourage him, and they take him up, and further up.

How does this happen? That wish comes from **intense willpower**. Then it is possible, and he keeps finding just such a path.

## Think Big, but Step by Step

So if someone says, "I want to become an actress," there is no problem in it. If there is intense willpower, some agency or some opportunity will come. But will a huge blockbuster film come directly at the start? Even for superstars this has not happened to this day. They too come up through small serials. Many people forget this.

It is not bad to think big. This is the statement of Shri Vidya:

> "If you think, think big."

But before thinking big, a whole series of smaller acts of willpower will come into it. Nature will test you many times. One who has a very big vision is brought down by nature many times to do small work, because a Karma Yogi has to take great care of other people too. All this happens to wipe out the **ego** within him. So the fruition may also come in small ways.

## Household Karma Yoga Is Also Karma Yoga

The Karma Yoga of a great work is one thing; and what Karma Yoga is needed to make four rotis at home? That is fine too, it is good. One should be good for the home as well. Right now I am speaking of great work. But it is not that there is no Karma Yoga in doing good at home. There is Karma Yoga on a small scale too. We will discuss that later.
