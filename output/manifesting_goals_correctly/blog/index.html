इच्छा नहीं, प्रबल इच्छा शक्ति — लक्ष्य को सही ढंग से साकार करना

❖

प्रस्तावना: केवल चाहने से कुछ नहीं होता

आज "manifestation" की बहुत चर्चा होती है — मन में कुछ चाहो, और वह मिल जाएगा। पर इस छोटे-से प्रवचन-अंश में आचार्य उपेंद्र जी एक गहरी चेतावनी देते हैं: हल्की-सी, क्षणिक इच्छा भी एक कर्म है, और उसका भी फल आता है — पर अक्सर वैसा नहीं, जैसा हमने सोचा था। लक्ष्य प्रबल इच्छा शक्ति से साकार होता है, passing thought से नहीं।

कर्मयोग — कर्म के द्वारा योग; ऐसा कर्म जो व्यक्ति को भीतर से रूपांतरित करे। फलश्रुति — किसी कर्म या साधना का निश्चित फल।

Seek करो, केवल wish नहीं

आचार्य जी आरंभ में ही "माँगने" और "केवल चाहने" का भेद स्पष्ट करते हैं:

"'Seek and it shall be given' — seeker को बोलते हैं। 'Wish and it shall be given to the wisher' — ऐसा नहीं है। Wishes से नहीं होगा।"

"'If wishes were horses, beggars would ride' बोलते हैं। Passing thought से नहीं होगा। 'हाँ, मैं राजा हूँ, मैं राजा हूँ' — उससे कुछ नहीं होगा।"

Passing thought भी कर्म है

पर क्षणिक इच्छा व्यर्थ भी नहीं जाती। कर्मयोग के विद्यार्थी को इसका उल्टा पहलू भी समझना होगा:

"ऐसे passing thought से क्या होता है, वो सीखना भी ज़रूरी है। कर्मयोग अगर सीखना है, तो उसका negative outcome भी पता होना चाहिए।"

"अगर आपने बोला — प्रबल इच्छा नहीं है, लेकिन थोड़ी इच्छा है — तो वो भी कर्म है कि नहीं?"

इसे समझाने के लिए आचार्य जी तीन उदाहरण देते हैं।

पहला उदाहरण: "आगे-पीछे गाड़ियाँ होनी चाहिए"

"'मेरे आगे-पीछे गाड़ियाँ होनी चाहिए।' क्या इच्छा है? मेरे आगे-पीछे गाड़ियाँ होनी चाहिए। पर यह प्रबल इच्छा नहीं है।"

"अब इसकी फलश्रुति होगी कि शायद कुछ साल के बाद या अगले जन्म में वो किसी बहुत important, busy traffic signal के ऊपर हवालदार बनकर हो सकता है।"

इच्छा पूरी हुई — गाड़ियाँ आगे-पीछे हैं — पर उस रूप में, जो उसे यह सिखाए कि वह इच्छा कितनी उथली थी:

"और फिर उसको ऐसा ही कोई गाड़ी वाला मिलेगा, झगड़ा करने वाला मिलेगा। या उसको गाड़ी का maintenance का खर्चा बहुत दिखता है, तो बोलेगा — नहीं चाहिए गाड़ी।"

दूसरा उदाहरण: Heroine जैसी dress

"बड़ी फिल्म heroine है ना कोई — 'उसके जैसा मेरे को dress पहनना है, उसके जैसा कुछ करना है।' प्रबल इच्छा शक्ति नहीं है, सिर्फ इच्छा है।"

"अगले जन्म में वो किसी heroine की धोबी बनेगी, जहाँ उसका designer dress उसको रोज़ धोना पड़ता है। इसमें कितनी तकलीफ़ है, कितना उसका waste of time होता है — यह lesson वो तभी सीखेगी, पक्का।"

"And then she will tell you — 'नहीं चाहिए अभी।' But वो इच्छा तो की थी, उसकी फलश्रुति तो आनी ही आनी है। और वो purpose उसमें से होगा कि वो thought serious thought नहीं था।"

आचार्य जी स्पष्ट करते हैं कि दोष इच्छा के विषय में नहीं, उसकी गंभीरता के अभाव में है:

"अभी heroine होना क्या बुरी बात है? क्या गाड़ी आगे-पीछे होना बुरी बात है? लेकिन यह passing thought था, इसलिए problem होता है।"

तीसरा उदाहरण: राजा बनने की क्षणिक इच्छा

"किसी को राजा बनने की प्रबल इच्छा शक्ति नहीं है, पर सिर्फ इच्छा आ गई। अब कहीं तो वह पैदा होगा — किधर का तो बड़ा prime minister, उसके घर का कोई servant या कुत्ता या कुछ बन जाएगा, जहाँ वह सब देखेगा।"

"ऐसे राजा के घर पर ही उसका जन्म होगा जो बहुत ही पीड़ित है, tension में होता है, ताकि उसको प्रकृति बोलेगी कि 'तू राजा मत बन।'"

प्रकृति ऐसा क्यों करती है? क्योंकि जिसने छोटे स्तर पर अपना काम ठीक से नहीं किया और सीधे ऊपर पहुँचना चाहता है, वह वैसे भी अच्छा राजा नहीं बन सकता:

"प्रकृति क्या कर रही है, समझा? Smaller time में जो ideas थे, वो ठीक से नहीं कर रहा था; sudden ऊपर जाना है। तो ऐसा आदमी वैसे भी राजा नहीं बनेगा।"

"That karma will take him over to such places. वो ऐसा lesson सीखेगा कि यह नहीं करना है — तो अच्छा है। But in reality, public को ऐसा राजा नहीं मिलना चाहिए।"

प्रबल इच्छा शक्ति: मार्ग स्वयं खुलता है

अब आचार्य जी उल्टा पक्ष दिखाते हैं — जहाँ इच्छा शुद्ध भी है और प्रबल भी:

"लेकिन कोई reverse हो सकता है — अच्छा वाला, जिसकी इच्छा तो अच्छी है। वो अपने आप ऐसे राजा, या ऐसी कोई political party में पहुँच ही जाएगा, जहाँ उसको encourage करने वाले मिलते जाएँगे, and ऊपर, ऊपर, ऊपर वो ले के जाएँगे।"

"वो कैसे हो सकता है? वो इच्छा रहेगी — प्रबल इच्छा शक्ति से होता है वो। It is possible. Then उसको ऐसा ही मार्ग मिलते-मिलते-मिलते जाएगा।"

इसलिए बड़ा लक्ष्य रखना अपने-आप में गलत नहीं है:

"तो वैसे 'मुझे heroine ही बनना है' किसी ने बोला, तो problem नहीं है कुछ। But प्रबल इच्छा शक्ति होगी, तो वैसी agency आएगी या कुछ।"

सीधे शिखर नहीं: छोटी सीढ़ियाँ

पर प्रबल इच्छा भी एक झटके में शिखर तक नहीं पहुँचाती:

"But starting में direct बड़ी blockbuster फिल्म आएगी क्या? किसी superstar का भी वैसा नहीं हुआ है आज तक। उनका भी — छोटे serial में से आते हैं, है ना? This many people forget."

बड़ा सोचो — पर अहंकार मिटाते हुए

आचार्य जी श्री विद्या के सूत्र की याद दिलाते हैं, और उसके साथ जुड़ी एक शर्त भी:

"It is not bad to think big. 'सोचो तो बड़ा सोचो' — statement है श्री विद्या का। But वो बड़ा सोचने से पहले, वो series of smaller इच्छा शक्ति — इसमें बहुत आएगा।"

"प्रकृति बहुत बार परीक्षा लेगी। जिसका बहुत बड़ा vision होता है, उसको बहुत बार छोटे काम करने को लाएगी, नीचे।"

इन छोटे कामों का उद्देश्य क्या है?

"Because कर्मयोगी को बाकी लोगों का भी बहुत देखना होता है। वो अहंकार उसके अंदर का wipe out होने के लिए सब होता है। तो यह fructify — छोटा-मोटा हो सकता है।"

जिसे बहुतों का भला करना है, उसे पहले नीचे से सबका जीवन देखना-समझना होता है; और छोटे काम भीतर के अहंकार को मिटाते हैं।

घर की चार रोटी में भी कर्मयोग

अंत में आचार्य जी स्पष्ट करते हैं कि यहाँ बात महान कार्य के कर्मयोग की हो रही है — पर इसका अर्थ यह नहीं कि छोटे, घरेलू कार्यों में कर्मयोग नहीं होता:

"महान कार्य का कर्मयोग — और घर की चार रोटी बनाने के लिए क्या कर्मयोग चाहिए? वो ठीक है, that is good, अच्छा बनना चाहिए घर के लिए।"

"महान कार्य की बात कर रहा हूँ मैं। पर घर में भी अच्छा करना है — वहाँ कर्मयोग नहीं होता, ऐसा नहीं है। Small scale वाला भी है, वो भी है।"

Not a Wish, but Strong Willpower — Manifesting Goals the Right Way

❖
Brahmarshi Acharya Upendra JiBased on a discourse · 7 min read
हिन्दी में पढ़ेंWatch the discourse

Introduction: Merely Wanting Achieves Nothing

Today there is much talk of "manifestation" — want something in your mind, and you will get it. But in this short excerpt from a discourse, Acharya Upendra Ji gives a deeper warning: even a light, fleeting wish is a karma, and it too bears fruit — but often not in the way we imagined. A goal is realised through prabal ichchha shakti (strong willpower), not through a passing thought.

Karma Yoga — union through action; action that transforms a person from within. Phalashruti — the definite fruit of an action or a spiritual practice.

Seek, Don't Merely Wish

At the very outset, Acharya Ji makes clear the difference between "seeking" and "merely wanting":

"'Seek and it shall be given' — that is said to the seeker. 'Wish and it shall be given to the wisher' — it is not like that. It will not happen through wishes."

"They say, 'If wishes were horses, beggars would ride.' It will not happen through a passing thought. 'Yes, I am a king, I am a king' — nothing will come of that."

A Passing Thought Is Also a Karma

Yet a fleeting wish does not go to waste either. A student of Karma Yoga must understand its reverse side too:

"What happens through such a passing thought — that also needs to be learned. If you want to learn Karma Yoga, you should also know its negative outcome."

"If you say — it is not a strong desire, but there is a little desire — then is that also a karma or not?"

To explain this, Acharya Ji gives three examples.

First Example: "There Should Be Cars Ahead and Behind Me"

"'There should be cars ahead of me and behind me.' What is the wish? There should be cars ahead of me and behind me. But this is not a strong desire."

"Now its phalashruti will be that perhaps after some years, or in the next birth, he may end up as a traffic constable on some very important, busy traffic signal."

The wish was fulfilled — there are cars ahead and behind — but in a form that teaches him how shallow that wish was:

"And then he will meet just such a car driver, someone who picks a fight. Or he sees the huge maintenance expense of a car, and he will say — I don't want a car."

Second Example: A Dress Like the Heroine's

"There is some big film heroine, isn't there — 'I want to wear a dress like hers, do something like her.' There is no strong willpower, only a wish."

"In the next birth she will become some heroine's washerwoman, where she has to wash her designer dresses every day. How much hardship there is in it, how much of a waste of time it is for her — only then will she learn this lesson, for sure."

"And then she will tell you — 'I don't want it now.' But she did make that wish, its phalashruti has to come, it must come. And the purpose that will come out of it is that the thought was not a serious thought."

Acharya Ji makes clear that the fault lies not in the object of the wish, but in its lack of seriousness:

"Now, is being a heroine a bad thing? Is having cars ahead and behind a bad thing? But it was a passing thought, that is why the problem arises."

Third Example: A Fleeting Wish to Be a King

"Someone has no strong willpower to become a king, but a wish just came. Now he will be born somewhere — some big prime minister somewhere, he will become a servant or a dog or something in his house, where he will see it all."

"He will be born in the house of just such a king who is very troubled, who is in tension, so that nature will tell him, 'Don't you become a king.'"

Why does nature do this? Because one who did not do his work properly at a smaller level and wants to reach the top directly could not make a good king anyway:

"Do you understand what nature is doing? The ideas he had in smaller times, he was not carrying them out properly; he wants to go up suddenly. So such a man will not become a king anyway."

"That karma will take him over to such places. He will learn the lesson that this is not to be done — so that is good. But in reality, the public should not get such a king."

Strong Willpower: The Path Opens by Itself

Now Acharya Ji shows the reverse side — where the wish is both pure and strong:

"But it can be the reverse — the good one, whose wish is good. He will automatically reach such a king, or some such political party, where he will keep finding people who encourage him, and they will take him up, up, up."

"How can that happen? That wish will remain — it happens through strong willpower. It is possible. Then he will keep finding just such a path, again and again and again."

So keeping a big goal is not wrong in itself:

"So if someone said, 'I want to become a heroine and nothing else,' there is no problem at all. But if there is strong willpower, then such an agency will come or something."

Not Straight to the Summit: The Small Steps

But even strong willpower does not carry one to the summit in a single leap:

"But will a big blockbuster film come directly at the start? That has not happened even for any superstar till today. They too — they come up from small serials, don't they? This many people forget."

Think Big — but While Erasing the Ego

Acharya Ji recalls a principle of Shri Vidya, along with a condition attached to it:

"It is not bad to think big. 'If you think, think big' — that is a statement of Shri Vidya. But before that big thinking, that series of smaller willpower — a lot of it will come in this."

"Nature will test you many times. One who has a very big vision, nature will bring down many times to do small tasks."

What is the purpose of these small tasks?

"Because a Karma Yogi has to look after other people a great deal too. All of it happens so that the ego inside him is wiped out. So this may fructify — in small ways."

One who has to do good for many must first see and understand everyone's life from below; and small tasks erase the ego within.

Karma Yoga Even in the Four Rotis at Home

Finally, Acharya Ji clarifies that he is speaking here of the Karma Yoga of a great work — but this does not mean there is no Karma Yoga in small, household tasks:

"The Karma Yoga of a great work — and what Karma Yoga is needed to make four rotis at home? That is fine, that is good, one should become good for the home."

"I am talking about great work. But at home too one has to do well — it is not that Karma Yoga does not happen there. There is the small-scale kind too, that also exists."

Summary: Key Teachings

  1. 1
    "Seek and it shall be given" — not "wish and it shall be given." Merely wanting, or repeating "I am a king," achieves nothing.
  2. 2
    A passing thought is also a karma — its phalashruti must come, but often in the form of a hard lesson.
  3. 3
    A fleeting wish for cars → a traffic constable at a busy signal; a wish for a heroine's dress → the heroine's washerwoman; a wish to be king → a servant in a troubled king's house. Nature shows that the wish was not serious.
  4. 4
    The fault is not in the object of the wish — being a heroine or a king is not a bad thing; the problem is the passing thought.
  5. 5
    With strong willpower, the path keeps opening — the right people and the right opportunities come together by themselves.
  6. 6
    The summit is not reached directly — even superstars come up from small serials.
  7. 7
    "If you think, think big" (Shri Vidya) — but nature tests one with a big vision through small tasks, so that the ego is wiped out and one can understand everyone's life.
  8. 8
    There is Karma Yoga even in small household tasks — the small-scale kind is Karma Yoga too.
