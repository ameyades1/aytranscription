*Do your actions alone decide your destiny?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains, through the Bhagavad Gita, that destiny comes not from the deed but from the samskara, the deep impression, it leaves behind, such as ego in charity.

💭 _What do your words and deeds reveal about your inner impressions?_
