# Chapter: The Three Gods and Three Goddesses: The Secret of Creation, Sustenance and Dissolution

## Three Gods, Three Goddesses

The three gods we call Brahma, Vishnu and Mahesh are spoken of together with three goddesses. Saraswati is always joined with Brahma, Lakshmi with Vishnu, and Parvati or Kali with Mahesh. That is why we say Mahakal and Mahakali.

Now we have risen much higher. We no longer simply narrate stories; that is for children up to twelve years old. I said earlier that these are goddesses, and so on; what did that mean? This must be understood.

## Creation: Brahma and Rajoguna

If I have to create something, produce something, build something, then the Brahma bhava is needed. What is Brahma's quality? Brahma's quality is said to be **rajoguna**. To start anything, action has to be taken. Whether you want to produce something, begin something, start a business, or do anything at all. Even for you to be born, someone had to take the initiative. Whatever has to be done, what is needed at the start? Rajas shakti, that is, Brahma.

## Saraswati: Knowledge Along with Creation

Along with it there must also be knowledge, which is why Saraswati is spoken of with Brahma. If you create with Saraswati, with learning and knowledge, it will be far better.

There is no such concept as something done without knowledge. Ignorance is merely the absence of something. Even that absence a person chooses as their own knowledge. For example, if you put your hand in fire, your hand will burn. You cannot say, "It was not my wish." "I will remain ignorant about fire": this was the knowledge you accepted, and you put your hand in thinking, "This will do nothing." The result that came in the end came according to that very knowledge. What we call ignorance, the absence of Saraswati, is also Saraswati of whatever level you have; call it impure, or call it little.

## Everyone Has Their Own Saraswati

That Saraswati is present even in a thief. He too has a reason why he should steal. In the same way, a lustful, adulterous person also gives a reason: "Well, there was Vishvamitra, there was Menaka, there were other apsaras too, so what about us?"

The smoker too has an answer: there was Swami Samarth, there was Gajanan Maharaj, they too used the chillum, so we smoked a cigarette. Everyone has a favourable explanation for themselves. The drinker also says that an article says a little alcohol is good as medicine, that it has benefits.

> "Everybody has got their own Saraswati."

The question is, of what level is that Saraswati, and how much? Is it knowledge one made up in ignorance, or knowledge confirmed and certified by someone else? That knowledge will go on to create something. Drinking and drinking, he will ruin his own liver; it will do something. So which knowledge you have taken in is extremely important.

## Saraswati Puja Before Lakshmi Puja

On the day of Lakshmi Puja, how many people know that Saraswati is also worshipped? Many did not know this at all. Before Lakshmi Puja begins, Saraswati Puja is performed. Look in any scripture, even an ordinary puja book, and you will find the same rite there.

That is why today we will read the **Devi Bhagavata Purana**, which is an authentic source. We are going to read five of its chapters. How long it will take, I do not know. Today there will be more knowledge and very little physical practice, because all actions culminate in knowledge.

## Sustenance: Vishnu, Sattvaguna and Lakshmi

So the first point is understood: creation through rajas shakti, together with Saraswati. What comes second? **Sustenance**. Once something is produced, it has to be maintained. For maintenance, **sattvaguna** is needed, as I said. And what is always needed along with it? Lakshmi.

Once a body is born, that body has to be maintained throughout life. From food to clothing, from exercise to medicines, something is needed for its upkeep. For this, wealth, money, that is, Lakshmi is needed.

This is a combination. What is Lakshmi's quality? That too is rajoguna. And what is Vishnu's quality said to be? Sattvaguna. So sattvaguna and rajas shakti have come twice. While creating, too, one must produce with sattvaguna through rajas shakti, and then with sattvaguna, through rajas shakti, one has to maintain it.

## Dissolution: Mahakal, Mahakali and Tamas

And finally, some day there has to be its end, its letting go. Either nature will make you let go, ask you to leave, force you to. One day comes when everything is swept away at once.

> "Either you have to leave the world, or the world will leave you."

Something or other will happen. Your husband will go, or some relative, or the business. You will go, and the business will remain as it is. Or if one is a king, his kingdom will go; or someone will conquer his kingdom, or he will lose an election. Something does happen. One day, one of the two is going to leave, and preparing for that will take time.

This is **tamas**. Tamas works on its own, on auto mode. Death does not come announcing itself, unless one is entering samadhi. Hence Kali, and with her Mahadev: Mahakal and Mahakali. This is tamas, tamas shakti, that destroys.
