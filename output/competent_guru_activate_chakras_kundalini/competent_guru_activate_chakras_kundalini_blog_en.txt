# The Competent Guru and Kundalini: Many Lifetimes of Progress in One Sadhana

Have you ever wondered why some people live their whole lives in the same groove? Some live only for themselves, some only for their family, and some entirely for the Guru.

Acharya Ji finds the answer in the seven chakras within us. He also explains how a competent Guru can transform this journey.

## One Chakra, Many Lifetimes

The **chakras** (energy centres) are levels of our consciousness. Acharya Ji says that rising from one chakra to the next can take many lifetimes.

A competent Guru, however, can raise a seeker three or four chakras at once in a single **sadhana** (spiritual practice). For some, the entire ascent can happen, from the **Muladhara** (the root chakra) all the way to the **Sahasrara** (the crown chakra). It is just like Valya the bandit, who became the sage Valmiki, the author of the Ramayana.

## Seven Chakras, Seven Ways of Living

Each chakra has a central attitude. Put simply:

- **Muladhara:** only me
- **Svadhishthana (the sacral chakra):** me and my family
- **Manipura (the navel chakra):** service, but for personal gain
- **Heart (Anahata):** compassion, but without discernment
- **Vishuddhi (the throat chakra):** leadership, the fire to change the world
- **Ajna (the brow chakra):** complete surrender, a life lived for the Guru
- **Sahasrara:** merging into the Guru or into God

## Do I Live Only for Myself?

People living at the Muladhara live purely out of self-interest. For their own sake, they are ready to kill or be killed.

Those at the Svadhishthana have moved a little further, yet their world ends at "me and my family." Acharya Ji asks: haven't you met people whose whole life goes no further than that?

## Service or a Bargain?

People who have reached the Manipura chakra do help the world, but they look for what they and their family will gain from it. Name and fame drive them.

Even when they call it social service, they set one condition: "Show us what's in it for us first, and then we'll do it."

*Is there a hidden bargain behind my service too?*

## Does My Compassion Come with Discernment?

When the heart chakra awakens, compassion arises. On its own, though, Acharya Ji calls it **idiotic compassion**, compassion without discernment.

Such people give a whole month's salary to the temple and leave their family to go hungry. They think, "Even if my own life falls apart, I must keep on helping." They never stop to use their judgment.

Acharya Ji gives the example of Sita Mata. Lakshmana had drawn the **Lakshman Rekha** (the protective line he drew around her hut) and told her not to give alms to anyone outside it. Sita Mata could not see through Ravana's disguise, and she ended up being carried away by him.

Ignoring the Guru's word and giving to anyone at all, even to the undeserving, simply because you feel like it: this is what life looks like when only the heart chakra has awakened.

*Is my compassion hurting me and the people I love?*

## From Leadership to Surrender

People at the Vishuddhi chakra are radiant. They can lead and they can change the world. They live for others, yet a thought lingers in the mind: "My name will be known too."

Those who reach the Ajna chakra live for the Guru. They are in **purna sharanagati** (complete surrender): the Guru, the Guru, and only the Guru. The Guru's work becomes the purpose of their life.

At the Sahasrara, the person has already dissolved into the Guru or into God.

## Seven Worlds We See Around Us

These seven levels are why we see seven different worlds around us. Very often, people spend their entire life, and even many lifetimes, at a single level.

*Which chakra's world am I living in today?*

## Shaktipat: Fast-Forwarding Through Lifetimes

This is where the Guru's role becomes clear. In **shaktipat** (the Guru's transmission of spiritual energy), when the Guru raises the seeker's **Kundalini** (the dormant spiritual energy), he breaks the bonds of all those lifetimes. He moves you forward by that many lives in one stroke. That is why Acharya Ji warns you never to treat sadhana as a game.

## When the Old World Falls Away

Once the Kundalini rises, you suddenly lose track of who you used to be and who you are now. Many people forget what their old world was like, and the wrong company they once kept.

Acharya Ji says you will experience this yourself within a few days. Either the wrong people will leave you on their own, or you will feel the urge to move away from them, or they will become better people through their bond with you. He calls this his guarantee.

## Key Takeaways

- Rising from one chakra to the next takes many lifetimes, but a competent Guru can raise you three or four chakras in a single sadhana.
- The seven chakras are seven ways of living: self-interest, family, service for gain, compassion without discernment, leadership, surrender, and merging into God.
- Compassion needs discernment and the Guru's guidance, or it turns into idiotic compassion.
- In shaktipat, the Guru breaks the bonds of lifetimes and carries your life forward, so sadhana is no game.
- Once the Kundalini rises, bad company falls away or changes on its own.
