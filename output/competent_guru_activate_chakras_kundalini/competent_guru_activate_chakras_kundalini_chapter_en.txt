# Chapter: The Competent Guru and the Rising of Kundalini

## One Chakra, Many Lifetimes

Rising by even one chakra takes many lifetimes. An ordinary person spends many births on a single chakra. But a competent Guru can raise three or four chakras at once in a single sadhana. For some, the whole ascent, from Muladhara to Sahasrara, can happen, just as Valya the robber became the sage Valmiki.

## Muladhara and Svadhishthana

A person living at the Muladhara chakra perhaps lives only for selfish ends, only for himself, and is ready even to kill or die for it. Above Muladhara is the Svadhishthana chakra. A person living at this level lives only for "me and my family." Have you seen all such people or not, who live only that much of a life?

## Manipura: Service for Gain

Then come the people who have reached the Manipura chakra. They do help the world, but there must be some gain for themselves and for their family in it. Such people often work for name and fame. In the name of social service, or on some other pretext, they will help only when they can see their own gain: "First show us our benefit, only then will we do it."

## The Heart Chakra: Compassion Without Discernment

After this come the people of the heart chakra. Their compassion is called idiotic compassion. Such people give their entire month's salary to the temple and leave their home and family hungry. This too is idiotic compassion.

Look at the example of Mother Sita. Lakshmana had drawn the Lakshmana Rekha and said not to give alms to anyone beyond the line. Mother Sita could not recognise Ravana, and as a result she had to go with him. This kind of giving shows that the heart chakra is awake, but discernment is not with it.

You must also have seen people who think, "Even if my own life is ruined, it does not matter, but I must keep helping people." They never use their intellect. They do not listen to the Guru, they keep doing service and giving charity as they please, and they keep giving even to the undeserving. This is the state when only the heart chakra is awake.

## Vishuddhi: Radiant Leadership

Above the heart is the Vishuddhi chakra. People of this chakra are radiant and have the capacity to lead. They can lead, they can change the world. You must also have seen such people, who do live for others, but with the feeling in the mind that "my name will also be made by this."

## Ajna and Sahasrara: Complete Surrender and Dissolution

Those who reach the Ajna chakra are people who live for the Guru. They are in **complete surrender**: only Guru, Guru, Guru. The Guru's work alone is the aim of their life.

And Sahasrara means that the person has himself dissolved into the Guru or the Supreme Lord.

## Seven Worlds Outside

Have you seen such different people or not? These are people of seven levels. Because of these seven levels, do you see seven different worlds outside or not? And it often appears that their whole life has passed on one single level. Just as I described, the whole life is like that, for many years, many births.

## Sadhana Is Not a Game

Now understand what the Guru sometimes does in sadhana. When in one sadhana your kundalini, your chakras, are raised, the Guru breaks the bondage of that many births and fast-forwards you by that many lives.

> "Do not take sadhana to be a game."

This is what **shaktipat** means. Have you now understood what happens when the kundalini shakti rises?

## The Old World Falls Away

Then suddenly you forget who I was before and who I am now. Many people simply forget what their earlier world was, whom they lived with, that they lived with the wrong people.

You too will experience it. After some days, either bad people will leave you on their own, or a wish will arise in you to move away from them, or they will join you and become good themselves.

> "This is my guarantee."
