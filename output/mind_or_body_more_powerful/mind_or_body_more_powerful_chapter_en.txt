# Chapter: First the Body, Then the Intellect: Mahakali, the First Worship

## Mahakali, Goddess of Body-Awareness

Mahakali is the goddess of self-defence, the goddess of body-awareness. That is why our first need is a strong, nourished body.

What do we see first today? Do we see the soul, or do we see the body? To be very frank, if someone asks, "Who am I?", isn't this the first answer that comes: my name is so-and-so, this body is called by this name, I am dark or fair, tall or long? Above all, what I see first is the body. The first importance goes to the body; the body is what I see first. That is why Mahakali comes first.

## Pen or Sword: What the Upanishads Say

The British said something: "The pen is mightier than the sword." They say the pen is superior to the sword. But open our Upanishads sometime and see. They say the body comes first, and the intellect comes later. Our Upanishads say that, given a choice, your body should first be strong and powerful. That is why the worship of Kali is taught first of all.

Why? Suppose someone gains great intellect, becomes a scientist, discovers a formula. And then another person comes along, a mindless thug, and puts a gun to his head. You composed a poem, created a work, and someone else stole it and walked away. It can be stolen in a minute. That intellectual is not of use through intellect alone. Along with it, he must be strong, and not only physically, but strong in every way.

## A Sound Mind in a Sound Body

That is why it is said, "A sound mind lives in a sound body." Swami Vivekananda said, first play football, then you will understand Vedanta. The same thing is said in our Chandogya Upanishad:

> **annamayaṁ hi somya manaḥ**
> "O gentle one, the mind is made of food."
> (Chandogya Upanishad 6.5.4)

Aruni Uddalaka taught exactly this to Shvetaketu. He said, fast for fifteen days and then tell me what happens. Shvetaketu went hungry for fifteen days. His body grew weaker and weaker, he felt dizzy, and he could not recite even a single mantra. Then Uddalaka says that the first strength is physical strength.

And the protection of whatever you have is absolutely essential. That is why the worship of Kali is the first worship. The first episode itself belongs to Kali.

## Think Big, but Protect First

Now suppose you have started a company. Naturally, the first feeling in your mind will be that it should expand and grow big. Shri Vidya says, "Think big." Fine, think big, but what will be the result? Competition, opposition and enemies are bound to come.

Then I must immediately get copyright, trademarks, patents, all of it done. This is the power of Kali. The intelligence must come first that I need to protect my creation before anything else. Not the sword first, but legal protection. And when my organisation grows a little more, don't you need to hire watchmen?

## Protecting Non-Violence Needs the Brave

When the sages had to perform a yajna, they said to Shri Ram, "Ram and Lakshman, come and stand there with your bows and arrows, O King, for the demons come."

> "For non-violent things, you need violent people around you."

If you want to do the work of non-violence, you need people beside you who can use force. That means the Kshatriyas, that is, the police, the army and our security guards; these are essential. And that is Kali.
