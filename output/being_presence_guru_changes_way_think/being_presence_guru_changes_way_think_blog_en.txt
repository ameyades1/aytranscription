# How the Guru's Presence Changes the Way You Think

A seeker from the Guru Sannidhya **Shibir** (a gathering for spiritual practice and teaching) shares their experience. Spending a few days with Gurudev, and having Gurudev spend those days with them, changed their whole way of thinking.

## When Every Part of Life Starts to Make Sense

**Sannidhya** (presence) means being near. At this residential Shibir, seekers spent several days close to Gurudev.

The seeker describes many revelations during that time. They touched every area of life: service to the Guru and the Guru's work, the means of earning a living, and relationships at home. Every single aspect of life brought its own revelation.

## One Teaching, Many Directions

When Acharya Ji teaches the Bhagavad Gita, he opens it up at different levels of life: the individual, the family, business, society, and even politics.

The same thing happened at the Shibir. A single insight began to connect with every part of life. The seeker's mind turned first to service to the Guru, the Guru's work, and work for the nation and the world, because that is where they are fully engaged right now. Gurudev has given them so much strength, understanding and **knowledge**.

Then a question arose: how could this same insight change things in the family? In business? In each place, it revealed something different.

*Have you ever taken one lesson and tried living it in every part of your life?*

## Even a Shared Meal Becomes a Classroom

The learning did not stay inside the teaching sessions. Living together, meeting one another, even sitting down to eat together: every moment brought some experience or example to light.

From these came a deep realisation about how to conduct yourself, how to hold back your own **action** (doing) and simply let things happen. In other words, let it be, and do not entangle yourself in anything.

## Play Brings Back a Lost Childhood

The biggest realisation came not in the daytime sessions but in the games at night. In these games during the Guru Sannidhya, Gurudev teaches seekers to play like children.

We have forgotten our own childhood. In the seeker's words, the way Gurudev taught them to bring it back was "mind-blowing".

*When did you last play like a child, without a single worry?*

## "I Am Here, So Why Are You Afraid?"

It was only a game, yet Gurudev's message came through it: Gurudev is with Anand, so there is no reason for Anand to be afraid. The way forward is to keep going and to think as big as Anand wants to.

For the seeker, this was the greatest realisation of all: Gurudev is with them. So let go of fear, think as big as you can, and take on the biggest work for the world that you can.

At the end, the seeker offered Gurudev heartfelt thanks, many times over, for this Shibir.

## Key Takeaways

- The Guru's presence brings clarity to every area of life: service, earning and relationships.
- Like the Gita, one piece of knowledge applies to the individual, the family, business and society.
- Living and eating together are chances to learn too.
- Holding back your own action and letting things happen is a deep realisation.
- Through play, Gurudev gives back a forgotten childhood.
- The Guru is with you, so think big and act big without fear.
