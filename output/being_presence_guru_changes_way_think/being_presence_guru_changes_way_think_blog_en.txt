# How the Guru's Presence Changes the Way We Think

A seeker from the Guru Sannidhya Shivir shares their experience. A few days of living with Gurudev, and of Gurudev living with them, changed their whole way of thinking.

## When Every Aspect of Life Brings a Realization

**Sannidhya** means nearness (presence). At this residential retreat, seekers spent a few days close to Gurudev.

The seeker says that many realizations (revelations) came during this time. They touched every area of life: guru seva and the Guru's work, personal means of earning a livelihood, and relationships at home. There were revelations about every single thing.

## One Knowledge, Many Directions

When Acharya ji teaches the Bhagavad Gita, he opens it up at different levels of life: the individual, the family, the profession, society, and even politics.

The same happened at the retreat. A single insight began to connect with every area. First the mind turned to guru seva, the Guru's work, work for the nation and work for the world, because the seekers are fully absorbed in it right now. Gurudev has given them so much strength, so much realization, so much knowledge.

Then the question arose: how can the same insight make a difference in the family? In one's profession? Everywhere it was realized in its own way.

*Have you ever tried carrying one lesson into every area of your life?*

## When Even a Meal Becomes a Classroom

The learning was not limited to the knowledge sessions. Living together, meeting one another, even sitting down to eat together, every moment brought some experience or example.

And from them came a deep realization: how to act, how to hold back one's own **karma** (doing) and simply let things happen. In other words, just let it happen, and do not entangle yourself in anything.

## A Lost Childhood Returns Through Play

The greatest realization came not in the daytime sessions but in the games at night. In these games in the Guru's presence, Gurudev teaches the seekers to play like a child.

We have forgotten our own childhood. How Gurudev teaches us to bring it back was, in the seeker's words, "mind-blowing".

*When did you last play like a child, without any worry?*

## "I Am Here, Why Are You Afraid?"

The game was only a game, yet within it came Gurudev's message that he is here, that Anand need not be afraid, and that he should go ahead and think as big as he wants to think.

For the seeker, this was the greatest realization: Gurudev is with us. So let go of fear, think as big as you can, and do as great a work for the world as you can.

In closing, the seeker offered Gurudev countless thanks for this retreat.

## Key Takeaways

- In the Guru's presence, realizations come about every area of life: service, livelihood and relationships.
- Like the Gita, one knowledge applies to the individual, the family, the profession and society.
- Living together and eating together are also chances to learn.
- Holding back one's own doing and simply letting things happen is a deep realization.
- Through play, Gurudev brings back our forgotten childhood.
- The Guru is with us, so think big and do great work without fear.
