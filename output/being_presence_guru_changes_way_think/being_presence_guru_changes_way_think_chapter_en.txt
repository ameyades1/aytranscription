# Chapter: How the Guru's Presence Changes the Way We Think

## Many Realizations at the Retreat

Gurudev, pranam.

I would especially like to speak about this residential retreat, the Guru Sannidhya Shivir, and say that it brought many realizations. Living with Gurudev, and with Gurudev living among us, so much was revealed about different aspects of life: whether guru seva and the Guru's work, personal means of earning a livelihood, or our home and the relationships within it. There were realizations about every single thing.

## One Knowledge, Many Areas of Life

Just as Acharya ji teaches us the Shrimad Bhagavad Gita, he teaches it through different aspects of life: first the individual, then one's family, then one's profession, society, and even politics. In the same way, we can relate one and the same insight to different aspects of life and see that yes, it can be useful here too.

The first thing that comes to mind is guru seva, because right now we are fully absorbed in it. Gurudev has given us so much strength, so much realization, so much knowledge. So the first thought is of guru seva, the Guru's work, work for the nation and work for the world, and how this insight can make a difference there. Then how the same insight can make a difference in our family, and in our profession. In every place it is realized in its own way, and the understanding keeps deepening.

## Learning in Every Moment

Whether in the knowledge sessions, or while all of us were living together and interacting with everyone, or even when we simply sat down to eat, many experiences came to us and many examples appeared before us. And then comes the realization of how we must act there, how we must hold back our own doing and simply let it happen; not entangle ourselves in anything.

## The Greatest Realization, Through Play

And the greatest realization comes through play. Once the knowledge session was over and the whole day had passed, the games played at night in the Guru's presence brought the most realizations. The way Gurudev teaches us to play like a child! We have forgotten our own childhood. How to bring that childhood back, as Gurudev teaches it, is astonishing. That is the most astonishing thing of all.

It was only a game, yet so many realizations come through play that you would not believe it. Gurudev is telling us:

> "I am here, Anand! I am here, why are you afraid? Go ahead. Think big, think as big as you want to think. I am here, why are you afraid?"

## "I Am With You"

This is the most important, the greatest realization: that Gurudev wants to tell us:

> "I am with you. Think as big as you can think; do as great a work for the world as you can."

Gurudev, countless thanks for this retreat.
