# Chapter: Twin Souls, Soulmates and Karmic Bonds

## Lakshmi's Curse and Separation from Narayana

Lakshmi carried a curse through many lifetimes, and it was given by her own father. The curse was: you will never be able to meet Narayana, and even if you do, separation will keep happening.

That is why, when she came as Sita, even though she came as his wife, she had to leave. When she came as Radha, she came close, yet remained distant from the relationship. In the lives of ordinary people this keeps happening anyway, but here it was for a divine play. The two of them will always love the most; they will always remain like husband and wife.

## The First Type: Twin Souls (the Divine Couple)

Such a relationship is called **yugal vivah**, also *twin flames*, and also the **divine couple**. It means they will always remain a couple; they are never separated.

> "It is the same soul, born in two bodies. They are not two separate beings at all."

This is called twin souls.

## The Second Type: Soulmates

The second type is the **soulmate**. These are two separate beings, but they come for positive work; they come with love.

## The Third Type: Karmic Bonds

The third type is the **karmic bond**. Such people come only to release a karmic bondage. These relationships mostly hold sorrow; grief and pain are greater.

> "Ninety-nine percent of people are karmic."

That is why we call it a karmic bond.

Many people also meet soulmates. They come and meet only for auspicious work; their work is done with love, and such people meet very quickly.

## The Divine Couple: Two Forms of One Soul

In our own language we have called the twin flame the divine couple; their yugal vivah has already taken place. The relationship of Radha and Krishna is called yugal vivah. They have no need for a physical marriage, because they are one.

It is one soul that wanted to have opposite experiences. It wants the experience of a woman and also of a man; how could that happen in one body? Hence two bodies. It wants to experience Shukra and also Guru; how can that be had at the same time? So initially such people are total opposites.

## One Soul, Many Bodies

It has also been said that the same soul can be born in two, three or four bodies. The practical meaning is this: as long as your own state remains the same, whether you marry once, twice, four times or ten times, a new person will keep coming from the same essence, born of the same part.

You say, "The same thing happens to me every time, love goes bad, there is nothing in it, there is nothing in marriage." In truth it is the same soul that has taken different forms. It knows that you are going to marry four times and that all four times you are not going to change, so the same soul is born in four places. Such souls exist.

> "When you understand love, you will understand all this. It is a very deep concept."
