## 6. FAQ

Kept in the report only (not added to the page or to the schema).

1. **ट्विन फ्लेम और सोलमेट में क्या अंतर है? / What is the difference between a twin flame and a soulmate?**
   In Acharya Ji's teaching a twin flame is one soul in two bodies, a pair that never parts; soulmates are two separate beings who come together out of love for a positive purpose.
2. **कर्म-बंधन वाला रिश्ता क्या है? / What is a karmic bond?**
   A relationship that comes only to release the bonds of past actions. It mostly brings pain, grief and suffering, and Acharya Ji says ninety-nine percent of people are in such bonds.
3. **ट्विन फ्लेम एक-दूसरे से इतने अलग क्यों दिखते हैं? / Why do twin flames seem like opposites?**
   Because the one soul needed opposite experiences, the woman's and the man's, the nature of Shukra and of Guru, which one body cannot hold at the same time.
4. **मुझे हर बार वही इंसान क्यों मिलता है? / Why do I keep meeting the same person?**
   Acharya Ji says that as long as your own state stays the same, a new partner keeps coming from the same essence; the same soul arrives in different forms. The question to ask is whether your own state needs to change.
5. **क्या राधा-कृष्ण ट्विन फ्लेम थे? / Were Radha and Krishna twin flames?**
   Acharya Ji gives Radha and Krishna as the example of the divine couple: one in essence, so they had no need of a physical marriage. This is his reading; Radha-centred traditions describe their relationship differently (flag 2).

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Who was cursed** (Sanskrit review, issue 1, Major). The blog says Lakshmi was cursed by her own father never to unite with Narayana; the known story (Bhrigu's curse) falls on Vishnu, and "never unite" contradicts Sita being his wife. Recommendation: apply the review's wording before publishing. No title, meta, snippet, FAQ or link anchor repeats the curse claim; the Lakshmi link anchors on "they will always remain as husband and wife".
2. **Radha as Lakshmi, and "no formal bond"** (review, issue 2, Major). Radha-centred sampradayas do not see Radha as a form of Lakshmi, and the Brahmavaivarta Purana describes Brahma marrying Radha and Krishna. Attribute to Acharya Ji as the review suggests. FAQ 5 attributes the Radha-Krishna example to him; do not use "Radha Krishna twin flames" as a title or keyword that presents it as scriptural fact.
3. **"Yugal vivah" and "twin flame" as terms** (review, issue 3, Minor). "युगल विवाह" in this sense is the speaker's term, and "twin flame" is a modern, New Age label. The SEO texts use "twin flame" only as the reader's search term and explain it as Acharya Ji's divine couple; the secondary keyword is "divine couple yugal", not "twin flame in scripture". Do not add "twin flame in Hinduism" or "Vedic twin flame" claims.
4. **One soul in several bodies** (review, issue 4, Minor). The standard account (Gita 2.22, 15.8, Gita Press) has the jiva in one body at a time. This is Acharya Ji's teaching; snippets and FAQ 4 attribute it to him. Apply the review's attribution in "एक आत्मा, अनेक चेहरे" / "One Soul, Many Faces".
5. **Venus and Jupiter note** (review, issue 5, Minor). The English adds that Jyotish links Venus and Jupiter with wife and husband; the Hindi lacks it. Add it to the Hindi as the review suggests. The Shukra link (to shukra_graha_chanting_wealth_relationships) fits either way.
6. **"Ninety-nine percent".** This is the speaker's own estimate, not a statistic. FAQ 2 attributes it to Acharya Ji; the English snippet says "most relationships". Do not put the number in a title or meta as a fact.
7. **Readers in painful relationships.** "Karmic bond" and "settle an old debt" could be read as a reason to stay in an abusive relationship. The talk does not say that, and the SEO texts must not either. Recommendation (editor's decision): stop_tolerating_toxic_love is in Related Reading; consider an inline link to it if the karmic bond section is expanded.
8. **Focus keyword placement.** Neither focus keyword is in the first 100 words (section 4). The title tags and meta carry the queries in the meantime; naming the three kinds in the opening line is optional.
