# Why Do You Keep Meeting the Same Person

Have you ever felt that relationships change, faces change, yet the story stays the same? In this short session Acharya Upendra Ji explains three kinds of relationships, and opens a secret that can change how we look at love.

## Lakshmi and Narayana: A Separation Lifetimes Old

The story goes that Lakshmi was cursed by her own father, and that curse followed her for many lifetimes. The curse was: you will never be able to meet Narayana, and even if you do, separation will keep happening.

That is why she came as Sita, as his wife, and still had to leave. She came as Radha, came close, yet stayed distant from the relationship.

All of this was for a **leela** (divine play). The two of them will always love the most; they will always remain like husband and wife.

## The First Type: Twin Souls, the Divine Couple

This relationship is called **yugal vivah**, in today's language a *twin flame*, and in our tradition the **divya dampati** (divine couple). They always remain a couple; they are never separated.

> "It is the same soul, born in two bodies. They are not two separate beings at all."

The relationship of Radha and Krishna is called yugal vivah. They have no need for a physical marriage, because they are one.

Then why two bodies? Because that one soul had to have opposite experiences. The experience of a woman and of a man; of Shukra and of Guru. In one body, at one time, this is not possible. So in the beginning such people seem completely opposite.

## The Second Type: Soulmates

**Soulmates** are two separate beings, but they come for positive work, with love.

They meet only for auspicious work, their work is done with love, and they meet very quickly.

## The Third Type: Karmic Bonds

The third is the **karmic bond**. Such people come only to release the bondage of old karma. These relationships mostly hold sorrow, grief and pain.

> "Ninety-nine percent of people are karmic."

*Recall the hardest relationship of your life. Did it come to teach you something, to settle some old account?*

## One Soul, Many Faces

Now the deepest point. The same soul can be born in two, three or four bodies.

This means: as long as your own state stays the same, marry once, twice, four or ten times, a new person will keep coming from the same essence, of the same part.

You say, "The same thing happens to me every time, love goes bad, there is nothing in marriage." But the truth is that the same soul is coming in different forms. It knows you will marry four times and will not change all four times, so the same soul is born in four places.

*If the same story keeps repeating, does the change need to happen in the other person, or in my own state?*

> "When you understand love, you will understand all this. It is a very deep concept."

## Key Takeaways

- Because of her father's curse, Lakshmi suffered separation from Narayana again and again: as Sita and as Radha.
- A twin soul, or divine couple, is one soul born in two bodies for opposite experiences; Radha and Krishna are the example.
- Soulmates are separate beings who meet for love and auspicious work.
- Karmic bonds exist to release old karma, they hold more sorrow, and ninety-nine percent of relationships are these.
- Until your own state changes, the same soul will keep entering your life in new forms.
