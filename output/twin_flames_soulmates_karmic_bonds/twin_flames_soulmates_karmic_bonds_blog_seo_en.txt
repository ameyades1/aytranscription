# Why Do You Keep Meeting the Same Person?

Have you ever felt that your relationships change and the faces change, yet the story stays the same? In this short session, Acharya Upendra Ji explains three kinds of relationships (twin flames, soulmates and karmic bonds) and reveals a secret that can change the way you look at love.

In short, Acharya Ji's answer to twin flame vs soulmate: a twin flame (the divine couple) is one soul born in two bodies to live through opposite experiences, as with Radha and Krishna, while soulmates are two separate beings who meet out of love, for good work. Karmic bonds come to release past karma and mostly bring suffering.

## Lakshmi and Narayana: A Separation Lasting Lifetimes

In the story as Acharya Ji tells it, Devi Lakshmi was cursed by her own father, and the curse followed her through many lifetimes. It declared that she would never be able to unite with Narayana (Lord Vishnu), and that even when she did meet him, separation would keep returning.

That is why, when she came as Sita, she arrived as his wife and still had to leave. When she came as Radha, she drew close to him, yet stayed apart from any formal bond.

All of this unfolded as part of a **lila** (divine play). The two will always love each other more than anyone else, and they will always remain as husband and wife.

## The First Kind: What Is a Twin Flame?

Acharya Ji calls this bond **yugal vivah** (in his usage, the union of twin souls). Today people call it a *twin flame*, and our tradition calls it **divya dampati** (the divine couple). Such a pair remains a pair forever and never parts. Acharya Ji explains that a single soul has taken birth in two bodies, so the two are not really separate at all.

The relationship of Radha and Krishna is what Acharya Ji calls yugal vivah. They have no need of a physical marriage, because they are already one.

Why, then, two bodies? Because that one soul needed to live through opposite experiences: the woman's and the man's, the nature of Venus and that of Jupiter, the planets Vedic astrology links with wife and husband. One body cannot hold both at the same time. That is why, at first, such people seem to be complete opposites.

## The Second Kind: Soulmates

**Soulmates** are two separate beings, but they come together out of love, for a positive purpose.

They meet only to do something good, they do it with love, and they find each other very quickly.

## The Third Kind: What Is a Karmic Bond?

The third kind is the **karmic bond**. These people come into your life only to release the bonds of past actions. Such relationships mostly bring pain, grief and suffering. Acharya Ji says that ninety-nine percent of people are in karmic bonds.

*Think of the hardest relationship of your life. Did that person come to teach you something, or to settle an old debt?*

## One Soul, Many Faces

Now comes the deepest point of all. Acharya Ji says that a single soul can take birth in two, three, even four bodies.

This means that as long as your own inner state stays the same, it makes no difference whether you marry once, twice, four times or ten. Each new partner will come from the same essence, carrying a part of the same soul.

You say, "The same thing happens to me every time. Love turns sour, and marriage has nothing to offer." The truth is that the same soul keeps arriving in different forms. It knows you will marry four times and fail to change all four times, so it takes birth in four different places.

*If the same story keeps repeating, does the other person need to change, or does my own state?*

Acharya Ji says that all of this will make sense once we understand love, because the principle behind it runs very deep.

## Key Takeaways

- Because of her father's curse, Devi Lakshmi suffered separation from Narayana again and again, both as Sita and as Radha.
- Twin souls, the divine couple, are one soul born in two bodies to live through opposite experiences; Radha and Krishna are the example.
- Soulmates are separate beings who meet for love and for good work.
- Karmic bonds exist to release past actions, bring more suffering, and account for ninety-nine percent of relationships.
- Until your own state changes, the same soul will keep entering your life in new forms.
