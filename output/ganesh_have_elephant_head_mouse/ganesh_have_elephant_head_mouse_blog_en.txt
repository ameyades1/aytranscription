# How Did Ganapati Become an Elephant and How Does He Ride a Mouse

## Have You Ever Asked This Question?

How many people have seen an elephant that walks like a human being? And how many elephants ride a mouse?

These questions may sound strange, but this is where understanding begins. Is Ganapati's form real or **symbolic**? This form was given for a reason, but we never understood that reason.

Imagine your child asks: "Mother, how did Ganapati become an elephant? And how does he sit on a mouse? Why doesn't the mouse get crushed?" What answer would you have?

*Has this question ever come to your own mind?*

## Every Deity Holds Something, Why?

The Goddess has six, eight, even eighteen hands. Vishnu holds the conch, the discus and the mace. Saraswati holds the veena. And Ganapati is described like this:

> **ekadantaṁ caturhastaṁ pāśamaṅkuśadhāriṇam |**
> **radaṁ ca varadaṁ hastairbibhrāṇaṁ mūṣakadhvajam ||**
> "One-tusked, four-handed, bearing the noose and the goad, holding the tusk and the boon-giving gesture, with the mouse on his banner."
> (Ganapati Atharvashirsha)

One tusk, four hands, the **pasha** (noose) and **ankusha** (the goad used to guide an elephant) in his hands, the boon-giving gesture, and the mouse on his banner.

Some people say that if the gods looked like us, people would not worship them. But it is not that simple. These are symbols, and until we understand their meaning, devotion will remain **andhashraddha** (blind faith).

> "Until you understand that symbolism, there will only be blind faith."

## The Idol: A Door Inward, Not Outward

We take a stone or a picture and believe it will bring us something. But nothing comes from outside.

> "The idol outside is a medium, to awaken the Supreme within you."

How many have ever received a direct answer from an idol and had their work done? Some twenty or twenty-five years ago we heard of a miracle of idols drinking milk. But it is not an everyday thing.

## Ganesha Is the Form of Omkara, but What Is Om?

In the Upanishads, Ganesha is called **Omkara-svarupa**, the form of Om. We close our eyes and chant Om. But is Om only a word?

When it is said that Ganesha is Omkara, we first need to understand what Om truly is.

## Where Is the Elephant in the Atharvashirsha?

Many people recite the Ganesha Atharvashirsha, and do its **sahasravartan** (a thousand recitations). But its meaning?

> **tvaṁ brahmā tvaṁ viṣṇustvaṁ rudrastvamindrastvamagnistvaṁ vāyustvaṁ sūryaḥ...**
> "You are Brahma, you are Vishnu, you are Rudra, Indra, Agni, Vayu and Surya."
> (Ganapati Atharvashirsha)

You are Brahma, Vishnu, Rudra, Indra, Agni, Vayu and Surya. But "you are an elephant" is written nowhere. So what is the connection between the elephant form and this hymn?

## A Finite Ruler Cannot Measure the Infinite

Here the worship of both **sakara** (with form) and **nirakara** (beyond form) is taught, and the two are connected.

Our body is finite. What we see has a limit. What we hear has a limit too.

Now think: can you measure the infinite with a finite ruler or measuring tool? Never.

The **Param Tattva** (the Supreme Truth) is formless and infinite. The infinite cannot be seen, and cannot be touched by any finite thing.

> "Infinite cannot be seen. Infinite cannot be touched by a finite thing."

## That Is Why the Rishis Gave Us a Form

The real worship is meant to be of that infinite, but we are unable to do it. So the rishis and sages gave a limited form, because the limited can see the limited, the form can see the form.

A plain or round stone could have been kept too. But it was given hands, feet and an elephant's head, so that the Supreme Principle could be described. That is why every hand, every object and every **vahana** (vehicle) has a meaning.

And none of this is outside. It is about the qualities within you. Shiva, Parvati and Ganesha are all within you.

*The form you worship: which quality within you does it point to?*

## So Many Modaks, Yet Why Obstacles?

So many modaks, so much durva grass, so many red flowers are offered. Then no **vighna** (obstacle) should ever have come to India.

Yet our country's happiness index is among the lowest, and India is called the diabetes capital. If everything is so sacred and going so well, then why is it so?

## Key Takeaways

- Ganapati's elephant form and mouse vehicle are symbols; without understanding them, devotion becomes blind faith.
- The objects in every deity's hands and their vehicles have a meaning.
- The idol is a medium to awaken the Supreme within; nothing comes from outside.
- Ganesha is the form of Omkara, and Om must be understood.
- The infinite cannot be measured with a finite tool, which is why the rishis gave a form.
- Shiva, Parvati and Ganesha all speak of qualities within us.
