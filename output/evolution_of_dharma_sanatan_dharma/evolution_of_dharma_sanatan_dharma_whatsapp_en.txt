*Why do we worship at all?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains how Sanatan Dharma grew through the Vedas, from praise to action, reflection and finally Self-knowledge. Just as love shows in deeds, praise became yajna, charity and worship.

💭 _Does your love for those closest to you show in your actions, or only in words?_
