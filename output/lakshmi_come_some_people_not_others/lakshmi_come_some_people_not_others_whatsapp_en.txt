*Why does Lakshmi come to some people and not others?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that Lakshmi is lakshya, a goal. Her red sari shows what keeps her: love for those you serve, full-strength action and sacrifice for society.

💭 _In your work, do you love the people you serve, or only the money?_
