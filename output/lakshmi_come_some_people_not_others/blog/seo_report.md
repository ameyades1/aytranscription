## 1. Summary

A short talk on who receives wealth. Acharya Ji reads Lakshmi not as an idol but as लक्ष्य (a goal, a focus): she comes when one gains the strength to pursue a goal, and the size of the goal (a household, a nation, the world) decides how much comes. Lakshmi is चंचल and does not stay when she comes alone; her red sari shows what she comes with: love for the people one serves, and rajoguna and यज्ञ भाव (full-strength action and sacrifice for society). Durga Saptashati 4.5 (Lakshmi in the homes of the virtuous, Alakshmi in the homes of the sinful) is balanced by the note that poverty may be a passing karmic phase or renunciation. Lakshmi's consort Vishnu is the Sustainer: the more one provides for, the more wealth comes. The main intent is informational with a strong emotional / life-problem layer: "लक्ष्मी किसके पास आती है", "घर में लक्ष्मी क्यों नहीं ठहरती" (the video thumbnail's own phrasing), "why Lakshmi does not stay". Results for these are dominated by vastu and upay listicles (remedies, dos and don'ts at home). A symbolic, values-based answer from Durga Saptashati and the Lakshmi-Vishnu pairing is a clear, distinct angle, but it must not be dressed as a remedy post (section 10).

## 6. FAQ

**1. लक्ष्मी कुछ लोगों के पास ही क्यों आती है? / Why does Lakshmi come to some people and not others?**
आचार्य जी के अनुसार लक्ष्मी लक्ष्य हैं; जिसका लक्ष्य जितना बड़ा है और जिसमें उसे साधने की शक्ति है, उसके पास उतना धन आता है।
According to Acharya Ji, Lakshmi is lakshya, a goal; wealth comes in proportion to the size of your goal and your strength to pursue it.

**2. लक्ष्मी को चंचल क्यों कहते हैं? / Why is Lakshmi called restless (chanchala)?**
लक्ष्मी अकेली आएं तो अधिक दिन टिकती नहीं। वे प्रेम, कर्मशीलता और त्याग के साथ आती हैं, तभी ठहरती हैं।
When Lakshmi comes alone, she does not stay for long. She stays when she comes with love, drive and sacrifice.

**3. लक्ष्मी की लाल साड़ी का क्या अर्थ है? / What does Lakshmi's red sari mean?**
लाल रंग प्रेम और हृदय का रंग है, और रजोगुण तथा यज्ञ भाव का भी: पूरी शक्ति से कर्म और समाज के लिए त्याग।
Red is the colour of love and the heart, and also of rajoguna and the spirit of offering: acting with full strength and sacrificing for society.

**4. क्या पैसा न होने का अर्थ है कि मैं पापी हूं? / Does a lack of money mean I am a sinner?**
ज़रूरी नहीं। दुर्गा सप्तशती देवी को पुण्यात्माओं के घर लक्ष्मी और पापियों के घर अलक्ष्मी कहती है, पर अभाव किसी कर्म का अस्थायी भोग या वैराग्य भी हो सकता है।
Not necessarily. The Durga Saptashati calls the Goddess Lakshmi in the homes of the virtuous and Alakshmi in the homes of wrongdoers, but a lack of money may be a passing fruit of past action, or renunciation.

**5. विष्णु से लक्ष्मी का क्या संबंध है? / How is Lakshmi connected with Vishnu?**
लक्ष्मी पालनकर्ता विष्णु की पत्नी हैं; आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि जितने लोगों का आप पालन करते हैं, उतना धन आता है।
Lakshmi is the consort of Vishnu, the Sustainer; Acharya Ji says the more people you provide for, the more wealth comes.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **Hindi meaning of Durga Saptashati 4.5 is incomplete (from the Sanskrit review, Major).** The Hindi gloss drops "स्वयं" and the third idea (बुद्धि in the hearts of the wise), so it differs from the English. Recommendation: apply the review's correction (full meaning, then the Lakshmi/Alakshmi point as commentary). Until then, the FAQ and snippets state only the Lakshmi/Alakshmi part, attributed to the Durga Saptashati, which the verse does support. No internal link is anchored on the verse.
2. **Half verse not marked as such (from the review, Minor).** Recommendation: "(दुर्गा सप्तशती ४.५, पूर्वार्ध)" / "(Durga Saptashati 4.5, first half)". The review also asks that "यजुर्वेदः स्वरूपम्" be marked as a fragment of the Madhyama Charitra viniyoga, not a verse.
3. **"The root of Lakshmi is lakshya" (from the review, Minor).** Both words share the root लक्ष्; Lakshmi is not derived from lakshya. Recommendation: apply the review's rewording. Titles, meta, slug and FAQ present "Lakshmi as lakshya" as Acharya Ji's teaching (a nirukti), never as etymology; "लक्ष्मी और लक्ष्य" is kept as a Low-priority keyword only.
4. **"Lakshmi alone is like an unmarried woman" has no traced source (from the review, Minor).** Recommendation: attribute it to Acharya Ji, as the review suggests. It is kept out of titles, meta, FAQ and social cards.
5. **Remedy and fear intent of the thumbnail query.** "घर में लक्ष्मी क्यों नहीं ठहरती" draws readers looking for upay, vastu fixes or fear of "dosh". The talk gives values (goal, love, effort, sacrifice), not rituals. Recommendation: use the query as a secondary keyword only, never promise remedies or guaranteed wealth, and avoid "how to attract Lakshmi" headlines.
6. **Poverty is not sin.** Alakshmi in "the homes of the sinful" can be read as blaming the poor. The blog itself says a lack of money may be karma's passing fruit or renunciation, and that the richest may be a thief. Recommendation: keep that balance in any snippet or social card that quotes the verse (done in FAQ 4).
7. **"Pauper to king" and the dasha line.** These are Acharya Ji's encouragement, not a financial or astrological promise. Recommendation: do not use "become rich" style claims in titles or ads.
