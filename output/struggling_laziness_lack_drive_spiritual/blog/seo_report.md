## 6. FAQ

1. **आलस्य क्यों नहीं जाता? / Why won't my laziness go away?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार आलस्य तमोगुण का प्रमाण है, और तमोगुण से सीधी छलांग नहीं लगती; उससे निकलने के लिए पहले रजोगुण में आना पड़ता है। / Acharya Ji says laziness is a sign of tamoguna, and you cannot leap straight out of it; you first have to pass through rajoguna.
2. **आलस्य कैसे दूर करें? / How do I overcome laziness?**
   कर्तव्य-बोध बढ़ाकर, यह भाव कि "मुझे यह करना ही है।" जब कर्तव्य का बोध होता है, तो तमस चला जाता है और हम रजस में आ जाते हैं। / By strengthening your sense of duty, the feeling that "I must do this." Once that awareness awakens, tamas departs and you rise into rajas.
3. **आलस्य दूर करने में गुरु की क्या भूमिका है? / What role does a Guru play?**
   प्रत्यक्ष गुरु, जो जीवन में हस्तक्षेप करे, सीधी छलांग करा सकते हैं, और संस्कार बदल देते हैं, जिससे कर्म की इच्छा स्वयं जागती है। / A Guru who is present and steps into your life can make a direct leap possible and transforms your samskaras, so the urge to act awakens by itself.
4. **प्रेय और श्रेय में क्या अंतर है? / What is the difference between preya and shreya?**
   प्रेय वह है जो अच्छा लगता है, श्रेय वह जो कल्याणकारी है। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि श्रेष्ठ कर्म को ही अपना प्रिय बनाओ। / Preya is what feels pleasant; shreya is what is truly good for you. Acharya Ji says to make the best action the one you love.
5. **गुणातीत अवस्था क्या है? / What is the gunatita state?**
   सत्त्व के बाद की अवस्था, जहां तीनों गुण आपके अधीन हैं: आराम करना हो तो आराम, कार्य करना हो तो कार्य। / The state beyond sattva, in which all three gunas serve you: when you want to rest you rest, when you want to work you work.

FAQ 5 gives Acharya Ji's description; it is not presented as a translation of Gita 14.21 to 25 (see the review's note).

## 10. Authenticity flags

From the Sanskrit review (`tools/review/struggling_laziness_lack_drive_spiritual.md`, status "Minor issues"), plus notes specific to SEO:

1. **Valya and Narada stated as fact, English only (Minor, review 1).** The English adds "Valya was a highway robber; after meeting Narada, he became Maharshi Valmiki, the author of the Ramayana", which the Hindi does not have. The robber story is popular tradition (the Adhyatma Ramayana credits the Saptarishis). Recommendation: remove the sentence or use the review's softened wording ("According to popular tradition..."). The link anchor "transform a Valya into a Valmiki" is the speaker's own example and is kept; no title, meta or FAQ uses the story.
2. **Narada glossed as "the wandering sage of Indian lore" (Minor, review 2).** Recommendation: "the divine sage, or devarshi, of the Puranas" (Gita 10.26).
3. **The whip in the gurukul (SEO, sensitivity).** "पीछे कोड़ा होगा" / "A whip waits behind you" is a figure of speech for strict outside discipline. Do not quote it alone in social snippets or quote cards, where it could read as endorsing physical punishment.
4. **Laziness as a health issue (SEO).** Readers searching "laziness" or "lack of drive" may be facing depression, fatigue or illness. The blog treats laziness only as tamas and does not address these. Keep titles, metas and snippets to the spiritual angle, and do not claim the teaching cures low energy or depression. Do not chase medical queries ("आलस्य का इलाज", "laziness cure") with this post.
