*Is the Veda really a book?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that Veda is a verb, the process of knowing this world and the world beyond, and Vedanta is where that knowing is complete, learned near the Guru.

💭 _If the Veda is a journey of knowing, how far along that journey are you?_
