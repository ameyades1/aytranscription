# Why Blind Love Is Dangerous

Is having a good heart enough? We often believe that the one who loves everyone is the best. But Acharya Upendra Ji places a deep question on this idea.

## When the Heart Is Large but the Eyes Are Closed

When only the **Lakshmi tattva** (the power of love, feeling and compassion) is within, it is a little mad. The heart is very large, it loves everyone, but it does not understand who is in front of it.

Take the example of Jambavati. Her husband causes her pain, and still she says, "I am a devoted wife, this is my dharma." Shri Krishna says let go, but she does not let go.

## The Story of Samba: "He Will Improve Tomorrow"

Samba, the son of Shri Krishna and Jambavati, was born for the very destruction of the Yadu dynasty. Gandhari's curse was that just as the Kauravas drowned in **putra moha** (blind attachment to one's child), this line too would be destroyed by putra moha.

And that is what happened. The mother stayed stuck in putra moha. Samba again and again took power from Shri Krishna and caused destruction, and the mother's heart kept saying, "He will improve now, he will improve tomorrow, he will improve later."

> "This is called idiotic compassion."

*Are you, too, saying again and again about someone that "they will improve tomorrow"?*

## Lakshmi Without Narayana

Only Lakshmi, without Narayana, becomes **rajoguna** (the quality of activity and attachment), and the end of rajoguna is always sorrow. That is why in every story, whenever Lakshmi was separated from Vishnu, she received sorrow.

She does not understand where to love. Where Vishnu said do not go, not even to your parents' home, she goes there too. That is why she received curses many times.

## This Is a Symbol, the Matter Is Ours

This is not about any physical Lakshmi; it is a symbol. Someone says, "My heart is so good," but all the other departments are shut. The whole month's salary goes straight to the temple.

Was there no love in the home, in the family? Is love only for the Guru and the temple? The family too has a right over you. You created it, so its upbringing and care are also your responsibility. Give whatever remains after that.

Some people even take loans to do works of name, fame or charity outside. There is a Marathi saying, "Rin kadhun san sajra kar," that is, taking a loan to celebrate a festival.

> "You are not supposed to do that."

## Kali, Saraswati and Lakshmi: All Three Incomplete Alone

The Lakshmi tattva alone is the most dangerous. If Kali and Saraswati are in excess, there is, symbolically, quarreling and hair pulling. And only Lakshmi sits there forgiving both.

That is why all three, in their pure, isolated form, are useless. Only after **divine Guru bhakti** enters them do they become useful.

*Does your compassion come with discernment?*

## Why Is Lakshmi Still the Most Beloved?

The danger is greater, and yet everyone finds Lakshmi the most dear. Because in her there is emotion, there is **bhava** (the feeling of the heart), there is love.

> "One who loves is better than one who becomes a stone."

That is why, among the three, Lakshmi is considered the best, and in the Durga tradition the first manifestation is said to be that of Lakshmi.

## Key Takeaways

- Love alone, without discernment, becomes blind compassion and can become a cause of destruction.
- Attachment like putra moha keeps empowering what is wrong, in the hope that "they will improve tomorrow."
- Lakshmi without Narayana is rajoguna, and the end of rajoguna is sorrow.
- First the care of the family, then charity; no charity or show on borrowed money.
- Kali, Saraswati and Lakshmi are incomplete alone; only through Guru bhakti do they become complete.
- Still, it is better to be one who loves than to become a stone.
