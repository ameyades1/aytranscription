# Chapter: Why Blind Love Is Dangerous

## Only Lakshmi: Much Heart, Little Discernment

When only the Lakshmi principle is present within us, it is a little mad. The heart is very large, so it loves everyone, but it simply does not understand who is in front of it.

Look at Jambavati. Her husband causes her pain, and still she says, "I am a devoted wife, this is my dharma." And when Shri Krishna says to let go, she does not let go.

## Samba and Attachment to a Son

Samba was the son of Shri Krishna and Jambavati, and he was born for the very purpose of destroying the entire Yadu dynasty. Gandhari's curse was that just as the Kauravas were drowned in attachment to their sons, so too would your line be destroyed by attachment to a son.

Samba's mother, that is, Shri Krishna's own wife, gets stuck in attachment to her son. Every time, Samba took power from Shri Krishna and caused destruction, and his mother too became a cause of that destruction. Shri Krishna is dear to her, and she is also the one who loves the son, yet her heart kept saying: "He will become good, he will improve now, he will improve tomorrow, he will improve later."

> "This is called idiotic compassion."

## Lakshmi Without Narayana

Only Lakshmi, without Narayana, becomes rajoguna, and rajoguna always ends in sorrow. That is why in every story, whenever Lakshmi has been separated from Vishnu, she has received only sorrow.

She does not understand where to love. She loves anywhere, whether her own family or anyone else. Where Vishnu would have said not to go, not even to her parents' home, she will still go. That is why on many occasions she has received a curse from that side.

## This Is a Symbol, About Our Own Lives

This is symbolic; it is not about any physical Lakshmi. Someone says, "My heart is so good," but all the other departments are shut. The whole month's salary goes straight to the temple.

Was there no love in the home, in the family? Was love only for the Guru, only for your temple? The family too has a right over you, a claim. Why did you make that family then? You created it, so you must also see to its upbringing and care. Give whatever remains after that.

Sometimes people even take loans to do all these works outside, call it for name and fame or for charity. There is even a Marathi saying, "Rin kadhun san sajra kar," meaning, take a loan and celebrate a festival.

> "You are not supposed to do that."

## Kali, Saraswati and Lakshmi: All Three Incomplete in Pure Form

If only the Lakshmi principle has come into you, it is more dangerous. The other two are the quarrelsome ones: when Kali and Saraswati are in excess, there is, symbolically, hair pulling. And only Lakshmi sits there forgiving both.

That is why all three, in their pure, isolated form, are useless. Understand this much. Only after divine Guru bhakti enters them do they become useful.

## Why Lakshmi Is Still the Most Beloved

The danger is greater in Lakshmi, and yet Lakshmi is the one everyone finds most dear. Why? Because in her there is emotion, there is bhava, there is love. It is said that one who loves is better than one who becomes a stone.

> "One who loves is better than one who becomes a stone."

That is why, out of the three, Lakshmi is considered better, and in the Durga tradition the first manifestation is said to be that of Lakshmi.
