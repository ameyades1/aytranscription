# The Hidden Meaning Behind Symbols

## Why Do Spiritual Teachings Speak in Symbols?

Have you ever wondered why our scriptures don't just say things plainly? Why all the stories, the symbols and the sacred signs?

Acharya Upendra Ji has a simple answer. The real teaching the Guru wants to pass on is deep and **subtle**. Its full meaning is hard to grasp and even harder to remember.

The Guru therefore has to explain it through ordinary language and everyday examples. A symbol is the bridge that carries you to that deeper truth.

The importance of symbols in religion, as Acharya Ji explains it, comes down to this: spiritual truth is too subtle to grasp or remember directly, so it is taught through everyday examples and symbols, just as every field of study uses symbols as memory aids. Blind faith begins when we cling to a symbol and lose its meaning. The Guru's direct experience unlocks what the symbol means.

## Every Discipline Has Its Own Shorthand

This isn't true of spirituality alone. In physics and chemistry, too, we write things down in symbols. Each one is tied to a meaning from daily life, and that link helps us remember which formula applies where.

Medical students rely on the same kind of memory aids to recall the names of the bones, the sections of the spinal cord and the ribs.

Acharya Ji draws an example from mechanics, a subject he studied in his engineering days, and adds that computer scientists must have shorthand of their own. Every field has been given something like this.

*Which symbols or memory tricks did you lean on to remember formulas when you were studying?*

## How Does Blind Faith Begin?

A symbol exists to remind us of the real principle and what it truly means. Trouble begins when we read some other meaning into it. If you draw a different meaning from a symbol and then cling to it, Acharya Ji explains, superstition sets in.

This is how **blind faith** is born: we hold on tightly to the sign and forget the meaning behind it.

*Am I holding on to a tradition for its outward form alone, or do I also understand what it means?*

## Science and Religion: A Mirror Image

Here Acharya Ji offers a beautiful comparison. In ordinary science, the textbook holds the knowledge, and the teacher uses symbols to explain it.

In our religious traditions it works the other way around. Everything in the scriptures is symbolic, while the **Guru** holds the real knowledge and the **direct experience** of it.

That is why, on the spiritual path, reading the scriptures is not enough. The key to their symbols lies in the Guru's experience.

## Symbols and Knowledge Together

Should we give up symbols, then? No. Science and spirituality alike need both: the symbol, and the real knowledge it points to.

## Key Takeaways

- Spiritual truth is subtle, so teachers explain it through symbols and everyday examples.
- Physics, chemistry, medicine and engineering all use symbols as memory aids.
- Superstition begins when you cling to the wrong meaning of a symbol.
- In science, the book holds the knowledge and the teacher holds the symbols; in religion, the scriptures hold the symbols and the Guru holds the real knowledge and direct experience.
- You need both the symbol and the real knowledge.
