# Chapter: The Secret of Symbols

## Why Are Things Said in Symbols?

The question arises: why are spiritual matters spoken of in symbols? The reason is that the true scripture a guru has to teach is very subtle, very deep. Its profound meaning is neither easily remembered nor easily understood. To explain it, one has to rely on ordinary language and ordinary examples.

## Symbols in Every Discipline

As I said, in physics and chemistry too we write certain symbolic notations. A new meaning emerges from them, connected to everyday life, and through that I am able to remember where that formula can be applied.

Those who study medicine have the same thing. To remember the names of all the bones there are symbolic aids. To remember all the parts of the spinal cord as well, and to remember the ribs too. Medical people have this too, don't they?

So people in every field must have been given something like this. When I was studying engineering, I gave an example from mechanical engineering. Computer science people will have their own.

## The Wrong Meaning of a Symbol and Blind Faith

That form is symbolic. Through the symbol in it, we are meant to remember what the formula is and what its real meaning is.

> "If one draws some other meaning from that symbol and holds on to it, blind faith begins."

I gave you an example of how blind faith begins, didn't I? In just that way, by holding on to the wrong meaning of a symbol, blind faith took hold.

## Science and Religion: Exactly Opposite Arrangements

In ordinary science the arrangement is the reverse. There the book holds the knowledge, and the teacher holds the symbols.

In our religions it is exactly the opposite. In the scriptures everything is symbolic, and the guru holds the real knowledge and experience.

## The Symbol Is Needed, and So Is Knowledge

Yet for both kinds of knowledge, both things are necessary. This much has settled in the mind:

> "The symbol is needed, and the real knowledge is needed too. Both are needed."
