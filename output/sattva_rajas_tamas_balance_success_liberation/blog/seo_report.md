## 1. Summary

A short, clear talk on the three gunas. Acharya Ji starts from the word itself: in Sanskrit guna primarily means a cord, and sattva, rajas and tamas are the three cords with which maya binds us. Even sattva binds (a gold chain is still a chain): if vasana remains, there is another birth, so the goal is mukti, not just goodness. Yet no guna should be given up: action needs rajas, knowledge needs sattva, rest and ending need tamas, and the real art is letting the right guna lead at the right time. He closes by linking the gunas to Mahasaraswati (white), Lakshmi (red) and Kali (black). The main intent is **informational** ("सत्व रज तम क्या है", "what are sattva rajas and tamas"), with a strong **practical / life** angle ("how to balance the three gunas", "why am I lazy when I should study"). Ranking pages are mostly definitions lists, Ayurveda/diet pages and "be more sattvic" advice. Almost none make the two points this talk makes: that guna means cord (so even sattva binds), and that the aim is balance by time and place, not eliminating rajas and tamas. That is this post's angle. The query is evergreen; a seasonal hook exists through the three goddesses in Navratri (Chaitra Navratri 2027: refresh 4-6 weeks before; check the panchang for dates), but the Devi angle belongs mainly to `awaken_divine_shakti_kali_lakshmi_saraswati` (see section 7, cannibalization).

## 6. FAQ

1. **गुण का असली अर्थ क्या है? / What does guna really mean?**
   संस्कृत में गुण का मुख्य अर्थ डोरी है; विशेषता गौण अर्थ है। सत्व, रज और तम तीन डोरियाँ हैं जिनसे माया हमें बाँधती है। / In Sanskrit, guna primarily means a cord; "quality" is the secondary sense. Sattva, rajas and tamas are three cords with which maya binds us.
2. **क्या सत्वगुण भी बंधन है? / Does sattva also bind?**
   हाँ। ज्ञान, सुख और विवेक सत्वगुण हैं, पर यदि वासना बची है तो फिर जन्म है। साँकल सोने की हो, तब भी बाँधती है। / Yes. Knowledge, happiness and discernment belong to sattva, but if any vasana remains, another birth follows. A chain of gold still binds.
3. **क्या रजोगुण और तमोगुण छोड़ देने चाहिए? / Should we get rid of rajas and tamas?**
   नहीं। कर्म के लिए रजस, ज्ञान के लिए सत्व, और विश्राम व संहार के लिए तमस चाहिए। कोई भी अकेला उचित नहीं। / No. Action needs rajas, knowledge needs sattva, and rest or bringing something to an end needs tamas. None is right on its own.
4. **तीनों गुणों का संतुलन कैसे करें? / How do you balance the three gunas?**
   समझें कि किस स्थान पर, किस समय कौन-सा गुण प्रधान हो: पढ़ते समय सत्व, काम के समय रजस, रात को नींद के लिए तमस। / Understand which guna should lead where and when: sattva while learning, rajas while working, tamas for sleep at night.
5. **तीन गुणों से कौन-सी देवियाँ जुड़ी हैं? / Which goddesses are linked to the three gunas?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार महासरस्वती सत्व (श्वेत), लक्ष्मी रजस (लाल) और काली तमस (काला) की प्रतीक हैं। / According to Acharya Ji, Mahasaraswati represents sattva (white), Lakshmi rajas (red) and Kali tamas (black).

## 10. Authenticity flags

From the Sanskrit review (`tools/review/sattva_rajas_tamas_balance_success_liberation.md`); none is fixed in the blog yet.
1. **Kali "dressed in black" (Minor).** Scripturally Kali's complexion is dark (Mahakali dhyana: नीलाश्मद्युतिम्); the black is her वर्ण, not her garment. Recommendation: apply the review's correction. The alt text and caption speak of cloths and colours only, not of Kali's dress; FAQ 5 gives the colour per goddess without saying "dressed".
2. **Mahasaraswati / Lakshmi / Kali (Minor).** The Saptashati scheme is Mahakali, Mahalakshmi, Mahasaraswati; the blog uses "Maha" only for Saraswati. Recommendation: use one form for all three. FAQ 5 and the about list follow the blog's current wording; update them if the blog changes.
3. **सत्व vs सत्त्व (Minor).** Gita Press spells सत्त्व. The Hindi focus keyword, secondary keywords, title tag and meta use "सत्व" because that is the blog's spelling and the common search form; the Hindi tag uses the existing "सत्त्व गुण" tag. If the blog is corrected to सत्त्व, update the title tag, meta, the link phrase "सत्व, रज और तम तीन डोरियाँ हैं" and the Hindi caption in seo.json, and keep "सत्व रज तम" as a secondary keyword (search spelling) while making the focus keyword "सत्त्व रज तम का संतुलन".

SEO-specific:
4. **"Success" in the video title.** The video promises "Balance, Success and Liberation" and the thumbnail "Unlock Your True Potential", but the blog says little about success. The titles and meta avoid "success" and "potential" so the page delivers what it promises. The category stays `success` as listed in gallery_topics.txt (see section 7).
5. **"Guna means cord, quality is secondary."** The dictionary sense (strand of a rope) is confirmed by the review; ranking "quality" as secondary in the Gita is Acharya Ji's reading. The direct answers and FAQ attribute it to him ("आचार्य उपेंद्र जी के अनुसार" / "According to Acharya Upendra Ji").
6. **Health claims.** The rajas example mentions rising blood pressure as an image of pressure and haste. Do not target or imply health queries ("gunas and blood pressure", Ayurveda diet) for this post.
7. **Goddess colours as a keyword.** The colour mapping is Acharya Ji's teaching and partly needs correction (flag 1); do not build titles or snippets on "Kali black dress" or similar.
