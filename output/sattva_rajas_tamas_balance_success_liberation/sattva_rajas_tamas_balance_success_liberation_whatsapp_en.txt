*Can even a good quality keep you bound?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that guna primarily means a cord, and even sattva binds. Yet life needs all three: rajas to act, sattva to learn, tamas to rest, each at the right time.

💭 _Could my good qualities also be keeping me bound?_
