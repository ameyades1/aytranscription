## 1. Summary

A practical talk on **what to ask God for**. Acharya Ji says that praying for a life without problems is itself the problem, since every avatar and great soul faced crises; ask instead for the intelligence, strength and talent to solve them, because the effort, and therefore the achievement, is yours. From there he gives the three kinds of people (who learn from others' mistakes, from their own, or never), a reading of **astrology as collected experience** to be heard from a qualified Guru, the one test of **atmabal** (do what strengthens you, drop what weakens you), and the **law of the catalyst**: rings, colours and puja may help confidence but never do the main work. It ends with obstacles growing as you rise (Mahakali and protection first), obstacles never arriving suddenly (brakes, blood sugar, accidents), and "stay young at heart, but be prepared". Main intent: emotional / life problem ("why does God not fix my life", "what should I pray for") with an informational side on astrology and remedies. The opportunity: prayer queries are served by generic devotional or prosperity-prayer pages, and remedy queries by astrology sellers; this post gives an honest middle way: prayer is right, remedies are not forbidden, but neither replaces effort. Category `karma` (gallery_topics.txt).

## 6. FAQ

1. **भगवान से क्या माँगना चाहिए? / What should you ask God for in prayer?**
   भक्ति, शक्ति, वैराग्य, आरोग्य, और कार्य के लिए बुद्धि, शक्ति और प्रतिभा। आगे का काम हम स्वयं करें। / Devotion, strength, detachment, good health, and the intelligence, strength and talent the work needs. The rest is ours to do.
2. **क्या भगवान से समस्या हटाने की प्रार्थना गलत है? / Is it wrong to pray for your problems to go away?**
   माँगना गलत नहीं, पर "कोई समस्या न आए" यह प्रार्थना ही समस्या है, क्योंकि महापुरुषों के जीवन में भी संकट थे। माँगो कि समस्या कैसे सुलझाऊँ। / Asking is not wrong, but the prayer "let no problem come" is itself the problem, since even great souls faced crises. Ask how to solve it.
3. **क्या ज्योतिष अंधविश्वास है? / Is astrology superstition?**
   आचार्य जी के अनुसार ज्योतिष बहुत से लोगों के अनुभवों का संकलन है, एक संकेत, जिसे योग्य गुरु से सुनकर नई योजना बनाई जा सकती है; उसे मानकर हाथ पर हाथ रखकर नहीं बैठना। / According to Acharya Ji, astrology is the collected experience of many people, a signal to hear from a qualified Guru and plan with, not a reason to sit back.
4. **क्या अंगूठी या पीला वस्त्र पहनने से काम बनता है? / Do rings or wearing yellow make things work?**
   ये उत्प्रेरक हैं: योजना तैयार हो, आत्मविश्वास हो और गुरु से पूछ लिया हो, तो इनसे आपत्ति नहीं, पर मुख्य काम ये नहीं करते। / They are catalysts: if your plan is ready, you are confident and you have asked the Guru, there is no objection, but they never do the main work.
5. **क्या विघ्न अचानक आते हैं? / Do obstacles come suddenly?**
   नहीं, सब धीरे-धीरे बढ़ता है, जैसे रख-रखाव के बिना ढीला होता ब्रेक; नियमित जाँच और रख-रखाव ही बचाव है। / No. Everything builds up gradually, like brakes that loosen without maintenance; regular check-ups and upkeep are the defence.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **"शास्त्र कहता है… आत्मबल बढ़े, वह सब करो" has no named source** (Sanskrit review, issue 1): it reads as a scripture quotation but no text says it in these words. Recommendation: adopt the review's correction ("आचार्य जी कहते हैं…" / "Acharya Ji says…") or name the source from the transcript. Until then, no title, meta, FAQ or snippet attributes the atmabal test to scripture; the FAQ does not use it.
2. **"दुर्गा की उपासना में पहला पाठ… महाकाली" does not name the Durga Saptashati** (review, issue 2): the claim is correct (prathama charitra, Mahakali as devata) but readers cannot tell which text is meant. Recommendation: the review's wording. The in-text link to real_role_maha_kali sits on this phrase and stays valid either way, but if the editor rewrites the sentence, the `links` phrase in seo.json must be updated to match.
3. **"Ganadhipati" glossed as "a leader of the people"** (review, issue 3): the word means lord of the ganas. Recommendation: the review's gloss. No SEO field uses the term.
4. **"दशा" may be a plain word in Hindi but a Jyotish term in English** (review, issue 4): the two versions may not say the same thing. Recommendation: check the transcript and align one side. The English keywords avoid "dasha" queries until this is settled.
5. **Shri Vidya "protection first" is not tied to a text or step** (review, could not verify). Keep it out of snippets and FAQ; do not target Shri Vidya queries from this post (become_billionaire_shree_vidya_secrets owns them).
6. **Astrology and remedy queries attract fear-selling**: the talk's view is that astrology is collected experience, a signal heard from a qualified Guru, and that rings and colours are catalysts that never do the main work. The keywords "is astrology superstition" and "do astrological remedies work" are kept because the post answers them honestly; titles and meta never promise that a remedy works, and the "Avoid" list in section 2 excludes gemstone and remedy-selling queries. Note also that the talk calls astrology "statistical data"; readers who treat Jyotisha as a Vedanga may read this differently, so snippets attribute it to Acharya Ji rather than stating it as fact.
7. **Health passage** (blood tests, immunity, white blood cells, heart trouble): these are illustrations of "obstacles build up gradually", not medical advice. No SEO field makes a health claim; keep it that way.
8. **"Every avatar faced crises" and "a problem-free life is not possible"**: for readers in real distress, snippets keep the blog's hopeful framing (ask for the strength to solve it) and never suggest that suffering should simply be accepted or that prayer is useless; the blog itself says asking God is not wrong.
