# Chapter: Do Not Ask God to Remove the Problem, Ask for the Capacity

## Crises Come into Everyone's Life

Look at the lives of the greatest of achievers, of avatars, of great souls. Every one of them faced crises. So the very wish "may no problem ever come to me" is itself the problem.

It is right to ask the Supreme, but ask how you may solve this problem, how you may return from *nara* to *Nārāyaṇa*, from the human to the divine. If you ask for that, it is fitting. "O Lord, give me devotion, give me strength, give me dispassion, give me health." All of this you may ask for. Give me the intelligence, the strength and the talent needed for the work; the rest I will do myself. I must know how to do the work.

## The Effort Is Yours, the Capacity Is God's

Someone says, "O Lord, get me married to that girl." Arre, you will have to speak sweetly, you will have to do everything, won't you? You will make her happy, you will enjoy the happiness, and you tell God, "Lord, you give it"!

God should pass the exam and you carry home the topper's certificate? You do the studying. Ask God for this: "Give me the capacity to study, to remember, the grace of Sarasvati; the capacity to solve the questions, to write well. On top of that I will work hard, and I will pass." Otherwise, what is your achievement?

> "O Lord, give me that talent; the rest I will do myself."

## Learn from the Mistakes of Others

Understand this much. Earlier we chanted "Om Namaste Gaṇapataye", to Ganapati. You have seen such great leaders. For what these great beings did, and for the crises that already came into their lives, read history. What happens when you read and hear history? From what has already happened through the mistakes of others, I learn.

> "The best person learns from the mistakes of others. The average person learns from his own mistakes. And the lowest person never improves from any mistake at all."

The lowest person simply does not learn; that is *tamas*. This is why the best disciple learns from the experience of others.

## Astrology: A Compilation of Experience

What is astrology too? It is a compilation of the experiences of so many people. Over time, when the Sun, the Moon or Mars came into a certain position, what happened: that is its calculation, the reckoning of time. Whether it proves true or false, millions of people have received this result, so why should I take the risk? It could come to me too. Would it not be better if I am prepared?

Astrology too is a kind of statistical data. Many people experienced this in such a period; whoever is running such a *daśā* has had such things happen. So can I not at least get an indication? That does not mean I sit idle, accepting it entirely; if I want to change it, I can make a new plan. But it should be heard only by going to a worthy Guru. In this way it too can be a means of removing obstacles.

## Do Whatever Increases Your Inner Strength

The scripture says: do everything that increases your inner strength. If listening to astrology is weakening your heart, weakening your mind, then do not listen to astrology. Then do the Guru's scriptural study, take your decision according to the scripture about whether to do this or not, and then take the risk with courage. Then do not look at the horoscope.

For some people, inner strength grows by hearing their horoscope, by hearing astrology; then by all means do that. For someone, it gives strength to go to work only after doing a puja; then do that. We have no objection to it. It just should not come from blind faith; its reason should be that I gain confidence in my plan.

## Remedies Are Catalysts, Not the Main Work

Now you say that wearing yellow clothes makes your work succeed; we have no objection. I will not call this blind faith; it has become a catalyst. You say that wearing a ring makes your work succeed; I will not refuse it, provided your plan is ready, you have full confidence in it and you have asked the Guru. Now if, in addition, you believe something helps, it is increasing your self-confidence. Whether it is blind faith or not, I still will not oppose it. Even great achievers do such things. If they do, very good. When you become *guṇātīta*, beyond the three qualities, you rise above all this; then it is no longer needed.

But "I wore this, so my work will succeed; a yellow shirt will make it succeed, a blue shirt will make it succeed": it does not happen like that. Is this much understood? I wore a ring and the work was done; I gave money to Guruji, he will do a remedy and my work is done: it does not happen like that.

> "These are all catalysts. A catalyst does not do the main work."

The catalyst does not do the main work. The main reaction is something else; the catalyst only helps to speed it up. Is this clear to all of you? Good.

## Mental Distress, Disease and the Obstacles of Height

Secondly, we spoke of what effect *ādhi*, that is mental trouble, has. *Vyādhi* means disease. For disease one should attend a camp like our "Science of Healing", where we spoke of the root cause of disease and of psychosomatic illness.

After that I said that the higher you rise, the more obstacles will come, because you are to become *Gaṇādhipati*, a leader of the hosts. You are no longer small, your responsibility has grown, your vision is bigger, so the evil eye will also fall on you. This is why in the worship of Durga the first recitation is always of Mahakali. When power, wealth and wisdom come, look even in Śrī Vidyā: what always comes first? Protection.

## The Levels of Protection

You will have to secure your copyrights, your patents, all of that. That is at the physical level. Then, for physical protection, what must you do? I spoke of it: CCTV cameras and that kind of protection. Then I said that a camera captures a picture; it does not catch the man.

> "If you want to do the work of non-violence, you need violent people."

That is, police, army, guards: you will need all of these. Then do that, and will your crises not gradually lessen? Will the obstacles not lessen in advance? Think honestly and tell me, do the chances not go down?

## Is Your Army Ready?

You have taken the proper medicine and tonic, you have built up immunity against that disease, your white blood cell count is good, you are getting regular blood tests: now is your army ready or not? Then the chance of a crisis coming is low.

## Obstacles Do Not Come Suddenly

Suddenly one day your sugar rises; suddenly one day you see that all these problems have appeared. Nothing happens suddenly. Everything grows gradually; we simply do not get regular checkups. If this wisdom comes, that I need checking and maintenance, the matter is solved.

The car is running and suddenly one day the brake does not work at all. It did not fail suddenly one day; it gradually became loose, because you probably did not maintain it. Is that right?

Obstacles mostly seem sudden. Now what about an accident? Did you drive slowly?

> "Start early, drive slowly, reach safely."

But I will always leave late and go fast, then hope for the best, then jump the signal. With this tendency, one day there will be an accident. It does not happen overnight. Whatever appeared suddenly today also happened slowly at some point, and I did not apply my intelligence.

Then it feels like you got away lightly. The constable did not see; that feels thrilling. Today the constable did not catch us; he saw, but we ran away. We did not get away! The Guru-principle has noted what you do. One day, suddenly, that change will come before you.

## The Crisis That Grows Slowly

The belly is growing little by little; it is visible. Cholesterol is rising, fat is rising, whatever is rising, you did not look. Then one day suddenly a heart problem comes, and you feel that obstacles do come suddenly.

> "Obstacles never come suddenly. It is a gradual process."

It is a gradual process. You had not applied that intelligence; you did not learn from the crises of others. Is it understood or not?

## Stay Young at Heart, but Be Prepared

Every day someone or other was going to the cremation ground. Someone or other must have passed away in your own home too. Death will not come to you suddenly one day. You are gradually moving towards old age, moving on and on, and you are not aware of it. Is that right?

Now even a person of ninety-nine feels, "I am still young", and that is why there is a problem. Yes, stay young at heart, but be prepared.
