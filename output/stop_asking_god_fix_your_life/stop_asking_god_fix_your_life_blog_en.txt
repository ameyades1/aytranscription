# Do Not Ask God to Remove the Problem, Ask for the Strength to Solve It

## Is a Life Without Problems Really Possible?

Look at the lives of the greatest achievers. Look at the lives of avatars and great souls. Every one of them faced crises.

So the prayer "may no problem ever come to me" is itself the problem.

Asking the Supreme is not wrong. But ask how you may solve this problem, how you may return from *nara* to *Nārāyaṇa* (from the human to the divine).

## What Should We Ask for in Prayer?

"O Lord, give me devotion, give me strength, give me dispassion, give me health." All of this you may ask for.

Ask for the intelligence, strength and talent the work requires. The rest I will do myself. I must know how to do the work.

> "O Lord, give me that talent; the rest I will do myself."

## The Effort Is Yours, and So Is the Joy

Someone prays, "O Lord, get me married to that girl." But you are the one who must speak sweetly; you must do everything yourself.

You will make her happy, you will enjoy the happiness, and you tell God, "Lord, you give it"?

God should take the exam and you carry home the topper's certificate? You do the studying. Ask God for the capacity to study, the power to remember, the grace of Sarasvati, the skill to solve the questions, good writing. On top of that I will work hard, and I will pass.

Otherwise, what is your achievement?

*Today, are you asking God for the result, or for the capacity to reach that result?*

## Three Kinds of People: Which One Are You?

Earlier we chanted "Om Namaste Gaṇapataye". We saw examples of great leadership like Ganapati.

To know what these great beings did, and the crises that already came into their lives, read history. What happens when you read and hear history? From what has already happened through the mistakes of others, I learn.

> "The best person learns from the mistakes of others. The average person learns from his own mistakes. And the lowest person never improves from any mistake at all."

The lowest person simply does not learn. This is **tamas** (inertia, the darkness of ignorance). That is why the best disciple learns from the experience of others.

## Astrology: Superstition or a Compilation of Experience?

What is astrology too? A compilation of the experiences of so many people.

When the Sun, the Moon or Mars come into a certain position over time, what happens: that is its **kāla-gaṇanā** (reckoning of time). Whether it proves true or not, millions have received this result. So why should I take the risk? It could come to me too. Isn't it good to be prepared?

Astrology is a kind of statistical data. Many people experienced this in a certain *daśā*, so I can get an indication.

This does not mean sitting idle, accepting it entirely. If I want to change it, I can make a new plan. But it should be heard only from a worthy Guru. In this way it too can become a way of **Vighnahartā**, of removing obstacles.

## There Is Only One Test: Inner Strength

The scripture says: do everything that increases your **ātmabal** (inner strength and self-confidence).

If hearing astrology weakens your heart and mind, do not listen to astrology. Do the Guru's scriptural study, decide according to the scripture whether to do this or not, and then take the risk with courage. Then do not look at the horoscope.

If someone's inner strength grows by hearing astrology, then by all means do it. If someone gains strength by doing a puja before setting out, do that. The only condition is that it should not be blind faith. The purpose should be to gain confidence in your own plan.

## Yellow Clothes, a Ring and the Law of the Catalyst

Someone says wearing yellow clothes makes their work succeed. Acharya ji says there is no objection to this. He does not call it blind faith; it is a **catalyst**.

Someone says a ring makes their work succeed. That is not forbidden either, provided your plan is ready, you have full confidence in it, and you have asked the Guru. After that, if anything additional increases your confidence, whether it is blind faith or not, there is no opposition to it. Even great achievers do such things.

Yes, when you become **guṇātīta** (beyond the three qualities of sattva, rajas and tamas), you rise above all of this.

But "I wore this shirt, so the work will succeed", "I wore the ring, so the work is done", "I gave money to Guruji, he will do a remedy": it does not happen like that.

> "These are all catalysts. A catalyst does not do the main work."

As in chemistry, the main reaction is something else. The catalyst only helps to speed it up.

*In your life, is some remedy taking the place of your effort, or only speeding it up?*

## The Higher You Rise, the More Obstacles

**Ādhi** means mental trouble, and **vyādhi** means physical disease. To understand the root causes of disease and psychosomatic illness, one should attend a camp like the Science of Healing.

The higher you rise, the more obstacles will come, because you are to become **Gaṇādhipati**. You are no longer small. Your responsibility has grown, your vision is bigger, so the evil eye will fall on you too.

This is why in the worship of Durga the first recitation is always of **Mahakali**. When power, wealth and wisdom come, look even in Śrī Vidyā: what comes first? **Protection.**

## Protection Is Also Sadhana

You will have to secure your copyrights, your patents, all of that. This is protection at the physical level. Put up CCTV cameras. But a camera captures a picture; it does not catch the man.

> "If you want to do the work of non-violence, you need violent people."

That is, police, army, guards. Do all this, and won't your crises gradually lessen? The chance of obstacles falls in advance.

The same applies to the body. You have taken the proper medicine, built up immunity, your white blood cells are good, you get regular blood tests: your army is ready. Then the chance of a crisis is low.

## Obstacles Never Come Suddenly

Suddenly one day the sugar rose, suddenly all the problems became visible. The truth is that nothing happens suddenly. Everything grows gradually; we just do not get regular checkups.

A car's brake fails one day suddenly. But it did not go bad in a day. It gradually became loose, because there was no maintenance.

Accidents are the same. The rule is simple: start early, drive slowly, reach safely. But we leave late, rush, jump the signal. With this tendency, one day there will be an accident. It does not happen overnight.

When the constable doesn't catch us, it feels thrilling, as if we escaped. But we did not escape. The **Guru-tattva** (the Guru-principle) has noted everything you do. One day, suddenly, that change will come before you.

The belly is growing little by little, cholesterol and fat are rising, but you did not look. Then one day a heart problem comes, and it seems the obstacle came suddenly.

> "Obstacles never come suddenly. It is a gradual process."

You did not apply your intelligence; you did not learn from the crises of others.

*What "slowly loosening brake" is there in your life that you are not seeing right now?*

## Young at Heart, but Prepared

Every day someone goes to the cremation ground. Someone in your own home must have passed away too. Death will not come suddenly either. We are gradually moving towards old age, but we are not aware of it.

Even a ninety-nine-year-old feels young; that is the problem. Acharya ji's advice is clear: stay young at heart, but be prepared.

## Key Takeaways

- Do not pray for the problem to be removed; pray for the intelligence, strength and talent to solve it.
- The effort is yours; only then is the achievement yours too.
- The best person learns from the mistakes of others, the average from their own, and the lowest never learns.
- Astrology is a compilation of experience; hear it from a worthy Guru and plan with it as an indication.
- Do what increases your inner strength; drop what weakens your mind.
- Remedies like rings, colours and puja are catalysts, not the main work.
- Obstacles grow with height, so arrange protection first.
- Obstacles do not come suddenly; regular checking and maintenance are the defence.
- Stay young at heart, but be prepared.
