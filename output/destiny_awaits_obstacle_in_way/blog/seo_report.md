## 1. Summary

The talk teaches that what we seek is already within us and that the work is only to remove what blocks it. Acharya Ji builds on Patanjali's farmer illustration (Yoga Sutra 4.3: the instrumental cause does not move nature, it only breaks the obstruction) and retells it as a field, a channel and a running tap with a stone lodged at the mouth: the water is tattvajnana, the channel is destiny, the stone is the debris of maya (desire, "me, me", selfishness). A second image, the sun that is "a very decent person" and will not force its way past closed curtains, stands for brahma bhava and atma bhava hidden by jada buddhi. The talk ends with three responses to the debris: the sattvic mind prays for obstacles to be removed (invoking Ganapati), the rajasic mind asks for more money and status and so adds debris, and the tamasic mind dumps debris on others and inverts dharma and adharma. The main search intent is emotional with an informational layer: people searching "भाग्य में रुकावट", "kismat mein rukawat kyon" or "what is blocking my destiny" feel stuck despite effort. Ranking pages for these queries are dominated by astrology remedies and manifestation content; an answer that locates the block in one's own desires, grounded in a cited Yoga Sutra, is a distinct and honest angle. "Yoga Sutra 4.3 meaning" and the farmer analogy are low-competition long-tail entry points where a clear explanation can rank.

## 6. FAQ

**Hindi**
1. **भाग्य में रुकावट क्या है?** आचार्य जी के अनुसार रुकावट माया का कचरा है: "मुझे बस पैसा चाहिए" जैसी कामनाएं, "मैं, मैं" का भाव और स्वार्थ।
2. **योगसूत्र ४.३ का अर्थ क्या है?** निमित्त (साधन) प्रकृति को चलाता नहीं, केवल आवरण (रुकावट) हटाता है, जैसे किसान मेड़ तोड़ता है और पानी अपने आप खेत में बहने लगता है।
3. **क्या लक्ष्य को बार-बार दोहराना ज़रूरी है?** नहीं। आचार्य जी कहते हैं कि "नर से नारायण" का लक्ष्य रटने की ज़रूरत नहीं, वह भीतर पहले से विद्यमान है; काम केवल रुकावट हटाने का है।
4. **सात्त्विक, राजसिक और तामसिक मन रुकावट के साथ क्या करते हैं?** सात्त्विक मन विघ्न हटाने की प्रार्थना करता है, राजसिक मन और पैसा व मान मांगकर कचरा बढ़ाता है, और तामसिक मन दूसरों के मार्ग में भी कचरा डालता है।

**English**
1. **What blocks our destiny?** According to Acharya Ji, the debris of maya: desires such as "I just want money", the sense of "me, me" and selfishness.
2. **What does Yoga Sutra 4.3 mean?** The instrumental cause does not set nature in motion; it only removes the obstruction, as a farmer breaks open the embankment and the water flows into the field by itself.
3. **Do I need to keep repeating my goal?** No. Acharya Ji says the aim of Nar se Narayan does not need to be chanted over and over; it already exists within you, and the work is to remove the block.
4. **How do sattvic, rajasic and tamasic minds handle obstacles?** The sattvic mind prays for them to be removed, the rajasic mind adds to them by asking for more money and status, and the tamasic mind dumps its debris on others too.

## 10. Authenticity flags

From the Sanskrit review (`tools/review/destiny_awaits_obstacle_in_way.md`) and the SEO check:

1. **Tap, channel and stone attributed to Patanjali (Minor, from the review).** The sutra gives only the farmer image (क्षेत्रिकवत्); the tap and stuck stone are Acharya Ji's retelling. Recommendation: apply the review's wording ("पतंजलि किसान का एक दृष्टांत देते हैं। आचार्य जी उसे इस तरह समझाते हैं…"). The title tags, meta descriptions, direct answers and secondary keyword already say "Patanjali's farmer analogy", not "Patanjali's stone in the channel".
2. **Meaning of Yoga Sutra 4.3 (Minor, from the review).** प्रकृतीनां is plural, and the embankment clause comes from the Vyasa Bhashya. Recommendation: use the review's suggested meaning. FAQ 2 follows the blog's meaning; if the blog is corrected, align FAQ 2 with it.
3. **Reference name (Minor, from the review).** "(पातंजल योगसूत्र ४.३)" is the standard name; the number is correct. The Hindi secondary keyword "योगसूत्र ४.३ का अर्थ" is unaffected.
4. **"trigunas" double plural (Minor, from the review).** Use "three gunas" or "triguna" in the English blog; the English tag and keywords already use "Three Gunas" / "sattvic rajasic tamasic mind".
5. **Duryodhana gloss only in English (Minor, from the review).** Factually correct; no change needed unless strict parity is wanted.
6. **No promise of wealth or quick results (SEO).** The talk calls the wish for "just money" part of the debris. Keywords like "remove obstacles to wealth" or "unblock luck fast" are deliberately not targeted, and no title promises material gain; "destiny is already flowing" is presented as Acharya Ji's teaching, not a guarantee.
7. **Tamasic people turning against the Guru (SEO).** The line on those who oppose dharma and the Guru should not be excerpted alone in snippets or social cards, where it could read as an attack on critics; keep it with the Duryodhana context.
