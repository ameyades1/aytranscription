# Destiny Is Already Flowing, Just Remove the Stone

Do you also feel that to achieve something big in life you will have to push harder, run more, ask for more? Acharya Upendra Ji turns this thinking around with one simple example.

His message: what you are searching for is already within you. The only work is to remove the obstruction lying in between.

## A Field, a Canal and a Stone

In the Yoga Darshan, Maharishi Patanjali gives an illustration. There is a field, a canal has been made to water it, and the tap is running continuously. Naturally, the water should reach the whole field.

But right at the start of the canal, a *roda*, some debris or a stone, has got stuck. The water is not moving ahead.

Now what? Open the tap harder? Widen the canal? No. Just remove that debris.

> **nimittam aprayojakaṁ prakṛtīnāṁ varaṇabhedas tu tataḥ kṣetrikavat**
> "The instrumental cause does not set nature in motion; it only removes the obstruction, like a farmer."
> (Patanjali Yoga Sutra 4.3)

This is the very sutra of the Yoga Sutras in which the example of the farmer (*kshetrika*) appears: means do not drive nature, they only remove the covering (the obstruction), and then the flow happens by itself.

> "The water was already present. The path was present. That debris was the problem."

## Must the Goal Be Repeated Again and Again?

Acharya Ji says the aim of **from Nar to Narayan** (from human to the Divine) need not be repeated ten times, a thousand times. Not this chanting of "I must do something for the nation, I must, I must" either.

Because all of this is already present within you.

In this illustration, the water is **tattvajnana** (knowledge of truth). The canal is your **destiny**, the entire script of your life. In knowledge, everything was already as it is.

Then what is the obstruction? The debris of **maya**. "I just want money; desire, desire, desire": this "I, I" and selfishness. Only this has to be removed. Within, **Brahman** is present in everyone.

*Which stone is stuck in your canal today: some desire, some ego, or some selfishness?*

## The Sun Is Very Gracious

Now a second example. The sun is already shining outside. It need not be brought separately. The house is dark because you have drawn the curtain.

And Acharya Ji says with a smile that the sun is "a very decent person." It will not force its way into the house.

> "You have closed the curtain, haven't you? I will not come in. I will wait outside. I will fall only up to the curtain. I will not enter the house."

> "As much as you open, that much I will come in. I am already present."

This sun is the symbol of the **sense of Brahman** and the **sense of the Self** (awareness of your true nature) within you. It is full of light. It stays covered only as much as the curtain of **dull intellect** covers it.

It does not have to be lit with a matchstick. It is already there.

## Three Intellects, Three Ways of Treating the Debris

Now the question is what we do with this debris. This is where the difference of the **three gunas** (sattva, rajas, tamas) shows.

The **sattvic intellect** prays: "May this debris not enter me. O Ganapati, may all obstacles be removed." Here, obstacle means this very debris.

The **rajasic person**, on the contrary, asks for more obstacles: a little more money, a little more honour, a little more respect. That is, a demand to increase the debris.

The **tamasic person** not only increases their own debris but also goes and dumps it into other people's canals. Sometimes they even try to shut off the tap. They cannot shut it, but they try.

They take dharma to be adharma and adharma to be dharma. Such people go against dharma and the Guru. Acharya Ji says, otherwise why would an enemy camp appear in the Guru's story at all? This is extreme tamas, and the story needs a Duryodhana too.

*When an obstacle comes in life, do you pray for it to be removed, or do you unknowingly enlarge it by asking for "a little more"?*

## Summary: Key Takeaways

- The water (knowledge of truth) and the canal (destiny) are already there; only the debris of maya must be removed.
- The goal of "from Nar to Narayan" need not be memorised; it is already within.
- The sun does not force its way in; as much as you open the curtain, that much light comes in.
- The sattvic intellect prays for obstacles to be removed.
- The rajasic mind increases the debris by asking for more money, honour and respect.
- The tamasic mind dumps debris on others' paths too and inverts dharma and adharma.
