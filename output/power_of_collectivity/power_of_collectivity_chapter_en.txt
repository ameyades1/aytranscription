# Chapter: The Power of Community

## The First Place Among Life's Riches

Wealth, respect, community, a wife, time and health: all of these are parts of *artha*, the prosperity of life. Of these, the first is **community**.

The one with whom society is connected, the one who has good speech, is the one people truly connect with. Speech means communication, whether it is a written conversation, body language or speaking aloud. Ask yourself: does society connect with you properly? And once society is properly connected with you, how difficult will business remain? Wealth comes on its own afterwards.

## Divine Wealth and the End of Disputes

The one who has **divine wealth** (*daivī sampatti*) settles even disputes with customers quickly. Someone else's dispute drags on in court for ten years. But a person of divine qualities will say, "Come, let me give you a little more", "Take a sample", "This order was cancelled; no matter, next time I will deliver it properly."

Many such disputes end in conversation itself. And the one who resolves matters through conversation, how much more time will he have!

## Even a Guru's Work Grows Through Community

Look anywhere, whether at a guru or at the guru-disciple relationship: first the community grows, and only then does the guru become great. Great here does not mean the guru's power. A guru's **work** becomes great when a community joins him. So certainly, community comes before divine wealth.

## How Big Is Your World?

A family need not always be large. Someone says, "I want only this much as my world." Fine. But even that small world has to be run with these same divine qualities.

Someone else will say, "I want to build a great organisation like World Health, I want to do something for world peace." Then the whole world becomes his world. He will have to apply this divine wealth that far, to the whole world.
