# The Power of Community and Divine Wealth

## What Comes First in Life?

Wealth, respect, community, a wife, time, health. All these joys of life are forms of **artha** (the prosperity of life). But among them, the first is **community**.

For the one people are connected with, the other paths begin to open on their own.

## Speech: The Bridge to Community

The key to connecting with society is **speech**, that is, communication. Whether it is a written conversation, body language or spoken words.

*Do people connect with you easily?*

Once society is properly connected, how difficult will business remain?

> "Wealth comes on its own afterwards."

## Divine Wealth: Disputes That Resolve in Conversation

**Divine wealth** (*daivī sampatti*) means the divine virtues within. The one who has them finds that even disputes with customers end quickly.

Where someone else's case drags through court for ten years, this person says: "Come, let me give a little more", "Take a sample", "This time's order is cancelled, no matter, next time I will deliver properly."

The matter is settled in conversation. And what he saves is the most precious wealth: **time**.

## A Guru, Too, Grows Through Community

This rule applies to a guru as well. First the community grows, only then does the guru grow.

> "A guru's work will become great when his community joins."

Great here means not power or influence, but the expansion of his **work**.

## Is My World Small or Large?

A family need not be large. Someone says, "I want only this much as my world." Fine, but even that small world has to be run with these same qualities.

Someone else wants to build a great organisation for world health or world peace. Then the whole world is his world, and he must apply divine wealth that far.

> "He will have to apply this divine wealth that far."

*How big is your world, and are you running it with divine qualities?*

## Key Takeaways

- Among all of life's riches, **community** comes first; wealth follows on its own.
- Good **speech** and communication bring community together.
- **Divine wealth** resolves disputes through conversation and saves time.
- A guru's work, too, grows only as the community joins.
- Whether your world is small or large, it must be run with divine qualities.
