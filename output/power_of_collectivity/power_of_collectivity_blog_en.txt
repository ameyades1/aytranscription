# The Power of Community and Divine Wealth

## What Comes First in Life?

Wealth, respect, community, a life partner, time and health: every one of these joys is a form of **artha** (prosperity, the abundance of life). Yet the first among them is **community**.

When people stand with you, every other door begins to open on its own.

## Speech: Your Bridge to Others

The key to connecting with people is **speech**, which means communication of every kind: written words, body language, or what you say aloud.

*Do people find it easy to connect with you?*

Once the right people are with you, how hard can business remain? Acharya Ji says that money comes later, on its own.

## Divine Wealth: Disputes That End in Conversation

**Daivi sampatti** (divine wealth) means the divine virtues within you. Those who have them find that even disagreements with customers end quickly.

Where someone else's case drags through the courts for ten years, such a person says, "All right, let me give you a little more," "Take a sample," or "Cancel this order, no problem. Next time I will deliver it properly."

The matter is settled in conversation, and they keep the most precious wealth of all: **time**.

## Even a Guru Grows Through People

The same rule applies to the Guru. The community grows first, and only then does the Guru grow. Acharya Ji explains that a Guru's work expands when people come together around it.

Growing here does not mean gaining power or influence. It means the reach of the **work** widens.

## Is My World Small or Large?

Your family does not need to be large. Someone may say, "This much is all the world I want." That is fine, but even that small world has to run on these same virtues.

Someone else may want to build a large organisation for global health or world peace. Then the whole planet becomes their world, and they must carry divine wealth that far.

*How large is your world, and are you running it with divine virtues?*

## Key Takeaways

- Of all life's riches, **community** comes first; money follows on its own.
- Good **speech** and communication bring people together.
- **Divine wealth** resolves disputes through conversation and saves time.
- A Guru's work also grows as the community comes together.
- Whether your world is small or large, it must run on divine virtues.
