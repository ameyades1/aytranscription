## 1. Summary

A short teaching (about two minutes of video) on why **community (समाज)** comes first among life's forms of wealth (artha), why good **speech/communication (वाणी)** is the bridge to people, and how **daivi sampatti (दैवी संपत्ति, divine virtues)** lets disputes end in conversation instead of court and saves time, the most precious wealth. The same rule applies to a Guru, whose work grows as the community grows, and to anyone's "world", small or large. The main intent is informational with a practical, self-improvement edge (people, business, relationships). The opportunity: queries like "समाज की शक्ति" and "power of community" are dominated by generic essays and quote pages; a short, spiritually grounded answer that ties community to inner virtues and to everyday business disputes is a distinct angle. The post is short (about 400 words per language), so it is best positioned for long-tail and featured-snippet queries and as a supporting post to the wealth cluster (true wealth, Lakshmi, daivi sampatti).

## 6. FAQ

**Hindi**
1. **जीवन में सबसे पहले क्या आता है?** आचार्य जी के अनुसार अर्थ के सभी रूपों में समाज सबसे पहले आता है; धन बाद में अपने आप आता है।
2. **समाज से कैसे जुड़ें?** वाणी से, यानी हर तरह के संवाद से: लिखी बातचीत, शरीर की भाषा और बोले गए शब्द।
3. **दैवी संपत्ति क्या है?** भीतर के दैवी गुण। जिसके पास ये हैं, उसके विवाद भी बातचीत में जल्दी सुलझ जाते हैं और उसका समय बचता है।
4. **गुरु बड़ा कैसे होता है?** पहले समाज बढ़ता है, तब गुरु का कार्य बड़ा होता है। यहाँ बड़ा होने का अर्थ शक्ति नहीं, कार्य का विस्तार है।

**English**
1. **What comes first in life?** Acharya Ji teaches that among all forms of artha, community comes first; money follows on its own.
2. **How do you connect with people?** Through speech in its widest sense: written words, body language and what you say aloud.
3. **What is daivi sampatti?** The divine virtues within you. Those who have them settle disputes in conversation and save time.
4. **How does a Guru grow?** The community grows first; then the Guru's work expands. Growth here means the reach of the work, not power.

## 10. Authenticity flags

1. **"स्त्री" vs "a life partner"** (from the Sanskrit review, minor): the Hindi list says स्त्री (woman/wife); the English softens it to "a life partner". Recommendation: editor's choice; keep "a life partner" for a general audience, or use "a wife" to match the Hindi exactly. The English link anchor and snippet follow the current English text.
2. **Artha grouping**: listing respect, community, a spouse, time and health under artha is Acharya Ji's own wide reading, not a scriptural definition. Titles, meta and FAQ present it as his teaching only; do not write "according to the scriptures" in any snippet.
3. **Daivi sampatti and the Gita**: the term matches Bhagavad Gita 16.1–3 (Gita Press), but this blog does not cite the Gita. Do not add a Gita reference to the title, meta or FAQ of this post; send readers to attain_divine_qualities_empower_spiritual for the Gita list.
4. **Business promise**: "money comes later, on its own" must not be turned into a guarantee ("get rich by…"). Titles avoid any wealth promise.
