# Chapter: Four Keys to Changing Destiny

## Everyone Speaks of India's Future, but Where Is the Plan?

Namaskar. You have heard it many times: India's future should be bright. Many people are saying it; we have been hearing it for ages, and by now even this statement has grown old. Everyone is saying it, everyone is playing guru, but no one has a plan.

If destiny is to be changed, four things are needed.

## The First Key: A Plan

The first is your own plan. There has to be some plan. There must be some foundation, some resolve (*sankalpa*) in the mind: I have to do this. Whether it is a small job or a business, or changing the whole country, becoming a world teacher (*vishwa guru*), whatever it may be, what comes first? Repeat it: first is the plan. There must be a plan, some effort (*purushartha*) that I have to make.

## The Second Key: Prarabdha

We can hold many desires. But alongside them the question is: do my old karmas, the karmas of past births, support my new actions of today, my *purushartha* (for *purushartha* speaks of new karma)? That is, the second part is *prarabdha*: does my destiny also support my vision?

It is not that you cannot go beyond destiny; it can be changed. But first the plan, and with the plan, second, *prarabdha*. If the two match, won't it be even better? One was the plan, the part of *purushartha*. If that plan is supported by *prarabdha*, won't the path become easier?

## The Third Key: Self-Confidence

Even with both of these, a third thing is needed, and its name is **self-confidence** (*atmavishwas*). First, faith in your own plan. And second, faith in your own *prarabdha*, that this is my destiny.

Some guru tells you, someone reads it from *nadi shastra*, some horoscope shows it, some palm reading shows it; there will be some *prarabdha* or other. Very well, suppose no one has ever looked at it; even then, no matter. You don't look at a horoscope, you don't look at the *nadi*, fine, no problem; you don't see the future. But your past karmas will still apply, won't they? Even if you don't believe in the future, in astrology or in *prarabdha*, even an atheist is working according to the very same *prarabdha*, isn't he? That is why the Patanjali Yoga Darshan, chapter two, verse sixteen, clearly states:

> **heyaṁ duḥkham anāgatam**
> "The suffering that has not yet come is to be avoided."
> (Patanjali Yoga Sutra 2.16)

We will understand its meaning in detail ahead.

## The Fourth Key: Guru's Grace

So that makes three keys. The fourth key is **the Guru's grace**, which comes above all of these.

## The Four Keys at a Glance

So what are the four keys of destiny?

1. **Plan:** your plan for your future.
2. **Prarabdha:** does your destiny support it? If it does not, the plan should be such that we go beyond our destiny.
3. **Self-confidence:** faith in your own horoscope and in your own plan.
4. **Grace:** the grace of the Guru.

Many people lack exactly this self-confidence. Even when the Guru tells them, they say, "Oh, will it happen? How will it happen?" And so it does not happen for them; even though it is in their *prarabdha*, it does not happen. On the other hand, many people make plans, dream big, but cannot follow through. It is because of these four that destiny is made or unmade.

## Why Look at Prarabdha?

Now the question arises: why should I look at my *prarabdha* at all? Why should I consult the *nadi* of Agastya Muni, or a horoscope, or anything?

Very well, set the rishis and munis aside. The great people of today, millionaires and billionaires, read their statements yourself. They say: millionaires don't require astrologers, but billionaires do. The bigger a person is, the closer they go to the Supreme or to a higher level, the more they understand that astrology (*jyotish*) is very important.

Because *jyotish* does not mean "when will I get a car and a bungalow." Six limbs of the Vedas are described, and among these *Vedangas* one of the most important is the eye. **The eye of the Veda is called *jyotish*.** Our rishis and munis said that the entire life could be planned easily.

Does it now make sense how essential it was to send a boy to the gurukul in childhood itself? There, a roadmap was available in advance: what my *prarabdha* is. After that comes whether to accept the destiny or not, whether to change it or not.

## Heyaṁ Duḥkham Anāgatam: Stop Tomorrow's Suffering Today

Rishi Patanjali has written very clearly in chapter two, sutra sixteen:

> **heyaṁ duḥkham anāgatam**
> "The suffering that has not yet come is to be avoided."
> (Patanjali Yoga Sutra 2.16)

**Heyam** means to destroy, that which is to be given up. What is to be destroyed? **Duhkha**, suffering. Which suffering? **Anagata**, that which has not yet come; that is what is to be destroyed.

You will say: how can I destroy a suffering that has not even come? It is just like this: if anger comes today, won't a heart attack come tomorrow? One hundred percent certain. There is no unconditional love, the blood pressure is rising, everything is there. So if we control it today itself, then it will work.

Today I eat very hot, very spicy food, so tomorrow I am bound to have some problem connected with the Muladhara chakra, some stomach problem or other. So I should take care right now. Or I am eating too many fats, so one day the problem of the belly, of obesity, is bound to come.

If I want good teeth tomorrow, I must clean them today, mustn't I? So life has to be planned. If someone tells you the symptoms of what is coming, they are saying exactly this: *heyaṁ duḥkham anāgatam*. To remove the coming suffering through a proper remedy, this is said to be your duty. I hope this first point is now very clear.

## The Four Keys and India's Destiny

So let us revise the four keys once more: what is needed to change the destiny of an individual.

First, the plan. If an individual needs a plan, then to change a country, won't the country need a big plan? So what is that plan, which no one used to speak of, that I will be discussing today: what is the plan for India, what Antar intends. Making the plan is no difficulty; the plan itself will tell.

After that, does destiny support it? *Prarabdha*, yes. This much we know for certain, that it appears in Antar's horoscope, in my horoscope, so it is definitely supporting.

Then self-confidence. That is itself the *sadhana* of the Self. If we embrace truth today, self-confidence will surely be there.

And last, the Guru's grace. This is the Guru's own work that is happening, so the Guru's grace will surely be there; it certainly will.
