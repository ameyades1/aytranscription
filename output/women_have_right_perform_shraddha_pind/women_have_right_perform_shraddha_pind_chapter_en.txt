# Chapter: Can Women Perform Shraddha and Pind Daan?

## The Questions of Pitru Paksha

As Pitru Paksha arrives, certain questions arise in our minds again and again, and we often do not know their right answers. Who are the Pitars (ancestors)? What is the true meaning of Shraddha and Tarpan? And what is the knowledge behind these traditions? Among them is one much-discussed question: do women also have the right to perform Shraddha and Tarpan? What do the scriptures say on this subject?

## The Scriptures Themselves Grant the Right

This question arises especially in the Kali Yuga: can women perform the Shraddha rite? They absolutely can. They certainly have the right, and it is the scriptures themselves that have given them this right.

Read texts like the Garuda Purana, or any of our other scriptures that speak about Shraddha, even the Vishnu Purana, and look there too. Nowhere in them is there any restriction saying that women must not perform Shraddha.

## Consent, but No Insistence

But no scripture says either that every woman must immediately go and perform Shraddha. The scriptures do not insist on it. They have given their consent that women may do it, but they have not forced every single one to do it. We must understand the reason for this.

A woman's make-up, her mind, is emotional by nature, full of feeling. If she were to experience something like *shmashan vairagya* (the detachment that arises at the cremation ground), if those feelings suddenly awakened within her and a kind of renunciation arose, there would be difficulty at home. There may be a small child, or others, whose care depends on her.

That is why women who are at a high spiritual level, who have become fully strong of mind, were told: you must certainly do it. For them there is no restriction.

## The Example of Mother Sita

If we look at our own history, Mother Sita herself performed the Shraddha rite of King Dasharatha, the father of Shri Ram. And she did it so wholeheartedly that all the ancestors began to appear before her very eyes. She saw the ancestors come in the form of Brahmins, partake of the food and leave.

Mother Sita did all this. There are many such examples in history, and yet the authors of the scriptures do not press it on anyone.

## The Monthly Cycle and the Science of Focus

Another important reason was this: how many days of the month a woman should do sadhana, twenty-five days, all thirty, or not during those five days, has a science behind it. Science too says that a woman's mental state can fluctuate during those five days.

Whether it is a rite for the gods or for the ancestors, it must be done with complete focus. Nothing should go wrong in it. The emotions of the one performing it must be steady. So, so that no mistake happens even by accident, women were told this. But it was never forced that every woman must do it.

## The Order of Shraddha: If There Is No Son, the Women of the House

Read texts like the Garuda Purana or the Vishnu Purana. They give the order of who performs Shraddha: the son, the grandson, the great-grandson, that is, the other members of the same family; then the cousins; then the people on both the mother's and the father's side. And if there is no boy, it is clearly said that the women of the house should perform this rite.

## The Direct Experience at Antar Yog

Antar Yog is an institution where around two to three hundred people perform the Shraddha rite at one time, and its results come. The special thing is that around sixty to seventy percent of them are women. They do it so wholeheartedly that they receive its results immediately.

Men are receiving results too, but women are receiving just as much, even more. From this alone we understand that there never was any such restriction.

## The Soul Is Neither Man Nor Woman

But if you look outside, women are not allowed to do this so openly, because outside it is a male-dominated society. In a male-dominated society it is held that only a man should do it.

> "Remember one thing: the soul is neither a man nor a woman."

To take the soul to a state of liberation, this is all there is: they are our own parents, our own grandparents, our own ancestors. Who will liberate them, that is all they need. So in this there is no bond of man or woman, no restriction at all.

## What the Ancestors Wish For

At another place in the Vishnu Purana it is written that the ancestors wish for only this:

> **api dhanyaḥ kule jāyād asmākaṃ matimān naraḥ |**
> **akurvan vitta-śāṭhyaṃ yaḥ piṇḍān no nirvapiṣyati ||**
> "Will there be born in our family some wise and blessed person who, giving up greed for wealth, will offer pind for us?"
> (Vishnu Purana 3.14)

There is no mention here that only a boy must be born. The ancestors say only this: may some wise, blessed person be born in our family. So women too can perform Shraddha and Pind Daan.
