## Checklist

Now met:
- Focus keyword within the first 100 words (both languages; before, the Hindi focus keyword appeared only in the Key Takeaways).
- The searched wording ("क्या स्त्रियाँ श्राद्ध कर सकती हैं", "can women perform shraddha") now appears in the body and is the focus keyword.
- Direct answer paragraph placed early; one H2 per language phrased as a search question.

Still unmet (1):
- No unsupported scriptural claims: the blog text still carries the Sanskrit review's three major issues (the "no son" order, नरः read as "no word for son", the Sita episode told as history). Fixing them means changing what the blog says about scripture, which is an editorial correction, not an SEO edit, so it is left for the editor. The new SEO text avoids all three.

Not done on purpose: "pind daan" / "पिंडदान" is still not in the body below the H1. The report ties it to renaming the Sita heading, which should wait until flag 3 is fixed, so it stays a secondary keyword.
