# Can Women Perform Shraddha and Pind Daan

As Pitru Paksha arrives, many questions arise in the mind. Who are the Pitars (ancestors)? What is the true meaning of Shraddha and Tarpan (offering water and food to the ancestors)?

Among them, one question is much discussed: do women also have the right to perform Shraddha and Tarpan? Brahmarshi Acharya Upendra Ji answers it on three grounds: the scriptures, history and direct experience.

## The Answer Is Clear: Yes

Acharya Ji says that women can absolutely perform Shraddha. This right was not granted later by anyone; the scriptures themselves gave it to them.

Whether the Garuda Purana or the Vishnu Purana, wherever Shraddha is discussed, nowhere is there a restriction written that women must not perform it.

> "They certainly have the right. It is the scriptures themselves that have given them this right."

## Then Why Is It Not Compulsory for Every Woman?

There is a subtle point here. The scriptures have given consent, but they have not insisted. No scripture says that every woman must perform Shraddha.

The reason is a woman's emotional nature. If during Shraddha **shmashan vairagya** (the detachment that arises from seeing death up close) awakens within her, there can be difficulty at home, where small children and the family depend on her.

So women who are at a high spiritual level and are strong of mind were clearly told: you must certainly do it. For them there is no restriction.

## Mother Sita's Shraddha

History offers a beautiful example. Mother Sita herself performed the Shraddha rite of King Dasharatha, the father of Shri Ram.

She performed it so wholeheartedly that all the ancestors began to appear before her eyes. She saw the ancestors come in the form of Brahmins, partake of the food and leave.

There are many such examples, and yet the authors of the scriptures do not impose it on anyone.

## Why a Steady Mind Matters

The second reason relates to the monthly cycle. On which days of the month to do sadhana, and not to do it during those five days, has a science behind it. During those days the mental state can fluctuate.

Whether it is **deva karma** (rites for the gods) or **pitru karma** (rites for the ancestors), it needs complete focus and steady feeling, so that no mistake happens even by accident. This rule is a precaution, not a prohibition.

*When you perform a sacred act, is your mind truly there?*

## The Order Given in the Scriptures

The Garuda Purana and the Vishnu Purana give an order of who performs Shraddha: the son, grandson, great-grandson, then other family members, cousins, and relatives on both the mother's and father's side.

And if there is no boy in the house, it is clearly said that the women of the house should perform this rite.

## What We See at Antar Yog

At Antar Yog, around 200 to 300 people perform the Shraddha rite at one time. About 60-70 percent of them are women.

They do it so wholeheartedly that they receive **phalashruti** (the direct fruit of the practice) immediately. Men receive results too, but women are receiving just as much, even more. This shows that there never was any such restriction.

## The Soul Has No Gender

In the male-dominated society outside, women are not allowed to do this openly. It is held to be a man's task. But Acharya Ji reminds us of a basic truth:

> "The soul is neither a man nor a woman."

The ancestors are our own parents, our own grandparents. All they need is that someone liberates them. In this there is no bond of woman or man.

*Could you be the one who leads your ancestors towards liberation?*

## What the Ancestors Ask For

The Vishnu Purana describes the wish of the ancestors like this:

> **api dhanyaḥ kule jāyād asmākaṃ matimān naraḥ |**
> **akurvan vitta-śāṭhyaṃ yaḥ piṇḍān no nirvapiṣyati ||**
> "Will there be born in our family some wise and blessed person who, giving up greed for wealth, will offer pind for us?"
> (Vishnu Purana 3.14)

Notice that the word "son" is not here. The ancestors want only a wise, blessed person. That can be a daughter too.

## Press the Switch, and the Light Comes On

Acharya Ji gives a simple example. Press the switch and the fan or light must come on, whether a poor man presses it or a king, a sannyasi or a thief, the old or the young.

In the same way, the sadhanas of Antar Yog, such as Ganesh Vidya, Shri Vidya, Saraswati Diksha, Pitru Mukti, healing and Shiva sadhana, give swift results to most people. That is why Acharya Ji invites everyone to come and have a direct experience.

## Key Takeaways

- Scriptures such as the Garuda Purana and Vishnu Purana give women the right to perform Shraddha; there is no restriction.
- The scriptures gave consent but did not make it compulsory for every woman, so that shmashan vairagya in an emotional mind would not affect the family.
- Women who are spiritually advanced and strong of mind were told to perform Shraddha without fail.
- Mother Sita herself performed King Dasharatha's Shraddha and saw the ancestors.
- The rule about the five days of the monthly cycle is for steadiness of mind, not a prohibition.
- If there is no son, the women of the house are clearly told to perform Shraddha.
- The soul is neither man nor woman; the ancestors want only a wise, blessed descendant.
