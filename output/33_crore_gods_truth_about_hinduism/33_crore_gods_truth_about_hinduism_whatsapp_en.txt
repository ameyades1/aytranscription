*33 Crore Gods, or One God?*

Acharya Upendra Ji explains that Hinduism, too, has only *one God*, the formless Omkara. Lakshmi, Ganapati and Hanuman are its many powers, just as one person plays many roles.

💭 _When you stand before an idol, do you see its form or its essence?_
