## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** Begins mid-stream, carrying on from the previous session on ऋणानुबंध and sukh-dukh ("where I am involved"): what one owes another, which may be money, harsh words, or even hurt, to be given with love and साक्षी भाव. The transcript notes a slide heading fragment ("युधिष्ठिर / पूर्व जन्मों के सम्बन्ध व संकल्प") at the start.
- **Closing:** Cut off mid-thought. He reads the slide "जब जीवन में अत्यंत शोक और ग्लानि घेर ले, तब आत्मज्ञान ही एकमात्र शांति का मार्ग है" and asks, "तो उन्होंने क्या बोला, कि अँगूठी से काम होगा?" The recording stops there.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) (recap) Sukh and dukh come only where "I am involved". This is ऋणानुबंध, a ऋण (कर्ज़ा) bond: something I owed to a wife, husband, boss, son or enemy [«कुछ तो मेरा ऋणानुबंध आ»].
2. (start) The debt may be money, or even a harsh word, because the other person gave one in a past life. The guru will tell you what it is.
3. (start) Sometimes you must give dukh, even "strike" the other person, but with प्रेम in the mind and साक्षी भाव [«सुख देना होगा, कभी दुख»].
4. (start) Slide: two forces lie at the root of life's struggles, प्रारब्ध and ऋणानुबन्ध.
5. (start) Slide: however unfavourable भाग्य is, पुरुषार्थ makes the way and the सद्गुरु gives direction.
6. (early) Introduces Maharshi Vyasa (Vedavyasa, Krishna Dvaipayana), who classified (divided, "तोड़-तोड़ के") the four Vedas and wrote the Mahabharata, the 18 Puranas and the Brahma Sutras.
7. (early) In Ashma Gita, Vyasa is the enlightened guide who resolves Yudhishthira's doubts. Yudhishthira was broken by sorrow, delusion, death and आत्मग्लानि even after winning the war.
8. (early) "आपके अंदर भी एक युधिष्ठिर बैठा हुआ है". Here a Vyasa sits at the व्यासपीठ, and the solution to your crisis will come over the next two days as you read [«तो आपके अंदर भी एक»].
9. (early) Slide: a living सद्गुरु does not only give उपदेश but removes the darkness inside the disciple. He asks whether some darkness was removed today: eye and stomach illness, and who was to come into one's life, were already understood.
10. (early-middle) For college youth: the 5th house (पंचम स्थान) in the horoscope covers both ब्रह्मचर्य and affairs. Some boy or girl will come by प्रारब्ध, perhaps only to spoil one's life [«अब युवा-युवतियों की बात»].
11. (middle) The पुरुषार्थ lies in how far to entertain that person. Meeting once and liking them is the full purpose of the 5th house. If the liking stays, tell your parents and siblings openly and let them talk. That moves it to the 7th house (विवाह). If the person is the real spouse the relationship will form; if not, it will be rejected, because they came only for व्यभिचार.
12. (middle) The person comes by प्रारब्ध for certain. Whether to keep them is decided by पुरुषार्थ, with the guru's knowledge and the knowledge of ब्रह्मचर्य. Even a love marriage passes through 11th house (friend) → 5th (love) → 7th (marriage), so "I should know that role."
13. (middle) Slide: "जब तक शिष्य उठ न जाए, परम गुरु थकते नहीं." That is why he says listen 11 times.
14. (middle) The कालातीत guru is called व्यास. He keeps breaking the teaching down: if one book doesn't work, then Patanjali, then Narada Bhakti Sutra. Once he takes a case, his प्रण is to win it.
15. (middle) The problem is that the guru cannot हस्तक्षेप (interfere), so he keeps prodding in different ways: "अभी कर, उठ, अभी नहीं तो कभी नहीं."
16. (middle) Slide: when there is भ्रम, चिंता and lost purpose, a living सद्गुरु gives शांति and also makes you steady in your धर्म. The परम गुरु is the "वेदव्यास" of your life.
17. (middle) The Veda is not understood directly. The Upanishad verse: no amount of learning or प्रवचन-listening gives the Self. Guru must do शक्तिपात, and it happens through your समर्पण.
18. (middle) Vaishampayana is introduced because the first shlok is his: he narrated the Mahabharata.
19. (middle) Janamejaya means "born to win". The victory meant is not Kurukshetra but winning "the hearts, the minds of the world", which should be learned from childhood. Second meaning (slide): one who seeks victory over suffering and inner conflict, the seeker.
20. (middle) Ashma means stone, or दृढ़ता. Here what is meant is दृढ़ता in adhyatma, "Foundation की". His parents named him so, "पत्थर की तरह नहीं" (not meaning hard as stone).
21. (middle) Slide: in Ashma Gita, the play of life and death is under ईश्वर and man is only निमित्त. Bhishma, Karna and others had died in the war, and there is no need to weep for relatives. Anyone can go suddenly by heart attack, so "you have to be prepared" [«तो तुम्हें रिश्तेदार के लिए»].
22. (middle) Don't stay stuck in गृहस्थ आश्रम and dependent on each other. Separate rooms for ladies and gents at the shibir symbolise this, besides ब्रह्मचर्य and decorum.
23. (middle) Cultivate some विरक्ति and distance, or the अर्धनारीश्वर स्वरूप of taking on the spouse's qualities. The husband should learn some cooking. The wife should go to the bank and learn where bills are paid. "One fine day the world will leave you in one clean sweep, या तो you will have to leave the world."
24. (late) Slide: Yudhishthira acted according to dharma by fighting adharma. "To do non-violent things, you need to have violent people around you": Army, Police and security [«मैंने अभी क्या बोला था?»].
25. (late) Pooja no longer happens in Pakistan, Bangladesh and Afghanistan, once part of Bharat, because the border was lost; perhaps there were too few क्षत्रिय. Lose the land and dharma goes; lose dharma and संस्कृति goes; then nothing remains.
26. (late) A strong क्षत्रिय is needed to protect borders, business and relations: trademarks, patents, CCTV, "security first". This is "काली विद्या" in the sense of Mahakali, not black magic.
27. (late) The rishis called Rama and Lakshmana to kill the rakshasas who disturbed their yajna. "Best if it is me", if I can fight.
28. (late) At times telling a lie is acceptable if the guru said so: "मेरा गुरु कभी गलत नहीं हो सकता". "Everything is fair in war"; with रणनीति, "कुछ भी चलता है".
29. (late) Slide: Ashma Gita teaches आत्मज्ञान and निर्लिप्तता: the जीवात्मा is different from the body, and the body's destruction is not the final truth. This is the crux of the earlier point that when तादात्म्य breaks, साक्षी भाव arises.
30. (late) Of the three goddesses, Saraswati (sattvic: veena, Veda, mala) does not fight. Without Kali's power one cannot even debate or do शास्त्रार्थ, whether fighting by शस्त्र or by शास्त्र. Lakshmi gives wealth and holds no weapon; only Kali does. (He loses his thread: "मैं क्या बता रहा था?")
31. (late) Mahakali stands with a sword, an eye on it, over her husband lying like a शव, "शिव भी नहीं". This is symbolic, not advice to wives: Shiva = आत्मा/अंतरात्मा; Kali = प्रकृति, my mind, intellect, ego and body, the जड़ part (also पार्वती, daughter of the mountain) [«तो महाकाली कैसे दिखाई हैं»].
32. (late) When one must fight, one sometimes has to put a foot on Shiva: set the अंतरात्मा and rules aside for a time. Mosquitoes, dogs (see Stories). "Sometimes you have to be the Kali."
33. (late) The negative side: when Kali's negative form, negative काली विद्या, comes, the अंतरात्मा is already dead. Then one can do मारण, मोहन, वशीकरण on others' souls. In war, "at times I have to forget these things", and rise above them.
34. (end) Slide: when शोक and ग्लानि overwhelm, आत्मज्ञान is the only path to peace. He starts asking whether a ring (अँगूठी) would do the work, and the recording cuts off.

## Key terms
- **ऋणानुबंध**: a bond of ऋण (कर्ज़ा, debt) from past lives; something owed (money, words, hurt) to a particular person.
- **प्रारब्ध**: the result of past karma; what will certainly come.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: one's own effort and choice, e.g. how far to entertain what प्रारब्ध brings.
- **साक्षी भाव**: the witness attitude; it arises when तादात्म्य (identification with the body) breaks.
- **व्यासपीठ**: the guru's seat; the place where "a Vyasa" sits.
- **व्यास**: the कालातीत guru, who keeps breaking the teaching down until the disciple understands.
- **शक्तिपात**: done by the guru; it works through the disciple's समर्पण.
- **पंचम / सप्तम / एकादश स्थान**: horoscope houses for love and ब्रह्मचर्य, marriage, and friends.
- **जनमेजय**: जन + मे + जय, "born to win" the hearts and minds of people.
- **अश्म**: stone, or दृढ़ता (firmness in adhyatma).
- **निमित्त**: a mere instrument; life and death are in ईश्वर's hands.
- **अर्धनारीश्वर स्वरूप**: taking on the spouse's qualities and abilities.
- **काली / काली विद्या**: Mahakali's power to fight and protect, not black magic. Its negative form means मारण, मोहन, वशीकरण.
- **शिव (under Kali's feet)**: the आत्मा/अंतरात्मा. **काली / पार्वती**: प्रकृति, the जड़ part (mind, buddhi, ahankara, body).
- **शास्त्रार्थ**: debate on scripture; it also needs Kali's power.
- **निर्लिप्तता**: non-attachment.
