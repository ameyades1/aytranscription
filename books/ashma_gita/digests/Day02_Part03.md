# Digest: Ashma Gita Shibir, Day 2, Part 3

## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** The session starts mid-sentence (`[«तो उसको वैसा ही officer मिलेगा»]`). He is continuing from Part 2: a person who escapes the king's दंड by bribery will keep meeting corrupt officers and police. This belongs to the commentary on the shlokas before shlok 10 (theft, taking others' property, punishment by the king or by nature).
- **Closing:** The session is cut off mid-thought during the commentary on shlok 11. The last words are `[«विचार द्वारा, मतलब ये सही है, ये गलत है»]`, while he is explaining that one should stop oneself from the wrong path through विचार. The transcriber's note says no end time was recorded.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) Someone who escapes punishment by bribing an officer will keep getting officers and police of the same kind. He thinks he is clever ("हर आदमी की एक कीमत होती है", "कीमत देना मुझे आता है"), but he has paid a bribe and committed a पाप without understanding it is a trap. `[«But वो समझ नहीं रहा है कि ये trap है»]`
2. (start) The difference between दाम (from साम, दाम, दंड, भेद) and bribe: money paid for a धर्मनीति purpose, to unblock a large task, can count as दाम. Money paid for personal gain is पाप. So even after the king punishes, the wrong carries on through bribery and the like. `[«किसको दाम में count करना है»]`
3. (start) When punishment is reduced, for example a jailed minister getting home food and comforts a thief does not get, the person comes out and does more damage, and even contests elections. `[«शिक्षा कम करो»]` (शिक्षा here is used in the Marathi sense of punishment.)
4. (early) Slide example of a corrupt officer: one day some case or disclosure exposes him. That is his दंड. Such games do not last long. `[«तो ये उसको दंड है»]`
5. (early) Scams will never end because it is कलियुग: one person does it, then a second, then a third. But a particular person's scam is exposed through some single case, when the limit of his पुण्य runs out. प्रकृति makes sure of it (quotes "क्षीणे पुण्ये मर्त्यलोकं विशन्ति"). We should not go on that path. The next scammer did not learn from the last one, which is why the next scam happens. `[«But उसका scam exposed कब होता है»]` One phrase here is unclear in the transcript ([अस्पष्ट]).
6. (early) Stealing a colleague's credit, prize or poem is also चोरी, "all type की चोरी". Done once, a person may escape, even for life. Done repeatedly, प्रकृति will not let go: "गुरुतत्त्व पकड़ेगा". He will be punished, by the king or by गुरुतत्त्व. `[«ये भी चोरी है ना»]`
7. (early) Wealth, honour or authority gained by breaking मर्यादा: life itself pierces the person like an arrow (धनुष्य/बाण image, "ऐसा कुछ बोला था ना हमने?", a reference back to an earlier session) (recap).
8. (early–middle) Chanakya Niti (spoken here, the slide comes later): wealth earned by अधर्म stays at most ten years. After that प्रकृति prepares to take it back with ब्याज (interest). Within ten years anything can happen, even the biggest scam. Some may run to fifteen. `[«And चाणक्य नीति है कि»]`
9. (middle) Age matters for the फलश्रुति. Someone who takes up wrongdoing at 64–65, or a bank manager who does a scam at 57 with two years left before retirement, has a different फलश्रुति from a 20- or 30-year-old, who will keep doing it for a long time. `[«अब ये करने की आदत किसी को ६५»]`
10. (middle) धन does not mean only money: another man's wife is also धन ("शुक्र ही बोला है"). A slip at sixty is punished differently and the person cannot do much more anyway. If व्यभिचार, corruption or taking others' rights starts before twenty, the potential for ruin is far greater. It starts with व्यभिचार and moves on to corruption money. `[«वैसे ही धन मतलब सिर्फ़ पैसा नहीं»]`
11. (middle) A minister's 20–25-year-old son who gets a post from the party ready-made and learns corruption is in more danger than his father, who started taking money at 65 while building the party. This leads into shlok 10. `[«कोई मंत्री बन गया»]`
12. (middle) Shlok 10: people aged twenty or thirty who take to such deeds will not live a hundred years. He stresses that the shlok was spoken at the time of the Mahabharata, not about today. `[«राजन्! जो बीस और तीस वर्ष»]`
13. (middle) Reason 1 for a short life: from an early age people's बुरी दृष्टि/नज़र falls on him ("ये मर गया तो अच्छा"), and he will face many problems. More on this "two or three shlokas ahead". `[«मतलब they will not have a long life»]`
14. (middle) If such habits come late in life, the punishment in this life is small and the person can only do it for a short time. If they come in youth, प्रकृति "बहुत डाँटती है, चिल्लाती है", people blame the parents' upbringing, and the punishment may last a whole lifetime. `[«ये आदतें गलती से late age में»]`
15. (middle) A police or criminal case at 25 spoils the passport and the chance to contest elections. Politicians have criminal cases that are "not proved", so they get off, but for ordinary people a mark against their name at twenty ruins their life. `[«शायद पच्चीस में अगर हो गया»]`
16. (middle) Once a label like "flirt" sticks at 25–26, or a juvenile has committed murder, who will marry him? Preventing such habits in childhood is the parents' responsibility. Such a child uses up the पुण्य of his father and forefathers. `[«पच्चीस-छब्बीस की उम्र में»]`
17. (middle) Two reasons he will not live a hundred years: (1) his पाप keeps growing; (2) he cannot live with his own guilt, keeps getting illnesses, and life gets harder at 30, 35 and 40. Having had his "मज़ा" through early व्यभिचार, he finds no excitement with a real spouse, and quarrels are certain ("शुक्र का rule"). `[«वो दो कारणों से सौ वर्ष»]`
18. (middle) "सौ साल" means long life. Being denied a long life is the first punishment, because he is already a पापी. `[«सौ साल तक जीवित, in the sense long life»]`
19. (middle) Slide: the shlok also points to spiritual and moral death. His comment: life becomes "है नहीं के बराबर". Even if his प्रारब्ध gives him 102 years, after a certain point he lives as if dead. This applies to women and men alike. `[«मतलब फिर उसका जीवन»]` `[«प्रारब्ध में इसके १०२ साल»]`
20. (middle) Who saves such a person? Only the गुरु. He can even become वाल्मीकि from वाल्या. "There is a solution". But for ordinary people with very heavy प्रारब्ध, "श्रीकृष्ण ने बोला है, तीन प्रकार" (this is developed after the Chanakya slide). `[«And इसके जीवन में बचाने के लिए कौन आएगा»]`
21. (middle) Chanakya slide comment: Chanakya, the expert on the Kautilya Arthashastra, would know this "research work". He gives references from here and there so the idea fits in the audience's minds. Ashma Gita will get more and more interesting, and these are all important concepts. `[«तो this is what आचार्य चाणक्य says»]`
22. (late) Gita 3.38: knowledge is covered by काम (wrong desires about सुख-दुःख) in three ways. `[«तो ज्ञान ढका जाएगा»]`
23. (late) (a) Sattvic desire that went too far, such as building a temple or doing good work with a slight wish to cheat, to overdo, or to get more मान, is like fire covered by smoke. A gust of wind clears the smoke and the fire (ज्ञान) shows at once. Not a big दोष. `[«पहली, सात्त्विक कामना»]`
24. (late) (b) Rajasic desire is like dust settled on a mirror. Wind will not remove it. The गुरु may give suggestions, but you must wipe it with effort and कष्ट. Why a mirror: it shows your reflection, your real स्वरूप ("reflected consciousness"). Even heavy rajasic पाप can be removed and आत्मज्ञान is still possible, but it needs more effort and much प्रायश्चित्त. `[«अगर तुम्हारी कामना रजोगुणी है»]`
25. (late) (c) Being like दुर्योधन, refusing to listen, is like the embryo covered by the जेर (womb membrane). It comes out only after the full nine months: understanding comes only after suffering the whole पाप, after प्रकृति strikes. Guru, parents, siblings, boss, even a king's punishment do not help until then. Do not go that far into तमोगुण. In कलियुग some learn only "मार खाकर". `[«And अगर दुर्योधन जैसे होते हो»]`
26. (late) He calls shlok 10 an important shlok ("Important श्लोक"). Slide example of youth addiction: nashā, gambling, mobile and obscenity at 20–30 destroy energy and health, so they look old at 40. His comments: the liver is ruined, hair turns white early, and "science ने भी स्वीकार किया है". वीर्यनाश can cause all kinds of harm, धननाश brings poverty, and अहंकार makes society one day "talk to" the person. Such habits should not start early, nor later, but youth and later life have different फलश्रुति. `[«इसमें तो liver-वीवर से लेकर»]`
27. (late) Slide example of a corrupt path for quick success: within 5–10 years, jail, illness or mental stress breaks such people. `[«Quick success की वजह से भी»]`
28. (late–end) Shlok 11: it shows a reality of society. The solution: first understand that I myself once took such a wrong path, in this birth or a past one, which brought poverty or suffering. The aim must be निवारण. Otherwise "ऐसे ही पितर बन जाओगे… आत्महत्या करोगे". `[«तो इसका solution क्या दिया है»]`
29. (end) औषध through बुद्धि/विवेक-बुद्धि means going to the गुरु for प्रायश्चित्त, learning the शास्त्र, and building आत्मबल. Stop yourself from कुमार्ग by विचार, "ये सही है, ये गलत है" (the session cuts off here). `[«तो निवारण करने के लिए बुद्धि के द्वारा»]`

## Main-text shlokas covered
- **Shlok 10** (middle): `ये च विंशतिवर्षा वै त्रिंशद्वर्षाश्च मानवाः। परेण ते वर्षशतान्न भविष्यन्ति पार्थिव॥`
  - Meaning: people of 20 or 30 who take to theft and other misdeeds will not live a hundred years.
  - Commentary: **long**, with several slides.
  - Text note: the slide read "वा" and "त्रिंशदवशश्च". The transcriber restored "वै" and "त्रिंशद्वर्षाश्च". Uncertain; check against Gita Press.
- **Shlok 11** (late–end): `तेषां परमदुःखानां बुद्ध्या भैषज्यमाचरेत्। सर्वप्राणभृतां वृत्तं प्रेक्षमाणस्ततस्ततः॥`
  - Meaning: seeing what befalls people from the painful conduct of beings everywhere, one should treat those great sufferings born of poverty with बुद्धि as medicine, that is, stop oneself from the wrong path by reflection.
  - Commentary: **brief**, and cut off. Probably continues in the next session.

## Other scripture cited

## Key terms
- **दाम**: one of साम, दाम, दंड, भेद. Money paid for a धर्मनीति purpose; for personal gain it is a bribe and पाप.
- **शिक्षा**: used in the Marathi sense of punishment/दंड.
- **दंड**: punishment, from the king, or from nature through exposure.
- **पुण्य की limit**: once पुण्य runs out, a person's wrongdoing is exposed.
- **कलियुग**: why scams never end, and why some learn only after being beaten.
- **गुरुतत्त्व**: the principle that catches repeated wrongdoers when the king doesn't.
- **प्रकृति**: nature as enforcer; it "डाँटती है, चिल्लाती है" and takes back wealth with interest.
- **धन**: not just money; another's wife is also धन.
- **व्यभिचार**: sexual misconduct, the start of the slide towards corruption.
- **फलश्रुति**: the resulting consequence, which differs by the age at which the wrong begins.
- **प्रारब्ध**: allotted destiny (e.g. 102 years), still hollowed out by early sin.
- **बुरी दृष्टि/नज़र**: people's ill will towards a young wrongdoer, a cause of short life.
- **वीर्यनाश / धननाश**: loss of vital fluid / loss of wealth, as consequences of youth addiction.
- **काम / कामना**: desire that covers ज्ञान; sattvic, rajasic or tamasic.
- **सात्त्विक / राजसिक / तमोगुण**: the three degrees of covering (smoke, dust, membrane).
- **जेर / उल्ब**: the membrane around the embryo.
- **प्रायश्चित्त**: atonement, needed especially for rajasic sins.
- **औषध / भैषज्य**: medicine: प्रायश्चित्त with the गुरु, शास्त्र, आत्मबल.
- **विवेक-बुद्धि / विचार**: discerning right from wrong to stop oneself from कुमार्ग.
- **आत्मबल**: inner strength.
- **निवारण**: removing suffering, which should be the aim.
- **पितर**: ancestor-spirit state, a warning about unresolved suffering.
