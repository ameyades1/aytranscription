**Opening:** The recording starts mid-sentence (`[«या आप हो, माता-पिता छोड़के»]`). He is finishing an earlier point: separation from loved ones is certain (you may go abroad for business, someone's life may end), so prepare for it. The English sentence "you will not get that entire..." is cut off.

## Teachings in order
1. (recap/continuing) (start) Separation from family is certain: parents may be left behind for business abroad, or a life may end. Since this is clearly understood, why not prepare for it? [«या आप हो, माता-पिता»]
2. (start) The lonely man ("अकेला मैं रह गया") is alone because he never built hobbies, a "दूसरा परिवार", friends or a wider social circle. He kept only "मैं और मेरा घर", so when they went, nothing was left. [«अरे, तेरी hobbies होतीं»]
3. (early) Shlok 52: no one gets to stay with anyone always. Even Ram and Sita had वियोग when Ravan took Sita. Continuous togetherness is impossible: eight office hours apart, and even trips to the bathroom. "मैं उसके साथ ही चिपक के रहूँगा" is impossible. [«रावण सीता को उठाके»]
4. (early) Even your own body does not stay with you. Youthful body, eyes, hair and nails all leave, and in old age black hair turns white or falls out. "अब ये तेरा साथ नहीं देता है, तो बाकी तेरा साथ क्या देगा?" [«Your own body: young age»]
5. (early) Shlok 53: the "राजन" is still Janak. Where are your father and grandfather now? Neither of you sees the other. [«अब ये राजन कौन?»]
6. (early) Those who have gone are gone. Do श्राद्ध for their उद्धार without worry. Let an ex-girlfriend be happy elsewhere ("मेरे लिए थी ही नहीं वो"). With a bad boss, think "मैं रद्दी employee था", learn the lesson and do better at the next job. [«गए तो गए। तो श्राद्ध»]
7. (early) He notes that the shlokas from here on are direct, "without explanation". [«अभी सारे श्लोक direct हैं»]
8. (early-middle) Shlok 54: heaven and hell cannot be seen with स्थूल नेत्र. Shastra is the only eye. So one must go to the guru or सद्गुरु, who gives ज्ञान चक्षु. You must start seeing God in the guru, who has the same ears, nose, hands and feet as anyone ("Lord in the form of"; गुरुर्ब्रह्मा...). [«तो कहाँ जाना होगा?»]
9. (middle) First God must be seen in the guru. Only then can the guru teach you to see God in others. If you cannot see it in him, he cannot show you anything. "Your first belief should be: यहाँ पे मुझे वो सब दिखता है", including the अदृष्ट part. Learn to see साकार and निराकार. [«पहले गुरु में वो दिखना»]
10. (middle) With ज्ञान चक्षु, a painful event becomes an occasion to give thanks: "ये रोने का time नहीं, ये और खुश होने का time है". What looked like नरक was actually स्वर्ग, and the war that came to you is a धर्मयुद्ध. [«मतलब ये दुःख की घटना»]
11. (middle) The suffering is what brought you to the guru, and the guru can lead to मोक्ष, so thank the suffering. Living in माया was the real problem. [«वो दुःख, कष्ट था»]
12. (middle) "Act according to shastra" means acting by गुरु आज्ञा, with शरणागति, not textbook knowledge alone. Then do यज्ञ, तप, दान and सेवा according to the shastras, perform श्राद्ध विधि properly, and begin becoming a little विरक्त. [«तो मतलब गुरु आज्ञा»]
13. (middle) Gita 15.10: only those with ज्ञानचक्षु see the soul, whether it is leaving the body, living in it or enjoying. "ज्ञान के गर्भ में गुरु रहता है". Ashma Rishi is saying you need a new चश्मा, new eyes, a cataract surgery done through knowledge. [«तो गुरु ही वो ज्ञान है»]
14. (middle) See every situation as "Lord in the form of xyz person has come to teach me a lesson", or as the result of periods in one's पत्रिका (horoscope) and the karma of many births. This must be accepted. [«Lord in the form of xyz»]
15. (middle) Keep doing पुरुषार्थ even after accepting. It may not erase the whole minus, but bringing minus 1000 down to minus 800 means a lighter "शिक्षा" (punishment). Do not make it minus 1200 by sinning more. Bring it to minus 600 or minus 100. "सुई तो लगेगी, चलेगा". [«भले पुरुषार्थ से पूरा minus»]
16. (middle) Shlok 55 is introduced as the solution. Keep ब्रह्मचर्य in youth: no girlfriend or boyfriend, no व्यभिचार. Mistakes before twenty or thirty can cause problems for the whole life, and corruption is out of question (partial recap: "क्या बोला था?"). [«मतलब जब उसका childhood»]
17. (middle) ब्रह्म चरति इति ब्रह्मचारी: study the Vedas, study generally, and avoid वीर्य नाश. [«ब्रह्म चरति इति»]
18. (middle) Then enter गृहस्थ आश्रम "if he wants". Follow its rules and earn both wealth (धनार्जन) and knowledge (ज्ञानार्जन). [«मतलब if he wants»]
19. (middle) Having children to repay the ऋण is "not compulsion", only general public belief. [«ये compulsion नहीं है»]
20. (middle) The way out of संसार चक्र is not difficult, and "जब जागो तब सवेरा". Start from here: thank everyone, repay debts as far as possible, resolve पितृ दोष, try for मुक्ति, and thank gods, people and nature. Act by the rules of your आश्रम and always keep यज्ञ भाव (sacrifice). [«Difficult तो कुछ बोला नहीं»]
21. (middle) Not having दोषदृष्टि does not mean never punishing. A warrior may have to cut off hands and feet, but not out of दोषदृष्टि. Mahishasuramardini kills Mahishasur for his good, and Krishna kills Kansa for his good, then later shows the विराट रूप through ज्ञानचक्षु. [«दोषदृष्टि न रखे मतलब»]
22. (middle) Slide on meaning: ब्रह्मचर्य is the first stage of mind control, study and self-purification, and without it गृहस्थ duty is like weightlifting. गृहस्थ is a key step from नर to नारायण, "if one has taken that path". पितृ ऋण is repaid through children "or through one's own साधना", देव ऋण through यज्ञ, मनुष्य ऋण through सेवा. [«अगर वो मार्ग किसी ने»]
23. (middle) Grihastha is not "मज़ा-मज़ा". The other three आश्रम depend on it: children (ब्रह्मचर्य), the elderly (वानप्रस्थ) and the renunciant gurus (संन्यास), who need दान. Earning is compulsory. "मैं गृहस्थ आश्रम में हूँ and भिखारी हूँ" means grihastha has not been done right. [«गृहस्थाश्रम में दो काम»]
24. (middle) The aim is मोक्ष, not rebirth as a dog, cat or bird. धनार्जन and ज्ञानार्जन are the main aims, and children are secondary. Wealth serves everything from oneself to the world, and knowledge serves one's own liberation: "जनहिताय स्वयं मोक्षाय च. That is grihasthashram's real role." [«धनार्जन तो करना ही है, and aim»]
25. (middle-late) Of the two, ज्ञानार्जन matters most. Less money is acceptable, but learning must not stop. To "no money, no job, how can I come to the guru?", his answer is to come to the यथाशक्ति classes, but come. [«थोड़ा धन कम मिला»]
26. (middle-late) If you do not get right knowledge by your own effort, प्रकृति teaches a harsh lesson ("फटका"). Learning from the guru first is better. [«ये पक्का है»]
27. (late) Not having दोषदृष्टि, seven points. (1) Give up fault-finding: see the person as "Lord in the form of xyz person came to teach me a lesson or play with me" (लीला). (2) Do not nurse negativity. See how the person corrects the fault, ask whether you can help, and ask what your active role is, beyond complaining. (3) Sahishnuta and kindness. (4) Look at their virtues. (5) No enmity or द्वेष. [«कुछ तो lesson सिखाना था»]
28. (late) Point 5 does not forbid Mahishasuramardini-style punishment. Krishna throws the चक्र only after a hundred mistakes. Chanakya: pamper for five years, discipline ("फटका") for ten formative years, then at sixteen treat the son as a friend and let go. [«तो इसका मतलब ये नहीं»]
29. (late) (6) Point 6 says only "दृष्टिकोण", not "व्यवहार": treat on merit. (7) समभाव and कृतज्ञता are the subtle threads of मोक्ष. [«सिर्फ दृष्टिकोण बोला है»]
30. (late) Parents' debt: they carried you and cleaned your मल-मूत्र, so push their wheelchair, take them to hospital and accept their adult diapers. Putting them in a वृद्धाश्रम leaves the debt unpaid, and caring for them is "the only way". Repaying while they live is better than पिंडदान after death. [«कभी तो उन्होंने मुझे गोद»]
31. (late) Society's debt is repaid through सेवा, दान and सहयोग, either yourself or through a worthy guru, e.g. "अपना महादान जैसा programme", as सत्पात्र दान with ॐ तत्सत् भावना. [«स्वयं भी कर सकते हैं»]
32. (late) Nature's debt: we take wood from trees, so plant trees, keep things clean and protect the environment. "We can serve the nature back." Repay all debts slowly through life. [«और प्रकृति का ऋण भी»]
33. (late) Shlok 56: marrying for children is suggested, not compulsory. Keeping the senses pure means seeing good things and having good touch, not putting in eye drops. [«वो compulsion होता है क्या?»]
34. (end) "Give up hope of happiness" does not mean giving up good living or "आत्महत्या करूँ क्या?". It means dropping हठ and ज़िद. Dream the biggest dream, as long as you do not get stuck in it. He himself dreams of विश्वगुरु, not for his own name but so that सनातन spreads everywhere, "not only for Hindus", and the problems of all religions get resolved. [«तो मतलब अभी जीने का»]
35. (end) Drop low-level आसक्ति. Worry does not get you more than your प्रारब्ध. Worry for big work (विश्व कार्य) is different, and the guru will take care of that too. Just do what you have been told. [«तो छोटा-मोटा क्यों सोचते»]

## Other scripture cited
- **Gita 15.10**: the Hindi translation is on a slide, and he spoke only «पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः». Full verse (from the transcript note, not spoken): उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम्। विमूढा नानुपश्यन्ति पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः॥ It supports the idea that only the eye of knowledge, given by the guru, sees truth.
- **गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णुः**: Guru Stotra, only the opening words, "वगैरह". It supports seeing God in the guru.
- **ब्रह्म चरति इति ब्रह्मचारी**: an etymology. It supports the view that brahmacharya includes study of the Veda.
- **Chanakya Niti** (attribution certain from the verse; he did not name it): लालयेत् पञ्च वर्षाणि, ताडयेत् दश वर्षाणि … प्राप्ते तु षोडशे वर्षे पुत्रं मित्रवत् [?]. It is **partial and garbled**, with the line order changed. The standard text is लालयेत् पञ्च वर्षाणि दश वर्षाणि ताडयेत्। प्राप्ते तु षोडशे वर्षे पुत्रं मित्रवदाचरेत्॥ It supports discipline without दोषदृष्टि.
- **"जनहिताय स्वयं मोक्षाय च"**: kept as spoken. It is a variant of आत्मनो मोक्षार्थं जगद्धिताय च. It sums up the role of grihastha.
- **ॐ तत्सत्** (associated with Gita 17): only mentioned, as the attitude for giving दान.

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- Prepare in advance for separation: hobbies, a "दूसरा परिवार", friends and a wider social circle.
- Do श्राद्ध for those who have died, without worry.
- Begin to see God in the guru first, then in everyone ("Lord in the form of xyz").
- Thank the suffering. Keep doing पुरुषार्थ to reduce karmic minus.
- Live by guru आज्ञा with शरणागति. Do यज्ञ, तप, दान, सेवा and श्राद्ध properly, and become a little विरक्त.
- "जब जागो तब सवेरा": start thanking everyone and repaying debts from today, and keep यज्ञ भाव.
- Keep up ज्ञानार्जन even when short of money, e.g. through the यथाशक्ति classes.
- Care for parents in old age yourself, not through a वृद्धाश्रम.
- Give दान through a worthy guru or programme. Plant trees and keep things clean.
- Think big without attachment, and do only what you have been told.
- No Q&A exchange in this session, only his rhetorical "समझा कि नहीं?".

## Key terms
- **सुयोग**: the chance to stay together. It never lasts.
- **स्थूल नेत्र**: physical eyes, which cannot see स्वर्ग and नरक.
- **ज्ञान चक्षु / ज्ञानचक्षुषः**: the eye of knowledge given by the guru, "a new चश्मा".
- **सद्गुरु**: the one who gives ज्ञान चक्षु. "ज्ञान के गर्भ में गुरु रहता है".
- **साकार-निराकार, अदृष्ट**: what must be seen in the guru, the form and the formless, including the unseen.
- **धर्मयुद्ध**: how a hardship can be seen with ज्ञान चक्षु.
- **माया**: living in it was the real problem.
- **शरणागति, गुरु आज्ञा**: what "act by shastra" means.
- **विरक्त**: becoming a little detached.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: effort that reduces karmic minus even if it cannot erase it.
- **ब्रह्मचर्य / ब्रह्मचारी**: ब्रह्म चरति इति, meaning study, the Vedas and no वीर्य नाश. Also मन संयम, अध्ययन and आत्मशुद्धि.
- **व्यभिचार**: what to avoid in youth.
- **गृहस्थ आश्रम**: not "मज़ा-मज़ा". It is धनार्जन plus ज्ञानार्जन and supports the other three आश्रम.
- **वानप्रस्थ, संन्यासाश्रम**: the elderly, and renunciant gurus who depend on दान.
- **धनार्जन / ज्ञानार्जन**: earning serves oneself up to the world, and learning serves one's own liberation, which matters more.
- **पितृ / देव / मनुष्य ऋण**: repaid through children (or साधना), यज्ञ and सेवा. He adds प्रकृति ऋण.
- **पितृ दोष**: to be resolved.
- **यज्ञ भाव**: "sacrifice भाव".
- **दोषदृष्टि**: a fault-finding outlook, which differs from rightful punishment.
- **सहिष्णुता**: "सहन करना वगैरह".
- **समभाव**: equal view; behaviour is "treat on merit".
- **कृतज्ञता**: gratitude, a subtle thread of मोक्ष.
- **लीला**: a person comes to "play with me".
- **पिंडदान / श्राद्ध**: repaying after death, which is less good than repaying while they live.
- **सत्पात्र दान**: giving to a worthy recipient, with ॐ तत्सत्.
- **प्रारब्ध**: you get only what it holds, and worry adds nothing.
- **आसक्ति**: "low level thinking".
- **विश्वगुरु**: his big dream for सनातन.
- **फटका**: a harsh lesson, from nature or from parental discipline.

## Sensitive or editorial items
- (middle) Guru-centrism: "पहले गुरु में वो दिखना चाहिए… नहीं तो वो आपको कुछ दिखा नहीं पाएगा". It is a strong claim of guru-dependence.
- (middle) Karma and horoscope (पत्रिका) explanations for hardship, and the "minus 1000" arithmetic.
- (middle) Youthful sexual mistakes "पूरे आयुष्य में problem", and वीर्य नाश. Presentation needs care.
- (middle, late) Divine violence and "हाथ-पैर काटने भी पड़ेंगे"; Chanakya's ताडयेत्, i.e. hitting children for ten years. This could read as endorsing corporal punishment.
- (late) Criticism of people who put parents in a वृद्धाश्रम ("the only way").
- (middle) "भिखारी" grihastha is judgmental. Children are called "secondary" and not compulsory, which may be worth keeping as a nuance.
- (middle-late) Institutional: the यथाशक्ति classes, "come to the guru"; the "महादान" programme for दान through the guru (fundraising, name unconfirmed).

## Boundary notes
Two good chapter starts: **shlok 52** (early), on the impermanence of every bond, which continues the earlier topic, and **shlok 55** ("फिर उन्होंने अभी solution दिया है"), where the text turns from the problem of grief to the solution of the four आश्रम, the three debts and no दोषदृष्टि. Shlok 56's commentary ends the session but continues the "solution" theme, so the next session (likely shlok 57 onward) probably carries it on.
