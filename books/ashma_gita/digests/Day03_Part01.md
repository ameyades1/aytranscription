# Digest: Ashma Gita Shibir, Day 3, Part 1

## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** He moves from the previous activity into "ज्ञान गंगा" and picks up Ashma Gita (Mahabharata, Shanti Parva) where it stopped, after shlok 16. He says the remaining shloks (he says about forty) will go fast because the main teaching is done and the video was already watched.
- **Closing:** Cut off mid-thought. Shlok 18's slide breaks off at "अप्रिय वस्तुओं के साथ संयोग," and he is in the middle of explaining प्रिय/अप्रिय and the reverse journey "नारायण से नर … नर से नारायण, वापिस घर वापसी" when the recording ends.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) (recap) Ashma Gita is an उपपर्व in the Shanti Parva, and they had reached shlok 16 [«तो अश्मा ऋषि के हम»].
2. (start) The rest will take about 30% of the time shlok 16 took, because the video was already seen and the text is "self-explanatory" [«तो अब जितना १६ को»].
3. (start) (recap) Earlier he spoke of a connection between प्रारब्ध and काल. The coming shloks (about 30 of them) give everyday examples of it, "which you need to correlate in life" [«प्रारब्ध और काल, इनका»].
4. (start) He is not hurrying. Only that much is left to teach, since "दो दिन में तो main सब सिखा दिया है", and the examples come from the ऋषि himself [«Not that जल्दी-जल्दी आगे»].
5. (start) A grieving person cannot take much theory; you must give examples ("अरे, इसको भी ऐसा हुआ था, देख"). Yudhishthira was in sorrow, so the ऋषि uses examples [«युधिष्ठिर दुःख में था»].
6. (start) Seeing that others carry the same sorrow makes one's own sorrow secondary and smaller. "Guru uses that technique", and Ashma Rishi uses it here: it happens to others too through प्रारब्ध and काल, "so you have to work on it" [«अधिकतर ऐसा होता है कि»].
7. (early) Shlok 16: the sukh or dukh that comes to beings must be borne, with no उपाय to avoid it. (recap) "No option": even अवतार must bear it, even Shri Ram [«Because हमने बोला था कि no option»].
8. (early) Set aside the avatars' cases. Their suffering may be taking on someone's हलाहल विष, a curse, a past birth, or लीला done on purpose "just to tell to the world" [«वो तो किसी का हलाहल»].
9. (early) Why such लीला: so that even a layman understands that पुरुषार्थ belongs to one birth, while the forces against it are कर्म of many births. "Minus १०० से … minus ९९ भी लगाया, तो भी minus में ही" [«पुरुषार्थ एक जन्म का है»].
10. (early) Even with a guru, "हल चुभने की जगह पे कम से कम एक सुई तो चुभेगी". Something still comes, and a person can keep crying over even that. There is no way to avoid it [«गुरु भी आ गया, तो हल»].
11. (early) Shlok 17: पूर्वावस्था, मध्यावस्था and उत्तरावस्था mean childhood, youth and old age. Suffering comes in some stage. Even a person who dies young meets sukh and dukh before going [«ये सब अपने जीवन का बोला है»].
12. (early) Even a tiny baby suffers (a stomach upset, mosquitoes) and cries, though it cannot say why. From birth to death sukh-dukh comes at least once. "Birth से ही आएगा, बोला है". Do not think it belongs only to old age. "Life start हो गई, मतलब you will experience this" [«छोटे से छोटा बच्चा भी»].
13. (early) The problem: dukh comes, but a person always wants its opposite, sukh every time. "यही उसकी गलती है", because he does not accept that dukh will come [«Problem क्या है? दुःख आता है»].
14. (early) In business he does not count on opponents or employee trouble. In marriage he expects only मज़ा and forgets the कर्तव्य that come with it. He does not prepare for the next आश्रम (वानप्रस्थ) [«I started a business»].
15. (early) (recap) As said before: from चित्त का भ्रम and अज्ञान comes अनिष्ट की प्राप्ति. Because a person has marked something as इष्ट, sukh and dukh arise. Otherwise he would think God started giving him dukh from childhood [«भ्रम से बोला न, चित्त के भ्रम से»].
16. (early–middle) First understand the logic of why dukh comes: "Once you understand this, तो problem ख़त्म होगा". Originally I am सत्-चित्-आनंद आत्मा, निराकार and सर्वत्र विद्यमान, and I came to earth in साकार form. "It is like a yatra", explained as a picnic [«ये दुःख क्यों आता है»].
17. (middle) We go on trips for the opposite of routine life: "We go for some opposite reason" [«मुंबई में थे, मुंबई से»].
18. (middle) Since I was सत्, here I will see असत्. Since I was चित्, I will see जड़त्व inside and around me. Since I was आनंद, I will see "दुःख ही दुःख". "I am born for that". This picnic spot lasts 70 to 125 years, whatever one's आयु, and in it "आपको आनंद लेना था" [«तो वैसे ही, मैं सत्-चित्-आनंद था»].
19. (middle) Enjoyment lies in the opposite of one's usual state. I forgot what I originally was, so now I seek a job, permanent रिश्ते and permanent money here. "ये सिर्फ temporary एक picnic थी, और उसमें opposite of what I am होना है" [«तो मैं original क्या था, वो भूल गया»].
20. (middle) On a picnic "I don't have to get fully involved". Enjoying the opposite is the nature of enjoyment: "If you want to enjoy, it is exactly the opposite" [«मैं थोड़े दिन के लिए picnic»].
21. (middle) We were सत्-चित्-आनंद ("ॐ तत् सत् वाला") and came here for a picnic. The problem is "मैं यहाँ पे स्थायी होना चाहता हूँ". Stuck in माया, we start to like असत्, though our original स्वभाव is सत्. It was a temporary "visa" to the माया world, and we forgot where home is: "हम खो गए" [«तो we were सत्, चित्, आनंद»].
22. (middle) The job was never yours. "मैं ही ज्ञानी. मैं ज्ञान लेने आया हूँ? आप पहले से ज्ञानी थे". The only knowledge needed was "मैं वहाँ रहता हूँ, यहाँ नहीं रहता हूँ" [«नौकरी कभी आपकी थी ही नहीं»].
23. (middle) So "दुःखालयमशाश्वतम्" is "३०००%" true, because there it was आनंद. If I don't learn to enjoy this opposite experience, there is conflict (झगड़ा) [«इसलिए "दुःखालयमशाश्वतम्" ३०००%»].
24. (middle) There it was अद्वैत, so I came to enjoy द्वैत. The supporting verse is एकाकी न रमते…: being alone I was bored, so I made the many, and "मेरा ही अनेक था" [«तो वहाँ क्या था? अद्वैत»].
25. (middle) सत्-चित्-आनंद-घन: घन means condensed. Vapour = आत्मा, all-pervading. Water = the state of मन-बुद्धि, fluid and still one (व्यष्टि and समष्टि). Ice = the body, जड़त्व, separate pieces in different shapes and natures. "But technically हम एक थे, ये भूल गए" [«एक भाप थी, उसको condense»].
26. (middle) Since there it was अद्वैत, आनंद and निर्गुण, I came to experience द्वैत, झगड़ा and the three गुण. Then I forgot my स्वरूप and do not know the map here [«वहाँ अद्वैत है, इसलिए मैं द्वैत»].
27. (middle) A person can settle in another city, but the journey "नर से नारायण" cannot be forgotten. Everyone has original programming "I am that सच्चिदानंद … निर्गुण … निराकार". The problem is that the picnic "real spot लगना चालू हो गई". "This is the जड़". All the remaining shloks can be settled by this one explanation [«मैं नर से नारायण हूँ»].
28. (middle–late) My real job is not this one. It is the प्रबोधन of people, "जन हिताय स्वयं मोक्षाय", and not doing it causes the problem. One must live in द्वैत, even a guru must, but without द्वैत बुद्धि. Behave as अद्वैत: "Lord in the form of", every ice cube is the same H2O, एकत्व [«मेरी असली नौकरी कौन सी थी»].
29. (late) Others are like parts of my own body placed elsewhere (finger, hair, nose, blood), all with प्राण. "ये मैं भूल चुका हूँ, यही problem है". "Concept wise तो समझा? जीवन में कब apply होगा, वो दूर की बात. यही सत्य है" [«मैं ही यहाँ बैठा हूँ»].
30. (late) Back to shlok 17: a person seeks sukh, but sukh was "there", in the आनंद state. Here dukh has to come in some stage, "नहीं आया, तो तुम्हारी picnic ही नहीं हुई". So "विपरीत सोचना छोड़ दो" [«कहाँ तक थे? तो वो सुख ही सुख»].
31. (late) Don't form "जन्मों-जन्मों का संबंध" with people met in passing on the picnic (hotel staff, the neighbour in the next room) [«दो दिन मज़ा करके वापिस»].
32. (late) Slide: one who hopes only for sukh will be unhappy in every stage. So don't seek sukh in any stage. Here dukh comes first and more. Sukh and dukh alternate, "But वो सुख itself भी दुःख है" [«तो हर अवस्था में सुख खोजना»].
33. (late) Being the साक्षी of sukh-dukh "was आनंद actually": watching one's own life like a story. "We will come into that" (deferred) [«सुख-दुःख का साक्षी होना था»].
34. (late) Slide: one who takes both as प्रसाद. This means not even enjoying them: "दुःख में शोक भी नहीं, सुख में आनंद भी नहीं". The state comes slowly. प्रसाद comes from भगवान [«Not even enjoying सुख या दुःख»].
35. (late) Slide: "वही सच्चा साधक है". "This is what अश्मा ऋषि का सार है" [«This is what अश्मा ऋषि का सार»].
36. (end) Shlok 18, अप्रिय संयोग: प्रिय means a benchmark I set ("ये मेरा अच्छा है … मेरे गुण से होता है"), and अप्रिय is its opposite. Coming into माया, the picnic, is the reverse journey "नारायण से नर". The return home is "नर से नारायण". Cut off here [«अब मुझे अप्रिय लग रहा है»].

## Other scripture cited
- **«एकाकी न रमते स द्वितीयमैच्छत्»**: Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.4.3. The reference is not stated by him; the transcript restores it. Spoken in garbled form as "एकाकी न रमन्ते स द्वितीयं ऐच्छती". Point: the One, alone and bored, created the many to enjoy द्वैत (middle).
- **«दुःखालयमशाश्वतम्»**: Gita 8.15 (restored from the spoken "दुखालयं शाश्वतं"). Used twice. Point: this world is necessarily a house of dukh because our origin is आनंद (middle, late).
- **«ॐ तत् सत्»**: a phrase only (cf. Gita 17.23; he gives no reference). Point: our original nature is सत् (middle).
- **«जन हिताय स्वयं मोक्षाय»**: as spoken. The standard form is «आत्मनो मोक्षार्थं जगद्धिताय च». Point: the real "job" is the प्रबोधन of people (middle–late).
- Phrases with no verse: सत्-चित्-आनंद-घन; नर से नारायण / नारायण से नर.

## Stories, examples and images
- (start) Comforting the grieving: with a grieving person you give examples, not theory. "अरे, इसको भी ऐसा हुआ था, देख". Ashma Rishi does this with Yudhishthira.
- (early) Avatars and Shri Ram: they too bear suffering, perhaps by taking on हलाहल विष, through a curse, a past birth, or लीला to teach the world.
- (early) Arithmetic: karma at −100; even +99 of effort in this birth still leaves you in the minus.
- (early) हल vs सुई: with a guru, a needle-prick in place of a plough (or शूल; "हल" is uncertain), and a person may still cry over the needle.
- (early) A crying baby: a stomach upset or mosquito bites; it cannot say why it cries.
- (early) Business and marriage: a person expects only gain and fun and ignores rivals, employees, duties and preparing for वानप्रस्थ.
- (middle) Picnic/yatra: from Mumbai to Lonavala, Mahabaleshwar, Delhi, Shimla or Andaman. We go for the opposite of routine.
- (middle) Running the same shop, seeing a dentist or job-hunting on a picnic, or working on a laptop: "then it is not picnic, वो business travel".
- (middle) Forgetting the picnic's purpose: worrying about a job and money there, "अरे, पत्नी किधर है? फिर वापिस एक से शादी करनी पड़ेगी क्या?"
- (middle) Spiritual retreat: a pleasure-seeker enjoys 3 days of pranayama at a retreat, while one told to do pranayama daily wants an outing (a possible nod to the Shibir format).
- (middle) Roller coaster: sweaty and against nature, yet enjoyed because it is not daily life.
- (middle) Shrikhand-puri: a treat for most, but the cook who makes it daily wants something spicy.
- (middle) Sedentary vs labouring person: activity is the first one's picnic; three days of rest is the second one's.
- (middle) Visa: life is a temporary visa to the माया world.
- (middle) Kumbh Mela: feeling lost, like those abandoned at Kumbh, where some children "गलत संस्कार वाले" go to leave their old fathers.
- (middle) Vapour → water → ice cubes: आत्मा → मन-बुद्धि → body. All H2O, but two ice cubes don't look like the same water. Ice can be shaped like a giraffe, as people come in different shapes and natures.

## Key terms
- **प्रारब्ध / काल**: forces from many births' karma, and timing. Together they explain everyday suffering.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: effort, which belongs to one birth only.
- **लीला**: an avatar's deliberate play to teach the world.
- **पूर्वावस्था / मध्यावस्था / उत्तरावस्था**: childhood, youth, old age.
- **विपरीत**: wanting the opposite of dukh, i.e. only sukh. This is the mistake.
- **इष्ट / अनिष्ट**: what one has "marked" as good, and its opposite. They come from चित्त का भ्रम and अज्ञान.
- **सत्-चित्-आनंद (सच्चिदानंद)**: our original nature. Its opposites here are असत्, जड़त्व and दुःख.
- **घन**: "condensed", as in सत्-चित्-आनंद-घन.
- **जड़त्व**: inertness, the "ice" state of the body.
- **व्यष्टि / समष्टि**: individual and collective. Named at the water stage, where everything is still one.
- **अद्वैत / द्वैत**: oneness, our origin; duality, which we came to experience. Live in द्वैत without द्वैत बुद्धि.
- **निर्गुण / तीन गुण**: there without गुण; here we experience the three गुण.
- **माया**: the temporary picnic world, on a "visa".
- **नर से नारायण / नारायण से नर**: the return home, and the reverse journey into माया.
- **प्रसाद**: taking sukh and dukh as given by भगवान, with no शोक in dukh and no आनंद (enjoyment) in sukh.
- **साक्षी**: witness. Watching one's life as a story is the real आनंद.
- **साधक**: one who takes both as प्रसाद.
- **प्रबोधन**: awakening or teaching people, the "real job".
- **प्रिय / अप्रिय**: a self-set benchmark of what is mine and good, and its opposite.
- **वानप्रस्थ**: the next आश्रम, which people fail to prepare for.
