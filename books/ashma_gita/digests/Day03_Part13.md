## Teachings in order
1. A विज्ञ पुरुष does पितरों का श्राद्ध when someone dies, and does not sit crying. He also says it is not only श्राद्ध; there are other कर्म too (start) [«विज्ञ पुरुष मतलब क्या»].
2. Ritual/worship of deities fits the loss: no money, then साधना of लक्ष्मी जी or श्री विद्या; and गुरु की पूजा. "यह करना है, न कि रोना है." This links back to losing wealth and relationships "अचानक" (recap) (start).
3. For those who have left, you pray for their good and for whatever is यथायोग्य for them. Apart from this the विज्ञ पुरुष has nothing to do (start).
4. Wish everyone well ("लोकाः समस्ताः सुखिनो भवन्तु"). If someone has died, do श्राद्ध; if wealth is gone, worship the deities; if health is gone, do महामृत्युंजय साधना (start).
5. If someone has died, free them in the proper way through a पितृऋण मुक्ति शिबिर. This does good to them and to yourself (early) [«कोई चला गया तो योग्य रीति से»].
6. For any lack, worship that thing's deity (शक्ति, भक्ति, लक्ष्मी: "उस-उस का देवता") (early).
7. धर्मानुकूल अनुष्ठान only: no चोरी, no shortcut, no वशीकरण अनुष्ठान to get someone married. यज्ञ also means having the भावना of sacrifice (early).
8. विधिपूर्वक धर्म, अर्थ, काम का सेवन is important. "Now he has started giving some solutions": up to now the text gave problems; from 43 onward come the solutions and the conclusion of the Gita, so "अभी सोना मत… अभी उठने का time है, 43rd" (early).
9. श्राद्ध and यज्ञ are not just कर्मकाण्ड. Placing a पिंड or saying "श्रीं" is not enough; you had to listen fully to the guru (early-middle).
10. These rites are a remembering of one's ऋण: पितृ ऋण, देव ऋण, ऋषि ऋण. All ऋणानुबंध must be settled, through some giving or taking (middle).
11. These are cleared "only" (stress on "ही") under the दीक्षा and guidance of a सद्गुरु. Nobody standing on the road, not even the biggest पंडाल or पंडित, can do it. As only a rich man can give wealth, only a जीवन मुक्त can free another (पितृऋण मुक्ति). Only a श्री विद्या गुरु can give a share of श्री. Only someone with control over प्रकृति can change your नवग्रह (नवग्रह शिबिर). The text means such a guru, not a guru who only gives knowledge (middle) [«तो "ही" शब्द बहुत महत्वपूर्ण»].
12. This means guidance from a guru who has had साक्षात्कार of सत् (middle).
13. Balancing धर्म, अर्थ and काम under a जीवन मुक्त guru leads to मोक्ष. मोक्ष is nothing separate: it is doing these three properly. अधर्म, अनर्थ in अर्थ (corruption), or a wrong or unfulfilled कामना each lead to a next birth (middle) [«मोक्ष मतलब अलग कुछ नहीं»].
14. मोह का क्षय = मोक्ष. When मोह is gone, प्रेम can arise; प्रेम is different. "Love is God" (middle).
15. Too much insistence on any one पुरुषार्थ causes imbalance and bondage: only धर्म, only काम or social service, or only money (family may break, धर्म neglected). It is like a tripod or three-spoked triangle that will not work unless all three are balanced (middle).
16. Example slide: श्राद्ध and पूजा as the practice of कृतज्ञता. पितृऋण मुक्ति शिबिर with a living सद्गुरु is a साधना to free the ancestors and a संस्कार that brings the family together. "We have courses for that also" (middle).
17. श्लोक ४४: the world drowns in the काल-समुद्र full of the ग्राह of जरा and मृत्यु, yet nobody understands. We see old age and death daily in the newspaper, but how many reflect on it? (middle) [«इस कालचक्र में, संसार में»].
18. Everyone sees deaths (WhatsApp forwards about celebrities) only "for sensation". Next day their own life is the same. Almost nobody thinks "this can come to me tomorrow"; the guru thinks about it every time (middle).
19. कालसमुद्र stands for our limited life, moment by moment. "any day is a good day" (middle-late).
20. ग्राह = crocodile, also linked to ग्रास/निवाला: काल can swallow you at any time. Body, relations and wealth are all caught, fleeting, in this sea (middle-late).
21. Most people do not study this नश्वरता. They do not understand अध्यात्म or that relations are temporary, and stay in अहंकार, भ्रम or "busy, busy". Until this truth is seen, आत्मज्ञान is not possible; first understand अनित्यता, काल and प्रारब्ध (one word unclear) (late).
22. Examples: the career race, where youth ignore coming old age and declining health; and social media addiction (late).
23. श्लोक ४५ is introduced as the start of फलश्रुति / "line of action". He recalls an earlier passage that even a great वैद्य gets disease (recap) and now gives the "reverse". Many Ayurveda वैद्य fall ill with their families; this shows the play of प्रारब्ध (late) [«फलश्रुतियाँ, सब अभी उसमें»].
24. श्लोक ४६: वैद्य drink bitter काढ़े and घृत and give them to others, yet cannot cross death, as the ocean does not cross its shore. When time is up you must go. Aside: "उस time पर भी allopathy का concept था" (late).
25. No उपाय, चिकित्सा or साधना can change a time set by प्रारब्ध. महामृत्युंजय, Markandeya-like practice, or the guru's science of healing can bring small changes, but "it is predestined". उपाय are for a better गति and some पाप का क्षालन (late) [«उसके लिए आपको महामृत्युंजय की»].
26. The last dose fails because the time is over: the same insulin, saline, dialysis or medicine worked before. Whoever accepts this gains in life. One should become steady in the immortality of the आत्मा (late).
27. Examples: death despite the best doctor; a heart patient who reaches the ICU in time and still dies, or dies in the ambulance; death from new strains despite vaccines and antibiotics. "time अगर covid से मरने का है, तो मरेगा". The remedy is to go to the guru (late).
28. Corona may come in a प्रलय काल. Only a person whose आज्ञा, विशुद्धि and हृदय चक्र are all damaged at once gets corona, or any respiratory problem. The nose starts at the आज्ञा point. The three causes he gives are: no unconditional love, bad speech, and never listening to the guru (end) [«आज्ञा चक्र, विशुद्धि चक्र, हृदय चक्र»].
29. The exhalation exercise done 100 times nonstop gives protection from corona for as long as you can do it, because it shows the last alveoli are working. He calls it a "secret", जगन्नाथ का प्रसाद, and a "guarantee"; "गुरु can tell" (end).

## Key terms
- **विज्ञ पुरुष:** a wise person; one who does श्राद्ध and worship instead of crying.
- **श्राद्ध:** a rite for those who have died; not just placing a पिंड.
- **यजन/यज्ञ:** worship of deities; includes the भावना of sacrifice.
- **धर्मानुकूल अनुष्ठान:** a ritual in line with dharma; no theft, no shortcut, no वशीकरण.
- **ऋण (पितृ, देव, ऋषि) / ऋणानुबंध:** debts and karmic give-and-take bonds that must be settled.
- **सद्गुरु / जीवन मुक्त गुरु:** a guru who has had साक्षात्कार of सत्; the only one who can clear these debts.
- **पुरुषार्थ (धर्म, अर्थ, काम):** they must be balanced; "अनर्थ" means misuse of अर्थ.
- **मोक्ष:** मोह का क्षय; doing the three पुरुषार्थ properly.
- **मोह vs प्रेम:** when मोह ends, प्रेम can arise; "Love is God".
- **जरा:** old age.
- **कालसमुद्र / कालचक्र:** limited life, moment by moment.
- **ग्राह:** crocodile; linked to ग्रास/निवाला.
- **नश्वरता / अनित्यता:** impermanence, which must be understood before आत्मज्ञान.
- **प्रारब्ध:** fixes the time of death; उपाय change only small things.
- **काढ़ा ("कडू"), घृत:** Ayurvedic decoctions and medicated ghee.
- **आज्ञा, विशुद्धि, हृदय चक्र:** linked to guru-obedience, speech and unconditional love; their damage causes respiratory disease.
