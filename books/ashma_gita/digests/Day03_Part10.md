## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** The session starts mid-thought, still on a previous topic: separation is fixed by fate, the person came only to teach a lesson, and ब्रह्मचर्य (the 5th house) means intimacy is allowed only if marriage is to happen. There are passing references to tattoos. The start of the recording is cut.
- **Closing:** It is cut off. The last slide says the shlok's message is that relations, body and world are अस्थायी (temporary). He then says "पहले दिमाग में यह आना चाहिए। Next." and the recording stops before the next slide.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) (recap) Nothing is permanent: meeting is fixed and so is parting (मिलना भी नियत है और बिछड़ना भी). The person came to teach a lesson, nothing more. [«इतना समझकर जाना चाहिए»]
2. (start) (recap) पुरुषार्थ in relationships means following ब्रह्मचर्य, as taught earlier under the 5th house. Intimacy is allowed only if marriage is to happen, and one should ask the guru, "इसके साथ शादी होगी क्या?" Then at least there is no sin of व्यभिचार.
3. (start) A mistake like a tattoo has no quick eraser. "Tattoo वाला, that is the worst thing to do." [«और उसका eraser भी»]
4. (start) Slide: grief at separation is natural (वियोग से दुखी होना स्वाभाविक है). Some sorrow is bound to come, and that is agreed.
5. (start) Slide: staying stuck in that grief is अविवेक. "देवदास बनना problem है." A short time of शोक is human, but drowning one's whole life in it is a waste.
6. (early) Ways to accept a parting: "वह मेरे लिए नहीं बना था", or विधाता did not want it, or she was meant to be someone else's. One can even think "अच्छा हुआ कि मेरी बला टल गई".
7. (early) A missed flight is also a lesson ("I should go on time"). Its meaning will become clear later. "उस पर टिक जाना अविवेक है." [«सादा विमान भी मेरा छूट गया»]
8. (early) Slide, fellow passenger on a train: the meeting and parting of beings is only a coincidence of time (समय का संयोग मात्र). Look at your relations this way from now on. This does not mean quarrelling every time. He attributes this to Ashma Rishi.
9. (early) Slide, family members separating: children go abroad, someone settles elsewhere after marriage, someone dies. There can be many reasons.
10. (early–middle) After कन्यादान, phoning the daughter every day undoes the दान. She now belongs to her new home: her surname, कुल and गोत्र have changed. Let her live her life there. He does not tell parents to cut contact, only not to be so attached. Through आसक्ति and मोह the mother can herself cause the daughter's problems. If you keep doing this, "फिर 'कन्यादान' शब्द मत बोलना." [«तो कन्यादान किया न»]
11. (middle) True दान is "इदं न मम": once something is given it is no longer yours. Giving while staying attached is like renting it out or leaving a deposit, not दान. If there is a daily SMS or phone call, neither the दान nor the वियोग has happened, and in the long run you will spoil her life.
12. (middle) Ashma Gita 37: काल treats the rich, who are served by beautiful women with song and music, the same way as the orphan who lives on others' food.
13. (middle) There is no अन्याय. काल moves evenly. If साढ़ेसाती comes, it runs its full course. Accept first that your situation is perfect justice according to your कर्म. [«अन्याय-वन्याय कुछ नहीं»]
14. (middle) Lying before Jagannath and crying "तूने मुझे दिया ही क्या है?" is "utter nonsense". God gave according to your पात्रता. Thank Him first. After that you may ask how to rise out of it. काल did its proper work with you and with the other person alike.
15. (middle) Slides: काल spares neither king nor pauper (न राजा को छोड़ता है, न रंक को). Beauty, honour and wealth cannot stop it. Death, disease, old age and sorrow apply equally to all ("इसका अलग नियम नहीं है"). Wealth, women, splendour and honour cannot protect anyone from काल's justice.
16. (middle) A great guru who turns corrupt ("थोड़ा इनसे पैसा निकालते हैं") will be punished in due time. His own past पुण्य and his ancestors' पुण्य may delay the fruit. Someone with no such पुण्य gets the blow quickly ("फटाक से फटका आएगा"), but the blow always comes.
17. (middle) प्रारब्ध and time work by exactly the same principle for everyone. Only the प्रारब्ध differs with each person's कर्म, so the timing differs too. Injustice never happens.
18. (middle) Slide: whoever understands the fairness of काल ("मतलब शनिदेव का सब समझ गया") learns to live with वैराग्य, कृतज्ञता and संतुलन.
19. (middle) Slide, Covid: even billionaires struggled for oxygen and everyone had to bear one law. In times of प्रलय everyone sees this equality. At other times we do not, and so we cry injustice.
20. (middle–late) Slide: an actress and an ordinary old woman both meet old age, loneliness and weakness. Fame does not guarantee a good old age, and being a lifelong hero does not make a heart attack milder. If I did what the hero did, I would get the same. He became a hero because of some other पुण्य, such as public speaking skills I never developed. He warns against comparison; the sentence is left unfinished. [«Because वह ऐसी थी»]
21. (late) Ashma Gita 38: across many births we have known thousands of parents and hundreds of wives and sons. Whose are they now, and whose are we? "यह प्रश्न खुद से पूछना।"
22. (late) (recap) Yesterday's story: Narada, a weeping king, and the soul of the king's dead son (see Stories). He says the same teaching is in the Garuda Purana (taught earlier), the Bhagavad Gita, the Bhagavata Purana and other texts. A person stays with us only as long as their time allows. We can try to call them back or say sorry, but "वह गया तो गया ही".
23. (late) पिंडदान: don't make a drama of rituals (kumkum for a सौभाग्यवती, how to pour water for an uncle) when you never offered them a glass of water while they were alive. People visit only at the ICU stage. The Antar Yog view: "जीते-जी पितर बनने ही मत देना उसको", meaning serve them while they are alive. After death the rites are a duty (कर्तव्य कर्म). For whose husband or wife one really is, read Shankaracharya's भज गोविन्दम्.
24. (late) Slide: in every birth we have been someone's son, daughter, husband, wife or parent. A past-life relation (the maid, the husband) is of no use now. Deal with what the relation is in this life. [«इस बार की कामवाली बाई»]
25. (late) Someone not mentally ready should not look at past lives. It is more श्रेयस्कर to do one's present duty and settle the present ऋणानुबंध. Only the mentally ready should go as far as past births. If a wrong feeling arises, such as towards the maid, don't act on it; go to the guru for correction.
26. (late) Studying past lives can be dangerous. (recap) If a son is the moneylender of a past life who has come to take what is owed, knowing that may kill your love, yet you still have to bring him up.
27. (late) Slide: those we are so attached to now will be unknown to us in the next birth and will have forgotten us. Krishna says in Gita 2.11 that the wise grieve neither for the dead nor for the living.
28. (late) When someone leaves, try once with साम, दाम, दंड, भेद to bring them back. If they still don't understand, then "तू तेरे रास्ते".
29. (late–end) The same applies to sadhaks who leave ("I have moved on... हमारा अभी दूसरा गुरु है"). He does not mourn "एक संख्या घट गई". Such a person may have come to teach a lesson that improves the institution, and he will correct whatever they disliked. "वह ब्रह्म आया था मुझे कुछ सिखाने के लिए". He has confidence in his knowledge and in what he gives.
30. (end) Slide: the shlok's message is that relations, the body and the world are all अस्थायी. "पहले दिमाग में यह आना चाहिए।"

## Main-text shlokas covered
- **Ashma Gita 37** (slide "श्लोक ३७"): only the Hindi meaning from the slide was read; the Sanskrit was not read or restored. Meaning: काल behaves the same towards rich men served by beauties with music and towards orphans who live on others' food. **Long** commentary, with many follow-up slides.
- **Ashma Gita 38** (slide "श्लोक ३८"): only the Hindi meaning was read; no Sanskrit. Meaning: in many births we have had thousands of parents and hundreds of wives and sons, so whose are they now, or whose are we? **Long** commentary, and the session ends inside it.

## Other scripture cited
- **Bhagavad Gita 2.11** (chapter and verse stated): given only as a paraphrase; the verse was not recited. The wise do not grieve for the dead or the living. His gloss "जीवित के लिए भी शोक नहीं करते" stays as he spoke it. Supports not mourning separations.
- **Garuda Purana, Bhagavad Gita, Bhagavata Purana, "सारे बाकी ग्रन्थ"**: named only as teaching the same thing as the Narada story. No text is quoted.
- **Shankaracharya, भज गोविन्दम्**: recommended for the question of whose husband or wife one really is. No verse is quoted.
- **"इदं न मम"**: the offering formula, used to explain true दान.

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- Look at all your relations as fellow passengers on a train from now on.
- Mothers of married daughters: keep in touch, but don't phone daily or interfere.
- In hardship, first thank God for what He gave by your पात्रता, and only then ask how to rise out of it.
- Ask yourself the question of Ashma Gita 38.
- Serve elders while they are alive rather than only through rituals after death.
- Don't look into past lives unless mentally ready. Take wrong feelings to the guru for correction.
- Read Shankaracharya's भज गोविन्दम्.
- If someone leaves, try once with साम, दाम, दंड, भेद, then let them go.
- Q&A: none. The only other voice is his imagined reply from the sadhak, "नहीं, पर मुझे आपकी चिंता है".

## Key terms
- **वियोग**: separation or parting; natural for a while, but not something to dwell on.
- **अविवेक**: lack of discernment, here staying stuck in grief.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: right effort; here, asking the guru whether marriage will happen and keeping ब्रह्मचर्य.
- **ब्रह्मचर्य / 5th house**: taught earlier; intimacy only where marriage is due.
- **व्यभिचार**: illicit relations, the sin avoided by पुरुषार्थ.
- **कन्यादान**: giving away the daughter; real only if one lets go.
- **दान / इदं न मम**: a gift that is no longer "mine"; the opposite is rent or a deposit.
- **आसक्ति, मोह**: attachment; the mother's attachment can harm the daughter.
- **काल**: time as an impartial judge; its चेष्टा (working) is the same for all.
- **साढ़ेसाती / शनिदेव**: the astrological period of Saturn; understanding काल is "शनिदेव का सब समझ गया".
- **पात्रता**: worthiness or capacity; God gives according to it.
- **प्रारब्ध**: destiny from past कर्म; works on the same principle for all but differs in content and timing.
- **पुण्य**: merit, one's own and one's ancestors'; it can delay the fruit of new sins.
- **प्रलय**: catastrophe, such as Covid, when equality becomes visible.
- **पितर / पिंडदान**: departed ancestors and the rite for them.
- **कर्तव्य कर्म**: duty, such as rites after death.
- **ऋणानुबंध**: karmic debt-bond between people in this life.
- **पंडित**: the learned or wise, in his paraphrase of Gita 2.11.
- **साम, दाम, दंड, भेद**: the four means, used here to try to bring someone back.
- **अंतर योग**: named as "we believe in अंतर योग", for the principle of serving people while they live.
