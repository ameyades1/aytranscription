## Teachings in order
1. (start) Right and wrong will be decided by शास्त्र and गुरु, so one must look into why something happened to one. In the past people went to a guru in childhood (ब्रह्मचर्य आश्रम) and learned अर्थ, काम and धर्म. If one did not, one should learn at the first फटका, at any age (18, 20, 25, 40). [«ये सही-गलत का decision»]
2. (start) Going to a guru after 70 is of little use. He cites शिव पुराण: after 70 the layers of कर्म and अहंकार have grown too much. A 70-year-old student with a 25–40-year-old guru will feel "what will he teach me?". Even with a guru, the layers take long to clear, because the guru will show that everything since childhood was wrong, and the ego of "how did I do so much wrong?" takes two or three more years to settle. [«88 को समझकर फिर»] (The number "88" is uncertain.)
3. (start) Physical उपाय (e.g. यात्रा) become impossible in old age. By the time प्रायश्चित is done the person is 82–85, and can no longer do राष्ट्रहित or गुरु कार्य. So come to the guru at the first दंड or मार: a mistake at 25 means coming at 25.5 or 26. First, विचार द्वारा, decide whether to continue on the path. [«उसके बाद physically उपाय»]
4. (early) Slide meaning: according to Ashma Rishi, the cure for the poison of दुःख is not some तात्कालिक भोग but बुद्धि रूपी औषधि, which is formed by learning from others' lives.
5. (early) उत्तम पुरुष learns from others' mistakes, मध्यम पुरुष from his own, and कनिष्ठ पुरुष never learns and keeps repeating the same mistake. [«उत्तम पुरुष दूसरों की गलती»]
6. (early) ज्योतिष and अध्यात्म are "the best form" of the first case. Reading the Mahabharata, Ashma Gita and Raja Janaka is learning from others' mistakes ("what Arjuna did, I need not do").
7. (early) ज्योतिष: thousands of साढ़े साती cases have already been studied. If the same combination is in my chart, "why take a risk?" Do the उपाय an expert guru or ज्योतिषी prescribes: "better to be safe than sorry". [«दूसरा है ज्योतिष»]
8. (early) Study people your own age (20–30). Mistakes made young bear fruit (फलश्रुति) later: anger that starts at 25 builds into a heart attack in the 50s–70s, and द्वेष develops into cancer later. By then (stage 2) it is too late to turn to शिव साधना or the science of healing. Stop it beforehand by watching others, or act at the first फटका. [«अपनी age के सारे»]
9. (early) Slide points: understand the causes of कष्ट, and then by विचार stop yourself from कुमार्ग. "At least learn from your own mistake" (मध्यम).
10. (early–middle) Only the उत्तम is wanted; the मध्यम and कनिष्ठ "चाहिए ही नहीं". He says this is his own statement, repeated in every Shibir. The purpose of studying all शास्त्र is to learn from others' mistakes. [«बार-बार शिविर में ये»]
11. (middle) The three गुण are the same in every युग. So what Duryodhana or Hiranyakashipu did, and the inner conduct of Shri Rama, Krishna, Shiva, Jesus Christ and Buddha, can all be studied: what good action, bad action and अहंकार each led to. [«तीन गुण वही हैं»]
12. (middle) Shlok 12 introduced: there are only two causes of repeated मानसिक दुःख. He limits this to mental suffering, a chain of दुःख after दुःख, as opposed to physical pain (needle pricks, falls). [«अगर तुमको continuous दुःख»]
13. (middle) First cause, चित्त का भ्रम: we take "uncle said this, wife said that" personally, instead of feeling "Lord in the form of chacha came to teach me a lesson", or Durga Devi with a बेलन, or a drunk husband as Shivaji in नशा. Lacking that feeling is भ्रम. [«चाचा ने ऐसा बोला»]
14. (middle) Second cause, अनिष्ट की प्राप्ति: too many अपेक्षाएँ. What you expected did not come; some other प्रारब्ध fruit came. He links this to the Gita's फल line (only through फल does दुःख come).
15. (middle) Solution (recap, "कल ही बताया था"): check your कर्म. Good action gives good fruit and bad gives bad. If something "bad" comes after good action, it cannot really be bad: it will be good in the long run, or carries a small lesson, or a small mistake to correct at a much larger level.
16. (middle) His own temple crowds: he does not ask why he gets no VIP treatment. He thinks God sent him there because some कायदा must change. [«तो अभी ये सब»] (see Stories)
17. (middle) Summary of the two causes: (a) अनिष्ट, "it never happens as I want", and (b) चित्त का भ्रम, "I went expecting one thing and something else happens", or misreading a scolding that was "Lord in the form".
18. (middle) Slide: no third cause is possible. Ashma Rishi said it plainly, and Vyasa confirmed it by repeating the story. [«ऐसा अश्मा ऋषि ने»]
19. (middle) In reality अनिष्ट does not come. You set an इष्ट or benchmark, and that creates the problem. You receive only what you are पात्र for. [«पर अनिष्ट की प्राप्ति»]
20. (middle) Drop अहंकार and first accept what you are पात्र for. If he were PM or CM he would get VIP entry; otherwise pay and wait. A time will come, if it is in प्रारब्ध, when people will listen. Being sent into the crowd was so he could understand people's suffering outside. [«अहंकार छोड़ के पहले»]
21. (middle) Because he is "उत्तम पुरुष, उत्तम शिष्य", the fruit cannot be अनिष्ट. This must be thought through by विचार, and he says he does it every day. [«तो I take it»] (self-praise; see Sensitive)
22. (middle) The फलश्रुति keeps turning out इष्ट. Living in a guru's सान्निध्य, you too receive that fruit. This ज्ञानसत्र is the fruit of that आशीर्वाद ("also learned yesterday", recap). [«फलश्रुति इष्ट ही होती»]
23. (middle) Restated: ज्ञान says अनिष्ट never really comes; a held इष्ट makes it अनिष्ट. Salary example (see Stories). A goal is not bad, but you must accept your कार्य or प्रारब्ध level and see how to grow from there. Not accepting it leads to कुमार्ग to get the extra amount. [«चालीस हज़ार पगार चाहिए»]
24. (late) Slide meaning: अनिष्ट means something bad or harmful happening to someone. Much दुःख comes not from events but from disorder in our thoughts (विचारों की गड़बड़ी).
25. (late) Slide: even when an event is truly painful, our प्रतिक्रिया decides how deep the दुःख goes. Example of Shri Rama's वनवास. [«हो गई घटना। श्रीराम»]
26. (late) Sita's abduction: Rama had no tension while Lakshmana did. Rama told the story of Raja Nriga and concluded that Sita's, his own and Lakshmana's कर्म are all at work. "I am not worried". Rama knew पुरुषार्थ, yet believed in प्रारब्ध, which must sometimes be borne quietly. [«सीता का हरण भी»]
27. (late) Your reaction decides the दुःख. दुःख does come (Rama too cried "सीते, सीते"), but come out of it as fast as you can.
28. (late) Slide: this shlok is an invaluable key to आत्मचिंतन. "You have to think."
29. (late) Slide example of चित्तविभ्रम: a student believes "I am a failure" while he keeps getting chances to try. His दुःख comes from भ्रम, not अनिष्ट. Failure is the stepping stone to success, so do not weep over failure (Edison; Vivekananda). [«Edison ये सोचकर बैठता»]
30. (late) He distinguishes kinds of falling. On a सात्त्विक, गुरु-certified path, falling a thousand times is fine. On कुमार्ग, fall once only. The same पाप must not repeat, though mistakes while attempting or experimenting are fine. A different पाप is "प्रगति की निशानी", since it means the earlier one was conquered; unknowing ones are still OK. [«हो गया तो ठीक है»]
31. (end) Slide example of अनिष्ट की प्राप्ति: with a balanced चित्त, even real दुःख (losing a job, a loved one's death) does not affect the mind for long. He begins the Trump example and is cut off. [«Donald Trump कौन है?»]

## Key terms
- **फटका / दंड / मार**: the first blow of consequence, a signal to go to a guru.
- **ब्रह्मचर्य आश्रम**: childhood stage of learning from a guru (अर्थशास्त्र, काम, धर्म).
- **प्रायश्चित**: atonement through prescribed उपाय.
- **उपाय**: a remedy prescribed by a guru or ज्योतिषी (e.g. यात्रा).
- **उत्तम / मध्यम / कनिष्ठ पुरुष**: one who learns from others' mistakes, from his own, or never.
- **बुद्धि रूपी औषधि**: the medicine of understanding, against the poison of दुःख.
- **कुमार्ग**: a wrong path. Fall on it only once.
- **फलश्रुति**: the resulting fruit of past actions.
- **चित्तविभ्रम / चित्त का भ्रम**: mental confusion. Misreading events, or expecting one thing while another happens.
- **अनिष्ट / अनिष्ट की प्राप्ति (अनिष्टोपनिपात)**: something unwanted or harmful happening. He says it is really created by one's own इष्ट or benchmark.
- **इष्ट**: the desired outcome or benchmark one sets.
- **अपेक्षा**: expectation, the root of अनिष्ट.
- **पात्र / पात्रता**: worthiness. One gets only what one is पात्र for.
- **प्रारब्ध**: destined fruit of past कर्म, sometimes borne quietly.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: effort. Rama had it, yet accepted प्रारब्ध.
- **प्रतिक्रिया**: reaction, which decides how deep the दुःख goes.
- **आत्मचिंतन**: self-reflection.
- **सात्त्विक / गुरु certified मार्ग**: a path on which repeated falls are acceptable.
- **तीन गुण**: the same in every age, so past lives are worth studying.
- **वलय**: (auric) field created by chanting crowds. Implied, not explained.
- **सान्निध्य**: the guru's presence, through which the fruit comes to disciples.
- **ज्ञानसत्र**: this knowledge session, fruit of आशीर्वाद.
- **पितृदोष / ऋण**: the father's debt, Rama's reason for going to the forest.
- **साढ़े साती**: Saturn's 7.5-year period in astrology.
