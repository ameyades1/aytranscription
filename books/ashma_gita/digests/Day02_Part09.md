## Teachings in order
Almost every point below is his reply to a participant's answer, which is mostly inaudible. Each is therefore partly a recap of the day.

1. (recap) Endurance (सहनशील) comes first. Only after a problem has been endured can one reflect on it. If it was never endured, the result is कुमार्ग. `(start)` [«सहनशील, मतलब ok»]
2. (recap) संयम (a participant's answer; he accepts it). `(start)`
3. (recap) Even an अवतार has to undergo प्रारब्ध for some time. He pushes for the lesson behind this "observation": being साक्षी, or how one reacts for a while, makes the difference. `(early)` [«अवतार को भी कुछ देर प्रारब्ध»]
4. (recap) Being विनम्र is itself the aim. Otherwise, when सुख and दुख come, there will be a problem. `(early)`
5. (recap) Keep आत्मिक संतुलन. `(early)`
6. (recap) सुख and दुख come because we keep some इष्ट or "benchmark" in the mind. `(early)` [«कुछ तो इष्ट मन में रखा है»]
7. (recap) He calls this "the most correct answer" and the crux of today: learn very early in life, immediately after the first mistake. "Earlier the better." There is no benefit in learning at eighty (the "80" reading is uncertain). `(early-middle)` [«बहुत जल्दी सीखना है जीवन में»]
8. The उत्तम पुरुष learns from others' mistakes. That is why one studies शास्त्र, and also ज्योतिष: to understand others' mistakes and learn from them. `(middle)` [«उत्तम पुरुष क्या? लेकिन दूसरों की गलतियों से»]
9. If there is a guru's "certified" path, follow it immediately and your पत्रिका (horoscope) will change. `(middle)` [«गुरु का certified मार्ग हो»]
10. The more सम भाव you keep, the better. `(middle)`
11. "खाली हाथ आए थे, खाली हाथ जाएँगे." He jokes that in between we speak sweet words, and afterwards comes "टिड़िंग टिड़िंग" (with a gesture). `(middle)`

14. Make some effort to cross जरा and मृत्यु, to become अमर. `(middle)`

17. (recap) The old mother / दादी माँ story: "the same will happen to me." Contemplation on how to leave the world must begin from today. `(late)` [«वो बूढ़ी माँ का समझा»]
18. Situations and death are the same for everyone, so they have to be faced. `(late)`
19. To die successful means success in आत्मज्ञान too, not just business, and being an example or inspiration to people. Otherwise, join such a work: even the मावळे become great. `(late)` [«सफल होकर मरना»]

## Key terms
- **सहनशील**: enduring the problem first, which is a precondition for reflection.
- **कुमार्ग**: the wrong path, which results when one has not endured.
- **प्रारब्ध**: destiny that even an अवतार has to undergo.
- **साक्षी**: a witness stance, which changes how one reacts.
- **इष्ट / benchmark**: an expectation held in the mind that causes सुख and दुख.
- **उत्तम पुरुष**: the best person, who learns from others' mistakes.
- **पत्रिका**: horoscope, which changes when one follows the guru's path.
- **सम भाव**: equanimity.
- **अनित्य**: impermanent.
- **हर्ष / विषाद**: joy and sorrow, which come in sequence, not truly mixed.
- **मावळे**: Shivaji's soldiers (Marathi).
- **जरा, मृत्यु, अमर**: old age, death, immortality.
