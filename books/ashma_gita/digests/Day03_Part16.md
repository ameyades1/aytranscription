## Opening and closing
**Opening:** The session goes straight on from the previous one's verse-by-verse reading of Ashma Gita. It starts with shlok 57 («Next, सत्तावनवाँ श्लोक») on how a king should take tax by rules and rule without राग-द्वेष.
**Closing:** It ends complete. He reads the last slide of the final summary, says «धन्यवाद», reads the पुष्पिका (closing colophon), and declares that the Shibir is fully over («अपना शिबिर भी पूर्ण रूप से समाप्त होता है»).

## Teachings in order
1. (start) Shlok 57: a king who takes wealth from the people as tax नियमपूर्वक (by rules) and follows राजधर्म free of राग-द्वेष wins सुयश throughout the चराचर लोक. [«Next, सत्तावनवाँ श्लोक»]
2. (start) Frame: Vyasa is telling this to Yudhishthira, and Ashma Rishi told it to King Janaka. So kings are being told their duty: take tax or money by rule, give service in return, and do राजधर्म without राग-द्वेष. [«अब ये बताया व्यास जी ने किसको»]
3. (start) Which kind of "king" you are is in your own hands. Anyone who heads a group is its राजा: a society secretary, a school principal, a temple trustee or पुजारी. Each must collect fees fairly and on time, not inflated, and give the services due. [«तो कौन-सा राजा हो»]
4. (early) The head of a family is also a राजा. He must give service, run the home well, pay bills on time, plan everyone's पगार (income) properly and invest. Such a person's सुयश spreads and they "become an inspiration": a true father, secretary or boss, up to a nation's or the world's king, "Direct विश्वगुरु". [«घर के, अपनी स्वयं की family के राजा»]
5. (early) Summary of shlok 57: take from those you serve by rule, do all your proper work, and get धर्म, अर्थ, काम done properly. [«तो नियमपूर्वक लोगों से»]
6. (early) Shlok 58: Janaka is called निर्मल बुद्धि वाले, a mind without मल (impurity). विदेह means he had attained विदेह मुक्ति and did not take the body as "मैं". Even so, grief came to him because someone had died. [«हाँ, यहाँ पे भी आ गया, निर्मल बुद्धि»]
7. (early) Hearing Ashma's युक्तिपूर्ण teaching, Janaka became शोकरहित and went home. The story of Ashma Rishi is complete. [«अश्मा का, मतलब अश्मा ऋषि का»]
8. (early) Shlok 59: the frame returns to Vyasa speaking to Yudhishthira. "Yudhishthira" is you yourself. Your problem, your युद्ध, has to be made a धर्मयुद्ध, understood in the presence of a guru (गुरु के सान्निध्य में). [«अब ये किसने किसको बोला»]
9. (early) "Leave grief and rise": just as Janaka let go of grief and Yudhishthira rose, you too should rise above शोक. [«जैसे जनक ने छोड़ा»]
10. (early) "Hold हर्ष in the heart": be glad that at least a door to heaven has opened, that you have been given something to fight for. [«कि मुझे कम से कम स्वर्ग का द्वार»]
11. (middle) This birth is your कुरुक्षेत्र, given in order to do something (कुरु = करना; he says this was explained on the first day, recap), and it will also be your धर्मक्षेत्र. Whatever work you choose will bring a battle and opposition. [«तुमको भी ये जन्म मिला है»]
12. (middle) Whatever you try will meet opposition, because प्रकृति and people of the three गुण will cause trouble. It links back to the earlier सफ़ेद बाल, काला बाल example (recap). [«कुछ न कुछ प्रकृति उल्टा-सीधा»]
13. (middle) क्षत्रिय धर्म means "keep a fighting spirit". Someone who weeps and lays down their weapons is not called a क्षत्रिय: "Useless". [«तो क्षत्रिय धर्म का पालन करो»]
14. (middle) "You have won this earth": you have already won this far, since you reached the guru, who has given you everything (धनुष-बाण, अस्त्र, शस्त्र, साधना, ज्ञान). So "enjoy it": live this life well and don't waste it weeping. "Do not neglect it." [«अभी तक यहाँ तक विजय पाई है»]
15. (middle) Final summary of all 59 shloks, point 1: no one, whether wise, rich or powerful, is untouched by life's sorrows. So how could I be spared? [«अंतिम सारांश पहले देख लेना»]
16. (middle) Point 2: only धैर्य, संयम and a mind joined to धर्म can lift you out of शोक, भ्रम and भय. However great the guru, nothing happens if you are impatient. If the guru says it will take a month of जप (four मण्डल) and you want it tomorrow, it won't work. There is no "Capsule". [«गुरु भी कितना भी हो»]
17. (middle) Point 3: wealth and knowledge can make life easier but cannot give inner peace. "धन पलंग दे सकता है, नींद नहीं दे सकता"; sleep comes only from मनःशांति. Wealth and relationships give comfort and moments of joy, not मुक्ति. [«धन पलंग दे सकता है»]
18. (middle) Point 4: decide out of उत्तरदायित्व and विवेक, not लोभ or भय (the "negative or positive" pulls). Take ownership with कर्तव्य भाव ("it is my duty, मैंने किया है"). Use विवेक by following शास्त्र and asking the guru. [«लोभ या भय से कोई decision»]
19. (middle) Point 5: as Vyasa lifted Yudhishthira out of grief, only a जीवित गुरु (living guru) can bring रूपांतरण within you. He asks whether that transformation has started: "Or only the Guru got enlightened and the book got enlightened?" [«तो क्या आपके अंदर वो रूपांतरण»]
20. (late) Point 6: whatever your प्रारब्ध or situation, first learn to accept it. With a steady mind, the path of धर्म and a living guru, life becomes purposeful, brave and glorious. A steady mind makes प्रारब्ध acceptable. Staying on धर्म keeps correcting you. The living guru can change things by शक्तिपात or संकल्प. You gain direction, courage to fight as a true क्षत्रिय, and in the end यश. [«जो है, पहले स्वीकारना सीखो»]
21. (late) "Are you ready now?": ready to fight your own धर्मयुद्ध like a क्षत्रिय, to fight "इस प्रलय के साथ", and to accept what is yours. [«बस, क्षत्रिय की तरह»]
22. (late) Are you ready to wake from sadness, आत्मग्लानि (guilt), doubt and silent surrender to circumstances? If you are first ready to wake from the body, this waking will happen. He asks for a real yes or no. [«आत्मग्लानि, guilt वगैरह»]
23. (late) Even after shlok 59, Yudhishthira was not fully free of grief and guilt. If you are, you are "युधिष्ठिर से बहुत better". Yudhishthira did not reject Vyasa but only said "ऐसा होता है क्या?" [«आप हो गए? तो you are»]
24. (late) Just hearing knowledge is not enough; Yudhishthira only heard, and nothing happened. It takes time and full समर्पण to reach the अंतःकरण. If your work gets done at once today, समर्पण has already come. [«युधिष्ठिर ने भी सिर्फ़ सुना था»]
25. (late) Vyasa had to keep talking with him for several chapters. Then Krishna had to tell, over three chapters, a story about Narada. (The transcript notes it is unclear whether Krishna or Vyasa told it.) The knowledge kept growing but the grief did not go. In the end प्रायश्चित्त was needed, which he explains as "प्रयासपूर्वक चित्त को शुद्ध करना" and which takes great effort. [«उसके बाद actually कृष्ण को»]
26. (late) The guru has to explain again and again from different angles so that the disciple's बुद्धि and भाव come into harmony (समरसता). Vyasa taught piece by piece with great patience. Nothing comes from a guru who teaches through three-minute viral videos. "It takes time." [«तो इतने angle से, व्यास की तरह»]
27. (end) This shows why a living सद्गुरु is essential to remove the disciple's grief and ignorance and set them on the right path. [«शिष्य का शोक और अज्ञान»]
28. (end) He reads the पुष्पिका, then says that with Ashma Gita taught into your lives, the Shibir is complete. [«इति श्रीमहाभारते शान्तिपर्वणि»]

## Key terms
- **नियमपूर्वक**: by rules.
- **कर**: tax.
- **प्रजा**: the people, i.e. those whom one serves.
- **राग-द्वेष**: attachment and aversion; rule must be free of them.
- **सुयश / यशस्वी**: good fame; glorious.
- **राजधर्म**: a ruler's duty, applied to any head (secretary, principal, family head).
- **निर्मल बुद्धि**: an intellect with no मल (impurity).
- **विदेह / विदेह मुक्ति**: not taking the body as "मैं".
- **कुरुक्षेत्र**: कुरु = करना; life as a field for action, which is also a धर्मक्षेत्र.
- **धर्मयुद्ध**: your life's struggle, fought according to धर्म under a guru.
- **क्षत्रिय धर्म**: "keep a fighting spirit"; not weeping or laying down arms.
- **उत्तरदायित्व**: ownership, कर्तव्य भाव, "it is my duty".
- **विवेक**: discernment guided by शास्त्र and the guru.
- **जीवित गुरु / सद्गुरु**: a living guru, necessary for रूपांतरण.
- **रूपांतरण**: inner transformation.
- **प्रारब्ध**: one's allotted destiny; to be accepted first.
- **शक्तिपात / संकल्प**: the means by which the living guru can change things.
- **आत्मग्लानि**: guilt.
- **समर्पण**: full surrender, needed for knowledge to reach the अंतःकरण.
- **प्रायश्चित्त**: "प्रयासपूर्वक चित्त को शुद्ध करना", purifying the mind with effort.
- **समरसता**: harmony of बुद्धि and भाव.
- **मण्डल**: a unit of जप practice (four मण्डल mentioned; not defined).
- **मनःशांति**: peace of mind, the true source of sleep and peace.
