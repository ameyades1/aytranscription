## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** Begins mid-thought, carrying on from the previous session's point about how to die. Die while doing something great, or in समाधि, not "कुत्ते, बिल्ली की तरह"; try to become अमर and keep working in youth (start) [«या और कुछ करते हुए मरना»].
- **Closing:** Cut off mid-sentence while he reads the अर्थ slide for shlok 16 on प्रारब्ध, at "हमारा कार्य केवल" (end).

## Teachings in order
1. (recap) Die either doing something or in समाधि; do not die "कुत्ते, बिल्ली की तरह". Try to become अमर, leave having done something big, and let your life be सफल. If old age comes, that is fine, but keep acting through youth (start) [«या और कुछ करते हुए मरना»].
2. Slide: only someone who has seen, understood and accepted जरा (old age) and मृत्यु is fit for life, "वही जीवन का अधिकारी है" (start).
3. The शास्त्र shows Dattatreya with a dog, a cow and a tree. Animals, plants and humans form one cycle. People who say "society में कुत्ता नहीं चाहिए" have understood neither अध्यात्म nor कर्म; "you are not in tune with the entire world" (early) [«इसीलिए अपना शास्त्र बताता है»].
4. Plants and pets belong in the home so that a child sees a whole life-cycle (seed, growth, old age, death) by about age twelve. A child sees few human deaths in the family, and may be away or too young when they happen (early) [«तो ये क्यों करने को»].
5. In the past homes had cows, bullocks and guard dogs so that people saw old age and death early. Seeing them, one should resolve: death will come to me too, but "वैसा बुढ़ापा नहीं and वैसा मरण मुझे नहीं चाहिए, कुछ और चाहिए". Nobody remembers a dog, an ant or a mosquito (early) [«इसलिए पहले घर में गाय»].
6. Shlok 15: no human can cross old age and death. Even with साधना, a boon to live one कल्प, च्यवनप्राश, रसायन, chemicals or operations, death still comes; "you cannot run" (early) [«ये तो fact है»].
7. However great someone is (Alexander, सिकंदर महान), death is certain. "Prepare yourself for that which is truth", but this does not mean brooding over "मैं कब मरूँगा" (early-middle).
8. Every birthday should remind you that you are moving towards death. That reminder was what the celebration was originally for (middle) [«हर birthday को याद»].
9. The birthday cake and candles: cake stands for अमृत in solid form, and the candle is a symbol of the दीया, a reminder to become अमर. He says this is in "their" religion too, but even they do not know it. Here अमृत is a liquid; there you eat cake (middle) [«वो cake देते हैं»].
10. The lesson: the body growing older means nothing. A birthday is worth celebrating only for what you have learnt and whether you know where you are going. Dogs and cats also grow in age (middle).
11. "पण काय वाढलं?": besides age, what grew? बुद्धि, experience, reflection, a count of mistakes? "वृद्ध होना मतलब वृद्धिंगत होना"; be ज्ञानवृद्ध, not only white-haired (middle) [«I ask, मज़े में पूछता»].
12. An old person's experience is at least knowledge of "how not to lead life". It must be passed on, and it must be knowledge validated by a guru. A person divorced three times who teaches "दुनिया में प्रेम होता ही नहीं" is "not in tune with the universe" (middle) [«वो बोलते हैं न, "अरे»].
13. Have the guru certify why the failure happened three times. Three failures mean the failure was yours, not that all bosses or all love are bad. "कम से कम ज्ञानवृद्ध होना चाहिए. फिर श्री विद्या सीखो" (middle) [«वो गुरु से certify करवाओ»].
14. Preparing for death does not mean planning suicide. It means preparing for अमरत्व in two ways: "खुद महान बनो, या महान वाले का साथ दो". Being a fan or a भक्त only is not enough; join the great one's कृति, at least in part (middle) [«तो ये क्या बोला है»].
15. Slide अर्थ: the shlok directly challenges human अहंकार. Science, war, politics and योगबल all have limits, and death takes everyone without discrimination. It is "वैराग्य को जाग्रत करने वाले बाण के समान सत्यवाणी" (middle).
16. Alexander left empty-handed. You came empty-handed and will go empty-handed, and film songs teach the same. In between, live with love; this is "exactly अध्यात्म" (middle) [«तो उसने खुद last में»].
17. Once you understand this, you may enjoy life fully, but only after understanding (middle) [«And ये सब समझने»].
18. Against misreading वैराग्य ("खाली हाथ... तो बीच में क्या?"): in between you may fill your झोली. We were born without clothes or spectacles, yet we wear them. "Everything you can possess, पर जाने से पहले खुद खाली होकर जाना चाहिए" (middle) [«लोग फिर वैराग्य के बाद»].
19. Link to पितृ ऋण मुक्ति and the गरुड़ पुराण (the वैतरणी river): prepare to leave everything behind. Hand it to a son or a successor, or give it as दान. Leave no responsibility behind, and since death gives no guarantee, prepare from today (middle) [«यही तो पितृ ऋण मुक्ति»].
20. Make a will "the moment you possess something", not at fifty to seventy. Applied to himself: the gurukul needs a will and a successor, and he thinks about who will look after the many children. "इसकी चिंता नहीं होती, पर चिंतन इसके ऊपर होता है" (middle-late) [«लोग बोलते हैं, will»].
21. Like a mother, the गुरु माँ has चिंतन about those kept "ज्ञान-गर्भ में", without tension. If something happens anyway, it was their प्रारब्ध. A mother should think about her children's future from their birth (late) [«तब तक इतने लोगों को»].
22. Slide: kings and billionaires also age, fall ill and die. Even a corporate head dies in hospital, but at least they did something. Learn from this positively, not from the negative: "अच्छी तरह से सीखकर जाओ, करके जाओ" (late).
23. Shlok 16: whatever सुख or दुःख comes must be borne helplessly. Rejecting it was in your hands earlier, not now (late) [«आपके नसीब में सुख»].
24. सुख can be dedicated to others but stays yours, like an award. दुःख or punishment cannot be shared at all. A wife can nurse her husband but cannot take his disease. A mother can arrange her child's study but cannot sit the exam; if he fails, it is his प्रारब्ध (late) [«तो वैसे सज़ा में»].
25. Personal: as a guru he became a VIP against his own wish, began meeting politicians and became somewhat diplomatic. He learnt that "भक्ति और शक्ति की युति के बग़ैर काम ही नहीं होता". Some things are अनिवार्य even at the highest level (late) [«अब गुरु बन गया»].
26. Responsibilities come with सुख, and सुख and दुःख each carry the other within them. What comes by प्रारब्ध must be undergone; the other person only shares दुःख in words (late).
27. A layman cannot avert it. A guru can reduce it, though not avert it: where a हल was to strike, at least a सुई will. Total change needs the guru's हस्तक्षेप, or such भक्ति that the guru takes your whole हलाहल, which is "very, very rare" (late) [«उसे टालने का कोई उपाय»].
28. This Gita is not about rewritten destiny or special cases. It teaches how to live when destiny cannot be rewritten, which is "९९% of the life, अवतार को भी" (late-end) [«And this Gita is not»].
29. Slide अर्थ (cut off): the shlok explains प्रारब्ध; some events are beyond our control; "हमारा कार्य केवल…" (end).

## Key terms
- **जरा**: old age.
- **कल्प**: a cosmic age; a boon to live that long still ends in death.
- **रसायन**: rejuvenating preparations or chemicals.
- **अमृत**: nectar of immortality; liquid in India, "cake" in the West.
- **वृद्ध / वृद्धिंगत / ज्ञानवृद्ध**: being old should mean having grown; elder in knowledge, not just in white hair.
- **वैराग्य**: detachment, awakened by the truth of death; it does not mean possessing nothing in between.
- **पितृ ऋण मुक्ति**: freedom from ancestral debt; connected to leaving responsibilities settled.
- **वैतरणी नदी**: the river of the गरुड़ पुराण that one should not fall into.
- **चिंता vs चिंतन**: worry vs reflective thought about the future.
- **ज्ञान-गर्भ**: the womb of knowledge, where the guru holds disciples.
- **प्रारब्ध**: destiny already in motion that must be borne.
- **विवश**: helpless, forced ("forcibly").
- **हस्तक्षेप**: the guru's intervention to change destiny.
- **हलाहल**: poison; the disciple's whole karmic load taken by the guru.
- **भक्ति और शक्ति की युति**: the union of devotion and power (politics) needed to get work done.
- **कृति**: action or work; join the great one's work rather than being a भक्त or fan.
- **मावळे**: Shivaji's soldiers, as workers for स्वराज्य.
