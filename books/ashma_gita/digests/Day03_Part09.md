## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** The recording starts mid-sentence (`[«और उसी में कोई आदमी»]`). He is continuing an earlier point that the cycles of nature, such as rain, are in the hands of काल, प्रारब्ध and प्रकृति, and that a person is only a माध्यम within them. This is the tail of the explanation of the shlok before 35 (probably 34).
- **Closing:** The recording is cut off mid-thought during the commentary on shlok 36. He is on the college girlfriend example, at the point that she "was temporary" and "came to teach me some lesson" (`[«वो उतना ही कुछ lesson»]`).

## Teachings in order
1. (start) Rain is in the hands of काल, प्रारब्ध and प्रकृति, and I am only a माध्यम in it: I may go to save someone, or be swept away myself. The विद्या is to understand the game of प्रकृति and काल, and that "I" created both. I don't see this now because the major part is अदृष्ट: I have forgotten it and it has become an invisible force. (Possibly a recap of the previous shlok's theme.) [«और उसी में कोई»]
2. (start) Only the गुरु can see the invisible part. He has the "चश्मा, microscope, telescope". The परमेश्वर has it too, and the जीवित सद्गुरु is called the साक्षात स्वरूप of the परमेश्वर. [«और वो invisible part»]
3. (start) The Guru tells the reason only when you are पात्र. If he sees अहंकार, he holds back ("अदृष्ट मुझे दिख रहा है, but I will not tell that answer"), because the person would shout at him and refuse दक्षिणा. Until there is acceptance, telling has no meaning. Many people reject पत्रिका (horoscopes) because of अहंकार. [«उसका कारण भी वो»]
4. (early) Cold, heat and the rain cycle all run by काल. So the trouble I am due to have also comes at its time. If I am to be swept away in rain, the rain's time must also come. [«तो ये सब समझ»]
5. (early) Lesson of the water-death story: काल and प्रारब्ध will not let go. If you move away from water, प्रकृति will bring the water to you, even by accident. [«तो उसको काल और»]
6. (early) The cycle is in काल's hands and we are its माध्यम. So our प्रारब्ध is also under काल, and human सुख-दुख come from काल. [«तो ये चक्र ही»]
7. (early) So look ahead: when your साढ़ेसाती or गुरु की दशा comes. "You have to plan your life accordingly." Read the पत्रिका sixteen years ahead to know the next दशा. [«तो इसलिए पहले ही»]
8. (early) Shlok 35 introduced. He says the coming shloks are "fast-fast", about a minute each. [«Next, ३५वाँ श्लोक»]
9. (early-middle) (recap) He had said earlier: don't get into that state at all. Keep a युवा मन and don't grow old in that way. Do some work that makes you अमर, and then death doesn't feel like death and old age doesn't feel like old age. [«पहले बोला था, ऐसी»]
10. (middle) Once you have accepted it, and the physical state has truly come, no number of mantras, medicines, homas or japa will work. [«पर अगर तुमने मान»]
11. (middle) The Guru will still tell you to do these things, not to save the body but for a good अगली गति. "Every action will be for that step." [«लेकिन फिर भी गुरु»]
12. (middle) Even for a child due to die in a year, the पत्रिका will prescribe दान and पुण्य (as the गरुड़ पुराण says). At least श्मशान वैराग्य will come and something will start. "These are hard core facts": even the best hospital fails when the time has come. [«अब शरीर बचना नहीं»]
13. (middle) Upayas work only if done early. In the diabetes case, the same पत्रिका was there at age 25 and at age 1. Remedies taken after the problem has arrived are of little use. [«पर अब ७२ के»]
14. (middle) Crossing-the-road image: "it carries zero meaning" to say after the leg breaks that you should have walked carefully. The sooner you understand, the better. [«अच्छी तरह से रास्ता»]
15. (middle) Once that time comes nothing will work, but the Guru still prescribes यात्रा and so on. If the person can't do them, the son must. That is why people used to have eight or ten children, so that at least one would do it ("such an understanding existed once"). [«But गुरु फिर भी»]
16. (middle) Shlok 36 introduced. He checks the audience is following the speed: the shloks are "self explanatory" and he is not just skipping forward. [«Next, ३६। हाँ»]
17. (middle) Shlok 36 is "important, about relations". In a flowing river, two logs come from different places, stay together for a while, then part. This happens by the river's flow and the विधि of काल. [«ये important है, about»]
18. (middle) In the same way people come and go in life. Today's meeting of "these pieces of wood, you and me" was तय and will not be permanent. Family relations are "for a particular time": a son goes to America by his own प्रारब्ध, and spouses may live in two places for work ("Divorce नहीं हुआ है"). It is काल की महिमा. [«तो इसी तरीके से»]
19. (middle) What to do: think "उतना ही मेरा साथ था". He is not saying you must break off when effort fails, but holding on too tightly (अति जोड़ के रखना) also causes harm. [«तो what should I»]
20. (middle-late) Train analogy (see Stories). The people in your compartment are family and closest relatives. If you are fighting with them from the start, the whole life goes badly. Solve that first, by small concessions rather than a full fight. Then you stop counting "when will Agra come". [«तो वही अपने जीवन»]
21. (late) Some companions last one year, some ten, twenty-five or a hundred, friend or शत्रु, according to काल and प्रारब्ध. [«किसी का एक साल»]
22. (late) Practice: settle the quarrel as far as possible, seeing "All is God". Forgive where the Guru says forgive. If an arrow must be shot, shoot it. The relation may stay as it is, but वैराग्य reduces the problem. [«मुझे practice क्या करनी»]
23. (late) The slide meaning: the shlok teaches वैराग्य, because all worldly relations are क्षणिक and formed by प्रारब्ध. Parents, siblings, spouse and children are logs in the ocean of time. Meeting and parting are both नियत. [«पहले ये सीख लेना»]
24. (late) Parting is not only through death. If someone doesn't want to stay or doesn't get on with you, that is their portion. "Enjoy the presence… enjoy the absence." Don't abuse someone for leaving, since it is their choice. You may try, but without आसक्ति, or your life will be ruined. [«मरने की वजह से»]
25. (late-end) College boys' breakup: she left, so perhaps she would have caused trouble, or "She was not meant for you", or one of you was not पात्र. Ask the Guru which it was. Becoming देवदास is not the teaching. [«वो आती नहीं है»]
26. (end) Don't ruin your life or get a tattoo for her. The body is not शाश्वत. The parents gave it, so they have the right over it. The Guru changes संस्कार from the womb with knowledge, so he and the परमेश्वर have a right too. She is not even married to you (not अर्धांग). A tattoo can't be erased and causes problems with the next girlfriend. She was temporary and came to teach a lesson. (Cut off here.) [«उसके लिए तुम्हारा जीवन»]

  - Meaning: medicine, mantra, homa and japa cannot save a person in the grip of old age and death.

  - Text note: "पुनर्जपाः" still needs checking against Gita Press.

## Stories, examples and images
- (start) A man swept away in extra rain. You might go to save him or be swept away yourself.
- (start) The Guru's "चश्मा, microscope, telescope" for seeing the अदृष्ट.
- (start) A disciple with अहंकार who would shout at the Guru, hit him and refuse दक्षिणा if told the reason.
- (early) **Water-death story** [«तो एक व्यक्ति की»]:
  - Source: told to him by a famous astrologer, now living, who knew one of his uncles. It was that astrologer's client, and the reading came from the astrologer's own guru.
  - The reading: death by drowning (a water-sign in the death house plus "Pluto जैसा कोई planet").
  - The man sold his riverside house and moved to hilly, urban areas. He was careful even while bathing, for two years.
  - Ending: while driving to the office, a water tanker beside him overturned, his car filled with water and he died.
  - He calls it a "सत्य घटना" and repeatedly says he trusted the narrator. His guesses about the window being open are his own speculation.
- (middle) A one-year-old son due to die. The पत्रिका still prescribes दान and so on, and श्मशान वैराग्य may begin. Also: "the best hospital, nothing worked".
- (middle) **Diabetes case** [«एक person थे, diabetes»]:
  - "That I know"; he says "ये अपने case की बात कर रहा हूँ".
  - Age 72. The नाड़ी reading (inferred to be Nadi astrology) said maximum five more years, to 77, and that no medicine would work.
  - The past-life chapter, "the 13th", said he was a pharmacist in a past life who gave wrong medicines. So in this life his medicines won't work, and he would die at 77 despite an 84–85 lifespan.
  - The remedy would have been to donate medicines to many people. He died at exactly 77, which he says he saw himself. He took 10–12 pills a day at the end.
- (middle) Crossing the road carefully, or else a broken leg.
- (middle) In the old days people had eight or ten children so that one would do the rites.
- (middle) Two logs (ओंडका) meeting in a river. Also "आज यहाँ इतने सब काठ के टुकड़े मिलने थे, आप और मैं".
- (middle) A son going to America. Spouses posted to two different centres in high posts.
- (middle-late) **Railway train analogy** [«It is something like»]:
  - Setup: the train has an engine, a guard and coaches. You sit in a six-person section on a 1.5–2 day trip to Delhi.
  - If you quarrel at the start ("why did you put your feet here, my luggage…"), the journey is spoiled and you want them to get off.
  - With good friendship you don't hold back a co-passenger getting off at Agra, and you don't go on to Delhi with them.
  - Curious glances at neighbours while going to brush teeth ("this one is eating a laddu, that one is fat").
  - The extra रजोगुणी person wanders to other coaches but finds peace only back at his seat.
  - Mapping: the coach is the home, the six are the closest relatives, neighbours are associates, wandering is travel and tourism.
  - Remedy: "ठीक है बाबा, मैं package इधर रखता हूँ". "टाटा, बाय-बाय, चलो उतर जाओ".
- (late-end) College girlfriend left: देवदास, "her expenses might not have been affordable for you later" (परवडने), and getting her tattoo on your body.

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- Read your पत्रिका early, up to sixteen years ahead, and plan your life around साढ़ेसाती and the दशा.
- Do the upayas early. For example, the remedy of donating medicines to many people to clear the ऋण.
- When death is certain, still do the दान, पुण्य and यात्रा the Guru prescribes, for the अगली गति. If the person can't, the son should.
- Settle quarrels with those closest to you, seeing "All is God". Forgive where the Guru says. Act without आसक्ति. "Enjoy the presence, enjoy the absence."
- Ask the Guru why a relationship ended.
- There is no Q&A. He only asks the audience rhetorical checks ("समझ में आया कि नहीं?", "Agreed?").

## Key terms
- **काल:** time, which runs the cycles of nature, human सुख-दुख and the meeting and parting of relations.
- **प्रारब्ध:** fate already set in motion. It is under काल and decides relations and events.
- **प्रकृति:** nature. It "brings the water to you".
- **अदृष्ट:** the unseen part of karma that I have forgotten. It is now an "invisible force", visible only to the Guru.
- **माध्यम:** an instrument or medium. "We are its माध्यम."
- **जीवित सद्गुरु:** the living true Guru, the साक्षात स्वरूप of the परमेश्वर.
- **पात्र:** worthy or ready. The Guru gives the reason only to the पात्र. In a relationship, one of the two may not be पात्र.
- **सखा, भक्त:** the disciple's states that are needed before the Guru reveals the reason.
- **पत्रिका:** horoscope.
- **साढ़ेसाती, दशा (गुरु की दशा):** astrological periods to plan around.
- **नाड़ी:** inferred to mean Nadi astrology. The "13th chapter" is the past-life chapter.
- **ऋण:** karmic debt, cleared by a matching दान.
- **अगली गति:** the next state after death. Remedies at the end serve this, not the body.
- **श्मशान वैराग्य:** the detachment that comes at the cremation ground.
- **वैराग्य:** detachment. It reduces the problem even if the relationship stays the same.
- **आसक्ति / attachment:** must not be there, or "your life will be finished".
- **क्षमा:** forgiveness, given where the Guru says.
- **रजोगुणी:** a restless person who wanders the train's coaches.
- **अर्धांग:** spouse, "half-body". A girlfriend is not yet this.
- **शाश्वत / नश्वर:** permanent and perishable. The body is not शाश्वत.
- **काठ / लकड़ा / ओंडका (Marathi):** a log.
- **युवा मन:** a youthful mind. The way to avoid the state shlok 35 describes.

## Sensitive or editorial items
- (start) The Guru as the only one who sees the अदृष्ट, and as the साक्षात form of God. There is also a reference to refusing दक्षिणा. This touches on the institution and on self-image.
- (early) The water-death story is presented as a true astrological prediction that came true. It is second-hand, through a famous living astrologer (unnamed) and an uncle. He hedges ("I am not… मैं घटना बता रहा हूँ").
- (early) Strong fatalism and an astrological determinism claim ("काल और प्रारब्ध छोड़ेगा नहीं").
- (middle) Health claim: medicines failed because of past-life karma (a pharmacist who gave wrong drugs). Remedies cannot save the body once the time has come, and "the best hospital, nothing worked". The person can be identified ("that I know", "अपने case"). The health details are a third party's.
- (middle) The remark about people once having eight or ten children so one would do the rites.
- (middle) The death of a one-year-old child as an example.
- (middle) Spouses living apart, and "Divorce नहीं हुआ है".
- (end) The girlfriend/tattoo passage, including the remark about her expenses. The tone is jocular and possibly gendered. There is also the claim that parents, Guru and God have rights over the body.
- The verse sandhi in 35 and 36 still needs checking against Gita Press.
