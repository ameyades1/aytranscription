## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** Picks up partway through the slides on Ashma Gita shlok 38, with its last summary lines ("आत्मा ही शाश्वत है", "जो बीत गया उस पर पकड़ बनाना व्यर्थ है"). He then moves on to shlok 39.
- **Closing:** The recording is cut off mid-thought during shlok 41. He has just asked whether शनि goes all the way round or only halfway, and said it orbits the sun in thirty years (`[«शनि पूरा का पूरा घूमता है»]`). He is probably about to link Saturn's cycle to the पत्रिका and lessons in attachment.

## Teachings in order
1. **(start, recap of shlok 38)** Slide: only the आत्मा is eternal. Holding on to what has passed is pointless. There is no need to become a "देवदास"; doing so only makes you sad and achieves nothing. `[«इतना clear समझा अभी? देवदास»]`
2. **(start, recap)** Slide: keep विवेक in life and understand the truth of the आत्मा, "in a positive way".
3. **(early)** Shlok 39 is about the जीव itself. First understand that this जीव has no relatives and is no one's relative. Relationships are roles in a नाटक: hero, heroine, mother, in-laws, villain. `[«अपने बारे में बात कर रहे»]`
4. **(early)** When the picture ends, the roles end too ("role gets over"). Family members are roles arriving from "some bigger नाटक". Understand the script and accept it. Learn your duties from your पत्रिका and the शास्त्र (rules for siblings, spouses, गृहस्थ, वानप्रस्थ), and play the character "जब तक है, तब तक".
5. **(early)** When a character goes off into the wings, we do not worry about them or spoil today's scene for them. If their re-entry is due, there are dialogues ready for it.
6. **(early)** The opposite extreme, "extreme वैराग्य", is also wrong. Knowing it is a drama does not excuse you from acting. You must still play the role while knowing it is a drama.
7. **(early)** He criticises people who read "unnecessary" things after marriage, put on a show of वैराग्य, go off "to the Himalayas" and neglect their duties while claiming a higher level. With साक्षी भाव you must still do the role you have been given: "You cannot escape that role."
8. **(early-middle)** The essence (सार): do the role perfectly, with सजगता, without attachment. Do not do it merely because "it is a drama". `[«तो role समझा, इसलिए»]`
9. **(middle)** Slide of shlok 39: like travellers on a road, family, wife, sons and friends meet only for a while. Strangers stay for moments, friends for longer, parents seem permanent, but nothing is guaranteed. "उसका मेरी film में role इतना सा ही था." `[«Strangers मिलते हैं कुछ»]`
10. **(middle)** Roles can change partway: a friend can turn out to be a villain, a brother can take you to court "like दुर्योधन". You must change your own role to match. "जिस रूप में भजोगे, उस रूप में भजूँगा."
11. **(middle)** Slides: across births the जीव has only brief companionship. Family, friends and spouse are "प्रारब्धजन्य पथिक" who walk a short way with you.
12. **(middle)** "सात जन्मों का रिश्ता is nonsense according to अध्यात्म." He explains "seven births" as the journey through the seven chakras, which may take forty births. A partner may or may not return. The phrase is used only to encourage people. He says this "with authority". `[«सात जन्मों का रिश्ता is»]`
13. **(middle)** Roles change in every birth. Last life's money lender may be this life's husband. A relationship may also come as punishment for excessive वासना ("अभी भोग तू"). This applies to every relationship, not only to husbands.
14. **(middle)** Slide: the delusion of "मैं तेरा हूँ / तू मेरी है" gradually blocks the path to मुक्ति. Neither "I cannot live with you" (द्वेष) nor "I cannot live without you" (मोह) leads to मोक्ष. द्वेष forces another birth as an enemy. The recording has the two phrases swapped; see the note in Sensitive or editorial items.
15. **(middle)** Avoid both extremes. Understand why someone is with you. Fight an enemy properly, by rule, as a धर्मयुद्ध. With a friend, follow the rules of friendship without मोह.
16. **(middle)** Slide: when the time is complete, the companionship ends, whether you like it or not. So do not hold प्रिय-अप्रिय and इष्ट-अनिष्ट strongly in the mind; setting such a "benchmark" is what causes the problem.
17. **(middle)** Gita 2.11 confirms this. Krishna was speaking of the Kauravas and others. You first grieve for people who are not worth grieving for, such as a son who never listens or a troublesome spouse, and you ruin your own life that way.
18. **(middle)** This was Krishna's first advice to Arjuna. When someone dies, they have taken their path to मुक्ति, and grieving holds them back. When a relationship breaks while both are alive, it was only meant to last that long.
19. **(middle)** Water in the hand: too strict or too free with children, spouse or relatives, and they will look for happiness elsewhere. Balance love with "प्रेम से डाँटना". This art has to be learned from a guru.
20. **(middle-late)** दंडनीति needs measure: the punishment should cause a little fear without breaking the person. At home a child needs दंड, but must still feel the mother's love. Do not be only महाकाली; be लक्ष्मी (giving) and सरस्वती (teaching) as well. "उसमें नहीं अटकना है."
21. **(late)** Shlok 40: a person of विवेक should reflect: where am I, where will I go, who am I, why am I here, and for whom should I grieve? Hardly anyone reflects like this. `[«वो चिंतन कौन करता»]`
22. **(late)** Even at a practical level: of roughly 400 crore women and 400 crore men, why did I marry this one? Why did "this sample" come to me, good or bad?
23. **(late)** He attributes to Shankaracharya the teaching that one should reflect on कोऽहम् / सोऽहम् before both quarrelling and loving. Ask who the कान्ता (wife) and the son are, and where they came from, since yesterday they were not here.
24. **(late)** A mother does not know before birth what her child will be like, yet मोह arises at once. The child might become a दुर्योधन or a thief and bring shame on her. Motherhood is natural, but a ज्ञानी mother should check the child's पत्रिका in childhood. If she comes to "सरस्वती दीक्षा" for correction, she herself becomes आत्मज्ञानी; "otherwise it is just a waste. कुत्ते बिल्ली को भी बच्चा होता है."
25. **(late)** On rape: why this particular person, out of crores? There must be some ऋणानुबंध. This does not mean the offender goes unpunished. Forgive in the mind for मोक्ष, and let the king give judicial punishment at the level of शरीर बोध. Without क्षमापना the matter returns next birth: being raped again, or the urge to commit it. If there is neither punishment nor forgiveness, it continues. Act "ज्ञानी जैसा", not out of द्वेष or मोह alone. `[«अरे, worst case उल्टा»]`
26. **(late)** Bhaj Govindam 23: who are you, who am I? See the world as without essence, like a dream, and give it up. He clarifies that you give up the attachment to ideas, not the world; the role must still be done.
27. **(late)** Would Shankaracharya lie? All this is like a dream; "it is your imagination. Imagination को change करना सीखो." संस्कार become so hard that changing a thought feels difficult. "साधना makes it loose", and the thought can change little by little.
28. **(end)** Shlok 41: the संसार turns like a wheel. Climates, the days of the week, and सुख-दुःख all come round in cycles. `[«ये संसार चक्र के»]`
29. **(end)** Fashion goes in cycles too, and so does traditional versus modern. Living with loved ones is अनित्य because it is part of a cycle.
30. **(end)** The cycle's aim is मोक्ष, "नर से नारायण". So your पत्रिका will be made to teach you the lesson of attachment. He then turns to शनि's 30-year orbit, and the recording cuts off.

## Main-text shlokas covered
- **Shlok 38 (end of it):** only the summary slides (items 1–2). Brief commentary.
- **Shlok 39:** Hindi meaning only; no Sanskrit spoken. "इस जीव का न तो कोई सम्बन्धी होगा और न यह किसी का सम्बन्धी है। जैसे मार्ग में चलने वालों को दूसरे राहगीरों का साथ मिल जाता है, उसी प्रकार यहाँ भाई-बन्धु, स्त्री-पुत्र और सुहृदों का समागम होता है।" Meaning: relatives are fellow travellers on a road. Long commentary (the drama analogy, the seven-births claim, the summary slides). The opening words were read twice, before and after the commentary.
- **Shlok 40:** Hindi meaning only. "अतः विवेकी पुरुष को… 'मैं कहाँ हूँ, कहाँ जाऊँगा, कौन हूँ, यहाँ किसलिए आया हूँ और किसलिए किसका शोक करूँ?'" Meaning: self-inquiry before grief. Long commentary (marriage choice, the mother, rape and ऋणानुबंध, Bhaj Govindam).
- **Shlok 41:** only the first line was read: "ये संसार चक्र के समान घूमता रहता है।" Meaning: the world turns like a wheel, so companionship does not last. Commentary had begun and was cut off.

## Other scripture cited
- **Bhagavad Gita 2.11** (Hindi translation on the slide; Sanskrit not spoken): "तू न शोक करने योग्य मनुष्यों के लिए शोक करता है… पण्डितजन शोक नहीं करते". Supports item 17–18: do not grieve for those who have died or for relationships that have ended.
- **"जिस रूप में भजोगे, उस रूप में भजूँगा"** (middle), in his own words. This probably echoes Gita 4.11 (ये यथा मां प्रपद्यन्ते), but he did not cite it. Supports item 10: change your role to match how others treat you.
- **Bhaj Govindam 23** (Shankaracharya): "कस्त्वं कोऽहं कुत आयातः का मे जननी को मे तातः। इति परिभावय सर्वमसारं विश्वं त्यक्त्वा स्वप्नविचारम्॥" The Sanskrit was on the slide; it is unclear whether he read it aloud. Supports self-inquiry and seeing the world as like a dream.
- **कोऽहम् / सोऽहम्** (late), attributed to Shankaracharya, not quoted as a verse. Supports reflecting before loving or quarrelling.

## Stories, examples and images
- **(start)** "देवदास ज्यादा बनने की जरूरत नहीं": brooding over the past only makes you sad.
- **(early)** The film actors: after the picture ends, the hero says to the heroine, "we got married in there, let's go on honeymoon". Absurd, because the role is over.
- **(early)** Wings of the stage: we do not worry about a character who has gone off stage; their re-entry has its own dialogues.
- **(early)** The "enlightened" hero: at the interval he realises it is only a drama. When the villain carries off the heroine and the director shouts at him to go after them, he says she will come back anyway, just wait three hours. Point: knowing it is a drama does not excuse you from acting.
- **(early)** People who go "to the Himalayas" after marriage and claim a higher level while neglecting their duties.
- **(early-middle)** About himself and his mission: "विश्वगुरु होना ही है भारत, इतना आशीर्वाद भी मिल गया", so why hold satsang late at night? He cannot just say it and go to sleep. "By that my role becomes more important… fast fast करना पड़ेगा."
- **(middle)** Strangers, friends and parents as travellers on a road. Some lose their mother at birth; a friend may come for two minutes, trap you and leave.
- **(middle)** The friend at the start of a film who later turns out to be the villain. A brother who later takes you to court, like दुर्योधन.
- **(middle)** Last life's money lender comes back as this life's husband. A relationship can come as punishment for वासना. A partner might return as a dog or as a maid (कामवाली बाई).
- **(middle)** Grieving over a son who never listens, or a troublesome spouse.
- **(middle)** Water in the hand, demonstrated: an open palm loses the water, a closed fist loses it too, and only cupped hands (अंजलि) hold it. Too strict or too free with children or spouse, and they look elsewhere.
- **(middle-late)** The king's fine: a ₹1 lakh fine for crossing a signal, and soon there is a "जिंदा या मुर्दा" reward on the king himself. Punishment should frighten a little but not break.
- **(middle-late)** महाकाली, लक्ष्मी and सरस्वती as the three faces of a mother: discipline, giving, teaching.
- **(late)** World population of about 800 crore: why this one spouse?
- **(late)** A mother does not know her child's face or nature before birth; the child might turn out a दुर्योधन or a thief. "कुत्ते बिल्ली को भी बच्चा होता है."
- **(late)** Rape: why this person out of crores? ऋणानुबंध; punishment by the king and forgiveness in the mind.
- **(late)** He shows the original book of the text.
- **(end)** Cycles: the days of the week, climates, fashion (long hair and shaved heads, sleeveless and full sleeves, bell bottoms and tight jeans, trousers going out and lungis coming in), traditional and modern.
- **(end)** शनि takes 30 years to go round the sun (cut off here).

## Key terms
- **विवेक**: discernment; keep it and understand the truth of the आत्मा.
- **साक्षी भाव**: the witness attitude with which you still perform your role.
- **वैराग्य (extreme)**: false renunciation used to escape duty; wrong.
- **सजगता**: alertness; the role is to be done with it, without attachment.
- **प्रारब्धजन्य पथिक**: travellers born of प्रारब्ध; family and friends who walk a short way with you.
- **सात जन्म**: in his reading, the journey through the seven chakras, not seven births with the same spouse.
- **मोह / द्वेष**: the two extremes ("can't live without you" and "can't live with you"); neither gives मोक्ष.
- **धर्मयुद्ध**: fighting an enemy by rule.
- **प्रिय-अप्रिय, इष्ट-अनिष्ट**: likes and dislikes; holding them as "benchmarks" causes problems.
- **दंडनीति**: punishment in measure, enough to cause fear without breaking.
- **कोऽहम् / सोऽहम्**: "who am I" and "I am That"; reflect on these before loving or quarrelling.
- **कान्ता**: wife.
- **ऋणानुबंध**: karmic debt-bond that explains why a particular person comes into your life, even an offender.
- **क्षमापना**: forgiveness in the mind; without it the matter returns next birth.
- **शरीर बोध**: the bodily or worldly level, where the king's justice applies.
- **स्वप्नवत**: like a dream; the world as imagination.
- **संस्कार**: hardened impressions that साधना loosens.
- **संसार चक्र**: the world turning in cycles.
- **अनित्य**: impermanent; said of living with loved ones.
- **नर से नारायण**: the aim of the cycle, which is मोक्ष.
- **पत्रिका**: horoscope; it sets one's duties and is "made" to teach the lesson of attachment.
- **सरस्वती दीक्षा**: the Foundation's initiation or programme for correcting a child's पत्रिका (as he refers to it).

## Sensitive or editorial items
- **(middle)** He says "सात जन्मों का रिश्ता is nonsense" and states his reading "with authority". This contradicts popular belief and may need care.
- **(middle)** A spouse as punishment for past वासना, or a partner reborn as a dog or maid. Possibly hurtful to readers.
- **(middle)** "I can't live without you मतलब द्वेष" is very likely swapped in the recording ("I cannot live with you" is meant). Check and correct.
- **(middle)** A ₹1 lakh fine for crossing a signal: a light jab at governments; harmless but topical.
- **(late)** Rape explained through ऋणानुबंध, with the claim that without forgiveness the victim may be raped again next birth. Highly sensitive; he does say the offender must still be punished. Needs careful editorial handling or omission.
- **(late)** "कुत्ते बिल्ली को भी बच्चा होता है" about mothers without spiritual knowledge; harsh.
- **(late)** A child possibly born "handicapped", mentioned in passing.
- **(late)** He promotes "सरस्वती दीक्षा" (an institutional programme) and pairs it with पत्रिका correction, an astrological claim.
- **(early)** Criticism of people who adopt वैराग्य after marriage and neglect their duties.
- **(early-middle)** "विश्वगुरु होना ही है भारत, इतना आशीर्वाद भी मिल गया": a nationalist note, and possibly an allusion to a blessing he received; also self-reference about his own role.
- **(end)** "पत्रिका तभी बनेगी ही": the original said "बनेगी ही नहीं", which reads backwards; check against the audio.
