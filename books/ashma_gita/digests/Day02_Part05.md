## Opening and closing
**Opening:** The recording starts mid-sentence (`[«उनका interview लिया गया था»]`). He is in the middle of a story about an interview with Donald Trump, which continues the previous Part's theme of not grieving over real losses but recovering from them.
**Closing:** The recording is cut off mid-thought (`[«कोई उससे अच्छा, युद्ध में मरकर, मरे ना»]`). He was on the point that everyone's end is the same, so one should at least join a great person's work, like the मावळे or the वानर सेना. He seems to be starting a thought about dying in battle as a better death.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) Even after going bankrupt, Trump said that if he sat in दुःख-शोक he could never get it back: "I have to return back. I have to conquer that." He recovered his business and became president. Grief does not get back what was lost. `[«उनका interview लिया गया था»]`
2. (start) He says he uses examples from wherever he sees them, America included, even though some people think of their own country as विश्व गुरु.
3. (start) Shivaji Maharaj gave up 23 forts in the Treaty of Purandar without reacting emotionally, because that was the best way at the time. Later he won back more, "शायद अड़तीस कुछ". Weeping would not have brought anything. `[«छत्रपति शिवाजी महाराज ने तेईस किले»]`
4. (start) When Sita was abducted, Ram knew it came from नारद का शाप, but he still did his राजधर्म and brought her back to Ayodhya. Accepting a loss does not mean doing nothing.
5. (early) Slide: in real sorrows too, if the चित्त is balanced, the sorrow does not affect the mind for long. Grieving for a few moments is fine. After that one should ask at once "मैं कहाँ गलत गया था". Out of ego we keep saying "वही गलत है" instead, and that is where the problem lies. `[«कुछ समय तक ठीक है»]`
6. (early) Social media: feeling inferior from looking at other people's happy pictures is चित्तविभ्रम. "उसका जीवन दोषपूर्ण नहीं, अपितु उसकी सोच का चश्मा धुंधला है."
7. (early) His father's advice about films and TV serials: why spend money to watch other people's wealth and love affairs? Learn from them if there is something to learn, and don't spoil your mind with unnecessary things.
8. (early) Each person gets what is in their प्रारब्ध. Should every poor person be unhappy on seeing a rich one? Lifestyles differ and so do sorrows. If you want to rise, learn what the other person is learning. `[«मेरे प्रारब्ध में उतना है»]`
9. (early) Shlok 13: sorrows come from the two causes and also from attachment to विषय. (recap) The two causes are चित्त का भ्रम and अनिष्ट की प्राप्ति.
10. (early) (recap) अनिष्ट can never really come. It only seems so because I had imagined some इष्ट. It is सापेक्ष, comparative, and प्रकृति gives exactly what is due. Dropping ego, I accept that if I received it, I am पात्र for it. `[«अनिष्ट की प्राप्ति कभी हो भी नहीं सकती»]`
11. (early-middle) To a wife who complains about her husband: "तू उसी की पात्र है". Solve it. If she becomes a proper पतिव्रता, he may become better than her friends' husbands. "There is something in you also, जो check करना होगा."
12. (middle) The exception is if the guru says "छोड़ दो". Then one should leave. But if the guru says "क्षमा कर", then once the guru comes in, "अलग scene चालू हो जाएगा".
13. (middle) Attachment to विषय means ममत्व. (recap of yesterday) "शर्मा जी का घर जला... मेरा घर जला". Even "मेरा पुत्र, मेरा पुत्र" counts as attachment. Do your कर्म with them, but do not be आसक्त.
14. (middle) Shlok 14: जरा (old age) and death are two wolves that eat everyone. The rich grow old too, get the same diseases and get the same treatment from the daughter-in-law at the end. "ब्राह्मण की death और शूद्र की death अलग... ऐसा नहीं है. Death is death." `[«अमीर भी बूढ़ा ही हो जाता है»]`
15. (middle) In an आत्मज्ञानी's home, a death is different. There is no रोना-धोना. They ask whether the person will become a पितर, do श्राद्ध properly and help the person towards नर से नारायण. `[«आत्मज्ञानी के यहाँ एकदम अलग»]`
16. (middle) "कहीं कुछ गया नहीं. थोड़े time के बाद तुझे भी वहीं जाना है." This understanding should come during the मृत्यु की साधना in Pitru Rin Mukti.
17. (middle) Nature did not give Shivaji or Vivekananda an easier death for being great. Vivekananda had "इकतीस रोग" and died at 39 without even reaching old age. Death is everyone's final destiny, and old age is its पूर्वदूत (forerunner).
18. (middle) The shlok is about natural death, not accidents, which can come at any time.
19. (middle) Mothers make children act out an old granny's walk and laugh at it. Old age is not something to laugh at or cry over. It is something to accept. Children learn this mockery and then tease their grandmothers. `[«एक बूढ़ी औरत होती है»]`
20. (middle) Chanakya Niti 17.18: the old woman says she is looking for her lost youth, "यौवन रूपी मोती". "तू भी यहाँ पहुँचेगा". We laugh only when we feel it will not happen to us.
21. (middle) Slide: every generation mocks the one before it. Branches forget that their life comes from the roots. He adds that this is why we do पितृ ऋण मुक्ति and पितृ तर्पण, to say thank you rather than laugh at them. Today's children lack these संस्कार.
22. (middle) He says he is giving references from different texts, and the same point is found everywhere. Chanakya said this 2000 years ago.
23. (middle) Whoever ignores this truth is caught in मोह and अहंकार. Thinking he will never grow old or die, he commits as much पाप as he likes. The shlok awakens वैराग्य and आत्मचिंतन.
24. (middle-late) Gita 7.29, freedom from जरा and मरण: Krishna's own body is gone, yet he is अमर as the Gita. Shivaji is अमर through शौर्य and स्वराज, and Jnaneshwar through the Jnaneshwari. Do work that lasts. `[«तो मरण से छूटना»]`
25. (late) His own work: the साधना, शिबिर and सत्संग, and the recordings. "इस रूप में जीवित रहना चाहूँगा". People are being transformed now, so why not 10,000 years from now?
26. (late) Freedom from जरा does not mean physically, since saints also aged. It means never growing old in the mind. Never say "मेरा एक पैर कब्र में है" or "आधी गोवरी श्मशान में पहुँच गई". Someone who says this has understood neither अध्यात्म nor कर्म.
27. (late) A युवा मन is not looking for a girlfriend at 85. It means working for a higher cause until death. If the body cannot run around, write, advise the next generation, do समाधि and साधना, "and ऐसे शरीर छोड़ता हूँ". Such a person has understood कर्तव्य कर्म. `[«युवा मन मतलब किसी higher cause»]`
28. (late) Krishna, Chanakya and Ashma Rishi all say the same, so "it must be true". A child may act out an old person for a real role in a serial, but not to make people laugh.
29. (late) Example slide on the ego of the young and powerful. Film heroes and heroines become egoistic and retire at 30 to 40. Many in that industry die of heart attack or cancer. There is cat-fighting and द्वेष, too much makeup, and much trouble behind the scenes, including affairs. Quick success and parties lead to wrong paths. Their end is often bad. "Some of them may be great", and the chances are just higher there. `[«ये film heroine अधिकतर»]`
30. (late) The weak and ordinary are not spared either. We must accept this. But dogs, cats and rats die the same way, so what did I do differently? Even if I could not become a guru or a king, I can help in their work.
31. (late) Gita 4.7 to 4.9: the अवतार's work. Know it तत्त्वतः. Whoever joins the work of such a guru or king gets the same फल and मोक्ष. Ask yourself whether you did that much in this life. `[«ये करने को श्रीकृष्ण बोलते हैं»]`
32. (end) The मावळे became अमर along with Shivaji: faceless and nameless, "but collectively they got that". The वानर सेना is still praised today, and they too will get a good गति. At least that much should be done, because everyone's end is the same. (cut off) `[«उसमें आज मावळे भी अमर»]`

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- Instruction: after a loss, at once ask "मैं कहाँ गलत गया था" instead of blaming someone else.
- Do not watch unnecessary content that spoils the mind.
- Do not teach children to mock the old.
- Never speak of being near death. Keep a young mind and work for a higher cause.
- Homework-like question: "उतना तो किया क्या इस जन्म में? देख लेना". Check whether you have joined a great person's work.
- Mentions the मृत्यु की साधना in Pitru Rin Mukti (not done in this session).
- Q&A: none. There are only rhetorical checks ("समझ में आता है कि नहीं?").

## Key terms
- **चित्तविभ्रम / चित्त का भ्रम:** a confused mind. "सोच का चश्मा धुंधला".
- **अनिष्ट की प्राप्ति:** getting the unwanted. Comparative (सापेक्ष), and it only seems so because one imagined an इष्ट.
- **पात्र:** deserving or fit. What you got is what you are पात्र for.
- **प्रारब्ध:** one's allotted share.
- **विषयों की आसक्ति / ममत्व:** attachment, the sense of "mine".
- **जरा:** old age, बुढ़ापा.
- **पूर्वदूत:** forerunner. Old age is death's पूर्वदूत.
- **पितर, श्राद्ध, पितृ तर्पण, पितृ ऋण मुक्ति:** rites for ancestors. Saying thank you to the roots.
- **नर से नारायण:** the soul's path, which one helps the departed along.
- **मरण से छूटना:** becoming अमर through lasting work.
- **जरा से छूटना:** never growing old in the mind.
- **युवा मन:** working for a higher cause until death.
- **कर्तव्य कर्म:** one's duty.
- **राजधर्म:** a king's duty.
- **तत्त्वतः:** in essence (Gita 4.9).
- **गति:** the destiny after death.
- **मावळे:** Shivaji's soldiers.
- **गोवरी:** a cow-dung cake. "आधी गोवरी" is a saying, possibly Marathi (uncertain).
- **वैराग्य, आत्मचिंतन:** dispassion and self-reflection.
