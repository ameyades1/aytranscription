## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** The recording starts mid-sentence (`[«बराबर? या टोना-टोटका»]`). He is finishing a point from the previous part: he does not favour टोना-टोटका (charms and rituals), but you need "light" on what is coming, because in the dark you are guaranteed to fall. He says sudden events can also be averted ("we will see in future, आगे next श्लोक में"), then moves to shlok 23. Some words after "उसका उपाय" seem to be missing.
- **Closing:** It is cut off mid-thought. He is listing four things needed to change one's भाग्य. He has given योजना (1), प्रारब्ध (2) and आत्मविश्वास (3) ("दो सौ टक्का सही है"), and the recording ends before the fourth.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) Recap and continuation: he does not favour टोना-टोटका, but some उपाय exists, and the "light" is necessary. You should at least see whether something sudden is coming in your life, because sudden events can also be averted. This is promised for a later shlok. [«टोना-टोटका हम prefer नहीं»]
2. (start) Shlok 23: birth in a good family, strength, courage, health, looks, good fortune and things to enjoy all come according to होनहार.
3. (start) होनहार does not mean a "promising child". It means "जो होना ही है", another name for प्रारब्ध. Whether you are born rich or poor, strong or weak, brave or timid, healthy or not, good-looking or not, all of it follows प्रारब्ध. "रूप सबको मिलता नहीं है." [«होनहार मतलब? होनहार बच्चा नहीं»]
4. (early) Nobody stops you from making effort (the beauty parlour example), but you cannot tell whether the result came from पुरुषार्थ or from प्रारब्ध. This is a रहस्य. Only a guru can tell, by comparing the original पत्रिका (horoscope) with what actually happened. "Otherwise it is always a रहस्य." [«पर तुमको beauty parlour»]
5. (early) Even an अवतार's life is a रहस्य to people. "Sometimes you have to accept and go." He says the whole of Ashma Gita is for this, "to accept and go", because the game of प्रारब्ध and time is very powerful "and you cannot answer". [«अवतार की life का रहस्य»]
6. (early) Gita 2.29: even a महापुरुष who knows the आत्मतत्त्व sees, describes and hears it "as a wonder". Such surprise reaches even that level, even an avatar's. So we should expect the unexpected in our own lives too: "Planning किया था क्या ऐसा? नहीं. It happened." [«उसको सब पता है, तो भी आश्चर्य»]
7. (early) Restatement: everything comes according to होनहार/प्रारब्ध, and why is a रहस्य. "हज़ारों जन्म research करो तो भी नहीं समझेगा. You have to accept and proceed." Why was a child born into a rich home and given more in childhood? [«इसका research मैं करता हूँ»]
8. (early) Nature/the exam does not adjust for unequal advantages (the school example). "जो है, वो है." "प्रकृति doesn't check all these things." [«स्कूल में दो बच्चे आ गए»]
9. (early) Some allowance can be made out of दया-माया (a separate batch for the handicapped), but not in the mainstream (the cricket example). "You have to choose wisely." It comes according to होनहार, and "पुरुषार्थ उसके बाद". [«दया-माया के अनुसार»]
10. (early–middle) Slide: होनहार = प्रारब्ध, set by संस्कार and कर्म of past births. In Sanskrit it is भवितव्य, प्रारब्ध or दैव. The shlok breaks human pride and the delusion of comparison.
11. (middle) Slides with his comments: beauty comes from some तप, freedom from disease from some पुण्य, and birth in a great family from भाग्य. Each is from past births. "तप मतलब उसका पुण्य-वुण्य."
12. (middle) Garuda Purana (as he cites it): a being does not jump straight to a ब्राह्मण birth. It rises step by step (पशु → चांडाल → upward), and a being does not come directly as an avatar. High and low birth come from प्रारब्ध. [«तो गरुड़ पुराण में दिया है»]
13. (middle) Gita ch. 16 or 14 (he was unsure which): someone who dies in तमोगुण is born as an insect, an animal or a चांडाल. So a person seen in low circumstances today may have risen from below or fallen from above. "Difficult! Now, Guru knows that answer." [«और भगवद्गीता chapter 16»]
14. (middle) A pet dog was not a dog in its past birth. It was probably a human connected with you who came through प्रारब्ध. A dog hit by your car is "not normal": it came to settle something with you ("chances are bright"; a 1% chance it was just an animal). Animals rising in the normal sequence do not come from humans and would live in the jungle. [«कुत्ता घर में पाला है»]
15. (middle) Working animals (the bullock, the police dog) do unpaid work for humans because they too were humans in past births. "It is exactly वैसा ही है", not just a मान्यता, yet it remains a रहस्य. [«बैलगाड़ी का बैल»]
16. (middle) Slide: this understanding frees us from अहंकार and makes us compassionate toward others.
17. (middle) Slide: when someone gets honour, beauty and happiness without effort, the right response is not jealousy but विवेक: it is the fruit of past births' कर्म. He adds that the same is possible for me in this birth if I do the same कर्म. Neither of us knows the other's past birth, "but yes, कुछ शास्त्र होते हैं, शास्त्र पढ़ना होगा." [«And इस जन्म में मुझे भी»]
18. (middle) Vivekananda anecdote: a rich person must have the qualities needed for wealth. If he behaves badly with you, that is your bad ऋणानुबंध. "वो गुण क्या है, वो check कर." [«स्वामी विवेकानंद को एक बार»]
19. (middle–late) Duryodhana had qualities too. He looked after his people (with corruption), and people joined him, just as corrupt politicians still get votes because they give something. But how long it lasts is another game: Duryodhana fell, lost the war and died. [«दुर्योधन है, राक्षस है»]
20. (late) Duryodhana ran Indraprastha during the Pandavas' exile, better than Yudhishthira had (as he states it), but with a corrupt intention: to win people's loyalty for the war. This is compared with politicians' freebies. "Is this the real way of life? ये real नहीं." [«ये पांडव जब वनवास में»]
21. (late) Charity and service done with a wrong intention: तत्कालीन फलश्रुति versus long-term फलश्रुति. The immediate fruit comes, but there is no मुक्ति from the पाप and a next birth is compelled. "Intention is more important than everything else." [«तत्कालीन फलश्रुति होती है»]
22. (late) Shlok 24: the poor who do not want children have many sons, and some of the rich have not even one. "विचित्रं विधिचेष्टितम्." He says the shlok is surprising even to him.
23. (late) The rich run after उपाय (महाराज जी, जगन्नाथ जी, पंडित जी, doctor, IVF), while some poor people and "कुछ धर्मों के लोग" say children are God's gift. "करता खुद है और बोलता है, बच्चा भगवान का है." It is part of प्रारब्ध, "but ये होते हुए दिखता है", so we have to accept it. [«And then वो यहाँ-वहाँ ढूँढते»]
24. (late) The विधाता balances things. The rich get comfort and then disease of the body. The poor person works hard, can sleep anywhere and is healthy. [«इसको अमीर, सब कुछ भगवान»]
25. (late) Whether a child comes has nothing to do with being poor or rich. If it is to happen, it keeps happening; if not, "कैसे भी नहीं होगा". No good or bad attaches to it: "है, तो है।" [«अब ये प्रारब्ध का है»]
26. (late) The frame of the text: Vyasa tells Yudhishthira, within which Ashma Rishi tells King Janaka. पुरुषार्थ is the औषध and comes later in the text. "But पुरुषार्थ has got its own limitations also. फिर गुरुकृपा उसमें लगती है." [«वेद व्यास युधिष्ठिर को»]
27. (late) Four things are needed to change भाग्य. **(1) योजना:** show the plan for money, marriage or a happy life. Most people have only a सपना. "If wishes were horses, beggars would ride." Christ's "Seek…": पुरुषार्थ is needed. Without a plan you live like animals and birds, आहार, भय, मैथुन, निद्रा, "nothing special". [«तो अगर आपको भाग्य बदलना है»]
28. (end) **(2) प्रारब्ध:** check it right after the plan. If the plan is ₹100 and प्रारब्ध gives ₹60, do not stop seeking the other 40. You need पुरुषार्थ and योजना to go beyond प्रारब्ध. Even then the फलश्रुति does not come without the third. [«योजना के बाद प्रारब्ध बोला»]
29. (end) **(3) आत्मविश्वास** in one's plan: "दो सौ टक्का सही है". The recording is cut off before the fourth.

## Main-text shlokas covered
- **Shlok 23** (slide "अश्मा गीता, श्लोक २३"): the Sanskrit is not available (not on the slide text, not audible). Only the Hindi meaning survives, and one word after "जन्म" is uncertain [?]. Meaning: birth in a good family, बल, पराक्रम, आरोग्य, रूप, सौभाग्य and उपभोग-सामग्री all come by होनहार. Commentary: **long** (most of the first half).
- **Shlok 24:** `सन्ति पुत्राः सुबहवो दरिद्राणामनिच्छताम्। नास्ति पुत्रः समृद्धानां विचित्रं विधिचेष्टितम्॥`. The slide read पुत्रः/दारिद्रणाम्/समृद्घानां, and the transcriber corrected these, so check against Gita Press. Meaning: the unwilling poor have many sons, some rich have none, and the विधाता's ways are strange. Commentary: **long**.

## Other scripture cited
- **Bhagavad Gita 2.29** (stated): «आश्चर्यवत्पश्यति … आश्चर्यवद्वदति … आश्चर्यवत् … शृणोति». He spoke only fragments, then read the full verse later, but that text is not in the transcript. The slide gives the Hindi meaning. It supports the point that even great souls and avatars find the आत्मा and events a wonder, so accept and go.
- **Garuda Purana** (paraphrase, no verse): a being rises birth by birth (पशु → चांडाल → … → ब्राह्मण). It supports the point that high and low birth come from प्रारब्ध.
- **Bhagavad Gita ch. 16 or 14** (he was unsure; paraphrase): dying in तमोगुण leads to birth as an insect, animal or चांडाल. The idea matches Gita 14.15 (मूढयोनिषु), but he did not cite it, so it needs checking. It supports the point that a low birth may be a fall from above.
- **Proverb:** "If wishes were horses, beggars would ride." A dream alone is not a plan.
- **Jesus Christ** (as he said it): "Seek, and it shall be given to the seeker". The usual wording is "Seek, and ye shall find". It supports पुरुषार्थ/योजना.
- **Vivekananda** (anecdote, see below).
- आहार, भय, मैथुन, निद्रा: the traditional formula, used without a source. A life with only these four is animal-like.

## Stories, examples and images
- (start) Light in the dark: if you cannot see, you are "guaranteed गिरने वाला". This is why you should know what is coming.
- (early) Beauty parlour, bleach and the doctor: for some it enhances looks, for others it makes them worse, so you cannot tell पुरुषार्थ from प्रारब्ध.
- (early) The guru compares the original पत्रिका with what happened.
- (early) Two children at school: one has three home tutors, बादाम-दूध and सरस्वती दीक्षा from his father's guru; the other has none. The teacher does not adjust the passing marks.
- (early) Cricket: a handicapped player gets no "slow ball" in international cricket (the "लिंबू-टिंबू, कच्चा लिंबू" image). A five-year-old put into international cricket would still be bowled out: "मैं तो जीतने आया हूँ".
- (middle) A pet dog, a dog hit by your car, the bullock, the police dog: all were humans in past births, here through प्रारब्ध.
- (middle) Vivekananda is asked why a person who does crooked work is rich. He answers: think with विवेक-बुद्धि, the man must have the qualities for wealth; his bad behaviour with you is your ऋणानुबंध.
- (middle–late) Duryodhana as a prince with qualities, who looked after his people, ran Indraprastha during the exile (claimed: better than Yudhishthira) to win loyalty, then fell in the war.
- (middle–late) Corrupt politicians still get votes: "दारू का अड्डा खोल के देता होगा", free food and money.
- (late) Beggars have many children; the rich run to gurus, Jagannath ji, pandits and IVF.
- (late) Joke, a parody of "Jack and Jill" (his own): "God knows what they did / They came down with a daughter."
- (late) The poor labourer sleeps anywhere and is healthy; the rich get disease ("पेट हो जाता है").
- (late) Bird building a nest: two twigs, two eggs, feeding worms, done. A life without a plan.
- (end) ₹100 plan versus ₹60 प्रारब्ध.
- (late) He says shlok 24 is "surprising" even to him (a personal remark).

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- Attitude to cultivate: "accept and go / accept and proceed" about what प्रारब्ध gives.
- Replace jealousy with विवेक, and check what qualities a successful person has.
- Read शास्त्र, and Mahabharata in particular ("महाभारत पढ़ो/पढ़ लो").
- Keep intention right in दान and सेवा.
- Make a योजना, check प्रारब्ध, and have आत्मविश्वास in the plan (the fourth step was not recorded).
- No meditation and no Q&A; the questions to the audience ("Is it making some sense?", "समझा कि नहीं?") are rhetorical.

## Key terms
- **होनहार:** "जो होना ही है", another name for प्रारब्ध; not a "promising child".
- **प्रारब्ध / भवितव्य / दैव:** destiny fixed by the संस्कार and कर्म of past births (slide).
- **पुरुषार्थ:** one's own effort. It comes "after" प्रारब्ध, is the औषध given later in the text, and has its own limitations.
- **रहस्य:** what has no answer: why something happened, or whether it came from effort or destiny.
- **गुरुकृपा:** needed where पुरुषार्थ reaches its limits.
- **तप / पुण्य / भाग्य:** past-life causes of beauty, health and noble birth. "तप मतलब उसका पुण्य-वुण्य."
- **पत्रिका:** horoscope. The guru compares it with what actually happened.
- **ऋणानुबंध:** the karmic bond of debt between people. It explains someone's bad behaviour toward you.
- **विवेक / विवेक-बुद्धि:** discernment, the right response in place of jealousy.
- **तत्कालीन फलश्रुति vs long-term फलश्रुति:** the immediate fruit of an act versus its long-run consequence.
- **विधाता की चेष्टा / विधिचेष्टितम्:** the strange working of the Creator/destiny.
- **दया-माया:** compassion-based allowance, as in a separate category.
- **तमोगुण:** dying in it leads to a low birth.
- **चांडाल, योनि:** low birth; species or womb of birth.
- **सरस्वती दीक्षा:** an initiation for learning, in the school example.
- **योजना, आत्मविश्वास:** the first and third of the four requirements for changing भाग्य.
- **आहार, भय, मैथुन, निद्रा:** the four animal functions.
- **टोना-टोटका:** charms and rituals, which he does not prefer.

## Sensitive or editorial items
- (start) Implied acceptance of some उपाय for averting events, despite "we don't prefer" टोना-टोटका.
- (early, middle) Claims that only the guru knows answers about प्रारब्ध and past births (guru authority).
- (middle) Caste language: a step-by-step rise through चांडाल to ब्राह्मण, and "नीच-उच्च" by प्रारब्ध. Sensitive.
- (middle) Factual claims: every pet or working animal was a human in a past birth; a dog hit by your car came to settle karma ("exactly वैसा ही है"). These are stated as fact.
- (middle) The Gita chapter is uncertain (16 or 14).
- (early) Health and poverty are attributed to past births: health by पुण्य, the handicapped and the poor by प्रारब्ध. Sensitive.
- (middle–late) Politics: corrupt politicians, freebies, "दारू का अड्डा". No names.
- (late) Mahabharata claims: Duryodhana ran Indraprastha during the exile, better than Yudhishthira; "चौदह साल, तेरह साल". These need checking against the text, and the transcriber's reading is uncertain.
- (late) "कुछ धर्मों के लोग" who have many children and wives and say God gives them: an implied criticism of a religious community. Highly sensitive.
- (late) The poor and beggars having many children, and the Jack and Jill parody joke about them. Sensitive in tone.
- (late) Health claim: the rich get disease and the poor are healthy.
- (late) Mild criticism of people running to महाराज जी, पंडित जी, Jagannath ji and IVF for उपाय.
