## Opening and closing
**Opening:** The recording starts in the middle of a sentence (start). He is telling the audience about a visitor from the Jagannath tradition, probably a sevak or priest whose name is not given. The visitor told him he had no अहम् भाव at all, and put a photo around his neck. This leads into the next shlokas of Ashma Gita: he recaps shlok 25 and moves on to 26.

**Closing:** The recording breaks off mid-thought during shlok 27, on the slide «इसलिए आज को श्रेष्ठ बनाना ही हमारा धर्म है». He is in the middle of the building analogy and stops at "...third floor से गिरकर ही मृत्यु हो गई तो?" (end).

## Teachings in order
1. **(start)** A visitor told him he was "अहम् भाव से शून्य" [«आप अहम् भाव से शून्य हैं»]. The visitor said that in everyone else he saw some trace of ego, and that the work of the ego-free one goes "fast fast".
2. **(start)** Why would anyone put a photo around the neck of an unknown person? He says it only happens with a companion from many births, a meeting like Radha-Krishna or Krishna-Sudama. "फोटो देखना, आपको रहस्य मिल जाएगा": the photo is linked to the visitor's daily श्रृंगार tradition of Jagannath ("वही थे, वही आए थे").
3. **(early)** The visitor could think of nothing beyond Jagannath. He objected to Shiva's photo in the Guru Gita. Acharya Ji replied that Shiva is "their" आराध्य and guru, but the visitor could not accept this. In the end, "he understood": the visitor said he saw in Acharya Ji what he sees in Jagannath.
4. **(early)** The visitor's reported remarks:
   - "विश्व गुरु का काम इधर से ही होगा"
   - all the four-dham gurus would be held together by Acharya Ji
   - he told some secrets, which Acharya Ji will not repeat, whose फलश्रुति is that विश्व गुरु will come soon
5. **(early)** The visitor also said it will happen only through अंतर योग, "और किसी से नहीं होगा". His reason was that everyone, himself included, is caught in some trap, and everyone has at least one percent ego. In Acharya Ji he saw "zero", and "zero सिर्फ जगन्नाथ में होता है". At the gate he promised to come again.
6. **(early)** He asks the audience: do you see it? Others come briefly and see it, and it is right in front of your eyes. "अनुभूति आती है क्या अभी?"
7. **(early, recap)** Shlok 25: the cause of death is fixed by प्रारब्ध at birth and is the "सेतु". It is decided, but in the rarest of rare cases, as with Markandeya Rishi, it can be changed.
8. **(early)** Shlok 26: in most people's lives nothing changes. They do not even know the सहस्रार was a closed lotus. Prarabdh changes only when the lotus opens (a realized master), or when one goes to someone whose lotus is open. Very few people have this happen.
9. **(early)** This is why sayings like these become accepted [«समय से पहले, किस्मत से ज्यादा»]:
   - "समय से पहले, किस्मत से ज्यादा किसी को कुछ नहीं मिलता"
   - "विधि का विधान बदला नहीं जा सकता"

   He calls them वेदों के वाक्य that are over-believed, because people "don't want to go to the guru". They will not leave अहम् भाव or practise शरणागति.
10. **(early-middle)** The shlok is about the general public, up to and including Yudhishthira, who is a good धर्मराज king and not an avatar. Even avatars often did not cross the setu, because they had a लीला to play. The rare exceptions are avatars, the great ones and realized masters.
11. **(middle)** The text says "प्रबल पुरुषार्थ", not ordinary effort. Such a person is not under दैव, whatever their prarabdh:
    - Markandeya was given sixteen years and instead lived sixteen कल्प
    - nature itself breaks its controls for him: rain is cancelled, or comes only as much as is needed for coolness
    - "गुरु के लिए... जैसा वह बोलेगा, वैसा होगा ही होगा"
12. **(middle)** "Destiny can be rewritten. Clear-cut बोला है", but only through प्रबल पुरुषार्थ, not a little effort.
13. **(middle)** Meaning of पुरुषार्थ, "पुरि शेते इति पुरुषः" [«पुरि शेते इति पुरुषः»]. The body is a पुर with nine gates, and the one who sleeps or lives in it is पुरुष. पुरुषार्थ is meaningful action done to attain that पुरुष.
14. **(middle)** Going to the office earns a salary, but if it does nothing towards मोक्ष it is "donkey work", "गधा-मजदूरी", because rebirth will come again.
15. **(middle)** One must go to such a rare पुरुष to change one's own prarabdh. They know the secret, and may change it in several ways:
    - by शक्तिपात
    - by making you do other तपस्या
    - through your प्रबल पुरुषार्थ in the form of सेवा in a big विश्व कार्य
16. **(middle)** Even in seva people quarrel and fail to learn अद्वैत भाव. "जितने लोग उतनी बुद्धि... उतनी प्रकृति, उतनी विकृति". Everybody likes independence, and people divide along the three gunas.
17. **(middle)** A group needs rules and discipline, as in the army or a गुरुकुल. Once the rules are accepted, willingly or not, अद्वैत भाव slowly begins.
18. **(middle)** A mother sacrifices only for her own son. The army teaches the reverse order: first the country, then one's unit, then oneself. "उसी का उल्लंघन होगा", meaning this is how the setu gets crossed.
19. **(middle)** He criticizes an incident: someone said "मुझे बस से क्यों उतारा... मैं दूसरी गाड़ी से जाऊँगी". He calls it a sign of ahankar that wastes many people's time: "That is not a gurukul way of living."
20. **(middle)** Ashma Gita as a whole is not about these rare persons. Sometimes even such a master says "यह भोगकर ही तेरा होने वाला है", and even Shri Ram could not shorten Sita's वनवास. What to do then "is all about अश्मा गीता".
21. **(middle)** On the slide "अर्थ": saints and siddhas who put off death, defeated disease ("science of healing") or left the body by choice (संजीवनी समाधि). The ordinary person is under prarabdh, while the siddha can cross it "the way he wants" and can control nature.
22. **(middle)** Why the siddha can cross it, using the "dream diagram" and E = MC². The siddha knows it is all कल्पना and changes the thinking at its source. C is the संस्कार or deep belief. Change it, and the thoughts become E after a while, and M, physical reality, follows.
23. **(middle)** Example: a wife expects "पति बुरा है", imagining he will shout. This becomes visualization, then संस्कार, and "you have manifested that... वह तुम्हारा creation है".
24. **(middle)** Sometimes the outcome does not change even after one's manifestation changes, because "सात जन्मों का प्रारब्ध अभी तक pending है". What to do then is the subject here.
25. **(middle)** Examples on the slides:
    - Markandeya: he does not retell the story, saying he told it shortly before
    - Bhagwan Dattatreya and many sadgurus could change their own or others' prarabdh
    - his own quote: "Destiny is pre-written, but it can also be rewritten"
    - in ordinary life we are bound by विधि, but sadguru's guidance, seva and blessing move us towards freedom

    He calls these "self-explanatory".
26. **(late)** Shlok 27 [«इस जगत में धनवान»]. Money buys doctors, medicines, injections and the gym, but it is no guarantee of a long life. These shlokas need only an example or two, and your own daily life shows it: "प्रारब्धवश, काल के वश".
27. **(late)** The poor person in distress lives to a hundred. Someone who wants to die does not die, because there is still sin to suffer, or something good still to come.
28. **(late)** Suicide attempts that fail:
    - the track changes, so the train goes another way
    - the fan falls
    - something happens to the stool

    "That is all प्रारब्ध का खेल". It must sometimes be accepted, and it can be for good or for bad.
29. **(late)** On the slide about a young person's sudden death and a poor man's long life: the poor man in a government hospital is kept alive when a free election-time योजना comes along. "जाना है तो भी जा नहीं सकता". Whether for a good or a bad reason, "भोग है, भोगना है".
30. **(late)** Once this secret is understood, one worries neither about death nor about anything else: "चिंता नहीं करूँगा, चिंतन जरूर करूँगा".
31. **(late)** Youth and wealth do not give immortality. Who has a guarantee of leaving this hall alive? A heart attack, a slip, an electric shock could happen. "Nobody can guarantee... even an अवतार."
32. **(late)** "The results are known but means are not": a guru knows the end, but not what happens along the way. Otherwise he would predict every minute. This is linked to "आश्चर्यवत् पश्यति, वदति, शृणोति", which applies even at the stage of guru.
33. **(late-end)** "इसलिए आज को श्रेष्ठ बनाना ही हमारा धर्म है". Fear of tomorrow is itself death. Living for today does not mean not planning: the planning is done today and the execution happens today. He cites "कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन".
34. **(end)** If something takes ten years to carry out and death comes next year, worrying was useless anyway. Building analogy: enjoy each stage of a six-storey building, the foundation and then each floor. The recording cuts off at "what if you die falling from the third floor while surveying it?"

## Main-text shlokas covered
Only the slides' Hindi translations were read; the Sanskrit original was not read.
- **Shlok 25 (recap):** disease, fire, water, weapons, hunger and thirst, calamity, poison, fever and falling from a height are the causes of death. The cause fixed by prarabdh at birth is one's "सेतु". Commentary: brief (recap only; covered in full in an earlier session).
- **Shlok 26:** no one is seen crossing this setu, and no one is known to have crossed it before. But a rare person who, by tapasya and प्रबल पुरुषार्थ, is not fit to stay under दैव is seen to cross it. Commentary: **long** (teachings 8 to 25).
- **Shlok 27:** in this world even the rich are seen to perish in youth, while a poor person in distress lives a hundred years and dies in extreme old age. Commentary: **long**, still in progress when the recording cuts off.

## Other scripture cited
- "पुरि शेते इति पुरुषः": an etymology of पुरुष, with no source given. It supports the meaning of पुरुषार्थ (middle). The transcript corrected it from "पुरुष येते इति पुरुष".
- Proverbs he calls वेदों के वाक्य: "समय से पहले, किस्मत से ज्यादा किसी को कुछ नहीं मिलता" and "विधि का विधान बदला नहीं जा सकता" (early). They show the common belief in fixed fate.
- "आश्चर्यवत् पश्यति, वदति, शृणोति": spoken incomplete, an allusion to Gita 2.29 (the transcript does not give the reference). It supports the point that even a guru does not know the means along the way (late).
- "कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन", Gita 2.47 (late-end). It supports doing today's work and leaving the results.

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- **(early)** He asks the audience whether they see the "zero" in him: "अनुभूति आती है क्या अभी?" They are told to look at the photo to find the secret.
- **(middle)** Instruction: go to a rare master to change prarabdh, through seva in विश्व कार्य, शक्तिपात or tapasya.
- **(middle)** Instruction: accept the rules of group or gurukul living and learn अद्वैत भाव. Do not act from ego, as in the bus incident.
- **(late)** Look at your own daily life for examples of prarabdh. Do चिंतन, not चिंता. Plan, but carry out the plan today.
- **Q&A:** no formal exchange. There are only rhetorical checks such as "समझ में आ गया?" and "Explanation कुछ है?"

## Key terms
- **अहम् भाव:** ego. The visitor said he saw "zero" of it.
- **सेतु:** the cause of death fixed by prarabdh at birth, the bridge by which one goes to the next world.
- **प्रारब्ध:** destiny fixed at birth. It can be crossed only in rare cases, and "सात जन्मों का प्रारब्ध" may still be pending.
- **दैव:** fate or divine control. The rare पुरुष is "not under" it.
- **प्रबल पुरुषार्थ:** strong effort, not ordinary effort, needed to rewrite destiny.
- **पुरुष / पुरुषार्थ:** the पुरुष is the one who sleeps or lives in the nine-gated पुर, the body. पुरुषार्थ is meaningful action done to attain that पुरुष (towards मोक्ष).
- **सहस्रार (closed or open lotus):** open in a realized master, and only then does prarabdh change.
- **शरणागति:** surrender to the guru, which people avoid.
- **अद्वैत भाव:** the sense of oneness learned through group discipline.
- **गुरुकुल:** a life of rules and discipline.
- **शक्तिपात:** one way a master may change prarabdh.
- **संजीवनी समाधि:** leaving the body by choice.
- **संस्कार / deep belief:** the "C" in his E = MC² model, and the thing the siddha changes.
- **फलश्रुति:** the fruit or result.
- **चिंता vs चिंतन:** worry vs reflection.
- **विश्व गुरु:** India as world guru, which the visitor said would come through अंतर योग.
- **लीला:** the reason avatars did not cross the setu.
- **कल्प:** a cosmic age, as in Markandeya's lifespan of 16 कल्प.
- **परवडता:** Marathi for "affordable".
