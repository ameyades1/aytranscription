## Teachings in order
1. (start) He announces that the Ashma Gita begins now and will be covered fast, and shows the opening line of shlok 1. Vaishampayan, who narrates the main Mahabharata, speaks it to Janamejaya. [«अब यहाँ से हम आते हैं अश्मा गीता»]
2. (start) He reads the introduction slide: Vyas teaches Yudhishthir through the dialogue of Janak and Ashma Rishi. The text shows the role of प्रारब्ध and काल, why सुख-दुःख and लाभ-हानि happen, and how to stay unaffected by them. To succeed, one must accept difficulties with शांति and धैर्य, because they are part of काल and प्रारब्ध.
3. (early) Chanakya's example shows the difference between प्रारब्ध and काल. Chandragupta was destined (प्रारब्ध) to become king, but the time (काल) had not yet come. People had to "align", and Ghananand first had to die, be removed or surrender. He says this book (the Ashma Gita) teaches how to handle all this. [«तो आचार्य चाणक्य जी को भी»]
4. (early) He reads the second introduction slide: the Gita's main aim is to teach a person to accept the course of events in life and to judge their own कर्म correctly.
5. (early) Acceptance: someone who understands that they will grow old and their hair will turn white or fall out, and who can "be one with that", has no problem with anything, "छोटी सी बात से लेकर युद्ध तक". "He accepts. जितना जल्दी करोगे उतना अच्छा." If you choose to fight instead, do the पुरुषार्थ properly. (Source garbled here.) [«तो एक दिन मैं बूढ़ा होऊँगा»]
6. (early) "We are never against any science, technology, modern सब". The problem is becoming accustomed to it (wording unclear). Do it after asking the guru.
7. (early) He gives the chapter heading: Vyas explains the प्रबलता of प्रारब्ध to Yudhishthir through the Ashma–Janak dialogue. He then explains why प्रारब्ध is so strong, and what happens without a guru. [«अश्मा ऋषि और जनक राजा के संवाद»]
8. (early) अदृष्ट is the karma of 7, 8 or 10 past births. It cannot be seen and sits as संस्कार and as कर्म, for example "-100 units of पाप". The पुरुषार्थ of one birth changes प्रारब्ध only if it fully cancels that load. Otherwise the load only gets lighter (-80, -50). [«सात-सात, आठ-आठ, दस-दस जन्मों»]
9. (early) "There are limitations of this birth." Some of this karma has to be suffered (भोगना) however much पुरुषार्थ one does.
10. (early) The guru can cancel the karma of even 10,000 births. It is cancelled by तपस्या and by गुरु का सान्निध्य, "स्वयं से नहीं होगा". On your own, -1000 only comes down to -998.
11. (early-middle) He explains shlok 1 (translation): Yudhishthir, grieving for his kin, wants to give up his life, and Vyas removes his grief. The frame now changes: the Janamejaya–Vaishampayan frame is closed, and inside it ("bracket में") Vyas speaks to Yudhishthir. [«अब you understood the entire story»]
12. (middle) Allegory: Vyas = "जो जीवित गुरु सामने आपके लिए बैठे हैं". Yudhishthir = the disciple who wants to fight their own sorrow, steady in battle, and who later has to live like a king.
13. (middle) He reads the अर्थ slide: when the outcome is painful even after doing everything right, a person often turns toward आत्मदाह or renunciation. Only a सद्गुरु's knowledge can save them then.
14. (middle) Pace of the course: the Gita "बहुत fast जाएगी". Only the important shlokas will be explained, and it will finish easily in two days.
15. (middle) Shlok 2: Vyas calls Yudhishthir पुरुषसिंह to encourage him, a king being like the lion, king of the jungle.
16. (middle) Which Gita for which problem: Ashma Gita is for an Arjun-type problem's opposite. Regret *after* acting ("शादी होने के बाद अभी, क्यों किया?") calls for the Ashma Gita, where the Bhagavad Gita "काम की नहीं है". Doubt *before* acting (e.g. no good marriage proposals, so "शादी करना ही छोड़ देती हूँ") calls for the Bhagavad Gita. [«ऐसी problem के लिए only solution»]
17. (middle) Before acting there was a chance for पुरुषार्थ (to do it, not do it, or change it). After acting the question is "now how to face that", for how long, and whether it will change. The answer is स्वीकार.
18. (middle) Shlok 2: "अश्मा ब्राह्मण के गीत" means his Gita.
19. (middle) Shlok 3: Janak, a king also in grief, is the example given to King Yudhishthir. Principle: "always a guru should use similar stories". [«अभी exactly same हुआ था»]
20. (middle) Doctor analogy (see Stories): the right testimonial must be about *your* problem. The guru people prefer first is one who solves the specific problem, for example teaching श्री विद्या for a money problem. Only if that still fails is the "स्वीकार" guru the correct one.
21. (middle) He claims that Gita 3.25–26 says a guru who hears your problem and at once says "ये सब मोहमाया है, छोड़ दे" should be left, and that Krishna says to go only to a guru who can solve your problem. That guru should have done it himself, or at least have experience of many similar cases (cancer example). "Check करो." [«और यही example भगवद्गीता में chapter 3»]
22. (middle) On 3.25: the ignorant do कर्म with attachment and without knowing why. Examples: MBA, marriage, business, war, daily पूजा and आरती done "क्यों करते हैं? कैसे करते हैं? नहीं पता". The विद्वान here means the guru, who acts in the same way for लोकसंग्रह ("Lakhs of लोगों को जमा करके").
23. (middle) If society does पूजा and he speaks only of ज्ञान, ten people will come and he will have to sit in the Himalayas. So his गुरुकुल must also first keep पूजा, पाठ and मूर्ति. Only then will people come to listen, leaving other temples.
24. (middle-late) On 3.26: there are two kinds of कर्म, शास्त्रविहित and शास्त्ररहित. Marriage (गृहस्थ आश्रम) and business (वैश्य वर्ण) are शास्त्रविहित. Running a liquor den or a brothel is sin, and a guru need not "demo" it. A guru must show all शास्त्रविहित कर्म by doing them himself.
25. (late) Krishna, who married many times, is the right person to answer questions about marriage. He was happy with none, happy with many, happy with the gopis.
26. (late) A guru must not create अश्रद्धा: "आज तक शादी करके कौन सुखी हो गया है? ... चल हिमालय में" is advice a guru should not give.
27. (late) Applied to Antar Yog: to make India an economic superpower he must learn श्री विद्या himself. He cannot live on alms, "Antar Yog cannot run only on donation". It must earn its own दक्षिणा and make its own product, and its office must be in Mumbai's Fort area, not a village. Only then can it talk about the economy. [«अगर मुझे भारत को economic superpower»]
28. (late) Shankaracharya had no money but had the power to make gold rain through the कनकधारा स्तोत्र. That is why he could write a work like सौंदर्य लहरी and people believed him.
29. (late) "भलीभाँति": to have people do पूजा, he must first do it the real way himself. Anyone, even a Himalayan renunciate, can teach brahmacharya. But शास्त्रोक्त काम ("शास्त्रोक्त काम मैं हूँ, कामदेव मैं हूँ", from the Gita) can be taught only by someone who has had contact with a woman.
30. (late) Shankaracharya and Mandan Mishra (see Stories): one must teach only what one has experienced (अनुभूति). "But he did not become preacher for that."
31. (late) If everyone is told to go to the Himalayas, a new kingdom with a king and a system will form there. That system already existed here, so why go?
32. (end) A guru who says at once "ये सब मोहमाया है, छोड़ दे" is not a true guru, even if it is मोहमाया. He should first show a solution and later prove how it is wrong. Otherwise "ऐसे गुरु से भाग जा". The doctor analogy is repeated. Cut off.

## Other scripture cited
- **Bhagavad Gita 3.25** (stated, Gita Press Hindi translation on the slide): the ignorant act with attachment, and the unattached wise one should act the same way for लोकसंग्रह. It supports the point that a guru must also do what society does (पूजा etc.).
- **Bhagavad Gita 3.26** (stated, Gita Press translation): the wise should not unsettle the ignorant who are attached to शास्त्रविहित कर्म, but should do all such कर्म well and have them do it too. It supports the points that a guru must not say "मोहमाया, छोड़ दे" and must show शास्त्रविहित कर्म by doing them. *Note:* his framing that "Krishna says leave such a guru" and "go only to a guru who can solve it" is his own interpretation, not the verse text.
- **"शास्त्रोक्त काम मैं हूँ, कामदेव मैं हूँ"**: a paraphrase attributed to the Bhagavad Gita. No reference was stated (it may correspond to Gita 7.11 or 10.28, not confirmed). It supports the point that शास्त्रोक्त काम can be taught only by one with experience.
- **Chanakya to Chandragupta**: «धैर्य के आसन में बैठने की तपस्या करो, क्योंकि समय आएगा... जब लोग तुम्हारा सिर्फ सुनेंगे ही नहीं, मानेंगे भी». It is a saying, not a verse, with no source given. It supports the point about प्रारब्ध versus काल.

## Key terms
- **प्रारब्ध**: destiny ripened from past karma, for example "प्रारब्ध में तो राजा बनना है".
- **काल**: the right time, which may not be now: "काल तुरंत नहीं है, time है".
- **प्रारब्ध की प्रबलता**: the strength of प्रारब्ध, because past births' karma outweighs one birth's effort.
- **अदृष्ट**: "जो दिखता नहीं है", the karma of many births sitting as संस्कार.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: one's own effort in this birth, which is limited.
- **स्वीकार**: acceptance, the Ashma Gita's answer once action is done.
- **गुरु का सान्निध्य**: the guru's nearness, by which karma is cancelled.
- **लोकसंग्रह**: gathering people, "Lakhs of लोगों को जमा करके".
- **शास्त्रविहित / शास्त्ररहित कर्म**: actions sanctioned by scripture (marriage, business) versus forbidden ones (liquor den, brothel).
- **मोहमाया**: what a false guru at once calls your problem.
- **भलीभाँति**: doing कर्म the real, proper way.
- **परकाया प्रवेश**: entering another's body, the siddhi Shankaracharya used.
- **पुरुषसिंह**: lion among men, an encouraging address.
- **अनुभूति**: direct experience, required before teaching.
- **श्री विद्या**: taught as the solution for money problems and for building the economy.
