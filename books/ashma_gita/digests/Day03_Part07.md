# Digest: Day 3, Part 7

## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** Begins mid-discussion. He is finishing an example about a builder who planned a building and flats but did not live to enjoy them. The point is that nobody knows destiny, so plan first for what comes after death. `[«तो building तुम्हारी थी»]`
- **Closing:** Cut off mid-thought. Under Shlok 31 he asks how far it is possible to cross माया if even great scholars are seen sinning, then begins "who are those ऋषि?" The text ends at «कौन से ऋषि?». The source transcript is marked "Completed till 12 minutes" of a 13:05–18:15 segment, so the session is incomplete.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) The builder had the building but never got to enjoy it: no building, no flat sold. "You don't know what is destiny." Don't count your money in advance, as in "so many flats at two crores each, eight on every floor". Planning is fine for a builder, but there is no guarantee you will be alive to enjoy it. Death is guaranteed. `[«तो building तुम्हारी थी»]`
2. (start) So plan first for where you go next. He gives the formula "जनहिताय स्वयं मोक्षाय च".
3. (start) This is not विरक्ति (detachment) that forbids planning the building. श्री विद्या teaches भोग plus मोक्ष, so plan for both. `[«तो ऐसा नहीं कि building»]`
4. (start) Example from Antar Yog: as it grows, there will be planning for whom to hand it to as successor, but no worry. If there is no one, he would give it to the government. Even if the government is corrupt then, the work goes to the right person if one's own work has been done. One should have that confidence. Today's sleep is not lost over it.
5. (early) He will try to train people, and does not know how it will grow. He repeats an earlier prediction: in two years, next the King cannot ignore it, after that the World cannot ignore it. He looks only at today. Planning "600 years ahead" is already done, so there is no worry. After the session he would go play cricket or go to the swimming pool. `[«क्या होगा? कब बनेगा?»]`
6. (early) "That is the life I live", because of that inner स्थिति. Do today's work for अमरत्व in case death comes today. `[«तो that is the life I live»]`
7. (early) Shlok 28: the poor can be long-lived, and those born to wealthy families die like moths. Not every poor person lives long, and not every rich person dies early, but the pattern is seen. Its secret lies in past births. A guru will not explain each case, and the mystery is hard to grasp fully. `[«ये भी reality में देखा है»]`
8. (early) Gita 4.17 "गहना कर्मणो गतिः": the course of कर्म is deep. One must know कर्म, अकर्म and विकर्म. He defines बुद्धिमान as one who always does only परमेश्वर's work. Gita 4.16 (meaning only, on the slide): even the wise are confused about कर्म and अकर्म. कर्मतत्त्व = "कर्म रहस्य".
9. (early) Example of lending one lakh: you cannot know whether it is repaying a past-life debt, so should you ask for it back? There is no answer, hence गहना कर्मणो गतिः. `[«तो यहाँ पे भी तुमने किसी को एक lakh»]`
10. (early-middle) We act with the विवेक बुद्धि of the moment. When the fruit comes, the guru, or the nagging person at home (कटकट), says what should or should not have been done. With prior गुरु शरणागति, the right बुद्धि and correct choices come on their own. Without it you keep doing wrong, wrong, wrong.
11. (middle) पतङ्गवत्: like moths drawn to a lamp flame, people drawn by वैभव, शक्ति and अभिमान bring about their own end. They die like insects, meaning they do not get मोक्ष.
12. (middle) Slide: the safety of life lies not in सुविधा but in surrender to विनय, विवेक and विधि. Life insurance and medicine do not secure life. The "real investment" is work for मुक्ति in whatever span you have. Retirement funds do not help if you die before retirement. "Be prepared for any day. Any day is a good day to leave this world." `[«जीवन की सिर्फ़ सुरक्षा»]`
13. (middle) This does not mean taking चिपळ्या and sitting in the Himalayas, which he does not do himself. Within your profession, ask: as a doctor, how do I reach मुक्ति, become अमर, become an inspiration? Or how can I help my guru's गुरु कार्य? That should be the first priority. A warrior fighting for the राष्ट्र is fine too.
14. (middle) Slide example: a poor labourer in a hut lives to 90–100 on simple food and hard work. This is the fruit of प्रारब्ध.
15. (middle) Shlok 29: the rich lose the power to eat and digest, while the poor digest even wood. Among the rich, diabetes comes, then no sugar, then no salt, heart problems, and multiple failures. The poor, having suffered all their lives, may die healthy. One gets wealth, the other may get health. A poor person who is also sick had very bad कर्म. "Otherwise it is always balanced." It is the play of प्रारब्ध. `[«धीरे-धीरे ऐसा होना चालू»]`
16. (middle) "God will not give everything for everyone." Accept this first. "I want this and that too" will never happen. Example: Draupadi. `[«God will not give everything»]`
17. (middle) The coming shlokas were meant to go fast and are self-explanatory.
18. (middle) Shlok 30: the दुरात्मा, driven by काल, thinks "I will do this". In bad times he does worse, such as theft. In good times he misuses the fruit of past births, such as name and fame, through अहंकार. He boasts of big social service but acts from negativity. Example: he starts a temple (पुण्य), then corrupt practices creep in. "That state should never be mine." `[«क्या श्लोक है! एक तो पहले»]`
19. (middle-late) "असंतोष से अपराध": a young person, burning at others' success, says "I'll do anything to get ahead". He does not consider the others' प्रारब्ध, effort or study. Envy leads to shortcuts and crime. Earlier this was said of people aged 20–30; in Kaliyuga a 15-year-old will start, for example by copying. First try to be a शुद्ध आत्मा, near the guru. A दुरात्मा with असंतोष moves to crime faster and more easily.
20. (late) Social media "भ्रमजाल": wanting followers is not wrong in itself. The दुरात्मा, "रजोगुणी and दुरात्मा", makes sensational fake videos, then shares abusive or obscene content in posts and reels. "मुझे चाहिए" never ends, and he goes into अधोगति. He takes shortcuts instead of coming to a guru or learning from others' good examples.
21. (late) Aside on listening: are you listening in a जागृत state? With सत्त्वगुण you stay alert because it concerns your मुक्ति. With रजोगुण you doze. With तमोगुण you snore. `[«But क्या जागृत अवस्था में»]`
22. (late) Shlok 31: the learned condemn hunting (हिंसा), gambling, association with women (ब्रह्मचर्य should be kept) and drinking (सुरापान). He says these fall among the "पाँच महापाप". Yet men versed in many शास्त्र are seen doing them.
23. (late) Story of Pingala (see Stories). Knowledge does not stop sin. Knowing which knowledge to use when comes only by गुरु कृपा. Otherwise, why would Krishna, after the whole Gita, say "सर्वधर्मान्परित्यज्य"? His gloss: forget all I said and start listening to what I say now, because that knowledge will not help you. `[«तो ये सब ज्ञान पाप करने से नहीं रोकता»]`
24. (late) Gita 3.33: even the wise act according to their प्रकृति. He explains स्वभाव as स्व = self and भाव = emotions or mind. The जीवात्मा is formed from it, and so is your पत्रिका (horoscope). So स्वभाव means प्रारब्ध. Even a ज्ञानी acts according to his पत्रिका.
25. (late) This is what Ashma Rishi says too: even a guru who teaches against sin may be seen sinning behind people's backs. Kings who should be ideals are corrupt. Neither knowledge nor post helped them. "Learn what not to do."
26. (end) If even great scholars do this, how possible is it to cross माया? "Which ऋषि…?" (cut off)

## Main-text shlokas covered
- **Shlok 28:** «अकिञ्चनाश्च दृश्यन्ते पुरुषाश्चिरजीविनः। समृद्धे च कुले जाता विनश्यन्ति पतङ्गवत्॥» The poor are seen to be long-lived; those born to rich families perish like moths. Commentary: long (Gita 4.16–17, the one-lakh example, पतङ्गवत्, the "real investment", the profession-and-मुक्ति point, the labourer example).
- **Shlok 29:** «प्रायेण श्रीमतां लोके भोक्तुं शक्तिर्न विद्यते। काष्ठान्यपि हि जीर्यन्ते दरिद्राणां च सर्वशः॥» The rich usually cannot eat or digest; the poor digest even wood. Commentary: moderate (illness of the rich, balance, Draupadi).
- **Shlok 30:** «अहमेतत् करोमीति मन्यते कालचोदितः। यद्यदिष्टमसंतोषाद् दुरात्मा पापमाचरेत्॥» The दुरात्मा, driven by time, becomes proud ("I will do this") and commits whatever sin he desires. **Uncertain:** «पापमाचरेत्» is restored from the raw «पाप्माछरेत»; check against Gita Press. The slide meaning reads «संतोषवश» where «असंतोषवश» would be expected, and it was read that way twice. Commentary: long (the temple example, असंतोष से अपराध, social media).
- **Shlok 31:** Sanskrit not read; meaning only. The learned condemn hunting, gambling, association with women and drinking, yet men versed in शास्त्र are seen engaged in these sins. Commentary: long (Pingala, Gita 18.66, Gita 3.33). Cut off mid-commentary.

## Key terms
- **प्रारब्ध:** past-birth fruit; it explains long life, health, the balance between rich and poor, and स्वभाव.
- **गहना कर्मणो गतिः / कर्मतत्त्व:** the deep, unknowable course of कर्म; he calls it "कर्म रहस्य".
- **कर्म, अकर्म, विकर्म:** to be known (not explained further).
- **बुद्धिमान:** one who always does only परमेश्वर's work.
- **विवेक बुद्धि:** the judgement of the moment we act by.
- **गुरु शरणागति / गुरु कृपा:** gives the right बुद्धि and choices automatically, and the knowing of which knowledge to apply.
- **पतङ्गवत्:** like a moth; self-destruction through attraction.
- **विनय, विवेक, विधि:** where life's true safety lies.
- **दुरात्मा:** an inherently bad soul; misuses both bad and good times.
- **कालचोदितः:** driven by time, good or bad.
- **असंतोष:** discontent, which leads the दुरात्मा to crime.
- **अधोगति / अधःपतन:** downfall.
- **स्वभाव:** स्व = self, भाव = emotions or mind; it forms the जीवात्मा and the पत्रिका; equated with प्रारब्ध.
- **प्रकृति:** one's nature (angry or calm) according to प्रारब्ध.
- **पत्रिका:** horoscope; even a ज्ञानी acts according to it.
- **सत्त्व, रज, तम:** shown as alertness, dozing and snoring.
- **पाँच महापाप:** he links hunting (हिंसा), gambling, women and सुरापान to them.
- **भोग plus मोक्ष:** the श्री विद्या ideal.
- **विरक्ति:** detachment, which he says is not what he means.
- **माया उल्लंघन:** crossing माया (end, cut off).
- **गुरु कार्य:** the guru's mission.
