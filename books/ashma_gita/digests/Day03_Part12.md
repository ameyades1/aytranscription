# Day 3, Part 12: Session digest

## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** The session starts in the middle of a point about astrology from the previous session. Saturn (शनि) takes thirty years to pass through the twelve houses and causes trouble in each house's relationships; साढ़ेसाती is the hard period when Saturn passes over the Moon (start) [«तो तीस साल में बारह»].
- **Closing:** It ends right after the seed-and-tree example and its closing comment that Krishna also taught faith in scripture. The last word is a logistics "Next.", so the slides carry on into the next session (end).

## Teachings in order
1. (recap) Saturn passes through 12 राशि in thirty years, about two and a half years in each house. Each house stands for a relationship, and Saturn causes trouble in it: mother (4th house), girlfriend/boyfriend and children (5th), servants (6th), husband (7th). (start) [«तो तीस साल में बारह»]
2. साढ़ेसाती happens when Saturn passes over the Moon. The Moon is "मेरा मन", so trouble in whatever the mind holds dear hurts the most. Saturn's other transits also taught something (for example "ये boss गया तो गया"), but we did not feel those as साढ़ेसाती. (start) [«पर साढ़ेसाती कब लगती है»]
3. Slide: brother, friend, father and mother turn like the संसार चक्र. Being with loved ones is अनित्य, like travelling with co-passengers; "Co-passengers हैं सिर्फ". संसार चक्र = भवचक्र, and भव means संसार. (early) [«तो यह भ्राता, मित्र, पिता»]
4. Slide: it does not stay still for even a moment. Someone who is ours today may be a stranger in the next birth, or belong to someone else later in this one. All relationships are प्रारब्धवश मिले संयोग. (early)
5. Every relationship can change. An employee moves to another boss, a girlfriend marries someone else, couples divorce and remarry. The same holds for wealth, and in the end for death. (early) [«इस जन्म का अभी का»]
6. Relationships are chance meetings brought by prārabdha, "न कि आत्मा के स्थायी साथी". Whoever understands this takes up वैराग्य easily, and without this principle nothing else will work. (early)
7. "अपने दायित्व निभाओ, लेकिन मोह से मुक्त रहो." The guru gives everything fully, like a mother, but lets the child who wants to sleep keep sleeping: "यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु, यथा योग्यम् तथा कुरु". A guru with मोह would keep saying "उठ, उठ". (early) [«तो गुरु सब कुछ भर-भर»]
8. Without मोह the guru is not bothered: that person's share of knowledge in their prārabdha was only so much. They may hear the recording years later, when "उसका time तभी आएगा". (early-middle)
9. Be like the दाई माँ (nanny). She serves the child completely while it is in her care and has no tension once she leaves. Even a real mother is told to live this way; he credits this to Shankaracharya and Krishna ("मैंने नहीं"). (middle) [«तो माँ भी बनना है»]
10. प्रेम is always equal from both sides, never one-sided. This holds whether it is girlfriend and boyfriend, husband and wife, mother and son, oneself and the nation, or even a dog. If it is not equal it is by default not प्रेम, because it works like an "equal and opposite reaction". In प्रेम "एक, एक बन जाता है, दो रहता ही नहीं". He will teach this fully with Radha-Krishna and the Narada Bhakti Sutra. (middle) [«प्रेम अगर होगा तो दोनों»]
11. A mother who says she loves her son while the son will not come, meet or understand has माँ का मोह, not प्रेम. Putra-moha was Dhritarashtra's problem with Duryodhana. Because of मोह, justice is not done, the duty is not done properly, and one cannot even scold ("चिल्ला नहीं पाओगे"). (middle)
12. Slide: do not drown in grief when a loved one leaves, because they were a सहयात्री, not a चिरसंगी. No one was going to stay forever: "One day, either the world will leave you or you will have to leave the world." (middle)
13. Shloka 42 (first half): the other world is neither seen nor seen before. Few have seen स्वर्ग or परलोक, so why do पुण्य for something unseen? Do it first for your own आनंद: "Charity begins with self, should not end with self, for the world." (middle) [«कितने जनों ने देखा है»]
14. Shloka 42 (second half): someone who wants his own कल्याण should not break the commands of scripture and should believe them. You have not seen it yourself, but someone has (अदृष्ट). Nobody has seen atoms, molecules or electricity either, yet we believe in them and take degrees in them without calling it अंधश्रद्धा. We know electricity by its फलश्रुति. (middle) [«खुद तो देखा नहीं है»]
15. In the same way the आगम शास्त्र report what the guru has seen and experienced about God, prārabdha, काल and ज्योतिष. The belief should rest on results, not अंधश्रद्धा: "Light लगनी चाहिए, तो electricity बोलूँगा." He says that denying results such as साधना, healing and a current felt in the hands is foolish. (middle) [«तो वैसे ही बोलते हैं»]
16. (Restated after the slides return) Even without seeing परलोक, one should trust the scriptures and not break their commands. (middle)
17. The वारकरी story (see Stories). We cannot see our own कर्म or the truth of scripture either, because they are अदृष्ट, so we should not break scripture's command. Krishna says the same in chapter 16. "विश्वास करना पहले सीखना होगा, and then शरणागति." (middle-late)
18. Slide: "प्रत्यक्ष अनुभव का अभाव सत्य का अभाव नहीं होता." We cannot see the wind, but a leaf moves, so inference is sometimes enough. Thousands have gone through साढ़ेसाती, so I should accept that it can happen to me, not that it surely will. If someone did पुरुषार्थ and things went well, I can too; a guru showed it can be done. "Belief दोनों तरफ से आना चाहिए." (late) [«कभी-कभी we don't see»]
19. Slides: the soul, परलोक and rebirth are known through श्रद्धा, शास्त्र and साधना. Rejecting them with only logic and the senses is incomplete knowledge, and so is rejecting them only because science does not say so. (late)
20. Slide: "बुद्धि वहाँ रुकती है, जहाँ श्रद्धा शुरू होती है." Hence "चप्पल, बुद्धि और अहंकार बाहर रखके आओ". (late)
21. Gita 16.22–24: someone free of काम, क्रोध and लोभ reaches परमगति. Someone who drops शास्त्रविधि gets neither सिद्धि, nor परमगति, nor even सुख. You may not want सिद्धि or मोक्ष, but you did want सुख, and that will not come either. Scripture is the authority for what to do and what not to do, and "शास्त्र कोई गुरु ही लिखते हैं". Do only the action ordained by scripture. (late) [«भगवद्गीता सोलहवाँ अध्याय»]
22. Ashma Rishi "certifies" exactly what Krishna says. When Vyasa told Ashma's story, Krishna was sitting nearby, and later it was Krishna who began teaching Yudhishthira. (late) [«अश्मा ऋषि ने क्या certify»]
23. Example: the tree is present in the seed. The unseen tree and even a whole forest are in the seed, yet we plant and tend it with full faith because botany or someone told us so. Scriptural faith should be like that. Rebirth and the next life are beyond the senses but proven by शास्त्र-प्रमाण. "Science पे अभी तक बहुत विश्वास किया, थोड़ा यहाँ क्यों नहीं करते?" Krishna taught this too. (late-end) [«तो बीज में forest दिखता»]

## Other scripture cited
- "यथेच्छसि तथा कुरु" (early): part of Gita 18.63. "यथा योग्यम् तथा कुरु" seems to be his own addition. Supports the guru giving freedom without मोह.
- Gita 16.22 (late): someone freed from the three gates to hell (he names काम, क्रोध, लोभ) reaches परमगति. Read in Hindi only.
- Gita 16.23 (late): whoever drops शास्त्रविधि gets neither सिद्धि, परमगति nor सुख. Read in Hindi.
- Gita 16.24 (late): scripture is the authority for कर्तव्य and अकर्तव्य. Read in Hindi. Supports obeying scripture. The Hindi was checked against Gita Press; no Sanskrit was read.
- The nanny teaching (middle): he credits it to Shankaracharya and Krishna, without a verse or reference.
- Narada Bhakti Sutra and Radha-Krishna (middle): only mentioned as future topics for प्रेम being two-sided.
- Tukaram (तुकोबा) and Jnaneshwar (ज्ञानोबा) (middle-late): named in the story as those who "saw" Vitthal. No verses.

## Stories, examples and images
- Saturn moving through the houses: trouble with mother, partner, children, servants, husband or boss (start).
- Co-passengers on a journey as the image for relatives (early).
- An employee changing bosses, a girlfriend marrying someone else, divorce and second marriage (early).
- A participant sleeping in class (early). Teachers used to throw chalk; now he can point a laser pointer and say "उठो", or tell the neighbour "पानी डालो". The sleeper may hear the recording three years later and say "मैंने तो सब कुछ attend किया था". Said humorously.
- The nanny (दाई माँ) who serves fully and has no tension after she leaves (middle).
- A mother who claims love but will not give the son property, and the son will not come to meet her (middle).
- Dhritarashtra's putra-moha for Duryodhana (middle).
- Electricity, atoms and electrons are unseen but believed (middle). He jokes: "पैर गीले करके आ जाओ, electric में हाथ डाल दो" to experience it. Light as the proof of electricity.
- During साधना something like an electric current comes into the hands, and sceptics call it hypnotism (middle).
- **The वारकरी story** (middle-late, "मैं हमेशा बोलता रहता हूँ") [«एक वारकरी था, old person»]. A 75–80-year-old वारकरी carries his 5-year-old grandson on his shoulders to Pandharpur. He has gone since his own grandfather carried him the same way, yet he has never seen Vitthal. His answer: when one dog sees a thief and barks, fifteen or twenty dogs in other lanes bark too, out of loyalty and trust. He likewise chants because Tukoba, Jnanoba and the वारकरी saints saw Vitthal.
- Wind is unseen, but the leaf moves (late).
- Thousands have gone through साढ़ेसाती; others did पुरुषार्थ and things improved (late).
- "चप्पल, बुद्धि और अहंकार बाहर रखके आओ" (late).
- The seed holds the tree and the forest; botany tells us so (late-end).

## Key terms
- **शनि / साढ़ेसाती:** साढ़ेसाती is Saturn passing over the Moon, which is the mind, so it hurts what the mind holds dear.
- **चंद्र:** "मेरा मन".
- **भवचक्र / संसार चक्र:** "भव मतलब ही संसार".
- **अनित्य:** not lasting.
- **प्रारब्ध:** what decides relationships and how much knowledge a person receives.
- **वैराग्य:** comes easily once you understand that relationships do not last.
- **मोह vs प्रेम:** प्रेम is equal from both sides and makes two into one; anything one-sided is मोह.
- **दायित्व:** duty.
- **दाई माँ:** the nanny.
- **सहयात्री vs चिरसंगी:** a fellow traveller vs a lifelong companion.
- **परलोक, स्वर्ग:** the unseen other world.
- **अदृष्ट:** the unseen (one's own कर्म, scripture's truths).
- **फलश्रुति:** visible results.
- **अंधश्रद्धा:** blind faith, as opposed to faith based on results.
- **आगम शास्त्र:** what gurus have seen and experienced.
- **शास्त्रविधि:** "ordained" by scripture; the highest thing scripture lays down.
- **शरणागति:** comes after विश्वास.
- **श्रद्धा:** begins where बुद्धि stops.
- **अव्यक्त:** the tree hidden in the seed.
- **शास्त्र-प्रमाण:** proof from scripture.
- **पुरुषार्थ:** effort that can improve bad periods.
- **वारकरी / वारी:** the Pandharpur pilgrim and pilgrimage.
