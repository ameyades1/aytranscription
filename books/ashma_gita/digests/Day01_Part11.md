## Teachings in order
1. (start) A person should keep doing श्रेष्ठ कर्म according to his विद्या and बुद्धि (Chanakya Niti 4.1 slide). श्रेष्ठ कर्म is learned from a guru. It is the same as श्रेय, as opposed to प्रेय [«अब श्रेष्ठ कर्म कहाँ से»].
2. (start) Karma done with संकल्प can change even things that seem fixed. Lifespan (आयु), wealth and much else can change [«आयु भी बढ़ सकती है»].
3. (early) Chanakya Niti 13.13: no one gets every wish of the mind fulfilled (सभी = 100%), because everything depends on दैव. So one should take up संतोष.
4. (early) Sometimes "we have to accept". Even पुरुषार्थ will not change some things, and then accepting and going ahead is right. No उपाय is needed; what is there has to be endured [«तो कई बार चाणक्य जी»].
5. (early) Ramana Maharshi's statement (what is destined not to happen will not happen, and the reverse; "the best course is to remain silent") is, he says, the सार of the whole Ashma Gita.
6. (early) When everything has been tried (like an operation), some periods have to be borne quietly, and some relationships have to be understood as they are, and one goes on. "न कुछ कर सकते, न छोड़ सकते" [«उतना part चुपचाप सहन»].
7. (early) Clarification: this does not mean destiny can never change. It means situations will come where you cannot change it every time.
8. (early) If India becoming विश्वगुरु is in प्रारब्ध, it will happen whatever other religions or negative people do. The audience has some role in it, or they would not be sitting here (applause) [«तो विश्वगुरु अपना होना»].
9. (early–middle) The nature of relationships (love, marriage, friendship): each gives joy and also tension. Whoever wants only सुख is in भ्रम, and whoever sees both equally is ज्ञानी. If one comes, the other has come too.
10. (middle) The reason: all three गुण are in everyone (wife, me, boss). No state lasts a whole life. "Only thing that is constant is change." Change = प्रकृति, and I myself change too (age, दशा, nature, बुद्धि).
11. (middle) Spouses will change ("पहले तू ऐसी थी"). Since you married, accept the person as they are, with their likes and dislikes.
12. (middle) Conflict comes when one of a matched pair (parent and child, husband and wife) suddenly rises in अध्यात्म. The other no longer feels the person belongs to the same world [«एक अध्यात्म में ऊपर»].
13. (middle) The same holds for राजा and प्रजा. A sudden sāttvik turn by either side alienates the other. "Non-stop change होगा."
14. (middle) Change has to be accepted, and सुख-दुःख will come. दशा changes with काल. Whoever understands both games goes on. After every night there is a morning, so neither दुःख nor सुख is permanent. This is what Ashma is telling Janaka.
15. (middle) Ashma Gita 5 (recap of the previous verse): सुख and दुःख attach themselves to a person from birth.
16. (middle) Ashma Gita 6: at any moment one of the two is present. There is no third state. आनंद is being the witness, but a witness of either सुख or दुःख. Accept that one of them is here now, and the other will come soon; it is a cycle [«पहले दोनों के साथ»].
17. (middle) The navagraha दशा cycle shows this design: good, mild and harsh periods alternate. "This is the rule", and whoever understands it is a विद्वान.
18. (middle) Applying it to guru and disciples: dependants are "शनि". A guru in a high दशा who traps people will face their time later (case, jail, defamation). So, out of पुरुषार्थ today, I make sure not to trap anyone and to do only what is धर्मयुक्त [«तो आज मुझे पता»].
19. (middle) The same applies to the wife (also "शनि"): don't fight too much. This is "not out of the fear"; her time will come, and he will have to listen and hand over. "एक, अभी सुख या दुःख तो होगा ही होगा."
20. (middle–late) The rest of verse 6: whichever comes, सुख or दुःख, carries off one's ज्ञान as fast as wind carries a cloud. However much आत्मज्ञान one has, getting stuck in a lottery win or a betrayal makes one forget it in a moment ("सब में भगवान है, इसमें नहीं") [«मतलब हवा से बादल»].
21. (late) तत्त्वज्ञान cannot be taught to the अति सुखी, अति ज्ञानी, अति रोगी or अति दुःखी [«इसलिए बोला जाता है, अति»].
22. (late) Slide meaning: it is not only दुःख that unsettles us; सुख also puts us in भ्रम ("मैं सबका राजा हूँ"). Both take away आत्मिक चेतना unless we are careful. Be सावधान in both.
23. (late) Both the very happy (celebrity) and the very miserable (beggar, the chronically ill) are भोग योनि and do not attain आत्मज्ञान. The celebrity has no time for सत्संग. Exhausting the desire for karma and pleasure, the celebrity will meet दुःख before death and say "नहीं चाहिए celebrity life".
24. (late) The beggar is living out past पाप. He chants God's names more than saints do, and will one day turn away from that state too. Neither is in a position to receive ज्ञान. He is not calling either one good or bad: "ज्ञान हर लेगा, साक्षी रहो."
25. (late) Slide: the verse points to the ideal of the स्थितप्रज्ञ, who keeps awareness in any situation.
26. (late) Gita 8.15 slide (apparently shown earlier, "ये वो ही था न"): great souls who reach Him are not reborn into the house of sorrows.
27. (end, recap of the day) Take-aways, partly from participants: sometimes पुरुषार्थ won't work; some periods must be waited out; neither सुख nor दुःख is permanent; ज्ञान should not go in either; don't become an extreme celebrity, or reach beggary, without a guru; in संकल्प सिद्धि, the bigger the संकल्प, the more people are involved and the more कर्म comes in between; whoever can accept change is not सुखी but आनंदी; संतोष; be silent when a situation simply has to be faced; fight like Yudhishthira so it remains धर्मयुद्ध; a guru has a lot of patience, a disciple less.

## Main-text shlokas covered
- **Ashma Gita 5** (slide number): «उत्पन्नमिममात्मानं नरस्यानन्तरं ततः। तानि तान्यनुवर्तन्ते दुःखानि च सुखानि च॥». Meaning: when the body is born, सुख and दुःख follow it. Commentary: brief (recap, shown as background for verse 6). Restored per Gita Press from garbled «उत्पत्रिमिम». It is unclear whether he recited the verse itself.
- **Ashma Gita 6**: «तेषामन्यतरापत्तौ यद् यदेवोपपद्यते। तदस्य चेतनामाशु हरत्यभ्रमिवानिलः॥». Meaning: one of the two is always present, and whichever comes carries off one's ज्ञान as wind carries a cloud. Commentary: long (the दशा cycle, the wind and cloud image, the अति list, भोग योनि, स्थितप्रज्ञ). Restored from «यद्देवोपपद्यते». It is unclear whether he recited the verse itself.

## Key terms
- **श्रेष्ठ कर्म / श्रेय vs प्रेय**: excellent action, learned from a guru; श्रेष्ठ = श्रेय.
- **संकल्प / संकल्प सिद्धि**: resolve; karma done with it can change lifespan and wealth. A bigger संकल्प involves more people and more कर्म.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: self-effort; sometimes it won't work.
- **प्रारब्ध / दैव / भाग्य / destiny**: what is destined; not always changeable, but not never changeable.
- **संतोष**: contentment, taken up because wishes depend on दैव.
- **उपाय**: remedy; sometimes none is needed, only endurance.
- **गुण (तीनों)**: present in everyone, hence change.
- **प्रकृति**: = change; always changing.
- **दशा / नवग्रह / पत्रिका / उच्च की दशा**: astrological periods, designed to alternate between good and harsh.
- **शुभ, अशुभ, क्रूर ग्रह**: planet types in the cycle.
- **शनि**: stands for dependants and disciples, also the wife.
- **आनंद / आनंदी**: being the witness; distinguished from सुखी.
- **साक्षी / witness**: but always a witness of either सुख or दुःख.
- **तत्त्वज्ञान, आत्मज्ञान, चेतना**: what सुख or दुःख carries away.
- **भोग योनि**: a life for experiencing results; both the celebrity and the beggar or chronically ill are this, and cannot gain आत्मज्ञान.
- **नाम-स्मरण**: chanting God's name.
- **स्थितप्रज्ञ**: one who keeps awareness in every situation.
- **विश्वगुरु**: India's destined role (his usage).
- **धर्मयुद्ध**: a fight conducted like Yudhishthira's.
- **नाड़ी शास्त्र, श्री विद्या**: mentioned in jokes.
