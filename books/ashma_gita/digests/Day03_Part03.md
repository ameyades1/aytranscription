## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** Starts partway through the अर्थ slide of a shlok whose number is not given (probably shlok 19, from the previous part): «देहावसान: शरीर का अन्त होना». The recording seems to begin in the middle of the previous discussion.
- **Closing:** Ends during the commentary on the shlok 22 examples. The last thought is complete: ज्योतिष as a torch that shows the खड्डा, and choosing how to deal with it is the new पुरुषार्थ or उपाय. No closing words, so the session is probably cut off here.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) The slide says this shlok breaks the ego's illusion of control ("मैं अपने जीवन का मालिक हूँ"). He corrects it: everything cannot change, only something can. There is a restriction, a limitation.
2. (start, recap) Who your boss or spouse will be, and the अर्थ and काम पुरुषार्थ, are fixed. Your profession is set by the previous birth. The patients who come to you are the ones you have a ऋणानुबंध with [«Dentist बनना है तो»].
3. (start) Money also follows the previous-birth संकल्प. If you resolved to serve everyone or to only give (no श्री विद्या principle), money will come and go, even while a neighbour in the same trade earns crores.
4. (early) Prarabdha's money stays pending even if you now serve निस्वार्थ. It will come from another angle, such as a government reward or donors, and can grow because श्री विद्या is now फलीभूत [«एक दिन अचानक सरकार»].
5. (early) Negative new पुरुषार्थ, such as wrong treatment, overcharging or selling organs, earns more than the prarabdha allowed. The extra goes back ब्याज सहित (he refers to Acharya Chanakya, from an earlier session) through jail, fines, a lost licence or damage [«ब्याज सहित जाएगा»].
6. (early) "पत्रिका अगर समझ ली होती तो limit में रहते तुम." Knowing प्रारब्ध matters just as much.
7. (early) The formula: do पुरुषार्थ to increase सुख and reduce दुःख, and finally reach मुक्ति by accepting in आनंद अवस्था.
8. (early) Even avatars do not change their own course ("वो अपना नहीं बदलते").
9. (early) The slide says पुरुषार्थ is necessary. No guru will tell you not to do पुरुषार्थ. Its result sprouts only on "प्रारब्ध की धरती". It circles around the prarabdha and does not change things abruptly.
10. (early) Example slide: birth and death are not in our hands. A poor child becomes an industrialist, and a royal child becomes a sannyasi (Buddha). "जिसको जाना है वो भाग के जाएगा."
11. (early-middle) Profit and loss cannot be controlled either. This is कर्म रहस्य. Only the guru knows the theory, and even he knows it case by case, through meditation, पत्रिका, hand lines or intuition. He has access to your संस्कार.
12. (middle) "Why me?" has no answer. An उत्तम पुरुष like Nachiketa understands that even such a result is for the good, for example that some karmas are cut. Others sit and cry.
13. (middle, recap) Yudhishthira had शोक, "युद्ध में स्थिर नहीं था". You study Ashma Gita to be steady in your own युद्ध. The उत्तम पुरुष learns from others' mistakes, those of Yudhishthira, Arjuna and Janaka.
14. (middle) Shlok 20: शब्द, स्पर्श, रूप, रस, गंध come and go by nature. He illustrates each one (see Stories).
15. (middle) These are the तन्मात्राएँ of the पंच महाभूत. Your prarabdha is made from their combinations. So today insults, tomorrow peace, the day after praise. If my सुख-दुःख depends on them, I keep getting whatever comes प्रारब्धानुसार.
16. (middle) The secret: prarabdha was based on my मन, on a कल्पना I made earlier. Change that कल्पना and everything changes automatically. Understanding the रहस्य of one's karma is enough.
17. (middle) Shlok 21: प्राणी means one with प्राण, including humans. It is not "animal", and not पशु (पशु means one bound in पाश). "Prani is not animal."
18. (middle) A second aspect after prarabdha (part 1) is काल (part 2): when. Wealth destined after age 48 will not come at 30, however good you are.
19. (middle) The dream is not wrong, but it bears fruit only in its time. The seed must sprout, then needs manure and water, then the season. What you sowed is prarabdha, and when it fruits is time.
20. (middle) Sitting, sleeping, moving, rising, eating and drinking: sometimes a sofa, sometimes the floor. All happen नियत by time.
21. (middle) नियत means "ordained": the highest order, like a Supreme Court ruling, विधि का विधान. Hence नियति, which no one can challenge.
22. (middle) पुरुषार्थ cannot bring forward what is fixed for a given year. You can create something new (today's श्री विद्या), but what was due comes at its time.
23. (middle-late) Shlok 22 begins the examples of नियति's play. Even a वैद्य falls ill. Healers receive हलाहल विष (others' karma). Heart specialists die of heart attacks and orthopaedists get joint problems. Sadhana is needed to cut this karma.
24. (late) Even the guru receives हलाहल विष (नीलकंठ). Selfless service does not exempt you: cleaning a gutter unpaid still spoils your feet. Being a vaidya, or having knowledge, does not exempt you.
25. (late) The strong become weak (someone stronger appears). The wealthy become helpless (no card, pickpocket, kidnap).
26. (late) "यह समय का उलट-फेर बड़ा अद्भुत है": राजा का रंक, रंक का राजा. Don't think too much about why.
27. (late) Read your पत्रिका early, in childhood, not afterwards. If it shows a heart attack, reduce anger and hectic running now and find the root cause in your habits. At 55 you cannot go back to 25.
28. (late) The रहस्य starts long before. It comes from the previous birth, and this birth's karma gives फलश्रुति after 30 to 40 years. "Same thing will not continue forever."
29. (late) A healthy athlete gets paralysis while a lifelong drinker lives to 90 to 95. This is no certification for alcohol ("उसी का lifestyle correct था"). Accept that it can happen and is the fruit of your own karma.
30. (late) Check in advance what will come: the medicine chapter of the पत्रिका, नाड़ी ज्योतिष via the guru, hand lines. Knowing the future is not bad.
31. (end) ज्योतिष is a वेदांग, the "eyes of Veda". ज्योति + ईश, "lord of the light", a torch. It shows the खड्डा but does not decide whether you fall in. Bridging it, going around or stopping is your choice. That new पुरुषार्थ is called उपाय.

## Key terms
- **प्रारब्ध**: fate fixed by the previous birth (part 1). It is based on the मन's earlier कल्पना.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: effort, which can be new, positive or negative. It acts only within prarabdha.
- **अर्थ / काम पुरुषार्थ**: in this session, fixed matters such as boss, spouse and money.
- **ऋणानुबंध**: a karmic debt-bond that decides who comes to you.
- **श्री विद्या (principle)**: the principle of receiving wealth or abundance. It can bear fruit and add to prarabdha.
- **निस्वार्थ**: selfless.
- **कर्म रहस्य**: the mystery of karma. The guru knows the theory and the individual case.
- **उत्तम पुरुष**: one who learns from others' mistakes.
- **तन्मात्राएँ**: शब्द, स्पर्श, रूप, रस, गंध, the subtle elements of the पंच महाभूत.
- **पंच महाभूत**: अग्नि, पृथ्वी, वायु, जल, आकाश.
- **प्राणी**: one with प्राण; "not animal".
- **पशु**: one bound in पाश.
- **काल**: time, the second aspect.
- **नियत / नियति**: ordained, the highest order, विधि का विधान, which cannot be challenged.
- **हलाहल विष**: others' karma absorbed by healers and gurus.
- **नीलकंठ**: Shiva, who holds the हलाहल.
- **पत्रिका**: horoscope.
- **नाड़ी ज्योतिष**: an astrology method, done through the guru.
- **ज्योतिष**: ज्योति + ईश, "lord of the light"; the eyes of Veda.
- **वेदांग**: limb of the Veda.
- **उपाय**: remedy; the new पुरुषार्थ chosen after seeing the future.
- **फलीभूत / फलश्रुति**: bearing fruit.
- **मुक्ति / आनंद अवस्था**: the final stage of acceptance.
