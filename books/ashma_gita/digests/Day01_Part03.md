## Opening and closing
**Opening:** The recording starts partway through Gita 1.1 («किमकुर्वत सञ्जय»). He is giving the Bhagavad Gita background that leads into Ashma Gita, starting with the "mine vs. Pandu's sons" split.
**Closing:** It is cut off mid-thought. He is explaining why Vyasa chose the Ashma–Janaka story for Yudhishthira and stops at «हो सकता है ना, इन लोगों के मरने का time आ चुका होगा». He has not yet started the Ashma–Janaka dialogue itself.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) In Gita 1.1, Dhritarashtra asks "किमकुर्वत", what did they do. Even in the first verse he separates "my sons" from "Pandu's sons" (मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव). He does not ask what is happening in "our family" [«किमकुर्वत सञ्जय, उन्होंने क्या किया»].
2. (start) This separation is where the problem of the whole Mahabharata begins: «उधर से ही problem चालू होता है, पूरे महाभारत का». He asks the audience to read the first two words of the sentence.
3. (early) Paradox: if Kurukshetra is a धर्मक्षेत्र, why was there a war there? And if war happened there, why is it called धर्मक्षेत्र? [«अगर वो धर्मक्षेत्र है तो वहाँ युद्ध क्यों हुआ»]
4. (early) A place with fighting every day is not a temple or धर्मक्षेत्र. Occasional scolding for discipline is a different matter.
5. (early) Meaning: धर्म = कर्तव्य कर्म, कुरु = करना. Kurukshetra is your कार्यक्षेत्र, your workplace, and dharma has to be done right there.
6. (early) He criticises splitting धर्म from कर्म: "we'll come to the temple after retirement, after sixty, after वानप्रस्थ". He calls this «यही पागलपन है». Both must be done «in and through everything». Your workplace itself is the place of your dharma. Phrases: "Work is worship", "customer is God, customer is King". Not doing this is the source of the problem "in the first place".
7. (early-middle) क्षेत्र, explained "in the 13th chapter" (of the Gita): क्षेत्र = खेती, a field where seed is sown and grain comes up. The body is a field. [«तेरहवें अध्याय में उसने बोला है, क्षेत्र मतलब एक खेती»]
8. (early-middle) There are three bodies, mapped onto the koshas: अन्नमय कोश (gross body); the subtle body (प्राणमय, मनोमय); and beyond them ज्ञानमय and आनंदमय, which he calls the "super causal body", कारण शरीर. Through all three we sow seeds of कर्म and संस्कार.
9. (middle) Fruit comes only at the right time, not at the press of a button (mango-seed analogy, see Stories). He mentions साढ़ेसाती and राहु की दशा as such times. The same holds for good deeds: a gift given today does not bring money tomorrow.
10. (middle) So you have to wait for the time; without patience nothing happens. Example: Antar Yog does not become world-famous or "Vishwa Guru" right after it starts.
11. (middle) We fail to live "धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे" because we sit doing अपना-पराया. Suffering and कष्ट begin there, whereas we are meant to reach आनंद स्थिति.
12. (middle) Background: Krishna taught the Gita to Arjuna before the war because Arjuna put his bow down; he could not fight his own people.
13. (middle) Arjuna had fought many wars before and was शक्तिशाली. He had earned many weapons, अस्त्र and शस्त्र, through much ध्यान.
14. (middle) शस्त्र = physical weapons (pistol). अस्त्र = a weapon with mantra, guided by the mind, like a "guided missile", "computer-directed"; hence ब्रह्मास्त्र.
15. (middle) Return to Kurukshetra "for me": the मन, बुद्धि and अहंकार are the field. How I use them matters. If I do अपना-पराया there, पाप-पुण्य keeps being produced.
16. (middle) Dakshina example (see Stories). Teaching out of the wish to teach, यथाशक्ति, brings less pain. «शास्त्र बोलता है, passion वाला ही काम करो, अपनी choice वाला करो, गुरु से पूछकर करो, तो दुःख-दर्द कम आएगा».
17. (middle) Gita chapter 1 is "अर्जुनविषादयोग": Krishna showed how to make योग out of Arjuna's sorrow. Arjuna's dilemma was how to fight Bhishma (grandfather), Drona, and his cousin Duryodhana.
18. (middle) Krishna prepared Arjuna for the "world war", the Mahabharata war. Yudhishthira, "called Dharmaraj", also fought, but had not heard the Gita first and thought he knew everything. Only Arjuna had विषाद; the other four brothers had none.
19. (middle) Ashma Gita background begins here [«अब यहाँ से अश्मा गीता का background»]. The other brothers fought out of द्वेष over Draupadi's वस्त्रहरण and the like ("चलो, करना है").
20. (middle) After the victory Yudhishthira should automatically have become king. Ashma Gita starts here, after the war: Yudhishthira now falls into Arjuna's earlier state. He has पश्चात बुद्धि (hindsight) that he, the truthful धर्मराज, did so much छल-कपट.
21. (middle-late) His remorse over Bhishma: Bhishma raised him, and he ordered Arjuna to shoot. Bhishma had vowed not to shoot at the Pandavs and would not strike a woman, so they brought Shikhandi in front, on Krishna's word. «छल किया… ये अधर्म मेरे द्वारा कैसे हुआ?»
22. (late) His remorse over Drona: Drona trusted him and asked about Ashwatthama, and he lied, "नरो वा कुञ्जरो वा". He felt nothing when he said it; the grief comes only after the guru's death.
23. (late) His remorse over Abhimanyu, sent into the चक्रव्यूह for strategy, and over Draupadi's five sons, killed by छल. "Because of me… युद्ध का decision मेरा था".
24. (late) His remorse over Duryodhana and all the cousins; "now everything feels शांति-शांति" (empty). See the quarrelling-couple analogy below. He asks which state was better for hearing the Gita, Arjuna's (before) or Yudhishthira's (after).
25. (late) Yudhishthira also recalls Jayadratha: Krishna said "शाम हो गई" and Yudhishthira "certified" it. Guilt: «मैं धर्मराज हूँ ही नहीं, मैं पापी हूँ».
26. (late) Yudhishthira wants प्रायश्चित्त: go to the Himalayas, or sit in अनशन and give up food, giving up his life for the Kauravas, "आत्महत्या जैसे". This parallels Arjuna's earlier talk of the Himalayas and संन्यास.
27. (late) The seniors present were Krishna and Vyasa. Vyasa comes and tells Yudhishthira: you must take the throne, your राज्याभिषेक must happen, it is your right, «तुमने कुछ भी गलत नहीं किया है». He defines शोक as "उल्टे-सीधे सब विचार".
28. (late) Vyasa explains this through Ashma Gita: the story Ashma Rishi told King Janaka of Mithila. Vyasa retells that dialogue to Yudhishthira. Even after much explaining, Yudhishthira did not understand. He says "we will come into this very soon."
29. (late) The book cover subtitle: «मनुष्य जीवन के प्रारब्ध और काल की भूमिका स्पष्ट करने वाला प्राचीन उपाख्यान». काल = a certain दशा must come. Good and bad karma both bear fruit at the right time (tree callback).
30. (late) Examples of time: becoming king, but not at age two; marriage, not at age eight (he clarifies he means the present age, not old child marriage). Both काल and प्रारब्ध matter.
31. (end) Meaning of अश्मा: stone, but in the sense of "rock solid", still, a piece of mountain rock. One who has तत्वज्ञान is also called अश्मा: "rock solid foundation", fundamentally and spiritually strong, «मन से एकदम मज़बूत», able to give knowledge to others.
32. (end) Ashma taught Janaka about प्रारब्ध and काल. Vyasa chose this story for Yudhishthira to suggest that perhaps those people's time to die had already come (cut off here).

## Stories, examples and images
- (early) Temple with fighting every day: that is a court or गुंडागिरी, not a धर्मक्षेत्र. Contrast: a mother scolding a child.
- (early) "Retire, then religion": the गृहस्थाश्रम-now, धर्म-after-sixty attitude, «यही पागलपन है».
- (early) "Work is worship; customer is God / King."
- (early-middle) Mango seed: it must be sown, watered and manured, and even then gives fruit only in May, not December. A seed in the pocket or a piece of wood gives nothing.
- (middle) Astrology images: साढ़ेसाती, राहु की दशा, as the "right time" of fruit.
- (middle) The obscure "श्रीविद्या फलीभूत" remark: today's दान does not bring money tomorrow (the wording is unclear).
- (middle) **Antar Yog:** starting the संस्था does not make it world-famous or "Vishwa Guru" at once; it needs work, sabhas and meeting gurus, and it takes time.
- (middle) अस्त्र vs शस्त्र: guided missile vs pistol; ब्रह्मास्त्र.
- (middle) Dakshina: "he didn't give that much dakshina", so pain follows. If I teach because I want to, whether two or two hundred come, there is less pain. (Apparently about his own teaching.)
- (middle-late) Mahabharata episodes: Draupadi's वस्त्रहरण; Bhishma on the bed of arrows; Shikhandi; Drona and "नरो वा कुञ्जरो वा"; Abhimanyu in the चक्रव्यूह; Draupadi's five sons; Jayadratha and "शाम हो गई"; Duryodhana and the cousins.
- (late) Quarrelling couple: the husband suddenly dies, and the wife feels "at least that quarrelling one should have been there". Yudhishthira feels the same about the cousins.
- (late) King at age two, marriage at eight: things happen only at their time.
- (end) Name "धोंडू-बिंडू" (Marathi names meaning stone): a stone-like name for a person, but अश्मा means rock solid.

## Key terms
- **धर्मक्षेत्र / कुरुक्षेत्र:** धर्म = कर्तव्य कर्म; कुरु = करना; the workplace where dharma must be done.
- **क्षेत्र:** खेती, a field; the body, mind and intellect, where seeds of karma and संस्कार are sown.
- **अन्नमय, प्राणमय, मनोमय, ज्ञानमय, आनंदमय कोश; कारण शरीर:** the three bodies; he calls कारण शरीर the "super causal body".
- **संस्कार:** seeds that bear fruit later.
- **अस्त्र / शस्त्र:** mantra-guided weapon vs physical weapon.
- **अपना-पराया:** the mine-vs-theirs division, the source of suffering and of पाप-पुण्य.
- **विषाद / अर्जुनविषादयोग:** sorrow, turned into yoga.
- **पश्चात बुद्धि:** remorse after the fact.
- **प्रायश्चित्त:** atonement (Yudhishthira's plan: अनशन, the Himalayas, giving up his life).
- **शोक:** "उल्टे-सीधे सब विचार".
- **छल-कपट:** deceit.
- **प्रारब्ध, काल:** destiny, and the necessary time or दशा for fruit.
- **अश्मा:** stone, meaning rock solid; one settled in तत्वज्ञान.
- **तत्वज्ञान:** knowledge of reality.
- **यथाशक्ति:** according to capacity.
- **दक्षिणा:** offering to the teacher.
- **साढ़ेसाती, राहु की दशा:** astrological periods, used as images of timing.
