## Teachings in order
1. (recap, presumably) (start) People excuse themselves by citing great figures: "Vishvamitra fell for Menaka and had no control over his anger, so what chance do I have?" But Vishvamitra later became a ऋषि, so "show your own effort" (तुम्हारा प्रयत्न तो दिखाओ). `[«विश्वामित्र वगैरह की कथा»]`
2. (start) People misuse Krishna's example ("वो करे तो रासलीला, मैं करूँ तो character ढीला"). They take knowledge from Krishna but apply it to justify themselves. Krishna never did it for that purpose.
3. (start) People do the same with saints: "Swami Samarth and Gajanan Maharaj took a चिलम, so my cigarette is no big deal." They read the knowledge and the life story (चरित्र) only "to certify this." Even a ज्ञानी sometimes feels pushed toward the same पाप, and that should not be you. `[«स्वामी समर्थ, अपने दूसरे»]`
4. (start) Even after gaining knowledge, especially merely "physical" book knowledge, a person's प्रारब्ध may still show in bad conduct. Examples are पंडा and temple people chewing पान. "That ज्ञान या भक्ति has not stopped him." Only a few of them are truly "हलाहल लेने वाले" (the sentence is incomplete). "ये दुनिया की लीला समझ लेना."
5. (early) Parents worried that a son has gone the wrong way should make the effort (पुरुषार्थ) to bring him back, but should first accept it, since there are many reasons. Wailing ("मेरे पेट में ऐसा कैसा उत्पन्न हुआ?") is pointless. `[«मेरा बेटा अभी गलत मार्ग»]`
6. (early) The पंच महापाप, from a slide: चोरी, सुरापान, ब्रह्महत्या, गुरुपत्नीगमन, and associating with people who commit these. On गुरुपत्नीगमन he adds that a person like this would have no regard for other women either, let alone ब्रह्मचर्य (partly unclear). On the fifth: living among such people brings the same thoughts, or some involvement.
7. (early) Shlok 32: all beings receive इष्ट and अनिष्ट through the influence of काल, and the only cause is अदृष्ट.
8. (early) Learned people are seen sinning, but they can change later. Sthulibhadra learned from "पिंगला वेश्या" and his वासनाएँ left. That knowledge helped him: not from books, but through her. Until a certain time the वासना was there, and then it went. `[«वो स्थूलिभद्र था»]`
9. (early) All of this is काल का प्रभाव. Your son will reform when it is due, perhaps in 2028 or 2032 according to some योग, and until then he must undergo it. Perhaps not even in this life or in seven births, and then one simply has to wait. "Check करो."
10. (recap) (early-middle) Earlier session's three images: the very तामसिक state is like "गर्भ के ऊपर जेर" and must be undergone until its फलश्रुति. The राजसिक state is like "दर्पण के ऊपर मैल," cleared by some पुरुषार्थ. The lightest is "अग्नि और उसका धुआँ," cleared even by a breeze. So it takes some people less time and less पुरुषार्थ, and others more. `[«गर्भ के ऊपर जेर जैसा»]`
11. (middle) Whom to consult: a genuine, समर्थ सद्गुरु, or readings such as नाड़ी, Agastya Muni, or भृगु संहिता from "समाधिस्थ गुरु," or some learned person who knows a method. Understand what they say, then make the right पुरुषार्थ (योग्य पुरुषार्थ). `[«Consult किसको करना होगा»]`
12. (middle) Good and bad keep coming because the world must keep giving सुख-दुःख. The single cause (एकमेव कारण) is अदृष्ट, the invisible. In spirituality what is invisible is my own past कर्म. Even when someone shows it to me, अहंकार won't let me see it.
13. (middle) अहंकार is an opaque black goggle like Gandhari's blindfold: with an ordinary goggle you see something, with this nothing at all. `[«इतना बड़ा goggle, काला»]`
14. (middle) अदृष्ट is the only reason I do or don't get a good person or good wealth, so this is what I must work on.
15. (middle) These shlokas are self-explanatory. He is clicking "next" because there isn't more in them, not to rush. After two days of teaching, the audience should see what Ashma Rishi wants to convey.
16. (recap) (middle) Be like Ashma: अश्मा means stone, rock, "rock solid." Stay firm in spirituality: change what you can by facing it, bear what you must, and move on toward मुक्ति. "ये उसकी गीता का सार था." Yudhishthira had to stay steady through the war and become king, "स्वयं के जीवन का राजा." `[«तो अश्मा जैसे बनो»]`
17. (middle) Shlok 33: who but काल makes and holds the wind, sky, fire, moon, sun, day, night, stars, rivers and mountains? Rivers dry up in summer and tides rise and fall. "Everything is controlled by time."
18. (middle) The sun comes and goes on its own time, and cyclones come at their time. The पंचांग calculates all of it, and two storms won't come together, apart from special cases of "विश्व-कार्य." Everything is pre-decided.
19. (middle) Who decided? "परमेश्वर ने! परमेश्वर कौन था? मैं स्वयं!" I write my own प्रारब्ध. `[«Decide किसने किया»]`
20. (recap) (middle) "Mind cannot understand negativity" (said earlier): thinking "rain should not come" works the same as "rain should come," so it pours either way. Hardly anyone thinks of balance. "आप ही वो रचयिता हो."
21. (middle) The umbrella illustration of layered प्रारब्ध: a pending अदृष्ट from four births brings the rain anyway. Good पुरुषार्थ in this life gets you an umbrella, a little lack of faith makes it flip or fly away, and if the दर्शन was not to be, no number of raincoats helps: the vehicle won't move and the police stop you. "काल का है." `[«आपको वो दर्शन होना ही»]`
22. (late) Once you accept it, any angle of thinking helps: "मैं पुण्यवान हूँ कि मैं बच गया," God saved me, or "मैं पापी हूँ" leading to "where did I go wrong?" Some reflection (चिंतन) will come. (He refers back to something said "a little while ago.")
23. (late) A missed flight has a lesson: be on time, or the गुरु saved me from an accident. Everyone's lesson differs and is understood only later, "सही काल पे ज्ञान आएगा." `[«Flight मेरी miss हो गई»]`
24. (late) Some people learn only after ten misses. Smokers and gutkha eaters read the warnings and carry on; they understand when it actually happens, and then it drops by itself. "Time will be the decider."
25. (late) Since प्रकृति depends on काल, and we are part of प्रकृति (the पंचमहाभूत are in every person), काल decides my प्रारब्ध too. Examples: I need breath, but a cyclone is too much; I need a little fire, not a volcano. `[«we are part of this»]`
26. (late) Slide (अर्थ): human अहंकार is nothing before cosmic forces run by काल. Cloud seeding forces rain, but प्रकृति returns it as प्रलय.
27. (late) Reflecting on this melts अहंकार and gives birth to विनय. Accept "अभी मैं पात्र ही नहीं हूँगा": it isn't the time, I'm not prepared ("be prepared"), or it isn't my task. It is a संकेत, and there is no reason to cry.
28. (late) "We are only a medium (माध्यम)." This is the beginning of ब्रह्मविद्या, which he calls Part I. Part II is the question of who gave me the role: I did, it was my own विधान. When I see that all creation was mine, ब्रह्मविद्या ends. `[«क्या हैं? सिर्फ medium»]`
29. (late) Slide examples: the sun rises and sets on time; fire always goes upward, and a fountain needs a pump; rain falls by gravity. Nature runs by rules, I am part of it, so I too am made to work by some rule.
30. (late) Air always fills empty space: empty a glass and air fills it. Nature is never empty. He also mentions low and high pressure belts (partly unclear).
31. (late) Nobody orders the elements; they follow the eternal laws of काल and सृष्टि. Fire won't become cold by decree, so certain things you must स्वीकार.
32. (end) Three गुण: a तामसिक person will behave तामसिक-ly. Read the शास्त्र and learn to classify; then ब्रह्मविद्या is understood.
33. (end) He reads shlok 34 (seasons, and human सुख-दुःख, come from काल), and the recording ends.

## Key terms
- **अदृष्ट:** invisible; per the slide, what is written in fate. In spirituality, one's own past कर्म, which अहंकार won't let one see.
- **काल:** time, which creates, holds and decides everything, including प्रारब्ध.
- **इष्ट / अनिष्ट:** the desired and the undesired, bringing सुख and दुःख.
- **प्रारब्ध:** destiny written by oneself; it can be pending from past births.
- **पुरुषार्थ:** effort; "योग्य पुरुषार्थ."
- **पंच महापाप:** the five great sins (see slides).
- **अश्मा:** stone, rock, "rock solid."
- **विनय:** humility, born as अहंकार melts.
- **माध्यम:** a medium; the beginning of ब्रह्मविद्या.
- **ब्रह्मविद्या:** begins with "I am only a medium" and ends with "all creation was mine."
- **पंचमहाभूत:** present in every person, linking us to प्रकृति.
- **तामसिक / राजसिक:** levels of obstruction (recap images); behaviour by गुण.
- **फलश्रुति:** the result's coming to fruition.
- **पंचांग:** the calendar that calculates natural events.
- **हलाहल लेने वाले:** those who truly drink poison, like Shiva (implied; the sentence is incomplete).
