## Opening and closing
**Opening:** The session picks up the reading of Ashma Gita with shlok 3, where Janak, grieving, puts his doubt to the Brahmin Ashma. It continues straight on from the earlier introduction to the text.
**Closing:** It is cut off in the middle of the Chanakya Niti 4.1 commentary. He is on the point that a person's first duty is कर्म, meaning कर्तव्य कर्म, meaning धर्म ("धर्म करो उसका"). The recording seems to continue in the next part.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) Shlok 3: grieving Videharaj Janak asks the wise Brahmin Ashma about his doubt. [«एक समय की बात है, दुःख-शोक»]
2. (start) The real ज्ञानी is someone who does not hesitate to ask the guru a question when the time comes, even if he is a king.
3. (start) Following the Prashna Upanishad: do one year of brahmacharya and tapasya, develop shraddha, and only then ask the guru. [«प्रश्न उपनिषद के अनुसार»]
4. (start) When asking, शरणागति, श्रद्धा, विश्वास and आदर towards the guru matter, and the Bhagavad Gita says so too.
5. (early) A true seeker goes to the guru at once when a problem comes and does not sit enduring it or waste time. But he goes with these conditions in place. [«सच्चा ज्ञानी वो है, जिसको problem»]
6. (early) The guru answers through his own ध्यान and तपस्या, not instantly "like a computer, like ChatGPT". If the disciple then does not listen, the guru's tapasya is wasted and the disciple incurs गुरु दोष and गुरु ऋण, because some karma was exchanged. [«वो पूरी तपस्या थी»]
7. (early) "Videha" means he had reached the level of "शरीर मैं नहीं हूँ" (विदेह मुक्ति). Even he had to hear Ashma, so such times can come to anyone. [«विदेह मतलब, विदेह मुक्ति»]
8. (early) If a king of Mithila at that level faced problems, then sorrow will certainly come to ordinary people. [«इस level के मिथिला के राजा»]
9. (early) Gita 4.34 teaches how to ask: go to तत्त्वदर्शी ज्ञानी. Bow first (शरणागति, नम्रता) and serve first; "फुकट में कुछ लेने का नहीं". Then ask simply, without कपट, and not to test whether the guru knows.
10. (early) Gita 18.67 says to whom the teaching must not be given: someone without tapas, without bhakti, without the wish to listen, or who finds fault with the Lord. That includes people who come only wanting "फलश्रुति, healing" at once.
11. (early) Shlok 4: Janak asks how someone who seeks कल्याण should decide when relatives or wealth arise or are lost. [«ब्रह्मन्! कुटुम्बीजन और धन»]
12. (early) Commentary on the question: even a videha-mukta had relatives die or wealth go. Some sorrow comes, if only for milliseconds. कल्याण means मोक्ष, मुक्ति or a higher state. So the question is "how should I react?" once sorrow has come, whether one is a ज्ञानी or not.
13. (early–middle) Slide: this question comes in every human life, whether with sudden loss of a loved one or with wealth gained or lost. It sheds light on the राग-द्वेष द्वन्द्व.
14. (middle) Family separation: should one stay drowned in grief? "नहीं!" One should do पितृ ऋण मुक्ति at once, which is good for the departed and for oneself. [«नहीं! तुरन्त पितृ ऋण मुक्ति»]
15. (middle) Sudden gain or loss of wealth (a lottery, a company closing) unsettles the mind both ways: one through excitement, the other through depression.
16. (middle) Ups and downs of honour: the same family praises you at a function and later speaks against you once your दशा changes. How do you stay steady? (The closing "तो स्थिर ना तुम?" is uncertain in the source.)
17. (middle) Fate is written differently: of twins raised together, one becomes healthy and lucky, the other weak. That is प्रारब्ध. Even one millisecond apart, the लग्न may have changed.
18. (middle) Shlok 5: Ashma says that when the body is born, सुख and दुःख follow it. [«राजन्! मनुष्य का यह शरीर»]
19. (middle) So "there is no escape", and there is no point running around saying "दुःख आया". You were born; recall "दुःखालयमशाश्वतम्" (recap). The four kinds of sorrow were mentioned earlier (recap).
20. (middle) If even 1% दुःख is in a सुख, Krishna, the shastras and Patanjali say it is all दुःख. "Very soon it will convert"; the more enjoyment now, the more sorrow later. [«तो वैसा जो सुख हम ढूँढ रहे»]
21. (middle) Expansion brings complications: enemies, black magic or बुरी नज़र, tax trouble, no proper successor, पुत्रैषणा, वित्तैषणा, लोकैषणा. "All this will come, जितना तुम विस्तार करना चाहोगे."
22. (middle) His father's teaching: "The more you possess, the more complications." Yet scripture also says to think big and keep a विशाल मन, and also that in the end all is मिट्टी. Choosing विशाल brings विशाल संकट; you chose it, and it comes as a सुख-दुःख mixture, not free of cost.
23. (middle) Yoga Sutra 2.15, the four kinds of दुःख, read from slides:
    - **परिणाम दुःख:** pleasure in enjoyment ends in loss (example: sexual pleasure, which ends in loss of bal, virya, tej, smriti).
    - **ताप दुःख:** fear of losing pleasures, jealousy at others having more, and the incompleteness of enjoyment.
24. (middle–late) ताप in daily life: "उसकी कमीज़ मेरी कमीज़ से ज़्यादा सफ़ेद कैसे?" ends in "उसका कफ़न मेरे कफ़न से ज़्यादा सफ़ेद कैसे?" Even पितर keep comparing pind-daan. Gaining something makes rivals in that field.
25. (late) **संस्कार दुःख:** impressions of past pleasures make you weep when the pleasures are lost. They also feed attachment and cause sorrow in later births.
26. (late) Even a beggar can live happily now; the pain comes from remembering past wealth. Shri Ram was happy at the राज्याभिषेक, in वनवास and on return, and "ये स्थिति आनी चाहिए". [«श्रीराम को नहीं लगा वो»]
27. (late) **गुणवृत्ति विरोध:** the gunas' workings conflict. Even in sattvic joy during meditation or satsang, worldly स्फुरण and तन्द्रा interrupt it.
28. (late) For the विवेकी all कर्मविपाक (karma-fruits) are दुःखरूप; what the public calls pleasure, the विवेकी calls sorrow. He does not keep weeping but moves on in साक्षी भाव, watching सुख-दुःख.
29. (late) Chanakya Niti 4.1: आयु, कर्म, वित्त, विद्या and निधन are fixed in the womb. "इसलिए तो उसको पत्रिका बोलते हैं."
30. (late–end) Yet Chanakya does not deny पुरुषार्थ. The root of all this is past-life karma and its संस्कार, which is प्रारब्ध. "प्रारब्ध लॉटरी नहीं है". He is not born only to suffer it out; he can do new karma in this birth. The body and diseases are also results of karma.
31. (end) Shastras say a person's first duty is कर्म. "अज्ञानी का कर्म से मन हटाना मत; ज्ञान देकर कर्म करवाना" (an unattributed reference, probably to the Gita). कर्म means कर्तव्य कर्म, which means धर्म. (Cut off here.)

## Key terms
- **विदेह / विदेह मुक्ति:** having reached the level of "शरीर मैं नहीं हूँ"; free from "I am the body" while in the body.
- **कल्याण:** मोक्ष, मुक्ति, going to a higher state.
- **गुरु दोष, गुरु ऋण:** incurred when the guru's tapasya, spent on answering, is wasted by not listening.
- **शरणागति:** surrender, नमन, नम्रता before the guru.
- **तत्त्वदर्शी:** a ज्ञानी who knows the परमात्म-तत्त्व.
- **पितृ ऋण मुक्ति:** a rite or practice to free oneself from the debt to the departed; good for both sides.
- **प्रारब्ध:** the fruit of past-life karma and संस्कार; "लॉटरी नहीं है".
- **लग्न:** the ascendant, which can change within a millisecond of birth.
- **परिणाम / ताप / संस्कार दुःख, गुणवृत्ति विरोध:** the four kinds of dukha (as defined in the slides above).
- **कर्मविपाक:** कर्मफल.
- **विवेकी:** someone who sees by discernment that all pleasures are dukha.
- **साक्षी भाव:** witness attitude towards sukha-dukha.
- **पुत्रैषणा, वित्तैषणा, लोकैषणा:** desires for sons, wealth and fame, which grow with expansion.
- **पत्रिका:** horoscope, the record of what is fixed at birth.
- **पुरुषार्थ:** effort, not denied by Chanakya.
- **कर्तव्य कर्म = धर्म:** the first duty.
- **फलश्रुति:** promised results; also used for the life-results fixed by प्रारब्ध.
