## Opening and closing
**Opening:** This is the first session of the Shibir. He welcomes everyone to Odisha, next to Jagannath Puri, and starts with the Gita background: karma and bhakti are already being done, but Krishna says ज्ञान is needed, and the ज्ञानी gets moksha [«ओडिशा की पावन भूमि पर»]. He has not started Ashma Gita yet.
**Closing:** The session is cut off mid-topic, during the third of the "four kinds of दुःख" (संस्कार दुःख), right after the पावभाजी and चाँद/गुलाब examples. The fourth दुःख never comes. After that there is one stray slide line, the end of Gita 4.34, which looks out of place.

## Teachings in order
1. (start) Karma and bhakti are already being done. Krishna also requires ज्ञान, and he calls the ज्ञानी the one who reaches moksha.
2. (start) What brings ज्ञान? "श्रद्धावाँल्लभते ज्ञानम्" is the qualification (श्रद्धा), but something must also be *done* to get it. He sends the audience to Gita 4.33 [«ज्ञान किसका परिणाम बोला»].
3. (start) Gita 4.33: all karma ends in ज्ञान, so ज्ञानयज्ञ is far better than द्रव्ययज्ञ. द्रव्यमय means hard work, spending money and any other tangible effort. ज्ञानयज्ञ means satsang, so "spending time over here is far far far superior."
4. (early) Life itself will bring some ज्ञान in the end, often through कटु अनुभव (marriage, fights, divorce). It may be the ज्ञान of unconditional love, or the conclusion "प्रेम होता ही नहीं है". If the lesson comes out backwards, that is अज्ञान [«तीस साल, बीस साल»].
5. (early) If a bitter experience (corruption, then jail) only leads to more karma ("देख लूँगा"), then "that karm has ended again in another karm". The चक्र continues and there is no moksha. Liberation needs ज्ञान.
6. (early) Learning early saves years of life. Examples: learning the purpose of marriage in ब्रह्मचर्य आश्रम, or learning "Honesty is the only policy. Forget the best policy" in Business श्री विद्या.
7. (early) "यावन्मात्र" means it is 100% guaranteed that all karma ends in ज्ञान. Puja, yajna and कर्मकांड are also karma. Their फलश्रुति is that one day you reach a real Guru, because karma alone does not give it.
8. (early) The usual journey: a person first acts from अहंकार and hits a bad phase. They then seek worldly (लौकिक) help from doctors, lawyers and engineers, for health, wealth or relationships. Nobody comes straight to a guru.
9. (early) The next step up is ज्योतिष (a वेदांग). ज्योतिष gives उपाय as an "idea": a shortcut such as oil on Shani, milk on Shiva, offerings to Hanuman, or दूर्वा for Ganapati.
10. (early–middle) The shastra wants ज्ञानयुक्त karma, not just karma: "idea नहीं, ideal बनाना था". The real उपाय is to bring the deity's qualities into oneself: Hanuman's भक्ति and शक्ति, Shiva's वैराग्य, and Ganapati's विवेक बुद्धि and मंगलमूर्ति state. गणाधिपति means leadership qualities, and विनायक means विशेष नायक. "ये होता है असली उपाय" [«हनुमान की तरह मेरे अंदर»].
11. (middle) Rings and टोना-टोटका are the starting point; one should not get stuck there. People cannot jump straight to a PhD; you start at KG. Since we still feel "मैं शरीर हूँ", the path starts with karma.
12. (middle) Mechanical कर्मकांड must grow into the next step: भक्ति, meaning offering with प्रेम. Example: "ए भिखारी, ये ले!".
13. (middle) Even action plus भक्ति lacks ज्ञान, meaning knowing *why* one acts. Marriage, love and children happen for animals too, so what new thing did you do?
14. (middle) Then comes धर्म, meaning one's कर्तव्य कर्म: why, how and for whom one acts. "How you do it makes a difference." After that comes ध्यान. The ladder is कर्म, then भक्ति, then ज्ञान, then ध्यान.
15. (middle) Shri Ram Gita: Ram tells Lakshman that karma is the cause of birth and rebirth, so it cannot be the cause of moksha. Moksha comes only when karma ends (his own paraphrase; no verse quoted).
16. (middle) ज्ञान must be received from a तत्त्वदर्शी ज्ञानी (Gita 4.34). Knowledge does not come at once. First comes साष्टांग नमस्कार and शरणागति, then सेवा ("भक्ति, कर्म दोनों आ गए"), then सरल questions without कपट.
17. (middle) Gita 4.35 and 4.36 are read: the ज्ञानरूप नौका carries even the worst sinner across. Sins go not through flowers or mala counts but through ज्ञान. With no पाप there is no सजा, so karma's purpose is already fulfilled.
18. (middle) Bhakti must also be ज्ञानयुक्त: whose bhakti, and where to surrender. Giving one's whole salary to a temple is wrong, because the wife (अर्धांग) has a right to half and the son also has a share. A guru must be tested to see whether they are true or false [«पूरा का पूरा पगार»].
19. (middle) The Bhagavad Gita is the base today, not the subject: "base से दूसरी गीता में जाना है." This is the first hint of Ashma Gita.
20. (middle) Gita 4.37: ज्ञानरूप अग्नि burns all karma to ashes, the way fire burns fuel, and the more flammable the fuel, the faster. ज्ञान comes through श्रद्धा, but first one must do proper (योग्य) karma such as जप, तप and यज्ञ. You learn either by getting beaten by the world or by the guru's path, and the guru tests you first.
21. (middle–late) Their pilgrimage to Puri (darshan and temples) is bhakti-filled karma. Its फलश्रुति is that they get to hear this teaching today, which will cut sins. Perhaps Jagannath called them here for exactly this.
22. (late) स्थान-माहात्म्य: Puri is one of the सप्तपुरी (with Ayodhya, Mathura and Kashi), a मोक्ष-giving पुण्यनगरी. Its vibrations (स्पंदन) increase the फलश्रुति of hearing, so more may "click" here.
23. (late) Summary of the background: ज्ञानरूप अग्नि burns all karma, so "ज्ञान ही लेने के लिए हम आए हैं". The next questions are which ज्ञान, and which sins to cut.
24. (late) सुख and दुःख are two sides of one coin. The more you seek सुख, the more दुःख comes. Asking Jagannath for that much सुख "translates" to asking for that much दुःख [«जितना मैं सुखी होने»].
25. (late) Patanjali's "four kinds of दुःख". The first is ताप दुःख: from the moment you desire something until you get it, there is suffering, even when the goal is तत्त्वज्ञान. "मैं नहीं, पतञ्जलि ऋषि बोल रहे हैं."
26. (late) The second is परिणाम दुःख: after you get the thing, troubles follow (cracks in the house, dents in the car), along with unknown factors, the law of familiarity (maintenance drops) and what society says.
27. (late–end) The third is संस्कार दुःख: past experiences stored as संस्कार become the yardstick and spoil the present ("पहले तुम दरवाजा खोलते थे"). Every act enters memory as संस्कार. "Every event you have to take individually." This is where it is cut off.

## Key terms
- **ज्ञान**: knowledge that ends karma and gives moksha.
- **ज्ञानयज्ञ**: satsang like this one; better than द्रव्ययज्ञ.
- **द्रव्यमय/द्रव्य यज्ञ**: hard work, spending money, any tangible effort and the time it takes.
- **कर्मकांड**: puja, yajna, aarti and counted offerings; still karma.
- **फलश्रुति**: the eventual fruit of a practice (e.g. reaching a real guru, or hearing this teaching).
- **उपाय**: a remedy. As an "idea" it is a shortcut; as an "ideal" it means taking on the deity's qualities ("असली उपाय").
- **टोना-टोटका**: small remedies such as rings; the starting point only.
- **लौकिक**: worldly (doctors, lawyers, engineers).
- **ज्योतिष**: a वेदांग; gives उपाय as ideas.
- **विनायक**: विशेष नायक. **गणाधिपति**: leadership qualities.
- **भक्ति**: action done with प्रेम and अर्पण, seeing परमेश्वर in the other.
- **धर्म**: कर्तव्य कर्म; why, how and for whom one acts.
- **ध्यान**: the step above ज्ञान.
- **तत्त्वदर्शी ज्ञानी**: the teacher from whom ज्ञान must be received.
- **साष्टांग नमस्कार / शरणागति / सेवा**: the steps before knowledge is given.
- **ज्ञानरूप नौका / ज्ञानरूप अग्नि**: knowledge as a boat across sin, and as a fire burning karma.
- **अर्धांग**: the wife as half of the husband; she has a right to half the income.
- **स्थान-माहात्म्य**: the spiritual greatness of a place. **सप्तपुरी**: the seven sacred cities; Puri is one of them, a मोक्ष स्थान. **स्पंदन**: vibrations.
- **ताप दुःख**: suffering from desire until the thing is attained.
- **परिणाम दुःख**: suffering after attainment (maintenance, unknown factors, familiarity).
- **संस्कार दुःख**: suffering from comparing the present with stored past impressions.
- **संस्कार**: every act enters memory as an impression.
- **law of familiarity**: care drops once something becomes familiar.
- **अज्ञान**: the "उल्टा" (wrong) lesson drawn from experience.

## Boundary notes

- The दुःख topic is cut off mid-संस्कार दुःख, with the fourth दुःख still owed, so it almost certainly continues into Day 1, Part 2. Ashma Gita itself has not begun.
