## Opening and closing
- **Opening:** The session begins partway through a thought that continues the previous part. He says that even with vaccines and antibiotics some people are not saved, and that one is saved if one is with the guru [«तो vaccine, antibiotic होने के बाद»]. He then goes on to shlok 47 and remarks that the Ayurvedic doctor has already been dealt with (the काढ़ा does not help) and that now allopathy will be too, "as said back in Mahabharata times". This refers to shlok 46 in the previous part.
- **Closing:** The session is cut off mid-thought while he explains shlok 52. He has just said that one's own child will go elsewhere carrying its own प्रारब्ध, and a daughter will marry and go somewhere [«लड़की है, शादी होकर»].

## Teachings in order
1. (recap) (start) Vaccines and antibiotics do not save some people; one is saved if one is with the guru [«तो vaccine, antibiotic होने»]. Shlok 46 showed that the Ayurvedic doctor's काढ़ा does not stop old age, and now shlok 47 does the same for allopathy [«आयुर्वेदिक डॉक्टर का तो»].
2. (start) रसायन means chemical medicine. Allopathy-like medicine existed in those times too: "हिन्दू धर्म में अपने पास सब था". It is not true that it is foreign or only modern; healing by रसायन happened then too [«रसायन मतलब chemical वाला»].
3. (start) Ashma Rishi saw this then, and Vedavyasa saw it even earlier: whatever you do, allopathy or chemicals, old age will still come. Medicine can lessen it but cannot stop it [«ये अश्मा ऋषि को तभी»].
4. (start) Whoever is to die will die, whoever is to age will age, and if medicine is to make things worse it will. This does not mean you should not take medicine. You should take it: गुरु कृपा will grow so that you go to the right doctor for the right treatment. You must do पुरुषार्थ, and after doing all that you must leave the rest to प्रारब्ध [«इसका मतलब दवा लेने का नहीं»].
5. (early) स्वाध्याय includes the जाप of a mantra, but it means स्व का अध्ययन, the study of the self. Gaining आत्मज्ञान is स्वाध्याय [«तो स्वाध्याय शब्द का मतलब»].
6. (early) He explains the other people in shlok 48. The दानी gives daily, and दान can be of clothes, wealth or knowledge (विद्या दान); there are many kinds of दान. The यज्ञशील पुरुष acts in यज्ञ भाव, with a feeling of sacrifice [«उसके अभ्यास में लगे हुए»].
7. (early) Not even rishis and munis crossed physical जरा and मृत्यु; at most they delayed it. Chyavan Rishi stayed young by eating chyavanprash, which is named after him. Swami Vivekananda had many illnesses. "तो आपका कैसे होगा?" [«Physical जरा और मृत्यु को»]
8. (early) So don't worry about it. The right attitude is: what has come was my कर्म; I accept how it unfolded through प्रारब्ध, and I will start from today. With this attitude more than half of the illness and pain goes away at once [«तो don't have to worry»].
9. (early) He holds सत्संग even with a fever of 103–104 degrees, because he knows the healing comes from the सत्संग itself and the रसायन treatment can come later. It heals "यहीं पे बैठे-बैठे", through कर्म योग: "Work is worship" [«मैं बोलता हूँ ना, १०३-१०४»].
10. (early) Even these people do not escape the body's जरा and मृत्यु; otherwise no rishi or muni would ever look old. A slide adds that even a great philanthropist may suffer fatigue and illness in old age [«वो मृत्यु, शरीर की जरा»].
11. (early) Every moment that has passed never comes back [«ये फिर समझना चाहिए कि»].
12. (early) Children are the richest because they have time, "ऐसा भगवान बोलते हैं". They can learn and have no responsibilities. That is why everything should be learned in ब्रह्मचर्य आश्रम, free of the tensions of marriage and earning. The child only had to study, and putting the child in a suitable school was the parents' job [«बच्चे सबसे धनवान होते हैं»].
13. (early-middle) Your years went by repeating the same things, गणपति, season after season. "कब मैं धीरे-धीरे पचास का हो गया?" You never did anything different. Shankaracharya will remind us of this next [«अभी तक आपके कितने साल गए»].
14. (middle) The कालचक्र only moves forward and never comes back. A wasted minute is a minute of life gone, and if it was not used productively some दोष attaches to it. That is why the saints say to say "हरि मुखे म्हणा" nonstop: at least no पाप is done. "be positive", do at least this much instead of useless work [«काल चक्र है, पर वो»].
15. (middle) Gone means gone. A missed job offer is gone; a new one may come, but not that one. A successful person grasps an opportunity as soon as it comes [«गया मतलब गया»].
16. (middle) This is why he says "ये मौका चूकना मत" about साधना. There is no guarantee you will be available for the next one. It is कलियुग, and life itself is not guaranteed. The guru might be posted abroad, your time might be up, or you might be posted to another town [«"ये मौका चूकना मत", ये»].
17. (middle) "try to catch everything." You took the chance of coming here with the guru, but some missed the lake session, where a lot happened, and some came here and slept. "चूकना मत" [«इसलिए try to catch everything»].
18. (middle) "मेरा गुरु गलत नहीं हो सकता." If the guru is keeping you awake now, this knowledge must matter to someone, perhaps tomorrow or soon; that is why he brought it. "waste is waste", and it will not come back [«मेरा गुरु गलत नहीं हो सकता»].
19. (middle) Slide: making good use of every moment is the key to a life that is धर्मशील, meaningful and searching for the self.
20. (middle) Bhaja Govindam 7: the child is absorbed in play, the youth in attraction to the other sex, and the old person in worry, so ब्रह्मज्ञान and आत्मज्ञान are never gained and no one loves the Lord. People say they will start after 60 or after retirement, but such people will never think of taking God's name, even at the end. The right time is to start in childhood, or "जब जागो तब सवेरा" [«बचपन में खेल में रुचि»].
21. (middle) Bhaja Govindam 8: if you reflect a little on "who is your wife, who is your son", life starts to feel like a नाटक. You will still do your duties, but without आसक्ति and without चिंता, though you will do चिंतन [«किया थोड़ा सा विचार?»].
22. (middle-late) Bhaja Govindam 12: the seasons come and go every year. "आत्मज्ञान कब लेंगे?" Recall your college life and ask yourself what you have done to yourself since then; you probably wasted life chasing something, and आत्मज्ञान never happened [«आत्मज्ञान कब लेंगे? देखते-देखते»].
23. (late) Sometimes life is wasted chasing non-existent things in the name of a dream, sometimes chasing अहंकार ("इसका बदला लूँगा"), and अद्वैत is never understood. Shankaracharya says that day by day one never found time for आत्मज्ञान [«कभी-कभी तो non-existent चीज»].
24. (late) Example slides: every sunrise and sunset is unique, and festivals and meetings become only memories, so live each moment with awareness and affection.
25. (late) Shlok 50 has nothing special to teach: trees, dogs, cats and humans all walk the road of death. "One fine day death will come searching for you", whatever you have done [«इसमें तो कुछ सिखाने वाली»].
26. (late) Shlok 51: नास्तिक people say the जीव is born from the body through the union of the parents, and आस्तिक people say the जीव came from the निराकार. Whatever the view, the body will one day be left and that road must be taken. Even scientists must accept this, since they can get the same diseases [«नास्तिक लोग क्या बोलते हैं?»].
27. (end) Shlok 52: it is no one's fate to stay always with "मेरे उन दस लोगों" once grown up. Learn from birds, which fly away and are not आसक्त, while we are. Each has their own प्रारब्ध: a child goes elsewhere carrying its प्रारब्ध, and a daughter marries and goes away [cut off] [«ये किसी के भी नसीब में»].

## Main-text shlokas covered
- **47** (start): रसायनविदश्चैव सुप्रयुक्तरसायनाः। दृश्यन्ते जरया भग्ना नगा नागैरिवोत्तमैः॥. Meaning: even physicians who know रसायन and use it well on themselves are seen broken by old age, like trees struck down by great elephants. Commentary: brief to moderate (रसायन = allopathy existed then; medicine plus पुरुषार्थ plus प्रारब्ध).
- **48** (early): तथैव तपसोपेताः स्वाध्यायाभ्यसने रताः। दातारो यज्ञशीलाश्च न तरन्ति जरान्तकौ॥. Meaning: likewise ascetics, people devoted to study and practice, givers and those devoted to यज्ञ do not cross old age and death. The text was restored to the Gita Press reading by the transcriber. Commentary: long (स्वाध्याय, kinds of दान, Chyavan, Vivekananda, the fever story).
- **49** (early): न ह्यहानि निवर्तन्ते न मासा न पुनः समाः। जातानां सर्वभूतानां न पक्षा न पुनः क्षपाः॥. Meaning: days, months, years, fortnights and nights, once passed, never return for any being. Commentary: long (time, opportunity, "मौका चूकना मत").
- **50** (late): सोऽयं विपुलमध्वानं कालेन ध्रुवमध्रुवः। नरोऽवशः समभ्येति सर्वभूतनिषेवितम्॥. Meaning: the impermanent human, helpless before time, must take the great unavoidable road of death that all beings take. Commentary: brief. The reading "कालेन" was restored by the transcriber.
- **51** (late): देहो वा जीवतोऽभ्येति जीवो वाभ्येति देहतः। पथि सङ्गतमभ्येति दारैरन्यैश्च बन्धुभिः॥. Meaning: whether the body comes from the जीव or the जीव from the body, meeting wife and relatives is like meeting travellers on a road. Commentary: brief to moderate (आस्तिक versus नास्तिक views). Restored readings: सङ्गतम्, अन्यैश्च. The boundary between slide text and his speech here is uncertain.
- **52** (end): नायमत्यन्तसंवासो लभ्यते जातु केनचित्। अपि स्वेन शरीरेण किमुतान्येन केनचित्॥. Meaning: no one ever lives forever with anyone, not even with their own body, let alone with others. Commentary: brief, cut off.

## Other scripture cited
- **Bhaja Govindam 7** (middle): बालस्तावत्क्रीडासक्तः… परे ब्रह्मणि कोऽपि न सक्तः॥. Supports: every stage of life is spent in attachment, so start from childhood or "जब जागो तब सवेरा".
- **Bhaja Govindam 8** (middle): का ते कान्ता कस्ते पुत्रः… तत्त्वं चिन्तय तदिह भ्रातः॥. The phrase "कुत आयातः तत्त्वं" was restored by the transcriber from a garbled raw reading. Supports: reflect now, and life looks like a नाटक; do your duty without attachment.
- **Bhaja Govindam 12** (middle-late): दिनयामिन्यौ सायं प्रातः… तदपि न मुञ्चत्याशावायुः॥. Supports: time plays and life ebbs, but desire never ends; don't postpone आत्मज्ञान.
- **"हरि मुखे म्हणा"** (middle): a line from a Marathi abhang; the saint is not named. Supports: chant God's name nonstop so that at least no पाप is done.
- **"ऐसा भगवान बोलते हैं"** (early): he attributes "children are the richest because they have time" to भगवान. No verse is given and the source is unclear.

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- (early) Attitude to illness: accept it as one's कर्म and प्रारब्ध, "आज से शुरू करूँगा". Take medicine and seek the right doctor (पुरुषार्थ), then leave the rest to प्रारब्ध.
- (middle) Chant God's name nonstop ("हरि मुखे म्हणा").
- (middle) "ये मौका चूकना मत": don't miss sessions or साधना, and "try to catch everything".
- (middle) Reflect on "का ते कान्ता…" now.
- (late) Self-inquiry: recall your college life and ask what you have done with yourself since then.
- There is no Q&A. He asks the audience rhetorical questions ("किसके मत के अनुसार?", "किया थोड़ा सा विचार?").

## Key terms
- **रसायन**: chemical medicine; "allopathy" existed in those times.
- **स्वाध्याय**: includes mantra जाप, but properly स्व का अध्ययन, gaining आत्मज्ञान.
- **दान / दानी**: daily giving, of clothes, wealth or विद्या; there are many kinds.
- **यज्ञशील**: one who acts in यज्ञ भाव, with a sense of sacrifice.
- **जरा, मृत्यु**: old age and death of the physical body.
- **पुरुषार्थ / प्रारब्ध**: one's own effort, after which the outcome is left to destiny.
- **कर्म योग**: "Work is worship"; through it, healing happens.
- **कालचक्र**: the wheel of time, which moves only forward.
- **ब्रह्मचर्य आश्रम**: the life stage for learning everything, free of responsibility.
- **आसक्ति / आसक्त**: attachment; birds are not आसक्त, humans are.
- **चिंता vs चिंतन**: worry versus reflection; drop the first and keep the second.
- **आस्तिक / नास्तिक**: believers say the जीव comes from the निराकार; non-believers say it comes from the body through the parents' union.
- **अद्वैत, आत्मज्ञान, ब्रह्मज्ञान**: used as the goal that people never reach while chasing other things.
