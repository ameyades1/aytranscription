## Teachings in order
1. आत्मविश्वास means reaching the level of आत्म तत्त्व. It is "not just ऊपर-ऊपर का belief". You say again and again, with understanding, that the thing "हो गया" (start) `[«आत्मविश्वास मतलब आत्म तत्त्व»]`.
2. Seeing a goal as already done is called visualisation. His own example: he dreams that "भारत विश्व गुरु हो गया है", and that is why he keeps working and meeting all the gurus (start).
3. To understand this, he says to read the whole माण्डूक्य उपनिषद्. It describes three states, वैश्वानर / तैजस / प्राज्ञ, which are जाग्रत / स्वप्न / सुषुप्ति (start).
4. जाग्रत means working through शरीर बोध (part one). The स्वप्न state "is very essential" and must be joined to waking life. So साधना and ध्यान have to be combined with visualisation (start) `[«तो when you are awake»]`.
5. A "visionary" is someone who first sees something and then acts on what he saw (early).
6. Shankaracharya's Vivekachudamani 11 says results do not come from crores of कर्म ("कर्मकोटिभिः"). It "starts with" विचार ("विचारेण") (early).
7. Gita chapter 18, verses 13–15 put अधिष्ठान first: "First is base". A thought has to come first, then कर्म is done on it, and then things start happening by themselves. "तो that dreamer लगता है" (early).
8. The स्वप्न state is to be lived "जीवित होते हुए सपना देखना है", by entering ध्यान अवस्था. After that, सुषुप्ति has to be joined in as well (early).
9. सुषुप्ति is deep sleep. It is not only slumber or hibernation, and one sees darkness there. A stage 4 cancer patient feels no दुःख in deep sleep, and a king feels no सुख, because "he is unaware". This state, समाधि अवस्था, is to be joined to waking life. "अ, उ, म, तब ओंकार पूर्ण होता है" (early) `[«सुषुप्ति मतलब क्या? Deep sleep»]`.
10. Summary of the method: work through शरीर बोध, plus visualisation, plus साधना, with समाधि अवस्था added later (early).
11. A sculptor sees the कृष्ण or जगन्नाथ inside the stone before he starts, and he chips away whatever is not that (early-middle) `[«तो एक शिल्पकार होता है»]`.
12. In the same way, a guru first sees the भगवान / परमेश्वर inside a person, not a पापी. He tries to cut away काम, क्रोध, लोभ, मोह, मद, मत्सर, दंभ, दर्प, भय, शोक, कठोरता, क्रूरता, कटुता, and then the Krishna image appears by itself. "मैंने थोड़ी बनाई, अंदर पहले से थी, मुझे वो पहले दिखी" (early-middle).
13. Third part (recap and continuation): आत्मविश्वास in one's own योजना and also in one's प्रारब्ध. When a guru says "तेरा काम होने वाला है" and the person does not believe it, the प्रारब्ध can fail. "Not believing the guru is now a new पुरुषार्थ", a new कर्म. Without belief in one's own plan, "प्रकृति बोलती है, नहीं". Fear does not work; what is needed is पूर्ण self-confidence (middle) `[«तो third part क्या था?»]`.
14. He says Shankaracharya stated in several places that the first rung of success is to say beforehand that the work "हो गया" (middle).
15. Fourth: गुरुकृपा. Without it, the other three together will still not bring the result. "You require all four to create something special." The story under discussion (from the earlier session) is about a man who only weeps over प्रारब्ध (middle) `[«Fourth thing is गुरुकृपा»]`.
16. Even negative work needs all four. A terrorist needs planning, प्रारब्ध, आत्मविश्वास, and the कृपा of a निषिद्ध गुरु (like शुक्राचार्य for the असुरs). Bad people often have more self-confidence than good people do, "कलियुग है" (middle).
17. The same applies to वशीकरण ("ये मैं promote नहीं कर रहा हूँ"), because this is the law of प्रारब्ध. For फलश्रुति you need all four: योजना, प्रारब्ध, आत्मविश्वास, गुरुकृपा. Good work needs a शुभ गुरु and bad work an अशुभ गुरु, but either way गुरुकृपा is required (middle).
18. On the IVF slide: people who have every resource still sometimes cannot have children. "कभी-कभी विधान स्वीकार करना पड़ता है, प्रयत्न करने के बावजूद" (middle).
19. Shlok 25: death can come in many ways. The verse lists a few, and today there are thousands more (covid, heart attack, suicide) (middle) `[«तो अनेक प्रकार से आदमी»]`.
20. If death by liver cancer is fixed at birth, then the life leading to it is also arranged. He will drink at some point, will be born into a suitable home rather than a संन्यासी's, and will find the friends that lead there (middle) `[«Liver cancer से ही जाना है»]`.
21. For a heart attack the arranged causes are a life without unconditional love, a hurried राजसिक life, or heavy fried food. Even someone born in a Brahmin family will have the big belly and get the matching environment (middle).
22. पुरुषार्थ can still act. If the horoscope shows a heart attack at 68, from age 25 or earlier he can reduce anger, control diet, slow down, and increase प्राणायाम and आसन (middle).
23. नियत means "ordained, higher", which is why the word नियति is used. The cause fixed at birth is the "सेतु" by which one goes to परलोक (middle).
24. Markandeya story (see Stories). Lesson: "हर चीज़ change की जा सकती है, ये सत्य है, but जब ये चारों चीज़ें मिलती हैं, तभी". Here भक्ति changed the fixed lifespan (middle-late).
25. To reach a high position or change प्रारब्ध completely, force is needed from three directions. (1) Your own पुण्य from many births: before calling someone corrupt, see that he rose on that. (2) The पूर्व-पुण्याई of your वंश, parents and ancestors. (3) The whole गुरु-मंडल परंपरा and its ज्ञान. "नहीं तो, हो ही नहीं सकता" (late) `[«एक, स्वयं का कई जन्मों»]`.

## Key terms
- **आत्मविश्वास**: going to the level of आत्म तत्त्व, not surface belief.
- **वैश्वानर/तैजस/प्राज्ञ; जाग्रत/स्वप्न/सुषुप्ति**: the Mandukya states. Waking means working through शरीर बोध, dream is visualisation, deep sleep is unawareness of सुख and दुःख and is linked to समाधि.
- **शरीर बोध**: body-awareness, the basis of waking action.
- **ओंकार (अ, उ, म)**: complete when all three states are joined.
- **अधिष्ठान**: base, the first factor (Gita 18).
- **विचार**: comes before कर्म.
- **योजना, प्रारब्ध, आत्मविश्वास, गुरुकृपा**: the four things needed for फलश्रुति.
- **फलश्रुति / कर्मफल**: the result.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: one's own effort. Disbelieving the guru is "a new पुरुषार्थ".
- **निषिद्ध गुरु / अशुभ गुरु**: a guru of forbidden or bad work, e.g. शुक्राचार्य.
- **विधान**: what is ordained and sometimes has to be accepted.
- **नियत / नियति**: "ordained, higher".
- **निमित्त / सेतु**: the instrumental cause of death, the bridge to परलोक.
- **कल्प**: unit of time, as in "16 कल्प".
- **पूर्व-पुण्याई**: ancestral merit.
- **गुरु-मंडल परंपरा**: the guru lineage.
- **अलक्ष्मी / लक्ष्मी**: the Devi's two forms toward sinners and the virtuous.
- **सत्त्व गुण / रजोगुण**: real सत्त्व awakens ज्ञान, सुख, वैराग्य, चैतन्य, विवेक शक्ति, while being drained marks रजस्.
- **विश्वगुरु / जगद्गुरु**: India's (and his) goal.
