## Opening and closing
**Opening:** The recording begins mid-sentence, criticising the idea that offering a set amount of water, chadars or candles brings results ("इतना-इतना जल चढ़ाओ…"). It then moves to the "अश्मा गीता की सीख" slides, which close the भूमिका (introduction).
**Closing:** The session is cut off mid-thought. He is explaining why a संकल्प involving two people (e.g. "पति भी मेरी सुने") takes time until their agendas align (end). The End Time in the source is blank.

## Teachings in order
1. Results do not come from fixed quantities of offerings (so much water, chadar or candle), in any religion. He asks, "तो किससे होता है?" (start) [«इतना-इतना जल चढ़ाओ तो होगा»]
2. (Slide) When great शोक and ग्लानि overwhelm life, आत्मज्ञान is the only path to peace. He links this to Krishna's "ज्ञान की नौका" (start).
3. You cannot come straight to ज्ञान. That is why the Jagannath temple and other तीर्थक्षेत्र exist (start).
4. (recap) The base is "कर्म के ऊपर भक्ति, भक्ति के ऊपर ज्ञान, ज्ञान के ऊपर ध्यान". ज्ञान is called the fruit of कर्म (start). [«कर्म के ऊपर भक्ति»]
5. The फलश्रुति of भक्ति is that you come before a Guru. A further फलश्रुति is that you meet the Guru in a तीर्थक्षेत्र. This is the only path to peace (start).
6. (Slide) If even the right path brings a painful result, accept it as ईश्वर का विधान. He calls this "बहुत important" (early).
7. How to analyse दुःख: either I did wrong and the bad fruit came, so correct it; or I believe I did right and still got a bad fruit (early). [«how to analyze, अगर मुझे दुःख»]
8. The real problem is अहंकार saying "मैंने तो सही किया था". That doubts ईश्वर का विधान (early).
9. Two possibilities follow. Either what I think is correct is actually wrong, "क्योंकि फल सत्य है". Or I am misunderstanding the fruit: the action was right, but the fruit is not the one I wanted (early).
10. Gita 2.47: you have no right over the fruit. Krishna gives the fruit by his choice, not yours, and what he gives for a good action must also be good. It may not feel good today, but it will be good in the long run (early).
11. Nachiketa example: sending him to death looks bad, but it ends in आत्मज्ञान. A painful phase may be "a transit phase" (early–middle).
12. A small loss in business may prevent a crore-sized loss later. "आज छोटे में वो समझा रहा था" (middle).
13. "सही चलके दुःख कभी भी नहीं आ सकता". Right action brings a right fruit, and apparent दुःख is an intermediate step. Wrong action with a wrong fruit means correct it. Right action with a "wrong" fruit means you misread the fruit (middle).
14. (Slide) Even those who follow धर्म can sometimes fall into शोक. Krishna, Dnyaneshwar Mauli, Eknath Maharaj and Shri Ram ("सीते-सीते करके रोने वाला") all shed tears, though they knew and had chosen their कर्म (middle). [«बड़े से बड़े श्रीकृष्ण की भी»]
15. The slide says "कभी-कभी", not always. If they faced शोक, we will too. Accept दुःख and keep going, but you are not told to stay sad forever (middle).
16. Gita 2.14: "तू सहन कर". Enduring is the beginning, not the end; "गीता वहाँ खत्म नहीं होती, गीता वहाँ से शुरू होती है" (middle).
17. The Gita sequence applied to a bad husband: first endure and don't divorce at once. Then understand the past birth (he attributes this to chapter 3), then कर्म योग and effort, then भक्ति, then ध्यान (ch. 6). Finally understand the विराट रूप, meaning it is like a dream: "मैंने ही कुछ कारणवश ऐसा पति create किया होगा" (middle). [«अब पति खराब आ गया»]
18. One who endures अन्याय is called पापी. Enduring poverty, by contrast, means not stealing or robbing. Go to a Guru to learn श्री विद्या and ask where you went wrong (e.g. not giving दान). Then "you can restore your destiny" (middle).
19. Great followers of धर्म are seen in शोक, but they do not immediately do wrong (middle).
20. The height of this is Shri Ram on his राज्याभिषेक day: told he must go to वनवास, he says "I am going to the kingdom of forest". He was not upset because kingship was never his aim (middle).
21. Ram's aim was सेवा: of the people in Ayodhya, and of ऋषि-मुनि, आदिवासी and animals in the forest. His काल and प्रारब्ध were sending him there. This is "साक्षी भाव", with no quarrel with प्रारब्ध (middle).
22. Jesus on the cross forgave and did not condemn his killers, and this made him great. That is साक्षी भाव. He knew he would rise after three days (Good Friday, Easter). "He was aware" (middle).
23. (Slide) आत्मबोध can wake them up again. Dnyaneshwar Mauli was taunted as a संन्यासी's son and made to cry over the शुद्धि पत्र, but "he could fight" through आत्मबोध. His अद्वैत state continued, with no द्वेष even towards enemies (middle).
24. (Slide) Your life is a battlefield; take refuge in the Guru and become the true king of your life. "ये इसका सार है". Otherwise you stay sad and poor (middle).
25. The भूमिका ends here. Shlok 1 of the Ashma Gita, where "आपकी किताब शुरू होती है", begins next. He asks who has already found answers in the introduction (middle–late). [«तो अश्मा गीता, यहाँ से श्लोक एक»]
26. What to expect from this Gita: little of the पुरुषार्थ or "changing destiny" part. It is about problems where you must accept and wait for a while, as even ऋषि-मुनि and अवतार did (late).
27. Making a संकल्प is no problem in itself. A small selfish संकल्प brings problems; one made for परमार्थ, निःस्वार्थ and निष्काम does not. But संकल्प सिद्धि must also happen (late). [«तो simple concept याद रखना»]
28. Even a निष्काम, large संकल्प brings दुःख if it is not fulfilled: becoming king for the people, or serving without money or donors (late).
29. Stages: संकल्प (1), निष्काम (2), reduced दुःख (3). Then a long interval may pass before सिद्धि, e.g. five years (late).
30. That interval, the काल described in the Ashma Gita, is your own कर्म. With good प्रारब्ध and a right संकल्प the fruit would come at once. The delay means "still you are not ready" (late).
31. What blocks you is your own कर्म, not the government or a neighbour; they can be निमित्त. Or you may not yet be fit to handle the position (late).
32. Someone who wants to be king but imagines kingship as power and fun, and has never done ground-level party or social work: प्रकृति will not hand the nation such a king. It will give many lessons, possibly stretching into the next birth (late).
33. A संकल्प only for oneself (e.g. "मैं बीमार से स्वस्थ हो जाऊँ") involves only your own कर्म in the interval (late).
34. A संकल्प for a happy married life involves two people's कर्म, and possibly a third party's (a spouse who quarrels, or cheats). So it takes longer (late).
35. The more people involved, the bigger and more "dangerous" the संकल्प. "भारत विश्व गुरु हो जाए" needs all Indians of every वर्ण, जाति, धर्म and gender, and other religions willing to accept India as guru, to align. Bigger संकल्प means more people, more time; if aligned, it can be instant (late–end). [«भारत विश्व गुरु हो जाए»]
36. In healing and शिव साधना: he wishes everyone well, each person wants their own healing, and others pray for them. So everyone is aligned, with no problem. The problem comes if the healer has दक्षिणा or another motive in mind (end). [«तो जब हम healing करते हैं»]
37. If everything is settled ("prepaid, preplanned"), with संकल्प, purity and प्रारब्ध aligned, the result is instant: the "now and here" result. "science of healing सब hit होगा" (end).
38. Why it sometimes takes time: wanting "पति भी मेरी सुने" involves two people, and nothing happens until their agendas align. The recording is cut off here (end).

## Other scripture cited
- **कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन** (Gita 2.47, stated). Supports: you have no right over the fruit, and what God gives for good कर्म is good in the long run (early).
- **"ज्ञान की नौका"** (Krishna). A reference to the boat of knowledge, probably Gita 4.36 but not recited or numbered. Supports: आत्मज्ञान as the way past शोक (start).
- **Gita 2.14** (stated by number, verse not recited), paraphrased as "तू सहन कर". Supports: enduring is the beginning (middle).
- **Gita sequence:** chapter 3 = "पिछला जन्म समझ ले" (his attribution; uncertain against the text), chapter 6 = ध्यान, विराट रूप near the end (middle).
- **Kathopanishad, Nachiketa:** a paraphrase of "मैं उत्तम शिष्य हूँ … कम से कम मध्यम शिष्य तो मैं हूँ" (cf. Katha 1.1.5, not recited). He also says Nachiketa learned श्री विद्या and freed his father from पितृऋण, which is his own claim. Supports: suffering after right action leads to good (early–middle).
- **Jesus:** "O Lord, please forgive them, for they do not know what they are doing" (Luke 23:34; the Bible reads "Father, forgive them"). Supports: साक्षी भाव (middle).

## Stories, examples and images
- **Offerings by quantity** (water, chadar, candle) as false formulas, across religions (start).
- **Jagannath temple and तीर्थक्षेत्र:** they exist because one cannot jump straight to ज्ञान (start).
- **Nachiketa:** sent to death by his father Vajashravas. At twelve he is confident he is an उत्तम (or at least मध्यम) शिष्य and reads the event as "something good on the way". He returns from Yama with the whole Kathopanishad, श्री विद्या, पितृऋण मुक्ति and आत्मज्ञान (early–middle).
- **Business loss:** a loss of ten thousand or a lakh now saves crores later (middle).
- **Tears of the great:** Krishna, Dnyaneshwar Mauli, Eknath Maharaj, Ram crying "सीते-सीते" (middle).
- **Bad husband:** a step-by-step Gita approach (endure, understand, कर्म, भक्ति, ध्यान, विराट रूप as a dream "I created") (middle).
- **Poverty:** don't steal; endure, go to a Guru, learn श्री विद्या, find the cause (e.g. not giving दान), restore your destiny (middle).
- **Ram's वनवास:** "Idea change हो गया है" (wording as in the source); "I am going to the kingdom of forest"; सेवा as his aim (middle).
- **Jesus on the cross;** Good Friday and Easter, aware he would return (middle).
- **Dnyaneshwar Mauli:** taunted as a संन्यासी's son, asked for a शुद्धि पत्र, had a buffalo recite the Vedas, and later the wall episode ("दीवार उड़ाकर दिखाई", read as the moving-wall story). He fought without द्वेष (middle).
- **Life as a युद्धभूमि; becoming the true राजा of your life** (middle).
- **Aspiring king** who has never done party or social work and sees kingship as power and fun (late).
- **Spouse example:** two people's कर्म, a quarrelling partner, a cheating partner (third party) (late).
- **"भारत विश्व गुरु":** called a "dangerous", huge संकल्प needing everyone's alignment (late).
- **Foundation healing / शिव साधना sessions:** aligned intent gives a "now and here" result (end).

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- Instruction for when दुःख comes: check whether your कर्म was wrong (then correct it) or whether you misread the fruit (then accept it as विधान) (early–middle).
- Instruction to first सहन करो, then take Gita's further steps; don't divorce at once; don't steal when poor; go to a Guru (middle).
- Rhetorical questions to the audience: "कितने जन को अभी तक answer मिला?" from the भूमिका; "are you now clear… What is expected in this गीता?" (middle–late). No answers are recorded.

- No meditation and no Q&A exchange.

## Key terms
- **आत्मज्ञान / आत्मबोध:** self-knowledge; the only path to peace; what lets saints "fight" without द्वेष.
- **ईश्वर का विधान:** God's ordering; accept painful results of right action as this.
- **फलश्रुति:** the fruit or outcome (e.g. of भक्ति: meeting a Guru).
- **फल सत्य है:** the fruit is true; if it is bad, either the कर्म or your reading of the fruit is wrong.
- **transit phase:** apparent दुःख as an intermediate step.
- **सहन:** endurance; "beginning, not end".
- **विराट रूप:** understanding that life is like a dream one created.
- **साक्षी भाव:** witness attitude; no quarrel with प्रारब्ध (Ram, Jesus).
- **प्रारब्ध / काल:** destiny and time, which send you where you can serve.
- **पुरुषार्थ / changing destiny:** the part this Gita will cover less.
- **संकल्प / संकल्प सिद्धि:** resolve and its fulfilment. The interval between them is your own कर्म.
- **निष्काम / निःस्वार्थ / परमार्थ:** selfless, for the higher good; such a संकल्प does not cause the "small selfish" problem.
- **निमित्त:** an instrument (government, neighbour), not the cause.
- **अद्वैत state:** Dnyaneshwar's non-dual state, with no द्वेष to enemies.
- **श्री विद्या:** to be learned from a Guru to restore destiny.
- **now and here result:** instant सिद्धि when संकल्प, purity and प्रारब्ध align.

- **Comparison of religions (start):** a remark on offerings of candles and chadars in "दूसरे धर्म". Possible sensitivity.
