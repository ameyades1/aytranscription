## Teachings in order
1. (start) Meeting unpleasant things (अप्रिय वस्तु) comes first in life, because settling them was your ऋणानुबंध: a spouse you don't get along with, a boss you don't get along with. Sometimes the guru says leave it. It causes दुःख only to the one who doesn't understand that this is a picnic. [«तो यहाँ पर अप्रिय वस्तु»]
2. (start) Losing what is very dear (son-attachment, wealth-attachment) also brings sorrow. The problem is not the object but my holding on to it. I made things प्रिय-अप्रिय, and that is राग-द्वेष. If all was God, come "to teach me a lesson, to play with me", the sorrow goes. [«At the same time अत्यंत प्रिय»]
3. (start) Shlok 18 is read: meeting the unpleasant, parting from the dear, अर्थ-अनर्थ and सुख-दुःख all come by the विधान of प्रारब्ध. "That is a नियम."
4. (early) प्रारब्ध is not a lottery. It is the संस्कार from the karma of past births plus the ऋणानुबंध formed with others, which I came to settle in this birth ("थोड़ा मुझे किसी का ग़ुस्सा सुनना ही है"). It always comes with दुःख. Even an अवतार, गुरु or सद्गुरु gets problems and solves them in some way. Everyone will get them. [«प्रारब्ध क्या है? Lottery नहीं है»]
5. (early) The one who crosses this has reached there while still alive. He calls it विदेह मुक्ति and also says जीवन्मुक्त. Such a person remembers every minute that this place is temporary and that the real home is elsewhere. [«तो जो उसे पार कर पाता है»]
6. (early) Running out of money is no problem: "मैं असली में भिखारी नहीं हूँ", the bank back home has everything and the return ticket is booked. In the same way the 70-90 years only had to be lived out, but "main सच्चिदानंद मुझे भूलना नहीं था". Forgetting this is the problem.
7. (early) Times of poverty and साढ़ेसाती come and they change. Pass them while doing पुरुषार्थ. Understanding प्रारब्ध first is "not blindly accepting"; पुरुषार्थ comes after it. [«मतलब कुछ time दरिद्रता का»]
8. (early, slide "अर्थ") People suffer because they want the dear always with them, never to face the unpleasant, wealth to grow and pain to stay away. He says none of this is possible: "ये सोच ही पहले उल्टी है". If you want that, take मुक्ति and go back; once you are born, all of it will come.
9. (early) We were born to enjoy these द्वन्द्व ("इनका आनंद लेने के लिए आए थे"). "You are not a human being who has come to experience the divine… You are a divine being who has come to experience the human form." You were that God, come to enjoy the human form. You did not come to search temples, शक्तिपीठ and ज्योतिर्लिंग. [«जबकि इन द्वन्द्वों को ही enjoy»]
10. (early-middle) The द्वन्द्व that causes असंतोष should have caused आनंद. A quarrel means the time to settle a debt has come. Whatever war comes unsought (अनायास प्राप्त) must be fought like युधिष्ठिर (recap: "बोला ना"). Money running short means "श्री विद्या सीखने का time आ गया", and the guru will take me toward मोक्ष.
11. (middle) Meeting the guru should feel like meeting someone from your own village or language who will take you home. Instead people get tense: "when will the guru finish, when is lunch?"
12. (middle) He is making Ashma Gita as simple as possible: where did we go wrong? This would be the essence of all the Gitas, and this one specifically deals with प्रारब्ध and समय.
13. (middle) प्रारब्ध is what you yourself asked for ("माँग के लिया था") and then forgot, and someone now reminds you. Whether a guru comes into your life, and who reminds you, you also decided. It depends on whether the guru is उच्च or नीच in the पत्रिका. Whatever the chart says, in this birth you must do it by पुरुषार्थ. [«प्रारब्ध lottery से नहीं आता»]
14. (middle) If the guru is नीच in your chart, don't cry. Seek the guru the way one seeks a doctor for cancer, even abroad. Body-love makes us seek doctors (शरीर-बोध). With आत्मबोध we would cross states to find the guru, the doctor for चिंता, शोक, दया, क्रोध.
15. (middle, slide) A person is not free in what comes, only in the reaction: "वह केवल प्रतिक्रिया में स्वतंत्र है". A nagging spouse or boss is given; how I react is mine. "Ninety percent of your problems come because of प्रतिक्रिया." [«इतना समझा? जो मिलना है»]
16. (middle) What looks like an action against you may itself be the प्रतिक्रिया of your own action in a past birth ("pending work"). "पाप प्रतिक्रिया में होता है… क्रिया में कम होता है."
17. (middle) There is less fault in क्रिया, because the doer is aware he is acting. प्रतिक्रिया comes as a reflex from संस्कार. When it has become a reflex, it has gone into संस्कार. Train-push example (see stories).
18. (middle-late) Keep unconditional love even in the reflex. Only then can the answer come through साम, दाम, दंड, भेद. Total tolerance is also wrong (snake story): "You have to be strict, थोड़ा-सा, कभी-कभी". Excess is wrong too (beating a child to a fracture). दंड is allowed, but measured, without forgetting unconditional love. Freedom in reaction = पुरुषार्थ. What you receive is not free; it comes from प्रारब्ध. The line "तीनों गुणों जैसी प्रतिक्रिया इतनी नहीं करनी है" is unclear.
19. (late, slide "उदाहरण") One person works hard and still loses; another earns without effort. That is not karma alone but प्रारब्ध's विधान. He says the same is in Bhagavad Gita ch. 16 and in Chanakya Niti (arthashastra).
20. (late) Dhirubhai Ambani said his secret was नियति/destiny: he kept doing his karma with no right to its fruit. People at the top know this, while those lower down say "मैं मेहनती हूँ".
21. (late) Accept first. Chanakya Niti, as he cites it: intelligence and karma of this birth have no relation to your wealth; it comes from past births, unless it joins with the guru's श्री विद्या. He says अर्थ and काम come from the past birth, while धर्म and मोक्ष come in this birth through पुरुषार्थ (partly unclear). [«First you have to accept»]
22. (late) Shlok 19: birth, death, and gain and loss during life all rest on प्रारब्ध. You don't remember your own birth (caesarean or natural, poor family), but its circumstances were fixed. Death (heart attack, natural, समाधि) is also fixed. He cites Chanakya Niti 4.1 as learned earlier (recap).
23. (late-end) Expectation maths: the dream is 100 and प्रारब्ध is 60. The 60 comes easily, the 40 shortfall brings sorrow, and पुरुषार्थ is needed only for those 40. Expect 20 when प्रारब्ध is 60 and it feels like a lottery. Expect 200 crore and even a big win feels short. "It is all on my idea", and then we blame प्रारब्ध. Desiring "और चाहिए" is allowed, but it needs much extra effort, and "that श्री विद्या होगी". [«तो मुझे सौ रुपए»]

## Practices, instructions and Q&A
- Instruction: remember सच्चिदानंद, your real home, every minute.

- Instruction: accept प्रारब्ध first, then do पुरुषार्थ, and seek the guru actively even if far away.

## Key terms
- **ऋणानुबंध**: a karmic debt-bond with someone, which you came to settle in this birth.
- **प्रारब्ध**: not a lottery. It is past-birth karma, संस्कार and ऋणानुबंध, which you asked for yourself and forgot.
- **विधान**: the law or rule of प्रारब्ध ("that is a नियम").
- **राग-द्वेष**: the making of प्रिय-अप्रिय, the real problem.
- **द्वन्द्व**: the opposites; you were born to enjoy them.
- **विदेह मुक्ति / जीवन्मुक्त**: reaching the goal while alive by remembering that this is temporary. He uses the two terms together.
- **पुरुषार्थ**: effort, and the freedom found in reaction. He divides it into अर्थ/काम (from the past) and धर्म/मोक्ष (from this birth).
- **क्रिया / प्रतिक्रिया**: action (aware, less fault) vs reaction (reflex from संस्कार, where पाप happens).
- **साम, दाम, दंड, भेद**: the four modes of response, all grounded in unconditional love.
- **शरीर-बोध / आत्मबोध**: body-identity (seek the doctor) vs Self-identity (seek the guru).
- **श्री विद्या**: the guru's practice that can bring wealth beyond प्रारब्ध.
- **नियति**: destiny = प्रारब्ध (Ambani anecdote).
- **साढ़ेसाती**: an astrological hard period that passes.
- **उच्च/नीच गुरु in पत्रिका**: the guru's placement in the horoscope, which decides how easily a guru comes.
