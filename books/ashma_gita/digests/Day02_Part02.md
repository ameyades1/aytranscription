## Teachings in order
1. (start) After success, a spouse may claim the credit: "I wore the burqa, I cooked, that is why he could stay there." "Everybody does that." [«पत्नी को अहंकार होता है»]
2. (start) With तत्वज्ञान one would say "हमारा मिलकर प्रारब्ध ऐसा था" instead. Without it, good or bad outcomes start to affect the चित्त. (The wording here was unclear in the raw transcript; see the notes.)
3. (start) The slide meaning: high family (उच्च कुल), सिद्धियाँ, special recognition, or awards like Bharat Ratna make ego arise automatically.
4. (start) (recap, from the Durga Saptashati teaching) How ego changes form. First comes natural ego (of रूप etc.). When that goes, there is the ego of suffering ("मैंने दुःख सहा है, तूने क्या किया है?"). Then the ego of the guru and of sadhana ("मेरे गुरु तो ऐसे हैं… मुझे तो साधना मिली"). Then the ego of siddhi ("मैं सिद्ध हूँ, मेरे पास शक्ति है"). The last is "मेरे गुरु से मैं बड़ा", and after that the guru himself breaks the ego. [«दुर्गा सप्तशती में सीखा है»]
5. (early) Slide: the person starts to think of himself as 'कुछ अलग', 'कुछ ऊँचा', superior to ordinary people.
6. (early) Ego also comes in suffering, even out of guilt: "मैं दुःख सह रहा हूँ" is itself an ego. Achievement-ego is "far better", because ego needs something real behind it (looks, money, a great father). "It should exist."
7. (early) मिथ्या अहंकार means ego with nothing behind it in reality: "एक दिन ऐसा होने वाला है, इस ज्योतिषी ने बोला है", "मेरा तो होने वाला ही था", or "actually तो मैं हूँ" when someone else is being honoured. It doesn't last long and gets no recognition. "सुख-दुःख दोनों में अहंकार आता है, बचके रहना।" [«मिथ्या मतलब कुछ किया ही नहीं»]
8. (early) The ego of inherited grievance: "four or six generations we have suffered injustice, the आंदोलन goes on". Suffering itself starts to feel like उच्च कुल. Agitate or not, it is no problem to him, but the बुद्धि will become भ्रष्ट and आत्मज्ञान will go. [«हमारे पूर्वज चार पीढ़ी से»]
9. (early) Slide: the three sentences "मैं कुलीन हूँ, मैं सिद्ध हूँ, मैं साधारण नहीं हूँ" fully water (सींच देते हैं) ego in the चित्त.
10. (early) This happens silently. A real disciple should start labelling it from this angle. Remember "गुरु ने बोला था": when दुःख comes, some ego will come; when सुख comes, a सूक्ष्म ego is coming. In both, "मैं विचलित होकर कुछ गड़बड़ करने वाला हूँ". The feeling "मैं अलग हूँ" or "अन्याय मुझ पर हुआ है" arises in suffering too. [«ये silently होता है»]
11. (middle) Slide: "सिद्धि से पहले साधना कठिन होती है, पर सिद्धि के बाद विनम्र बने रहना और कठिन होता है।" Achieving is hard, sustaining the position is harder, and sustaining it with विनम्रता and तत्वज्ञान-आत्मज्ञान is harder still.
12. (middle) That last is possible only when आत्मज्ञान has come and वेदांत is "अश्म की तरह rock solid". [«अश्म की तरह rock solid»]
13. (middle) Shlok 8: a person with that ego becomes आसक्तचित्त in भोग, squanders ancestral wealth step by step (क्रमशः), and comes to think grabbing others' wealth is good.
14. (middle) This applies whether the person is happy or unhappy: "सुख का भी भोग होता है, दुःख का भी भोग होता है". He becomes attached to "मेरा दुःख, मेरा सुख". क्रमशः means step by step, not suddenly. "This is प्रकृति का नियम।" The guilt-ridden waste money on negative "उपाय", the happy on parties or overconfident investments.
15. (middle) Grabbing others' wealth gets justified as normal: "दुनिया करती है रे पाप", "पापी पेट का सवाल है". His reply: "पेट पापी नहीं था, तू था." Others earn honestly. [«पापी पेट का सवाल है»]
16. (middle) Slide example: children say "पिता का कमाया है, जो चाहूँ करूँ". The parents failed to give संस्कार ("let the child enjoy what we didn't"). That freedom will ruin the child.
17. (middle) Your comfort came from your प्रारब्ध, but in upbringing the child must learn the electric bill and switching off. "He has to earn on his own." If a child gets a car from childhood, let him also travel hanging from a train sometimes. Otherwise he won't handle it when he becomes poor, as is "guaranteed", and will start to see corrupt practices as proper.
18. (middle) Slide: when the wealth runs out, they see no wrong in seizing others' wealth, rights or resources. Corruption grows, even in politics, because things came free (party and election through the family name).
19. (middle) Slide (heading: भोग में चित्त डूब जाने का परिणाम): one sunk in luxury loses विवेक and justifies seizing others' rights. Extreme comfort lowers tolerance.
20. (middle) Slide: "जिसने अपने भीतर का संयम खो दिया, वह बाहर की संपत्ति चाहे जितनी भी जोड़ ले, अंततः उसे भी खो देगा।" Without विनम्रता people leave him and the business won't last. A monopoly lasts only while प्रारब्ध is good, until a competitor offers better service.
21. (late) Shlok 9: as a व्याध (hunter or butcher) stops deer with arrows, kings stop by दंड the person who crosses मर्यादा and seizes others' wealth.
22. (late) Once the habit settles in the mind, through extreme happiness ("मेरे बाप का है") or extreme suffering ("इतनी चोरी से क्या होगा, बाद में प्रायश्चित कर लूँगा"), it does not leave.
23. (late) If corruption has settled in a minister's mind and the public keeps electing him, it only grows.
24. (late) An ordinary employee with no दंडनीति in the office will do something wrong. "फिर एक दिन वो दंड गुरुतत्व देगा", or the king, as the shlok says. Beyond a limit प्रकृति won't tolerate it. Society bears it for a while, but only up to a limit.
25. (late) Gita 9.21 (he said 9.20): enjoyment lasts only while पुण्य lasts; after that comes a fall again, with rebirth and the cycle of सुख-दुःख.
26. (late) There was some पुण्य or service behind the wealth (one's own, or the "पूर्व पुण्याई" of father and ancestors). When विनम्रता goes, the person falls back, and the cycle repeats.
27. (late) God or प्रकृति waits ("आज सुधरेगा, कल सुधरेगा"). When the limit of the परीक्षा ends, the king punishes, at the point of crossing मर्यादा. Small theft passes for a while.
28. (late) Theft doesn't last long: someone always reports it, and a pattern forms. "राजा ऐसे ही नहीं पकड़ता… कोई तो report करता है।"
29. (late) As ego and selfishness grow, thieves who joined together fall out, and one exposes the corruption. "प्रकृति ऐसा करवाती है।"
30. (end) Under दंड some reform and some get worse, for example by bribing the king or the police. Such a person finds someone just like himself, "और अभी पुण्य बाकी है" (cut off).

## Key terms
- **अहंकार:** ego, which changes form: natural, of suffering, of guru and sadhana, of siddhi, and finally "मेरे गुरु से मैं बड़ा".
- **मिथ्या अहंकार:** ego with nothing real behind it ("कुछ किया ही नहीं, तो भी अहंकार"); it doesn't last.
- **सूक्ष्म अहंकार:** the subtle ego that comes with सुख.
- **तत्वज्ञान / आत्मज्ञान:** what lets one say "हमारा मिलकर प्रारब्ध" and stay humble; बुद्धि भ्रष्ट means losing आत्मज्ञान.
- **प्रारब्ध:** destiny or past karma that brings wealth or comfort.
- **साक्षी भाव:** the witness state that comes after leaving ego.
- **सिद्धि / साधना:** attainment / practice.
- **विनम्रता:** humility, harder to keep after success.
- **अश्म:** rock, as in "rock solid वेदांत".
- **आसक्तचित्त:** a mind attached to भोग, whether of सुख or दुःख.
- **क्रमशः:** step by step, not suddenly.
- **संस्कार:** values that parents should give.
- **विवेक:** discrimination, lost in luxury.
- **संयम:** inner restraint.
- **व्याध:** hunter or butcher, one who kills animals.
- **मर्यादा:** the limit whose crossing brings दंड.
- **दंड / दंडनीति:** punishment / the policy of punishment (absent in offices).
- **गुरुतत्व:** gives दंड in the end ("दंड गुरुतत्व देगा", kept as spoken).
- **पुण्य / पूर्व पुण्याई:** merit, one's own or ancestral.
- **प्रायश्चित:** atonement, used as an excuse for theft.
- **प्रकृति का नियम:** nature's law.
- **आवागमन / पुनर्जन्म:** repeated birth.
