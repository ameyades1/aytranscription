# Book plan: अश्मा गीता (Ashma Gita)

## 1. Header

- **Source:** Ashma Gita Shibir by Acharya Upendra Ji, held near Jagannath Puri, Odisha, over 3 days.
  There are 36 recorded sessions: Day 1 has 11, Day 2 has 9 and Day 3 has 16. The clean transcript
  is about 104,218 words.
- **Working title:** जब जीवन आपको तोड़े, अश्मा गीता जोड़े. This is the Shibir's own title, from
  its title slide (D1P06 start).
- **Subtitle:** क्या आपके हाथ में है और क्या नहीं: महाभारत के शान्तिपर्व की अश्मा गीता पर प्रवचन
- **The text:** Ashma Gita (अश्मगीता) is a short उपपर्व in the Shanti Parva of the Mahabharata
  (राजधर्मानुशासन पर्व, chapter 28 in the Gita Press edition). It has three nested frames.
  Vaishampayana narrates the Mahabharata to Janamejaya. After the war, Vyasa consoles Yudhishthira,
  who blames himself for every death and wants to give up the throne, food and life. To do this,
  Vyasa retells an ancient dialogue in which the sage Ashma answers the grieving King Janaka of
  Videha. Ashma's teaching is that सुख and दुःख follow the body from birth, that प्रारब्ध and काल
  govern gain and loss, meeting and parting, old age and death, and that the wise person grieves
  for none of it. He ends with what to do instead: श्राद्ध, यज्ञ, the āśramas, the three debts and
  no दोषदृष्टि. The Shibir covers all **59 shlokas** as numbered on his slides (1–59, plus the
  पुष्पिका). On Day 1 he reads them only up to shlok 6, after a long भूमिका built on the
  Bhagavad Gita.
- **The book's arc** follows the Shibir's own movement through its five stages:
  1. **भूमिका:** why ज्ञान is needed, what दुःख is, the Bhagavad Gita background, Yudhishthira's
     guilt, the characters, and life as a धर्मयुद्ध (D1P01–D1P05).
  2. **Entering Ashma Gita:** prarabdh against purusharth, ऋणानुबंध, the "lessons" slides, and
     shlokas 1–6 (D1P05 late to D2P01).
  3. **The roots of sorrow:** ego, indulgence, wrongdoing, the two causes of mental sorrow, old age
     and death, and bearing what comes. This covers shlokas 7–16 and the Chitraketu story (Day 2).
  4. **The play of प्रारब्ध and काल:** shlokas 17–35 (Day 3, first half).
  5. **Relations, impermanence and the solution:** shlokas 36–59 and the final summary (Day 3,
     second half).

## 2. Chapter table

Session codes: D1P03 = Day01_Part03. Positions are start, early, middle, late and end, as in the
digests.
