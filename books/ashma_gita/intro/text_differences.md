The captions, profiles and Foundation page in `intro_pages.html` were checked line by line against the
photos of the booklet (OneDrive `Cover pages`, 14 photos). Every word matches, nothing is missing or
added, except the changes below. Decide for each: keep the change, or restore the booklet's form.

## Design, not text

- The booklet's dividers are a lotus between two rules (photo page) and a scroll ornament (end of
  profiles); the intro uses ✦ ❁ ✦.
